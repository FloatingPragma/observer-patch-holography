## Transfer to the full pole and complete component probe

quark at the same x and creates a down–anti-up pair there. Therefore the

    6c^2(1-q) <= ||P_out W psi_Lambda||^2 <= 7c^2.

## Separate same-source energy shell

The isolated complete proton and pion bands are continuous in momentum.
Exact rational half-angle phases refine two endpoints where their summed
energies lie strictly below and above the full Lambda interval. The
intermediate value theorem therefore supplies a kinematic energy shell.
The same electron anchor, strong length, speed and strong/strange inputs
are used on both sides; no coefficient is refitted. This is an energy-band
statement, not the construction of the full five-body scattering states.

A nonzero compact N5 current response and an open energy shell do not imply
that the source's interacting on-shell scattering projector has nonzero
current spectral weight. That transfer, the full weak vacuum and all other
weak channels, and a physical four-dimensional particle/current dictionary
remain required before a Lambda decay amplitude or lifetime can be claimed.
