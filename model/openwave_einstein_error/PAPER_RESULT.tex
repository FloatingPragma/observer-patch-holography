\paragraph{Quantitative null reconstruction.}
The explicit nine-direction inverse has sharp maximum entrywise error
constant $2+\sqrt3$: a uniform null-charge error $\epsilon$ gives a
trace-free tensor error at most $(2+\sqrt3)\epsilon$ in every entry.
Ward residuals propagate the remaining metric coefficient along the actual
difference graph. Centered differences require two-step connectivity;
checkerboard coefficients supply an exact counterexample to replacing it
with nearest-neighbour connectivity. A vacuum or trace input remains
necessary to determine the metric coefficient.
