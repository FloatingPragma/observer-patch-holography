# Particle masses and thermal records

Twenty executable calculations connect particle readouts, finite response
vertices, compiler resources and population dynamics. Their
[source-domain comparison](PROOF.md) distinguishes exact consequences of
declared laws from empirical mass calibration. The thermal detector joins
particle quantum numbers and number distributions on one calibrated
source. Finite spectroscopy, Majorana-phase observables, quantum bath
relaxation and prescribed expansion supply additional records on that
source. Exact archive compression reduces coherent lepton storage while
preserving the complete output. Physical particle identification requires
its interaction and clock attachment.

Run `python3 -m model.particle_cosmology_results --check` to authenticate the
receipts and their shared inputs. The scientific executors and tests live
in the twenty packages named in `results.json`; this comparison does not
replace their independent execution.
