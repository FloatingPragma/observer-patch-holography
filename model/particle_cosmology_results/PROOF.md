## Logical content

Each result is a consequence of its declared source, preparation,
reference, clock and apparatus. The comparison authenticates those
results and rejects conflicting shared input hashes, failed receipts and
unsupported physical promotions. Authentication establishes the identity
of the evidence being compared; it does not prove a physical transfer.

The `thermal_particle_records` consumer supplies an actual transfer:
joint charge, colour Casimir, helicity, momentum and energy eigenspaces
of `particle_joint_mass_identity` define commuting group-number operators
on the same quadratic source whose Gibbs preparation is computed by
`particle_thermal_history`. Its proof retains the entire fermion Fock
space, all capped boson words, colour coherence and untouched references.
This finite number instrument is distinct from the one-excitation probe
instrument. Its initial low-occupation probability is evaluated before
the latter's probe intervention.
