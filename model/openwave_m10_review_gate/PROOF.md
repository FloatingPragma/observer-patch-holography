## What the certification establishes

The M10.1 record reports agreement in seven exact quantities for the
equal-weight twelve-direction scalar dispersion. Three independently
written upstream implementations are executed in separate empty output
directories. Their entire JSON results must equal their committed outputs;
all exact expressions, scaling checks and negative controls are retained.
This replay knows the results and makes no blindness claim. The upstream
method note describes its own independent-method review and its limits.

The common expansion is

    omega² = k²-a² k⁴/20+(a⁴ k⁶/840)[1+(16/75) I6(n)]+O(k⁸),

where I6 is normalized to one on an icosahedron vertex direction. The
remainder follows directly from the cosine Taylor bound. Agreement
certifies the arithmetic of this declared object. It does not identify a
physical field, ruler, preferred frame or observed dispersion. The upstream
review assigns no criterion cell to it. The source package makes the same
distinction between an executed supporting calculation and a Nature match.

## The native-process gate

M10's explicit admission terms require the criterion observable to be
computed on the carrier's own repair/memory process in-platform. A declared
Hamiltonian, field, clock, action or coupling is an allowed comparison
construction. Such a component supplies no native-process implementation
proof merely because its labels or spectrum agree with another component.
The OPH mission does not require a unique architecture; this requirement
concerns the consequences of the architecture whose implementation is
claimed.

An executable bridge needs a pinned native program and apparatus,
preparation and reference maps, a map from actual event records to the
observable, and a bound comparing the complete output instruments over a
specified domain. Failures, no-operations, clocks and resource costs belong
to the same comparison. A field theorem may shorten that proof once its
hypotheses are established for the native process.

The three original Taichi tests in `openwave_admission_packet` concern the
exported180-coordinate source. This receipt retains their actual source
binding without presenting them as native twelve-port implementations.
Their mass coefficients have target exposure. Neither those replays nor
the definition-only dispersion certification supplies an unexposed physical
prediction. No application, comment, upstream pull request or award is made
by this package.
