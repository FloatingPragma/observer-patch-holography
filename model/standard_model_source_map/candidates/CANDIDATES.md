## The strongest actual charged lead

The primary Q6=6 regional endpoint has the exact weak/color-singlet weight and G1 source spinor transformation of a charged-lepton-singlet-type candidate. Under the all-left-Weyl convention it is the charged-lepton conjugate component; source conjugation reverses its charge. Its opposite endpoint and Wilson link remain in the full experiment. The complete pair is even. Coherent charge and spin counters give original energy records, but their pair transfer is not a single endpoint mass. A charged lepton also needs its other chiral component, physical flavor, interactions and a controlled dressed threshold or resonance.

## Other source excitations

The neutral LL e^c trion motivates a neutral-lepton-composite or sterile-like hypothesis; it has no established active-neutrino current dictionary. The color-singlet u^c(d^c)^2 trion motivates a neutral antibaryon/neutron-type composite hypothesis, with its opposite increment conjugate. Baryon/lepton number, physical spin and named composite identity remain hypotheses. These analogies sit outside the eighteen elementary targets. The isolated A1u gauge transition near 63 source units is a rare neutral collective mode, with original probability retained. Its cubic label and gauge origin do not identify a photon, gluon, Z or Higgs. Native gauge-current directions motivate boson candidates, but do not supply their massless or massive propagation.

## Assignments ruled out or unsupported

- **Excluded: particle_primary_regional_fermion:regional_endpoint_irrep → photon.** direct charge- and CAR-preserving component map under Q=T3+Q6/6. The charged odd endpoint has comparison Q=+1 and G1 central sign−1; the photon benchmark is neutral and bosonic.
- **Unsupported: particle_primary_regional_fermion:internal_pair_probe_irreps → photon.** identification of the full compensated pair as one propagating photon. Even parity and zero total charge do not supply a massless transverse field, its dispersion or a single-particle detector.
- **Excluded: particle_primary_composite_spin:weak_pair_charge_six → active_neutrino.** direct map of the local singlet to an unbroken lepton-doublet component with the same weak currents. Weak singlet and weak doublet have different current action. A separate Higgs-dressed/current dictionary could evade this direct-map exclusion and is unproved.
- **Excluded: particle_primary_composite_spin:color_triple → quark.** direct color-current-preserving elementary quark assignment. The composite is a color singlet; the quark benchmark is a color triplet.
- **Unsupported: particle_primary_reduced_sector:isolated_A1u_cluster → Higgs.** named physical identity from one finite A1u gauge line. A cubic total-state irrep does not determine intrinsic Lorentz spin. No radial-Higgs current, vacuum or physical pole dictionary is supplied.
- **Excluded: charged_lepton_right_conjugate → three_lepton_generations.** deriving three flavors by repeating one internal component. One Q6=6 weight and one regional spinor do not distinguish electron, muon and tau.
