# Candidate dictionary domains

The producer recomputes the fifteen internal weights with the existing
source algebra, then compares them with the frozen particle map. It
authenticates the protected-defect catalogue, primary charged regional
readout, exact composite spin kernels and isolated gauge-sector transition.
No empirical mass is used in these computations.

The status **possible** means that a native internal representation or
current direction supports a physical hypothesis. It does not identify a
particle. **Unsupported** means the computed properties do not select the
proposed named species. **Excluded** applies only to the exact direct-map
domain stated beside each comparison; it is not a no-go theorem for every
different dressing, state, source or current dictionary.

The existing component and catalogue IDs are retained. Primary excitation
IDs are qualified by their owning package and exact receipt field or
signed multiplet label. They are not inserted into the protected-defect
catalogue. A finite cube irrep is not assigned an intrinsic Lorentz spin,
and native occupation parity in the fifteen-state register is not
identified with fermionic exchange. Actual regional CAR parity and its
even compensated pair are taken from their physical source construction.

The Q6=6 singlet component motivates a charged-lepton-singlet-type
hypothesis. The same source entry is listed for electron, muon and tau;
its flavor index is null. Exact gauge-singlet trions do not have the
unbroken weak-doublet currents of an active neutrino. A different dressed
physical dictionary requires its own proof. The finite A1u gauge line
has no inferred intrinsic spin or named boson identity.

The color-singlet u^c(d^c)^2 component permits a neutral-antibaryon-type
analogy; its conjugate increment reverses that interpretation. LL e^c
permits a neutral-lepton-composite analogy. Both require physical
baryon/lepton-number and field dictionaries and sit outside the eighteen
elementary target rows. They are not promoted to observed composites.

The embedded finite-sector ratio certificate preserves one common energy
zero in its rational bounds. It excludes the direct member-zero mapping
of these neutral thresholds to the charged-lepton hierarchy, rather than
altering a source coefficient to improve a fit. The static loop-fusion
receipt preserves its rare original sector weight and complete sequential
apparatus. Different fused and disjoint energy windows are not moving
incoming particles or physical scattering cross sections.

Separate source domains retain their executed interaction evidence: the
four-mode contact echo and thirty-two-row native pointer controls are
executed, while the fine-grid incoming-packet program is not. Its exact
signal certificate, source code, proofs and frozen packet receipts are
authenticated independently of the primary source. No negative primary
execution flag erases those separate finite controls.

All eighteen target channels retain benchmark quantum numbers from the
existing atlas. These comparison data do not change any native source
weight, clock or mass coefficient. Target mass fields, physical particle
assignments and flavor assignments are empty. Mutation checks reject
promotions of these fields and loss of a target or protected catalogue ID.
