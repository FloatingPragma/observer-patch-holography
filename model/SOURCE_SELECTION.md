# Source, state and parameter contract

[CCF-LT-2](SPEC.md) is the official executable source family. A calculation
identifies its graph, complete Hamiltonian, auxiliary configuration,
preparation, reference, instruments and observation horizon. Labels shared
with another source do not transfer its numerical results.

## Calculated consequences

Exact checks establish native CAR action, complete polynomial recovery,
Hermiticity, charge-flow and Gauss preservation, compatibility of the actual
mass matrices, and the local-shard sum. Literal basis-column action includes
all occupation complements and both geometry layers. Finite matrix witnesses
calculate their own complete records, current/field response and meter work.
Their preparations and Hamiltonians define the scope of those numbers.

## Same-source identification

A physical species, rest mass, decay, clock or field is attached through
its actual preparation, interactions, complete detector and units. A pair
energy gap `2m` is recorded as `2m`. A stationary eigenstate's global phase
is not a self-starting observable clock. Calibrating an energy scale is
separate from deriving the dimensionless spectrum. A source scalar or a
capacity-potential minimum does not establish a cosmological constant.

The physical comparison also requires state selection, a common spatial
and temporal geometry, controlled errors and refinement, and independent
observations. The [dependency map](particle_joint_reconstruction/DEPENDENCIES.md)
records these interfaces. Other component models retain their own premises;
substituting their spectra or clocks for this source requires a proved map.
