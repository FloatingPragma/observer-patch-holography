On an eight-cell finite gauge source with 119-state Peter–Weyl links,
retain the complete interacting Hamiltonian, its physical ground
preparation, and a static compensating reference of charge \(+1\).
The local Gauss generators imply, for every cell subset \(S\),
\(F_S=\rho_S^Y+\mathbf1_{0\in S}Y_{\rm ref}\), where \(F_S\) is minus
the sum of the central hypercharge endpoint currents crossing the cut.
On an arrival effect selecting one nonblank link and local singlet
matter at every nonarrival cell, the origin Gauss constraint has a
one-dimensional link kernel. Its opposite endpoint fixes the total
arrival matter-plus-scalar charge to \(Y=Q=-1\). Thus
\(F_S=\mathbf1_{0\in S}-\mathbf1_{y\in S}\) holds exactly on each
arrival output. The cut-flux and charge spectral measurements commute;
all mismatch records have zero probability, including on retained
preparation and nonarrival branches. Full-Hamiltonian bounds at hopping
\(2^{-34}\) and source time \(2^{-24}\) give each of the six joint
arrival-charge-flux atoms probability between
\(6.430004784\,10^{-38}\) and \(1.274554272\,10^{-37}\).
The finite attachment includes the scalar and reference charges and
requires no current/winding identification. It supplies no physical
fine-structure constant or electromagnetic continuum limit.
