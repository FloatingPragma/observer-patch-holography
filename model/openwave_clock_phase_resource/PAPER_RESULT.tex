The phase boundary admits a branchwise proof. An energy-invariant joint
source and battery state, an energy-conserving unitary, and a battery
readout commuting with battery energy give an energy-invariant conditional
source state for every outcome. Exact execution retains all $4\times81$
native input and battery-word records. A two-copy native exchange creates
relative coherence in a degenerate energy block, whose free frequency is
zero; its interaction-driven population frequency is $2\lambda$, with
$\lambda$ a declared coefficient. An energy-mixing battery readout creates
the missing phase and has explicit mean work $m(p-1/2)$. These results
exclude the stated finite phase-free collision route without excluding
other declared observer architectures.
