The staggered tensor records admit an explicit co-location map: keep each
diagonal entry and average the four surrounding off-diagonal entries.
If $d_i(ab)$ bounds the actual neighbouring record variation, its transport
error is at most $\tau=\max_{a\ne b}(d_a(ab)+d_b(ab))/2$.
The existing sharp nine-null decoder therefore gives the staggered remainder
bound $\delta=(2+\sqrt3)\epsilon+\tau$ and the connected-edge metric-term
bound $a w+8\delta$. This supplies a finite record-transport premise;
physical curvature acquisition and nonlinear Einstein dynamics are not derived.
