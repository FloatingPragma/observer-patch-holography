# Ordered source correlations and the family coefficient

## Complete source and analytic reduction

Use the complete reset-port-two experiment in
[`native_family_record_memory`](../common_source/native_family_record_memory.py).
Its twelve-input transition matrix is `T`, its stationary distribution is
`pi`, and its actual geometric projectors are `P_j`. The probabilities of
the C and E events are exactly `1/12` and `1/6`. All 128 raw labels per
memory input are summed with their original weights. The logical pulse is
`exp(-ih V_j)`, with `V_j=X tensor P_j`. This pulse service and its coherent
clock are declared parts of the construction.

Write `L_j=-i[V_j,.]`. In row-major operator coordinates the complete
classical-quantum channel has blocks

`M(h)_(j,i)=T_(j,i) exp(h L_j)`.

It acts on twelve six-state matrix blocks, hence has dimension 432.
The marginal sums those blocks. The source also retains classical outcome
records; the response studied here sums their complete law without
conditioning on a record. These reduced response calculations trace that
archive. They do not replace its state by an independent blank.

The reset contraction gives a strict gap between the stationary memory
eigenvalue one and its other eigenvalues. Near `h=0`, analytic finite matrix
perturbation therefore gives a 36-dimensional slow invariant subspace.
Fix its coordinates by requiring that its embedding `E(h)` have identity
marginal. If `A(h)` is its induced step, then

`M(h) E(h)=E(h) A(h)`, `A(0)=I`.

The reduced generator `G(h)=log(A(h))/h` uses the principal logarithm
continuous at zero. This is an invariant-subspace calculation. Its
finite-step generator is not asserted to be a Lindblad generator, and its
embedding need not preserve positivity on every arbitrary marginal input.
The complete source channel is completely positive and trace preserving.

## Exact sum over ordered correlations

The [all-lag finite theorem](../particle_memory_response/PROOF.md)
owns the first two generator coefficients and their reduced finite-time
error. The producer calls its exact three-ray calculation and verifies
that the twelve-port covariance intertwines it under the map that merges
ports nine and ten. The projectors on those two ports are equal. The
calculation below evaluates the next generator coefficient and checks the
full twelve-input slow space without changing the source domain.

Let `Pi=pi 1^T` and `F=(I-T+Pi)^-1`. The stationary identity and strict
memory contraction imply

`sum_(l>=1)(T^l-Pi)=F-I`.

Define the real integrated covariance matrix

`C=(F-I/2-Pi/2) diag(pi)`, `S=(C+C^T)/2`, `A=(C-C^T)/2`.

Expanding the invariant-graph equation through second order gives

`G(h)=L_bar+h G_2+O(h^2)`,

`L_bar=sum_j pi_j L_j`, `G_2=sum_(j,i) C_(j,i) L_j L_i`.

The half equal-time term comes from the exponential pulse and logarithm;
the other terms sum every nonzero correlation lag. No correlation tail
or failed record is dropped. The mean Hamiltonian is `X tensor Y`, where
`Y=sum_j pi_j P_j` is the existing exact source coefficient.

The antisymmetric part of the covariance yields

`G_2 = -i[I_2 tensor J,.] + sum_(j,i) S_(j,i) L_j L_i`,

`J=(1/(2i)) sum_(j,i) A_(j,i) [P_j,P_i]`.

For this source it is exactly

```
J = 603 i / 6666724 *
    [[0, 3+sqrt(5), 0],
     [-3-sqrt(5), 0, -2],
     [0, 2, 0]].
```

This is a nonzero imaginary Hermitian matrix generated by time ordering.
The source pulse has a supplied coherent time parameter; no oriented phase
is inferred from a classical event probability alone.

Every principal minor of `S` on its four-port support is nonnegative by
exact rational arithmetic, and its rank is three. Its spectral
factorization therefore expresses the symmetric term as a sum of
Hermitian-jump Lindblad dissipators. Noise and coherent correction occur
at the same order. Discarding the noise would change the complete response.

Since `X^2=I`, the coherent correction has the Nambu factor `I`, whereas
the mean coefficient has the factor `X`. Its projection onto `X tensor Y`
is exactly zero. The expectation of `J` in each real eigenvector of `Y`
also vanishes. Thus this first correction supplies neither a first-order
change of the source Yukawa-shaped coefficient nor a first-order shift of
its six simple Hamiltonian eigenvalues.

## The explicit correction to the family coefficient

The same expansion determines the coefficient at order `h^2` without a
fit. Write `M(h)=M_0+h M_1+h^2 M_2+h^3 M_3+...` and let `W` denote the
marginal. Set `E_0=pi tensor I`, `R=(F-Pi) tensor I`. Then

```
A_1 = W M_1 E_0
E_1 = R M_1 E_0
A_2 = W (M_1 E_1+M_2 E_0)
E_2 = R (M_1 E_1+M_2 E_0-E_1 A_1)
A_3 = W (M_1 E_2+M_2 E_1+M_3 E_0)
G_3 = A_3-(A_1 A_2+A_2 A_1)/2+A_1^3/3.
```

The executable evaluates these identities independently on the full
432-dimensional channel. Its exact producer instead expands `G_3` into
64 ordered words `L_a L_b L_c` on the actual stationary support. For the
specified `V_a=X tensor P_a`, whose single and triple traces vanish,
the canonical Hamiltonian component of each word is

`-sym(V_a V_b V_c)`
`- [V_a Tr(V_b V_c)+V_b Tr(V_c V_a)+V_c Tr(V_a V_b)]/6`.

Here `sym(B)=(B+B^dagger)/2`. Substitution of `V_a=X tensor P_a` gives
`X tensor Y_3`, with a real symmetric algebraic matrix `Y_3`. The receipt
retains every exact matrix entry. Hence the canonical Hamiltonian of the
slow generator has expansion

`H_coh(h)=X tensor Y+h I tensor J+h^2 X tensor Y_3+O(h^3)`.

The canonical Hamiltonian component is fixed by traceless-jump convention:
for the superoperator `G`, form `B_(i,k)=sum_j G_(ij,kj)` and take
`i(B-B^dagger)/12`. Applying this projection to the independent full
invariant-graph calculation gives the exact `Y_3` to numerical precision.
The algebraic word expansion, rather than numerical precision, proves the
coefficient formula.
