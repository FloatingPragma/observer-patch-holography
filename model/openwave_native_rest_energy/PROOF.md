# A fixed native rest-phase and energy-attachment test

The candidate is the fair C/E native repair/hold process with uniform
independent seam selection. Its graph, weights, coin, memory rule and
source states are fixed before comparison. No particle mass sets an

## Source phase and Hamiltonian boundary

All eigenvalues of the unread one-load channel Phi are real and strictly
positive. The constant population mode has eigenvalue one; every other
mode decays on the connected finite graph. The argument of each eigenvalue
is therefore exactly zero, giving the outward phase-frequency interval
[0,0] per attempted event. This is an event-phase statement. A decaying
positive mode is not a stable nonzero rest pole, and [0,0] does not identify
the physical particle as massless.

The distinction can be quantified without a fitted Hamiltonian. Starting
from a pure occupied port i, Phi produces a diagonal density with largest
eigenvalue 1-d_i/(2M). For every unitary U,

\[
 \tfrac12\|\Phi(|i\rangle\langle i|)-U|i\rangle\langle i|U^*\|_1
 \ge d_i/(2M).
\]

Use the projector onto U|i> as a two-outcome trace-distance witness: its
probability in the mixed output is no greater than that output's largest
eigenvalue. This proves the lower bound. Also Phi-I is self-adjoint in
Hilbert-Schmidt operator space, while -i[H,.] is skew-adjoint. Equality on
the same algebra forces both to vanish, contradicting nonzero native
population transfer.

This does not prohibit another source reconstruction. The positive
operator -log(Phi), a coherent dilation or an explicitly supplied coherent
source-word service could participate in a distinct quantum attachment.
A physical time/algebra/state/readout transfer and its reconstruction
conditions would then need to be proved. No categorical conclusion about
all OPH architectures or all Euclidean transfer constructions follows.

## Native records do not observe an offdiagonal phase

Each native raw Kraus operator is a nonnegative scalar times a partial
injection of occupation basis states. A complete finite history is a
product of such maps, including any record-conditioned choice of seam,
law, hold, timeout or resource-stop identity. Its product is again a
partial injection. Hence K_h^* K_h is a scalar times a diagonal domain
projection and

\[
 \Pr(h)=\operatorname{Tr}(K_h^*K_h\rho)
\]

depends only on the source occupation diagonal. Every raw record history
has identical probability for any two source preparations with that same
diagonal. This holds with an idle reference, because the record probability
uses its partial trace; it does not equate all possible reference-sensitive
quantum output experiments.

The executable checks all 15,625 raw histories of three overlapping seam
events on the full three-port binary carrier and all 64 input operator
columns, including zero maps and the unused load sectors. It checks Kraus
completeness and equality of the full history law for two coherent phase
preparations. A supplied Hadamard analyzer distinguishes the preparations;
that positive control explicitly lies outside the repair/hold grammar.

## Retrospective common-scale check

Calling a relaxation rate a rest mass would require an additional
attachment. Even that counterfactual fails for the complete one-load
coherence band. Write lambda=1-x. Since

\[
 x\le-\log(1-x)\le x/(1-x),
\]

the ratio of any two such decay rates is at most
x_max/[x_min(1-x_max)]. On the three tested graphs its largest bound is
30/23. Assigning the slowest rate the inherited electron anchor therefore
puts every rate in this counterfactual band below 0.000667 GeV, far below
the inherited muon and tau coefficients. The complete population spectrum
and other possible readouts are not subject to this coherence-band ratio
bound.

The electron anchor and calibrated charged-lepton values are taken from
the existing common-source receipt, not re-estimated. These targets were
exposed, so the comparison is retrospective. No physical GeV rest-energy
interval is issued: the required energy/clock and stable-pole attachment
is absent for the tested repair-only member. The next prospective test
must freeze an explicit native coherent/readout transfer, a physical
normalization and an unused species before evaluation. Preparation and
record memory are supplied resources, with no asserted zero work or free
reset cost. No Nature or OpenWave criterion promotion follows.
