# Particle pairs and transverse radiation

The actual native charged current converts a prepared particle-hole pair
into two opposite-momentum transverse radiation quanta in a declared finite
quantum extension. An exact source coefficient gives a positive full-H
probability bound; complete longer-time records retain the vacuum background,
energy budget and photon-cutoff dependence. Physical annihilation rates and
the electromagnetic coupling require their own attachment.

Run `python3 -m model.openwave_radiative_pair --write` or `--check`.
The [proof](PROOF.md) states the finite source, resonance choice and controls.
