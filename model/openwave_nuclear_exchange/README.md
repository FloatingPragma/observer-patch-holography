# Nuclear binding and partner splitting

The calibrated native-current hadron action supports a bound six-quark
spin-triplet state. A positive edge weak-current term lifts the complete
spin-zero isotriplet partner above its two-baryon thresholds. All 27 levels
at spin projection +1, and all 27 levels in each fixed partner isospin
component, are retained with fragment thresholds and preparation failures.
The excluded deuteron binding comparison fails, and other nuclear channels
require their own tests.

See [the proof](PROOF.md) and [complete results](results.json).
Run `python -m model.openwave_nuclear_exchange --write` or `--check`.
