# Transport of physical composites

Three source defects can move together as a physical gauge singlet. On
the actual interacting eight-cell source, the first neighbor-to-neighbor
transport moment is exactly −3/4 at order three, for both signed odd
sector pairs with positive energy thresholds. A full-source error bound
proves a positive short-time transition probability. Coherent spatial
preparations have distinct energy distributions. Recovering an isolated
relativistic particle branch and its GeV rest mass requires additional
spectral control. See the [proof](PROOF.md) and [exact receipt](results.json).

Run `python -m model.particle_primary_composite_transport --check` and
`python -m pytest -q model/particle_primary_composite_transport`.
