The initial counting law is uniform on the sector. The sector, scheduling

Count every attempt, including zero current. This is the complete-attempt
clock, distinct from the incident-clock transfer `B(I-A)^(-1)` in
`observer_time_fields/core.py`. That incident transfer has a nontrivial
similarity metric and permits only field insertions commuting with `I-A`.
No such restriction is silently removed here.

Let C_xy count the sixty actual seam/coin branches taking configuration x to
y, and P=C/60. The certificate independently executes all 3960 branches.
The binary native rule gives P=(1/30) sum_s (I+S_s)/2, where S_s exchanges

so P_xx>=5/6. For every real vector v, symmetry and 2ab>=-a^2-b^2 give

    v^T P v >= sum_x (2 P_xx-1) v_x^2 >= (2/3)||v||^2.

The row-sum norm bound gives P<=I. Therefore 2I/3<=P<=I, and
H=-log(P) is a well-defined positive real symmetric operator. Connectedness
of the native token graph makes its eigenvalue one simple, with normalized

## The observable words generate the full real matrix algebra

Let N_a(x)=x_a be the twelve occupation fields. Their diagonal projectors
separate configurations: for each x,

    Q_x = product_(a:x_a=1) N_a product_(a:x_a=0) (I-N_a)

is the rank-one coordinate projector. The certificate checks all 66 products.
For a token-graph path x=x_0,...,x_m=y with P_(x_i,x_(i+1))>0,

    Q_x0 P Q_x1 P ... P Q_xm
      = (product_i P_(x_i,x_(i+1))) E_xy.

Every coefficient is positive. Connectedness therefore supplies every
matrix unit E_xy. All 4356 units are checked by independent sparse word
multiplication; the longest selected path has four steps. The real algebra
generated by the native transfer and occupation fields is exactly M_66(R).
Its canonical complexification is M_66(C). Choosing this complexification
as the physical scalar-field interpretation is a separate assertion; real
path data do not select an orientation of complex conjugation.

## Native count moments and their positive density representation

For an integer L>=1, native closed paths with uniform initial configuration
have complete return probability

    r_L = sum_x (P^L)_xx / 66 > 0.

Nonreturns remain in the complete law with probability 1-r_L. Let a selected
configuration path x_0,...,x_m have transfer coefficient c>0. The native event
of following those configurations, taking L further unrestricted attempts,
and returning to x_0 has probability

    p_(L,path) = c (P^L)_(x_m,x_0) / 66.

Define a reconstructed matrix-unit moment by the scaled count statistic

    ell_L(E_(x_0,x_m)) = p_(L,path) / (c r_L).

It is independent of the chosen nonzero path because the result is
(P^L)_(x_m,x_0)/sum_x(P^L)_xx. Extend ell_L real-linearly on the full
matrix-unit basis, then canonically complex-linearly. These definitions use
native probabilities and ordinary linear reconstruction. They do not call
the quantum runtime or assume a trace probability rule.

Finite index summation gives the representation

    ell_L(A) = Tr(rho_L A),
    rho_L = P^L / Tr(P^L) = exp(-L H)/Tr(exp(-L H)).

Since P is strictly positive definite, rho_L is positive definite and has
trace one. For every positive complex matrix A, writing A=B*B gives
ell_L(A)=Tr((B sqrt(rho_L))*(B sqrt(rho_L)))>=0. Consequently ell_L sends
effects to [0,1], sends I to one, and is additive on all coexistent matrix
effects. These are precisely the mathematical hypotheses of
`EventAlgebra.IsEffectValuation` in `FiniteBuschGleason.lean`. The Born form
is derived for this reconstructed functional; uniqueness also follows
directly because the 4356 matrix-unit moments fix every matrix coordinate.
This is an analytic source attachment of that theorem, with exact finite
count certificates; it is not an additional Lean formalization of the whole
native path reconstruction.

The inverse-temperature multiplier of this reconstructed Gibbs family is
the closed length L in attempt units. The finite Gibbs/KMS identities apply
to H and rho_L. The observed native working-state distribution after return
is only diag(rho_L); the dense rho_L is a state of the reconstructed transfer
algebra. The construction does not prepare that density on the native quantum
working carrier.

## Finite observer error through the reconstruction

The current-only adapter executes the same P; this packet compares every
transition entry to its independent endpoint enumeration. A successful first
census supplies the initial configuration, and the complete signed-current
word supplies every subsequent configuration. Closure and the word identify
the filtered path events used above. Census failures are retained.

For the displayed L in {1,2,4}, each matrix-unit event requires at most L+4<=8
body attempts. Use eight census seed scans, 64 retries, 1024 discarded
native warmup attempts and a preceding covering sweep for collision inputs.
The adapter's uniform complete-law bound is at most
delta=741/10^20=7.41e-18, with at most 11398 native attempts per experiment.
This bound includes the initial and terminal censuses and preparation;
the separate normalization experiment uses the same allowance.

Word coefficients satisfy c>=60^-4, and r_L>=1/66 because P has eigenvalue
one and positive remaining eigenvalues. If a and r are the ideal numerator
event and return probabilities and their finite-apparatus counterparts are
a_hat and r_hat, each differs by at most delta. Since
|ell_L(E_xy)|<=1 and r_hat>=1/66-delta>0,

    |a_hat/(c r_hat)-a/(c r)|
       <= delta(1/c+1)/(1/66-delta)
       <= 634/10^11.

For a complex matrix effect E, ||E||_F<=sqrt(66)<9. The entrywise bound gives
||rho_hat-rho_L||_F<=66*(634/10^11), so every reconstructed effect statistic
has error at most 594*(634/10^11)=3.76596e-6. The raw reconstructed matrix
is not asserted positive or exactly normalized at finite apparatus error.
These are systematic error bounds on population statistics. No finite-sample
estimation uncertainty or practical sample-cost claim is included. The exact
ideal functional remains the positive state proved above.

## Operational scope and the positive result

The full matrix algebra and its unique positive count functional are stronger
than a diagonal record-algebra representation. Non-diagonal real projections
have well-defined reconstructed weights that resolve the off-diagonal entries
of rho_L. Phase-conjugate projections have equal weights for this real state.

A matrix unit is obtained by rescaling a filtered path word, and an arbitrary
matrix effect can require signed or complex linear combinations of such
statistics and experiments at different lengths. Those combinations are not
asserted to be one native acceptance event or a physical measurement. Thus
the constructed `IsEffectValuation` is distinct from the native operational
quotient in `OperationalBornBridge.lean`. The latter additionally requires
actual effect implementations and implementation-independent event weights;
the diagonal native-grammar obstruction applies to that different obligation.

Full configuration access, preparation accuracy and the choice of attempt
clock retain their explicit implementation conditions. Universal native
complex measurements, source-selected complex phases, unitary real-time
apparatus, physical time and laboratory quantum predictions are not supplied
by the count-functional reconstruction. There is no conflict between this
positive reconstructed quantum description and the native-only measurement
reachability boundary.
