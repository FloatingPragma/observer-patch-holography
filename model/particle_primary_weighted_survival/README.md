# Energy resolution and observation strength

Smooth number-density observations of bounded total strength cannot excite
a fixed positive energy on the selected growing source. This statement
covers complete coherent histories, temporal filters and retained quantum
references. A quantitative strength bound identifies which observations
escape that obstruction. The original local phase probes have precisely
the larger strength outside its domain; their finite-energy survival needs
a selected-state spectral estimate. See the [proof](PROOF.md).

Run `python -m model.particle_primary_weighted_survival --write` and
`python -m pytest -W error model/particle_primary_weighted_survival`.
