# What the finite theorems contribute to the particle map

The [five theorem bridges](theorems.json) specify each input, exact output
and physical identification separately. They are a reading map of existing
results, without an additional source-to-particle theorem.

The rank-three slow band gives a carrier coordinate frame and exact
recorded displacements. A field excitation's position needs its own
detector. In the specified ordered-spin continuum, the actual field Gram
constructs a coordinate conjugate to that same representation's spatial
translation generator. Its uncertainty bound has explicit states and
operator domains. The carrier quotient and the field position theorem
therefore play complementary roles without being interchangeable.

Source event counts and observer modular parameters are related by a clock
dictionary. On the binary total-two source, complete attempts reconstruct
`H=-log P` and `rho_L=P^L/Tr(P^L)`. Their modular automorphism is
`rho_L^(is) A rho_L^(-is)=P^(iLs) A P^(-iLs)`. With the conventional
Heisenberg action `exp(i tau H) A exp(-i tau H)`, the same parameter is
`tau=-Ls`. The sign and factor are part of the declared convention.
The native stochastic transition `P` is distinct from the reconstructed
unitary analytic continuation. A tracial reference has trivial modular
flow. Physical elapsed duration, repair attempts, weak-pulse angle and host
execution time are not identified by adding two of these quantities.
The 66-state reconstruction and the ordered-spin field use different
declared sources. Their generator and state symbols are not identified by
placing the two theorem roles beside one particle illustration.

The finite Born theorem gives a unique state representation when weights
are nonnegative, normalized and additive on coexistent effects. Native
closed histories provide a positive reconstructed matrix functional; a
complete complex detector implementation has a separate operational
interface. Record consensus determines which result is shared. It supplies
neither the entire complex instrument algebra nor a choice of outcome.

The gauge theorem constrains a response algebra under injectivity,
commutator closure and internal rotation implementation. The matter image
and finite Gauss-invariant Higgs vertex then supply exact representation
and interaction statements. Their coefficients, physical vacuum, chiral
propagation and continuum interpretation have separate source conditions.
The algebraic result is stronger than a dimension count and weaker than a
prediction of every interaction strength and mass.
