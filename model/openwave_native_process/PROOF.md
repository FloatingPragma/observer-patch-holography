# Complete native events on a causal record order

## Declared source

The source is the unmodified `native_program` interpreter, with fair endpoint
coins, equal C/E law weights and repair probability one. The elementary graph
has twelve ports and thirty native seams. The retained `port_pair` federation
has 240 or 960 ports, with 630 or 2520 seams. Its binary fixed-number sector
is global; intercell repairs need not preserve a cell's particle count.
The H66 and H78 total-two carriers are separate local controls. Preparation,
seam selection, finite record storage and initialization are declared inputs.

The endpoint map sends `(n_a,n_b)` to the floor/ceiling balancing of their
sum, with a fair coin choosing the placement. It emits the signed current
`j=n_a-n'_a=n'_b-n_b`. C records this current and E records the initial
endpoint difference, from which the current is decoded together with the
coin. No field Hamiltonian is an interpreter instruction.

## Exact instrument and conservation

For each law, coin and raw register value, the implementation calls the
original endpoint rule and groups precisely the columns with that record.
Each resulting map is a partial injection. Its Kraus operator is the partial
permutation multiplied by the square root of its rational weight. In the
equal mixture this weight is 1/4. Every source column belongs to one record
for each law/coin pair. Thus the sum of all `K* K` is identity. Incoming
columns also sum to identity on the binary endpoint carrier. The four
endpoint states and all sixteen matrix units are checked exactly.

The complete alphabet has 25 slots: the zero-probability hold, eight C raw
law/coin slots and sixteen E slots. Padding, inadmissible differences and
zero law weights are retained. In a pure-law control the other law's slots
have weight zero. Tensor identity on all other occupations and an arbitrary
reference gives the complete instrument on every finite global sector.
Each Kraus map preserves total load and obeys the stated current identity.
These facts apply before any selected-outcome normalization.

## Partial order and memory

Each event has a distinct prepared record slot and a fixed seam. Events that
share an endpoint have the supplied causal order; disjoint events have no
ordering edge. Their Kraus operators act on disjoint source factors and
different record slots, so they commute outcome by outcome. This is the
actual factor and Kraus attachment required by the finite slot-locality
theorem. The executable checks all 256 four-port matrix units and all 625
pairs of raw slots. Adjacent exchanges of independent events connect all
linear extensions of a finite partial order. Their complete terminal
instruments are therefore identical after the explicit permutation of
event-addressed record coordinates, including every unused outcome.

Two independent local repairs followed by their joining seam provide a
three-event fork/join witness on both federations. The unchanged interpreter
executes both linear extensions, including all 15,625 raw histories for

and an explicit exact-zero complement indexed by the raw Cartesian product.
The two-point preparation is entangled with an untouched two-state reference.
The complete reference marginal and global particle number are checked.

This relabelling does not identify different terminal FIFO states. The

only the supplied repair probability to 1/2 gives probability 1/4, with a
positive hold branch and the same complete alphabet. Every H66/H78 source
column is independently reference-tagged in the local completeness control.
All initialization, native attempts, record writes and exports use the
interpreter's declared service counts; they are not an energy calibration.
