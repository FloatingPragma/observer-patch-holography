\paragraph{Compatible finite curvature.}
Placing scalar, vector and symmetric-tensor records at parities
$0$, $e_b$ and $e_a+e_b$ makes the same linearized Ricci polynomial compatible
with half-step differences. Its Bianchi identity and pure-gauge kernel are
exact, while its scalar Laplacian is the original nearest-neighbour operator.
The actual derivative graph connects scalar neighbours and removes the
collocated centered stencil's checkerboard ambiguity. Full boundary halos
remain present. A co-located null-tensor instrument and physical curvature
and stress identification are additional requirements.
