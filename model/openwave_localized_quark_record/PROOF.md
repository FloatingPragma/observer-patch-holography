## Why a one-point field is forbidden

Let rho be a physical density operator invariant under each local gauge
unitary. If a local operator A transforms as exp(i theta q)A with q nonzero,
cyclicity gives Tr(rho A)=exp(i theta q)Tr(rho A) for every theta. Its mean
is therefore zero. The native charged hole and an undressed single open link
both satisfy this obstruction. A nonzero phase assigned to either by itself
would be an inadmissible order parameter on this carrier.

Let B be the path-dressed native transfer

    B = sum_ij r_i* (U_path)_ij c_xj P_refvac /sqrt(3).

The reference-vacuum projector tests the full reference, including spectators.
Pointwise unitarity of U_path and the CAR give
B*B=N_family P_refvac/3 <= P_refvac. Its range has one reference excitation,
orthogonal to its domain, so B squared is zero. Thus X=B+B* and
Y=i(B-B*) are self-adjoint contractions on the full carrier. The four effects
(I+X)/4, (I-X)/4, (I+Y)/4, (I-Y)/4 sum to identity. Their positive square roots
give a complete gauge-invariant instrument. To retain constraint failures,
first test the full Gauss projector G. The combined pointer effects are
G(I+X)G/4, G(I-X)G/4, G(I+Y)G/4, G(I-Y)G/4 and I-G. Since X and Y commute
with G, these five effects sum exactly to identity. The seed test on success
uses P_seed and G-P_seed; its failed preparation is an absorbing record.
It does not acquire a second copy of the constraint-failure effect.
This proof retains every
fermion occupation, every link representation and an arbitrary idle quantum
