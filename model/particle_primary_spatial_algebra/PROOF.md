# Spatial translations on a regular physical observable algebra

## Domain and reuse

The finite inputs are exactly
[`particle_common_contract.member(n)`](../particle_common_contract/PROOF.md):
spacing `a_n=2^-n`, side `L_n=2^(2n+1)`, per-link degree `ell_n=n+1`,

The [common filtered algebra](../particle_primary_field_algebra/PROOF.md)
provides a simultaneous bounded-probe moment construction and positive
canonical time energy. Its point probes alone have no established
continuous spatial action. Spatial sums use an n-dependent number of
point probes and are not automatically in the norm closure of that menu.
This construction explicitly adds a countable menu of spatially smeared
physical probes, with the same source, preparation and clock. A single
diagonal subsequence can preserve the original menu and this extension.

The [finite Gauss proof](../particle_gauss_ground_state/PROOF.md#6-a-constrained-spatial-ground-subsequence)
supplies the invariant exterior-blank state extension. The
[field-locality proof](../particle_field_causality/PROOF.md) supplies
finite/infinite dynamics comparison by a boundary Duhamel estimate and
fixed-regulator local dynamics. The actual primary coefficients are
those in [primary locality](../particle_primary_locality/PROOF.md).
The cell-averaging and spatial-translation method in
[`continuum_field_net`](../continuum_field_net/README.md#continuous-spatial-translations-and-positive-energy)
is reused for bounded gauge-invariant observables. Its separate CAR
source, chosen translation-invariant ground and noncollapse are not
transferred. `Lean/Variational/TranslationInvariantComposedInstance.lean`

## A physical infinite-lattice comparison algebra at each regulator

Fix n. On the infinite cubic lattice use the same finite CAR and link-word
factors as the finite member, with its even quasi-local algebra. Take the
subalgebra G invariant under every local gauge action. For each vertex x,
the Haar projector P_x onto its local gauge singlets is a finite-support
operator. Different vertices' gauge actions commute, so P_x is invariant
under all of them. Every A in G commutes with P_x. Thus P_x is central
in G, even though the individual nonabelian generators at one vertex do
not commute.

Let J be the closed two-sided ideal of G generated by `I-P_x`, for all x,
and let `G_phys=G/J`. The infinite empty-Fock/constant-link product state
satisfies every P_x and annihilates J. It has unit expectation one, so
J is proper. The quotient is a nonzero physical observable C* algebra.
This is a global constraint quotient; it does not project a subregion's
boundary-charge sectors to a separate singlet.

Integer lattice shifts beta_m preserve G and carry P_x to P_(x+m).
They therefore act by automorphisms on G_phys. The homogeneous infinite
Wilson interaction has exactly the selected local terms on every site,
bond and plaquette. Each complete term is even and gauge invariant.
The fixed finite cutoff gives bounded local factors and interactions;
the reused locality construction yields its point-norm continuous
quasi-local time group. It fixes every P_x and preserves J. Homogeneity
gives `alpha_t beta_m=beta_m alpha_t` on G_phys.

This comparison algebra is not the finite open box with periodic links
added. In particular, an integer shift is not asserted to preserve the
finite box's Gauss constraints or Hamiltonian. No isometric embedding of
its restricted physical algebra into G_phys is assumed: infinite states
can have boundary sectors absent from a finite zero-external-charge box.

## The same finite state and a controlled bulk dynamics comparison

Embed the selected finite density and its reference into the infinite
ambient system by tensoring invariant blanks onto every exterior matter
and link factor. The finite source includes its outgoing dangling links
and imposes zero charge at every ghost endpoint. Consequently the
extension satisfies all infinite P_x: interior constraints are the
selected ones, old ghost constraints see only invariant added factors,
and all other constraints act on invariant blanks. The resulting state
on G_phys reproduces every original finite-source observable and
reference marginal. It need not be stationary under the infinite
Hamiltonian before taking a limit.

Decouple the finite box by deleting only hopping and plaquette terms
whose owner supports cross its boundary. All electric and onsite terms,
including the owned dangling electric links, stay unchanged. The dynamics
of an observable on the box factors is then exactly its original finite
Hamiltonian evolution, independent of the chosen exterior evolution.
Every retained or deleted whole interaction is gauge invariant.

For a side-L cube there are exactly `6L^2` crossing bonds and
`12L^2-3L` crossing plaquettes. The latter follows by counting, for each
coordinate plane, `2L^2-L` inside anchors with an outside forward owner,
and `2L^2` outside anchors with one inside forward owner. The norms
`30/a` per full-Fock bond and `1/(7a)` per plaquette give

```
C_boundary <= (180+12/7)L^2/a = (1272/7)L^2/a.         (1)
```

Let O_x be one of the physical contractions from the concrete charge or
plaquette menu, with at most s=1 or3 owner cells. Suppose all these cells
are at graph distance at least r from any crossing support. The primary
cone, with `W=5232/7`, and Duhamel's identity give

```
||alpha_t^infinite(O_x)-alpha_t^box(O_x)||
 <= min(2, 2s |t| C_boundary exp[-r log2+2W|t|/a]).    (2)
```

The electric terms are removed by the onsite interaction picture, so
their cutoff-dependent norms do not enter W. Equation(2) is first an
ambient all-state bound. It applies to the physical state extension and
arbitrary untouched references by restriction, without a factorization
of the physical Hilbert space.

For weights w_x of total absolute weight at most C and a common collar,
linearity gives C times(2). There is no factor equal to the number of
smearing cells: apply the bound to each local seed before summing weights.
For fixed physical support in `[-R,R]^3`, centered coordinates
`a(c-L/2)` and one forward plaquette step give the safe collar

```
r_n=L_n/2-ceil(R/a_n)-2.
```

For any fixed source-time extent T the exponential in(2) tends to zero
faster than its polynomial prefactor: `r_n` grows as `4^n`, whereas
`T/a_n` grows as `2^n` and `C_boundary` as `2^(5n)`. The executable
uses `log2>=1/2`, exact rational logarithm ceilings and factored dyadic
errors. It does not allocate enormous powers or spatial matrices.
Small levels that do not contain the probe support receive only the
trivial cap2, rather than a false bulk estimate.

Fixed temporal filters, finite words and fixed finite source detector
histories inherit this comparison by integration, product telescoping
and continuous functional calculus. In particular, for effect difference
delta the two-outcome square-root instrument has all-reference error at
most `sqrt(2delta)`. These finite comparisons keep the actual source
preparation and reference. Their pointer marginals share the common
limiting moments; no growing-reference limit or efficient implementation
is inferred.

## Spatially regular smears and an exact continuous group

At regulator n identify the infinite sites with `a_n Z^3`, centered on
the original box. For compactly supported integrable f define

```
w_n,z(f)=integral_(a_n(z+[0,1)^3)) f(x) dx,
A_n(f)=sum_z w_n,z(f) O_n,z.
```

The seed is any fixed charge label/axis or normalized plaquette plane.
It is zero when its charge is absent at that regulator. The sum is
finite for compact support and converges in norm for every f in L1.
The same local observable definition is used on all sites. It follows
directly that

```
||A_n(f)-A_n(g)|| <= ||f-g||_1,
beta_m A_n(f)=A_n(f_(a_n m)),    f_y(x)=f(x-y).         (3)
```

No field normalization diverging with n is inserted. The finite source
version retains the cells actually inside its box; for each fixed compact
support it agrees with the local algebraic expression for all sufficiently
large n. Equation(2) compares its clock with the infinite clock. Both
versions are then smeared by a fixed triangular time filter as in the
common algebra proof. Integer shifts commute with that filtering.
These bounded averages can have only scalar, classical or zero connected
responses in a limit. Their construction supplies no noncollapse witness.

Take the bounded product of the infinite physical algebras and quotient
by norm-null sequences. Inside it let A_reg be the unital C* algebra
generated by the classes of filtered A_n(f), for a countable dense menu
of compact rational piecewise-linear spatial test functions, rational
time translates and positive rational filter widths. Include adjoints.
The L1 bound extends this menu to every spatial L1 function by completion.

For y in R3 set `m_n(y)=floor(y/a_n)` componentwise. At each n its
lattice shift is an isometric automorphism. It induces an automorphism
of the full product quotient. Its restriction to A_reg sends the class
of A_n(f) to the class of A_n(f_y), because

```
||f_(a_n m_n(y))-f_y||_1 ->0.                          (4)
```

This restriction preserves the regular algebra. The rounded maps do not
form an exact R3 group on the full product quotient: their lattice
composition can differ by a unit step. On A_reg, however, that difference
has norm tending to zero by(3). Thus their restrictions form an exact
group beta_y with inverse beta_(-y). L1 translation continuity makes it
point-norm continuous. For a test function with weak L1 derivatives,

```
||beta_y A(f)-beta_z A(f)||
 <= sum_j |y_j-z_j| ||partial_j f||_1.                (5)
```

The same bound holds after time filtering. The time group and spatial
group commute on every generator, hence on A_reg. This is a continuous
space and time action on one regular physical observable algebra, using
the original source time. It does not construct Lorentz boosts.

## The selected limiting state and its precise comparison map

Use the actual finite states and their invariant exterior extensions,
without spatial averaging. On a countable dense polynomial menu extract
one subsequence of all their expectations. Positivity and norm bounds
give a state on A_reg, as in the common filtered-algebra proof.
For every fixed finite word, (2) makes its infinite-system expectation
and its finite-source expectation differ by a quantity tending to zero.
The finite source is stationary. Its physical mean ground excess is
`epsilon_n<=2^(-n-4)`. The shared word limits therefore give a stationary
state and a canonical GNS time generator L>=0 by exactly the existing
finite downward-weight argument. One does not assign a positive stationary
spectral measure to the nonstationary infinite extension at finite n.

The comparison is equality of all limiting word moments on the shared
regular probe menu. Its GNS identification maps each polynomial cyclic
vector to the same named polynomial vector; equality of all Gram
matrices makes this map unitary on the completed cyclic spaces. It
intertwines those observable representations and time evolution. It
does not assert equality of finite physical operator norms, a norm
isomorphism with the frozen unsmeared point algebra, or a spatial action
on every original point probe. Including both menus in one diagonal
extraction preserves their compatible state restriction.

Nothing in this argument proves `omega beta_y=omega`. The open-box
preparation can be influenced by boundaries or select a symmetry-broken
ground. Replacing it by a homogeneous average changes the experiment and
would require an additional preparation/coin specification. No such
replacement is made.

If spatial invariance of this same state is established, then the
canonical GNS maps `[b]->[beta_y b]` are strongly continuous unitaries
fixing Omega. They commute with the canonical source-time unitaries,
and Stone's joint spectral theorem supplies commuting spatial momentum
generators. This last implication is conditional. Without invariance,
beta_y need not preserve the GNS null space, so the canonical spatial
unitaries and vacuum momentum cannot be inferred. Existence of a spatial
action on an abstract C* algebra alone is insufficient.

For mass readout, Fourier test-function labels are therefore not declared
measured momentum. A zero transfer label is also not an individual
excitation's zero vacuum-relative momentum. The actual spatial generator,
same-state invariance, initial/vacuum momentum control and a surviving
spectral branch must be supplied to the energy-to-mass readout. No GeV
scale or particle mass is calculated by this construction.

## Exact controls and failure cases

The executable integrates normalized spatial hat functions exactly on
finite cells and verifies total weight one, translation intertwining and
the derivative bounds. Crossing interaction counts are enumerated on
small complete cubes, independently of the analytic bounds. Source
members2,4,6 give the actual same-clock bulk transfer plans.

On C(S1), time identity and spatial rotation are commuting continuous
automorphisms. The evaluation state `omega(f)=f(0)` has positive time
energy zero but is not rotation invariant. For `f(theta)=cos(theta)`,
`f-1` is GNS null, while its rotation through pi/2 has squared GNS norm1.
Thus spatial rotation has no canonical unitary on that one-dimensional
GNS representation. Four exact matrix samples check these identities.
This is a logical countercontrol, not the primary preparation.

A separate three-site open hopping matrix is changed by its cyclic site
permutation. Its squared Hilbert-Schmidt defect is4. This checks the
boundary error in treating a finite cyclic coordinate label as a
conserved momentum. No periodic replacement of the primary box is made.
Neither control determines whether the actual primary limiting state
is translation invariant or has a nontrivial particle response.
