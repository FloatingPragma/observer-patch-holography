The native two-load source gives an exact small bridge to the finite
quotient theorem. Its 3,960 seam/coin branches on 66 states descend to three
pair-distance outcomes, with stationary probabilities `5/11`, `5/11` and
`1/11`. Distance is a declared diagnostic of the full classical source
state. Implementing that readout from the observer's native current-only
records requires a separate instrument; identifying gauge sectors requires
an operator map.

The same checker distinguishes a one-sided sector sampler from a two-sided
link sampler and a sector-preserving repair. It tests the input measure,
rejection records and ergodicity before applying a stationary-law theorem.
It supplies no physical temperature or gauge coupling.

With a declared known preparation and addressed records, the
[bounded current-record observer](RECORD_OBSERVER.md) reconstructs pair
distance without reading working source loads. Its complete distance law
is within one percent of equilibrium after 39 native attempts, with a
298-bit retained logical-register budget.
