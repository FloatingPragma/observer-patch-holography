# Frequency search in the free, retained-coin density response

Use the sharp source at port zero on the 20-cell sphere in both mobile preparations of the [retained-coin experiment](PROTOCOL.md). Keep the uniform even written-sign sector and all 630 seam terms active. Keep every state in the 32- and 2,730-state connected components. No temporal or spatial frequency selects either component.

Each port patch performs terminal sharp reads of the coins on its five or six incident seams. At a hop the occupied port loses one incident arrowhead and the new port gains one. Thus initial occupation plus the change of local head count is the terminal occupation. Coin readings for neighbouring patches commute. The patches can pool these locally derived counts over fresh preparations. Exactly one terminal address is occupied on each trial; store its complete histogram on the geometrically reachable addresses, counting all fixed zeros elsewhere. This detector observes density. A spatial phase of the untraced joint load–coin wave is a different observable.

Choose full-screen, unweighted port-Laplacian eigenspaces nearest to λ = 0.12, 0.32, 2.9, 5 and 7, as in the blank double experiment. Project the fixed launch address into each eigenspace and normalize. A vanishing projection is a blind spatial mode. Compute the spatial density contrast by weighting patch outcomes by these real profiles. The full-screen geometry determines the weights and the squared graph wave numbers independently of their temporal evolution.

From exposure 0 through 32 at increments of 1/24, generate exact occupation probabilities and independently draw 65,536 complete terminal records per setting. The first half including exposure 16 fits a constant plus one sine/cosine pair. Search angular frequency from π/4 through 56 in steps no greater than 0.03, then refine the best training peak. The lower cutoff permits two training cycles; the upper cutoff bounds the native energy differences by the row sums. Validate that same fitted curve on all later exposures. Require positive explained variance of at least 90% on both halves relative to a constant, and a fitted amplitude exceeding five times the largest single-exposure sampling error. Expect agreement between the exact and count-based verdicts. Retain every unresolved mode.

A detector positive control uses the same sharp launch and law with a prepared order in which port zero and its lowest equal-degree neighbour come last, in that order. The single eligible link makes an exact two-state component. Its launch-port occupancy supplies a native single-frequency response. Require the search to recover its frequency within 1% on held data. This is a different preparation of the same free operator.

Only if at least three spatial modes in one mobile preparation pass, use the two smallest passing λ values to calibrate the density-line hypothesis ω² = m² + c²λ and predict the other passing modes without further fitting. Require m² and c² positive, distinct frequencies resolved to at least one training-window Fourier interval, and at most 5% relative frequency error at every held λ. A pass would concern density oscillations in native exposure. An observer-clock dispersion of a matter amplitude needs a physical joint phase reader and a separate clock calibration. A broad response or inaccessible line leaves that question open.
