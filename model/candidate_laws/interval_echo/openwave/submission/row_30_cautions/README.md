## The ring source

The periodic wall admits a uniform mode in the sector with dimer-flip product $F=f$, where $F=\prod_i X_i$ and $f=-(-1)^n$. The ring simulator preserves this sector when applying the source Pauli product $O$. The written protocol measures the individual local factors of $O$. Two endpoint factors anticommute with $F$. Reading either endpoint and forgetting its individual result erases flip parity even when the observer later multiplies all the signs. Terminal factor reads remain valid because there is no subsequent flight. The open-strand source starts maximally mixed and does not require the ring's initial flip parity. [Original local protocol](../../row_30_collective/PROTOCOL.md) · [Original sector generator](../../row_30_collective/rest/experiment.py)

On five dimers at exposure 2.15 the sector-preserving and local-factor procedures give correlations 0.126453 and 0.163145 for the same pair of physical strings. Reanalysis of both exposed rings, including the half-contrast controls, finds RMS residuals of 0.989–0.998 binomial standard deviations against the instrument actually simulated, and 4.730–6.335 against the originally described local source. Each comparison uses all 20,480 temporal pair bins per ring and coupling. The earlier values 15.000003 and 14.999997 belong to the former instrument. [Direct projector test and archived count comparison](detector.json)

The replacement starts from a maximally mixed set of dimers. In one ensemble each site measures its factor of $O$ before flight. In the other it measures its factor of $fOF$. Both are Hermitian Pauli strings since $O$ and $F$ commute. For terminal string $B$, let $C(A,B)=\mathrm{Tr}[B(t)A]/D$ for a $D$-dimensional orientation space. All local signs contribute. The observer can reconstruct the desired sector correlation by addition,
$$
 C(O,B)+C(fOF,B)=\mathrm{Tr}\!\left[B(t)O(I+fF)/D\right].
$$
This recovers the sector correlation across ensembles. No individual trial has to retain the chosen sector. Equal pooling halves the signal. Doubling the pooled mean restores the normalization. The frequency and spatial-direction estimators tolerate that common amplitude factor. The separate counters retain their own shot noise. Direct projector checks on five and six physical dimers reproduce the two arm means and their sum to within $5.5\times10^{-14}$. [Local-source implementation](ring_repair.py)

## What the written connector does

The coherent sign-even, coin-even magnitude-two write yields link energies $(32,32,32,0)$ for the endpoint loads (00,01,10,11). The sign-odd, coin-even sector yields $(33,33,33,0)$. In the two coin-odd sectors all four loads have the same energy, 32 or 33. Those sectors cut the orientation coupling. The full 99-state seam check tests all sixteen columns, all eighteen sharp zero-write spectator columns, and a local dimer interference measurement. At addressed exposure $\pi/32$, the probability of the positive dimer readout is 0 with the connector unread, 0.001204 with its sign recorded, 0.5 with its coin recorded and 0.500602 with both recorded. The recording operation dephases the corresponding pointer at commissioning and averages all outcomes. [Seam calculation](memory.py) · [Parity table and detector probabilities](memory.json)

On the isolated open strand, independently recording each written connector coin gives equal weight to the two coin parities. With no further collar operations these are static sectors for load-only observations. A scalar link term separates the two sides. Given the same initial remote marginal in each sector, a source intervention on one side can change a terminal click probability on the other by at most $2^{-d}$ across $d$ such links. Only sectors with no cut on the unique path can transmit the change. This bound concerns the difference induced by that intervention. Conditioning on individual records, other paths, inverse writes or further repair events define different experiments. There is no general exclusion of matter waves in the committed family. ## Conditions set by the audit

V3 binds the nominated state and detector to public acceptance and separates missing records from rejected measurements. Its even-hold waves passed the odd-hold spatial test on all three freshly reserved boards. The source-label substitution failed on each. The v2 rejection and the new acquisition are separate records. [Successor, data and public status](../row_30_confirmation/README.md)

1. Declare the prepared material, unread memories, addressed schedule, detector and inference range as one candidate. Have the accepted candidate revision identified in both the protocol and its guard. Demonstrating phase transport under committed repairs would be a separate way to broaden the claim.
2. Describe both ring instruments and their exposure history. For a claim of independent spatial identification, predeclare a count-based profile check with a control that can actually separate the measured profile from the launch label. For a claim that low-wave-number counts select one continuum law, use an experiment with sufficient resolving power.
3. Distinguish a completed rejection from missing or invalid records in the executable. Preserve both outcomes in the receipts and test them before freezing a successor. Bind the adjudicated source and its intervening history, then acquire fresh inputs only under public terms. This audit withdraws the release recommendation for the earlier bytes.
