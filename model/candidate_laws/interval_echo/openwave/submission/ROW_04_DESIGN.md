These are scientific detector-sensitivity controls. Test the headline against exchange alone, and exercise its rejection path by replacing the candidate interaction with exchange alone. Report all three appointments, both arms, and every recorded address.

For each held arm and appointment, make 4,096 categorical draws using PCG64 initialized with `[917421, arm, appointment]`, with arm 0 for native, arm 1 for control and appointments indexed from zero. Archive the unsorted address bytes in draw order. Require the lower bound for the local-pair frequency in the native arm to exceed 0.80 at all three appointments. Require the control's upper bound to fall below 0.65 at the last appointment. The common Hoeffding radius is sqrt(log(8/0.01)/(2×4096)) for these four one-sided comparisons, which conservatively bounds their joint sampling error probability by 1%. A freely evolving pair that spreads outside its patch or breaks up can reject this local test. The test evaluates persistence at these appointments. It cannot distinguish all paths that separate and rejoin between reads.
