The ceilings were 554.07, 1137.74 and 963.37. Replacing the measured wavelengths with the launch labels on those same counts gave 2,786,515, 792,450 and 1,794,431. The lattice forecast from two disjoint coupling measurements agreed with 22 frequencies within 0.000575 in dimer-clock units. Fourteen low-wave-number lines agreed with the continuum approximation within 0.003165. On the original long-wavelength pilot a matched parabola also passed the continuum tolerance. Sensitivity to the two shapes comes from the wider lattice band. Each of the three v3 cohorts replayed from its saved binding and selectors.
