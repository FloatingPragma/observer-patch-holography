# Detecting an excess and a vacancy on the same material

Two observers can jointly detect opposite material units after any undisturbed free wait on one 20-cell spectroscopy board. One observer reads an excess at 9 or 11, the other a vacancy at 228 or 230. Each of the four address pairs has probability one quarter given successful preparation. This is an exact finite result for a supplied pair. The energy comparison also gives a counterexample to inferring equal constituent energies from a pair total. [Native pointer and flight calculation](verify.py)

## Source and detectors

Fill all 240 ports with one unit, install the earlier-port arrow on every physical seam and set each private degree reference to one. Supply an excess at 5 with the head changes along either of the donor paths to 5, and a vacancy at 239. The two starting defects compensate in the total. The excess uses the two historical tip pairs from the spectroscopy criterion. For the vacancy use the largest address and its first two adjacent degree-five minima. Both charge signs see the same fixed heads, stock and references. The original row 4 release used empty stock and zero references. [Donor source](../DONOR_RELEASE.md) · [What the two donor columns distinguish](../prefix_location/README.md)

Isolate each pair of parent-to-tip contacts and its private reference. The two seam queries and the central contrast query give the same five-answer signed preparation for each sign. The accepted amplitude is proportional to the difference of the two tip words. Each patch accepts with probability 1/8. The two patches act on disjoint registers, so the probability of both acceptances is **1/64 of the supplied sharp-parent starts**. The complementary probability is 63/64. The parent loads, prefix and arrow preparation are inputs. This probability measures the filters’ yield from those inputs.

At readout a blank probe carrying one unit compares its load with the material port. The native echo writes their signed difference to the probe. Reading that difference and undoing the echo gives the sharp outcomes +1 at an excess, −1 at a vacancy and 0 at neutral filling. The disjoint probes can read the two signs in the same run. On the accepted preparations these readings are independent of the free wait. Changing the relative tip sign for the excess opens a return amplitude to its parent. At port 0 a sharp excess can move through five contacts and a sharp vacancy has zero outgoing moves on the same chart.

## What the two energy comparisons distinguish

The free exchange generator vanishes on the sign-even arrow words used here. Let $\lambda$ be the eigenvalue of twice the agreement generator relative to the all-one material with its specified references. The physical generator difference on these words is $\kappa\lambda/2$ in native units. The verifier applies the complete two-defect generator to each paired preparation, including the written heads. ## Preparing or erasing a pair

Let $N_0$ and $N_2$ count ports that read zero and two. On **all 99 native local inputs**, both the free exchange and agreement matrices commute with both counts on their two endpoints. Summing contacts conserves the global counts separately. The diagonal degree corrections preserve them too. On this free law a vacancy and excess cannot form from all-one material or erase into it, even if they meet. This conclusion applies to every seam-memory input and every free agreement strength.

The addressed echo distinguishes a specific preparation from autonomous production. With both ports at one, a supplied equal superposition of the two coin words at signed saved contrast +2 is taken by one echo to a blank seam with loads (2,0). The −2 preparation gives (0,2). An actual pair-production or annihilation experiment must account for the source, its work and the outgoing records under its chosen interactions. The calculation here covers the local resource conversion and the separate, supplied two-parent flight. A distinct experiment with an added contact measures outgoing energy in two material wings.
