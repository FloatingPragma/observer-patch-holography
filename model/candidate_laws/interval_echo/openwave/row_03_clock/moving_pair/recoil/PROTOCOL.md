Use the same amount-one and amount-two source, the same local order preparations and the same three owner boundaries as the [moving-pair experiment](../PROTOCOL.md). Its empty-host formation energy and the sign of the attraction are known when designing this test. Expose the whole 20-cell screen for zero, one or four exchange-clock units. Keep every contact during free flight. Read an undisturbed dimer at one eighth of each interval on independent copies.

Its gradient is concentrated on the three outgoing bridges. It supplies no linear ruler across the board. Confirm the two phase settings on a native split-and-recombine occupied dimer of each amount, and include a sham. Boundary location and launch timing are supplied. The alternate flight uses exchange only, with the same entrance, pulse services and detectors.

Directly read the number of amounts outside the emitter, distinguishing both amounts arriving adjacently in the original next owner. Do not select a spatial or energy band.

Report the measured sign whenever different, and retain the zero-age control, the changed interaction and all three routes.
