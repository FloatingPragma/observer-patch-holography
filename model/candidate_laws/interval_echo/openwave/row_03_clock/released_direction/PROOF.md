## Native source and signed record

For native coupling the unit-direction blocks have one state below the common uniform-load threshold per parity. Compressing to χ-perpendicular gives I₂ ⊗ A plus κ or κ+1, which is positive there. The Rayleigh quotient on χ is below −5.5 relative to the threshold on both enrolled boards. The negative ground state is therefore simple and at least 5.5 units from the other levels. Applying the sparse matrix from the native write to each computed ground vector gives residual below 5×10⁻¹⁵. This isolation makes each target ground energy unambiguous at the precision reported here.

Let w_e and w_o be the source probabilities on the two normalized ground vectors g_e and g_o.
