# OW-03 · A clock inside a localized vacancy

The [upstream criterion](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/d4fc3e4046c472f4ad38468df8dddfa1f97bac97/MODELS.md#L103) asks whether the particle starts an internal oscillation at its rest energy divided by the reduced Planck constant. The M10 cell is untested. Its earlier collision-channel example supplies a phase resource. The registered experiment below uses sharp load inputs, a prepared filled medium and gateway monitoring. The previously reported coherent compact-packet beat and the 35-unit vacancy transfer are known in advance. Inspection of the local eigenprojectors motivated the opposite-port receiver and its positive prediction. This is a prospective count check of a known spectral prediction. 1. Use the first cell with two antipodal gateways on each spherical board. When there is none use cell zero. Select the first untouched antipodal axis whose two ports are both outside the gateway axes. Start a vacancy on the lower-address end with no phase pulse. Run the 1,024-round no-gateway-click source on an otherwise isolated unit-filled cell. Use one attempt per enrollment. Retain failures; release successful copies with every sample contact active. On 320 cells repeat at half the preparation pulse duration. On the 80-cell board use a second antipodal launch axis and halve the sample contrast in a separate control. The detector and physical time unit stay fixed. Test a changed free law that removes sample exchange while keeping all diagonal contrast terms and the released input.
2. Compare the internal records at launch and at its opposite port. The sum is an even spatial observable. A single cosine plus a constant predicts every subsequent independent read on an antipodal-gateway cell. The frequency is predicted from the local exchange graph before generating counts. A missing or shifted line, or a failure of that line to predict held observations, would refute the readout. On the no-antipodal-gateway board the monitored source is predicted to be stationary. Observing a spatial oscillation there would refute that comparison.
3. At 129 increasing appointments run a separate exchange dimer for one of eight equal subintervals. Estimate the total exposure from its swap fraction, on the dimer's first increasing half-cycle. The experiment supplies equal subintervals, addressing, a reference timer, source monitoring and terminal detector settings. Fresh realizations make the individual reads. Fit a single sinusoid of unknown frequency, phase and offset to the first 65 appointments without using the scheduled exposures, generator eigenvalues or held records. Predict the remaining 64. Compare with the constant forecast using the same records. Require a detected oscillation to agree with the predicted frequency to 1%, peak-to-trough variation above 0.03 of all enrollments, and a held probability RMS error below 0.006. Frequency is unidentified for constant counts.
4. On fresh launches estimate kinetic energy of the same state by a uniformly sampled native contact parity measurement. Count presence, symmetric and antisymmetric outcomes and every source failure. On blank-collar vacancy states the static part is 32 in the interior at registered contrast, relative to the uniform host, and 16 at half contrast. Reuse separately deposited six-seam transfer readings for the stationary state of kinetic energy three. Energy differences to that material reference are finite-host observables. Read them independently of the time trace, and compare achievable internal frequencies with the reference.
5. Bound the entire one-vacancy sector on each board before choosing any spatial receiver. A positive rest-clock requires an internally observable line at the independent energy, a source of rest-energy calibration and a free sharp preparation. A bounded spectral exclusion applies to all fixed final effects in the specified sector; a nonlinear multiplication of readings or a driven external oscillator changes the test.

Store all trial counts at every appointment. The seed is 178137 and each instrument uses 262,144 enrollments per setting. The protocol uses native exchange and energy units with ℏ=1.

### Unmonitored launch

Read the vacancy's return to the original port at 65 appointments, without initial filtering, shaping or phase drive. Check two integer patterns against the actual full one-vacancy matrix. Their overlap with the sharp launch bounds a persistent frequency component even if the returning wave contains many other lines. The sampled time series by itself only covers the observed window.
