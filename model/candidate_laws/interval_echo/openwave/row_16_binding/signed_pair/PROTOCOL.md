# Joining the charged and neutral material standards

Use the positive (two excesses and one vacancy) and neutral (two excesses and two vacancies) stocks previously calibrated in OW-14, on two independently shuttered twelve-port cells with the same gateway mask. Compare the same combined inventory of four excesses and three vacancies in the first cell with one undisturbed unit-filled reference cell. All thirty internal blank contacts and all native private references act continuously during free evolution. Report the charge of each separated source, its energy, and its charge response on the same material convention. The signed two-material threshold and the joined charge quadrupole have not been computed for this design.

For each inventory choose the sharp word with minimum diagonal ordinary energy, breaking ties by the first OW-14 enumeration index. Include the empty host subtraction. Establish a rigorous lower threshold for the sum of the two independent sector grounds by minimizing the sharp contrast plus private potential; establish an upper on each by its sharp diagonal. Test the joined source against this charged-plus-neutral threshold and against all other two-piece channels conserving both occupancies. Record connectedness, adjacency of like and opposite signs, and whether the seven defects still partition into disjoint connected pieces with the original three- and four-defect signed inventories. Multiple possible decompositions do not assign a constituent identity.

Include the unprojected sharp source and contrast-free law at the same nominated word. The cyclic residue is a spatial polarization mod three. Measure the mean formation energy by sampling one native contact or reference at a time; bound energy weight at or above the paired threshold from the variance of the exact generator. Read free-flight words and a balanced opposite-charge probe at times zero, one quarter and one native unit. The signed phase of the probe is calibrated with individual 0, 1 and 2 loads. Read the signed quadrupole along the specified gateway axis and along a transverse orthonormal pair by weighted address sampling. Report both the cell origin and the centroid of the seven defects with unit mass weights. The centroid is a supplied mass convention. The probe, units and selected orientation are fixed before acquisition. Positive and negative shape calibrations, absence of source, phase-inverted probe and removal of contrast are controls.
