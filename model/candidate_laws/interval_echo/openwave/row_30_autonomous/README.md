## Independent measurements of the velocity coefficient

The lower band’s zero-momentum frequency magnitude against the trimer was 1.65732. Separately, opposite single-band packet launches near |q| = 0.307682 gave measured centroid velocities −0.49519 and +0.49365 channel cells per local clock unit. For this original calibration, the group-velocity identity for the trial Klein–Gordon curve gives c² = |v|ω/q and c = 1.67033. That identity spends one speed parameter on the calibration line. A second speed estimate uses two separately measured local couplings. A one-unit occupation oscillation on a prepared physical collar gives a = 4.00051. A degree-reference Ramsey measurement gives Δ = 7.99993. Neither calibration uses the wave frequencies, group velocity or Klein–Gordon relation. To see what sets the coefficient, gauge the alternating site signs so that the four-site matrix has hopping +a. Subtract the common band midpoint. The two central eigenvalues are ±ω, with

`ω(q)² = v² + 2a² − 2a √(v² + a² cos²(q/2))`, where `v = Δ/2`.

The small-q coefficient of q² is `c² = a³ / (4 √(a² + v²))`. The local measurements give c = 1.68206, within 0.71% of the packet calibration. This is a velocity coefficient of the envelope, expressed in units of the prepared channel spacing and patch time. The finite couplings do not yield exact Klein–Gordon dispersion at all wavelengths.
