# Polarized material signals across native cell boundaries

**Two independently distinguishable circulating preparations reach a receiver three cell boundaries away under free Interval Echo evolution.** At the same receiver the longitudinal response is at least as large as the strongest transverse response. Resolving the frequency or varying the incidence direction does not reveal a transverse-only doublet at the specified sensitivity. The local criterion for recovering Maxwell dynamics and transverse waves at the limiting speed is open. ## What circulates and what reaches the observer?

Place an ordinary unit on empty material, or prepare a vacancy in the uniformly filled material. The twelve normalized icosahedral port vectors are rᵢ. The three Cartesian dipole orbitals at a cell have amplitudes rᵢ·e/2 for three orthogonal axes e. The facing gateway fixes the longitudinal axis z. The other two orbitals x and y have zero amplitude on that gateway. Superposing them as (x ± iy)/√2 drives opposite native seam currents with the same initial occupation at every port. Both currents have zero initial divergence and nonzero signed circulation about the gateway. The source sets this phase and local orientation. The receiving cell can sort its modes with a coherent analyzer and read the remaining local arrivals and misses separately. In every mode-detection event the original unit or vacancy is physically in the receiving cell. A second instrument uses a stored reference to read both quadratures of each of the nine transition amplitudes. Source and analyzer share a port frame and a phase reference supplied by the experiment. [Native law](../../SPEC.md) · [Complete port measurements](results.json) · [Integer counts](counts.npz)

On 80 freely connected cells, at 64 exchange times, the transverse block of the measured three-step transfer matrix has singular values **0.17834 and 0.02821**. These quantify the two independent amplitudes an analyzer can recover in the transverse plane. The largest longitudinal row or column has norm **0.17950**. From opposite circular inputs the receiver's positive circular channel returned 186,367 and 309,554 clicks out of 67,108,864 each. The corresponding negative-channel counts were 1,055,056 and 644,909. Switching the source reverses a local circulation and changes the receiver record. Across all 6,480 quadratures the simultaneous 99% error per real component is at most 0.000648, implying errors below 0.00184 on each transverse singular value and 0.00159 on the longitudinal norm. The result resolves both transverse amplitudes. The longitudinal result rejects a transverse-only interpretation of these dipoles on this route. On two cells joined by just their original connecting seam, both transverse orbitals are exactly trapped for all time. Each is an eigenmode of the isolated cell with a node at the only exit. All additional gate terms in the material sector have support on the two gateway ports and annihilate these extended source vectors, including for a vacancy. The axial source has an antinode there. At time 16 it gave 62,440,538 axial detections in 67,108,864 launches in the single-bridge unit test associated with the 80-cell route. Restoring the other federation contacts permits both transverse orbitals to travel by other paths. Closing the final bridge removes axial transmission as well. On empty material switching off contrast preserves the one-load dispersion up to a common energy. On the filled host the vacancy's local degree energy changes with contrast and its receiver response changes. [Single-bridge proof by invariant-subspace projection](test_maxwell.py)

## Frequency and direction

For a line projector P and three addressed source and receiver modes S and D, D†PS is the complex transfer residue. Its transverse two-by-two block tests whether both components reach the receiver at that frequency. The third row and column check polarization mixing and independent axial transmission. For an amplitude above 0.001 the predeclared discrimination test requires the weaker transverse component to reach half the stronger and longitudinal leakage to stay below one tenth of the weaker. This comparative test concerns a locally transverse rank-two channel in the three measured orbitals. A material sector could contain other field operators or an independent mode superposed with longitudinal material noise.

The complete finite spectra have 103 and 399 frequency groups for the one-load sector on 20 and 80 cells, respectively. The vacancy has 189 and 613 groups. Every shortest path from cell zero to distance three or less is enrolled, including different shortest paths to the same endpoint. Across **23,608 frequency–path–material comparisons**, no group passes all three requirements. Finite coherent time filters with 256 and 512 launch arms also fail those gates at every surveyed resonance and direction. The records list every line with sufficient unconstrained second singular value and the outcomes for each physical gateway frame. At the strongest source-selected frequency the shorter physical filter was also read with a coherent pointer and an independent clock-rejection port. ## Do the polarized and sharp signals travel together?

The independently launched sharp gateway packet and three dipole sources evolve on the same intact 80-cell board. At each half-tick a separate cohort reads whether the unit is anywhere in the receiving cell. The table gives the first sampled time when the proportion reaches 0.5%, with the simultaneous 99% interval in brackets. Each entry includes all 33,554,432 attempts per time and launch. These are independent terminal observations, so the first threshold crossing measures the rise of an arrival distribution.

| 1 | 0.5 [0.5, 0.5] | 27 [23.5, 28] | 9 [9, 9.5] | 0.5 [0.5, 0.5] |

A constant speed with a fixed launch delay predicts the third time as twice the second time minus the first. The x calibration predicts 31 [29, 35.5]; the receiver reads 17.5 [17, 17.5]. A detector farther along the chosen ray can cross earlier because the entire federation remains connected. At the lower threshold of 0.1% the two transverse three-step times are 12.5 and 25.5. A sharp pulse and two distinguishable transverse inputs therefore have different operational transit times at both thresholds. Continuous-time exchange has arbitrarily small short-time tails along a connected path, from the first nonzero power of its hopping operator. No strict finite support speed or physical speed of light is calibrated by these counts. Identification as radiation would require independent electric and magnetic response on a shared transverse propagating mode and a common operational speed. ## Reproduction

The source is the registered blank-memory generator. Relative to a sector constant, the unit Hamiltonian is half the native graph Laplacian. The vacancy receives the additional diagonal −2κ(6−dᵢ) at port degree dᵢ. Independent checks assemble the reduced material matrices from the full local 99-state operator, propagate the vacancy by direct exponential action and independently average 256 controlled evolutions to test the filter and clock-rejection probability. The default commands verify the stored outcomes. The experiment uses simulated shots, ideal coherent preparations and readers, supplied exchange-clock units and an icosahedral chart for each local frame.
