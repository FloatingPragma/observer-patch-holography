## What cancels the drift

Let the next receiver's dark and bright click probabilities be $a_t,b_t$, chosen using any preceding history. Conceal its independently uniform four-way preparation label until the response endpoints are fixed. The receiver responds only to the ideal exit bit. The complement includes loads outside the addressed pair. Conditional on the history, the signal click rate is $(1-p)a_t+p b_t$ for ideal exit probability $p$. Let $S_t,D_t,B_t$ be indicators of a click on the respective signal, dark, and bright preparations. The score $Z_t=S_t-(1-p)D_t-p B_t$ then has conditional mean zero for every history. Its values lie in $[-1,1]$. Conditional Hoeffding gives $\mathbb E[e^{u Z_t}\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]\le e^{u^2/2}$. Iteration and minimization of the exponential bound give $\Pr(|\sum Z_t|>N\epsilon)\le2 e^{-N\epsilon^2/2}$. Exchange the signal and half-standard labels to apply the same bound to the control. Summing the failure bounds for the 302 true scores proves joint coverage, including contrasts chosen within the enrolled menu after the records are read. Correlation between the reported intervals is allowed. Conditioning on a passed standard need not preserve the nominal level; the guarantee bounds the unconditional chance of reporting a wrong interval or falsely rejecting a valid standard. The receipt contains eight batches of four click/miss pairs per bank. One multinomial per batch draws precisely the count law of the simulated trial bank. At the following batch the response depends on the preceding click total. The decoder uses all outcomes with the fixed enrollment. The signal interval inverts the score inequality at the observed reference span. A compatible weak or negative span gives [0,1]. A failed half check or an empty intersection with the unit interval suppresses the directional estimate. Detector endpoints and generating amplitudes are unavailable to the decoder. The finite grid contributes at most half of one $2^{-32}$ bin. [Acquisition](measure.py) · [Reference and failure tests](test_receiver.py)

Even unlimited calibration counts on these standards cannot distinguish the two.
