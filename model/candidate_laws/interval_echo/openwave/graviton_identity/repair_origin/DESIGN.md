# Quadrupole modes of registered repair

Use the original one-load generator on the intact cell and the full 20-, 80- and 320-cell federations. Construct the five traceless quadratic harmonics from the regular icosahedron in the native port labelling. Check that they span one energy level and transform as the traceless symmetric tensor representation under every proper rotation. Measure the gap to the uniform one-load state by preparing their coherent superpositions with reference phases 0, π, π/2 and −π/2. Record all twelve terminal port outcomes at native exposures j/4 for j from zero to sixteen. Estimate the frequency from counts by unwrapping the two measured quadratures. Compare to a diagonal-only version with the same carrier and apparatus. This measurement uses a supplied exposure scale.

At each cell on each intact federation, insert the same five angular waves with zero amplitude outside the home cell. Test whether their algebraic coupling to the other cells has a kernel. Count the independent states in that kernel and check their full Hamiltonian residual. For one source cell per board (cell zero), use a sharp material load and native launch pulses to prepare a dark quadrupole from that kernel and a bright quadrupole selected by the first intercell contact. Let the loads fly independently. At times 0, 1, 2, 4 and 8 retain all twelve outcomes in the cell across the contact and quiet. A nonzero remote arrival from a proposed trapped mode falsifies trapping on this source. A bright arrival is evidence of moving material. Distinct tensor transformation, energy, intercell escape and gravitational coupling require their own readings.
