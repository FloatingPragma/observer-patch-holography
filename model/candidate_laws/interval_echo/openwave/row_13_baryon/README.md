# A bound three-load state with a measurable colour-singlet component

Three sharp loads on a face of one Interval Echo cell, followed by an energy measurement, give a stationary trimer below the one-load plus two-load threshold. Three separately addressed receivers can extract genuine tripartite colour entanglement from this state. On the local triplet outcome, the squared overlap with the three-fundamental SU(3) singlet is **44.98%**. A noncentral collective turn has a measurable dark port. Assigning physical quarks to the three loads or identifying an entire free baryon as colour neutral requires further evidence. OpenWave lists its static baryon row as untested on M10. ## A sharp-source preparation

Keep all thirty within-cell interactions active during the free release. The surrounding federation contacts are shuttered. The three-unit binary sector has 220 addresses. Using the same host reference in isolated one- and two-load cells, their separate ground energies add to 129.76437 exchange units. The trimer line at **100.44847** has four spatial states and lies 29.31590 units below this separation cost. Filter a sharp face into this energy. The filter accepts 19.952% of launches. The resulting state puts 99.761% of its weight on triangular faces at every free time.

Divide the twelve ports among three readers. A reversible routing transfers each address to a separate port in its owner's receiver cell. Accepting one load per receiver preserves coherence among addresses inside the receiver. Each observer uses a supplied spatial mixer to put three orthogonal local modes onto the three fundamental orbital modes of its calibrated colour instrument, with other modes recorded as misses. Isolated registered binary exchanges and addressed port phases synthesize the real basis changes. In particular, a charge is not assigned by dividing the total material load into thirds. An occupied tip still gives an ordinary unit of material contrast. ## Binding and colour on the same launches

A finite timing instrument recombines 1,048,576 possible free delays at the target trimer energy, in increments of 1/32 exchange unit. This is a specified coherent analyzer at a line identified in the spectral search. Its accepted state has 0.00347% probability outside the selected line. Its trace distance from the ideal filtered state is at most 0.005887 at every subsequent free time. A local triplet and singlet test on the state without a face flag gives the 44.985% sharp-source fidelity above.

On independent copies make a single binary measurement of membership in any of the twenty faces. On a positive record test three local triplets and then the determinant. Resolving which face was occupied would destroy coherence between face alternatives. At free wait 32 the complete observed histories are:

The joint success frequency is 6.204% of 524,288 enrolments. One-sided exact binomial lower bounds on the conditional singlet frequencies exceed 44.5% for both the sharp energy filter alone and the finite filter with a face measurement (simultaneous coverage at least 99%). The calculated conditional singlet fraction after the two earlier flags is 45.019%. At waits zero and eight it is 44.993% and 45.003%. Applying the trace-distance allowance to the ideal joint and admission probabilities separately gives a lower bound of 43.7% for this conditional read at every free wait. The corresponding calibrated read *without* the collective face flag also exceeds one third. Recording each face separately and discarding its label gives a joint determinant probability of only 4.673% per source success at wait 32. The two instruments preserve different coherences.

## What strictly stationary colour assignment is possible?

The geometry-only decoder uses three sharp addresses per receiver. The signed determinant then occupies six equally weighted configurations. All **2,217,600** inequivalent labeled address layouts up to receiver order and simultaneous colour relabeling were counted. Exactly 560 put four of the six configurations on native triangular faces; none put five or six there. Thus an inverse-prepared ideal determinant can have face probability **2/3** in this class. An unchanged sharp extraction without local mixing previously gave at most 2.11% in the common frame. The geometry-selected coherent face component, released for eight free units, gives face probability 98.583% and joint face-then-singlet probability 59.088% given source admission. Removing contrast gives 37.138% and 2.450%. In that preparation the source supplies the relative signs. [Complete layout, controls and count records](results.json)

A stronger stationary test allows arbitrary local coherent colour frames in groups of sizes 3/3/6, 3/4/5 and 4/4/4. Assign unused ports to any group to include smaller codes. These 42,735 allocations have 437 incidence-symmetry classes. For each allocation let P be the native configurations with one load per region. With H the complete generator, A is its doubled integer matrix restricted to P, and B is its doubled transfer from P to the complement. Every eigenvector supported in P must obey both Bv=0 and BAv=0. On 434 representatives B has full column rank modulo 1009. The stacked matrix has full column rank in the other three. Full modular column rank implies full rank over the complex numbers, so every stationary state has some probability outside those three spatial registers. The exact certificate also holds for exchange alone. Among all 22 distinct lines below the one-plus-two threshold the greatest achieved one-per-region probability is **99.871%**. A small amount of material outside the three regions coexists with the entanglement observed inside.

The colour frames, local extraction, energy comparator, isolated cell and joint analyser are apparatus. The calculations address a finite bound composite and accessible three-triplet correlations. The one-cell records do not identify a transportable free proton or neutron, fractional electric charges, or a physical colour field of the constituents.
