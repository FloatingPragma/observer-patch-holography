# Independently clocked propagation in the critical guide

This follow-up uses the registered κ=8 schedule with δ=1/8 and f=2/65 on the longest 45-dimer guide. The static boundary energies are known. Fix a detector based only on the signed domain-wall record algebra. In the Majorana representation every observable iΓ(u)Γ(v) for a real orthonormal pair u,v is a Hermitian parity-preserving observable. It decodes to even products of measured wall Pauli operators. Preparing its plus eigenspace on a maximally mixed fixed-parity background gives an amplitude for a similarly prepared receiving mode equal to the determinant of the two-by-two matrix of transported overlaps. No boundary-energy selection enters this propagation measurement. An ideal coherent decoder to prepare and read these collective material observables is supplied.

Choose two orthogonal real packet envelopes (a Gaussian of standard width 3 and its first derivative) at one position in the wall coordinate. Put the same two-component orientation on every wall site, using either (1,1) or (1,−1), and also (1,0) and (0,1) as orientation controls. Start each packet at site 14 and site 31 on separate guides. At times 0, 32, 64, 96 and 128 physical pulse periods scan translated copies of the same detector across all wall positions in intervals of 1/8 of a wall spacing. Enroll all settings with seed 201128 and 2^22 simulated reads per setting. Track a peak only while its centre is at least six sites from the end at both 32 and 96 periods. A track is qualified only if its peak at each time exceeds 0.75 contrast, translates at least four sites and is reciprocal on the mirrored release. All other orientations and failures are retained. Select both signs of any qualified transport and average the absolute peak displacements per physical time. Each wall spacing is two guide links. Multiplying velocity in wall sites per physical time by π gives the proposed limiting magnitude of guide-length times boundary gap: k_min is π/(2m) to leading critical boundary order, R=2n+1, m=n+1. Report finite packet and boundary resolution and any discrepancy with the independently measured gap and all-size Cayley bounds. Output generation may compress the full integer count banks.
