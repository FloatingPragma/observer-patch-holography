## Can the charge be prepared at rest and then set moving?

Sixty addressed native contacts around the sealed cycle prepare its zero-momentum state from a localized charge. The exposures are fixed before testing and use only the registered adjacent-pair propagators. The inverse source and a second contact word impart one or two momentum steps after the charge has waited freely at rest for three exchange times. The synthesis residuals are below $3\times10^{-11}$ in state norm. A separate twelve-pulse receiver on three consecutive rails resolves the antisymmetric pair by the terminal load at its middle port. Repeating that read on a uniformly sampled edge gives the entire exchange contribution to the energy. A quarter-exchange on a fresh copy gives the oriented current. Both detectors count every start.
