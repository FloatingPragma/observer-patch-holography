## Why the full federations exclude the target ratio

Let sᵢ=nᵢ−1, S=Σsᵢ=−1, and D=Σsᵢ²=2p+1. Relative to the filled host the diagonal potential, before multiplication by eight, is

    C = Σ_edges (sᵢ−sⱼ)² + Σᵢ (6−dᵢ)(sᵢ²+2sᵢ).

The native exchange term is positive semidefinite. Drop every external edge contrast to get a lower bound. For each owner, enumerate its 3¹² words over the actual three gateway geometries up to proper rotation. Its local potential obeys C_owner ≥ 3D_owner/4 + 3S_owner/2. For D=1,3,5,7, minimizing over all partitions among occupied owners sharpens the total C lower bounds to 4,11,14,18; for D≥9 the linear bound is at least 5.25. This proves **E ≥ 32** for every state in the entire negative-unit blank sector on all three boards.
