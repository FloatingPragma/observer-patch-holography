# Does exclusion of stock overlap give a strict energy threshold?

The nine-input contact comparison on a blank board gives a nonnegative joined-minus-separated energy when κ ≥ 1/2. Strengthen the finite conclusion on a connected board when each sign occurs at least once. Test whether the product of the independently cold positive and negative stock distributions has nonzero probability of a collision at matched material addresses. Every joined word maps to two stocks with zero collisions at every address. A separated product with a unique ground level, next energy gap Δ, and collision probability c would then place the joined energy at least Δc above the separated ground.

Enroll the same four inventories and 12-port matched cell as the original experiment. Use native κ=8 and its κ=4 control. The geometries, references, rates, inventories and starts are fixed before calculating the product spectra or collision rates. On separate finite preparations of the two fragment-ground stocks, measure all twelve port loads sharply. Compare the two observer words and record the binary answer of whether any common address contains both signs. Draw 2^20 independent simulated word pairs per setting; binary multinomial sampling of their exact model law gives the same record probabilities. Report independent fragment ground gaps, unique ground overlap, complete collision banks and simultaneous 99% lower count limits. The two independent ground preparations and exact fragment Hamiltonians are supplied. A sharp pair of disjoint words and a sharp pair sharing port 0 calibrate the detector. A missing positive lower limit in the finite sample is unresolved. A failed gap or disconnected material can prevent this proof of strictness. An exponent or range for physical nuclear binding is outside this test.
