# Can a single energy receiver resolve more than one bound trimer level?

Use the binding survey's three-vacancy stock with its original nine private references. Its subthreshold ground is established; its excited energies have not been inspected for this test. Randomly choose one of all 220 sharp vacancy triples with equal probabilities. Read a single native finite Fourier clock on every original launch, keeping every output. The same detector is used without change at contrasts eight, four and zero. The clock measures the isolated twelve-port material at rest on the board. It uses a supplied coherent superposition of 65,536 uniformly spaced delays with increment 2π/512, so its energy outputs cover a full period of 512 exchange units with spacing 1/128. Use 2²⁶ starts per contrast and seed 26112697. A filled sharp host at zero formation energy is the clock standard.

Declare a quarter exchange unit as the maximum distance between an accepted clock bin and its putative spectral level. A trial with energy outside that bin's quarter-unit window, modulo the clock period, can enter it with probability at most 1/[65,536 sin(π/(4·512))]². This follows by summing the finite geometric series and bounding its numerator by one. For every clock bin, compare its exact one-sided 99% simultaneous lower probability (union bound over three full scans and the host standard) against this maximum. Prove that no energy from the rest of the sector aliases into the subthreshold interval by using the sharp census lower potential and its upper potential plus thirty units of exchange. Use the surveyed *lower* limit on the cheapest proper breakup of the same three vacancies. Only accept windows with upper edge strictly below this limit. Rank qualifying bins by count from largest to smallest, breaking ties by smaller frequency, and retain windows disjoint from all higher-ranked retained windows. Report their number as a lower bound on the number of different energies below threshold. In the changed laws use their own independent fragment bounds. A single qualifying window, unresolved windows and zero accepted windows are all possible outcomes. A second level in the same finite stock would supply a material excitation level; it would not measure a nuclear isotope or a halo lifetime.

## What the blind clock measured

The native receiver resolves at least **eight different three-vacancy energies** below every proper same-sign partition on independent, identical cells. The complete sharp census bounds the native trimer spectrum between 48 and 150 exchange units and the cheapest breakup from below by 80. The eight accepted clock-bin centres were 52.40625, 60.4453125, 68.8828125, 69.9453125, 76.421875, 77.09375, 77.71875 and 78.328125. Each identifies an energy within one quarter unit. Their disjoint windows lie wholly below 80. These are lower bounds on the number of distinct energies; nearby levels can contribute to a single window. At half contrast the receiver resolves six levels below 40, with centres 28.328125, 32.390625, 36.609375, 37.203125, 37.734375 and 38.2734375. The exchange-only stock has its ground at the breakup floor zero and admits no level below it.

A filled host returned the zero bin on all 67,108,864 original launches. The quarter-unit off-window leakage of any line into a particular bin is at most 0.00009894655. Summing over arbitrary line populations preserves the bound. An exact one-sided binomial test requires at least 7,084 counts for a bin after allocating total error 0.01 to the 262,144 bins of all three scans and the host standard. The eight retained native bins recorded 419,134 to 996,500 counts, each from the same single native scan. Every energy bound lies inside one 512-unit clock period; the upper and lower potential bounds and the clock margins exclude contamination of the positive subthreshold windows from another period. A separate assembly of the three-vacancy operator from the actual federation addresses verifies that each certified window contains an eigenvalue. The held decisions use clock counts and the independent breakup and support bounds. The experiment supplies a spectrum for the confined trimer stock. Identifying physical isotopes, resolving a halo and clocking its lifetime remain open.
