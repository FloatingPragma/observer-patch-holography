# Sensitivity to the path written in the seam memories

The four-site channel gives a measured half-gap near 1.657 about the spectral midpoint −4, in native units. This comparison tests how much of that gap depends on preparing the repeated cell motif. Keep the same 320-cell board, sequence of 194 enrolled cells, gateway entrance and exit in each cell, sign-even active records, other empty writes, native degree references, coupling and continuous free exposure. Change only the internal route between the same endpoints. Choose the shortest simple internal path, with breadth-first exploration in ascending physical port order. Allow the third gateway inside a cell and count it at its actual degree. This rule has no requested number of sites per cell.

Calculate the full finite one-load spectrum on both accessible paths and count all modes in the open interval (−5,−3). It lies strictly inside the gap claimed for the periodic interior. If the alternative admits modes inside it, report their total weight in the middle half of the path and the largest probability at a physical port. An end mode alone is insufficient evidence of closing the interior gap. Also report the exact free terminal probability in the next cell on a sharp launch in the middle cell at exposures 0.5 and 1. The alternative changes the medium, with every seam of the federation active under the same law. These are generator and local arrival diagnostics. A result on these two preparations does not establish the dispersion of every memory background.
