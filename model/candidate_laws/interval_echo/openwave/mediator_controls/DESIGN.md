# A scalar control for spin-sensitive mediator observations

Use the registered Interval Echo one-load generator on one intact twelve-port cell. Set the north port to 0 and find its antipode by graph distance. Of the two proper rotations by one step of the northern neighbor pentagon about this axis, use the lexicographically first permutation. Label the northern ring from its smallest-numbered vertex and the southern ring from that vertex’s antipode, advancing by the same rotation. The sender addresses only the five northern neighbors during its preparation. Every seam evolves freely during the release. The receiver reads the five southern neighbors and retains a quiet outcome for all remaining ports.

Prepare circular modes with orbit indices +1, −1, +2 and −2 on the northern ring. Prepare standing cosine modes of orders 1 and 2 in all five rotated orientations. Run exposures 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2 in exchange units. Check rotation covariance and equal southern position records for opposite circular signs at every exposure. For the standing modes, record the populations, their discrete Fourier orders and the equal-prior message accuracy for orientation 0 versus 1. Repeat the circular sign comparison after an addressed π/2 exchange on the first southern ring seam. This receiver tests relative phase between two ordinary position amplitudes. Turn off all hopping in a changed-law control while retaining every native diagonal term.

At the declared exposure 1, sample separate position and phase-sensitive pointer counts. Use 8192 independent copies of each binary alternative, keep every quiet read and choose the higher-likelihood label for each outcome using the model probabilities. Give exact ties to the first label. Use independent reproducible PCG64 streams per comparison and label. Report the exact single-read information as well as the simulated counts. The sender supplies the orbit profile and the exchange exposure. The detector has no internal polarization or spin register. The ring Fourier orders are defined modulo five.

Separately replay the frozen intercell Interval Echo message study and its no-encoding arm using its own independent receipt checker. Its external-seam launch and plain position receiver differ from the single-cell angular experiment. Track the zero and final exposure to give the existing count and denominator for each finite board.
