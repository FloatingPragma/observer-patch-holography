### What would an observer call Λ?

A station can send two pulses, count its own ticks until each labelled echo returns, and compare these intervals with those it measured earlier. To compare with the sky, observers would also need to identify a spectral line against a local frequency standard and infer a distance by a separate brightness or angular-size measurement. Observers could then ask whether one law predicts the *joint* distance and redshift records over directions and epochs, with the actual selection, resolution and undetected sources included. Supernova fluxes and BAO angular and radial scales supply examples of distinct astronomical measurements. Our timed material relay has no calibrated luminosity or spectroscopic line.
