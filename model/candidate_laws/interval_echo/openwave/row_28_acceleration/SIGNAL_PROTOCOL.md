# Comparing successive signals and snapshot ranges

The settling survey yields positive early range growth and fragmentation. This follow-up tests the same histories with clocks at the receiver and a second ruler, and adds dilute initial matter to distinguish catastrophic fragmentation from a general settling effect.

On a blank collar an addressed pi pulse transfers a tag, the far mailbox latches its arrival, and an inverse pulse restores the probe and seam. A written magnitude-two collar prevents transfer. Read-and-undo leaves the deposited collar and material intact. The feasibility test applies the complete 99-state matrices separately to a blank collar and both measured written signs. For this follow-up keep those addresses when they give a missing return. On separate copies emit a labelled query to each at rounds 0, 4, 48 and 52.
