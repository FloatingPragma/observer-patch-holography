# Reading the dimer inventories with all connectors present

Put one vacancy and one double on each dimer. On fresh copies choose its two sharp orientations with equal probability, independently on each dimer. After an unobserved native flight of duration $t$, read both rails of every dimer. Count an exit when at least one total differs from two. Let $w_e>0$ be the coefficient of physical connector $e$ between distinct dimers and $p_e$ the probability of a blank on that memory before release. For any fixed joint preparation of the connector memories independent of the sharp choices, the measured exit probability satisfies

$$
\lim_{t\to0}\frac{P_{\rm exit}$t$}{t^2}
=\tfrac18\sum_e w_e^2 p_e .
$$

The graph has one seam per physical port pair. All four seams between a given pair of dimers may be present. The memories can be entangled with one another or with spectator registers. The formula concerns inter-dimer contacts, with the exterior shuttered and with all other active terms preserving the balanced dimer subspace. Distances, the order of the dimers and the contrast coefficient do not enter the result.

On the four sharp endpoint words the written block evolves memory alone. For an unlike pair the blank block swaps the loads with amplitude $-1/2$ in the generator. Its diagonal cost is $4\kappa+1/2$. Both the swap and the diagonal cost vanish on equal blank endpoints. The native-column check evaluates the two coefficients of the conjugated seam generator separately on all 44 input columns. [Exact column certificate](connector_guard.py)

Fix one initial rail word. A single exit labels the two affected dimers and the direction of transfer, because one reads 00 and the other 22. Each of those dimers held a single rail of each kind. The initial word and the exit thus identify the precise two rails swapped. Distinct active crossings have orthogonal output words even if they share a dimer. Squaring the first-order exit amplitude from seam $e$ gives $w_e^2p_e/4$. Averaging the sharp choices multiplies this by the probability $1/2$ that the two endpoints differ. Linearity gives the same expression for mixed memories. Finite-dimensional unitary evolution justifies the short-time expansion.

Exact protection of all the enrolled inventory records forces the sum to vanish. Positivity gives $p_e=0$ on every active crossing. The written-block action then contributes the same operator on memory for every orientation. Its inter-dimer ordering energy, defined by the difference of work readings for aligned and opposite dimer orientations, vanishes. Multiple ordinary connectors therefore cannot produce the protected ordering term of the guarded guide on the complete raw dimer stock using a common passive memory preparation. A material that changes inventories, a smaller encoded set of inputs, correlated orientation-dependent preparations or a powered controller requires its own experiment. In particular this calculation does not decide the freely propagating wall experiment or the actual energy of a daughter in the driven guide.

It checks the native single-contact columns and then sums the amplitudes into actual final rail words before squaring. It tests every sharp input and blank/written pattern for chains of two and three dimers with all four physical connectors on each neighboring pair, with unequal seam weights.
