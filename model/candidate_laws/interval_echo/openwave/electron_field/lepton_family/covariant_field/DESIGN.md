# Unitary transport on every material and field transition

Replace each native undirected transition of positive weight by parallel transport through an arbitrary unitary on a common finite-dimensional internal space. Attach the inverse to the reverse transition, keep the native scalar diagonal port potential and keep the positive contact and ring weights. Derive dimension-independent variational bounds, then reproduce the scalar witness keys. Include a separately dressed matched neutral vacuum using a sharply localized trial state to bound its unknown ground energy.

Verify the operator implementation on a pure-gauge SU(2) connection with varying local frames. Construct a curved connection by fixing a breadth-first spanning tree to the identity and assigning the two noncommuting SU(2) quarter-turn generators to every remaining material and ring edge respectively. Record actual physical configuration cycles and full-sector spectral extrema. Use no laboratory values or tunable masses in the construction. Classify what other terms, preparations and graphs fall outside the result.
