# A cyclic register transported by existing material contacts

Use the verified 48-cell ring with the three-port degree motif 6, 5, 6, its material hopping weight 4, and its degree-five energy 8. Give the separated charged end an additional three-state register. Forward passage along route seam e adds a fixed integer aₑ modulo three to the register and reverse passage subtracts it. Retain the same contact diagonal, degree term and neutral reference. Include all three total shifts around the ring and all three Fourier characters. Compute lowest charged energies, their ratios, and the Koide quotient from all allowed momenta. Check the full spectrum against an independent real-space assembly on the physical route and verify relabeling equivalence for arbitrary seam allocations. Use repeated motifs at 12, 24, 96, 192 and 384 cells as finite-size controls, distinguished from the verified 48-cell fixture. Test character-changing matrix elements and degeneracy under character conjugation.

Separately examine a product register whose positive balanced circulant M supplies an additive energy s M†M on top of the field's lowest charged energy. Derive its Koide quotient and conservation of register character for every s>0 and every phase in the positive chamber. No particle masses, phase fit or external energy calibration enter either test.
