Study reducing configurations with one state per distinct physical port, enabled hop weight four on every active edge, and no additional dynamical ring or head state. The resulting operator is four times the active graph Laplacian plus eight on physical degree-five ports. Results about the scalar operator apply to a field preparation only if that reduction is established there.

Use all three stored federations. The preliminary census gave largest connected components of degree-six ports of sizes 6, 6 and 12. Test every possible connected subgraph under this reduction by bounding its extreme energies. Treat closed cycles, open paths and arbitrarily branched guides separately. For unpinned components report the zero-energy state and positive finite-mode span with open or untwisted cycle boundary conditions. For pinned components prove a lower bound using local generalized Rayleigh quotients and verify each bound in exact rational arithmetic. Use the largest multiple of one thousandth strictly below the smallest local eigenvalue as the trial bound. Retain controls with the onsite term suppressed and with the longest sampled unpinned arm attached to a native degree-five port. Compare physical ratios only after computing these bounds.
