# Transporting a signed spin on the interacting contact ring

A sender injects or removes one unit of material at site 0 while retaining a spin reference. After one binary energy measurement and a free wait, a receiver on the opposite side recovers the transported spin. On eight sites, the receiver can read the signed unit and its neighboring spin on the same run. Every probability below uses the finite model. The spinor field, the calibrated injection and detection ports, and the spectral filter are supplied apparatus. ## What makes the packet move

The archived periodic Weyl chain has speed $1/8$ and on-site repulsion $U=1$. On rings with four or eight sites the published onsite spin frame converts its kinetic term to hopping $t=1/16$ without changing the contact interaction. Prepare the half-filled singlet from an alternating spin word, retaining refusals. The local pump exchanges a signed unit with a reservoir and writes one of two spin inputs. Each pump or drain has amplitude normalization $1/\sqrt2$ and a complementary refusal. For holes the positive-spin port removes a down spin. The read of local charge is occupation relative to one per site. [Archived source and complete four-site frame check](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/tree/3bcacf1276ddb3ead35be2b9adcd63b6643e2d35/model/mass_generation_audit)

Select the smallest interval from the charged-sector floor that contains two distinct energies the pump can populate. This follow-up choice used the four-site spectrum. A spin-3/2 level inside that interval is unreachable from the singlet with this pump. On the two reachable energy subspaces, translation through half the ring has opposite signs. After the wait $\pi/(E_2-E_1)$, the higher energy component acquires exactly that relative minus sign. Free evolution then translates every admitted input, including one entangled with the sender's reference, up to a common phase. The same linear receiver then gives equal counts for the half-beat and translation experiments, including terminal refusals. The half-ring displacement also preserves the local spin axes in the archived frame. The endpoint contains two orbital modes and the local spinor port has positive weight on the direction selected by continuation. The other translation component reaches a different level. This links the measured energies on one source with a specified probe and leaves the interpretation as a single isolated particle pole open.
