# A signed spin arrival with a measured partner

One observer holds the compensating charge. Another receives the moving end one bond past its launch. Both store the endpoint spins in independent neutral memories. On the eight-cell Wilson source, **at least 38.3399%** of original starts per sign admit this addressed transfer. The spin comparisons give a Bell value of $2\sqrt 2$. A spin mark applied at the launch flips the transverse correlation at the receiving end and leaves the longitudinal comparison unchanged. The same spin law holds on the accepted energy records described below. ## The two receivers

Use member zero at source revision `3bcacf1276ddb3ead35be2b9adcd63b6643e2d35`, including its polynomial preparation, gauge constraints, idle reference and energy clock. Choose one of the 48 directed two-bond paths $y,x,z$ uniformly before the run. For the positive arrival, check that the two negative-beta spin modes of the charge-six singlet at $y$ are filled and that its two positive-beta modes at each of $x,z$ are empty. For the negative arrival exchange the two beta sectors and exchange filled with empty. Check both path links for the constant character. All these local projections commute with the gauge constraints. Each path has its own success and complementary refusal. Every other source register keeps its conditional correlations.

Use the native regional scalar pair instrument on the first bond. On the admitted input its creation operator is an isometry. It gives equal coherent amplitudes for the two spin transfers and writes the compensating link character. Apply the isolated, beta-projected Wilson hop on the second bond for duration $\pi$ in the source units. Its matrix element has magnitude $1/2$ on either spin. Shuttering the other terms, including the link’s electric detuning during this pulse, is part of the apparatus. The second link takes up the transfer flux. The local charge counters can bracket each operation without reading the initial charge. With a positive arrival the increments at $y,x,z$ are first $(-6,6,0)$, then $(0,-6,6)$. The negative arrival reverses both records. The changes satisfy the original Gauss constraints at every site. The numbers give Wilson hypercharge in its integer normalization. At both ends read the single resident spin of the relevant two-mode beta sector. A local SWAP exchanges its spin index with an independent blank memory and leaves a fixed resident spin at that end. The other modes and the two charges are retained. Grouping the six active modes first in the fermion ordering makes the even composite operation the identity on the other matter modes. On each accepted path the two memories factor from the residual source and its original reference as a spin singlet. A relative sign from annihilating the second occupied row is included. Setting the transverse spin mark at $x$ before the hop changes the $XX$ comparison from −1 to +1. The $ZZ$ comparison stays −1. Leaving the second contact closed gives a vacant receiving doublet for an excess, or a completely filled one for a vacancy. In either case the occupation-one receiver refuses.

Choose $Z,X$ at the holder and $(Z+X)/\sqrt2,(Z-X)/\sqrt2$ at the receiver. The four signed coincidence averages in that order are $(-1,-1,-1,+1)/\sqrt2$. Reading and forgetting the spin in the $Z$ basis at $x$ preserves the two charge transfers and changes the four-setting Bell value to $\sqrt2$. The probes, isolated hop, neutral spin memories and analysis directions are supplied instruments. This is an exact finite detector calculation on a prepared pair. ## Admission on the original preparation

Let $D_6$ count all positive-beta occupations and negative-beta vacancies in the singlet. Let $C_\ell$ be the native Casimir of link $\ell$. The charged-string theorem bounds the expectation of $D_6/16+(5/18)\sum C_\ell$ by $d<0.205533451$. These are bounds on the failure of specified local readings derived from the original source-energy bound. They do not require a measurement of every mode or a replacement of the source by a filled reference. [Charged-string allocation][charged] · [Source and clock][contract]

The union of the three local matter refusals is bounded by the number of offending modes. Each path role visits each of the eight sites equally. Charge conjugation of the original preparation exchanges positive-beta occupations with negative-beta vacancies. For either sign the mean matter refusal is therefore at most $3\langle D_6\rangle/16$. Each path uses two of the twelve interior links. The first nonconstant Casimir is at least $3/10$, so the mean link refusal is at most $(5/9)\langle\sum C_\ell\rangle$. Together these imply admission of at least $1-3d>0.38339964$. The statuses of remote links, other matter species and the rest of the cube are unrestricted. ## An energy record on the same accepted runs

Apply the common source clock before the local filters and after storing the spins. Choose the two spin-analysis settings on the neutral memories independently of the clock. Write $K$ for the accepted source operation on a fixed path with the spins stored. On the six active modes it maps one sharp input word to one sharp output word. On each selected link it maps the constant word to the oriented character. The spin factor of the complete operation is the singlet in the two memories, or its marked version. Source-clock operations after the SWAP leave that factor intact. Storing the spins changes their resident orientations. The energy interval below is the work of this complete intervention on the source.

The onsite energy increases by six in native units. The electric commutator adds $63/4$ on each of the two links, with exact transfer centre $75/2$. For the two selected hopping terms the single-link Haar bound gives $\sqrt{40}$ per commutator. On each selected link the constant and the output character separately have zero hopping compression. The outgoing term and the returning term use orthogonal input and output sectors. The same norm bound therefore applies when a second link projection is included in $K$. The three distinct plaquettes touching the path contribute at most $6/7$ together. These estimates use the full parent operators before word compression. The rank-one link maps lie inside the actual word cutoff on both sides. [One-link norm and clock bounds][charged]

For the other hopping terms, let $V$ map each of the six active spin rows to its neighboring modes on the remaining bonds. All six columns have support outside the active set. The two endpoints are separated by two bonds and there is no triangle. A single remaining bond contributes squared norm $1/2$ per row, in either orientation of the actual Wilson coefficient. The two endpoint sites each have two such bonds and the middle site has one. Thus $\sum_i\|Ve_i\|^2=5$, pointwise in the other link fields whose singlet characters have unit modulus. On a sharp active occupation word, hopping on distinct active rows gives orthogonal new occupations. Each spectator creation or annihilation operator has norm at most $\|Ve_i\|$. Hopping into or out of this word has norm at most $\sqrt5$. Both the input and the fixed output of $K$ have the same total active occupation. The cross-hopping changes it by one, so the input supports and output supports of the two terms of the commutator are orthogonal. This gives $\sqrt5$ for their difference. Terms using only spectator rows commute with the matter map.

It follows that $\|[H,K]-(75/2)K\|\le 2\sqrt{40}+\sqrt5+6/7<\sqrt{248}$. The selected preparation is stationary. The left hand side squared bounds the centered second moment of the original accepted two-energy measure. Chebyshev’s inequality with radius 30, outward rounding by $1/8$ and the inherited two-clock event loss at most $1/32$ give

\[
 P\bigl(\text{signed arrival and recorded transfer in }[59/8,541/8]\bigr)
 \ \ge\ 1-3d-248/900-1/32\ >\ 0.07659409.
\]

This lower bound is per original start for either fixed sign setting. For every accepted clock bin of positive probability the same spin coincidences hold, because the neutral memories factor before the final clock. The energy includes both ends, two gauge links and the interventions. A single-particle rest mass requires a separate calibrated branch comparison. The statistical weights here are analytic bounds. The calculation does not enumerate the interacting energy histogram. [Finite clock readout][clock]

Charge conjugation preserves the full Hamiltonian, its original energy zero, the preparation and the gauge constraints. It exchanges the accepted inputs and the two reset outputs up to phase, reverses both compensating link characters and maps each refusal to its conjugate. The six-mode calculation checks this action using the native Clifford matrix on all 64 local words. Each stored spin pair has the same coincidence law in the matched detector frame. Therefore the two conjugate instruments have equal joint classical clock and spin probabilities after reversing the signed charge labels.
