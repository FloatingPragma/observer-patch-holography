## Closing the species assignment

Use one source, one arrival criterion agreed before the read, and one calibration record for each sign. Record which sign arrived, where it arrived, the source refusal and the remote compensation. Randomize the subsequent intervention on identically heralded arrivals. Retain the signed arrival and an independently resolved common energy branch when taking the spin readings, or prove that the spin instrument has the stated action throughout that admitted branch. An unresolved mixture could otherwise give a mass line on one component and a spin signal on another. Incompatible angular settings can use fresh copies after the same admission. Keep missed arrivals, detector failures and source preparation probabilities in the joint record.

1. **Electric charge.** A receiver at the arrival and a calibrated external probe must agree on opposite unit electric charges. Reverse the applied field and the probe sign. Include the zero-charge and admitted fractionally split controls in a quantization claim. A native unit of occupancy or a unit of Wilson hypercharge needs an explicit map to electromagnetic charge.
2. **Rest mass and recoil.** Use the same arrival family, calibrated clock, momentum ruler and compensation standard. Compare the two charge signs with each other and compare their low-momentum curvature with their vacuum-referenced rest line. In a specified relativistic regime test $E(p)^2-E(0)^2=c^2p^2$ on held readings using an independently established signal speed. The physical benchmark is $m_e c^2$ near 0.511 MeV. If that value sets the unit, the independent comparison is the recoil or a mass ratio that was withheld from calibration. [Particle Data Group electron listing](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/listings/rpp2025-list-electron.pdf) · [Independent trapped electron mass comparison](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.75.3598)
3. **Spin and statistics.** For both signs compare two nonparallel calibrated angular axes, a one-end full turn against an untouched reference, and two full turns. Read the interference phase with a sham geometric turn and the same elapsed time. Compare the exchange of two intact like charges with a route restoring every observable guide record. A spin-to-cyclotron frequency ratio measured on the same sign-tagged branch provides a held magnetic test. Experimenters have measured both frequencies for trapped electrons and positrons. [Electron and positron frequency comparison](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.59.26) · [Electron magnetic moment measurement](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.071801)
4. **Electron and positron identity.** Demonstrate conjugate detector responses, equal rest mass and stability in the specified domain. To identify the full Standard Model species, resolve the lightest charged colour-singlet lepton and its conjugate using their calibrated weak response or a finite theorem establishing that assignment. A supply of two spin labels and a common fitted energy also fits other charged doublets. Pair creation or a channel with two outgoing photons supplies an independent cross-check when its preparation, photon detectors and energy budget are established. The finite implication can replace direct measurement of any consequent. The needed evidence is the measured antecedent on the candidate itself.
