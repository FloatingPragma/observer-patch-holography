# Writing a fresh reference at the source after preparation

**IT'S NEVER ABOUT THE STATE, IT'S ALWAYS ABOUT WHAT OBSERVERS MEASURE, COMPARE AND RECORD AS SHARED AGREED-ON CONSENSUS FACTS.**

Can a receiver recover quantum information that first existed on a signed charge at the source after the shared preparation ended? Which stations must compare records? Use the existing asymmetric three- and four-fan guides, the two physical charge signs, the outer-ring code and the two archived appointments 27.0625 and 30.8125. Add zero wait as a source-locality control. The previous coarse-clock experiment already had a remote signal at zero subsequent wait. It cannot answer this delayed-choice question. 1. On the original source use an equal, incoherent mixture of the two archived inputs, with all other fans in their calibrated neutral vacuum. On the main arm apply the existing binary energy clock to the entire guide. Pool its refusals. Read the charge at source hub 98 and project its ring onto the two-dimensional local code without resolving its direction. Pool charge or code refusals separately. On a control arm bypass the clock on the same stock.
2. Only after that source herald, bring up a fresh Bell pair. Swap its source half with the accepted local ring code, keeping or discarding the old source register locally. The stored reference has never interacted with the preparation clock. All other fan records, including correlations among them, are left in place. This is a supplied source instrument. Launch without any further energy reading. On independent copies repeat the coarse energy measurement immediately after the swap to quantify the cost of localization.
3. After the fixed free wait, read the receiver's enclosing signed charge, its hub occupancy and its outer-ring code. On repeated launches measure each pair of local Pauli axes on the retained reference and the receiving ring. For each subset of the other stations compare that subset's neutral-ring vacuum flags. Trace every unrequested record. Include the empty subset, every singleton, and all larger subsets. Report the click probability per original preparation and per successful fresh write. Every refusal remains in the original-preparation ledger. List all inclusion-minimal sets that show a negative partial transpose, including an empty list when no set succeeds. The exhaustive subset comparison is specified here before reading its outcomes.
4. Zero wait and parking the material bridges after writing must give exactly zero receiving charge. A reader with the reference dephased in its preparation basis must give a separable result. Halve the bridge rates only during flight, keeping every stock, window, local measurement and clock appointment fixed. This is a changed-law control for flight. The full local information transfer could fail at either appointment, with any number of station reports. A positive rate of classical arrivals alone leaves the reference test open.

For the prior two-line band also enclose the maximum overlap between any sharply localized launch at the source hub and the band, first with the full hub and then with the local ring code. On a separate read ask how often the signed charge is in the source or receiver cell for either band input. Certify position bounds with the exact-integer residuals and count of band lines. The tests use the written-field comparison law, a selected guide, a supplied source memory and detector gates. They bear on a charge carrying internal information. Row 4 names particle stability and row 30 measures a frequency–wave-number relation. Neither test asks only for information transfer, and this experiment supplies neither a lifetime of the physical electron nor its dispersion.
