# The receiving guide can keep the phase after signed material leaves

The same late four-fan arrivals give observers two spatially separated records.
A material counter at 1010 registers one vacancy or one excess.
At the same terminal appointment, the now unoccupied fan at 1045 and the
untouched reference at 98 still give a nonseparable comparison. This holds
for both signs on the original four-fan generator once the original
charge-and-face arrival has been heralded. It disproves the reading that this
particular receiving-face witness must follow the material load.

## Same preparation and two separate receivers

Use the actual asymmetric route 98–241–1011–1045 of the 320-cell fixture
and its clock-bypassed source. On a fresh copy the first analyzer accepts

move freely. The terminal sharp signed-material counter at **1010** and the
head analyzer on **1045** commute. The reference at **98** supplies a third
comparison. All three records can be joined on the same run. The original
1045 witness compared the reference only while the load was at 1045.

## Direct comparison of phase marks

The isolated bridge term provides an exact control. For each of 32 admitted
microscopic alternatives it acts on a pair consisting of the two load
locations and one reversed boundary head, with generator
$4\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$.
The internal head words on both sides of the bridge are untouched. It
follows that the terminal 1010 read succeeds with probability
$\sin^2(4t)$ conditional on the first gate. The 1045/reference comparison
on those successes is exactly the captured comparison. Applying a supplied
phase mark to the second 1045 orientation changes this comparison, while
it leaves every joint 1010-head and 98-reference report law exactly
unchanged when 1045 is unread. This follows from the identity action on
the internal heads and invariance of the partial trace under the mark.

Under the full native generator, this mark is not invisible for all waits.
At the late second wait 1/16 it changes the 1045/98 fixed expectation from
about −0.008902 to +0.334423. The absolute change of **any** binary
1010-head/98-only test probability given sharp terminal charge is at most
$5.620\times10^{-5}$, and the unconditional sharp 1010 probability changes
by about $1.139\times10^{-7}$ per original start. The bound uses the
reduced density of the previous fan's heads and the reference with the
1045 fan unread, including the actual available sharp-head and local
face/reference comparisons. By second wait 1/4 the corresponding general
bound is about 0.047178, and the change in the sharp 1010 rate is about
0.000162670. The native guide can feed information back into the records
beside the load. The calibration establishes exact locality of the mark
only for the isolated bridge.

The observable result is a sharp, conjugate pair of mobile material
records plus a coherently detachable face record. It narrows which existing
measurements can serve as evidence of an intrinsic traveling degree of
freedom. A test for one would have to reproduce the comparison near the
moved material after the old guide is unread or independently reset, along
with an independent material/electric calibration. This experiment leaves
other bound modes open and does not identify the signed material as electric
charge or establish a physical constituent, electron, or positron.
