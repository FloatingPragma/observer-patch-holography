It bounds each projected amplitude using the certified band-frame error and divides a lower bound for the joint probability by an upper bound for the terminal probability. Every claim uses the appropriate denominator.

Each case uses a particular coherent source input. Its coefficients can depend on the chosen wait. The inputs were selected to expose conditional behavior. These are neither typical Bell-source trials nor a bound for a single source held fixed over every wait. A measurement that resolves which of the two energies passed will change the preparation.
