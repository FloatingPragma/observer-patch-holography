# Exchange of two charges prepared with field-energy endpoints

Use the 12 ports and 20 triangular rings of the same written cell and the same reference head word as the one-cell parity-string test. Choose all pairs of distinct external gateways with a common internal neighbour. This neighbour is the centre of a three-arm junction. Use each spare neighbour of the centre in turn as the third arm. Both source charges have the same sign and each has a separate frozen compensator beyond its own gateway. Repeat for excesses and vacancies. Geometry, sources, pulses and filters are specified without an electron mass or a fitted phase.

Starting on arms A and B, require the six successive transfers A to centre, centre to spare, B to centre, centre to A, spare to centre, centre to B. The material origins then exchange. Each isolated enabled written contact is pulsed for π/8 and has unit transfer amplitude. Switch off the other terms during each pulse as in the controlled winding experiment. In the preparation basis, allow the three specified material contacts and all 20 ring flips to enumerate the accessible head words at each two-load position.

Test all choices of one incident triangular face for each of the four sites. Require distinct faces and a positive field energy at both occupied endpoint faces at each of the six two-load positions. At a given position prepare or read the equal-amplitude field over its accessible words with a sign given by the dual parity string joining the two assigned faces. A material move sees one face change while the spectator face stays fixed. After each pulse project on the required material positions and that coherent field pattern. Include projection failures. The product of the six signed matrix elements gives the accepted amplitude relative to an ideal coherent identity arm with matched phase. Its square is the unconditional six-read success probability. Record the signs, zero channels, the complete assignment counts, and the largest success probability of each sign. Separate settings with vertex-disjoint endpoint faces at every step.

Use the uniform field, a field with both endpoint faces held at their source locations, and the same charge motions with only position readouts as controls. Following a path with its inverse gives nonnegative amplitude. The sequence requires the complete charge exchange irrespective of the chosen field reader. Repeated coherent field projections are supplied apparatus. The same exchange with only position checks tests whether its sign persists without them.
