# Searching the port alphabet for a conjugate of the persistent pair

The row 4 source places load 1 on ports 0 and 1 of cell zero and load 0 elsewhere. Test every uniform permutation of the three port labels as a proposed partner. This gives six ordered choices of the background load and the distinct load on the two exceptional ports. Keep the source addresses, sphere, blank collars and zero-load reference registers fixed. This tests a conjugation acting only on port labels. A changed background requires its own preparation.

On the 20-cell sphere run all 630 seams continuously at the registered contrast strength. At native exposures 0, ¼, 1 and 4, measure the two exceptional ports on an internal seam of the starting cell or record quiet. Include all 31 outcomes and 65,536 simulated preparations per setting. Undo each native terminal echo after its sharp flag is copied. The flag must resolve equal exceptional loads on every input in the binary sector, including when the two available loads differ by two. Use both the written contrast and outgoing loads.

An exact conjugation of the free generator by the proposed permutation may add one constant to the energy. Reject it if the generator difference takes distinct diagonal values on accessible pair configurations. Check separately whether terminal record distributions distinguish the two preparations. Call an exact record difference resolved when total variation exceeds 10⁻⁷. For sampled counts, use a two-sample, simultaneous Hoeffding bound on the largest address-frequency difference, with family error at most 1% across 31 outcomes, five alternatives and three positive exposures. Record every comparison. An indistinguishable finite sample leaves that comparison unresolved.

Compare against exchange alone on the full screen, which is invariant under every port permutation, and against an isolated echosahedron with uniform degree. The latter isolates the effect of the fixed degree-reference registers. Check the projected potential against direct sums of native squared contrasts and reference terms. A positive partner identification would also need an independently established electromagnetic current with opposite charges. The experiment tests neither this current nor transformations of the seam memories.
