## What a native load pin does

Pin a set $S$ of vertex loads to specified sharp values throughout the flight. Its compressed generator is $P_sHP_s$ on the pin subspace. Delete $S$ from the physical contact graph and take each remaining connected component. Assign the memory of an edge with exactly one endpoint outside $S$ to the component of that endpoint. An edge wholly inside $S$ retains its private memory. No projected edge term or degree term now touches two such factors.
