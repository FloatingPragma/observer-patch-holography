# Counting the two outgoing modes

Use the existing four-dimer ring, three-dimer wings, ordered and wall-pair sources, and fixed exposure 32. Determine the one-wing normal frequencies from the measured interaction coefficients and the fixed-end boundary, independently of the collision. Decode every recorded energy pair into normal-mode occupations on the native, cut and zero-contrast arms. Check the spectral identification directly against finite orientation Hamiltonians at multiple lengths and both source phases. Report the gap and the number of bulk branches for a uniform extension of the fixed-end guide.

The existing energy counts show that when each wing carries one excitation the two are commonly in different modes. The phase in that pair of alternatives has not been read. Before calculating the phase, assign the two lowest single-mode frequencies to local logical levels zero and one in each attached native wing. Condition only on the ordered source and this local admission before choosing the readout setting. Fix each mode's real phase by positive transition amplitude to the ground under the outer-dimer swap. A local energy-dephased pair can carry the measured frequency correlation and should have zero X/X correlation. The coherent two-mode analysers are supplied.
