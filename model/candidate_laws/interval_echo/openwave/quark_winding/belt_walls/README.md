# Fractional material transport on a prepared icosahedral belt

A period-three density wave on ten native ports has two mobile defects. One carries a local excess of −⅓ and the other +⅔ relative to the periodic material background. Both shift the density pattern by one site across the core. The experiment measures this phase slip, the local excess and the motion of ordinary loads on the same preparations. A separate written-head experiment records the transported material. The interpretation is a fractional polarization of the prepared medium. The electromagnetic unit and the six quark flavours have no assignment from these data. [Design and controls](DESIGN.md) · [One-wall observations](belt.json) · [Calibrated receivers](receivers.json)
