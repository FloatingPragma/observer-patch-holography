# Changing the three receivers

Two specified partitions of the twelve ports gave a nonzero collective centre response on accepted triangular addresses. As a follow-up, enumerate all partitions into three unlabelled sets of four. Every port still transfers to a single distinct material port. Each receiver must contain exactly one load for the colour read. Keep all partitions, even any with no accepted triangular face. Record the number of accepted faces and the worst rational diagonally dominant bound on the ideal dark probability over coherent triangular inputs. A nonpositive bound is inconclusive. Independent proper rotations of receiver frames commute with the one-load centre projector.

Also ask how often an ideal three-triplet antisymmetric singlet, returned through the inverse swaps, would put all three loads on a face of the original cell. Sum its squared projection over accepted face addresses, keeping other ports and other cells in the denominator. Use the common incidence frame for the exact calculation. Give a separate bound allowing different receiver frames or different sharp destination ports, derived from the calibrated triplet weight of a sharp port. Compare the result with the bound-face weight under free repair. This projection is a comparison to a supplied singlet and makes no assumption that the dynamics prepares it.
