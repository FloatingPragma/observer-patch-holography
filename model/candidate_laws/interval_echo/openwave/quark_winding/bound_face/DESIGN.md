# Charge and collective colour on a three-load face

Use the native blank-memory Interval Echo seam at κ = 8. Use its twelve-port cell, binary three-load sector and all internal contacts during flight. Test the first and second triangular faces in carrier order, on fresh sharp releases at zero and one exchange time. On fresh copies from each sharp face also select the lowest energy of the intact cell with an ideal energy measurement. Keep the selection probability. Use exchange alone as the changed flight and binding law, with the same sources and readers. The large ground-state face weight at κ = 8 is already known. The constituent and collective reads below test additional properties.

Measure the signed seam difference between a chosen port and a sharp reference using the full 99-state echo. Calibrate the pointer on all nine local input words, including vacancy and excess against a reference of one. Also compare the bright ports of a controlled native contact against an idle reference for probe loads zero and two at time π/32, in both phase quadratures. Retain exchanged outputs. Apply the calibrated reads on separate copies to each of the three birth ports. Record their unconditional marginal occupations and the previously occupied face conditioned on a terminal face address. The signed material unit is the load relative to its declared background. Attaching it to laboratory electric charge requires another measurement.

For colour, isolate the same prepared cell with three empty neighbours on the twenty-cell federation. Assign the three ordered launch-face vertices to separate receiving cells in increasing cell order. Assign the remaining nine ports cyclically by address, giving four possible addresses per receiver. Send each whole source register to the identically numbered local port in its assigned cell by a transposition along a shortest physical path; reverse the intermediate swaps to restore transit registers. Check the permutation and every blank local π-swap. An ideal count of total occupancy at each receiver retains coherence among port addresses. Use all branches with exactly one load per receiver for the guarded colour test. Publish every other occupancy pattern and every failed source.

Apply the calibrated one-load SU(3) centre word in all three receivers in one interference arm, then undo the routing in both arms. Read bright and dark on the complete returned registers, tracing over all material outcomes. Compare sham, centre followed by its inverse, and a half-angle turn generated by the same traceless colour axis. Use identical registered port frames. As independent reader inputs supply three colour triplets in an antisymmetric singlet and three identical triplet orbitals orthogonal to the half-turn axis. The latter has trivial collective centre and a nontrivial half-turn. Estimate finite-pulse errors against the ideal words. Decompose accepted inputs by their number of triplet outcomes, and calculate the weight of the three-triplet antisymmetric singlet in these calibrated frames.

Finally test every coherent superposition supported on triangular addresses that pass the specified routing. Compress the ideal collective-centre dark effect to that entire subspace, and bound its least eigenvalue against finite-word error. A resolved dark response excludes a singlet for this extracted action. A null centre alone cannot establish a singlet. Report this bound separately from the freely evolved and ground-state reads, which also include nonface configurations. No intrinsic three-load gauge action, fractional electric source or string winding is supplied by this test.
