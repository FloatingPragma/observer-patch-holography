# When two face readings require a string

Two observers on separated faces find an exact linear energy law **if the rest of their material retains even face parity**. Allowing additional odd faces caps the minimum energy at two mismatched bridges, even when both original readings are held exactly. The local material can produce that extra pair through one reopened native contact.

The two endpoints here carry a measured binary parity. OW-22 asks for the static inter-charge potential with a Cornell tension of about 1 GeV/fm. Establishing the connection requires an independent colour-source reader on the same excitation, an effective distance and energy calibration, and evidence for the separation regime governing that source. **OW-22 stays open.** The upstream M10 author construction uses a prescribed colour chain. The present calculation instead uses neighboring load comparisons in a prepared medium on the registered candidate. ## What observers do

The parties survey the exterior bridges of their cells by exchanging port and neighbor address records. The observed cubic graph is planar and 3-connected on all three surveyed boards, so its facial circuits are fixed up to reflection. Two faces are neighbors when they share a bridge. A ruler counts shared-face crossings on the shortest surveyed route. Its unit is one face step. A physical radar survey in an emergent metric could give a different ruler.

Each bridge has two ordinary ports carrying sharp load 0 or 2. Its two owners compare those load outcomes. A face flips its bit each time the bridge reports disagree as its boundary is traversed. Odd means its bit flipped an odd number of times. Each disagreement registers at two faces, so a lone odd face is impossible on a closed board. The energy compared here is pair creation work relative to the zero-energy even background.

On the supplied half-filled medium each owner starts with six doubles and six vacancies. Its three gateway ports start empty. Each has a distinct occupied, adjacent private port. Opening their internal native seam for a duration π switches their occupancies with unit probability. Any chosen set of mismatches can thus be prepared, one gateway per selected bridge, without changing any owner's inventory. A detour does not change the endpoint readings. No path field or term proportional to its length is added.

During the initial work measurement every exterior bridge runs the registered blank-memory seam generator. Internal contacts and degree-five private-reference services are parked. A sharp six-double inventory guard on each owner gives the usual ideal projected generator. A supplied coherent reference arm compares an exposed word to a zero-energy reference via controlled evolution and two quadrature reads; an eigenstate writes its relative energy into the advancing phase. Seven rescalings after the shortest clock appointment unwrap the phase. Each quadrature retains both outcomes through its count and fixed denominator. The terminal ternary load read is sharp in the guarded preparations. A coherent mismatched bridge always produces opposite loads at its ends even though the individual outcomes fluctuate. The study supplies the controller, clock settings, initial medium and parity readout. All counts are synthetic.

## Exact minima on the exterior bridge medium

Write M for the bridges whose two ends disagree and d for the face distance between the two odd records. Blank native exchange and contrast give the measured local generator

\[
 h = (I-S)/2 + 8(n_a-n_b)^2 .
\]

A sharp disagreement has native energy 32.5. Swapping the two unequal ends changes both owner inventories, so the guard removes this off-diagonal transition. Every accepted basis state consequently has energy exactly 32.5 times the number of disagreements. The diagonal bound extends to every superposition in the guard.

With owner guards removed, the exterior seams are disjoint. On the two-dimensional space spanned by 02 and 20, each has eigenenergies 32 and 33, with lowest eigenvector proportional to 02 + 20. Matched 00 and 22 both have zero energy. After the sharp gateway-switch preparation, filter each mismatched contact on exchange-even. The filters act on disjoint ports, commute with the mismatch record and preserve the global number of double loads. Their product attains exactly 32 times the number of mismatches. All other ports remain available for the original global inventory. The sampled single-contact filter accepted 16,780,646 and refused 16,773,786 launches. Every route-level preparation refusal is also counted. On the longest shortest route, the accepted fraction is one in 2,048; 2,097,152 usable launches and 4,293,822,378 refusals are in the receipt.

In the face adjacency graph, the odd vertices of the edge set M are precisely the measured odd faces. When exactly f and g are odd, their component must contain both because each component has even total odd parity. It contains a path between them, so M has at least d edges. Any shortest path attains equality, and any triangular detour leaves the odd faces unchanged. This proves a ground energy over **every state in the specified sector**, including coherent states. The route used to launch the pair does not enter the lower bound.

When only f and g must be odd, a single bridge suffices if they are neighbors. At larger distances at least two bridges are needed because no bridge can touch both. Selecting one bridge incident to each attains the bound. This allows additional screened sources and breaks a long string into two local fragments. The exact ground values are therefore

| Public constraints | Fixed inventory on each owner | Owner guards removed, global load fixed |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Exactly two odd faces | 32.5 d | 32 d |
| Two specified odd faces, other readings free | 32.5 min(d, 2) | 32 min(d, 2) |

The energy per occupied link follows from an independently interrogated native contact. The fact that a path is forced follows from the **specified source observable and the condition on all other face records**. Neither observation establishes that physical colour charge obeys this condition.

## Measurements and a test for screening

The registered 20-, 80- and 320-cell boards respectively yield 12, 42 and 162 face meters. Every shell from the reference face was tested through 3, 5 and 11 steps. An independent exhaustive search visits all 2,048 reachable parity vectors and checks all 66 unordered source pairs on the smallest board. A separate test enumerates optional physical bridges using a minimum-cost update on parity masks. Ground potentials on the two larger boards follow from the graph proof and the measured saturating preparations.

| Preparation at 11 face steps | Inventory guard | Owner guards removed |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Isolated native bridge | 32.50288 | 32.00154 |
| Shortest route | 357.50321 | 352.00475 |
| Detour, same two endpoints | 389.99790 | 384.00079 |
| Two source readings held, bulk free | 64.99964 | 64.00213 |

The isolated native bridge calibrates each meter in reciprocal-clock units. Each bank uses 131,072 launches per quadrature and appointment. The simulated counts give a simultaneous phase-work half-width of 0.02680 at the final appointment, using Hoeffding bounds for 8,192 possible quadrature settings and a failure budget of one in a million across both files. The first appointment resolves the branch even for 480 mismatches. A factor of four between successive appointments preserves branch uniqueness within these bounds.

We also reopened one internal gateway-to-private-port contact on the shortest route, holding both source readings. During the exposure its exterior bridge remained coupled and the two physical owners retained their inventories. An interior disagreement can then vanish while two additional odd faces appear at its ends. In the native basis of the gateway, its private partner and its exterior neighbor, the accessible configurations have loads 200 and 020. The extracted native block in that order is

\[
 \begin{pmatrix}65&-1/2\\-1/2&32.5\end{pmatrix}.
\]

At the first maximum the extra-pair probability is 0.0009458501. The 320-cell run recorded 31,537 extra pairs in 33,554,432 launches. The two held sources remained odd in both outcomes. Shuttering the internal contact or removing exchange gives exactly zero. Removing contrast retains the same alphabet, bridge placement and exchange symmetry, and raises the peak to 0.8. Thus the all-even background condition, though conserved on the disjoint exterior bridge enrollment, can fail when an ordinary internal seam opens. Pair creation here is coherent and reversible. These runs do not measure a thermal relaxation rate or full-board equilibrium.

## All native contacts active, dilute source

A separate bound uses ordinary sharp states with two double loads on a vacant board and **all** blank native seams and private-reference services running. At fixed separation choose a port on each of two bridges incident to the held faces. For adjacent faces one mismatch suffices and the second double may be parked at a private port. Every port has six service slots. An unequal physical seam costs 32.5 in this sharp state and a loaded private-reference service costs 32. Thus the two doubles cost at most 12 × 32.5 = 390 at every separation. The initially chosen two separated private doubles cost 389 (325 on material contacts plus 64 on private references). Two adjacent private doubles are also even and cost 324 (260 on material contacts plus 64 on private references). Both references and the actual port pairs are retained in the receipt. Positivity of the full native generator gives

\[
 -324 \le E_{\text{two held odd readings}}-E_{\text{all even}} \le 390 .
\]

These are true variational bounds for the all-contact, fixed-two-double medium with the indicated hard readouts. The receipts store actual trial supports and their full-seam expectations at every measured separation. The argument extends to further boards with these fixed degree, enrollment and source properties. The bound excludes a positive asymptotic linear lower bound in this dilute family. It allows a finite linear window. At global half filling a separate all-contact proof bounds the two-pin excess work from above by 19,129 native units, uniformly in separation and without any per-owner guard. It checks every source pair on all three boards and applies to coherent many-body grounds. The exact all-contact energy and the extent of any linear window remain open.
