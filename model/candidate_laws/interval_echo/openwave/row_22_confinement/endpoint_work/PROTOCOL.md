# Relocating a material endpoint on an occupied federation

Enrol the 20-, 80- and 320-owner boards. Fix the first owner as a source marker. Take its lowest-index neighboring owner as the original endpoint. Choose the farthest owner with the same set of external ports and, separately, the farthest owner with a different set. The two arrangements are prepared on separate copies. An instantaneous nonlocal transport operation is outside the apparatus.

Prepare an extensive texture of sharp loads on all owners and of blank or empty-written memories. Use the frozen default random generator with seed 221037 plus the board level, uniform ternary loads, fair memory-write choices, and set the original endpoint owner uniformly to two and the two enrolled destination owners uniformly to zero. A separate acquisition uses each unmatched placement on all three boards. At every differing local port label put load 2 on the gateway side and load 0 on the other side before transposition. Keep all other loads and all collars as in the first acquisition. The native private-reference work must then be positive, equal to 32 per differing label, and vanish at zero contrast.
