That complement isolates the source dimer before its first write, so the specified donor-routing method cannot reach it. This costs one extra unit precisely on $a=1$. Add one 33 pin at the source complement and one 32 pin at the primary to give a second unit and a scalar. Other triangle legs and their two cuts are unchanged. Test the first admissible routing of this design. All spectral, timing, control and count settings remain as registered.
