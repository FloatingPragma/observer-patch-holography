After full conversion, opposite incoming order phases gave exactly the same observations on the charged daughter at both calibrated stations. Rejoining the daughter and the unmeasured donor recovered the phase. At owner 17 the final two-port neutral order 20 was observed on **262,144 of 262,144** starts for one phase and **zero of 262,144** for the other. A sharp donor read before reversing the conversion reduced these counts to 131,467 and 130,648, compatible with equal probabilities. ## What the observer does

The downstream receivers in owners 17 and 8 have calibrated blank entrances (207,206) and (106,96), respectively. This experiment prepares its two neutral orders on those entrances from independently stocked loads. A blank half exchange applied to 02 or 20 prepares the two orthogonal phases. On fresh copies with the half exchange omitted, the sharp stocks calibrate the entrance orders. The input hatches open on unit stock before the preparations. The run needs no measurement of which order was prepared to trigger the subsequent gate. A neutral wave arriving from an upstream source would need this same access while preserving its own coherence. Each local ladder first operates with its guard at two. A calibrated blank exchange path swaps that guard with the parked zero on the spare flag rail. The ladder then operates with guard zero. The apparatus returns every intermediate relay, including rails traversed during a coherent gate, because the full native blank pulse acts as an exact ternary swap. On each sharp stock the two ladder rails carry the signed daughter and the third rail supplies its compensating donor. A signed terminal read uses the existing zero standard. The two observers can also reverse the gates with the donor still coherent, return the two loads to the blank entrance, and apply a final physical half exchange. The reversed sequence replaces each primitive dwell by its complement in the exact period of the contact. The stationary collar energies and blank-contact eigenvalues are integers at strengths eight and four, so a positive wait of 2π implements their identity. Direct multiplication recovers all 27 columns of the forward gate. [Board routes and inverse pulses](apparatus.py)

On the native full pulse, the inputs (02, donor 1) and (20, donor 1) give (01, donor 2) and (21, donor 0), respectively, both with amplitude i in the blank-stock convention. The negative and positive daughters therefore leave orthogonal donor words. ## The finite access criterion

Let A(x,e) and B(y,e) be the amplitudes on daughter and remaining material for two branches of one fixed total charge. If each branch gives a distinct sharp daughter charge, charge conservation places their amplitudes on disjoint compensating charges. For every x and y, summing A(x,e) times the conjugate of B(y,e) over the same unreported e gives zero. Every effect on the daughter alone contracts precisely this reduced interference matrix. The two balanced phase preparations therefore have identical daughter marginals. The Lean file proves the disjoint-support statement, the zero cross term and contraction of signed-tag distinguishability under every stochastic read or downstream decision based only on that tag. The included refusal outcome preserves normalization. [Finite proofs](ChargedTags.lean)

For the enrolled pulse angles, write α=kθ/8 where θ is the programmed angle. The two working columns of the complete dual-guard matrix on these inputs are the corresponding neutral word times exp(−iα/2) cos(α/2) and its converted word times −exp(−iα/2) sin(α/2). Distinct converted charges leave distinguishable donor records, while both unconverted neutral orders share donor 1. The trace distance of the reduced daughters for the two phase inputs is cos²(α/2). A blank terminal half exchange on the two daughter rails measures this entire difference. At native angles 0, π/2 and π it gives 1, 1/2 and 0. At half contrast and the original full duration it gives 1/2. This surviving partial-pulse signal occupies the unconverted neutral branch.

A separate internal-tag control starts with a full native converted daughter of either fixed sign. A blank exchange puts its signed load on either entrance rail with the same compensating donor. A half exchange prepares two orthogonal phase settings; a second half exchange then distinguishes them by the occupied rail on every simulated start. The charge read is identical for the two settings, and the internal rail records differ. Consequently the bound on reading a signed tag does not constrain an independent internal label at fixed charge. This establishes which phase information the addressed converter transfers. A physical weak-flavour tag still needs a separately identified charged species at a production or detection vertex. [Executable controls](test_tags.py)
