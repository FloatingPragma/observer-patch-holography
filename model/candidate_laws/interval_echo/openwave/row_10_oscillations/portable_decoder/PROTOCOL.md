# Can two observers reuse a single winding decoder?

Train only on the deposited owner 12, wait 256 training cohorts of the joint three-recipe experiment. The two reference phases are equally likely. For each phase and each of the three sharp exits following a distant pair, choose the recipe with the largest conditional training frequency for that exit. Break ties toward the lowest recipe index. Freeze those six assignments for every receiver, time and control. The earlier six-appointment experiment found small conditional discrimination with separately trained receivers. Its results motivated testing transfer. The new appointments below have not been decoded or computed for this test.

At owners 12 and 17 enroll waits 384 and 768 in exchange units, each for zero winding and both opposite windings, retaining all seven original-start bins of the joint apparatus. Keep all local contacts, stocks and output conventions. Simulate independent multinomial banks with the original exposure of 2^52 per setting and seed 26112673. Repeat with a sharp first source-direction read whose outcome is forgotten, and with contrast four on free neutral flight alone. Also retain a panel-blind reference on a received-pair population with equal prior probability of the three recipes (chance 1/3). No controls will be used to retrain the decoder.

Report conditional correctness for each recipe and panel, equally weighted three-way accuracy, and all-start pair efficiency. Give 99% simultaneous exact binomial limits with the existing 4,096-comparison allowance; report a missing rate if a cohort yields no enrolled pairs. Ask separately (1) whether the fixed label map beats 1/3 in each native receiving cohort and (2) whether its balanced accuracy for the two winding signs alone, counting a guess of zero as incorrect, exceeds 1/2. A high score on the first need not identify both signs. As a second, predeclared two-sign decoder, use the identical training and exit definitions restricted to the two sign stocks; compare its held sign discrimination with a sharp first direction read. Record the expected gain per trillion original starts above uniform guessing when pairs alone are decoded and every absence is assigned a uniformly random label, with arithmetic that retains the low pair yield. Use independent cohorts for each flight setting and control. The origin and the distant observer must exchange classical records to form a coincidence.
