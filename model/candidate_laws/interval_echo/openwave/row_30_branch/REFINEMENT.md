# The connected 56-cell geometry

The fixture scan found 56 (2,2,2) cells in one connected component on the 80-cell sphere. The 20-cell results, their variation by geometry and this connectivity were known before this spectral test. Include the complete component. Report the sizes of every geometry class and its largest connected component on all three fixtures. No 320-cell frequency claim is made.

Use the κ = 8 full 460,320-state binary two-load Hamiltonian. For a bounded-memory eigenvalue calculation solve on all configurations of relative graph distance at most six and seven in succession. This is a numerical truncation only. Measure each Ritz vector's residual against the *full* native operator, including its omitted rows. Compare the two bandwidths, first and last centroid shifts, and linewidth gates. Declare a numerical ambiguity if the largest full residual is above 1% of the first nonzero frequency shift or the two radii give inconsistent verdicts. Keep the actual numbers. Every omitted configuration has zero attraction and the omitted kinetic principal block is positive semidefinite.

Use the 80 lowest numerical modes. Split them at their largest internal energy gap. Assign the (2,2,2) class to the cluster containing the larger total squared projection of its 56 normalized internal-seam orbitals. Report both weights, the gap to the 81st state, the cluster sizes and all class widths. As a completeness check, count eigenvalues below the midpoint between the 80th and 81st Ritz values and between the two internal groups. If e is one such midpoint, A is the retained block, D is the omitted block and B their coupling, the Schur correction obeys

    0 ≤ Bᵀ (D − e I)⁻¹ B ≤ ||B||₁ ||B||∞ / |e| · P_boundary .

Thus the eigenvalue counts of A and of A minus this boundary penalty bracket the count for the full H at e. Apply this check at radius seven. Report any differing counts. Eigenvalue indexing and residual arithmetic here use floating point.

Repeat the induced and harmonic-extension squared-wave-number conventions of the class-specific study. The other class's uniform orbital supplies a rest centroid after projection into its own cluster; use half the separation from the tested class's zero-momentum centroid as the candidate gap. The upper energy cluster has positive-frequency increments with increasing energy and the lower one with decreasing energy in this two-band interpretation. Freeze speed at the first nonzero momentum and measure all held errors. Also test positive centroid shifts without that two-branch interpretation. Keep the registered criteria of 80% absolute weight, at least one held nonzero wave number, RMS linewidth at each momentum below 10% of the first shift, less than 5% relative frequency-shift error and separation from the tangent parabola on the held domain. Group degenerate geometry modes; retain all distinct spatial classes. No eigenmode launch, frequency-sensitive detector or physical mass clock is supplied by this calculation.

The radius-seven residual exceeded the resolution gate for both first shifts despite numerical agreement of the centroids at the two radii. A separate precision follow-up extends the same computation to relative distance ten. The radius-six and radius-seven receipts, all spatial classes, the first-anchor rule and the 1% gate stay in the comparison. The reduced counts and the unchanged full-operator residual are reported for the precision run.
