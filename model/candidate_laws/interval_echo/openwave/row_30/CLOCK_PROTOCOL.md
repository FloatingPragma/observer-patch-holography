# Held time readout triggered by a stationary patch

Use only the 320-cell installed guide at δ=0.2. At the first guide B station with a neighbouring off-guide clock and three available connected off-guide bank cells, attach the standard Interval Echo stationary source, material hand and count registers. Leave the clock's source, routing and storage cells undriven by the waveguide. At the end of each complete two-matching period the clock takes one native repair opportunity. Protect the wave while its pointer and hand are serviced. The first appearance of a chosen positive tick count opens the B-rail detector on that patch. Both the zero-winding source and the two signed harmonics closest to 0.23 are held preparations. Use real-space propagation for periods 0 through 192 and clock thresholds 0 through 32. Take 2²⁵ independent preparations at each threshold (seed 306141). Retain the local B counts and quiet.

Execute at least one complete gate-level timing trajectory on the actual connected patch. For the ensemble fold the independently derived fair positive-repair law into the native B-detector probabilities. Bound and report the probability that the 32nd tick falls beyond the 192-period window. Fit the frequency to each count history under this waiting-time kernel using a free oscillation amplitude. The fit receives no momentum, applied detuning or KG relation. Report the undamped-period frequency, its conversion to the ensemble mean home-tick scale, and the held KG residual with the gap measured at zero winding. Use the held spatial count estimate from the primary experiment. A fourfold high-momentum window or a single noisy clock history is outside this follow-up. Besides the physical trigger, a frozen clock and frozen tagged vacancy are supplied control facilities.
