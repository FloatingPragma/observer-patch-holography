# A signed daughter from the arriving neutral pair

At either of two downstream receivers, a driven material contact can extract one constituent of an arriving vacancy–excess pair and leave a signed daughter. The same trial retains the order of the incoming pair, the two outgoing port loads and the balancing donor change. An independently stocked guard selects which orientation converts. The input to the ladder consists of the two arriving material loads themselves.

The simulation continues the held two-port count banks of the five-owner release experiment. The two original launches, three winding preparations, two flight configurations and two receiver choices give 24 banks of 2⁴⁶ starts. They use the release experiment's fixed observation times, 2,048 for the intact side heads and 256 for the retired heads. Instrument calibrations are evaluated before the program opens the archived count file. ## How a receiver works

1. The observer addresses the blank seam across ports (207,206) in owner 17 or (106,96) in owner 8. Echo writes the signed difference in the seam head. For either extreme order, this leaves both endpoints at unit filling. The other contrasts stop the protocol. The ordinary equal pair (1,1) has the same net charge as a vacancy–excess pair and gives zero contrast. The full 99-state echo, its inverse and independent balanced standards establish which port originally held the vacancy.
2. With the flight stopped and the extreme pair held in that head, zero-contrast shutters open on neighbouring unit loads. Two magnitude-two collars, a guard, donor, flag and signed-load meter can be stocked on the actual federation. The code verifies every route against the rim, prior guide, private reference, parked loads and new collars. It checks every intermediate transposition restores the other ports. A separate off-guide bank supplies six initially isolated balanced stock pairs. The guard, input sign and pulse are separately set. Each alternative receiver uses its own reset apparatus.
3. The observer's reading of the orthogonal signed head controls a physical swap or hold on two flag rails. Their complementary (2,0) or (0,2) record persists through the rest of this trial. This read and classical control, together with the addressed contacts and frozen stocks, are supplied detector operations. Undoing the echo restores the original two loads. The receiver swaps those loads directly through two adjacent hatches onto its local ladder. Owners 17 and 8 use (hub, target, guard, donor) addresses (208,215,209,214) and (98,107,100,101), respectively. Owner 17 draws supplies through the blank 206–106 crossing after the flight stops. No fresh charged pair is substituted at the workstation. [Board geometry, stock and native echo checks](apparatus.py)
4. The gate opens the target–donor contact for π/2, temporarily echoes the hub with its guard, and dwells for π/64 on each stored collar at registered coupling 8. It undoes the temporary echo and holds the hub–target collar for 3π/64 before closing the target–donor contact for 3π/2. These times are in the registered contact units. The pulse is the established full-setting inverse **on the accepted sharp inputs, up to phase**. The calculation evaluates its complete local columns. The meter independently reads the two ladder rails, donor and two flag rails against zero and restores every relay. Swapping the two ladder loads back leaves the daughter on the actual receiving ports. ## What the records distinguish

Of 1,688,849,860,263,936 original free-panel starts, 161,881,790 delivered an extreme pair to the selected ports. With guard 2, all 80,942,914 arrivals in order (0,2) leave (0,1) and change the donor from 1 to 2. The 80,938,876 arrivals in the opposite order remain neutral. With guard 0, exactly that opposite order leaves (2,1) and changes the donor from 1 to 0. No converted word has a mismatched flag or donor. The two guards are alternative continuations of the same input distribution. Within each continuation these are joint records of a single event following its projective arrival read. The pulse columns on those occupied inputs have deterministic outputs, so their conditional counts follow directly from the original sampled counts. [Joint counts, complete missed categories and calibration](results.json)

The pulse-off and saturated-donor controls leave every accepted arrival neutral. Closing the two launch-side gates gives zero downstream pair triggers. Halving the contrast strength for the inverse ladder with the register acquisition times held fixed gives conversion probability 1/2 on its selected standard. The absolute probabilities of conversion are specific to this prepared, triggered contact. These records resolve the order of a material dipole and its conditional, charge-compensated response. Electric neutrality alone cannot distinguish it from an empty patch. An autonomous weak interaction, a light neutral elementary state, flavour, helicity and a decay source would require additional measurements. This experiment supplies a scoped material-current result for the neutral-particle catalogue.
