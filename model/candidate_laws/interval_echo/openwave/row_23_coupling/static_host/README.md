# Can the ordinary host polarize between two stationary charges?

A native excess and vacancy interact across a shared seam. When the same material standards are held three links apart on the ordinary unit-filled host, the measured connected energy is zero. This finite-range result extends exactly to every pair of nonadjacent held sources on the blank federation, even with every bulk contact open. The obstruction concerns this specific preparation. A previously prepared chain of mobile dipoles transmits a boundary energy, and other preparations remain candidates for OW-23. ## Why the ordinary host gives a contact interaction

Let P hold the two material standards at their respective loads. A blank native seam between loads m and n contributes a diagonal energy

$$e(m,n)=\tfrac12\mathbf{1}_{m\ne n}+\kappa(m-n)^2$$

and its only off-diagonal operation exchanges unequal endpoint loads. The prepared word has a unit on every unguarded port. An exchange between two such ports leaves the word fixed. Every exchange that could change it would change a pinned load and is eliminated by P. All degree-reference energies act on single sites. Thus the supplied word is an exact eigenstate of the guarded full-board generator. The same argument applies to every individual blank-seam hold in any order. A varying diagonal source potential can change only the phase of this word. In particular every bulk charge and every material charge fluctuation about the unit host vanishes at every exposure. This extends the blank-law multiset conservation to an operational obstruction for static polarization measurements on this preparation. [Exact blank-law identities](../../../../contrast_memory_spherical_v1/law.py) · [Direct full-board test](test_static.py)

Every surviving energy is a sum of edge terms and one-port costs. If the two guards are nonadjacent, each term depends on at most one of the two signs. It cancels in U even when the sources share a neighbor. If they are adjacent, only their common edge survives. The connected energy is −2κ−1 for either like-sign pair and 2κ−½ for either opposing pair. Its signed and even projections at κ = 8 are exactly −16.25 and −0.75. These predictions concern the initially sharp host and ideal fixed-source sectors. An occupied medium, written memories, or freely released source ports can explore larger sectors. The argument does not identify the unit-filled preparation with the physical vacuum.

## How the same clock detects a mediated response

The comparison couples 11 to the symmetric superposition of 20 and 02 with matrix element −8 in exchange units. Apply the same change on every active seam. It preserves total load, port-interchange symmetry, the alphabet and routing. The outside guards remove pair conversion and exchange at either endpoint. On the middle seam both processes remain available. On the ordered interior words (11,20,02) the acquired generator retains the full native middle-seam block plus the two diagonal edge energies e(left,n₁) and e(n₂,right). The four source ports have zero degree-reference correction. Consequently opposite boundary sources shift the energies of the two dipoles in opposite directions. A nonzero connected energy and a reversal of the observed polarization are possible. The control measures both. Shuttering the middle seam removes them. The supplied clock starts in a uniform superposition of 4096 positions. Its branches open the same static Hamiltonian for u times 2π/128 exchange-clock units. The inverse Fourier comparator produces an energy bin of width 1/32. A terminal native occupation measurement resolves the central words. The acquisition computes the coherent return for every hand and every outcome. The most frequent energy bin is selected by the observer from the marginal counts. The unwrapped spectrum lies in [−16,112) exchange units for every enrolled block.

For the comparison write the interior Hamiltonian as a scalar a on 11, a block B on the two dipoles, and an off-diagonal vector v joining 11 to B. If B−aI ≥ dI with d positive, the variational bound gives the lowest energy at or below a. The eigenvector equations then bound its squared overlap with 11 below by 1/(1+‖v‖²/d²). The smallest certified lower bound across the nine settings is 0.77854. For native and cut flight the prepared word itself is stationary. The nearest clock bin captures at least 4/π² of a single line. At circular distance two or more bins a single bin gets at most 1/16, by the finite geometric sum for the clock. Even if every other line falls on one distant bin, it cannot overtake the nearest bin within the simultaneous counting bounds. The mode is therefore within two spacings of the populated static line, giving ±0.0625 on the signed interaction. The mean of four neutral-subtracted energies has the conservative error ±0.25. Its conversion-control value is unresolved.

The counting allowance uses Hoeffding bounds over the complete marginal clock and material frequencies. Numerical allowances cover rounding the three spectral energies to a fixed grid of 2⁻³⁰, their word-projector entries to 2⁻³⁶ and the terminal joint probabilities to multiples of 2⁻³⁶. The largest controlled hold is below 202, so the spectral rounding changes amplitudes by less than 10⁻⁷. The portable calculation reproduced every integer from the unrounded initial acquisition.
