    K = [e(2,2) − e(2,0) − e(0,2) + e(0,0)]/4 = −(2κ+1/4).

## A distance-dependent fluctuation with zero mean interaction

Consider two nonadjacent guards and define D_s by taking half the difference between its positive and negative energy with the other local inputs held fixed. For a neighbouring active load n, [e(2,n)−e(0,n)]/2 = g(1−n), where g = 2κ+1/4. Its constant private offset drops out of a covariance. The four-sign combination of full energy variances is

    (1/4)Σₐᵦ ab Var(H_ab) = 2 Cov(D₁,D₂).

Indeed the mixed term of the **noncommuting** operator square is D₁D₂+D₂D₁. All pieces containing the bulk Hamiltonian cancel. The boundary operators commute, so subtracting the squared means gives the stated classical covariance of their jointly readable values, even though internal bulk exchange was fully active. The same algebra applies to any common mixed input.
