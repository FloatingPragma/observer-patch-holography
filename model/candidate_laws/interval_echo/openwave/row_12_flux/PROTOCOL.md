## One complete flux cycle

A full flux quantum gives a distinct test of which parts of the response survive changes in the wall shape. Track the upper wall band as an external flux increases from zero to 2π. Bound the gap everywhere using a 64-point grid and the maximum absolute row sum of the flux derivative in the microscopic port basis. Obtain a lower bound for the wall weight of any upper eigenstate from its single sharp-wall state in each momentum sector and the norm of its coupling to the complement, using the same grid and a Lipschitz bound on the two energies and the coupling. Release the sharp wall and a same-load, contiguous nonwinding preparation independently at momenta one and three. In each case ramp the external twists through 128 midpoint steps over 16 exchange units. Apply the inverse final port rephasing and measure the momentum and sharp-wall flag on every trial. Compare the intact, same-total compact and zero-contrast preparations with the momentum permutation fixed by integer load alone. All arms use the same 262,144 launches and seed 120439. A zero-wall result from a compact preparation can challenge the wall interpretation of a flux-cycle label while leaving the exact momentum identity intact.
