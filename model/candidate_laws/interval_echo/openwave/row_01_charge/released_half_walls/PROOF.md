# Native reduction and the two charge readings

## Source and closed-stock preparation

Each work ring follows an induced five- or six-owner cycle. Short chordless paths inside its owners close it. Every site has two active blank neighbours. All its remaining geometrical contacts and its private missing-contact references see zero outside. Exterior exchanges across the ring boundary are parked. For the binary material, write $n_j=A s_j$, with $s_j$ zero or one and ordinary load $A=1$ or $2$. On unequal neighbours the blank 99-dimensional term has diagonal $1/2+\kappa A^2$ and swaps them with amplitude $-1/2$. Equal neighbours give zero. The four other contrast slots contribute $4\kappa A^2\sum s_j$, constant throughout the sector. Removing this scalar leaves the matrix in the numerical acquisition. All ring words in the conserved sector are included. The two native ternary digit tests resolve 0, 1 and 2 with two comparisons against sharp references and have restored routing on both receiving stations. Distinct number readings therefore give orthogonal outcome effects. The complete commuting word measurement is obtained by taking their product. An optional closed-stock implementation routes it between a sharp reservoir and the insertion site by a transposition that restores intermediate loads. Its product with the ring state acquires the same exterior scalar in every ring word. The primary release uses the zero exterior, and the closed realization has the identical ring restriction. ## Two changes of alternating order

The alternating pattern measured at an occupied site is $(-1)^j(2s_j-1)$. It reverses across a bond exactly when the occupancies agree. Define the measured signed bond reading $b_j=s_j+s_{j+1}-1$. Thus a 00 bond reads $-1$, opposite occupancies read zero and an occupied pair reads $+1$. On a periodic ring of $N$ sites, $\sum b_j=2\sum s_j-N$. Adding or removing one occupied site fixes this sum to $+2$ or $-2$ on every term in the evolving superposition. A single sharp insertion into the alternating reference creates exactly the two adjacent enrolled walls. In a region, a wall is a reversal of the two-valued spatial pattern. The ends of the reversed region have opposite orientation. [Exact ring sum](WallCharge.lean)

For a positive addition let $m$ count the number of 00 bonds. There must be $m+2$ occupied-equal bonds. A negative removal interchanges the signs. The sharp diagonal energy is $C-2cm$ for $c=1/2+\kappa A^2$. The sharp initial word has $m=0$ and zero off-diagonal expectation. Its total energy stays $C$. On any word at most $N$ exchanges are possible, each of magnitude $1/2$, so the absolute off-diagonal expectation is bounded by $N/2$. Hence $2c\,\mathbb E[m]\le N/2$ at every time. The probability of more than two walls is at most $\mathbb E[m]$, which gives the uniform all-time retention lower bound $1-N/(4c)$. This high-energy band is selected by the ordered preparation. The proof includes all additional wall pairs and makes no infinite-ring stability assumption. [Variational and probability step](WallCharge.lean)

## Independent electrodes and charge profiles

On a sharp port with any of its three allowed loads $n$, the two native contrast references give $n^2$ and $(n-2)^2$. Their difference is $4(n-1)$. Two nonnegative holds, reduced modulo the contrast period, therefore give $\exp(i\theta(n-1))$ up to a common phase. This supplies an independently calibrated linear potential, including on double loads. The two-word apparatus prepares one exchange superposition and uses the native half exchange followed by a sharp load read. An additional quadrature plate gives the orthogonal phase component. Its setting uses the local trit contrast of the two source words. On the held sources one wall moves by two adjacent bond spacings. The ordinary material moves by one site spacing in the same direction for an occupied-equal wall and the opposite direction for a vacant-equal wall. The measured voltage phases therefore distinguish response per wall step $+A/2$ or $-A/2$ relative to the separately prepared standard. This is a response to an applied potential along the ring. For an arc of consecutive bonds, the triangular electrode measures $A\sum_{j\,\mathrm{in\ arc}} b_j/2$. Each interior site occurs twice and each boundary site once. Both alternating references have identical weighted baseline. When precisely one sign-$\sigma$ wall lies in the arc, this charge is $\sigma A/2$ on that very sharp word. It is a joint single-copy polarization reading, so a mixed source with only a half marginal mean is distinguishable. Summing this bond read over the entire ring yields exactly the conserved inserted load. A flat electrode on the same sites couples to their integer total against an integer reference and always gives phase $+1$ at $2\pi$. The distinct full-turn outcomes are a physical consequence of the edge weighting. The exact transport identity and this voltage comparison leave both readings independently testable on the same released preparation. [Independent profile checks](test_half_walls.py)
