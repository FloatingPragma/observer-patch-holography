Given a parked sharp word, each sensed source load couples to rail $a$ through the physical term $\kappa(n_a-n_j)^2$. Rail $b$ has no ring contact. The equal total number of physical and private contrast slots on the two rails cancels their probe-only energy difference. For a defect of signed load $s=\pm1$ on $a$ relative to $b$, the resulting diagonal difference is $-2\kappa s(n_j+n_{j+1})$. The active probe exchange is $-1/2$. Ring and exterior terms common to the two branches contribute only a scalar to their evolution. The two enrolled mounts have no mutual edge. Thus changing a distant part of the parked word while keeping the four contacted site loads fixed preserves the complete conditional probe dynamics for any dwell and any subsequent read confined to the probes. A simultaneous full-ring census can distinguish the altered word. When the ring exchanges remain open a distant alteration can propagate; the parked-word equivalence is a different experiment.
