## A finite bank at every refinement

At level $j$ use $N_j=2^{48}(j+1)^2$ independent preparations per bank. There are $B=35$ banks for the seven cuts on blank, ordinary, held, half-defect and blind-pulse preparations. Give the level failure budget $\alpha_j=0.01/[(j+1)(j+2)]$. Hoeffding's inequality and the union bound give
\[
 \varepsilon_j=\sqrt{\frac{\log(2B/\alpha_j)}{2N_j}}+2^{-b_j},\qquad
 b_j=40+\lceil\log_2(j+1)\rceil .
\]
The last term covers the dyadic probability grid used by the synthetic emitter. At least 99% of repetitions of the entire countable protocol satisfy the frequency bounds **simultaneously at every level** because $\sum_j\alpha_j=0.01$. No independence across the seven shapes is required for this simultaneous bound. Independence of the copies within each terminal bank is required. Summability also gives almost-sure eventual coverage and convergence of the normalized sampling error. It does not guarantee coverage of every finite prefix in every run.

For a two-stock entropy difference, including held minus blank and held minus ordinary, the error divided by $\ell_j^4$ is at most
\[
 u_j=2(4096)\sqrt2\log(3)\,\varepsilon_j
     =O\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{\log(j+2)}}{j+1}\right)\longrightarrow0 .
\]
Every level therefore uses finitely many operations while the additional uncertainty in the entropy itself is $o(\ell_j^4)$. Exact analytic probabilities are used in the proof of the pulse response, and are absent from the count-to-entropy estimator. Arbitrarily fine implementations can outward-round the inverses on successively finer grids: an additional amplified-phase error bounded by $2^{-b_j}$ adds at most $4096\,\log(3)2^{-b_j}$ to the normalized two-stock uncertainty. A fixed gate or timing bias has to be controlled in accumulated phase to $o(1)$ for this coherent limit.

The direct alternative counts ordinary one-exposure endpoint outcomes with $N_jm_j^2$ independent copies per bank. Its frequencies obey the same Hoeffding bound divided by $m_j$ with the same allocated failures. A digital emitter can use $b_j+\log_2m_j$ probability bits. Clipping to the known physical probability range and using $|h'|\le\log3$ proves the same $o(\ell_j^4)$ entropy-error condition even without coherent repetitions. At $1/512$ that schedule needs about $7.13\times10^{29}$ direct copies per bank. The coherent schedule there uses $2.5333\times10^{15}$ copies and $16,777,216$ stock exposures per copy. The archive samples their model-binomial sufficient statistics. A hardware experiment at those resources is a distinct undertaking.
