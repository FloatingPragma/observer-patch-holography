# Energy current on the full centered source

The same centered Weyl/contact clock has an exact energy-dipole identity
on its full Fock space. It identifies the interaction term that must be
controlled before the dipole can implement a geometric boost. It does
not assume the spin projection, a free vacuum, or a particle label.

Write `K=T+sum_x V_x`, where

    V_x=U[n_xup n_xdown-(n_xup+n_xdown)/2],
    T=dGamma(h)=sum_edges [a_x* t_xy a_y+h.c.].

The original Weyl links or the unitarily matched signed-Hubbard links may
be used. The state, probes and instruments must be transported together
when comparing those charts. For any real site coordinate X_x, distribute
each bond energy equally over its endpoints and define

    B_X=sum_x X_x V_x
          +sum_edges (X_x+X_y) T_xy/2.

Let `b_xs=2(n_x,opposite-1/2)a_xs` be the actual composite canonical
operator of `COMPOSITE_CAR.md`. Exact source commutators give

    i[K,B_X]=dGamma(i[h^2,X]/2)+J_X,
    J_X=(iU/4) sum_edges (X_y-X_x)
          sum_st {t_xy(st)[b_xs* a_yt+a_xs* b_yt]-h.c.}.       (1)

Here `h.c.` conjugates the whole preceding scalar-weighted bracket.
Equation (1) acts on all occupation sectors. Its two terms are Hermitian
and preserve total occupation number. Additive constants in V_x have no
effect on (1).

For the quadratic part, the canonical anticommutation relations give
`[dGamma(A),dGamma(B)]=dGamma([A,B])`. Thus
`[h,(Xh+hX)/2]=[h^2,X]/2`. The onsite energies commute. For an edge, the
remaining mixed commutators combine to

    (X_y-X_x)[V_x-V_y,T_xy]/2.

Finally `[V_x,a_xs*]=(U/2)b_xs*` and
`[V_x,a_xs]=-(U/2)b_xs`, which proves (1), including its coefficient and
orientation, for arbitrary finite graphs and link matrices. The composite
transforms as the original spinor under onsite spin rotations because its
onsite double-occupation phase commutes with those rotations.

`boost_current.py` reconstructs the literal source along each of the three
Weyl axes, at c=1/8 and U=1, on three sites and all64 Fock states. It verifies
(1) exactly, including complex links, Hermiticity and occupation
conservation. For integer site coordinates each interaction/centering
remainder has `Tr(J_X*J_X)=1/16`. At U=0 it vanishes. The one-particle
restriction agrees with `i[(h-U/2)^2,X]/2`; this separate check shows why
part of the remainder is the chosen energy reference. Its nonzero value
alone is not evidence of non-Gaussian dynamics.

## Relation to the actual spatial translation

For the infinite cubic Weyl source with physical spacing a and c=v/a,
the kinetic symbol satisfies

    h(k)^2=(v^2/a^2) sum_j sin^2(a k_j) I.

In a Fourier convention where position is `i partial_k`, the jth free
current divided by v^2 is therefore

    P_j^(a)(k)=sin(2a k_j)/(2a).

The physical translations by two lattice spacings commute; their selected
low-momentum branch has generator k_j. On a one-body window `|k_j|<=P`,
Taylor's remainder gives

    |P_j^(a)(k)-k_j| <= (2/3) a^2 P^3.               (2)

On its q-particle exterior power, the second-quantized difference is at
most `(2/3)q a^2 P^3`. This is a bound on that specified kinetic window,
not on arbitrary interacting histories or the unbounded full Fock space.
The finite open-box checks of (1) do not supply momentum eigenstates;
(2) is the separate infinite-bulk symbol calculation.

For a boost generated by B_X/v^2, the interacting term J_X/v^2 in (1)
must have the correct limiting action on the same observable histories,
and those histories must retain controlled spatial momentum. A bound on
its expectation alone would not suffice. Ground-state invariance,
transformation of the full observable algebra and the common causal cone
also require proof. The celestial null-ray parametrization and finite
null-preservation theorem accept these operational inputs; they do not
construct them. Equation (1) supplies the actual source remainder to test.
