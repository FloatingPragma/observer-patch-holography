## A volume-independent band with original probability

Let the probe site have d neighbours and let the graph have maximum degree D.
For eta=n_opposite-1/2, the actual local derivation is

    B=[K,a*]=sum_j t_ji a_j*+U a* eta,
    {B*,B}=(d c^2/4+U^2/4)1 = S 1.                    (1)

Here eta commutes with a and with the neighbour fields, eta^2=1/4, and
every cross anticommutator vanishes. Equation (1) is an operator identity
on the full Fock space. Particle-hole invariance, combined with hidden-spin
invariance if its convention exchanges flavours, equates the two measures.
Consequently each original measure has second moment S/2. No isolated pole
or gap is required.

The local estimates ||[K,a*]||<=(U+cD)/2 and
||[K,n_opposite]||<=cD imply

    ||[K,[K,a*]]|| <= (U+cD)^2/4+UcD
                       <= (U+cD)^2 = M.                 (2)

For the hopping term in the second derivation, use the maximum degree of
the neighbouring sites, rather than only the degree of the probe site.
Each original fourth moment is at most M^2. The estimates depend only on
finite neighbourhoods and therefore pass to the ground-state limit; the
positive ground generator realizes the same derivations on the cyclic
local vectors.

For either normalized measure 2mu, the random variable X=E^2 has mean S
and second moment at most 2M^2. Cauchy--Schwarz, or the elementary
Paley--Zygmund inequality, gives original weight at least S^2/(16M^2)
on X>=S/2. The original second-moment bound gives weight at most
S^2/(32M^2) on X>16M^2/S. Subtracting yields

    mu({E: S/2<=E^2<=16M^2/S}) >= S^2/(32M^2)>0.        (3)

This is an original subprobability bound, with the preparation failure
weight retained. For c=1/8, U=1 and d=D=6 it reads

    mu({E: 35/256<=E^2<=2744/5}) >= 25/100352.

The interval is deliberately broad. It neither rules out weight below
the interval nor supplies a particle pole, a mass gap or Lorentz dispersion.
It proves that both odd charge responses retain finite positive source
energy and original weight at fixed couplings as volume grows. The complete
local history bound controls finite-time local CAR response scores without
sending U to infinity. Bounded spectral approximants also need their
resolution and time-tail errors; a sharp infinite-volume energy projector
is not itself a local intervention. The attempt schedule and physical
duration are declared inputs.

## Exact source-generated composite response on a dimer

For the actual two-site source, let h be the four-mode hopping matrix with
links c/2, alpha=U/2>0, and Delta=sqrt(U^2+4c^2). The scalar-shifted source
H=K+U has ground energy (U-Delta)/2. The positive clock is H-E0.
The doubled retarded space has signed generator
mathcal L=L direct-sum (-conjugate(L)). Its source CAR isometry has columns

    V_i=(a_i* Omega) direct-sum conjugate(a_i Omega).

Thus V*V=1, while its signed moments are computed directly by

    (M_k)_ij=<a_i L^k a_j*>+(-1)^k<a_j* L^k a_i>.

Exact full-source arithmetic gives M1=h, M2=h^2+alpha^2,
M3=h^3-alpha^2 h. The residual columns
V1=(mathcal L V-Vh)/alpha are an isometry orthogonal to V. The next
compression and residual satisfy

    V1* mathcal L V1=-3h,   residual*residual=0,
    mathcal L|response = [[h,alpha 1],[alpha 1,-3h]].     (4)

This finite composite mode is generated by the original interacting source.
It is not an independently inserted field. The vanishing residual is checked
from M4, so (4) gives the whole cyclic response, rather than only two moments.
The Dirac block with lower diagonal -h disagrees with the actual third moment
by -2alpha^2 h. The presence of a nonzero off-diagonal interaction alone
therefore does not establish a Dirac mass.

The two positive source energies and original probabilities, for each mode
and each charge direction separately, are

    E_low=(Delta-c)/2,   w_low=1/4+c/(2Delta),
    E_high=(Delta+c)/2,  w_high=1/4-c/(2Delta).           (5)

At c=1/8,U=1 these energies are sqrt(17)/8 minus or plus 1/16.
Each pair of weights sums to 1/2. The executable reconstructs the full
16-state Hamiltonian and all six distinct source energy projectors. A
uniform choice of four local modes, followed by addition/removal and energy
readout, has effects a_i Pi_E a_i*/4 and a_i* Pi_E a_i/4. All 48 records,
including zero probabilities on the ground, are retained; their effects
sum to the identity on every source input. The ideal odd transfer instruments
require the declared fermionic reference or reservoir service. Completeness

Equations (1)--(5) concern source charge and source energy. No Standard Model
