# Finite records, observer meanings and shared descriptions

The executable produces finite apparatus events. Observers store those
events, assign meanings using declared dictionaries and compare the results.
A compatible field description summarizes their joint records on a stated
experimental domain. It is not an additional objective continuum universe
generated by the program. This construction calculates the complete record
law, its copying and comparison operations, and a finite gluing example.

## Reused results and declared experiment

The [four-bin instrument](../particle_atlas_horizontal/MEASUREMENT.md) supplies
the exact state `psi=(1,1,0,0)/sqrt(2)` and two alternative apparatus choices.
Direct detection has projectors `P_j=|j><j|`; the Fourier analyzer uses
`F4[k,j]=(-i)^(kj)/2` and projectors `F4† P_j F4`. Its complete probabilities
are respectively

\[
 p_X=(1/2,1/2,0,0),\qquad p_K=(1/2,1/4,0,1/4).
\]

These are two experiments on the same declared preparation. Every event
identifier includes its apparatus setting. The record comparison does not
assign simultaneously sharp position and momentum to a single event.
Physical length, translation momentum and a particle identity require the
corresponding observable dictionary. The four finite labels alone do not
establish a canonical position-momentum commutator.

The [complete pointer contract](READOUT_CONTRACT.md) supplies
`U=sum_j P_j tensor shift^j`, a unitary on every field/pointer input.
On a blank pointer the branch operator is `P_j`. The record probability
is `p_j=Tr(rho P_j)`, with post-record state `P_j rho P_j/p_j` when
`p_j>0`. Zero-weight outcomes remain in the complete menu; they have no
conditional state on this input.

The finite Born result
[`operational_record_probabilities_unique_born`](../../Lean/EventAlgebra/OperationalBornBridge.lean)
represents complete record laws by a unique density when its equal-effect
and universal effect/coexistent-refinement premises hold. The two displayed
apparatus choices supply a concrete quantum instrument, not that universal
premise by themselves. This endpoint consumes its stated state and instrument;
agreement between observers is not an additional rule selecting probabilities.

The projective record span has the commutative public algebra proved in
[`PublicRecordAlgebra.lean`](../../Lean/EventAlgebra/PublicRecordAlgebra.lean).
The selected instrument is explicitly the compression `P_j rho P_j`.
Its certain-state invariance satisfies the finite
[`instrument_with_projective_effects_fixing_certain_states_is_lueders`](../../Lean/EventAlgebra/InstrumentSelectionByCertainStates.lean)
hypotheses. Repeatability alone is not used to infer this instrument.

## Write and copy an orthogonal record

Let each observer have five memory codes, with code 4 reserved for failure.
Alice encodes events as `(0,1,2,3)`; Bob uses `(2,0,3,1)`. For either
encoding `e`, the declared copying service is the complete permutation

\[
 C_e|j,a\rangle=|j,(a+e(j))\bmod5\rangle.             \tag{1}
\]

It is unitary on all twenty pointer/memory inputs. Starting from a blank
memory writes `e(j)` and leaves the pointer intact. Two such controlled
copies commute because they share an orthogonal control basis and have
different targets. The complete experiment on blank memories gives

\[
 V|\psi\rangle=\sum_j P_j|\psi\rangle
       |j\rangle_D|e_A(j)\rangle_A|e_B(j)\rangle_B,
 \qquad V^\dagger V=I.                              \tag{2}
\]

The code evaluates the same map on every input column. Tracing or recording
the orthogonal pointer gives its original four Born weights. Each observer
can read its local code repeatedly with the same result, absent an intervening
memory-changing operation. Repeating the same projective source measurement
without intervening dynamics also gives
`p(j,k)=delta_jk Tr(rho P_j)`.

Equation (1) copies a classical record basis. On a superposition of two
pointer labels it produces an entangled state; the reduced pointer loses
its off-diagonal coherence. It does not output two copies of an unknown
quantum state. The reversible-evaluation construction in
[`RecordGluingPrinciple.lean`](../../Lean/Geometry/RecordGluingPrinciple.lean)
supplies the same retain-input-and-write principle, with exact involution
and blank-register theorems for bit registers.

## Meanings, disagreement and every failure

Each observer's dictionary decodes its local code to the same event ID.
The complete ideal joint law is

\[
 \Pr(j,a,b)=p_j\,1_{a=e_A(j)}1_{b=e_B(j)}.             \tag{3}
\]

An individual run writes one event record. The distribution in (3)
describes repeated preparations and the complete ensemble of records;
an observer's agreement does not choose a particular outcome or replace
that distribution. A deterministic source that supplies raw history
weights can be inserted at this interface only with its own implementation
and state-to-record receipt. This example does not derive the four-bin
preparation from the bare C/E memory program.

To include read failures, choose independent declared stochastic services
`A(a|j)` and `B(b|j)`, each normalized over all five codes. The original
law and its complete branch operators are

\[
 \Pr(j,a,b)=p_j A(a|j)B(b|j),\qquad
 K_{jab}=\sqrt{A(a|j)B(b|j)}P_j,\qquad
 \sum_{jab}K_{jab}^\dagger K_{jab}=I.                  \tag{4}
\]

The implementation retains all 100 branches, including zeros, and tensors
every operator with the identity on an arbitrary retained reference. All
single-observer and shared-event laws are marginals of (4). Thus their
compatibility is derived from one complete experiment, rather than imposed
by discarding disagreement. The test with Alice failure probability `1/20`
and Bob failure probability `1/10` has failure probability `29/200` and
agreement probability `171/200`; neither is renormalized.

If Bob misinterprets the codes for events 0 and 1, the Fourier experiment
has disagreement probability `3/4`. Repairing his dictionary restores
agreement without changing any raw pointer event, local record or event
weight. The correction is justified by the specified code conversion,
not by consensus inventing an unseen event. A noninjective meaning such as
even/odd bins sums the original weights. It loses the distinctions within
each fiber. The existing `lossless_injective` and `transcript_capacity`
theorems exclude a lossless decoder when such distinctions were discarded.

## A concrete finite descent receipt and its limits

For the four event IDs, take observer domains `{0,1,2}` and `{1,2,3}`.
Their union covers the event set. A local section is an event function
with values in one common codomain, with ordinary restriction as its
overlap map. The certificate uses bin labels; choosing complex values gives
the commutative function algebra. If the functions agree on `{1,2}`,
assigning either local value at each event gives a well-defined global
function. Coverage makes it unique. The code
constructs that function and rejects an overlap mismatch or an uncovered
event. This is a concrete receipt of the restriction/gluing interface in
[`ObserverNetDescent.lean`](../../Lean/QFT/ObserverNetDescent.lean), with
literal coverage supplied here. That module explicitly does not derive
descent from isotony or finiteness alone.

This glues event functions, not arbitrary probability marginals or a
quantum field state. The observer comparison also retains the distinct
conditions isolated by
[`OperationalOverlapEvidence.lean`](../../Lean/QFT/OperationalOverlapEvidence.lean):
common restricted records and equal readout weights are separate statements.
Both follow here from the shared pointer experiment and the stated dictionaries.

If a complete finite law `p` approximates a proposed effective law `q`
within total variation `epsilon`, a common meaning map `m` obeys

\[
 \tfrac12\sum_y\left|\sum_{j:m(j)=y}(p_j-q_j)\right|
 \leq\tfrac12\sum_j|p_j-q_j|\leq\epsilon.             \tag{5}
\]

The complete-instrument contract propagates source and apparatus errors
to these original records before interpretation. A separate dictionary
or approximation error adds by the triangle inequality. Convergence to
continuum field predictions requires such bounds on a stated family of
experiments, together with the relevant locality, state and energy-momentum
conditions. A finite bin diagram or record agreement alone supplies none
of those continuum hypotheses. In an admitted relativistic sector,
`E(p)^2=m²c⁴+c²p²` defines rest energy `E0=mc²`; a finite repair cost is
not assigned that interpretation without the same-sector dispersion and
unit dictionary.
