and contact coupling $G=1/64$, and extend only the outgoing lepton ring to

coordinates. The filled neutral reference and charge identity

pair without a particle cutoff. The exact charge and spin sector has

subspace to $6+36L^2$ states. In its parent/emission decomposition,
\[
 H=\begin{pmatrix}A&GC^\dagger\\GC&D\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 C^\dagger C=24I_6,\qquad
 P_{\rm pair}(t)=\frac{3}{512}t^2+O(t^3).
\]

\[
 \dot a(t)=-iAa(t)-G^2\int_0^t C^\dagger e^{-iD(t-s)}C a(s)\,ds .
\]

supplies a memory kernel rather than an assumed decay rate.
