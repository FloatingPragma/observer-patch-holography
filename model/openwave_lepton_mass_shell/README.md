on the same finite source. Exact source embeddings fix their charge and
spin, and complete rest and spatial probe records retain every valley,
ancillary output and reference. The [proof](PROOF.md) distinguishes these
three free mass values from an identification of physical leptons.

three branch minima as the full charged-particle spectrum.
