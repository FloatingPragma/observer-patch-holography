particle and antiparticle occupation code. A declared positive square-root
kinetic law and explicit spinor filters give a free Dirac comparison with
controlled finite momentum, time and volume errors. The same native ground,
charge references and source clock support complete bounded detector records.

The [proof](PROOF.md) distinguishes the full native source from its exact
free sector and its continuum readout. The [receipt](results.json) records
an executed 16-state detector circuit, all sixteen retained pointer outcomes,
preparation errors and capacity. Its construction uses exact spectral and
programmable finite-matrix apparatus services; an efficient passive native
implementation, interacting charged field and Standard Model identity are
outside this result.
