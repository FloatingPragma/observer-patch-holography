# Charged-field source interfaces

The finite free construction in this directory uses actual native ground
and charged spectral vectors. Its kinetic law, spatial replication and
field readout are declared architecture. The existing source packages
supply the following distinct interfaces.

| Interface | Actual result | Transfer boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `coherent_defect_wave/native_routing_join` | A changed routing/contact clock transports a paired-flux object and gives a finite native-dependent face response. | Its four-component coin belongs to the routed object. Its identity collision rule is not a fermionic exterior lift, and its native-empty Dirac route does not acquire a native mass correction. |
| `coherent_defect_wave/chiral_source` | A sitewise exterior-code construction carries every canonical occupation and the actual native currents. | Its whole-object odd grading and Weyl kinetic law are declared choices. It does not identify the charged eigenstates of the interacting eight-cell source. The local antisymmetrized-prefix encoding is reusable with actual odd eigenvectors. |
| `coherent_defect_wave/chiral_regulator` | A separate spectral Weyl derivative and its filled negative-band reference have a controlled free limit. | The quantum-link blank is not an identity-valued link. The filled free reference is not the actual interacting native ground. |
| `coherent_defect_wave/chiral_joint/field_limit` | The full quantum source has exact dressed local parity and a constructive low-energy invariant graph with a complete generator-residual bound. | Blank-link compression forbids motion of one fermion between sites at every time. Its virtual quartic response is not a Dirac band. Its residual theorem can transfer a comparison only after the actual residual and reference are supplied. |
| Native/affine charged join | Actual native charged G1 vectors couple to the finite affine cone through explicit spectral matrix units, with all photon and native complements retained. | The selected motion is spin-independent affine dynamics. The fixed-reference cone has no translation-invariant momentum shell. The five-state exclusion code is not a four-mode canonical Fock carrier. |

Three elementary obstructions rule out a direct relabeling. First, on a
vacuum-plus-doublet code, the anticommutator of one lowering operator with
its adjoint annihilates the other doublet state; it is not the identity.
Second, the affine motion is proportional to the identity on the native
G1 index. It supplies no three Clifford matrices or controlled momentum
refinement. Third, using the opposite-charge doublet as the other two
components of a same-charge one-particle Dirac Hamiltonian would make its
off-diagonal kinetic blocks change the conserved charge.

The construction here uses two native copies for each charge. The pair
contains the required two-occupied spin singlet, so its four-state code is
the complete Fock space of a doublet. Four copies give the canonical
particle/antiparticle carrier. The unchanged positive native energies
support a positive square-root kinetic law, while explicit spinor filters
combine particle annihilation with antiparticle creation into a Dirac field.
The source has no filled negative-energy sea. This is an explicit free
architecture and a different Hamiltonian from the interacting affine cone.

The positive/negative-energy change of representation has the classical
precedent of [Foldy and Wouthuysen](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.78.29).
The frame identities and complete finite instruments used here are proved
directly. Local unitary lattice routes to Weyl and Dirac equations, such as
[Bialynicki-Birula's construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9304070), are
alternative kinetic laws; they do not establish this source's charged
ground or canonical occupation code.

The free boundary charge here is not an interacting electromagnetic
Gauss-law continuum. The distinction is substantive: continuum results
on [Gauss law and charged-field representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.11009)
involve electromagnetic observables and charge-induced changes of their
representations. Those hypotheses are not supplied by an internal charge
label, a finite reference register or the present free source. Neither
electromagnetic locality nor an interacting charged mass shell is imported.
