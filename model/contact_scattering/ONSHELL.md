# An on-shell lattice scattering theorem and a certified threshold

The supplied Weyl/contact action now has a genuine **two-fermion lattice
scattering operator**, beyond the earlier positive-broadening response.
Every premise of the trace-class wave-operator theorem is satisfied on its
physical exchange subspace. At zero total momentum the contact spectrum
reaches zero energy with an exactly bounded quadratic density, retaining
all eight Weyl nodes. This advances CP-06 on the existing action. It does
not identify zero model energy with a physical vacuum, construct photons,
or establish a chiral many-body continuum.

## The wave-operator premises on this action

For each total momentum K, take normalized Brillouin measure on T^3 and
the closed Hilbert space

```
H_K = {psi in L2(T^3,C^4): psi(K-k) = -SWAP psi(k)}.
H0,K(k) = h(k) tensor I + I tensor h(K-k),
h(k) = -c sigma.sin(k),
H_U,K = H0,K + U |d><d|,   d(k) = (|up,down>-|down,up>)/sqrt(2).
```

The exchange involution commutes with H0,K, and d belongs to H_K with norm
one. The multiplication operator H0,K is bounded self-adjoint with norm
at most 2 sqrt(3)c. The contact is self-adjoint rank one with trace norm U
for U>=0. Restriction to H_K preserves these facts. Thus all hypotheses of
the Kato--Rosenblum theorem hold: both wave operators

```
W_+/- = strong-lim_(t -> +/-infinity) exp(it H_U,K) exp(-it H0,K) P_ac(H0,K)
```

exist and have range H_ac(H_U,K). Consequently S=W_+* W_- is unitary on
H_ac(H0,K). This is an application of an established theorem, not a new
abstract scattering result; see the trace-class scattering discussion in
[Simon's account of Kato's work](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00528) and the
[finite-rank scattering construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3568).

Here “complete” means complete **on the absolutely continuous subspaces**.
Point spectrum, including the existing isolated repulsive pair, stays in
the Hilbert space but is not an incoming free scattering state. No claim
of complete many-body scattering or absence of singular spectrum elsewhere
is inferred. There is no spatial-spacing limit and no swap of finite-volume
and vanishing-broadening limits.

Let g_+(E)=a(E)-i*pi*rho_0(E) be the boundary value of the same free contact
resolvent. On the contact-generated spectral channel, the stationary
rank-one formula gives

```
tau_+(E) = U/(1-U*g_+(E)),
S_contact(E) = 1-2*pi*i*rho_0(E)*tau_+(E)
             = (1-U*a(E)-i*pi*U*rho_0(E))/(1-U*a(E)+i*pi*U*rho_0(E)).
```

The absolute value is one. It differs from one wherever U>0 and rho_0>0.
On the orthogonal reducing complement of the cyclic subspace generated by
d, the two Hamiltonians coincide, so S is identity on its ac part.
Orthogonality to d at only one instant is not enough to assert this
reducing-subspace property. The code checks the formula algebraically and
labels its a,rho arguments as inputs, rather than reporting invented
on-shell numerical lattice amplitudes.

## Exact density with every node retained

At K=0 the singlet resolvent at k is

```
<singlet,(z-H0,0(k))^-1 singlet> = z/(z^2-4*c^2*sum_a sin(k_a)^2).
```

The singlet has weight 1/2 at each of the two eigenvalues
`+/-2c |sin(k)|`. The remaining two spin eigenvectors have zero energy and
zero singlet weight. Those dark zero-energy states are retained; they do
not enter rho_0 or P_ac. For `0<|E|<2c`, put r=|E|/(2c). The map
q_a=sin(k_a) has eight preimages with Jacobian
`prod_a (1-q_a^2)^(-1/2)`. Coarea on the radius-r sphere gives

```
rho_0(E) = E^2/(4*pi^2*c^3) * A(r^2),
A(x) = average_(n in S^2) prod_a (1-x*n_a^2)^(-1/2).
```

No low-momentum pocket has been selected. Expand each factor by its
binomial series. Positivity and uniform convergence for x<1 give

```
A(x) = sum_(N>=0) A_N x^N,
A_N = sum_(i+j+k=N) [prod_a binom(2a,a)/4^a]
                         * [(2i-1)!!(2j-1)!!(2k-1)!!/(2N+1)!!].
```

Every A_N is positive and at most one: each binomial factor is at most
one, and the sum of unweighted sphere moments is at most the multinomially
weighted sum `average (n_1^2+n_2^2+n_3^2)^N=1`. Therefore the remainder
after degree M is at most `x^(M+1)/(1-x)`. The first three coefficients
are `1, 1/2, 11/40`. `onshell.py` uses rational arithmetic for this bound
and an alternating-series Machin enclosure for pi. Independent spherical
quadrature and direct four-spin matrix inverses check the attachment.
Floating quadrature is a diagnostic separate from the exact certificate.

The density is positive and real analytic on each open interval
`(-2c,0)` and `(0,2c)`, and its formula extends analytically through zero.
The remaining spectral measure is separated from any smaller neighborhood
of zero. The principal-value transform is consequently continuous there:
on a symmetric local interval subtract rho_0(E) in the numerator before
integrating the singular kernel, and integrate the separated tail normally.
Evenness gives a(0)=0, while rho_0(0)=0. Thus g_+(E) tends to zero.
The rank-one resolvent identity then proves

```
rho_U(E) = rho_0(E)/|1-U*g_+(E)|^2 > 0  (0<|E|<2c),
rho_U(E) ~ rho_0(E) ~ E^2/(4*pi^2*c^3)  as E -> 0.
```

The interacting contact response therefore has no spectral interval missing
around zero. At the same time the earlier `U>2 sqrt(3)c` result supplies an
isolated repulsive pair above the free band. These are compatible features
of one lattice action. Neither the pair energy nor the zero threshold is a
mass measured above a derived physical vacuum. In particular this result
does not turn the model's doubled Weyl content into the Standard Model.

## Reproduce

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python -m model.contact_scattering.onshell
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python -m pytest -q -W error model/contact_scattering/test_onshell.py
```

The retained `onshell_results.json` records exact bounds and their displayed
decimal values. The proof is the argument above plus the named imported
theorems; passing finite numerical tests is not used as an infinite-volume
proof. Detector bandwidth, asymptotic source preparation, physical units,
the common defect/reference attachment, chiral refinement and gravitational
coupling remain open.
