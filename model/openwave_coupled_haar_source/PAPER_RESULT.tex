\paragraph{Two interacting gauge loops.}
The full Haar Gauss quotient of two adjacent SU(2) plaquettes has one basis
vector for each admissible spin triple $(j_1,j_2,J)$. Its electric energy is
$\alpha[3j_1(j_1+1)+3j_2(j_2+1)+J(J+1)]$; the shared-link spin $J$ couples
the two loops. Wilson multiplication has exact squared-$6j$ matrix
coefficients, independently checked by Clebsch--Gordan Haar integrals.
The source retains every representation. Finite proof coordinates carry
a positive bound on their entire infinite complement. This construction
concerns a finite interacting gauge graph and supplies no spatial
continuum or physical particle identification.
