\paragraph{Radiation-era capacity histories.}
With conserved matter and ideal radiation, the gamma-one future-event
branch obeys
$d\Omega_x/d\log a=\Omega_x(1-\Omega_x)(1+r+2\sqrt{\Omega_x})$,
where $r=\rho_r/(\rho_m+\rho_r)$.
An outward Taylor enclosure controls the branch through $z=10^6$.
Its early radiation-era fraction scales as $a^2$, whereas fixed capacity
gives $a^4$ and the Hubble-area control retains a constant fraction.
The present amplitudes and radiation content remain supplied; these are
conditional expansion histories, not derived particle abundances.
