# Neutral composite energy and propagation

All six signed local composite families admit a complete experiment whose
original selected-state probability distinguishes neighboring from
nonneighboring cells inside a specified positive final-energy band. The
calculation uses their literal fermion operators, the physical gauge carrier,
the same source clock and all forty local rotation components. It retains
failed interventions, other energy records and the quantum reference.

The [proof](PROOF.md) and [receipt](results.json) give exact rational
one-link energy means and finite original probability bounds. The probability
floor is extremely small and capacity-specific. These records do not identify
neutrinos, neutrons, a relativistic dispersion or a physical mass.
