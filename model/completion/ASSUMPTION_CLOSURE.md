# Assumptions and source-specific theorem use

The [current inventory](assumption_closure.json) reads the official
[CCF-LT-2 dependency graph](../particle_joint_reconstruction/dependencies.json),
84 canonical premise rows, 16 selected component claims and their explicit
assumptions, imported results and premise edges. It reads current repository
files and uses the current acceptance-node owners. It requires no Git revision.

Each result names its state, algebra, operation, reference, clock and domain.
The complete local-capacity source, Wilson comparison, coherent lepton cycle,
finite population law, calibrated comparisons and conditional gravity theorem
have separate interfaces. A source join requires an actual comparison map;
matching labels or values does not establish it. Pure Yang-Mills premises
require their own pure-gauge source.

The eight retained finite mathematical applications keep their proof anchors,
independent checkers and exact finite hypotheses. Their numerical conclusions
and source domains are preserved. None discharges a canonical physical premise
or proves the coupled CCF-LT-2 spectrum, continuum or Einstein hypotheses.
The three additional finite comparison records retain their calibration and
physical-map obligations. Wider claim evidence is listed as unreviewed pointers.

Coverage is exact for the selected set: every named assumption and imported
edge appears once, and every conditional graph assumption lists all its
consumer nodes. Missing rows, duplicates, unmatched theorem hypotheses,
changed source interfaces and physical promotions are rejected. This is a
checked disclosure contract, not a proof that every listed statement is true.

The native execution component claim remains a multi-package claim with its
own named services; it is not the official whole Hamiltonian. In particular,
finite instrument execution, thermal benchmark counts and a calibrated mass
comparison retain distinct premises. Every proposed source join remains
unestablished, and no independent physical comparison is added here.

`--write` to regenerate it from the reviewed policy, and
`python -m pytest -q model/completion/test_assumption_closure.py` for its
coverage and corruption controls.
