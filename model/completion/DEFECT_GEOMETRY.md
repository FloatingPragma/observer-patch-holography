# Charged excitations and responsive native geometry

The [campaign contract](defect_geometry_campaign.json) covers five issues
with four source-specific results. Finite interacting cores have charged
spectral poles with positive residue. The original odd open cube supplies
one such core without a mass texture. A bounded radial texture has exact
localized zero modes on the original infinite lattice, protected within
an explicitly inversion-odd Hamiltonian perturbation class. A fixed native
instrument makes its three-dimensional response Gram change with the
electric energy of fresh matter probes.

The [interacting pole theorem](../defect_core_response/README.md) consumes
the actual full-Fock ground and charged projectors of each finite parent,
including the [unmodified odd cube](../native_odd_box_core/README.md).
The pole residue is a bound on an observable fermion correlation, rather
than an energy level alone. The odd cube's zero mode is a boundary standing
wave, and its controlled large-box charged gap tends to zero.

The [bounded lattice index](../bounded_defect_index/README.md) retains the
supplied texture and all native valleys. Its nonlocal spatial-inversion
index has the same sign for both texture orientations. This index is
distinct from the continuum internal index, and it does not transfer the
finite linear-texture interaction theorem to the bounded radial family.
Native repair protection is not inferred from either Hamiltonian result.

The [energy-responsive Gram](../matter_geometry_response/README.md) is
derived from complete source histories under one fixed instrument. The
rank-three band is uniformly isolated, and a rational certificate proves
a finite change in its geometry. Repeated mean dynamics requires fresh
independent probes; an exact correlated-input countercontrol exposes that
condition. The proper-code result concerns ideal source controls, with
rounded and physical service errors requiring their own bounds.

A common interacting relativistic theory and Einstein geometry require
state, action, proper-clock and refinement maps between these sources,
together with regional entropy/area stationarity. The campaign preserves
every canonical physical premise and gate status while recording the
finite source join that the pole theorem supplies.
