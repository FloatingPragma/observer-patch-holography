# Defect spectra and conditional Einstein dynamics

The [campaign contract](spectrum_einstein_campaign.json) assigns five issues
to three scoped results. A specified fermion mass texture has a conditional
bulk and defect spectrum, with all native valleys retained. Finite rotations
of the actual ordered spin ground have an exact nonlinear texture-energy
formula and a controlled static long-wavelength limit. Ten explicit timelike
regional readouts give a quantitative Einstein tensor reconstruction under
matched entropy, area, stress and refinement hypotheses.

The [fermion spectrum](../topological_defect_spectrum/README.md),
[spin texture energy](../spin_texture_energy/README.md) and
[conditional gravity proof](../matched_einstein_limit/README.md) retain
their distinct sources and assumptions. The gapless bulk tuning and gapped
defect regime differ. A supplied texture does not establish its dynamical
selection or protection by native repair. The spin-texture energy bound does
not identify a stable particle pole, and the finite thermal area coefficient
does not instantiate regional Einstein stationarity.

The campaign proves no common physical model by combining those labels.
The canonical premise statuses and completion gates retain their own
acceptance conditions. The contract and independent mutation checks bind
each result, state, clock, refinement and remaining physical identification.
