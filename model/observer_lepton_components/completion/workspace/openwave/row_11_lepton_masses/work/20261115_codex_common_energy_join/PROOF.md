Platform row 11 remains untested. This written proof constructs a conditional
query program from the actual signed-current operator and the inherited
family analyzer projector. No Hamiltonian is evolved in this construction.
Its neutral grading, coherent queries, weights, reflection and phase clock

alone has class F. It supplies a precise potential N+ implementation target,
and does not establish physical lepton minima or an accepted cell.

    U_e Q_e U_e = -Q_e,

    D_0 = T_D* S_D T_D = (1/3) sum_e (sigma.n_e) J_e.          (2)

This is D/(3 ell) in the prior equal-length notation. It is a contraction.
On the reference arm every Q_e=0, so the same C_e flips its query bit and

On invalid input we instead use the declared flagged identity completion.

## 4. One program, one compression, and the exact commutator condition

    S|_D=tau_z S_D tensor I_(family apparatus, qA),

## 5. The complete phase law and its common reference

For Bv=lambda v and u=Tv, if |lambda|<1 define

    b_lambda=sqrt(1-lambda^2),
    z=(Su-lambda u)/b_lambda.

u,z are orthonormal and

    W u=lambda u+b_lambda z,
    W z=-b_lambda u+lambda z.                               (9)

The two eigenvectors (u-i z)/sqrt(2) and (u+i z)/sqrt(2) have phases
lambda+i b_lambda and lambda-i b_lambda, respectively. At lambda=+1 or
-1 the vector u itself has that phase; there is no omitted divided-by-zero
branch. Let K=ran T+S ran T. Its orthogonal complement is invariant under S
and R_T; on K-perp, W=-S, hence its phases are +1 and -1 with their actual
multiplicities. These remaining sectors are retained, although ideal signal
preparation does not excite K-perp. The invalid flag has W=I as specified.

For any integer n>=0,

    T* W^n T=T_n(B),                                        (10)

where T_n is the Chebyshev polynomial. This follows by iterating (9), with
the endpoint cases included. It describes a measurable correlation, not
postselection on a favorable eigenphase branch.

For clarity one may report all phases relative to the common phase +i:
Z=-i W. For a signal value lambda, its branches then have angles

    -arcsin(lambda),       -pi+arcsin(lambda)  (mod 2pi).     (11)

Thus for the pair lambda=+/-r there are near-reference angles
+/-arcsin(r) and partner angles +/-[pi-arcsin(r)]. Both pairs, all signs,
all endpoint degeneracies, K-perp angles +/-pi/2, and the invalid angle
-pi/2 remain. Equation (11) is a reporting convention with one common
origin; it does not make negative or large-angle branches disappear.

On the fixed dressed reference D_0=A=0, so B=0. For every reference signal
u_R, z_R=S u_R is orthogonal to u_R, and (u_R-i z_R)/sqrt(2) has W phase +i.
This gives an explicit stationary reference for Z. Its orthogonal partner
has Z phase -1 and remains present. Preparation of this reference uses the
declared queries and coherent superposition; it is independent of the family
eigenvalues. Choosing one reference state fixes the phase origin, not a
rule to remove source spectral branches. All branches are accessible to
the complete quadrature read in section 8.

## 7. Linear versus square-root map in this clock

The exact finite-tick response in (13) is arcsin(w_M alpha_f). It is not
sqrt(alpha_f); it is also not exactly a linear mass map at finite weight.
The coefficient alpha_f enters the same B that carries current, and the
quadratic relation in (8) follows from the declared *anticommuting grading*.

A controlled common weak-query refinement can be stated without selecting
a family-dependent clock. Fix v_D,v_M>=0, v_D+v_M=1. Add a third selector
branch of weight 1-eta whose operation is X on a fresh blank query and whose
compression is zero. Give the two original branches weights eta v_D and
eta v_M. The full SELECT is still an involution and

    B_eta=eta(v_D tau_z D_0+v_M tau_x A),
    e_eta(d,f)=arcsin(eta r_v(d,f))/eta,
    r_v(d,f)=sqrt(v_D^2 d^2+v_M^2 alpha_f^2).                (14)

All phase partners and complements from section 5 remain. Taking one tick
to have declared duration eta, for 0<eta<1 the uniform elementary bound is

    0<=e_eta-r_v
       <=eta^2 r_v^3/[6(1-eta^2)^(3/2)].                    (15)

Indeed, (1-t^2)^(-1/2)-1 <= t^2/[2(1-eta^2)^(3/2)] for 0<=t<=eta;
integrate from 0 to eta r_v and divide by eta. Since ||B_eta||<=eta,
r_v<=1 and the bound holds for every signal mode. Thus a *single declared
refinement* gives zero-current response v_M alpha_f and the same limiting
current relation e^2=v_D^2 d^2+v_M^2 alpha_f^2.

This is a theorem about observer queries and a specified scaling of their
tick. It does not prove that native physical time scales as eta, nor a
physical momentum normalization for d, nor source selection of v_D/v_M.
Both SELECT branches must finish coherently at one declared tick boundary;
their different native-word depths, preparation/inverse costs and any
padding are part of that implementation. Counting one abstract oracle call
as one physical tick does not follow from the circuit. N-dependent family
queries in particular need their own common resource/action accounting.
Errors per tick must vanish faster than eta for fixed-duration convergence;
the fixed finite chart certificates do not establish that family of hardware
implementations. No full Lorentz continuum is imposed on the static row.

The scalar first-hit construction has its different same-menu law and gives
sqrt(alpha_f) for one selected flag, or alpha_f for two coincidences, in its
own refinement. Substituting that clock's square-root rule into (13)-(15)
would change the program. The full-CAR lepton_mass_compiler also has a linear
effect mass law, with its supplied neutral field, hopping and action clock.
No part of that Hamiltonian is installed here or imported as evidence for
this program. These constructions show why an effect alone cannot choose
the energy exponent. Beta's fractional-path ambiguity and the physical
selection of a family triplet remain unchanged in all comparisons.

## 8. Complete records, errors, and link continuity

Prepare any signal/reference state psi on the full query space, keep all

terminal coordinate x, including event padding, query-one, all count words,

    p_+(x)=|(psi(x)+zeta (W^n psi)(x))/2|^2,
    p_-(x)=|(psi(x)-zeta (W^n psi)(x))/2|^2.                 (16)

Their total is one and their signed sum is Re(zeta <psi,W^n psi>). Mixed
inputs and arbitrary references follow by linearity of the complete probe

implementing controlled W, reference preparation and final full-coordinate
readout remains an explicit service. Committing any intervening flag defines
another program whose spectrum must be derived again.

||S_actual-S||<=epsilon_S and ||R_actual-R_T||<=epsilon_R, telescoping gives

    ||W_actual^n-W^n||<=n(epsilon_S+epsilon_R).               (17)

## 9. What this resolves and the next exact obligations
