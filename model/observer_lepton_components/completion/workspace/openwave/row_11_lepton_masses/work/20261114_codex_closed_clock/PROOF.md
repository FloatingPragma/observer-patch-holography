and retain its complete output. For the family/reference marginal let

    E_δ(ρ) = Tr_anc[V_δ (|a0><a0|⊗ρ) V_δ†].                  (8)

Tracing defines this marginal; it is not a successful-outcome selection or a

Differentiating (8) at δ=0 gives exactly

    E'_0(ρ) = -i[A_N,ρ].                                      (9)

This identity also holds after tensoring any untouched reference. Since P_N
is a norm-one Hermitian projector, its commutator superoperator has completely

    ||E_δ - Ad_exp(-iδA_N)||_diamond ≤ 4δ².                    (10)

## 4. Controlled convergence of the count clock

Let K~Bin(N,p) and X=βK/N. Its mean is θ=pβ and its variance is
β²p(1-p)/N. For any real q, the scalar Taylor remainder around that mean gives

    |E exp(iqX)-exp(iqθ)| ≤ q² β² p(1-p)/(2N).                 (12)

The linear term vanishes by centering. Applying (12) to the q=1 and q=2

    ||A_N-A(pβ)||
      ≤ (2ab+4b²) β²p(1-p)/N

Duhamel's formula gives ||exp(-isA_N)-exp(-isA)||≤|s| ||A_N-A||,
and the diamond distance of their conjugation channels is at most twice this.
Combining with (11) proves the full two-parameter bound

      ≤ min(2, 4s²/M + 2|s|[(2+√2)/27] β²p(1-p)/N).            (14)

## 5. What is stationary, and what the chosen action means

Fresh identical count preparations and a frozen analyzer make A_N constant
across collisions. They do not drive β toward any distinguished value. The
limit θ=pβ is an accumulated preparation phase. The inherited θ=2/9 is
obtained at the specified β=1. This value of β means a unit coupling-duration
product relative to the declared control clock. It is still a supplied
operational premise of this construction.

A consistent change of time units does not change β: replacing T by cT and κ
by κ/c leaves κT, all records and all ratios unchanged. Changing T while
holding the coupling κ fixed changes the physical preparation. Changing κ
relative to the energy pulse or propagation clock changes the program. Those
changes must not be described as harmless changes of units.

Keeping one unreset source count ancilla throughout successive phase kicks
is also a different experiment. With a single Q, N commuting kicks combine to
exp(iβ JQ), whose reduced coherence is 1-p+p exp(iβΔj). It differs from the
fresh-source law (1-p+p exp(iβΔj/N))^N. For example, at β=2π and Δj=1 the
former is exactly one, while the fresh-source limit is exp(i2πp), nontrivial
for p=2/9. This analytic counterexample uses no numerical scan. The source's
return-and-reset implementation and this fresh retained implementation both
state how the desired count ensemble is restored; neither follows by simply
leaving the original unreset tick to run.
