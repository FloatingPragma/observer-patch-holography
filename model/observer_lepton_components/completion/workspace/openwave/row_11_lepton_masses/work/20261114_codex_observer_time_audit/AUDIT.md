dictionary. The source hypothesis requires the same energy and clock for rest, propagation and
interactions. The missing attachment is therefore concrete:

   the stationary/reflected record family and any proposed real-time interventions have that same

## The clock freedom is constrained once a common time is claimed

Suppose `U(t)` and `V(s)` describe the same full observable dynamics with a continuous common time
reparametrization `V(t)=U(tau(t))`, and the clock respects composition. Locally on a nontrivial faithful
time action, `tau(t+s)=tau(t)+tau(s)`; continuity gives `tau(t)=a t`. The generators are then related by
`H_V=a H_U+cI`, where the scalar c is invisible to Heisenberg observables. On disconnected observable
blocks an independent central constant can remain on each block until an inter-block comparison is
available. Periodic finite dynamics require the equality modulo the common period; a continuous lift
has the same local linear conclusion.

Consequently a common time-unit change rescales all energy differences together. Choosing a nonlinear
map of each spectral level or a separate time scale per family is a different dynamical identification,
not merely changing the clock unit. Such a construction can be investigated, but it owes its own
record-level theorem. This is why fitting an exponent in `lambda^p` cannot by itself complete row 11.

For the simple one-unit icosahedral source, the three nonzero Laplacian levels are
`5-sqrt(5), 6, 5+sqrt(5)` (multiplicities 3,5,3). The corresponding canonical transfer energies are
`-log(1-lambda/60)`. Since `2<lambda<8` and
`x<=-log(1-x)<=x/(1-x)`, their largest/smallest ratio is less than `60/13`.
Thus the three positive one-unit transfer levels cannot give the requested hierarchy through a common
clock scale. This statement excludes only that direct assignment, not other correlation sectors,
memory objects, reference offsets or a larger observer construction.
