# Colour strings and spatial mass records

An open colour chain has an exact electric cost proportional to the span of its three quarks. A native colour identity attaches its two-cell member to the five-input baryon and nuclear source without changing their singlet records. Executing the same coefficients on chains of two to five cells gives complete finite spectra and position records. The larger-chain neutral baryon band approaches 0.9396643 GeV, while its axial response changes to about 1.6658.

The [proof](PROOF.md), [source](source.py) and [receipt](results.json) include a uniform relative-confinement bound and a failed shared-length comparison between nuclear quadrupole and string tension. These finite results do not identify a physical continuum spectrum or string-breaking process.

Run `python3 -m model.openwave_colour_string --check` and `python3 -m pytest -q model/openwave_colour_string/test_string.py` from the repository root.
