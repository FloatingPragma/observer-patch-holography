# Local energy controls produce current records

A finite control of the selected source's local energy produces a nonzero
current response on its original prepared state. The construction retains
both detector outcomes and the quantum reference, and supplies explicit
pulse, probability and work bounds. Its energy generator obeys the finite
conservation theorem through spatial coarsening. The [proof](PROOF.md)
distinguishes this source response from a proper metric or an Einstein
law. Run `python -m model.particle_primary_energy_response --check`.
