The native fractional charges admit a continuous quotient-group lift.
For $G=(SU(3)\times U(1))/\mathbb Z_3$, the universal-cover kernel is
$\{(e^{2\pi i k/3}I,2\pi k/3):k\in\mathbb Z\}$, so $\pi_1(G)=\mathbb Z$.
An explicit generator has electromagnetic phase lifts $2\pi(2/3)$ and
$2\pi(-1/3)$ on the actual quark coordinates, compensated by colour.
Complete 120- and 364-state Gauss carriers satisfy the same endpoint
identity. Their electric eigenstates have uniform conjugate-angle density;
the continuous group loop does not establish a localized classical texture
or a quark rest mass.
