\paragraph{A native-state axial calibration.}
On the complete two-cell native fermion carrier, a positive sum of number,
graph-gradient, colour, spin, weak and baryon-number squares admits a
split-site three-quark colour singlet. Four empirical inputs, the proton,
neutron and charged-pion energies and the magnitude $1.2753$ of the axial
coefficient, determine four source constants. The native weak current is
unchanged. Its vector coefficient is exactly one, while an exact
three-dimensional invariant-sector residual certificate encloses the axial
coefficient in $[1.27529999995,1.27530000005]$. Only about $3.58\%$ of the
state is co-located. The correlations across the two cells therefore evade
the multiplicity-one local scalar-action constraint $g_A=5/3$.
Complete 120-state baryon and 64-state meson spectra, full occupation
preparation failures and reference-stable error bounds are retained.
This axial agreement is a calibration. A positive edge-colour square
changes coloured disconnected-copy thresholds but vanishes on every
original two-cell singlet, including a separated quark--diquark state;
it supplies no two-cell spatial-binding or continuum-confinement proof.
