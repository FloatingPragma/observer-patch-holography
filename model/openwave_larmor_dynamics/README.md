# A magnetic dipole that precesses

A confined finite particle rotates in a weak external magnetic field
while its own magnetic field follows its actual quantum current. The
spin and the independently measured dipole complete matching precession
cycles. An exact operator bound makes the quasistatic self-field unique
for every particle state. The source retains its external confining well,
finite lattice and transverse-field approximation; physical electron
identification and continuum magnetic precision require separate tests.
Its state-dependent evolution is a semiclassical mean-field law, without
a linear quantum-channel claim for arbitrary entangled inputs.

Run `python3 -m model.openwave_larmor_dynamics --write` to compute the
complete records, or use `--check` for a fresh source-bound replay.
The [proof](PROOF.md) specifies the dynamics and error limits.
