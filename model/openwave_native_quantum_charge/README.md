# Native charge of a quantum particle

The dressed spin-half particle can be attached to the actual native electric
current. A complete weak-singlet reference is exactly neutral; a canonical
particle-hole transformation then gives the charged Dirac variables with no
charge offset. All native families and complementary modes are retained.
The confined positive-kernel source and its conjugate copy have a complete
charged energy floor. Physical particle identification and local gauge
completion require separate matching. See the [proof](PROOF.md) and
[complete records](results.json).
