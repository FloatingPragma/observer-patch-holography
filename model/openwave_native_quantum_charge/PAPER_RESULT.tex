\subsection{Native electric charge of a dressed quantum state}
Fill all seven native weak-singlet coordinates in both spin states and
leave the doublets empty. Every original gauge current annihilates this
reference; its electric charge is exactly zero. For each actual signed
Higgs pair define $d_L=c_L$ and $d_R=s\epsilon c_R^\dagger$. The canonical
CAR transformation preserves both spin components and gives
$Q_{\rm pair}=q(N_d-2)$. The three up, three down and one lepton charges
sum to zero, so the full native current obeys
\[
 Q_{\rm native}=\sum qN_d
\]
without a reference-charge offset. The mass pairing becomes a Dirac
bilinear. A constant chirality rotation identifies its positive absolute
kernel with the corresponding quantum rotational block, including its
actual current derivative and field coupling. This attaches one charged
block, rather than identifying all sixteen Clifford coordinates with one
native family.

The declared extension retains all native pairs, families and neutral
coordinates, together with a charge-conjugate native copy. Its neutral
confined Weyl kernel has energy at least $1/2$, as follows from
$T^2=(5/2)T$ and the exact positive decomposition of
$N_{\rm spatial}+T/2-I/2$. The ordered positive CAR lift gives
$H\ge16N_{\rm charged}+N_{\rm neutral}/2+N_{\rm photon}$.
The light charged ground energy lies below $16.5$, every additional neutral
costs at least $1/2$, and every two-charged sector costs at least $32$.
Thus each charge-one sign has a four-dimensional lowest band containing
two spin-half doublets. The complete preparation selects one line with
actual native charge of magnitude one and dressed $J^2=3/4$.
The absolute kernel, trap, conjugate copy, fixed Higgs background and
empirical electron unit are explicit; local longitudinal gauge completion
and a physical particle dictionary do not follow from this attachment.
