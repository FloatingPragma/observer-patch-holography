\paragraph{Complete native Higgs action and the charged floor.}
Retain the fifteen-coordinate matter module, all three family characters,
all momenta and the full spin-epsilon pairing kernel.  With the unit
neutral Higgs background, the three native invariant tensors define a
rank-seven directed matrix $D$ satisfying
\[
 D^2=0,\qquad D^\dagger D+DD^\dagger=P_{\rm ch},\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}P_{\rm ch}=14,\qquad Q(1-P_{\rm ch})=0.
\]
The declared common family choice $Y_u=Y_d=Y_e=\operatorname{diag}(e_k)$
gives seven charged Dirac channels per character and valley, with
energies $\sqrt{\sum_i\sin^2(ap_i)/a^2+e_k^2}$.  The remaining internal
coordinate is neutral.  On the complete quasiparticle Fock space,
\[
 H-E_{\rm ground}\ \geq\ m_{\min}|Q|,\qquad m_{\min}=\min_k e_k>0.
\]
Indeed every charged occupation has energy at least $m_{\min}$ and
charge magnitude at most one, and every zero-energy occupation is
neutral.  The opposite-leg pairing ground has zero charge.  A charged
lepton rest mode attains the charge-one floor.  The side-four census
retains all $64$ momenta and $11520$ Nambu rows; its $48$ zero-energy
CAR modes are neutral.  Removing either quark Higgs channel restores
charged zero modes.

The complete controlled implementation has $42s$ disjoint four-CAR
factors and retains all $324000s$ enlarged CAR coordinates and all
ancillary outcomes.  Its unhalved trace error obeys
$\min\{2,42sR^5|t|^5/(10n^4)\}$ for
$R=(7/3)(4/a+1)$ and $n\geq R|t|$.  Fixed native leg creation gives
success probability $1/2$, with failed creation and all four occupation
outcomes retained.  The source choice removes the massless charged
counterexample of the charged-lepton-only action; it does not change
that counterexample's validity for that earlier action.  Equal up,
down and lepton family coefficients are explicit architecture and
produce equal family spectra.  They do not reproduce unequal physical
quark masses, determine the family selection, or identify interacting
particles.
