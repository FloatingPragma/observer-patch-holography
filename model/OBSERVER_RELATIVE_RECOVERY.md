# Observer records and recovery

The [CCF-LT-2 specification](SPEC.md) fixes the private joint state and
complete observation protocols. Observers compare records made by those
protocols. Effective fields, particles, geometry and clocks are descriptions
of the resulting compatible observations.

## Complete records

A record identifies the instrument, preparation context, reference labels,
outcome and declared time/work account. Its probability follows from the
actual joint state and Kraus operation. Every complement, failed preparation,
no-arrival and capacity outcome belongs to the instrument. Degenerate
projectors retain unresolved private occupations and inactive memory.
A copied public label is an orthogonal classical record, not a copy of an
arbitrary private quantum state.

Charge and flux outcomes use the same oriented `G=BE-n-r` as the Hamiltonian.
Physical preparations have zero mismatch probability by Gauss preservation;
mismatch labels remain available on the full kinematic carrier. Current
expectations and premeasurement energy are operator diagnostics unless a
separate complete meter is specified. Their backaction cannot be inferred
from the charge/flux meter alone.

## Energy and memory

Free evolution conserves complete `H_total`. Local energy, matter energy,
field energy and auxiliary energy may exchange through the specified law.
Terminal measurement work is evaluated with the Hamiltonian of the declared
experiment. References are retained through coherent comparisons; tracing
out their memory is a separate operation with its own record law.

Finite capacity and observation horizon are part of a protocol. An exhausted
record carrier requires a complete exhaustion outcome or a specified
export/erasure operation and its work. Infinite fresh blanks are not a
zero-cost ingredient.

## Recovery and physical identification

A state valuation on the actual accessible algebra determines its
Hilbert/GNS representation. Comparing two descriptions requires their state,
algebra and instrument maps, including retained references. General Born,
channel and commuting-observable results apply under those hypotheses.
They do not assert that a narrow detector menu implements every observable.

The source's graph positions are chart data. Observer-reconstructed distance,
common propagation metric, regional stress, laboratory clock and physical
energy scale require explicit maps and controlled observation errors.
The [proof dependency map](particle_joint_reconstruction/DEPENDENCIES.md)
records which mathematical results have these additional premises. Separate
finite diagnostic records are not full-native observer histories without
an exact source reduction or comparison theorem.
