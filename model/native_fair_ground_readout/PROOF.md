## A quantitative vacuum test for the faithful reference

Diagonalize P with eigenvalues lambda_a in [alpha,1], and put
E_a=-2M log(lambda_a). For a bounded A define the positive spectral measure
of Tr(rho A* exp(itH) A exp(-itH)) by

    mu_A = sum_ab (lambda_b/Tr(P)) |A_ab|^2 delta_(E_a-E_b).

Relabeling a,b proves the exact reflected-measure identity

    mu_(A*)(-dE)=exp[-E/(2M)] mu_A(dE).                 (1)

In particular alpha mu_A(B)<=mu_(A*)(-B)<=alpha^(-1)mu_A(B)
for every measurable B. For a Hermitian A, positive and negative spectral
weights are therefore comparable by the same uniform constant. This is
ordinary finite-temperature behavior; H itself is nonnegative.
It does not exclude a relativistic QFT in a thermal state.

It does prevent using this same reference as the positive-energy vacuum
required by the existing continuum_field_net/spin_canonical_field ground
constructions. If a family of uniformly bounded observables and all their
adjoints has limiting correlation measures, with the same alpha>0, (1)
passes to compact continuous test functions supported away from zero.
If all negative energies vanish in a proposed vacuum limit, all positive
energies vanish as well. On a cyclic algebra closed under adjoints the
limiting GNS dynamics is static. Equivalently rho is Gibbs for H at inverse
temperature 1/(2M), not a zero-temperature preparation in this counted
clock. A clock change alone does not change its finite-state eigenweights.

## Capacity-dependent regional ground state

For a region of r distinct ports, exact partial trace gives mutually
orthogonal Dicke blocks with probabilities

    w_k=binomial(r,k)binomial(V-r,q-k)/binomial(V,q),
    rho_R=sum_k w_k |Omega_(r,k)><Omega_(r,k)|.         (8)

These are not the regional marginals of P/Tr(P). For q/V tending to p,
the limiting probabilities are binomial(r,k)p^k(1-p)^(r-k).
Sampling r positions without replacement versus with replacement couples
the two counts except on a repeated position, whose probability is at
most r(r-1)/(2V). Coupling Bernoulli(q/V) with Bernoulli(p) gives the
additional r|q/V-p|. Since all blocks in (8) are orthogonal,

    trace distance <= min(1,r(r-1)/(2V)+r|q/V-p|).      (9)

The limiting states are compatible under all finite-region restrictions.
They equal the uniform phase average of product spin-coherent states with
occupation p. Thus the state refinement is explicit and independent of
any guessed third spatial coordinate. Both p and the global sector remain
declared source choices; passive repair conserves q and does not select p.
Limits p=0 or 1 are allowed but can make the accessible number-preserving
ground response trivial.

The evaluator uses the equivalent expression
binomial(r,k)(q)_k(V-q)_(r-k)/(V)_r, with falling factorials of length at
most r. It does not allocate a global occupation sector or form the huge
integer binomial(V,q). Fixed-region evaluation therefore remains practical
when V itself is a very large exactly represented integer.

The regional state has rank at most r+1, one rank-one vector in each
number sector. It is not faithful on a full regional number-preserving
matrix algebra whenever a block has dimension greater than one. Its
logarithm on the full algebra is not the faithful modular generator used
by the separate P/Tr(P) gravity branch. Restricting the ground to its
support does not recover that omitted full algebra.

This boundary has a concrete local witness. On any seam e=(a,b), let
Q_e=(I-S_e)/2. Then Q_e is a nonzero local projection, Q_e Omega=0, but

    ||Q_e n_a Omega||^2=q(V-q)/(2V(V-1)).              (10)

For density p in (0,1), the limit is p(1-p)/2>0. Thus the projection can
remain a nonzero represented local observable while annihilating the
ground. A limit retaining both these operators and their word moments
does not have a separating vacuum for that regional algebra. It cannot
be inserted into a relativistic theorem requiring that property. This is
conditional on the observable/region limit: microscopic operators may be
absent or vanish in a separately defined continuum field scaling, so
(10) is not a no-go theorem for every possible relativistic scaling.

For a connected graph, the one-unit sector of G is exactly its graph
Laplacian. In other sectors the unchanged -2 n_a n_b term remains in
each exchange bond; it cannot be deleted while retaining this operator.
Neither fact supplies a relativistic mass shell. The existing native
occupation spatial limit has spectral dimension two and diffusive
long-wavelength scaling. Taking sqrt(G) would change the dynamics and
would require a separate source implementation and locality proof.
