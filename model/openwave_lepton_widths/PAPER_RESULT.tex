\paragraph{Native leptonic widths and their physical units.}
The actual family current and three-body phase space give
\(\Gamma=G^2M^5f(m/M)/(1536\pi^3)\).
With the conventional weak normalization, \(G=2\sqrt2G_F\).
All nine calibrated massive-neutral channels are retained for each charged
transition; a rational bound places their total width within
\(1.7\times10^{-8}\) relative to the massless-neutral value.
The widths are \(3.00876260\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm{GeV}\) for the muon-like
electron channel and \(4.04898950\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm{GeV}\),
\(3.93790076\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm{GeV}\) for the two tau-like leptonic
channels. Their ratio is \(0.972563836\), compared with the unused PDG 2025
fit \(0.9730\pm0.0022\). The weak coefficient is a calibration input with
muon-lifetime ancestry; the muon result is a normalization check and lacks
radiative corrections. The positive-frequency source has an exact
parent-plus-all-daughters invariant sector. Rational Volterra and finite-bin survival bounds transfer to its calibrated
massive-neutral member: exact angular coarea gives
\(\|\sqrt{\rho_m}-\sqrt{\rho_0}\|_2\leq2\mu_{\max}^2\),
so the inclusive probability error is at most \(4Gt\mu_{\max}^2\).
This controls a finite weak-lifetime interval with all angular dark channels
retained.
Hadronic tau channels and a total physical tau lifetime are not derived.
The source and proof are in \texttt{model/openwave\_lepton\_widths}.
