# Family clocks and Higgs spectra

A finite family instrument gives a balanced three-level amplitude. Its
second tick has equal family amplitudes. An explicit collision
construction produces the squared amplitude as a Hamiltonian, with a
controlled channel error. A separate quantum Higgs memory has complete
elementary and composite spectra on one stationary state and clock; its
self-energy increases heavy/light ratios at every positive coupling.

These are finite conditional constructions. They supply no transfer to
the primary spatial source or identification with measured particle masses.
The [proof](PROOF.md) specifies the operators, complete records and domains;
[provenance](PROVENANCE.md) records the inherited architecture.
