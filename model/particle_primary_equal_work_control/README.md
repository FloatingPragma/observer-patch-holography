# Equal work does not identify a particle

An exact alternative detector on the selected Wilson source has the same
mean work as the critical phase detector, at every capacity. It randomly
applies an ordinary local fermion parity and retains its coin, origin,
energy and quantum reference records. Its probability of acting tends to
zero. Its complete energy law therefore becomes elastic while its mean
work stays positive and its second moment grows as inverse spacing.

The source, state and clock are unchanged. The apparatus is different;
this proves no collapse of the critical phase response. It identifies
which work and moment summaries cannot replace that response's spectral
calculation. See the [proof](PROOF.md). Replay with
`python -m model.particle_primary_equal_work_control --check`.
