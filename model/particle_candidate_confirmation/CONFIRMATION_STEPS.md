# Confirming candidate particles

Candidate tests within the existing joint dependency map; no additional physical gates.

The six tests use one source, state, reference and clock. A finite source
result supports a test without establishing its physical interpretation.

1. **Source operator and records.** Bind the actual source operator and accessible current/field records, conserved charges, conjugate channel and complete detector, including failure and boundary compensation.
2. **Joint energy and momentum response.** Calculate the original populated joint spectral response using the operational translation generator and the same clock.
3. **Physical transformation and statistics.** Attach physical Lorentz spin, chirality, exchange and current transformations; finite cube labels alone do not establish these.
4. **Interactions and distinct species.** Test the current vertices, distinguish flavors or polarizations, and retain extra modes, compensation and competing assignments.
5. **Controlled mass observable.** Use the appropriate threshold, resonance or renormalized mass definition and bound volume, cutoff, capacity and numerical errors.
6. **Independent physical comparison.** Attach one common unit convention, preserve calibration ancestry and compare complete records with experimental uncertainties.

## electron, muon, tau

A charged source spinor and its scalar dressing have complete compensated pair-energy records. Flavor labels are unassigned.

| Test | Required calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Joint energy and momentum response | Resolve a charged energy-momentum threshold while retaining the opposite endpoint and flux; their total energy cannot be divided by two to define a lepton mass. |
| Interactions and distinct species | Identify electromagnetic and weak current responses and three distinct source flavor channels. Test conjugate records and allowed decays. |
| Controlled mass observable | Specify the charged infrared threshold or resonance convention; distinguish stable electron and unstable muon/tau readouts. |

## electron_neutrino, muon_neutrino, tau_neutrino

The internal weak-doublet neutral component exists. The tested neutral singlet trion has different unbroken weak-current transformations.

| Test | Required calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Joint energy and momentum response | Construct a populated neutral weak-current channel and its energy-momentum response; a singlet composite name does not supply it. |
| Interactions and distinct species | Derive the production/detection flavor dictionary, mass eigenchannels and oscillation probabilities. Specify the neutrino operator and test its lepton-number consequences. |
| Controlled mass observable | Extract mass eigenvalues or controlled splittings and the mixing matrix; flavor names are not mass eigenstates. |

## photon

Finite polar and axial loop channels have controlled source transitions in a rare empty-matter sector.

| Test | Required calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Joint energy and momentum response | Identify an unbroken electromagnetic current and populated transverse response with a massless dispersion law that survives refinement. |
| Interactions and distinct species | Identify the two physical helicities and electromagnetic vertices, with longitudinal gauge redundancy and charged infrared records treated explicitly. |
| Controlled mass observable | Bound the invariant mass at zero in the same energy-momentum convention; a finite loop energy gap is not a photon mass. |

## gluon

| Test | Required calculation |
| --- | --- |

| Interactions and distinct species | Derive non-Abelian self-interactions and their coupling, plus the color responses of matter. Eight algebra directions are not eight free observed particles. |
| Controlled mass observable | Control the massless short-distance gauge-field description and infrared gauge-invariant spectrum without assigning a free gluon pole mass. |

## Higgs

The Higgs-conjugate composite supplies a neutral radial scalar with a capacity-uniform total detector bound and a separate nonzero original dynamical response. Total success includes elastic events.

| Test | Required calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Joint energy and momentum response | Measure a gauge-invariant scalar response and establish its populated branch in a demonstrated Higgs phase. |
| Interactions and distinct species | Construct consistent scalar, vector and fermion vertices. The native-number obstruction to one sharp scalar serving every bare Yukawa channel requires an explicit resolution. |
| Controlled mass observable | Extract a scalar resonance and width around the selected physical phase; neither a scalar operator nor a zero one-point function establishes or excludes that phase. |

## Neutral composite hypotheses

Use the six signed conserved-increment labels, calculate populated joint energy/momentum responses, attach physical currents and spin, and test interactions before any neutrino or baryon assignment. Retain the explicit multiple-excitation alias of the seven-state counter.
