# Domains of candidate identity tests

The candidate correspondence preserves the exact internal representation
and the distinct source-excitation and protected-object identifiers.
Three independent calculations test charged elementary representations,
neutral composites and gauge-sector detector channels. Their receipts bind
the same declared source where a common-source statement is made. A
comparison quantum number does not change the Hamiltonian or preparation.

The consumer authenticates the transitive producer hashes and every embedded
result digest. It selects the actual member-zero result by its source ID,
not its position, and compares every rendered summary and source-ID list
with the authenticated producer. Cross-family exclusions retain their
supporting receipt. It retains all eighteen elementary comparison targets and
links repeated flavor hypotheses to one shared source-family calculation.
The six signed neutral composite channels have their own source identity
test; their baryon or lepton interpretation is a separate hypothesis.

The physical confirmation fields are empty because these computations
supply source algebra and detector selection rules, without a physical
Lorentz transformation, particle dispersion or measured mass attachment.
An exclusion of a specified direct assignment does not exclude a different
dressing, symmetry-breaking state or effective dictionary. The ten-node
joint construction retains its existing acceptance conditions.
