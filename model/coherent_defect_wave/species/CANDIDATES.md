# Source objects and particle comparison

The IDs describe objects in the literal finite construction. A comparison
label states a testable hypothesis; it does not convert a source label into a
particle name. The source has one protected regional flux class with five
positions and two gauge representatives per position. Those ten words are
not ten particle species.

| Source ID or family | Confirmed observable content | Particle comparison | Confirmation condition or exclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| `regional-flux-odd` | Boundary Wilson sign -1, one regional defect, five allowed positions, paired link representatives | Protected excitation candidate | Derive its dispersion, rotation law, statistics and complete interaction records in the same reference; no electric or color charge follows from odd Z2 flux alone |
| `wave-triplet-j0..2` | Three identical central-link-blind wave channels; actual controlled response of links to waves | Internal triplet candidate | The three copies have identical spectra. Assigning them three charged leptons with different masses is incompatible with corresponding branches of this clock |
| `wave-doublet-j3..4` | Two identical central-link-sensitive wave channels; reciprocal coherent interaction with the flux | Internal doublet candidate | A two-level internal register is not physical spin or weak chirality. Supply source currents and spatial transformation operators |
| `shared-link-CAR-r` | Antisymmetric wave occupations, one dynamical link register, conserved N and Q5, exact constrained two-wave readout | Finite fermionic excitation architecture | CAR is a declared architecture input; prove physical Spin and relativistic propagation before calling the occupations observed fermions |
| `aux-even-n3=2-n2=0` | (3bar,1,-2/3), dimension3 | Up-quark singlet conjugate representation | The auxiliary representation needs an explicit map to propagating one-fermion fields; no quark mass or confinement follows |
| `aux-even-n3=1-n2=1` | (3,2,1/6), dimension6 | Quark-doublet representation | Supply chiral kinetic operator and nonabelian currents on the same source; color labels alone do not give QCD |
| `aux-even-n3=0-n2=2` | (1,1,1), dimension1 | Charged-lepton singlet conjugate representation | Supply a charged-lepton state, its opposite chirality and an actual mass-generating interaction |
| `aux-even-n3=3-n2=1` | (1,2,-1/2), dimension2 | Lepton-doublet representation | Supply chiral propagation and its current couplings; the doublet contains no measured lepton mass ratio |
| `aux-even-n3=2-n2=2` | (3bar,1,1/3), dimension3 | Down-quark singlet conjugate representation | Supply the joined physical field map, interactions and spectral mass observable |
| `central-link-sign` | Finite Z2 transport character `diag(I3,-I2)` | Gauge structure precursor | It does not identify photon, gluon, W or Z states; continuous local gauge generators, polarizations and their dispersion are absent from this identification |
| `empty-wave-Gauss-plus` | Five independent U_F-fixed vectors within odd regional flux | Matter-vacuum subspace | A unique physical vacuum and a zero-flux reference are not selected by empty occupation |

No row contains a GeV mass. Assigning the auxiliary lepton labels an empirical
mass table would add calibration. The equal spectra of the triplet copies
are an exact restriction of this source, rather than a rounding discrepancy.
Changing the law or adding a justified interaction may break that symmetry;
the replacement requires its own source and clock receipt.
