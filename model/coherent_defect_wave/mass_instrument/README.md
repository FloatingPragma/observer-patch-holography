# Reading particle and hole energies

A finite particle reservoir prepares an excitation of the filled graded-flux
reference. The same Hamiltonian measures its energy and relative pair count.
All thirty-six detector records are retained, including failed preparations
that create a neutral pair. A finite battery accounts for the apparatus work.
The free rest energy is two source units under the declared face-count mass
law. A physical unit reference, interacting mass and Standard Model identity
require independent attachments. See the [proof](PROOF.md), [execution](model.py)
and [exact records](results.json).
