# Strings and screened charge

The same finite interacting source assigns a linear flux energy cost to
a single unscreened charge separated from its fixed compensator. Its low
charged energy bins have uniformly tiny remote single-charge probabilities.
An exact state with a distant matter pair demonstrates why that bound does
not cover dynamical screening. A mobile charged excitation requires spatial
and energy-momentum records beyond the boundary-local line. Finite-regulator
bands and a continuum Coulomb sector have different spectral tests: the
latter can require dressed or inclusive thresholds without a sharp charged
mass pole. See the [proof](PROOF.md), [architecture assessment](COULOMB.md)
and [receipt](results.json).

and `pytest -q model/coherent_defect_wave/chiral_joint/interacting_charge_localization`.
