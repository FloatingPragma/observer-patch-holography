# A controlled atomic reference

The actual onsite Hamiltonian is bounded on every charged and neutral sector.
With unit link penalty, this isolates the product of its local ground
doublets on any declared finite periodic cube. A complete hopping bound
gives an explicit interval with a controlled stationary low-energy band.
Exact elimination also gives positive spin exchange about that atomic
ground manifold. At the original unit hopping strength, a separate quench
experiment has certified spin-dependent link records and complete failures.

The stationary-band interval excludes unit hopping. These finite gaps and
records do not identify a particle mass or a thermodynamic phase. See the
[proof](PROOF.md) and [exact receipt](results.json).
