# Charge moving under the source clock

A localized elementary removal from the actual interacting source moves
charge to neighboring cells under the same Hamiltonian. Six distinct
quantum flux routes have strictly positive arrival probabilities. The
compensating reference stays at the origin. Exact current algebra and
full source bounds include the imperfect physical preparation and every
failure, giving a positive unconditional arrival bound. The same prepared
packet has positive charged energy-bin records in a separate detector
setting. Energy-resolved propagation and a free charged dispersion are
outside this result.

See the [proof](PROOF.md) and [receipt](results.json). Replay with
`python -m model.coherent_defect_wave.chiral_joint.interacting_charged_transport --check`
and `pytest -q model/coherent_defect_wave/chiral_joint/interacting_charged_transport`.
