# Matter-generated link loops

The unchanged periodic lepton/Higgs source generates transitions from blank
quantum links to two closed-loop states. Exact elimination of 67 matter
configurations gives nonzero, energy-dependent loop couplings on a complete
five-state retained space. The original Hamiltonian also gives strictly
positive finite-time probabilities for both loop records, with every other
outcome retained. The singlet loop begins with two actual hops; the doublet
loop also requires two quantum Higgs interactions.

These are finite two-link observations with a retained charged matter state.
A spatial plaquette theory, autonomous gauge dynamics and a physical photon
require separate constructions. See the [proof](PROOF.md) and
[exact receipt](results.json).
