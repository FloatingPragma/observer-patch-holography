\paragraph{Infinite-photon confined spin-half band.}
On the complete finite matter shells, the transverse photon oscillator can
be extended to its full Fock space. The positive form
$H=\sqrt{16^2+B_\infty^2}+N_{\rm m}+N_\gamma$ has two reduced levels below
$17$ and all other levels at least $17$. Projection to the matter/photon
vacuum identifies the low space equivariantly with spin $1/2$; its exact
Casimir is $3/4$ and its energy is a scalar on that space.
For a trial with photon number at most $C$, fourteen additional photon
levels evaluate $B_\infty^{14}\phi$ exactly. The global estimate
\[
0\leq\sqrt{16^2+x}-p_6(x)
 \leq \frac{33x^7}{2048\,16^{13}},\qquad x\geq0,
\]
then gives a vector residual and Temple enclosure without an operator-norm
bound on the infinite field. Three independent finite witnesses enclose the
same infinite-source energy and certify photon tails. The spatial trap,
field coupling and electron calibration are retained inputs; the result
removes the photon cap and does not establish a spatial continuum or an
observed-electron magnetic moment.
