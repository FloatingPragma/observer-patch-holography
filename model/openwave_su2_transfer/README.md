# Positive gauge-field histories

A heat-kernel transfer on the full SU(2) link space gives nonnegative
time-reflected history probabilities and the same continuous Hamiltonian
clock as the pure-gauge source. Executed character-space controls retain
every outcome of their finite measurement and compare the transfer with
the Hamiltonian evolution.

The [proof](PROOF.md) establishes finite-regulator reflection positivity.
Restoration of four-dimensional Euclidean symmetry and a joint continuum
limit require separate estimates.

Run `python -m model.openwave_su2_transfer --check`.
