\paragraph{Native lapse and finite curvature.}
For the complete two-state geometry source, the weak-source derivative of
the actual reference-normalized clock lapse is
$-2\sinh(q)\tanh(qF)/\omega$.
Its linear-profile error is bounded by
$2\sinh(q)(qF)^3/(3\omega)$.
Commuting lattice derivatives give exact linearized Bianchi identities on
the declared metric variation, including every boundary halo. Their
curvature Laplacian differs from the nearest-neighbour Poisson Laplacian by
$(a^2/4)\sum_i\Delta_i^2$; that correction is retained. This identifies a
finite response and error budget, while the physical metric assignment and
nonlinear Einstein limit remain open.
