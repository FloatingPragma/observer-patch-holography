\paragraph{Complete low nuclear bands on finite graphs.}
Exact native colour projectors give local three-quark and six-quark
singlet dimensions20 and50. On an $L$-cell graph their complete locally
colour-singlet six-quark space has dimension
$50L+400\binom{L}{2}$. Its compression is diagonal in the full spin/isospin
and cell-placement basis. The retained coloured complement has an explicit
Casimir floor $C$, and hopping has off-block norm at most $b=6tz$.
If $a=\max(PHP)<C$, every low level lies within
$b^2/(C-a)$ below its compressed value; the next level is at least $C$.
Paths and cycles of two to four cells retain the same fitted parameters,
all excited bands and complete low/high/failure instruments. Both the
spin-one/isospin-zero and spin-zero/isospin-zero channels are rigorously
bound. The additional singlet has no established physical identification,
and these finite bands do not establish the observed nuclear spectrum.
A separate exact multiplicity-one result shows that a
scalar deformation within the colocated native nucleon irrep cannot change
its axial matrix element $5/3$; that requires a different state or current
construction.
