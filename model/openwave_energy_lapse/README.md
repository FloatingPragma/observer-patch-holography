# Universal energy response on a finite geometry

A declared lapse multiplies the complete ground-referenced native Hamiltonian.
Every excitation then has the same energy-to-geometry response. Positive
elastic gluing gives attractive mutual energies, with rational bounds on the
actual finite geometry minimum. The stiffness, preparation service and lapse
coupling are explicit inputs; physical Newton strength and moving bodies
require a separate identification. The [proof](PROOF.md) and [receipt](results.json)
state the full domain.

Run `python3 -m model.openwave_energy_lapse --write` or `--check`.
