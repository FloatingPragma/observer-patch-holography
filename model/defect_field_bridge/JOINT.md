# One complete repair, field and clock experiment

The active field is the full four-mode CAR space of two sites carrying the
actual internal component `(27,78)`, with charges `(+1,-1)` and calibrated
mass `0.000487788762347831 GeV`. The source prepares one positive active
particle in a spatial superposition entangled with a which-path reference.
A fixed negative particle at an untouched third site neutralizes it. All
128 addresses of the seven-bit preparation are executed and retained,
including the complete spectator space and the reference columns.

Each event performs the complete source-implemented C or E repair. The
primary policy holds collisions coherently within its existing zero-current
record. The separately flagged collision hold is retained as a control.
The observed signed current sets the common
duration `tau*(1+j/4)`. The field then executes these source words in order:

1. The corrected quasiparticle-to-bare chart on both sites.
2. The two routed bare hopping terms `(+i/2)c0†c2+h.c.` and
   `(-i/2)c1†c3+h.c.`, including every FSWAP sign.
3. The inverse chart, followed by the channel-correct Higgs-phase pair gate
   on each site.

The phase correction in the chart matters: diagonalizing the static mass
alone does not align the driven Higgs term. All sixteen field-input columns
are source-executed and compared with an independent CAR Hamiltonian
product and analytic chart. Electric charge and fermion parity are checked.
This is the declared ordered product, not the exponential of its summed
generators and not an execution of the entire 1080-mode theory.
Links from the two active sites to the other native ports are omitted in
this bounded experiment. The active block is not asserted to be an
invariant subspace of the full twelve-site propagation.

The experiment repeats the event. The second repair therefore sees actual
collisions generated by the first field evolution. Both field and the two
faithful source clock hands share the same duration multiplier. The clock
is prepared once, evolves through both events without reset, and has all
four final outcomes and both purifiers retained. Its record-only inverse
encloses the total common action time without receiving the programmed
duration or any matter energy.
The rational inverse uses an explicit `1e-9` probability grid and increases
each binary marginal's error bound by `1e-9` for that rounding. Full quantum
outputs and their raw probabilities remain unchanged in the archive.

Each law and collision policy retains all `32*32*4=4096` native-history and
clock outcomes, totaling 16384. Collision population is measured by its
field projector before the second event; it does not require a new flag.
The final unread field states of the two complete policies are compared.
On this odd-active-parity witness, E already records binary odd endpoint
occupancy separately from a nonbinary hold, so changing the extra collision
flag need not change its unread state. C can retain the corresponding
one/three-particle coherence in its common zero-current outcome. The
combined operator effect is checked on the full 64-dimensional field/clock
input algebra, and the complete spectator/reference/purifier marginal is
unchanged after summing all records. No successful branch is normalized and
no zero, collision, padding or small floating output is removed. All sixteen
active states remain, including the three-particle states created from the
initial one-particle active field.

The compact archive stores the complete preparation, native Kraus matrices,
three field unitaries, nine clock instruments and all joint probabilities.
These factors reconstruct every complex final amplitude; repeated history
tensors are not stored. Resource records give the preparation once, each
native event, the current-selected field words, and each complete clock
word. Proper-code failure slots remain counted with zero ideal probability.
The error admissions are numerical source checks, not outward-rounded or
hardware guarantees. Complete field-history isometries are compared to the
ideal CAR product on every input column. Combining that bound with the
complete clock-instrument and preparation bounds gives an explicit bound
on the entire output vector and all-outcome total variation, without a
factor proportional to the number of histories. This finite joining witness does not itself establish
the full physical identification of the theory's particles.
