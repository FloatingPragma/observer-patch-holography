# Conserved sectors with a positive excitation threshold

Three pairs of physical occupation sectors have full-space energy floors
strictly above the unchanged source's ground. Two pairs have odd fermion
parity. Exact gauge-singlet vectors and their interacting variational
dressings bound each sector's lowest excitation. A complete preparation
retains the displaced source and an opposite sector in physical memory;
its energy clock has original excited-window probability at least 255/512.
The [proof](PROOF.md) states the actual source and error domain. Physical
momentum and dispersion are required before calling these energies masses.

Run `python -m model.particle_primary_sector_threshold --write` and
`python -m pytest -W error model/particle_primary_sector_threshold`.
