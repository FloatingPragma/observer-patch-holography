# Spatial Haar blocking and its retained source clock

This construction changes the spatial graph. The coarse cube has twelve
links and six faces. Subdivide every coordinate interval into two: the fine
cube has 27 vertices, 54 links and 36 elementary faces. No representation
cutoff or finite-group replacement is used.

## An isometric gauge-covariant embedding

Each coarse link is the ordered product of its two fine links. Product Haar
measure makes these twelve products independent Haar variables. Every other
fine variable is integrated with normalized Haar measure, including the
redundant coordinate along each subdivided link. Thus pullback is an
isometry J and its adjoint is conditional Haar integration. Internal gauge
transformations cancel in each link product; all remaining local gauge
transformations act as the coarse endpoint transformations. The physical
Gauss spaces are therefore respected.

The Casimir acts equally on either member of a product link. Consequently

`J* sum_fine C_e² J = 2 sum_coarse C_e²`.

Every elementary fine face has an independent link not used by a coarse
boundary path. Its fundamental Haar integral vanishes, giving

`J* sum_36 W_fine J = 0`.

At physical spacing ratio one half, preserving the compressed electric
coefficient requires `x_fine=x_coarse/4`, with `x=g²`. This does not match
the magnetic interaction and is not a running-coupling derivation. The
source time has not been rescaled to hide that difference.

## Exact induced interaction

Consider the positive magnetic Euclidean factor, with its irrelevant scalar
factor removed:

`K_kappa=E[exp(kappa sum_36 W_fine) | coarse links]`.

This is a conditional expectation of a strictly positive bounded function,
so it remains positive and gauge invariant. It is a mathematical blocking
of the magnetic factor, not an autonomous full-Hamiltonian evolution or
an asserted observer intervention without work.

The four fine faces tiling a coarse face are the unique four-face surface
with its subdivided coarse boundary. The exact program enumerates every
subset of at most four of the 36 fine faces and checks its edge parity.
Every smaller subset fails the coarse-boundary parity test. Repeated faces
reduce this parity support, so they cannot produce that boundary either.
Fundamental Haar convolution on the disk gives

`E[product_four W_fine | coarse links]=W_coarse/64`.

An independent signed delta/epsilon contraction integrates the four fine
normalized traces against the coarse character. The exact integral is
`1/128`; since `W=chi/2` and the character has Haar norm one, this independently
verifies the coefficient `1/64` for every coarse face. The fourth Taylor
factorial cancels the permutations of the four distinct faces.

Up to degree four, no other nonconstant coarse invariant can occur. A
nonconstant Gauss network on the coarse cube contains a cycle of at least
four edges, hence at least eight fine boundary edges. Four insertions can
produce that boundary only by the enumerated coarse-face tiling. A larger
representation or multiple coarse cycles requires more insertions.

The constant second moment is `36/4=9`. In the fourth moment, a single
face contributes `E W^4=1/8`; two distinct faces each squared contribute
`1/16`. These facts follow by integrating their private Haar links.
No set of four distinct elementary faces is a closed surface. Thus

`E(sum W)^4=36/8+6 binom(36,2)/16=963/4`.

It follows that the exact conditional expansion is

`K_kappa=1+(9/2)kappa²+(321/32)kappa⁴
 +(kappa⁴/64) sum_six W_coarse+R_kappa`.

The central fine-link sign choice
`s_x=1`, `s_y=(-1)^x`, `s_z=(-1)^(x+y)` flips all 36 elementary faces and
preserves every two-link coarse product. Haar invariance therefore makes
the entire conditional function even in kappa. The fifth-order term is
zero. Since `|sum W|<=36`, Taylor's remainder gives the uniform bound

`||R_kappa||_infinity <= exp(36|kappa|)(36|kappa|)^6/720`.

The coefficient of a coarse W has error at most twice this bound: use
`chi=2W`, character norm one and `||chi||_1<=1`. Rational exponential bounds
and outward rounding enclose the coefficient. Declared steps `10^-5` and
`10^-6` give positive lower bounds. The same estimate at `10^-4` is
retained as an inconclusive control, without claiming the coefficient is
actually negative.

This is an executed nonzero interaction generated by actual spatial
Haar blocking. Taking the logarithm, composing with the electric transfer,
