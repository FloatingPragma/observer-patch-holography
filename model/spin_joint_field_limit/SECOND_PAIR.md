# Two canonical pairs on the specified positive-field joint family

## 1. Source, family and the mixed momentum commutator

This argument uses the joint family in this package, not an interchange
of limits in `spin_canonical_field`. Set

    p=n^-2,  B=J n^-5,  n>=2,
    H_B=J sum_<xy>(S_x.S_y-1/4)-B sum_x epsilon_x S_x^1.

The reference omega_B is the positive-field infinite-volume state supplied
by `spin_fixed_field_locality`; its local marginals have that package's
explicit finite-source approximation. The uniform gap B/8 passes to the
local Poincare inequality by taking the finite-volume ground inequality.
The family retains this H_B clock throughout the argument.

Use the four actual source fields

    Q3(F)=p² sum epsilon_x F(px) S_x^3,
    P2(G)=-p sum G(px) S_x^2,
    Q2(F)=p² sum epsilon_x F(px) S_x^2,
    P3(G)=+p sum G(px) S_x^3.                                (1)

The canonical pairs are (Q3,P2) and (Q2,P3). The second pair is constructed,
not appended by a label. At finite p its mixed momentum commutator is

    [P2(G),P3(H)]=-i C_p(GH),
    C_p(h)=p² sum h(px) S_x^1.                              (2)

The norm of C_p can grow as 1/p. Vacuum convergence alone would not make
(2) vanish on a full time-history representation. Sections3-5 supply the
additional strong phase estimates without assuming the second CCR pair.

All tests below are real smooth functions supported in [-R,R]^3, R>=1.
Write N0=(2R+1)^3, f0=sup|f| and f1=max_i sup|partial_i f|; for h also
write h2=sup|partial_1² h|. A support has at most N0 p^-3 sites, and at
most6N0 p^-3 edges meet it. An edge difference of p^k f(px) is at most
p^(k+1)f1. Constants can be enlarged to a common support box for products.

## 2. The gap controls the longitudinal vacuum fluctuation

C_p commutes with the selection field. The exact source double commutator
and the transverse bond norm1/2 give

    q_B(C_p Omega)<=J/4 sum_<xy>(p²h(px)-p²h(py))²
                    <=(3/2)J N0 h1² p³,
    Var_B(C_p)<=12 J N0 h1² p³/B=12 N0 h1²/n.                (3)

The beta translation symmetry implies omega_B(S_x^1)=epsilon_x m_B,
with |m_B|<=1/2. It follows from covariance of the fixed-B source and
uniqueness of its open-exhaustion limit. For the alternating test sum,
shifting the lattice by e1 twice gives the exact identity

    sum epsilon_x h(px)
       =(1/4) sum epsilon_x [h(px)-2h(px+pe1)+h(px+2pe1)].

The three translated supports and the second derivative bound imply

    |omega_B(C_p(h))|<=3 N0 h2 p/8.                         (4)

Equations(3)-(4) make C_p Omega tend to zero. Beta is used only for
this alternating mean. It is not the common physical translation of
all four fields; see Section7.

## 3. An auxiliary transverse phase with a noncircular history proof

For a=2,3 define a smaller actual source rotation

    A_p^a(f)=p³ sum f(px) S_x^a,  ||A_p^a||<=N0 f0/2.        (5)

The exact residual internal U1 makes its mean zero. The exchange and
selection terms in its double commutator have the respective norm bounds
3J N0 f1² p^5 and B N0 f0² p³/2. Therefore

    q_B(A_p^a Omega)<=(3/2)J N0 f1² p^5+B N0 f0² p³/4,
    ||A_p^a Omega||²<=12N0 f1²/n^5+2N0 f0²/n^6.             (6)

These statements use the positive-field gap; they do not assume a
transverse canonical covariance or the extra CCR pair.

Let a preliminary history consist of a fixed finite number of the four
source Weyl pulses in (1), their time transports at times T/(Jp), and
phases generated by any fixed finite list of A_p and C_p profiles.
The source energy-kick inequality gives all these vectors energy at
most E_* Jp, with E_* independent of n. The four main field kicks are
bounded by the parent construction, including its selection-field terms.
The two auxiliary kicks obey the explicit estimates here. No canonical
commutation relation is needed for this energy assertion.

The A_p phase commutators with Q and P pulses are bounded in operator
norm by the corresponding amplitudes times

    ||[A_p^a(f),Q_b(F)]||<=N0 f0 F0 p²/2,
    ||[A_p^a(f),P_b(G)]||<=N0 f0 G0 p/2.                    (7)

Its commutator with a C phase is O(p²), and with another A phase is
O(p³), by the same onsite calculation. Their bounds hold on every input.

For any source phase V=exp(isA), write its energy-kick constant as
sqrt(q_B(V psi))<=sqrt(q_B(psi))+|s| kappa sqrt(Jp).
The established positive-generator time comparison gives, on the
specified energy-bounded history vectors,

    ||[V,exp(-iT(H_B-E_B)/(Jp))]psi||²
       <=4|T||s| kappa sqrt(E_*)+10|T|s² kappa².             (8)

For A=A_p^a the sufficient value is

    kappa_A²=(3/2)N0 f1²/n^8+N0 f0²/(4n^9),                (9)

so the time-commutation norm in (8) tends to zero. Move an A phase
through the preliminary history using (7)-(9). At the vacuum use (6).
This proves

    (exp(is A_p^a(f))-I) W_p Omega_B ->0                    (10)

for every such fixed history, including histories containing C phases.
The proof does not use (2) or its asserted vanishing.

## 4. Transport of the longitudinal phase

The C_p phase commutator with a Q pulse is O(p) in operator norm.
With another C phase it is zero; with an A phase it is O(p²).
Its energy kick has

    kappa_C²=(3/2)N0 h1² p²,                                (11)

so its time-commutation norm in (8) tends to zero as well.
The remaining equal-time crossing is a C phase and a P pulse.
For onsite angles a=s p²h and b=t pG, the exact spin rotation estimate

    ||e^(iaS1)e^(ibSa)e^(-iaS1)e^(-ibSa)-e^(-ab[S1,Sa])||
       <=(|a|²|b|+|a||b|²)/4                               (12)

gives, after summing sites, an operator-norm remainder at most

    (N0/4)[s²|t| h0²G0 p²+|s|t² h0G0² p].                 (13)

The leading factor is a phase of A_p^b(hG), with its actual sign fixed
by the spin commutator and pulse convention. It tends to the identity
on every preliminary history by (10). Thus the C phase can also pass
a P pulse strongly on those histories. After all crossings, (3)-(4)
control its action on the vacuum. Consequently

    (exp(is C_p(h))-I) W_p Omega_B ->0.                    (14)

No uniformly bounded norm for C_p, and no strong convergence of the
unbounded C_p itself on general history vectors, is asserted or needed.
The sequence A phase -> C phase is essential to avoid circularity.

For a finite-source ground whose marginal on the support of h is within
half-trace distance delta_n of omega_B, the mean in (4) changes by at most
N0 h0 delta_n/p. The variance estimate (3) holds directly in that finite
ground with the same gap. Taking delta_n=o(p), as the parent's rational
box planner permits, retains the vacuum conclusion. Bounded complete
histories use their separately retained locality and preparation errors.

## 5. The mixed momentum Weyl relation

For single-site X=iaS2 and Y=ibS3, |a|+|b|<=1, Taylor expansion through
cubic order gives

    e^X e^Y e^-X e^-Y
      = exp(-iabS1) exp(-ia²bS3/2) exp(+iab²S2/2)+E,
    ||E||<=2(|a|+|b|)^4.                                  (15)

Here is an explicit remainder proof. Put C=[X,Y] and
D=[X+Y,C]/2. The fourth derivative of
exp(uX)exp(uY)exp(-uX)exp(-uY) has norm at most q^4,
q=|a|+|b|. For exp(u²C)exp(u³D), use ||C||<=q²/8 and
||D||<=q³/16. Differentiating the two factors four times and applying
the product rule bounds its fourth derivative by4q^4 for0<=u<=1.
Their derivatives through order three agree at zero, so Taylor's
integral remainder is at most5q^4/24. Splitting the two cubic spin
components of D costs at most |a³b³|/16. These bounds imply (15).
The producer verifies the derivative polynomials and the cubic identity.

In (15) set a=-s pG(px), b=t pH(px). Site factorization gives

    e^(isP2(G)) e^(itP3(H)) e^(-isP2(G)) e^(-itP3(H))
      = exp(+ist C_p(GH))
        exp(-is²t A_p^3(G²H)/2)
        exp(-ist² A_p^2(GH²)/2)+E_p,
    ||E_p||<=2N0(|s|G0+|t|H0)^4 p.                         (16)

For every fixed amplitude/test choice, the condition in (15) holds
for all sufficiently large n. Equations(10), (14) and (16) prove that
the mixed momentum Weyl group commutator tends strongly to identity on
every fixed preliminary history.

The Q3/Q2 commutator has operator norm O(p). Q3/P3 and Q2/P2 commute
exactly. The two canonical Q/P pairs have the same macroscopic source
phase M_p(FG)=p³ sum epsilon_x F(px)G(px) S_x^1. Its gap/mean and
history estimates in the parent construction apply to all four fields:
its equal-time commutators are norm-small and its energy kick is small.
If m_* is the selected positive order limit, each pair therefore has
symplectic coefficient m_* integral FG, and every cross pair commutes.
These are operator Weyl relations on the full bounded-history GNS space,
not only vacuum two-point identities.

## 6. Regularity and the complete internal circle action

The preliminary history construction and (10)-(16) did not assume
regularity of P3. With the full equal-time Weyl relations proved, the
parent's uniform transverse vacuum bounds and energy-kick estimates
supply regularity by the same time-crossing argument as
`spin_canonical_field`, Section6. Small field phases pass every Weyl
factor with a small scalar symplectic phase and pass time transports
using (8). Their vacuum differences are controlled by the source
second moments. This proves strong continuity on all history vectors.

At each finite source, the residual internal rotation exp(i theta sum S1)
commutes with H_B and preserves its unique ground state. Its action is

    (Q3,Q2) -> (cos(theta)Q3+sin(theta)Q2,
                -sin(theta)Q3+cos(theta)Q2),
    (P2,P3) -> (cos(theta)P2+sin(theta)P3,
                -sin(theta)P2+cos(theta)P3).                (17)

The source exponential of a rotated momentum is identified explicitly,
rather than by an unbounded-generator assumption. The onsite Zassenhaus
expansion is

    exp(X+Y)=exp(X) exp(Y) exp(-[X,Y]/2)
       exp([X,[X,Y]]/6+[Y,[X,Y]]/3)+O((|a|+|b|)^4),         (18)

for X=iaS2, Y=ibS3 and |a|+|b|<=1. Taylor's integral remainder gives,
for example, the uniform constant100 in the final term. The producer
checks the cubic coefficients and the fourth-derivative norm polynomial.
On the source scaling, the extra quadratic and cubic factors in (18)
are precisely C and A phases and the summed remainder is O(p).
Thus (10) and (14) identify the source rotated exponential with the
product of the two commuting limiting momentum Weyl operators.
The corresponding configuration splitting has an O(p) norm error.

Source state invariance, test continuity
and regularity implement (17) as a strongly continuous internal U1 on
the full time-history representation, fixing the vacuum and commuting
with the same time generator. Its2pi action is identity on the source
observable algebra, hence on the cyclic representation. This is an
internal symmetry; it assigns no Lorentz spin or physical gauge group.

## 7. Spatial and state-family boundaries

Use even microscopic translations for all four components. They preserve
the field-selected source and commute with its internal U1. Their scaled
steps2p tend to zero, so the parent's test-continuity argument gives common
continuous translations in the field limit. The odd beta translation
contains an internal pi rotation about axis2 and acts differently on
Q2/P3. It cannot be substituted for this common translation action.

These results apply to the explicit joint positive-field family and its
same-source finite approximants. They do not identify that full history
state with the original prescription that removes B before field scaling.
They do not prove a sharp dispersion branch, scattering, physical spin,
a nonabelian current attachment or a measured particle mass.
