# Stable dynamical attraction and complete-input charge work

Keep the complete native nuclear action, electron conversion and Gauss-cycle
field. For every four consecutive distinct sites let
O_b=product_x 1_(N_x=3). It is a gauge-invariant bounded projector. Attach a
neutral harmonic field with full carrier L²(R), canonical q_b,p_b, and

    H = H_EM + sum_b [omega/2 (p_b²+q_b²-1) + g q_b O_b].

The declared values are omega=1/16 from the existing electric coefficient
and g=1/8 from the existing native hopping coefficient. No helion or alpha
target selects either coefficient. These choices extend the source; they
are not asserted to follow from its electromagnetic action.

The four-site projector vanishes identically on total quark number below12.
It therefore changes neither the electron/two-cell light calibration domain
nor the N=9 triton and helion sources. It is a local occupancy gate, with no
species-specific fitted negative energy. For the three/four-cell path there
are zero/one four-site blocks respectively.

Write a=(q+ip)/sqrt(2). Since O acts on matter and commutes with a,

    omega a* a+g q O
      = omega(a*+g O/(sqrt(2)omega))(a+g O/(sqrt(2)omega))
        - g² O²/(2omega).

Thus H>=H_EM-c sum_b O_b>=H_EM-c number_of_blocks, where c=g²/(2omega).
The bound is extensive. Linear oscillator coupling to bounded matter is
infinitesimally form-bounded with respect to the oscillator quadratic form;
the closed semibounded form defines the full self-adjoint Hamiltonian.
All oscillator levels and field fluctuations are included. The native
hopping does not commute with O: a literal allowed hop sends the occupation
pattern(3,3,3,3) to(2,4,3,3). Completing the square therefore does not replace
the full dynamics by a static potential or shift all exact excited levels.

On a native quartet trial each cell has exactly three quarks, hence O=1.
The normalized coherent state with q mean-g/omega has oscillator energy+c
and interaction energy-2c. Its total contribution is-c. Tensoring this exact
full-oscillator trial with every inherited fixed-charge quartet variational
state subtracts c per block from its variational energy. Combine those
upper bounds with the full-operator lower bound. All native matter and
integer cycle-flux complements remain present. The resulting ground
enclosures retain the full oscillator complement, with no oscillator cutoff.
Finite Fock diagonalizations are independent diagnostics only.

The alpha lowering allowed by the declared field is c times the unchanged
electron conversion. If the inherited alpha lower bound exceeds the held-out

g²>=2omega Delta/unit. This necessary bound is not used to choose g and is
not sufficient for a match. Helion is unaffected and its upper bound excludes

identity is U(4L)-invariant even though the Hamiltonian need not have this

    E[dGamma(T)²] = (N Tr(T)/(4L))²
