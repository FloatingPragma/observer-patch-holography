\paragraph{Constrained scalar, matter and radiation transfer.}
The retained canonical capacity scalar has an explicit linear attachment
to Einstein gravity, ideal dust and ideal radiation. With $N=\log a$ and
$h=H_N/H$, the full metric--fluid--scalar equations give the exact Hamiltonian
constraint identity $C_N=-(3+2h)C$ and the spatial Einstein trace equation.
The executable evolves the metric, rather than freezing it, and transports a
deliberately nonzero constraint by the same homogeneous law. Regular growing
initial data, an earlier-start check, tolerance refinement and an independent
Einstein--de Sitter solution control the numerical transfer. For the inherited
background inputs, present comoving matter transfer relative to a
same-density cosmological-constant control is approximately
$0.97585,0.97001,0.91494,0.90822$ at $k/H_0=0.1,1,10,100$.
These are conditional linear-model differences, not observed matches or a
CMB fit; the model supplies no native scalar or primordial state and omits
free-streaming species and recombination. The constraint identities are exact, while the ODE
outputs have numerical convergence diagnostics rather than interval error
certificates. Source: \texttt{model/openwave\_capacity\_transfer}.
