# Regional charge, equal splitting and the physical criterion

Let B be the integer incidence matrix of the declared native links and let
G=BE-rho. A complete native charged hop changes both BE and rho by the
same integer boundary. The joint coherent walk, electric diagonal phase
and bounded-link overflow identity therefore preserve every Gauss sector.
For any set of vertices R, summing G=0 over R cancels internal edges and
gives Q_R=sum_R rho=sum_boundary_R E. The source-charge and electric-flux
operators are diagonal in the complete joint basis, so this is an operator
identity with a joint sharp instrument, including on arbitrary quantum
references. It is not an identity between expectations alone.

Every eigenvalue of Q_R/q_star is an integer. If an odd charge q were two
equal sharp charges a and b, then a=b and a+b=q would imply 2a=q, a
contradiction in the integers. An expectation value q/2 in a delocalized
state does not provide such a sharp split. The even control q=2 permits
1+1 and must be retained. The numerical experiment executes all twelve
single-port cuts and four larger cuts after zero, one and two actual joint
steps, with the electric phase enabled and no intermediate readout. Every
output weight is retained; full inverse evolution restores the input.
The integer operator argument applies to all cuts and link-window sizes
with the declared overflow identity, beyond these executed preparations.

Adding a closed integer link chain changes no boundary charge. This is the
relative-chain meaning of topological protection in this construction.
It neither defines a continuum soliton winding nor identifies its carrier
with the observed electron. A physical charge unit q_star is declared.
Choosing an architecture, its registers, connectivity and services is
permitted; uniqueness or a priori inevitability of that hardware is not a
premise of this result. Matching the physical electron and the submitted
platform process remains a separate test of its observable consequences.
