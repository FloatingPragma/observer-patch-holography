# A pure SU(2) gauge source

The full compact-group link space on a three-dimensional cube has a unique
vacuum and a strictly positive finite-volume gap. At the declared coupling,
the eight-vertex cube has gap between 10.5 and 21.788 source energy units.
Its local plaquette curvature has positive vacuum variance and finite excitation
energy. These bounds include every link representation. They require no
finite-spin truncation or matter-sector substitution.

The [proof](PROOF.md) and [receipt](results.json) retain the volume and spacing
dependence. They supply no four-dimensional continuum mass gap.
