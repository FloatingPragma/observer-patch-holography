On the full gauge-invariant Haar link space of an open three-dimensional
\(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) cube, the positive Hamiltonian
\(H=\alpha\sum_l C_l+\beta\sum_p(1-\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}U_p/2)\)
has a unique vacuum. The complete electric physical gap is \(3\alpha\).
At \(g^2=8\), spacing one and eight vertices, min--max and the constant-state
residual give an interacting vacuum gap between \(21/2\) and \(1024/47\)
in source units. A local plaquette has vacuum variance at least \(47/256\)
and unnormalized centered-vector energy form at most \(4\). Every link representation is
retained. The lower bound deteriorates with volume and both energy bounds
scale as the inverse spacing; no four-dimensional continuum mass gap follows.
