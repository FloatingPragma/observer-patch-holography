# Baryon energies with the full weak contact

The native neutron preparation spreads across several interaction-dressed
energy lines. The proton preparation has a single line carrying more than
99.9% of its spectral weight. Complete finite spectra retain the contact,
filled-reference shifts, virtual charged channels and preparation failures.
The same electron unit gives positive line-dependent charged-energy budgets.
These finite measurements do not establish relativistic physical poles or
a continuum beta endpoint.
