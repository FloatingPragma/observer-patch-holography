infinite chain. Two declared local degree counterterms preserve the entire
two-cell action and calibrate the bulk neutron/proton energies and native
axial current. The neutron enclosure is approximately
0.939565387–0.939565457 GeV; the axial enclosure is 1.274757–1.275843.

spectral records retained. A three-dimensional particle interpretation requires
additional results.
