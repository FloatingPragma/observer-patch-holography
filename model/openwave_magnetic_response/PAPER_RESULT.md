# Magnetic spin response of the native kinetic family

The complete sixteen-row native kinetic matrices obey the Clifford
relations. For a declared Peierls gauge extension with scalar mass `m`,
their finite Hamiltonian satisfies
`H^2=m^2+Pi_x^2+Pi_y^2+Sigma_z i[Pi_x,Pi_y]`, where
`Sigma_z=-i gamma_x gamma_y` and `S_z=Sigma_z/2`. Thus a smooth uniform
external field gives the nonrelativistic magnetic term `q B S_z/m`,
corresponding to tree-level `g=2`, without an inserted Pauli coefficient.
Exact internal projectors retain all eight valley labels and reduce the
largest 266256-row example to two 16641-row spatial operators. Tapered
finite-packet energy responses give coefficients `1.95802`, `1.98942`
and `1.99734` at spacings `1`, `1/2` and `1/4`; coherent spin-phase
measurements agree, and an explicit square-root truncation bound is
retained. The gauge extension, mass and external field are supplied.
No self-sourced dipole, physical electron identification or radiative
anomalous moment is inferred from this external-field calculation.
