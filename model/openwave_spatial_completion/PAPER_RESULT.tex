\paragraph{Spatial completion and trap-release threshold.}
On the full spatial and photon carrier, the declared positive source
$H_\Omega=\sqrt{16^2+B^2}+\Omega N_{\rm m}+N_\gamma$ has an exact isolated
spin-half doublet whenever
$\min(\Omega,1)>v_0=3/64+(3/8192)(5/7)^{5/2}<1/16$.
Complete-shell core and form convergence, together with oscillator
coercivity, give convergence of fixed low energies without equating a
truncated square root with a compressed infinite operator.
Gaussian width-$L$ trials give
\[
E_\Omega\leq16+\frac3{64L^2}
 +\frac{3L^2}{8192(1+2L^2/5)^{5/2}}
 +\frac{3\Omega}{4}(L^2+L^{-2}-2).
\]
Hence trap release has threshold $16$, with no normalizable state attaining
it. This excludes a demonstrated self-bound localized ground, not an ordinary
free-particle mass threshold. The retained electron unit assigns that
threshold approximately $0.000509529040252\,\mathrm{GeV}$, conditionally.
