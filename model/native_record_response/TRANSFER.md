# Positive transfer from reflected native records

The default weighted H66 source has a positive Euclidean transfer
reconstruction on a 66-dimensional Hilbert space generated by its actual
scalar record histories. The reflection must reverse each raw record as
well as its position in the history. Treating incoming contrasts as
reflection-even gives an exact negative form.

The domain is the stationary population law and complex-valued cylinder
functions of the complete raw records. Positivity of this population
transfer is not a claim about the full quantum superoperator or
operator-valued quantum histories. Native quantum working states and
untouched references retain the separate scope proved in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md).

## The same source determines a positive transfer

Use the actual column-stochastic matrix `P`, its stationary vector `pi`, and
`Pi=diag(pi)` from `response.py`. Detailed balance is
`P Pi=Pi P^T`. On functions of the 66 configurations use

```text
<f,g>_pi = sum_x pi_x conjugate(f_x) g_x,
Q=P^T,                  Q^dagger_pi=Q.
```

This is a finite complex Hilbert space. The standard-coordinate transfer is

```text
S=Pi^(1/2) Q Pi^(-1/2)=Pi^(-1/2) P Pi^(1/2).
```

Its ground vector is `Omega=sqrt(pi)`, corresponding to the constant
function one. Neither ket is asserted to be a coherent preparation on the
native working carrier.

The exact source minimum is `min_x P_xx=791/990`. For any complex function,
detailed balance gives the Dirichlet identity

```text
<f,(I-Q)f>_pi
 = (1/2) sum_(x,y) pi_x P_(y,x) |f_x-f_y|^2
 <= 2 max_x(1-P_xx) <f,f>_pi.
```

The first expression is nonnegative. Therefore

```text
(296/495) I <= Q <= I   in L2(pi),
(296/495) I <= S <= I   in standard coordinates.
```

All seams have positive weight and the two-token graph is connected, so
the eigenvalue one is simple. The positive logarithm
`H=-log S` is thus uniquely determined, nonnegative, and has a unique
ground direction. Its 65 nonzero eigenvalues are `-log(lambda_j)`, where
the exact source response proves the 65 simple factors `0<lambda_j<1`.
No coefficient or target spectrum is inserted.

The logarithm has an exact rational approximation in function coordinates.
With `r=199/495`,

```text
H_m(Q)=sum_(j=1)^m (I-Q)^j/j,
0 <= -log Q-H_m(Q) <= r^(m+1)/((m+1)(1-r)) I.
```

The bound follows from the positive logarithm series and the spectral
theorem. It uses the `L2(pi)` operator norm, or the unitarily equivalent
standard norm. Ordinary unweighted Euclidean norms on population columns
are a different convention.


The certificate checks the exact adjoint partial map and this weighted

## Reflection positivity for every scalar record cylinder

Let `F` be any complex function of finitely many future records. Summing
its values against all complete words defines a backward insertion `L_F`.
Its conditional expectation on the initial configuration is
`f_F=L_F 1`. Unspecified events are summed with their full native channel.
Different cylinder lengths can be padded with such unobserved events.

Reflect the past word using the raw-label involution. If `n>=0` complete
unobserved attempts separate the past and future, direct composition gives

```text
E_pi[conjugate(F_i(reflected past)) F_j(future)]
 = <f_(F_i), Q^n f_(F_j)>_pi.
```

Indeed the chronological backward product is
`L_(F_i)^dagger_pi Q^n L_(F_j)`, with the complex conjugation included by
the adjoint. Its expectation between constant ground functions is the
displayed expression. Equivalently, condition on the two configurations
at the reflection cut and use weighted marked-history reversal.
This argument includes arbitrary cylinder functions, all raw record
addresses and every history length; it is not an inference from selected
two-point tests. For complex coefficients `c_i` its quadratic form is

```text
<sum_i c_i f_(F_i), Q^n sum_j c_j f_(F_j)>_pi >= 0.
```

For `n=0` this is the squared Hilbert norm; for `n=1` it is bond reflection
positivity. General nonnegative integer `n` follows from the positive
transfer. The spectral positivity argument is the finite-mode algebra of
[`SourceLaw.ReflectionPositivity`](../../Lean/SourceLaw/ReflectionPositivity.lean).
The marked-history factorization and its weighted source attachment are
proved here in prose with exact executable controls, not an additional
Lean formalization or an application of a Gaussian-measure theorem.

The quotient by zero reflection norm is exactly 66-dimensional. The
constant cylinder gives the constant function. The conditional expectation

`Q^k d_e`. The existing exact rank certificate for
`[1^T;D;DP;...;DP^12]` is 66, so these record-cylinder functions span
every function on the source configuration set. Thus the Hilbert space is
generated by observable record statistics; its construction does not



where `u_j` are the nonstationary orthonormal eigenvectors of `S`.

## Quantum and field boundary

calculus defines `exp(-s H)` for real `s>=0` and `exp(-it H)` for real `t`,
but it does not make each fractional power a native stochastic channel

The reconstructed Hilbert space is distinct from the quantum working

records, rather than additional single-shot measurement effects.
