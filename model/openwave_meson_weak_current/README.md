# Charged-current conversion of a bound meson

The native quark and lepton currents convert a bound heavy-family meson into
a lighter bound meson and a charged-lepton/neutral-hole pair. Exact colour,
spin and charge reductions retain every output and preparation failure.
A declared source-resonant box gives about 99.49% emission for the prepared
state; the original probability includes its approximately 24.83%
preparation success. The port pulse, coupling and resonance are architectural
choices, without a physical kaon mass or lifetime fit. See [the proof](PROOF.md)
and [the complete records](results.json).

Run `python -m model.openwave_meson_weak_current --check`.
