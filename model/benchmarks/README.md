# Benchmarks

A benchmark is a construction whose observers show clock, wave or particle phenomenology on an architecture that
its makers supply instead of the fixed echosahedral federation, for example a periodic lattice with directions of
its own. The [scorecard](../evaluation/SCORECARD.md) scores laws on that fixed federation and counts such an
architecture as hardcoding, so a benchmark is no
candidate law and the evaluation does not score it. It shows what a phenomenon looks like when the architecture is
given, which a candidate on the federation can be compared with.

| Benchmark | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [eight_slot_torus](eight_slot_torus/README.md) | a walk on a supplied four-dimensional torus with eight slots per address: travelling clocks, signals, a pair formed in an encounter, its matter waves, six-station ranging, transverse pulse clocks and clocks formed by ordinary capture |
