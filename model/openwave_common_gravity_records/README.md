on all 343 sites of a three-dimensional cube. Its source-on packet arrives
later and shifts toward the source. The clocks, both geometry coordinates,
field vacuum, failed arrivals and detector work are retained in the complete
joint experiment. No source strength is fitted separately to the clocks
and the packet.

metric response. Finite-strength clock ratios differ; a quantitative common
weak-probe limit is proved. These records do not establish physical Newton
strength or an Einstein continuum.
