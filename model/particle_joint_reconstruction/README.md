# Common matter, field and geometry records

The [official construction](../SPEC.md) generates one native matter and
field Hamiltonian with local-capacity energy response. The
[dependency map](DEPENDENCIES.md) separates its checked finite operators
and complete diagnostic records from the physical spectrum, common
spacetime and Einstein attachments. Its [machine register](dependencies.json)
binds every acceptance condition to the same source and observation domain.

The [conditional chiral gauge-Higgs calculation](CANDIDATE.md) and
[Wilson positive-energy calculation](POSITIVE_ENERGY.md) are separate
mathematical comparisons. Their calibrated coefficients, vacuum and
refinement are not predictions of this common source.

Run `python -m model.particle_joint_reconstruction --write` after the
CCF-LT-2 runtime and official claim register have been generated; use
`--check` to verify the saved dependency result.
