The [nine-node dependency map](DEPENDENCIES.md) joins the physical field,
particle and pure Yang-Mills requirements. Its [machine register](dependencies.json)
maps each component requirement to one acceptance condition, preserving the
source, state, reference, clock and observation domain.

The [positive-energy consumer](POSITIVE_ENERGY.md) applies the existing
finite downward-record theorem to this exact cooling family. Its scope is
stationary physical source probes. The limiting statement retains explicit
correlation, normalization and continuity hypotheses; it does not assign a
particle species or certify a gap.
Fixed triangular time filtering supplies a continuous response subsequence
for uniformly bounded probes, without a noncollapse or full-field claim.

The [conditional candidate](CANDIDATE.md) reuses the three-family chiral
gauge-Higgs calculation and its empirical inputs. It verifies the inherited
quadratic masses and a separate neutrino completion. It supplies no proof
of an interacting relativistic continuum, physical pole matching or a
primary-source implementation of the ninety-mode chiral extension.

The replay [receipt](results.json) admits the first three finite requirements
using actual typed consumers of the sixty-mode primary program. Editing a
status cannot admit a physical continuum claim. A pure-gauge instance must
supply its own source and preparation or a proved transfer map. The photon
in the conditional full theory excludes a positive global vacuum gap;
the pure Yang-Mills spectral criterion has a separate domain.
