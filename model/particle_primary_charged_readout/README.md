# Charge transfer and energy in one experiment

A finite coherent meter records the opposite endpoint charges transferred
by a physical pair-creating intervention. Its two couplings cancel the
unknown initial charge without measuring it. The source's complete energy
records and original probabilities are preserved, including the populated
charged-pair band. A later absolute-charge readout has its own disturbance.
The [proof](PROOF.md) distinguishes this operational transfer record from
a particle species or rest mass.

Run `python -m model.particle_primary_charged_readout --write` and
`python -m pytest -W error model/particle_primary_charged_readout`.
