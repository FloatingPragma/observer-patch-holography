additivity of separated colour-singlet clusters. With the same positive
inverse-rest-operator propagation rule, full relative-position bounds give
$m_\Lambda\in[1.11568462,1.11568488]\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and

unit. These inherited-calibration bulk energies retain their shifts from
the two-cell fits. The proton and pion ground-band energy sum crosses the

strong-sector energy shell. The unfiltered weak current requires an

the shell crossing alone.
