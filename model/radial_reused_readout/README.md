# Reading one prepared field

A detector can distinguish the ground doublet from either charged state of
the finite interacting radial source with one field preparation. Thirty-two
native pointer rounds have error below one in 2,500, including every control
failure. The exact repeated instrument retains field coherences and accounts
for the work performed by measurement. Reusing the field gives a mixture of
two native response histories, with the [proof](PROOF.md) and
[replay](results.json) fixing their probabilities. Fresh apparatus and a
controlled readout interval without uncompensated Hamiltonian evolution are
inputs; this is not an autonomous detector or a continuum particle theorem.
