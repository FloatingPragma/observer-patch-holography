`interacting_block_quadrature/results.json` and

Replica capacities are selected from exact source-only error inequalities.

a degree-two cycle at capacity one. It is a separate finite graph and

two free comparison packets. The output is a projected packet orthogonal

frozen native trial and agreement-filter bounds control the preparation
