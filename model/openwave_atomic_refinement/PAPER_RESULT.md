The source-generated electrostatic potential admits independent volume
and spacing controls. On nested cubes at the same spacing, the discrete
maximum principle makes the Dirichlet Green potential more attractive;
zero extension and the min-max principle then make every ordered
reflection-sector energy nonincreasing. No corresponding monotonicity
of gaps is assumed.

At spacing 1/3, increasing side length from 24 to 40 changes the computed
ground-to-odd gap from 0.4209531711 to 0.4203713410. At fixed side length 24,
refining spacing from 1/3 to 0.15 changes the gap from 0.4209531711 to
0.3834078435 and reduces the second-shell splitting from 0.0055004523 to
0.0004106464. The largest source has 4,019,679 spatial sites and uses 512,000
even-sector rows. The hydrogenic comparison is 0.375 in the supplied mass
and coupling units. Spectral residuals and positive-vector ratios are
finite numerical diagnostics; neither successive-grid differences nor
those residuals certify a continuum error or a physical hydrogen match.
