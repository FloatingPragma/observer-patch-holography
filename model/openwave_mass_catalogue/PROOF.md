# Complete energy instruments under a GeV calibration

Let a fixed source have a self-adjoint Hamiltonian H, reference energy E0,
initial density matrix rho, clock parameter t and complete POVM (M_x).
A positive scale u defines H_GeV = u(H-E0 I) and tau = t/u, with tau in
GeV^-1. Functional calculus gives

    exp(-i tau H_GeV) = exp(i t E0) exp(-i t H).

The scalar phase cancels from every Born probability, including preparation
failure and all apparatus-complement outcomes. The spectral measure is pushed
forward by E -> u(E-E0); its weights, ranks, degeneracies and normalization
are unchanged. A width Gamma becomes u Gamma, while a lifetime t becomes
t/u in inverse GeV. Conversion to seconds additionally requires a physical
clock attachment and hbar in GeV seconds. The unit map supplies neither a
particle identification nor an interaction or continuum transfer.

For positive independent enclosures E in [e0,e1], reference-subtracted anchor
energy A in [a0,a1] and measured anchor G in [g0,g1],

    G E/A lies in [g0 e0/a1, g1 e1/a0].

If E=A is the same random or uncertain quantity, its image is exactly G;
independent interval arithmetic can overestimate this correlated case.
The catalogue treats the electron anchor as this identity. Its narrow analyzer
intervals enclose numerical calculation only; they omit experimental anchor
uncertainty and architecture/model error.

The executable checks a noncommuting four-level Hamiltonian, a coherent
preparation, a shifted reference and a complete four-outcome position
instrument. An exact rational three-line measure independently checks the
energy-time products and retains failure probability one half. Negative tests
reject source/reference/clock changes, missing failure weight and nonpositive
units.

## Source-qualified particle accounting

The three charged Higgs-Wilson band minima have one colourless charged Dirac
family per analyzer eigenvalue. The positive Wilson term leaves their rest
values unchanged. The same three exact rational rest numbers also occur in
the quark-vacuum reducing sector of the 192-mode interacting matter member.
Equality of these numbers does not identify the two full Hamiltonians.

The calibrated native neutral tensor supplies three Dirac mass eigenvalues.
Active and right-chiral sterile coordinates have the same masses, as do the
corresponding antiparticles. Electron, muon and tau flavour labels denote
current-coupled superpositions, not three additional mass eigenvalues. Normal
ordering, the lightest mass 0.001 eV and the six supplied oscillation parameters
are explicit inputs.

The complete 17-species quadratic comparison retains its original conventions:
quark and charged-lepton MSbar parameters at 91.1876 GeV, an optional Weinberg
neutral prescription, and tree boson coefficients at a supplied loop vacuum
expectation value. These coefficients are not physical interacting pole-mass
predictions. The Weinberg and Dirac neutral constructions have distinct
lightest-mass choices and are retained separately.

The vectorlike control retains all six light and six heavy quark branches,
all 256 original occupation outcomes and its preparation failure. Their charge
labels do not establish an observed quark dictionary; the light-family ordering
fails the physical charm/strange assignment. Charged Wilson corner modes are
saddles or maxima, so their GeV energies are not additional particle minima.
The neutral zero modes of that member are retained explicitly.

For interacting baryons, prepared-state spectral means and certified energy
windows are reported with their original preparation failure. A fitted mean
is not substituted for a stationary eigenline, and a finite eigenline is not
substituted for a continuum nucleon pole. Each row links the source that defines
its preparation, reference, interaction and instrument.
