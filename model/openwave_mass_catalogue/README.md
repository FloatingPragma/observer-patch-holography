# Particle energies in GeV

The catalogue joins exact unit conversion with source-qualified masses and
complete energy instruments. Its charged band minima are 0.00051099895069,
0.10565941449590446 and 1.776984970813427 GeV, using the electron anchor and the
retrospective analyzer phase 2/9. Calibrated Dirac neutrinos, interacting baryon
energy windows, the 17-species quadratic comparison and retained extra branches
carry their own input ancestry and physical interpretation.

Run `python -m model.openwave_mass_catalogue --write`, then `--check`.
`PROOF.md` states the unit theorem and the limits of the particle dictionary;
`test_catalogue.py` checks complete normalization, source separation and
interval propagation. Conversion to GeV does not supply a physical clock,
quark confinement matching or an interacting particle pole.
