A Wilson link joins a charged fermion factor inside the observed region
to an opposite-charge factor outside. Both endpoints, the link and the
quantum reference are retained. On the unchanged selected source, a
complete spin analyzer records the scalar and vector pair coefficients
with positive original energy probability. The regional endpoint has the
actual two-component G1 source transformation law.
An alternative complete detector records the endpoint's plus or minus
one-half spin-projection increment and opposite charge increments, without
measuring their unknown initial values or changing the original energy law.

The measured transfer is the energy of the complete charged pair
intervention. An isolated charged particle and its GeV mass require a
separate dressing, dispersion and physical-scale derivation.
