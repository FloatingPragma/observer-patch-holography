# Allowed members and controls

The official model family is [CCF-LT-2](SPEC.md). Its finite oriented graph,
rotor cap, source parameters, auxiliary configurations, preparation and
instrument determine a member before evolution. There is one common law
for matter, field and total-energy response.

## Carrier

Every matter vertex has all 96 native CAR coordinates. The executable
generator provides an edge, a triangle and a tetrahedral sphere times a
radial interval. Coordinates, lengths and orientations are supplied chart
data. Every face obeys `B C^T=0`. A finite flux cap retains all integers
in its interval, with zero shifts beyond the wall and no cyclic wrap.

## Source parameters

The default signed Dirac speed and Wilson coefficients and electric and
magnetic weights equal one. Leptonic and quark contact strengths are
`1/4096`. Quark-family shifts and rotation, all retained calibrated matter
inputs, and both auxiliary configurations are specified in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md).
No positive-band projection or selected decay projector defines this source.

## Integrity and dynamics controls

The executable checks reject a Hermitian word with a wrong link shift,
a nonclosed face and corrupted relevant source components. Reversing a
correctly transformed edge preserves its identities. Removing the magnetic
term, signed kinetic, geometry link or lapse coupling defines a named
control. Complete outcomes and the actual changed Hamiltonian are retained.

A control tests its stated algebra or response. It does not establish that
the default parameters are physically unique. A short-time matrix element
does not establish an outgoing decay or an observed radiation spectrum.

## Component mathematics

Other packages specify independent finite constructions and conditional
proofs. Their existence is not a choice between competing official laws.
The [dependency map](particle_joint_reconstruction/DEPENDENCIES.md) gives the
checked interfaces required for their use by this model.
