# Complete finite source interfaces

The completion receipt replays each owning finite construction and compares
its fresh result with the retained child receipt using type-sensitive JSON
equality. Duplicate keys and nonfinite values are rejected. Its source
closure includes every owning receipt and transitive source binding. This
provides joint custody and reproducibility; it does not identify different
source laws, prepared states or clocks merely because their results appear
in one object.

addresses and the full remote archive are outside the binned comparison.

## Physical identification boundary

These interfaces do not select global occupation density, a physical vacuum,
a common relativistic action or a source-selected chiral matter theory.
The fair growing source and the weighted H66 controlled transfer experiment
have distinct reference and clock constructions. The finite gauge/Higgs,
defect and continuum results require their stated source joins. No measured
particle assignment, collider prediction or canonical physical-premise
discharge is added by this aggregate.

`model.verify` registers the aggregate producer, which freshly replays these
three child producers and checks their retained outputs. Its recursive
source-binding scan also checks their nested pins and receipt digests.
The observer regression collects the owning package tests and the aggregate
replay test on every matching push. Separate direct registration would
repeat the same child work; the aggregate entry supplies their replay path.
