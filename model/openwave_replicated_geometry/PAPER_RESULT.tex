\paragraph{A complete-register classical limit.}
For $Q_N=(q/N)\sum_i Z_i$ and
$H_g=\omega_N\sum_i(I-X_i)/(2N)$, the source normalization
$\omega_N=\omega N\cosh(q/N)^N\tanh(q/N)/\sinh q$
preserves the original weak far-field response. The relative vacuum lapse
variance is at most $e^{q^2F^2/N}-1$. The mean-Bloch bound and spectral
Jensen inequality place the complete quantum ground above the minimum of
$g_N(z)=\omega_N(1-\sqrt{1-z^2})/2+Ee^{qz}+Pe^{qFz}$.
A spin-product trial gives an upper error
$(Ee^q+Pe^{qF})(e^{q^2/(2N)}-1)$, without discarding any register sector.
This is a static single-mode classical limit. The geometry gap closes as
$1/N$, while spatial dynamics, preparation costs and physical gravitational
normalization retain separate requirements.
