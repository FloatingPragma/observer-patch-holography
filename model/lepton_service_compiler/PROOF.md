expansion. The chronological word V†, Z0, V implements

    H = V Z0 V† = I-2|q><q| = H60 direct_sum I4.

The result does not rely on a successful state-preparation column alone.

Let D_N be minus identity on the native sixty coordinates and identity on

    R64 = D_N H Z0 H† = (2|u><u|-I60) direct_sum I4.

## Analyzer and exact execution shortcut

The native address record partitions the two-address space into three
disjoint masks of sizes 500, 250 and 250. Let phi be the equal superposition
of their three normalized characteristic vectors, padded by zeros to 4096
coordinates. The existing real-state compiler emits a 135171-pulse word
W with W e0=phi. Its action is fixed on every other vector by the same
unitary word. The binary amplitude-tree proof in the source compiler
applies because phi is normalized and real; the last tree level retains
signed amplitudes. Each conditional Y rotation is expanded into commuting
Walsh-Y terms and each term is decomposed into native pulses.

For b<=12 define

    |0><0| = 2^(-b) product_j (I+Z_j)
            = 2^(-b) sum_S product_(j in S) Z_j.

The commuting expansion, including the identity term, emits a complete
word for exp(-ih|0><0|). Therefore the compiled conjugation is exactly

    W exp(-ih|0><0|) W†
       = I+(exp(-ih)-1)|phi><phi|.                 (1)

Equation (1) is an all-input identity on the full padded source. It also
holds after tensoring with any family and reference. The executor obtains
phi by replaying the actual native preparation word on e0, then evaluates
(1) as a low-rank action. No target vector is installed in that execution.
This shortcut follows from the unitary word identity and includes every
input column. Independently, the complete W†, blank-phase, W pulse sequence
is replayed literally on two source columns, one with nonzero padding.
These two controls test the implementation; they do not supply an
all-input numerical precision certificate.

The raw source-executed vector is never renormalized. Floating norm and
direction drift are reported separately. In exact arithmetic the word is
unitary and normalized by construction. Thus applying (1) to a raw floating
column is an approximation to the proved word identity; its displayed
residual is a software diagnostic, not a hardware error bound.

    U = R† D_theta† [I+(exp(-ih)-1)|phi><phi|] D_theta R.

This is exactly the original `lepton_cycle.instrument.mass_instrument`

and H. Its two Kraus operators are P=H|0><0|H† and I-P. The joint four
operators are P tensor P, (I-P) tensor P, P tensor (I-P), and

both_no. Their squared sum is identity on the entire padded source.
Numerical replay composes the actual executed H and H† around both

Each address pair is swapped into a fresh archive register after recording
the return. A qubit SWAP is the emitted three-CNOT word. Each CNOT consists
of three native pulses including its exact phase. The four input columns
are checked against SWAP. Twelve such disjoint swaps exchange the whole
source with a freshly prepared pair. On an arbitrary branch A_SR,

    SWAP (A_SR tensor |u,u><u,u|_F) SWAP†
       = |u,u><u,u|_S tensor A_FR,

where all correlations with the family and untouched reference survive in
the archived old source. This is a unitary relocation, not a deletion or
measurement of source coordinates. The separately supplied blank service

The family-diagonal theorem gives source vectors A[r,k] for each of four
returns and three family basis states. The executable compares the whole

    A[r1,k] tensor ... tensor A[rn,k].            (2)

The receipt stores the full 4096-component complex factors and every one

Small wrong-family and padding residuals from floating source replay are

the exact ideal-word equality supplies the theorem. Hardware pulse,

## Service failures and source-correlation controls

For proper-code dimension d, the coherent valid export maps all d code
coordinates through one Kraus operator. Each of the 6-d complementary
processor coordinates has a retained failure operator. Their adjoint
products sum exactly to I6. Complete words may append a first-failure
record at every service position. All such records have probability zero
on the ideal invariant-code trajectory; none is conditioned away.
Replacing coherent export by separate measured code coordinates changes a
superposition by unhalved trace distance 2(1-1/d), as directly checked for
d=2 and d=4. Coherent proper-code service is therefore an actual premise.

A source with the specified pure marginal |u,u><u,u| cannot be correlated
with any reference: positivity restricts the joint state to that rank-one
source support, so the joint state factors. A weaker address-histogram
check does not establish this premise. The negative control correlates
family one with alternating phases on thirty first-register addresses.
Both source branches have the same uniform address histogram; their
coherent source states differ. The complete compiled cycle then produces
different original four-outcome probabilities. The receipt explicitly
states that this control does not preserve the full pure source marginal.

## Resource statement and scope

The receipt separates fresh-pair preparation, native pulse words, two
coherent load/export events per pulse, return-test basis changes, supplied
binary readout, twelve-bit SWAP and growing quantum archive. All zero-angle
pulse requests are retained in its counts. A three-cycle bound has four
fresh pairs including the terminal active pair, thirty-six archived source
qubits and six classical return bits. These are bounded service resources,
not a spatial field simulation or an autonomous preparation theorem.

The result implements the declared prepared lepton cycle from native
response pulses plus the specified services. It derives neither the
services' physical accuracy nor an additional physical Yukawa value.
