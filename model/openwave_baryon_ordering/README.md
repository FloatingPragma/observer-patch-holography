# Native baryon ordering and intrinsic charge records

The complete two-cell CAR action has positive colour, colourful spin and
colourful weak-current squares, with coefficients1,1/4,1/4 and hopping1/8.
Its exact colour-singlet baryon embedding reduces2024 occupation states to120.
Integer residual certificates enclose every reduced eigenvalue and prove an
isolated spin-half/isospin-half ground band. Exact rational preparations retain
all2024 occupation records, failed preparation and the opposite-charge reference.
The neutral member has a positive inner and negative outer intrinsic graph
charge profile. The graph number centre defines that observable; a physical
form-factor interpretation is not established.

Free vectorlike gaps enter as declared number-energy coefficients. They give
neutron-like energy greater than proton-like energy, but their difference is
strictly less than the electron threshold. Ground-state beta decay is therefore
forbidden in this construction, and its absolute energies fail nucleon matching.
No measured quark or nucleon mass is used to choose the displayed coefficients.
The balanced-current extension is exploratory architecture; its choice is not
forced by the observer algebra. The free-to-interacting matching rule requires
its own implementation proof before it can be called a common physical source.
