## What the observers operate

The owners in order are 0, 4, 7, 17 and 12 on the registered 20-cell federation. The sources at 4 and 17 each place one sharp load on port 5 and another on port 7, counting locally. Addressed exchanges and a refocused contrast contact prepare equal, same-phase amplitudes for these two occupied ports and for occupied ports 0 and 1. A fresh native echo writes the same memory on 00 and 11 at the source seam. The source memory may be left unread. The comparison T write has the same sharp material transition probabilities and leaves orthogonal records for these alternatives. Its inclusion at even one source removes the transferred X correlation. [Empty/full source experiment](../../interval_echo_consensus/empty_full/README.md)

A full exchange transfers the entire state of two neighboring ports. Eight routes on actual seams implement endpoint transpositions and restore every intermediate port. The two written seams are protected. After transfer, Alice and Charlie each hold one dual-rail load. Bob holds one from each source. A local two-load Bell analyzer at Bob uses exchange and refocused contrast, then two terminal signed echoes. Its two bits give the eigenvalues $b_X$ and $b_Z$ of $X\otimes X$ and $Z\otimes Z$. Bob logs the bits before either outer setting and sends no information to the analyzers. Each outer setting is an independent sharp dimer on ports 2 and 3, a half contact and a fresh signed echo. Local pulse timing selects the measurement, followed by a signed contrast read. Unused contacts are isolated by the supplied schedule. [Pulse and route implementation](circuit.py) · [Analyzer projector and full-distribution tests](test_swapping.py)

For CHSH, Alice chooses Z or X while Charlie chooses $(Z\pm X)/\sqrt{2}$. In the later comparison, multiply Alice's sign by $b_Z$ for Z and by $b_X$ for X. This is a setting-dependent relabelling fixed in advance for each earlier central record. The four conditional scores therefore each obey the Bell-local limit under independent settings. The pooled comparison has the same limit. Bob's syndromes are never an acceptance flag. Each outer marginal is independent of the remote setting, even conditional on the earlier syndrome, in the analytic circuit. The physical count banks also agree within the simultaneous allowances.

For the independent-source witness, both outer observers choose $(Z\pm X)/\sqrt{2}$. Average $\langle A_x b_Z C_z\rangle$ over the four settings to obtain $I$; average $(-1)^{x+z}\langle A_x b_X C_z\rangle$ to obtain $J$. With the native apparatus, $I=J=1/2$ in the ideal calculation. A bilocal model has independent source variables, local responses at Alice and Charlie depending on their respective source and local setting, and a central response depending on both sources. Settings must be independent of both sources and each other, and Bob's bits must be inaccessible to the wings until the comparison. If $u_A^\pm=(A_0\pm A_1)/2$ are formed from Alice's conditional mean signs, then $|u_A^+|+|u_A^-|\le1$, also at Charlie. Source independence gives $|I|\le E|u_A^+| E|u_C^+|$ and $|J|\le E|u_A^-| E|u_C^-|$. Cauchy–Schwarz then gives $\sqrt{|I|}+\sqrt{|J|}\le1$. The bound has stronger assumptions than ordinary Bell locality. [Branciard, Rosset, Gisin and Pironio, *Phys. Rev. A* **85**, 032119](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.4502)

## Which controls are native

The phase-controlled circuit uses exact single-port number phases. A follow-up replaces every required phase with ten ordinary timed contacts between the same active port and two initially empty neighbors. The vacuum is fixed. On one load the even and odd combinations of a contacted pair acquire phases with energies 8 and 9. Multiplying the ten two-port matrices with outward-rounded interval arithmetic bounds the vector error by $7.55\times10^{-15}$ for $-\pi/2$, $2.51\times10^{-15}$ for $+\pi/2$ and $6.10\times10^{-15}$ for $\pi$. The two guard ports are returned to vacuum to these bounds. Coherent spectator configurations have the same error bound because each orthogonal spectator subspace carries the same three-port operation. The certification covers the vacuum and one local load with empty guards. [Exposure lists and interval certificate](compiler.py)

The full native run uses at most twenty such gates. A telescoping comparison on the ideal input subspaces bounds the final vector difference by $20\times10^{-14}$, including the environments of all recorded or forgotten instruments. Unitaries and isometries preserve norms and a subsequent measurement contracts trace distance. Every event probability therefore differs by at most $2\times10^{-13}$, and every signed correlation by at most $4\times10^{-13}$. Quiet central reads have a predetermined positive score. The independent sparse occupation replay with actual native pulses reproduces all 12 recorded count arrays with the fixed simulator seed and probability rounding. This makes separate single-port phase generators dispensable for this circuit. The durations, prepared guard ports, individually selectable contacts, sharp preparations and Born pointer/update rule are supplied. Four-step owner separation is a graph distance. Spacelike ordering in a calibrated observer metric and derivation of the Born instrument from deterministic repair remain open.

## Reproduction

Run from the repository root with the pinned model requirements. The first two commands check the stored integer banks using the two implementations. The tests also compare every joint probability against independently derived parity-projector formulas. Simultaneous 99% intervals cover independent simulated counting noise across 2,048 allowed signed comparisons. Each signed mean of $n$ trials has radius $\sqrt{2\log(4096/0.01)/n}$. The intervals do not estimate error in the physical model.

```sh
python -m model.experiments.interval_echo_quantum.swapping.measure
python -m model.experiments.interval_echo_quantum.swapping.measure --compiled
python -m pytest -q model/experiments/interval_echo_quantum/swapping
```
