# A mobile two-state charge with equal-energy partners

One vacancy carries an arbitrary two-state port amplitude from owner 0 to owner 4 along two prepared material paths. The receiving owner detects **76.51%** of mixed launches after 13 native time units. Every input has the same theoretical arrival probability. There are two states at each of the 13 distinct formation energies, and both can be read in the receiving cell. The two logical labels say which path contains the charge. After arrival, a native path swap and a path phase anticommute. Two physical perpendicular half-turns of the received charge give a positive order sign. ## Which material carries the two states?

The two 13-port paths begin in the same ordinary icosahedral cell, traverse separate intermediate owners and reach the same receiving cell. The experiment writes the 93 ordinary contacts leading off the two paths on filled endpoint loads. Six internal degree references are written with the physical mobile vacancy and zero reference. The four path-end buffers retain their ordinary private contact. The source and receiver use the first interior port of each path at their respective ends. The full board has one excess and one vacancy. The compensator lies outside the isolated paths; a local ternary read counts the traveling load relative to the filled material. The adjacent buffers in each end cell supply the two sharp input states. A half exchange and a private-buffer phase prepare and analyze the six Pauli states. Two full swaps move the addresses from the buffers to the launch gateways. With both buffers filled, an equal-load echo seals the last bond between paths. The receiving operation reverses this construction. An arrived vacancy, at either gateway, leaves the buffer-bond ends filled for undoing the mark. The inverse routes it to a buffer. The orthogonal output includes all other positions and head outcomes. For the spatial test all interior receiving collars are reopened on filled loads, parking a touched buffer at its own gateway during that echo. This supplies blank ordinary contacts for the complete twelve-port half-turn words. Addressed contacts, clock, phase reference and controlled comparison are laboratory services.

Its endpoint diagonal is 0.5 for a vacancy and 32.5 for an excess. The full two-path generator is the identity on the logical address tensored with this path matrix. ## What the receiving cell can measure at fixed energy

An open real tridiagonal matrix with every subdiagonal nonzero has a simple spectrum. The endpoint equation determines the full eigenvector from its first entry. If the component at the first interior port vanished, the eigenvalue would have to equal the endpoint diagonal. Evaluating the continuant of the doubled path matrix there gives −46071357982815 for the vacancy and 26220121864289 for the excess. Both are nonzero. Reflection gives the same statement at the receiving gateway. Thus the spectral projector from the two source gateways to the two receiving gateways is a nonzero scalar times the identity at **each** of the 13 distinct energies. The two amplitudes can survive selection of one exact line.

There is a complementary bound for an arbitrary finite single-particle material cut. At nonreal energy $z$, if the left-to-right Hamiltonian block is $V$, the off-diagonal resolvent satisfies $G_{RL}=(z-H_{RR})^{-1}V G_{LL}$. Its rank is at most the rank of $V$. Multiplying by $z-E$ and taking the limit at any eigenvalue $E$ yields the same bound for the off-diagonal spectral projector, since the fixed-rank upper bound is closed. Local preparation and detector maps cannot increase this rank. A single scalar contact therefore permits at most one receiver-accessible component at a sharp energy. The two matched paths saturate the rank-two bound. An interacting material seam with several active head channels requires the rank of its full off-diagonal block. ## Which rotations act on the arriving charge?

In the first energy-filtered spatial bank there are 116,826 bright, zero dark and 4,077,478 other outcomes when the two perpendicular material half-turns occur in opposite orders. A repeated half-turn gives 117,638 bright and zero dark. The same receiver gives 117,513 dark, zero bright and 4,076,791 other outcomes for the logical swap and phase applied in opposite orders. The geometric pulse words positively exchange the ordinary loads of the decoded receiving collar. This measures the spatial response of the received orbital charge in that collar; the material guides, distant path labels and phase apparatus stay in the laboratory frame.

A further test prepares the equal-rail superposition in the 17.00504810 line. A native pi phase confined to one receiving gateway flips its logical label in 116,390 of 4,194,304 starts and transfers 1,165,744 out of the standing-mode subspace. Applying the phase on the entire second path flips every label and preserves the selected mode. If $w$ is the single-rail weight at the addressed site, the three probabilities are $(1-w)^2$, $w^2$ and $2w(1-w)$. More generally, any operation acting identically outside a patch and preserving this exact spatial eigenfunction leaves the logical amplitudes fixed whenever one untouched site has nonzero weight. A distributed path label can supply a degenerate transported qubit. Assigning physical spin or a lepton mass requires spatially localized rotation and inertial measurements on the same physical dressing.
