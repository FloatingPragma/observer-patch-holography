# Current, winding and the receiving algebra

**Local current.** Orient each physical contact from endpoint $a$ to endpoint $b$, with $c$ its direction bit in that convention. Exact integer compression of all 99 native columns gives two 18-dimensional unit-contrast sectors, one for each contrast-reversal parity. Every nonzero matrix entry in either sector satisfies

$$n'_a-n_a=c'-c=n_b-n'_b.$$

The four off-diagonal entries per sector transfer one unit and flip the head. Thus on a graph of these contacts each regional operator defined by $G_v=n_v-\sum_{e\,\mathrm{out}}c_e+\sum_{e\,\mathrm{in}}c_e$ commutes with the Hamiltonian. Orientation reversal exchanges endpoints and complements $c$, which preserves the current statement. The equality concerns this native binary direction record. An independent Maxwell field strength and the coupling of that field to this dressing require their own source and probe experiments.

**Finite free sector.** Put a vacancy on port 0 of the oriented $N$-cycle and all heads in direction zero. For $x=0,\ldots,N$, let $|x\rangle$ denote the vacancy at $x\bmod N$ and the first $x$ heads reversed. The only enabled forward step reverses the next zero head. Its inverse restores the previous head. Thus every native column stays in this span, and all $N+1$ states communicate. At even contrast parity a full host contact has energy 8. An enabled pair has diagonal 4 and off-diagonal $-4$. After subtracting the host constant, the restricted generator is

$$H=-4(D+A),$$

where $D,A$ are the degree and adjacency matrices of the path on $N+1$ words. The two endpoint words have the same material address and orthogonal head records. The exterior zero marks have zero Hamiltonian on their entire local sector. The native exchange pulse at $\pi/8$ swaps each enabled pair up to its known common phase and is idle on blocked words.

**Locality at a fixed charge position.** At port 0 the two endpoint words span two states with the same material load. Their head factors disagree on all 15 links. Tracing even one of those links kills the off-diagonal element between the words exactly. Any instantaneous observable confined to a proper subset of the heads can read populations, but has zero matrix element between the two branches. Reading the relative phase requires a circuit that engages the full support or a period of propagation, as in the free-read protocol.

**Successful directed winding.** Starting at port 0, apply the addressed full pulse on each link in clockwise order. Retain a trajectory only when its vacancy appears at the expected next port after each pulse. On the fixed-current sector this product is $W=e^{i\theta}|N\rangle\langle0|$. In particular $W^2=0$. For any normalized state with endpoint amplitudes $a,b$, the modulus of its forward-winding expectation is $|\overline b a|\le (|a|^2+|b|^2)/2\le 1/2$. No nonzero state in this sector acquires a repeatable scalar $+1$ or $-1$ from this operation. These statements concern the clockwise success branch. The complete sequence of pulses when all outcomes are retained is unitary. Reverse transport restores the material and the original head word. **Two interference experiments.** The two durations $\pi/16$ and $3\pi/16$ of the first contact create orthogonal two-rail states on $|0\rangle,|1\rangle$ with equal populations. The two preparations differ in their relative phase. Following the other 14 contacts with full pulses moves the second rail to $|N\rangle$. The material charge returns to the source while the phase is distributed between two winding records. Inverting that preparation distinguishes the two inputs at the source with unit contrast. In the free-read cohort the receiver simply waits and reads its own port. Dephasing the two endpoint words gives the arithmetic mean of the two coherent predictions at every appointed time.

For the strictly local launch, omit the 14 routing pulses. At an interior pair $r,r+1$, the exterior head bits on the two possible arrivals are identical. The projected channel on these two rails therefore has one Kraus operator with entries $K_{ij}=(e^{-itH})_{r+i,j}$ for $i,j\in\{0,1\}$. Material presence at either receiver port flags acceptance. A half pulse on that same seam mixes the two paths and acts trivially on the exterior. In contrast, after projection onto one specified foreign port its head word is unique in this sector. Amplitude and phase information across the remote pair can be transported even though a single sharp foreign address cannot host a two-state spin in this preparation.

**Certified rank.** At $t=3/2$, the largest absolute row sum of $-tH$ is 24. For the degree-96 Taylor polynomial of $e^{-itH}$, the integral remainder in operator norm is bounded by $24^{97}/97!<10^{-16}$ because the exponential along the imaginary axis is unitary. The code evaluates the two required columns over Gaussian integers with common denominator $96!$. Moving each exact unitary entry by at most $\epsilon$ moves the two-by-two determinant by at most $4\epsilon+2\epsilon^2$. A rational squared-modulus comparison then proves $|\det K|>1/10$ for the fixed foreign pair $r=7$. Its floating-point magnitude is 0.110021381193.
