# Spatial turns, magnetic bands and material memory

**Geometry and free response.** Orient the twenty faces of the guarded icosahedral cell outwards. Assign oriented link integers $l_{ij}=-l_{ji}$ modulo twenty so each face sum is $q$. A spanning tree of the dual graph solves these equations by giving its parent edge the sum of the demands below it. The root sees $-19q$, equivalent to $q$. Set $z=\exp(2\pi i/20)$. An ordinary vacancy crosses a seam with matrix element $-1/2$. A private endpoint phase, an ordinary contact and its inverse endpoint phase replace this with $-z^{l_{ij}}/2$. The diagonal per vacancy is $5(8+1/2)$. The five incident contacts are retained, while external contacts and private terms are parked between the specified services. Energies use this guarded generator and its filled reference. No keV conversion enters. The symmetric product uses all thirty contacts forward and backward with half steps. Its one-time-unit norm error relative to the sum is 0.08783 at 32 slices and 0.00559 at 128. The exact magnetic symmetry belongs to the sum. The finite selection experiment uses the actual 128-slice product and records refusals.

**Lifting the rotations.** For a proper rotation $r$, define $U_r|i\rangle=z^{u_r(i)}|r i\rangle$, with $u_r(0)=0$ and
$$
u_r(j)-u_r(i)=l_{r i,r j}-l_{ij}\pmod {20}.
$$
The circulation on every face is zero, so the correction is single valued. The complete port lift is synthesized from the calibrated private phases and positive spatial permutation contacts. It commutes with the magnetic Hamiltonian by the link equation. For commuting involutions $r,s$ the two orders have identical port permutations and differ by $u_s(i)+u_r(s i)-u_r(i)-u_s(r i)$. Enumerating every port and all fifteen perpendicular pairs gives $10q\pmod{20}$ for $q=0,1,-1,2$. Site rephasing and a constant phase multiplying either involution cancel from this difference. The compensated order is a finite spatial rotation observable in a prescribed external field. A single rotation without comparing orders retains an arbitrary phase convention. **Why one spinor fits.** The lifts compose projectively because two lifts of one rotation differ by a site-independent phase on the connected graph. Their conjugation action on operators is an ordinary representation. Averaging it projects onto the commuting operators. The trace of the average is $\sum_r |\operatorname{tr}U_r|^2/60$. The integer fixed-port census gives numerator $192+12(z^4+z^8+z^{12}+z^{16})=180$ at unit flux. The commutant thus has dimension three. For a unitary projective representation with this common multiplier, its decomposition into irreducibles gives the dimension as $\sum m_j^2$. The only possibility is three inequivalent constituents, each with multiplicity one.

The eigenvalues computed at unit flux are 40.3733729791, 42.1367287360 and 43.4510565163. The rank certificate uses grid $M=2^{24}$. Integer square roots of the half-angle formulas enclose the real and imaginary parts of $z$ to $2/M$ each. The rounded generator has norm at most one before forming powers. Each Gaussian integer multiply followed by rounding introduces error at most $1/M$ beyond the $3/M$ generator error. For powers through nineteen the link error is below $100/M$. The Hamiltonian has five off-diagonal entries of magnitude $1/2$ per row, giving operator error below $250/M$. Realify the rational Hermitian matrix, and use integer fraction-free elimination to count negative pivots at the cuts $41\pm1/1024$ and $43\pm1/1024$. Every leading principal minor used is nonzero. After removing the twofold duplication from realification, the lower counts are two and six on both sides of each cut. These counts transfer to the exact Hermitian generator by the norm bound. **Passive collars.** For either sign $s=\pm1$ use one load $1+s$ in a unit-filled host. On a written seam define $b=c$ for the magnitude-one contrast labels and $b=0$ otherwise. The code checks the complete exchange and contrast columns in integer arithmetic, including the spectator pair, all ten written labels and the blank-to-written off-block. In particular a current integral on one written edge telescopes. A closed path returning all material records has zero net crossing on every written edge. A monitored second lap of a directed simple loop with an initially written edge has zero probability to follow the same forward crossings twice without rearming it.

Multiplying an off-diagonal crossing on edge $e$ by $e^{i\alpha_eJ}$ is conjugation by the diagonal collar unitary $D=\exp(-is\sum_e\alpha_e b_e)$ on a fully written board. Diagonal terms and the private degree reference commute with $D$. This holds in the full one-defect sector, including entangled coin states. **Blank-seam material dressing.** A unique vacancy in a blank cell can also carry any number $k$ of mobile doubles among the eleven other, unit-filled ports. External compensating charges can set the total excess. Protected vacancy-return paths include transpositions of the cargo on every edge away from its root: visit an incident triangle by a path avoiding the two exchanged addresses, circle once and reverse the approach. The graph of these edges is connected. The full path group on cargo labels is $S_{11}$. This is a statement about invariance under *all* such paths. Restricting the apparatus to a subgroup can preserve additional directions. [Single-mark path group](../../../interval_echo_gauge/loop_transport/HOLONOMY.md)

The $k$-double space is the complex permutation representation on $k$-element subsets. On a permutation with cycle lengths $d_i$ its character $h_k$ is the coefficient of $x^k$ in $\prod_i(1+x^{d_i})$. The 56 cycle types, counted by their exact conjugacy class sizes, give an identity Gram matrix for the six virtual characters $h_j-h_{j-1}$, $0\leq j\leq5$, with $h_{-1}=0$. A norm-one virtual character is an irreducible character up to sign; positive dimension fixes that sign. Their dimensions are $1,10,44,110,165,132$. Telescoping and subset complementation give one copy each through $j=\min(k,11-k)$. Hence every fixed cargo composition has just its uniform line and no irreducible doublet under the whole path group. In particular the phase from circling a face on such an invariant line is positive.
