# A magnetic doublet stored in the direction heads

A charge crossing one triangular face can read a magnetic phase from three prepared material heads. The same thirty heads give opposite phases to a vacancy and an excess. All forty face readings, twenty per sign, recover the prescribed single quantum of flux from an independent interferometer. An effective spinorial doublet appears when a monitor repeatedly returns each head to its prepared state. Removing that service makes every input in the doublet leave the prepared head sector. ## One lap and the repeat test

Use all thirty internal seams of one guarded icosahedral cell on the registered federation. Place a single vacancy or excess on a unit-filled host and park its opposite charge outside the shell. The apparatus supplies addressed contacts, isolated external and private terms, a coherent route reference and the initial head stock. Choose a directed link angle $\alpha_e$ on each increasing edge so the outward sum is $2\pi/20$ on every face. In the two even magnitude-one directions, store $(|0\rangle+e^{-i\alpha_e}|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$. The stock is common to both charge signs. A balanced path control with an odd or even head, a directed lap and a successful erasure can prepare a complex direction on an individual triangle. Preparing all thirty while the internal contacts are progressively occupied is a supplied initial condition in this experiment. Port-bank starts count trials with that stock in place. The native vacancy transfer on one edge takes $|0\rangle$ to $|1\rangle$ on the successful branch. Its overlap with the stored head is $e^{i\alpha_e}/2$. The opposite charge reverses that direction. The measured one-lap overlap is therefore $e^{\pm i\pi/10}/8$ with route survival $1/8$. The real and imaginary detector contrasts determine its angle. Each simulated setting has 1,048,576 starts, including every refused route. Rounding the observed angles to the twenty root-of-unity settings identifies every face correctly.
