# Rotating the three heads with the receiving charge

Receiver port 160 in owner 13 has three physical magnitude-one seams, on 157–161, 158–166 and 159–165. Eighteen of the thirty internal seams carry silent zero-contrast writes. Nine are blank. Five proper icosahedral rotations fix the sharp port, including the identity. **Only the identity preserves the set of three magnitude-one seams, even when the port may move.** Among the fifteen half-turns, ten retain none of the three edges, three retain one, and two retain two. The receipt records the port permutation and the three head-sector comparisons for every proper rotation. [All 60 turns](rotation_domain.json) · [Actual source and receiver graph](apparatus.py)

For a physical seam $e$, let $P_{e,s}$ project onto a blank head or a written head with magnitude $s=0,1,2$. These four sectors have dimensions 9, 18, 36 and 36 including the endpoint loads. Rebuilding the complete native exchange and contrast terms from the integer write pattern gives 180 and 90 nonzero entries respectively, with zero entries between distinct head sectors. The verification checks both cross blocks as well as the blank and written matrices. Every term on a different seam acts as identity on this head. Every interval of free evolution, addressed exchange or contrast dwell therefore commutes with all four projectors on each seam. Products, time-dependent choices of such Hamiltonians and branches of instruments whose Kraus operators also commute preserve the same sector. This includes arbitrary coherence in the occupants, directions, sign parity and external references subject to the prepared magnitude mask. Let $P$ select the prepared magnitude pattern and $P_R$ its image under a proper rotation. For each of the 59 nonidentity turns, at least one edge has orthogonal initial and rotated sectors. Hence $P_R P=0$ and $P_R U P=0$ for **every** pulse sequence in this class. The same holds after acceptance conditioned on commuting readouts. A vacancy waveform can move, or change phase, while these heads stay anchored. The result concerns physically moving the *whole* prepared receiver head pattern. It does not determine the phase of every cycle returning to the original pattern.

The addressed native echo is an explicit operation outside this class. Acting on a blank seam with loads (0,1) writes magnitude one with probability one, and the inverse restores the original blank column. Rotating a dressing by coherently unwriting, routing and rewriting its unknown head state would therefore require a different experiment with reference-phase and return checks. A slow change of coefficients of the same commuting generators preserves the exclusion. A geometric cycle within the original sector can carry its own phase, and an adiabatic transport of a continuously moved head pattern would require operators that mix the fixed sectors. An externally reassigned material frame changes which physical operation is being tested.
