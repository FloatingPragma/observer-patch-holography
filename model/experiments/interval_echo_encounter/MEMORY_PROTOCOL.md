# Retained native write on a scheduled seam

Use the 20-cell sphere, uniform 36 separated preparations on cell 0, κ = 8, waits one and four, and the first internal seam (0,1) from the [readout protocol](PROTOCOL.md). At the first service apply the registered 99-state native echo U to the two loads and the seam's blank working memory. Compare retaining the coherent write, immediately applying the inverse U† to erase it, and bypass. Include every preparation and every coherent branch. The service, preparation and final read are supplied. No branch is selected and the clock is idle.

For the following four units all seams keep their registered free Hamiltonians. Use the full native term U G U† on the written seam and the blank-reducing native terms elsewhere. Establish the exact invariant subspace entered by the write using the 99-state matrices, including an explicit leakage check for doubled endpoints and unrepresented memory states. At the final reading the finite patch resolves its 30 internal seams or quiet. The primary observable sums the internal seams whose two endpoints both differ from 0 and 1. Retain every terminal outcome and report whether the signed difference (retained minus erased) in disjoint-seam yield has absolute magnitude greater than 10⁻⁶.

As a simulator diagnostic compute the mean bound-band weight of the reduced loads immediately after the write. Use the same blank-sector projector onto energies below −5 as in the [binding band diagnostic](README.md). Sum every memory state. Test whether its increase over erase exceeds 10⁻⁶. Interpret this as population in a specified load subspace at that instant. The written seam’s free generator is part of the subsequent joint evolution. The working memory is private to the seam and the required observer evidence comes from the local terminal reader.

Check the erase arm against bypass to absolute error 10⁻¹⁰ over all terminal outcomes. Check that all 36 joint states have conserved norm and that their binary load/memory subspace is invariant within 10⁻¹⁰. Check the source lift, retained generator and load probabilities independently on the one-cell carrier. Archive either sign of the primary result and every quiet outcome.
