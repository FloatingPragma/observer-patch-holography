# Regional signed charge of the full moving-pair band

On the 20-cell Interval Echo board, the moving bound pair produces an uncompensated regional load on fewer than **0.0992%** of spectrally admitted launches across three sources, 60 regions and a million exchange units of free motion. This is a simultaneous 99% upper confidence limit on the modeled count probabilities. Every member of the admitted ensemble has a working meter. At the launch cells the comparable lower limits range from 27.28% to 46.06% for mixtures matching both single-port marginals. A separate numerical operator bound gives **0.107054%** for the probability that the two signs occupy different owners, for every state of the 24-mode spectral cluster and every time under its free Hamiltonian. Any union of whole owners can carry uncompensated material charge only on this distinct-owner event. The same bound therefore applies to any shape of such a region. The 60 measured owner balls are the observational sample. This bound allows opposite signs at different ports inside one owner. The microscopic charge variable is occupation relative to unit filling.

## How the instrument handles every pair

Each of three candidate meter sites has two ports outside the survey region, in separate owners for the three sites. Read both public material occupations at the first site. Use it if the loads are (1,1), otherwise retain their word and repeat at the next site. Only one excess and one vacancy occupy the original board. They can spoil at most two sites. The code verifies this on all 57,360 ordered inputs for every surveyed region. Some native settings used a fallback on **39.50%** of admitted launches. Since the eligibility projectors commute with regional charge, summed-over eligibility records preserve its initial distribution. Individual records can disturb coherence. [Address selection and native route verification](geometry.py)

Exchange a prepared (2,0) neutral meter for the two unit loads at the accepted site. Each regional port in turn meets the same meter via a native endpoint transposition, a refocused diagonal contact and inverse routing. Intermediate port occupations are restored. On blank seams at κ=8 the target load is n=1+q and the source arm chooses r=0 or 2. The diagonal interaction is V(n,r)=8(n−r)²+½·1[n≠r]. Its energy difference between the arms is −65q/2, giving the relative phase exp(i·65tQ/2) after a whole scan, where Q is the sum of the target charges. The common phase can depend on the local squared charge. It does not enter the meter probabilities.

At a total contact dwell of 2π/65 per target a bright parity read means Q=0 and a dark read means Q=±1. In the dark branch a native π contact returns the known meter orientation to (2,0). A second scan at π/65 with the native Y quadrature distinguishes +1 from −1. Two targets and a separate compensating port admit an independent five-qutrit calibration with all 243 computational words. Omitting Y reduces sign success to 50%; halving both interrogation times reduces correct charged labels to 42.68%. Terminal probability defects in the native calibration are below 3×10⁻¹⁴. The programmed routes, supplied sharp load observations, source and clock are apparatus. The pulse word is diagonal on target load between routing swaps. Exact sharp charge does not imply a minimally disturbing instrument on the pair. [Native calibration and controls](meter.py)

## Denominators and stability

Each of the 540 panels enrolls 67,108,864 original launches. Approximately 13.27% pass the same 24-line spectral filter. In the charge intervals the only exclusion is this initial filter failure. Both the signed words and the separate filter-refusal count are present for every panel. The simultaneous 99% intervals invert the binomial Chernoff tails with an allocation of 0.01/(2×540) to each tail. The model’s numerical error allowance for each enrolled native source is separately reported at about 4.12×10⁻⁶ in probability.

For a regional charged-event projector F, the maximum across the bound cluster equals the largest eigenvalue of B†FB, where B is an orthonormal basis of its 24 states. Replacing F by the indicator of different owners gives one upper bound for every owner union. The operator perturbation from the approximate spectral projector is bounded by twice its norm error. The code adds allowances for the finite Gram products, basis orthogonality and the eigensolver residual. The distinct-owner maximum is 0.00107052281 with numerical allowance 1.08×10⁻⁸. The exact spectral cluster is invariant under the free Hamiltonian, so time evolution and classical mixing cannot increase this worst-case bound. These are finite-board numerical enclosures using the source’s spectral error estimate. A physical electric-charge identification requires additional evidence.

For the machine-readable alphabet, encode a rejected two-port load word as 3a+b and remove word 4 from its rank.
