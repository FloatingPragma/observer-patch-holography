# Reading the moving pair with two separately prepared material probes

Use the frozen 20-cell unit-filled sample, original three launches (0, 5 and 10), the certified lowest 24-line filter, and the one-million-exchange-time flight. Keep filter failures and all missed arrivals. Flag both defects in the same cell at least two owner steps from launch before the contact test. The flag uses only ownership and distance. The local probe receives neither defect sign nor precise address.

Freeze the captured sample for a terminal contact measurement. Choose a sampling port uniformly among all degree-five ports, or among all degree-six ports. Use copies for each contact and probe sign. For sampling port p, precompute three mutually disjoint neighbouring rail seams q–r outside p, with p–q an actual seam and r degree five. Test q–r for equal loads with the native signed echo and undo. Choose the first available pair. Because this sector has at most one excess and one vacancy, equality on this pair certifies host loading. Two defects cannot spoil three disjoint pairs. This routing and the ideal terminal source that supplies an independent excess or vacancy at q are measurement apparatus.

From sharp loading (s,1+e,1) on p,q,r split the probe with a native q–r half contact. Apply a p–q contact for 1/32 exchange time, a degree-five reference at r for π/16 and an inverse splitter at q–r. Read the full signed q–r echo. Repeat on fresh copies at 1/64 as a held dwell. All five signed bins count. The first dwell calibrates sensitivity to sharp source charges and the second checks whether the sign separation survives a different exposure. A host blank and separately sharp excess and vacancy sources are the primary calibration. Keep the pulse and reader fixed in an exchange-only contact control that changes the sampled interaction while leaving source formation and meter calibration operations unchanged.

Give scores +1 and −1 to terminal contrasts e and −e, zero to the other three. Subtract the two opposite-probe scores and use their half-difference response to one sharp excess as the charge unit. Separately retain their mean after subtracting its measured blank response. Report estimates on each port class and the sum over the board. A calibrated signal above 0.8 unit on the regular ports, below −0.8 on the gateways and a net magnitude below 0.1 unit on all three held arrivals at both times constitute the signed-response test. If the two individual probes have a nonzero common shift, report it as a residual interaction. Also compare the baseline two-bin source/arrival flag with its native strict bound-pair meter. This is a contact response test on terminal samples, with no supplied electromagnetic action, external long-range field or physical keV calibration.
