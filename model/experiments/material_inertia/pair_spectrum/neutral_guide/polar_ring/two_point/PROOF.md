## What persists at arbitrarily late times

At the source, take the connected ground correlation of the same swap at two appointments, subtracting the elastic contribution. Its complex amplitude is accessible by comparing a path that flips at the first appointment with one that flips at the second and reading the two interference quadratures on fresh preparations. Write the amplitude as a finite sum of exponentials with frequencies equal to positive energy transfers. At each distinct transfer the coefficient is the sum of the local spectral weights of all states at that frequency. Every coefficient is nonnegative.

Averaging the squared magnitude continuously from zero to time T eliminates each cross term at distinct frequencies as T tends to infinity. The limit therefore equals the sum of the squared coefficients. Grouping equal frequencies can only increase this sum compared with squaring their individual nonnegative weights. For the N selected momentum lines with combined weight at least W, Cauchy–Schwarz gives a lower bound of W²/N on the infinite-time mean squared magnitude.
