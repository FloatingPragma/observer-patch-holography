A vacancy and an excess on a unit-filled five-owner ring have two stable contact branches. A clock measures their recoil on 20 ports. Its two fitted masses, 30.004 and 31.965 in link and exchange units, predict the vacancy's spatial fringes on physical rings of 30 and 40 ports. A vacancy and an excess mounted separately give signed responses of opposite sign. These are simulations of material measurements.
