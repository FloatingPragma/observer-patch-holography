# The response of an independently stocked probe

Write $s$ for the normalized input of one thirteen-rail source bank and $p$ for the separate probe input. Both have one unit, and the source's thirteenth rail starts empty. In the native binary sector, one matched contact fixes 00 and 11. On 10 and 01 it has diagonal amplitude $d=e^{-i(\kappa+h/2)t}\cos(ht/2)$ and exchange amplitude $e=i e^{-i(\kappa+h/2)t}\sin(ht/2)$, with exchange rate $h=1$ in the registered law. These expressions follow from the blank columns of the native 99-state generator. Native full swaps bring each of the thirteen pairs together and restore the routes. The sources are independently prepared. Their sign controls reverse the winding phases in a given pentagon.

Put $a=d^2$ and $b=e^2$. On reading one unit in each bank, the amplitude for distinct output labels $i,j$ is $a s_i p_j+b s_j p_i$. For identical labels it is $s_i p_i$. Inverting the source preparation and finding its unit back at the launching address contracts the first label against $s$. The probe then has unnormalized amplitude
$$
K_s p=\big[aI+bss^\dagger+(1-a-b)\operatorname{diag}(|s|^2)\big]p .
$$
Both units in a single bank at $i<j$ have amplitude $de(s_i p_j+s_j p_i)$. Summing their squared magnitudes in that bank gives $|de|^2(\|p\|^2+|s^\dagger p|^2-2\sum_i|s_i p_i|^2)$. The other bank has the same bunched probability. A different returned source mode takes all remaining probability after these two cases and the successful return. The second encounter acts directly on the unnormalized first return. Every measured probability is therefore per original start. A separate sparse calculation in the [test](test_probe.py) applies all thirteen physical gates to the 325 two-unit configurations and independently reads the three failure weights and the returned amplitudes.

## What the receiver can reconstruct

On a marked ring let $R_q=\operatorname{Im}(s_qs_q^\dagger)$ for positive winding. Each $R_q$ is real and skew. Conjugating the source changes its sign while preserving the diagonal contact. Half the difference of the two returned amplitudes is $ibR_q$. The unrelated probe reference receives amplitude $a$ for either winding. Prepare $(|j\rangle+|r\rangle)/\sqrt2$, where $r$ is the reference. A balanced receiver with reference phase $\phi$ counts the signed difference between its two exits on the addressed pair $i,r$. This is $\operatorname{Re}(e^{i\phi}\bar a(K_s)_{ij})$ on the full ensemble. For a single encounter choose $\phi=\pi/2$. Half the difference of the two winding reads gives $g(R_q)_{ij}$, with $g=\cos^2(ht/2)\sin^2(ht/2)$.

For the two-source experiment the input choices at each mark are its center or antipode on equal, separately recorded cohorts. Their orientation-odd mean is $X=(R_q+R_{\bar q})/2$. The sum of the twelve ring matrices vanishes: every pair sharing a pentagon receives two opposite contributions from its two common marks, and antipodes share none. Thus $X$ is exactly the source coefficient $(I+J)e_q/2-\mathbf1/12$ acting on the twelve port drives. The other source similarly yields $Y$. Alternating over both signs removes every term even in either source. Forward-minus-reverse visit order and phase zero give $g^2(XY-YX)_{ij}$. This equality holds at every enrolled dwell. It also gives the weak-encounter coefficient after division by $g^2$ and passage to zero dwell. [Formal signed-order identity](OrderResponse.lean)

For the three enrolled coordinates, the two common marks are $(0,5)$, $(5,7)$ and $(0,7)$. Their coefficients in each individual $R_q$ are oppositely signed differences on that triangle with magnitudes $\sin(\pi/5)/5$, $\sin(2\pi/5)/5$ and $\sin(\pi/5)/5$. The specified reciprocal weights cancel all twelve single columns. The two-source order column has a nonzero cycle. This is a second check on a different statistic from the matrix-rank calculation.

## A confidence bound on the number of directions

Each of 66 probe input/output pairs has its own independently sampled cohorts. Stack the twelve measured single columns and two measured order differences into a real $66\times14$ matrix $M$. The fixed native forecast, computed from the five neighbors and the registered exchange clock, provides the leading thirteen left and right singular directions $U,V$. They are selected independently of the terminal counts. If $U^TMV$ is invertible for the population mean, the mean matrix has rank at least thirteen. Any signed port assignment with at most twelve generators closed under these two commutators must instead have rank at most twelve under this same linear probe readout. This condition permits arbitrary coefficients and gains within that space.

The observed letter on one start is $-1,0$ or $+1$. For a single column the two sign differences from two stations give four weights of magnitude $1/4$, each on $2^{29}$ starts. For a two-source column the two orders, four signs and four mark placements give 32 weights of magnitude $1/16$, each on $2^{28}$ starts. In either case $\sum w_b^2/N_b=2^{-31}$. Projection onto any fixed unit left and right vectors preserves this bound by independence of the cells of $M$. For $m$ such entries, a two-sided Hoeffding and union bound gives the simultaneous radius $\sqrt{2\log(2m/\alpha)/2^{31}}$. Use $m=169$ and $\alpha=0.005$ here. The spectral norm of the compressed error is at most thirteen times this radius.

The nine event weights use a $2^{-26}$ grid with the residual assigned to the largest bin. A signed read moves by at most nine grid ticks, or eighteen after an order difference. The spectral contribution is at most $18\sqrt{66\cdot14}\,2^{-26}$. The remaining 0.005 error budget covers the single-station forecasts, order and held coordinates, and finite quantile sampling. In a conditional binomial with at most $2^{29}$ draws, Hoeffding confines the ideal count to at most $2r+1$ central integers except for mass $\epsilon=2^{-80}$, where $r=\lceil\sqrt{2^{29}\log(2/\epsilon)/2}\rceil$. Each central bin and each tail interval incur at most one $2^{-52}$ tick from the uniform midpoint grid. Coupling successive draws and summing over all conditional bins bounds the difference in coverage. The confidence calculation treats pseudorandom draws under the stated ideal sampling law and does not estimate apparatus errors in a laboratory. [Count decoder and error budget](readback.py)

The thirteenth lower singular bound is positive for the measured response. This excludes identifying the twelve signed responses of this instrument with a complete closed twelve-dimensional port action. A closed quotient in a restricted sector remains possible. Assigning an angular representation to a freely traveling charged particle requires an independent record on that sector. The evidence uses the coherent return to the offered source winding. Summing over all final source modes without recording that return implements a different instrument.
