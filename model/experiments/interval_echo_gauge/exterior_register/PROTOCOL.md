# Five occupied modes at two material stations

Enroll cell zero and its least-numbered most distant owner on the twenty-cell Interval Echo federation. In each cell take the lexicographically first five disjoint ordered internal dimer edges with a degree-five marked endpoint, ordering each edge by the smallest eligible ordered pair. Each dimer holds one vacancy. Its two positions encode the absence and presence of an exterior mode. Enroll three colour slots followed by two weak slots. Give an occupied colour slot charge −2 and an occupied weak slot charge 3 in units of six times hypercharge. The readout uses these supplied weights.

Send each of the ten source rails through its own sequence of full internal and bridge exchanges. Each sequence interchanges just its endpoints and restores every intermediate register. Protect the four external load standards used by the two observers. Each observer probes both rails of each dimer with the zero and double standards through native echo, agreement read and undo. Keep every raw click word. Decode a malformed register as a refusal. Use a separate coherent flight for phase tests.

On every sharp five-bit input read the sender, deliver it and read the recipient. Repeat the same route after setting exchange on all external links to zero, retaining the contrast, appointments, internal exchanges, readouts and physical addresses. Independently classify the two complete received and sent banks by measured colour and weak occupation. Use degree and decreasing colour count to order rows within each nontrivial degree. Include both neutral endpoints in the raw record. Evaluate every subset of the ten remaining rows. Record nonemptiness, conjugacy exclusion, and the four integer anomaly sums in the order gravitational, mixed colour, mixed weak and cubic charge. Compare the entire output to the finite exterior selection theorem. Challenge each survivor by toggling each one of its ten input rows.

For an independent test of the wedge signs, allow addition at mode j only on a zero reading of that dimer. Apply a private phase of π to each preceding marked rail and interchange the two rails at j. This is the ordered exterior insertion with mode 0 first. Test the creation and adjoint operators on the full 32-dimensional code. In separate flights prepare one lower mode i in the plus state, occupy any subset of the three spectator modes and leave j empty. Apply insertion at j, transport the five dimers and measure i in the x basis by a second dimer rotation. Use every i < j and spectator subset. Run an ordinary insertion that omits the prefix phases with all other pulses fixed. Also challenge a previously occupied j, recording the refusal. Hold all source occupations and the detector convention fixed across the two sign arms.

The operator targets are perfect complete-register delivery for sharp starts, refusal for a bank that cannot cross the boundary, the same mask certificates from both observers, unit anticommutators on equal modes and zero on unequal modes, and opposite deterministic output bits for the two coherent insertion arms. These are design tests of the finite register. Native matter multiplets, dynamical charge conservation and physical fermion statistics have separate acceptance criteria.
