## The channel on a physical material mark

Fill every port with one unit. Give port $r$ load zero and exactly one other port load two. All seam memories start blank. Coherent superpositions of the excess address and arbitrary untouched references are allowed. During each transport pulse open a single chosen blank contact and park the other contacts and degree-reference terms. The native 99-dimensional seam Hamiltonian preserves the nine blank words. On those words its generator is

$$h=(I-T)/2+8C,\qquad C|a,b\rangle=(a-b)^2|a,b\rangle,$$

where $T$ interchanges the two loads. Here $T^2=I$, $[T,C]=0$, and $C$ has integer eigenvalues. A pulse of duration $\pi$ therefore applies exactly $T$. In particular the vacancy moves to its chosen neighbor with certainty even when the neighboring port carries the excess. Every step sends a basis vector to its exchanged basis vector with amplitude $+1$. The induction on steps gives the channel on every complex superposition, also when entangled with a reference. For comparison, changing the contrast coefficient to $15/2$ assigns relative amplitudes $i,-1,-i$ to three excess positions around one triangle. The coherence statement uses the actual coefficient. A rooted vacancy walk that returns to $r$ permutes the remaining $m$ labels. Starting the excess at any one of these labels measures its image. Thus these labels certify faithfulness. The exact kernel consists of the walks whose successive swaps return every remaining label to its original port, computable by a single pass through the walk. The based walk group modulo this kernel is $S_m$ below. No nonidentity permutation gives a scalar operator or an identity channel on the full excess fiber. The group center is trivial for these $m$. The walk kernel itself can contain noncentral words.

There is also an exact relation to the original freely evolving source. Between *different* adjacent vacancy fibers the generator block is $-P/2$, where $P$ is precisely the same load exchange. Consequently each first-order crossing in positive time has phase $i$ times the same normalized permutation. If the scalar phase is retained, the group of based **leading crossing words** is the pairs $(i^L,P)$ with $(-1)^L=\operatorname{sgn}(P)$. Each of the $L$ swaps is odd on all labels and the vacancy returns. Any achieved $P$ can be reached with either allowed phase by adding one backtrack. The scalar kernel of the projection to the channel is then $\{I,-I\}$. A backtrack produces a common scalar even for a mark far from that edge. These phases concern crossing amplitudes in the leading expansion. Finite all-open flights include the other trajectories and their weights. [First-order block and finite-wait data](README.md)

## Why the group is the full symmetric group

**Theorem.** Let a finite undirected graph contain a triangle and be connected after the deletion of any single vertex. Then rooted vacancy walks generate $S_{|V|-1}$ in the material fiber described above, at any root.

1. Start with the triangle. One lap permutes its two occupied labels by a transposition. Changing the vacancy root by walking along a path conjugates the groups by the resulting bijection of labels. This applies on each connected subgraph.
2. Suppose on a two-vertex-connected subgraph $S$ every old permutation occurs. Add a simple open ear $u,x_1,\ldots,x_k,v$ with distinct old endpoints and new interior. Start the vacancy at $u$ and choose an old vertex $y$ different from both endpoints. There is a simple path in $S$ from $v$ to $u$ avoiding $y$. Following the ear and this return path gives a simple cycle. Its permutation $C$ is one cycle on the visited occupied vertices, including $v$ and every new vertex, and fixes $y$. The old transposition $(v,y)$ is available. Its conjugates by the powers of $C$ supply $(x_j,y)$ for every new vertex. Old permutations and these transpositions generate every permutation of the enlarged set.
3. Adding such an ear preserves connectivity after deleting one vertex. Deleting an old endpoint leaves the remaining ear attached to the other endpoint. Deleting an ear interior leaves its two pieces attached to the connected old subgraph. For any proper current subgraph in the prescribed graph, a component outside it must attach to at least two distinct old vertices, as otherwise removing its only attachment disconnects the graph. There is a new open ear through that component. Iteration finishes in finitely many steps. Remaining edges can only contribute permutations already in $S_m$.

The verifier reconstructs a spanning ear sequence from the executable adjacency, checks each edge and every membership condition, and hashes the complete certificate. Thus the conclusion for each registered board follows even without a family-level combinatorial assumption. An independent labelled configuration search on a six-vertex two-ear graph enumerates all $5!=120$ based permutations and both permitted crossing phases per permutation. Two triangles meeting at one articulation give only four permutations at the articulation, providing a check on the connectivity hypothesis. ## Which doublet reaches the next owner?

Choose consecutive ports $a,b,c$ and a common neighboring vacancy center. The two triangle turns give the transpositions $(ab),(bc)$. Routes from the shared vacancy root to that center avoid the three marks. On their three-position space the operations preserve the uniform mode $u=(1,1,1)/\sqrt 3$ and its two-dimensional complex orthogonal complement. The complement carries the real standard representation of $S_3$ extended to complex amplitudes. On the 80-owner board one closed route of 210 pulses takes the ordered triple $(2,4,5)$ to $(39,40,41)$. If $W$ is its permutation, the two destination operations are exactly $W(ab)W^{-1}$ and $W(bc)W^{-1}$ on the entire 959-position fiber. All codewords and both halves of a reference-tagged doublet are transported coherently. The ideal entanglement fidelity is one. Erasing the three position coherences reduces this fidelity to $1/3$.

For a phase-sensitive binary test, put $v=(1,\omega,\omega^2)/\sqrt3$, $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$. Send $(u+v)/\sqrt2$ and measure the receiving projector onto $(u+iv)/\sqrt2$ after the two different orders of local turns. Each order fixes $u$ and rotates $v$ by one of the opposite cube-root phases. The two probabilities are $1/2+\sqrt3/4$ and $1/2-\sqrt3/4$. Out of 262,144 simulated starts per order there were 244,476 and 17,555 clicks, respectively, with every start counted. The receiver and preparation use two real Givens rotations on actual neighboring contacts with addressed degree phases. This is a driven cross-owner transport experiment on the model. In the complete fiber all commuting matrices have one diagonal entry and one off-diagonal entry because the group is transitive on positions and on ordered pairs of distinct positions. The orthogonal projectors in the commutant are therefore $0,P_u,P_\perp,I$, now with $u$ the uniform vector on *all* $m$ positions. Consequently the full group has no invariant two- or three-dimensional subspace on these boards. The local doublet moves to a new subspace under the specified transport. ## A complete measurement grammar on the invariant algebra

For a state $\rho$ define $p=\operatorname{Tr}(P_u\rho)$. The complete set of invariant effects is

$$E=\alpha P_u+\beta P_\perp,\qquad 0\leq\alpha,\beta\leq1.$$

The probability in the model's operational state/instrument rule is $\alpha p+\beta(1-p)$. Conversely, every normalized positive finitely additive probability on these effects has this form. Additivity and positivity make each axis monotone, which extends normalization on rational multiples to real multiples by rational bounds. The two axis weights add to one. For an untouched reference the invariant block coefficients can instead be operators on the reference. Uniform averaging of an untagged state over all loops gives $pP_u+(1-p)P_\perp/(m-1)$.

Here is a native instrument for every compatible pair of these scalar effects. Keep the vacancy at $r$ and span the connected punctured graph by a tree. Each occupied leaf whose folded subtree has size $b$ is rotated into its parent of current mass $a$ using cosine $\sqrt{a/(a+b)}$ and sine $\sqrt{b/(a+b)}$. Exactly $m-1$ such rotations send $u$ into one sharp port. Reading that port versus all other ports realizes the invariant binary effect. In this one-excess sector an addressed contact on the two possible excess positions has block $8.5I-X/2$ while the unit/unit word has zero energy. An isolated degree-five reference supplies relative frequency $8(2^2-1^2)=24$. Conjugation with permutation pulses on the punctured graph routes any chosen address to that reference for an arbitrary single-position phase. This implements the cosine/sine rotations, including removal of the common two-position phase relative to the other positions. The measured effect uses coarse-graining of a full terminal address. The more detailed address can disturb the orthogonal sector, which matters when specifying an instrument for subsequent quantum measurements.
