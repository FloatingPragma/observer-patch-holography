By restarting at first passage, the probability of ever reaching (x) is the expected visits at (x) from the eight central source corners divided by expected visits starting at (x). Source age matters. For example, at short-guide address (7,4,4) in zero-based coordinates the hit probabilities are 0.007568 at four ticks, 0.06960 at 64 ticks and 0.07764 in the long-age limit.
