# When several local overlap repairs cause an exterior tide

On the 80-owner carrier, observers can register a message circuit on which three repaired overlaps suffice to produce an inward change in the initial relative acceleration of two exterior material probes. All single repairs and all two-repair preparations leave this pair unchanged. Source measurements predict the sign, and a separate simulated route-time acquisition resolves it. The same pair is silent when all native message routes are available. Routing is part of the experimental apparatus. An unrestricted ranging experiment also tests initial material release and eventual first arrival at two beacons. Keeping the ordinarily measured selective histories and energies fixed, the entropy-stationary modular repair gives both loads the same change in their trajectory records.  p_i=a_i+\frac{b_i}{4}

\]

Independence of the initial chains supplies the cross-chain term. Blank exchanges connect all material permutations. ## How far the effect reaches with all native routes available

At two blank hops or more from every written endpoint, the native release stars have unit blank transfer for both probes. Survey the 20- and 80-owner carriers at all ports and the 320-owner carrier at its external ports. Retain the common initial chord and equal actual radii in every source and ordinary arm. On the 320-owner carrier a single repair affects 20 of 8,787 such chords, reaching a minimum distance of 18 blank hops from the written endpoints for one pair. Eight dispersed chains give 235 inward changes among 6,956 eligible chords. On the 80-owner carrier with eight dispersed chains, 263 of 5,568 chords move more inward. In the latter group the full change is weaker than the sum of the separate repairs on all 263 active chords. The two-source held route counters on all three carrier sizes separately resolve an inward change. These surveys use a common minimax center of the written endpoints on each board. The original station-219 survey used a different center. [Complete shell and clearance profiles](collective_tide.json)

For an equal-chord exterior tide to change when *every* individual repair is silent, at least one cross-range must shorten under the collective intervention alone. With positive link discounts, every baseline fastest route at that range must be blank, since repairing a paid link on a fastest route makes it shorter. Let the fully repaired route use \(k\) transmitting contacts and \(b\) blank steps, with full price \(c_f\). In a single-repair experiment let the changed and unchanged links cost \(c_s,c_o\). The integer saving \(d_{\rm blank}-b\) must satisfy
\[
 k c_f < d_{\rm blank}-b \le c_s+(k-1)c_o.
\]
Test all port pairs in 30 carrier and packing settings, including fully packed arrangements of up to 30 sources. Eighteen settings lack an integer window for any available \(k\). In the other twelve, four or eleven paid contacts are required to reach the first admissible window, and no fully repaired fastest route crosses more than three. Consequently none of these unrestricted settings has a range switched on by otherwise individually silent repairs. This explains the difference between the deliberately enrolled circuit and the more connected native geometry. It permits other differences from linear addition. Reopen native writing by visiting the three contacts once per sweep and choosing write or exchange with equal probability at each appointment. The combined gates connect all 2,110 words, have positive probability of staying put and preserve the uniform population. The third-link success difference begins at 0.003011, reaches 0.006482 by sweep 16, and falls to 0.0000645 by sweep 512. By sweep 4,096 the total-variation distance is below \(3.34\times10^{-9}\), bounding the difference of *any* subsequent probability read on these two word populations.
