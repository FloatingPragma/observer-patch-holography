# Does a written overlap select an inward response in isolated material?

Use the same 240-port ordinary sector, one measured written seam, blank comparator, double probe launch and exterior cuts as in the undisturbed-medium experiment. Both boards receive identical ordinary preparations. Evolve the single load freely for an age uniform on [0,T] before inserting the probe on each new copy. Use T = 1/32, 4, 64, 1024 and 65536 in native time. No feed, drain, position read or thermal contact occurs during this evolution. Measure the source on separate copies. Credit the predicted change in enclosed probability acceleration at insertion; identify the sign of a sustained orbit separately.

Use the uniform in-phase launch, a uniform random port, and two mixtures of ordinary launches with opposite phases on disjoint nonadjacent pairs. The last two have identical single-port and nearest-neighbor interference probabilities at preparation. To select an informative pairing, calculate the first-cut response of the written and blank generators at age window 64, greedily pair the as-yet unpaired nonneighbors with largest absolute real response entry (lexicographic tie-break), and set each pair's sign from that entry. Evaluate the frozen pairings at the other windows. The selection window is a designed sensitivity test. Keep the blank and written sources and cuts fixed. Tabulate all enrolled cuts even where the sign is unresolved.

Measure site counts and a randomized two-port real interference setting for the derivative of the summed bond quadrature at each cut. Decode using only these counts, the observed seam map and the earlier ordinary gain calibration. Repeated draws of a fixed preparation in this executable model simulate the record bank. Use 2^46 trials per setting and a simultaneous one-percent error allowance for at least 10,000 events. Check the measured combination against the finite exterior theorem. Independently contract the full two-load generator with the launch and the enclosed-position projector to verify the theorem's observable for every ordinary preparation. For the uniformly distributed age, bound the magnitude of any mean current derivative by twice the norm of its quadrature divided by T.

Also reconstruct the absolute stationary field budget of the measured thermal preparation using only its position counts, ordinary clock calibration, and written map. Sum the local differences and enclose the positive and negative parts separately. Compare these observations with the degree bound for every normalized preparation. All outputs use the native time and port calibration.
