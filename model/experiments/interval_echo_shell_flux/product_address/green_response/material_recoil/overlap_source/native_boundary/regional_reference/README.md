## The charge actually tested

Start with the same retained sharp native write $P$. One operation exchanges memories between independent copies to give $E$. The other tests whether the echo returns to blank, then applies a phase change on half the bright outcomes to give $F$. Both treatments require $17/8$ units of work. They preserve the same individual memory and load distributions and have the same initial spatial energy shares. Fix the reference by taking the ordinary equilibrium state $e^{-\beta H}/Z$ of the closed 24-port board in the one-load, unit-written-magnitude sector, then tracing out sites outside the region. The accessible modular operator is $K_R=-\log\rho_R$ on its support. This supplied equilibrium reference and sector specify which modular charge the argument tests. On a symmetric cut the occupied principal block of the reduced Gibbs state has the same four projectors. Its logarithm and spectral projectors act independently in these channels. ## What does predict the material response?

The probe reading is the mean distance from the source in graph hops, obtained from the change in port occupations when an additional far load is released. Subtract the no-additional-load reading and average the two mirror launches. In the signed-negative source subspace order the four basis states by the memory coin and the endpoint occupied by the original load. Two operations on independently prepared sources yield $u$, the probability that the coin agrees with the outgoing endpoint of a native write, and $v$, the probability that repeating the native echo returns to blank. The latter is the projection onto the coherent sum of the two matched words.

In both the one-load and two-load sectors the free generator preserves the left-owner load minus the memory coin. This forbids coherence between the unmatched words and the matched two-dimensional block in the induced source response. The mirror swaps the unmatched pair and exchanges the matched words. Mirror averaging thus equates the unmatched diagonals and makes the two matched diagonals and their real off-diagonal entries pairwise equal. The mirror exchanges the *launches* as well as the source states. These constraints apply at all times and to the infinite-time mean. They give the exact operator form
$$\langle R(t)\rangle=a(t)+b(t)u+c(t)v.$$
Three preparable standards determine the coefficients on the same board. At time four the coefficients are $2.48759844,-0.40674261,+0.53706662$. The retained source has $(u,v)=(1,1/2)$, the exchanged source $(1/2,1/4)$, and the feedback source $(1,1/4)$. Thus the outward shift of $+0.069105$ and inward shift of $-0.134267$ follow from records accessible **before** launch.
