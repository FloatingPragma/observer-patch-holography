**Yes, provided the energy is supplied.** On the 320-owner board one ordinary load can commission 64 simultaneous unit-contrast records. The full native energy rises from 50.5 to 581.75. Addressed echo pulses supply 259.25 units in expectation and sharp reads supply 272. Once every service stops, the path retains at least 496 units of local energy for all free times. Any one-load all-blank control has at most 18 on that same path, so the local excess is at least **478**. Its spatial support is the prepared path. The experiment establishes storage and its price. It provides no microscopic law setting the number or arrangement of such paths in a galaxy. [Priced experiment](TRAIL.md) · [Physical paths, native work and controls](trail.json)

Every set of $j$ trail edges carries $8.5j-D_j$ above the matched ordinary energy, with $0\le D_j\le16.25$ at every free time. Evacuating the load gives an exactly additive regional modular energy, with the detector work priced. A separate six-port converter can deposit one permanent record per ordinary donor. ## How a single load commissions 64 permanent marks

We used a geometry-only shortest path to the farthest port on each of the three original native boards. At each fresh seam, start from one sharp load on the entering port. The supplied controller repeats an echo followed by a sharp load and memory read until the load reaches the next port with a unit head. It keeps all failures. The three reachable words are the original blank B, the written state C with the load still at the entrance, and the successful written exit A. From B the chances of C and A are 1/2 each. From C the chances of B, C and A are 1/2, 1/4 and 1/4. Every attempt thus has success probability at least 1/4. A link terminates almost surely, with the chance of more than r attempts at most (3/4)ʳ. A union bound puts the probability of needing more than Mr attempts for the M-link trail below M(3/4)ʳ. Expected visits to B and C are 3/2 and 1. The final state at each successful link is unique. Two independently prepared completed trails have identical sharp records and physically swapping their memories costs **zero**. A half exchange can move the load but leaves a blank head. On each completed edge the mean change of the full native energy **at the sharp reads alone** is 17/4. The three accessible outcomes have different frontier memories. All other Hamiltonian terms are diagonal between them, so these work changes follow directly from the full local quantum states immediately before and after the sharp read. After commissioning, the full free Hamiltonian commutes with every seam's magnitude. On an empty ordinary pair 00 with any coherent |d|=1 head, the local term is κI+(I−R)/2 and has minimum energy κ. On the rest of that magnitude sector it is positive. An ordinary unit touches at most two path edges, so M simultaneous magnitude-one links retain at least κ(M−2) energy **on the path itself** at every subsequent time, even when the load delocalizes or becomes entangled with the heads. For the all-blank control each empty link costs zero and an occupied one has maximum energy κ+1. Thus the same path has at most 2(κ+1) on any one-load blank state. These operator inequalities give the last column at κ=8. No continuing repair current is required for this bound after preparation.
