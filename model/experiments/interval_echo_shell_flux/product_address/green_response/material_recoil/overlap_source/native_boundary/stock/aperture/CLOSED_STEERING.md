# Changing the pairing on the same closed board

The exact middle-port identity, its bond price and the occupied-word weights are known. Test an addressed preparation that preserves the full ordinary inventory on every trial. Start with the uniform native class. Leave all originally rejected or already retained trials in place. For an accepted word i keep it with probability min(1,z_i), where z_i is its target-to-native probability ratio. On the remaining trials choose a target sharp word in proportion to the positive part of z_j−1. Use the physical three-contact path, native echo and native half-exchange plus sharp records to reach that word on the same board. At every step choose an allowed native appointment with a nonzero chance of moving one hop closer in the measured word graph. Keep all failed appointments. Termination must follow from the finite transition law.

Use the minimally changed conditional law that preserves the native distribution within each two-head cell. Enroll 32,768 independent starting boards at the uniform native stationary law, a separate random seed and all original rejects. Report the number of interventions, their duration, path checks, before/after source and middle-load counts and maximum violation of the local identities. The controller is an experimental preparation. Native formation of a preferred galactic pairing and the energy its controller consumes are separate questions. The aggregate energy and galaxy comparisons are known before this run.
