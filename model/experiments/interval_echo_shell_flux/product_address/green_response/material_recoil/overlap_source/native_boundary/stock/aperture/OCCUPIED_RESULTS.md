## Why the instantaneous source can vanish while later motion changes

For a background of ones and a single defect of load \(t=0\) or \(2\), the live exchange coefficient is \(-1/2\). On a written contact, its two singly occupied endpoints have a measured additional energy of 0, −32 or −33 in the frozen ordinary parity sectors. Putting the defect at either endpoint removes that energy. Let \(P_e\) indicate either endpoint, and let \(a_e\in\{0,32,33\}\) be its positive removal cost. The free defect generator is \(H=K+V\) with \(V=\sum_e a_eP_e\). The ordinary part \(K\) includes the native six-slot degree correction. Exact integer comparisons reconstruct both mobile generators, the compressed ordinary clamp, and the written columns. Each defect propagates freely over the live links throughout the flight. \[
 -\partial_t\operatorname{Im}\bar\rho_{uv}-\operatorname{Re}[K,\bar\rho]_{uv}
 = \sum_h p_h(V_{h,u}-V_{h,v})\operatorname{Re}\rho_{h,uv}.
