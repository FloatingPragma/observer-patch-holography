## Do the clock rates predict the gravity inferred in galaxies?

Between two consecutive radii, the potential rises by at least \(GM_{\rm floor}(<r_i)(1/r_i-1/r_{i+1})\). Summing these increments gives a lower bound without interpolating the extra mass.
