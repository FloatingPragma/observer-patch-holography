# What can two local observers establish about the source of extra gravity?

In the executable model, two observers can establish a signed overlap correlation by reading one written contact each. A separate energy measurement asks whether the ordinary material on that same preparation accounts for the correlation at the calibrated temperature. It accounts for too little. The second test is relevant to the proposed identification of the measured charge with an energy that gravitates. Gravity could be much too weak for either local observer to measure directly.

Each observer records one written magnitude and the preparation identifier. Accept the pair if both contacts are written with magnitude zero or two. In the new bank there were 3,869 rejected preparations and 1,024 accepted pairs. Sorted by the first observer's readout, the joint counts were

The source preparation samples the connected 2200 inventory uniformly. Its exact accepted table is proportional to \((48,176,112,112)\). Its cross-product odds \(R=P_{00}P_{22}/(P_{02}P_{20})\) equal \(3/11\). At fixed one-contact marginals, drawing the two contacts independently changes \(P_{22}\) by \(D=1/14\), giving a connected modular increment \(-D\log R=0.092806\) nats. The earlier, larger source bank measured \(0.272669<R<0.272791\). The observer's inputs are the local symbols and their pairing. The component calculation independently predicts the population sampled by those records.

A second hypothesis predicts \(R\ge0.5946035\) from ordinary energy readings under the assumptions below. For the small source bank the enrolled test rounds the null down to \(R\ge0.59\). Conditional on the measured row and column totals \(n_A,n_B\), a table with \(k\) double-two receipts has weight \(\binom{n_A}{k}\binom{N-n_A}{n_B-k}R^k\). Increasing \(R\) shifts this conditional law toward larger \(k\), since the ratio of its new and old weights increases with \(k\). The largest lower-tail probability for the null is therefore at \(0.59\). Summing those weights exactly gives \(1.91\times10^{-7}\) for the observed source. The independent-pair control gives \(0.999999\) and the boundary control \(0.8684\). The controls use prepared accepted contacts. The count, stopping rule and significance level were fixed after the large-bank odds and spectral bound were known, before drawing these three banks. ## What energy do the ordinary instruments assign?

Use the unclamped 240-port filled chart, the two written seams and the same thermometer, \(\beta=\log(8/7)\). An ideal simulated spectrometer on a uniform material address measures a level \(E\) and accepts with probability \(\exp[-\beta(E-L)]\), where the common lower energy \(L\) is fixed per tracer. The accept frequency measures the thermal trace up to a known dimension and common factor. Separate banks sample the sharp contact lines and the complete magnitude blocks. The latter retain the entire written background energy of each line. We compare these energy records with the marginal-preserving repair. Gibbs equilibration *across* protected head lines is a testable preparation hypothesis. The law preserves them during the read.

A written magnitude zero has two lines, both with filled energy and defect cost zero. The four lines of a written two have respective (filled energy, contact removal cost) \((0,32),(0,33),(32,0),(33,0)\) in native units. These are simultaneous eigenlines of the complete native contact columns. For a vacancy or excess load, let \(K\) be its measured inactive generator, \(P_A,P_B\) the endpoint projectors, and \(z_{ab}=\operatorname{Tr}\exp[-\beta(K+aP_A+bP_B)]\). Sum \(z_{ab}\exp[-\beta(e_a+e_b)]\) over each pair of magnitude blocks to get \(Z_{ij}\), counting all lines with their multiplicities. Use \(R_{\rm bath}=Z_{00}Z_{22}/(Z_{02}Z_{20})\). Every choice of two additive magnitude prices cancels exactly, even if they are chosen to reproduce the two single-contact marginals.

## Why increasing the number of loads cannot recover the measured odds on this preparation

Allow any fixed nonnegative integers of independent vacancy and excess copies. They share the same two written lines throughout each trial. For one species write \(u_a=z_{a0}/z_{00}\), \(v_b=z_{0b}/z_{00}\), \(r_{ab}=z_{ab}z_{00}/(z_{a0}z_{0b})\). At fixed sharp lines multiply the single-species factors over all copies. Only then sum the lines. The pooled odds are the average of \(\prod_s r_{ab,s}^{m_s}\) under two independent line weights proportional to \(p_a\prod_s u_{a,s}^{m_s}\) and \(p_b\prod_s v_{b,s}^{m_s}\). Here \(p_a\) is the isolated filled-head Gibbs weight and \(m_s\) the inventory of each species. This order retains correlations induced by sharing the head lines.

For species \(s\), let \(t_s\) bound each active single-line attenuation from above. Every pair satisfies \(r_{ab,s}^8\ge t_s\) on the full law, including the measurement errors. Put \(T=\prod_s t_s^{m_s}\). The resulting mean odds are at least \(T^{1/8}\). The isolated probability of an inactive line is \(p_0=0.0129000\). Under either tilted weight the active probability is at most \(f=(1-p_0)T/[p_0+(1-p_0)T]\). A pair containing an inactive line has odds one and every other line pair has positive odds, giving the separate lower bound \(1-f^2\). Splitting at \(T=1/64\) gives **\(R_{\rm bath}\ge0.59460355\)** for every inventory, including zero copies. An arbitrary independent magnitude price cannot change it. The proof assumes the stated spectra, a common temperature and fixed inventory. It leaves room for interacting material, a different preparation law or another observer-measured stress. A direct equilibrium explanation would require an additional joint free-energy difference of at least \(\beta^{-1}\log(0.59460355/0.272791)>5.83\) native units. With just one excess load the missing price is 9.69 units. These are requirements on the proposed explanation. A price inferred by inverting source counts cannot also serve as independently measured gravitating energy.

## Does granting the charge its thermal energy price explain the orbits?

Grant that \(q/\beta\) gravitates at the ordinary energy-to-mass conversion. The local log odds vary with filling and even change sign. The astronomical calculation uses that local value on each imaged patch, the two earlier ordinary insertion spectra and the four-owner normalization. All **56 forecasts** on ten previously examined disks give **26 of 369** speeds within two quoted errors, equal to ordinary matter alone. None reproduces a whole measured curve. The largest change in circular speed is **0.181 km/s** or less. These forecasts use the registered spherical weak-field continuation, seven common capacities, two tracer maps and two access rules. No orbit sets a price or a radial weight. For the OPH gravity claim the necessary link is to the conserved stress in the finite fixed-volume balance. Measuring its exact source locally would discharge that premise. The equilibrium test establishes a real correlation that this ordinary thermal model cannot generate, and the orbital comparison limits one proposed conversion to gravitating mass. The construction still needs a source whose independently measured stress, geometry and abundance entail the attraction observers infer.
