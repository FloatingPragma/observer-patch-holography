Record the signed written contrasts, park both ordinary inputs, and test native full-pulse transfer of a new ordinary single from a randomly chosen side. Retain the memory after a failed probe. Measure the summed signed contrast on independent source preparations. Compare a double probe and an exchange-only preparation from blank on the same alphabet.

Keep the chosen physical ports. Compare uniformly sampled source contacts with a measurement strictly at the arrival vertex of the walker, using a uniformly selected starting even vertex. Enroll equally spaced and contiguous sets of 1, 4 and one quarter of the available source contacts, as well as empty and full standards. Derive an all-placement bound in the dilute limit. Include the ambiguity from storing opposite orientations of the same sources at fixed inventory.

Also test a probe directly on the original third memory, both when that one source persists and when each appointment samples an independent source. A repeatable receiver of a fluctuation would close a measurement premise; its spacetime coupling needs its own records.
