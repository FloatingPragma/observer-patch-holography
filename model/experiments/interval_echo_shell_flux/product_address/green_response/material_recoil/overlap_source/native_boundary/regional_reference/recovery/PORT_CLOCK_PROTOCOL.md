# Can moving the loading standard reproduce the clocks?

Use the same 24-port board, three equipped memories, paired/empty baseline and two clock readers as in the [joint flight experiment](JOINT_RECORDS.md). Its fixed-address result and inward range are known. Give a mobile unit access to every port, one per preparation, independently of the clock exposure. Randomize the source coin fairly. Keep the paired half of the baseline fixed in every loading arm. In each overlap arm change only the paired half. Compare the two routed endpoints, the inward band of measured source agreement 0.67–0.69 and a directly propagated interior at 0.68. Use the two clocks alone, without a launched tracer. Count the four sign pairs on fresh simulated preparations after supplied exposures 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 12. The observers receive sign pairs and apparatus-setting labels. They receive no address of the injected unit or hidden propagation state.

Any static probability distribution on addresses predicts a convex combination of the 24 loading changes. Positive strength in linear response enlarges these to a cone. Seek a bounded linear score on recorded sign frequencies that is strictly positive for the whole specified source interval and nonpositive for every positive loading profile. Fit the score to population predictions, then freeze it before evaluating independently seeded samples. Report what a physical one-unit lottery can achieve even if the amplitude-free cone test is inconclusive. Record each fork of this test on the contrast-stiffness-zero control. A positive control uses a supplied probability 1/2 of admission, split equally over ports 0 and 23. Compare the middle routing with the independently propagated endpoints. Cross-check the no-tracer two/four shutter with the archived joint clock counts. These tests concern incoherent, positive, static load profiles with the same equipped memories and readout. A time-dependent or coherently prepared field and a metric limit have different hypotheses.

Use seed 193241 and 2^30 simulated draws per four-outcome bank. Bound all individual frequencies jointly by Hoeffding's inequality with failure probability 0.01 across both laws, all preparations and all exposures. Bound differences from the shared baseline without presuming cancellation. A measured separator may be weakened by rounding towards zero provided the resulting score is evaluated afresh. Report probability units and retain negative or unresolved controls.
