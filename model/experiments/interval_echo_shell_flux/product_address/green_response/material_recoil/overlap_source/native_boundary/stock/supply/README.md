# What ordinary fuel can leave in a native written record

One ordinary unit can transfer the complete energy spectrum of its donor bond into one permanent written contrast. Its position follows the ordinary source wave before capture. A sharp fuel preparation produces exactly one nonzero record per trial, including when its wave reaches many candidate sites. The six-port transfer needs switched native operations that exchange energy with the material. The free Hamiltonian on an unmodified blank board produces zero writes. These results distinguish a funded, persistent local energy from the energy required for an extra gravitational source. [Frozen protocol](PROTOCOL.md) · [Recorded outcomes](results.json)

## Whole-board energy bound before constructing an instrument

With all memories blank, a single ordinary unit obeys $H=48I+L/2$, including the original private degree references. The maximum degree is six on each federation, so $0\le L\le12I$ and the entire initial energy distribution lies within $[48,54]$. The native free generator commutes separately with the blank and every magnitude sector of each head. This statement holds for entangled inputs and at any exposure. [Candidate specification](../../../../../../../../../candidate_laws/contrast_memory_spherical_v1/SPEC.md) · [Exact local integer certificate](module.py)

Suppose an operation deposits $M$ empty magnitude-one heads, leaves every other memory blank, and retains the original ordinary unit away from both ends of every marked link. The expectation of each former blank term at those empty ends was zero. On this support all remaining terms give the same ordinary energy expectation as on the blank board, at least 48. Each written link contributes at least 8 for every coin and coherent sign state. The final whole-board expectation is at least $48+8M$. Already for one separated mark this requires at least two units of net work for *any* initial blank one-load state. For the previous sharp start with energy 50.5, the bound is $8M-2.5$. The inequality also holds in mixtures or coherent superpositions over deposit positions, since the free generator preserves the magnitude at every location. It applies at the instant of separation. The previous 64-link trail left the unit at a marked endpoint and has its own less restrictive lower bound. [Ordinary source and trail work](../README.md)

## Converting a genuine ordinary bond

Use five native links in the order E—C—A—B—D—F. The donor AB starts blank, as do the two near and two far parking links. Other incident contacts and degree references carry an idle coherent write of ordinary 00, which has zero energy on every ordinary load. Prepare $\alpha|10\rangle+\beta|01\rangle$ on AB. Apply the native echo on AB, swap A with C and B with D, then swap C with E and D with F. Finally echo the two now-empty near links into dark zero heads. Every swap is a native blank exposure of $\pi$ at the candidate's fixed coupling 8. Cold donors receive exactly the same operations and finish with magnitude zero. There are no reads, retries, or replacements in the capture procedure. [Direct full-operator contraction](module.py)

In a hot donor the echo retains the original orientation as the sign label and the output coin chooses the parking wing. After parking, both ordinary donor endpoints are empty. The signed reversal on that written link interchanges its two possible input orientations. The final record therefore carries the original donor's reversal-even or reversal-odd spectral probabilities, $|\alpha+\beta|^2/2$ at 8 and $|\alpha-\beta|^2/2$ at 9. Both statements hold at the level of the isometry, including entanglement with other addresses. The original ordinary unit remains in its own parking wing. Once all services are released, the dark near contacts keep the ordinary load out of the empty core. The record position and donor energy are conserved while the ordinary unit can oscillate between middle and far parking sites.

The entries give the expectation of the **same complete material Hamiltonian** after each pulse. In the isolated single receiver the parked ordinary energy has mean 8.5. A work source must cover the changes during capture even when the initial and final material means agree. These numbers price the work exchanged with the modeled material. A physical autonomous pulse generator and its quantum state remain unspecified.

There is also an exact obstacle to an energy-conserving replacement acting on this isolated material alone. In the initial one-unit blank sector on the six-port path, $2H=17D-A$, with degree matrix $D$ and adjacency matrix $A$. The desired separate record and parking wing each have energies 8 or 9, so the complete terminal state is supported on total energies $16,17,18$. The integer determinants $\det(17D-A-2EI)$ at those three energies are respectively **1248, 288 and 1656**. Thus the initial and terminal total-energy supports are disjoint. A unitary commuting with this material's Hamiltonian has zero transition amplitude between them, even though the ordinary donor's local spectrum transfers exactly and whole-system mean work vanishes. This spectral check is specific to the isolated five-link device. A larger bath or a different closed material would need its own energy ledger. ## Where one load actually deposits

Each owner gets two disjoint donor dimers on a connected four-port core, with one distinct two-link parking wing per endpoint. The frozen topology rule maximizes the number of original local gateways retained, then takes the lexicographically first working layout. All actual links between core ports remain active, including links between owners. Other material is prepared with coherent zero heads. The external timetable isolates the parking wings for an unaddressed ordinary flight and restores them for a single uniform capture. Since the unit is on the core when the wings are isolated and when they are restored, these switches exchange exactly −8.5 and +8.5 with the material. The starting address is the smallest core port on each board. The choice of the geometry, initial fuel, exposure, service word and cold background is part of this experiment. ## How many records a finite preparation pays for

With one exchange-even unit in each of $B$ selected donors, the same uniform word deterministically produces exactly $B$ reversal-even stationary records and retains all $B$ ordinary units. Every cold donor writes a zero head. The result uses the finite one-pass geometry and input stock. The numbers below include every blank link and every prepared zero head. The initial and final ordinary configurations differ, as do the connecting-link energies.

| Owners | Initial ordinary fuel units | Initial material energy | After middle parking | Final record energy | Final ordinary energy | Net work on material |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20 | 40 | 821.5 | 1,000 | 320 | 340 | −161.5 |
| 80 | 160 | 3,256.25 | 4,000 | 1,280 | 1,360 | −616.25 |
| 320 | 640 | 13,042 | 16,000 | 5,120 | 5,440 | −2,482 |

Identical operation on a completely cold board gives zero stock, and preparing only the two root donors gives two records. Enlarging the board gives a larger stock only when ordinary fuel is supplied over the enlarged region. The one-pass result and the spatially separated all-blank bound do not constrain every possible externally powered, repeatedly switched material. The measured positive record energy here accounts for energy already supplied to a prepared ordinary system. A physical source of the switching work, a mechanism selecting a halo-scale spatial distribution, and a gravitational response at fixed total ordinary supply remain open.
