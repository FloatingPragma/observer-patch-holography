# Does ordinary pair occupancy activate a stored source without a pulse clock?

Integer inspection of the native contact shows that a written zero and a written magnitude two have the same material generator for a single ordinary unit. Two ordinary units at opposite ends of the seam give a different magnitude-two head block. Test whether that distinction selects a weak exposure and an extended change in measured motion under uninterrupted free evolution.

Use the original measured written seam on the 240-port board with all other seams blank. Keep the same kinetic cut on both source arms. Enroll two indistinguishable ordinary unit loads at distinct ports. Verify all native contact columns on ordinary words 00, 01, 10 and 11, including all output words and head leakage. Calibrate the conditional endpoint energy by comparing parity-resolved head fringes with the endpoints empty and with an ordinary unit at each endpoint. Include both coin parities and both sign parities of the stored double, and a written-zero control. Derive the exact passive material generator and the dependence of its strength on the simultaneous ordinary endpoint reading.

Prepare the same real in-phase uniform pair over all distinct ports. The initial endpoint probability is fixed by the address count. For each independently calibrated sector evolve the full two-unit motion on the same carrier up to native times 1, 4, 16 and 64. Measure the joint endpoint frequency and the enclosed fraction of ordinary units at two, three, four and six hops about the written seam. Publish all signed differences from the written-zero control. Use independent finite-count banks. Also test an incoherent uniform pair with the same initial sharp position distribution. Check its time dependence by a finite identity. The measured enclosed probabilities describe ordinary material; connecting them to a distant test probe or an astronomical stress needs its own implication.

For a source-only modular test use the existing 2200 stationary repair preparation on the first two actual seams, retaining the trials where both are written and each has magnitude zero or two. Count both outcomes and rejected trials. Compare that native joint distribution with the product of its measured marginals on the same two seams. Use the joint distribution itself as a reference on the measured four-outcome algebra, and report the change of the connected logarithm when the two records are independently paired. Test whether a one-unit ordinary detector can distinguish the arms at any time. A conclusion on this measured algebra leaves open more general references, material fillings and probe observables.

On the same source pair, prepare two ordinary units coherently on the endpoints of the first or the second seam. For the local phase test park each alternative, dwell for 1/32 native time, recombine and count the two outputs. Predict the fringe change from the measured source joint table, the independently read conditional endpoint frequencies and the native parity probabilities of a sharp head. Include an independent held fringe bank. For the unparked experiment use the same complete uniform ordinary-pair launch and all four times and radii specified above, enclosing the union of the two sources. Read both the native joint and independently paired preparations. Keep the four binary conditional responses so the measured covariance can be multiplied by the independently computed mixed difference. Report finite statistical resolution and retain any nulls. Distinguish the contact-pair signal from a gravitational interpretation. Both source arms retain the same two written seams and all calibration uses ordinary units.
