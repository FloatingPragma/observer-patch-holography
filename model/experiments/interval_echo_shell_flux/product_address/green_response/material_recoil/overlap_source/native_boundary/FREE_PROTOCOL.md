For each graph distance from the written seam publish the enclosed difference of actual energy and load. Share each interaction equally between its endpoints. Keep negative responses. Record times 0, 1, 4, 16 and 64 and the infinite-time projected mean for the two-owner assembly. Test whether the positive global shift guarantees a positive shell at every radius and time, whether the native dynamics alone project a flat-speed law, and whether native loads accompany any excess.
