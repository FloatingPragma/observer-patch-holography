# What can an observer infer from a local source measurement?

On the 240-port occupied chart, two readers can infer a change in the entropy of a distant boundary's enclosed records from a measurement at the two written source contacts and an independent calibration of the region. At 192 markers the prediction is about 0.106 nats per unit fraction of source re-pairing. The same observers can calibrate how the boundary responds to an ordinary load. Applying that calibration to the entropy change predicts a loss of several crossing links. Counting the links gives exactly zero change on this source variation. This closes the inference to regional entropy for the specified preparation. The proposed gravity inference through this boundary observable fails. ## The local reading and its independent prediction

Start with the earlier 2200 preparation. The accepted joint source counts estimate the two-head law \(P\), the connected charge \(q\), and the reference log odds \(\ell=\log(P_{00}P_{11}/P_{01}P_{10})\). Re-pair the two readings independently at fixed marginals. On this source \(\ell=-\log(11/3)\), and the measured displacement \(D=-q/\ell\) lies between 0.071367 and 0.071490. The exact native table gives \(D=1/14\). The regional apparatus can be calibrated on four sharply prepared head pairs without using the held re-pairing arm. For **any** regional reading with reference score \(g\), let \(g_{ij}\) be its mean at sharp head values \(i,j\), with the clamp, parity and material prepared by the registered conditional law. Direct expansion of the four source probabilities gives
\[
  \delta\langle g\rangle = D(g_{00}-g_{01}-g_{10}+g_{11}).
\]
The sign and size of each observable change are determined by its four readings. This identity also applies to the entropy tangent once its score is fixed from an independent reference. ## What does an observer see when the source labels are withheld?

The primary entropy includes the written source records. At 192 markers on the selected fixture their sharp-label entropy contributes about \(0.092806\) nats to the slope. In fact the conditional law of parity and phase given the two head magnitudes is a product of two one-head channels. Their mean conditional entropies cancel under a re-pairing at fixed marginals, and the residual sharp-label derivative equals \(q\). Conditioning on ball membership contributes another \(0.013080\)–\(0.013083\) nats. For the excess, the remaining mean within-sector material entropy contributes \(0.0000915\)–\(0.0002181\) nats. The ordinary relative material energy measured from the site and bond readings gives \(0.0003215<\beta\,\delta E<0.0003229\) nats. The additive reference energies of the two individual written contacts cancel at matched marginals. Assigning the entire \(0.106\) nats to an ordinary heat increment fails this direct energy measurement.

In a follow-up to the enrolled comparison, give a second regional reader only the ball membership, defect detections and terminal interference ports. Withhold the clamp, magnitudes, parity and receipt labels. This reader reconstructs a separate material score for each ball from fresh native copies. It is the negative logarithm of its measured material operator. Freeze these coefficients. An address reading or a randomly selected pair of ports at one of two phases then measures the score using only outcomes that this reader can record. The choice of ports and the weights use only the ball membership. Four independent sharp-source calibrations and \(D\) predict the difference on unsorted native and re-paired arms.

## An interior interference reading at fixed total heat

Weakly read whether the mobile defect is at one address and discard the result. A fraction \(r\) of trials receives this intervention. For fixed source labels the material derivative is \(r(\Pi_x R-R)\), where \(\Pi_x\) removes the coherences between \(x\) and the other addresses. An ordinary observer measures the corresponding work \(W_x=-2\sum_{j\ne x}H_{xj}\operatorname{Re}R_{jx}\) using fringes on neighboring links. For interior and exterior ports \(x,y\), dilution rates \(W_y/(W_x+W_y)\) and \(W_x/(W_x+W_y)\) deposit equal mean ordinary energy. In a normalized Gibbs preparation at inverse temperature \(\beta\), the full entropy derivative within each conserved sector is \(\beta\,\delta E\). This applies with the same \(\beta=\log(8/7)\) in every measured sector.

At the initial reading, an exterior intervention leaves the entire interior marginal unchanged. An interior intervention changes its entropy tangent by the sum of the two relative entropies between the local operator and its pinching. Indeed \(\log(\Pi_x R_B)\) is block diagonal, so its trace against \(R_B-\Pi_x R_B\) vanishes. A balanced measurement on \(x\) and one interior neighbor gives probabilities \(m+z,m-z\) before the intervention and \(m,m\) after it, plus a common idle outcome. Monotonicity of relative entropy then gives the lower bound \(z\log[(m+z)/(m-z)]\) with \(z\) the absolute measured real fringe. This proves a positive regional entropy tangent directly from a resolved fringe. Both selected sites and all their neighbors lie on their respective sides in every enrolled mask. The reduced material records confined to the boundary therefore agree initially on both interventions. The protected-area records remain identical at every tested age because the timing survey parks the mobile material. ## Precision and replay

All counts are simulated. Each individual detector setting has \(2^{52}\) ideal trials, sampled in aggregate. A position bank and both quadratures on all 28,441 unordered pairs reconstruct a 239-port material operator for each clamp, species, fixture and source-energy sector. Each quadrature estimator is the sample mean of outcomes \(+1/2,-1/2,0\). Its variance is at most one quarter of the two ports' combined detection probability. A simultaneous Hoeffding bound on the diagonals gives an upper variance bound for Bernstein intervals. A Frobenius bound on the matrix errors gives a trace distance bound below \(1.25\times10^{-5}\), and restriction to a ball cannot increase it. Every reconstructed material operator has positive spectrum. The entropy continuity bound, the individual count intervals and the reference-logarithm errors all enter the reported slope intervals. The union bound budgets 1% for these new detector banks. Forecasts also use the previously measured source interval. Detector calibration, coherent storage and equilibrium preparation are assumed ideal in this executable comparison. These sampling guarantees do not enclose floating-point roundoff. A laboratory sensitivity estimate would require a specified physical implementation.
