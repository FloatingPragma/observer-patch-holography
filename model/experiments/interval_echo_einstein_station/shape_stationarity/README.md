Distinct eigenvalues of the rational symmetric H and entries of its spectral projectors are algebraic. In a diagonal entry at least one projector coefficient is nonzero because they sum to one. For every native hopping edge at least one coefficient is nonzero because their eigenvalue-weighted sum is the nonzero hopping amplitude.
