An observer changes the correlations of two written source registers while preserving the probabilities of each register separately. Its derivative on the 20-cell screen is **+1.826 × 10⁻⁷ squared hand units**. Hence any positive multiplier of this area adds to the measured entropy slope for this preparation. In this four-dimensional space take the derivatives of both log volumes and one log area, computed from the six independent clock ranges for each ruler. ## Can either ruler, or a positive combination, measure the required signed area?

At equal proper volume and matched ordinary stress the proposed spatial collar law predicts \(dA=-(\kappa/2\pi)dK\) to leading small-ball order. A positive collar charge must reduce the rim area. On the two native tetrahedral rulers a fixed-volume transfer gives the opposite sign for **both** areas. On the 20-cell preparation the charge rises by 0.161753 nats per unit transfer, and the first-return and free-pulse areas rise by \(7.25151\times10^{-8}\) and \(2.82552\times10^{-7}\) squared hand units per unit transfer. Separate sharp reference and source samples measure \(0.16064\pm0.00328\) nats per unit transfer (one counting standard error). These are local hardware units. No numerical choice for a positive Newton constant can change that sign.

Both single-word marginal laws are fixed. On a fixed launch the conditional probe Hamiltonian is a common operator plus a term depending only on the first word and another depending only on the second. The mean of the entire operator is consequently fixed, including any restored single-word energy offsets. To keep both volumes fixed, prescribe two entries of the three-by-three word law and solve the two volume constraints for the other two independent entries. At the reference, the two-by-two Jacobian is nonsingular by exact rational elimination on the deposited clock moments. This proves a local common-volume surface by the implicit function theorem. Rational bounds on the face-area square roots certify the signs of the derivatives for those moments. Both finite preparations at parameters \(+1/32,-1/32\) have positive probabilities; 60-digit solutions match the target volumes within \(3\times10^{-50}\) fractionally. That is a numerical tolerance on those inputs. At 80 and 320 cells the locally volume-matched secants have the same signs. Transferring the 20-cell increasing tangent without adjustment leaves both fractional volume slopes below \(9\times10^{-15}\). The directions were selected on the original 20-cell population data.

For any differentiable candidate area \(F(A_1,A_2)\) increasing or constant in each argument at the reference, \(dF=F_1dA_1+F_2dA_2\ge0\) on the first transfer. The measured \(dK>0\) rules out cancellation with any positive area coefficient. Allowing \(F\) also to depend on the two held volumes leaves this result intact. A second transfer increases the first area by \(2.26725\times10^{-6}\) and decreases the second by \(8.83438\times10^{-6}\) per unit transfer. Thus simply rescaling the two rulers by positive factors cannot make them measure the same area response. Their projected log-area gradients have cosine −0.99796 on all three boards. A further stress term or another geometric instrument requires independent physical identification. These tests exclude the stated assignments on the operational tetrahedra. The local station addresses stay fixed as the host grows. The observations give no bound on the residual divided by the fourth power of a shrinking proper radius.
