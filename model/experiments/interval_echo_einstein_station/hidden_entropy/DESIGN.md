# Entropy variations among stationary repair states

On the intact one-load board distribute one load uniformly at random over the descendants of base face zero. In the selected cell prepare one of two orthogonal orbital quadrupoles which vanish at every gateway. The source knows the chosen mode and site. The regional observers receive only terminal clicks. The two modes have the same native energy and the same exterior occupation history. Test a half-and-half reference and a three-to-one reference, and perturb each mixing probability by one sixteenth in both directions. Allow the whole free Hamiltonian to act.

At release and at audit exposure four, cell observers use independently compiled inverse preparations to measure the two modes and their real and imaginary superpositions on four separate ensembles of 262,144 copies. Each instrument reports the descendant cell and a sharp return or its inclusive complement. Pooling the mode readouts defines a two-state regional meter. Check that both references retain their preparation contrast through flight and that the finite entropy difference is within 0.04 nats per unit probability of its predicted slope. A passive exterior detector pools the complete probability for occupation outside the parent, including a quiet reading. Compare the two hidden modes at native exposures zero, two and four. A bright orbital launch in each of the same source cells tests whether the exterior apparatus can register an escaping load. Run a changed kinetic law that increases each internal edge weight by the number of gateway endpoints on that edge, retaining every open edge, the exterior exchange and all carrier relabelling symmetries. Record all changed-law outcomes. A nonzero native boundary reading or a nonstationary native logical record would falsify the predicted operator reduction.

Entropy stationarity here means variation with preparation at fixed exterior records. A stationary density matrix under time evolution and a vanishing first variation of entropy are tested by different readings. Fixed proper volume, a physical vacuum reference, and an area capable of responding through active multi-load interrogation have separately stated requirements in the Einstein theorem.
