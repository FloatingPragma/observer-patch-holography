# A distance chart from the labelled charge receiver

Use the complete native terminal-port kernel for each launch port at each of the three calibrated appointments, with either charge separately and with the equal prior on signs. Treat each measured table as a Markov kernel for a single launch and read. Use the standard diffusion distance between two rows, the square root of the sum of their squared endpoint-probability differences divided by the stationary endpoint weights of the vacuum kernel. Repeated launches describe that reader; the Markov kernel is not an independent assumption about unmonitored field propagation.

Train a rank-three classical multidimensional scaling chart on addresses zero through seven at each appointment. Locate addresses eight through eleven by squared distances to the eight anchors. Hold all six distances among those four addresses out. Require every held distance within 5% of its independently measured value. Record the full centered Gram spectrum, the fraction of its sum beyond rank three, and held errors. Test the vacuum and both signs of the selected field perturbation at angles 1/64 and 1/128 using these same formulas and vacuum weights. Apply the chart reader also to the full sampled endpoint records for the held perturbations. Use a supplied, full-rank Euclidean 12-station configuration as a positive control. A successful chart concerns this endpoint-distance instrument and awaits a separate proper-time/geodesic calibration.
