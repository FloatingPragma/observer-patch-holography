## A source whose radial records agree

$$
\Pr(a,b,c)=[1+t a(b-c)]/8,\qquad |t|\leq 1/2.
$$
The other faces receive independent fair signs. This is a positive two-sided preparation near $t=1/4$. Every single tag is fair, and the pair correlations on $AB,AC,BC$ are $t,-t,0$. ## A signed fourth-order coefficient and its independent readout

Let $p=2n_A n_B/[N(N-1)]$ for a ball containing $n_A$ and $n_B=n_C$ sector stations. This is the unconditional probability of one of the two affected sector pairs in either write order. At each full admitted pair the density is diagonal in orthogonal word records; seeing one word leaves the corresponding marginal fixed. With $H$ the entropy in nats, the addressed regional derivative is
$$
S'_t=p\sum_{ij}D_{ij}\log\frac{Q_{ij}-tD_{ij}}{Q_{ij}+tD_{ij}}.
$$
At $t=1/4$ this equals $p\log(947661/1183693)/144$. The fraction follows by grouping the 81 word cells by the two native integer weights $2592Q_{ij}$ and $2592D_{ij}$. [Word probabilities](../../word_entropy.py)

Score each material word by the cyclic order $0\to32\to33\to0$ of its two measured seam energies, assigning $+1$ to a forward step, $-1$ to its reversal and zero on a tie. Multiply the two scores. Keep its sign for an admitted $AB$ placement, reverse it on an admitted $AC$ placement and score zero elsewhere. The conditional energy-score response to a unit change in tag correlation is $1/36$ in both sectors. Thus the mean signed score is $p t/18$. The independently computed factor $\log(947661/1183693)/8$ converts its slope to the regional entropy slope at $t=1/4$ for every one of these regions. The factor depends on the reference. For this particular one-parameter family the angular mean directly identifies $t$ whenever $p>0$. [Energy calibration and independent held counts](../README.md)

In the midpoint family let $m$ be the subdivision frequency and $H$ the home eccentricity. The distance proof gives $6m-3\le H\le6m$. For any integer $R\to\infty$ with $R/m\to0$, the home face contains $\lfloor(R+2)^2/4\rfloor$ stations and each reflected face contains $\lfloor R^2/4\rfloor$. Substitution into $p$ with $N=20m^2$ gives $p/(R/H)^4\to81/200$ along the full sequence. The four deposited levels use $R^2=2m$. The radius that shrinks is $\ell=R/H$ in units of the board eccentricity. The elapsed $7R$ appointments grow.
