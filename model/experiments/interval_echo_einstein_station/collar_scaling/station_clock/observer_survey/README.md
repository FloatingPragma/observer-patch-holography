# Which collar charge do typical observers see?

A corner station touches a fivefold vertex of the icosahedral screen. An observer selected uniformly over all stations typically sees the sixfold geometry inside an original face. In the same seven-appointment clock, with the same source and one shared calibration of distance, the distinct-pair admission probability divided by the fourth power of radius tends to **6561/1600** for a typical observer. At the distinguished corner the limit is **729/256**. The typical-observer coefficient also gives the limit of the population mean. Independently tagging the twenty original faces leaves that mean coefficient intact. A protected route geometry is insensitive to the tags at every center, so averaging its signed residual over observers preserves the positive entropy response. ## Counting the regular centers

Let the subdivision frequency be m, with N=20m² triangle stations. Use R→∞, R/m→0 and even R. Write ℓ=R/H, where H is the corner station's eccentricity in completed station hops. The independently counted calibration gives H/m→6. Every observer uses this same standard. Multiplying all clock readings by seven appointments cancels in ℓ.

The dual graph of the infinite triangular tiling has 3s triangles at distance s≥1 from a specified triangle. To count them, label upward triangles by (i,j). After pairs of hops their distance in the upward triangular lattice is max(|i|,|j|,|i+j|). The hexagon of radius k contains 1+3k(k+1) upward triangles. From it one further hop reaches downward triangles in the union of that hexagon with its translates by (−1,0) and (0,−1). Summing its horizontal rows gives 3(k+1)² positions: the rows at j=−k−1,…,−1 have lengths k+1,…,2k+1 and those at j=0,…,k have lengths 2k+2,…,k+2. Taking successive differences gives the stated sphere count. Hence

$$
 B(R)=1+3R(R+1)/2,\qquad n=B(R)-B(R/2)=9R^2/8+3R/4.
$$

In each original face take the central triangle remaining after removing R+1 rows parallel to each side. Its side length is m−3(R+1) when positive. Every start there has its entire R-ball in that face: one dual hop changes each lattice height by at most one. Thus the fraction of excluded centers is at most 1−(1−3(R+1)/m)² for sufficiently large m. This tends to zero. This bound counts seam-adjacent centers conservatively.

There is a uniform upper bound B(R)≤C(R+1)² even for starts near seams and corners. Realize the subdivided incidence by flat equilateral triangles of side 1 in the original twenty planar faces. Along an R-hop path the displacement between triangle centers in ambient Euclidean space is at most R. Each reached triangle lies in the ambient ball of radius R+1 about the start. Each face plane cuts a disk of area at most π(R+1)², and each unit triangle has area √3/4. This bounds the count uniformly, including at folds. This auxiliary embedding counts the finite paths.

The closed ball itself has single and distinct-pair limits 27/10 and 729/100 in the same normalization. For a regular start in the annular survey, n/(Nℓ²) tends to (9/8)·36/20=81/40. For two uniform distinct placements the corresponding fraction is n(n−1)/(N(N−1)ℓ⁴), with limit 6561/1600. Both normalized quantities are uniformly bounded at exceptional centers by the preceding disk estimate. Their expectations therefore tend to the same limits, and the mean squares of their deviations tend to zero. If the two words share a tag only within each original face, the regular-center ball still lies in one face. The distinct-pair coefficient and mean have the same limits. At the corner this variant instead reduces the coefficient fivefold. [Corner with independent tags](../ancestor_limit.py)

On the reversed-tag source the inclusive entropy derivative of each addressed regional word memory is the eligible pair probability times the measured conditional derivative κ. The normalized observer-mean derivative tends to (6561/1600)κ. This is the mean of the entropies with the observer's address known. Discarding that address defines another observable. The survey is of the routed screen; identifying those regions with three-dimensional proper balls would require independent geometric records. [Reproducible counts](measure.py)

## What can incidence prove about the fastest clock?

For the variable-duration protected clock, a local change of gateway labels can change even the existence of a full-sequence area coefficient. The following comparison keeps the same cell template, the protected port, every dual adjacency and the three distinct gateways per cell. It can retain any finite prefix of deposited boards. It tests what these hypotheses imply about their continuation. The deposited board labels stay fixed in the actual experiment.

The punctured native template admits three gateway ports at mutual distance one and another triple at mutual distance two. Assign one such triple in each cell to its incident dual edges in sorted neighbor order. If an entire route uses mutual gateway distance c then a simple path crossing h seams costs (1+c)h−c plus its two local station legs, each between zero and three. A shortest route can be chosen without a repeated cell: an excursion returning to the same cell spends at least two external steps and can be replaced by the in-cell connection of length at most two. The resulting uniform metric differs from (1+c) times dual distance by a bounded amount.

Use the slow triple (c=2) everywhere on one subsequence. On the other put the fast triple (c=1) inside dual distance q=⌊m^(3/4)⌋ of the corner, and the slow triple elsewhere. Set R of order √m on both. The home eccentricity H satisfies H/m→18 on both subsequences. A slow dual geodesic bounds every mixed range from above by 3h+4. For a target outside the patch consider the last fast cell of a shortest route: its dual distance from home is at most q, and each intermediate slow cell after it costs two in addition to its external crossings. Thus the distance is at least 3(h−q)−3. Apply these bounds to a dual farthest station and use q/m→0.

A path taking at most R appointments stays inside the fast patch on the mixed subsequence. More generally the inner distance is (1+c)h+O(1) up to the survey boundary: the dual geodesic stays in the patch and the global minimum transit cost is one. The previously proved corner count B(t)=(5/4)t²+O(t) therefore squeezes the station count inside either routed radius s=R or R/2 between B((s−O(1))/(1+c)) and B((s+O(1))/(1+c)). The bounded displacement changes the count by O(R). For ℓ=R/H the single-placement closed-ball limits are 9/4 (slow) and 81/16 (fast patch). For the annulus the limits are 27/16 and 243/64. The distinct-pair limits are the squares of the corresponding single limits. Alternation prevents full-sequence convergence for this admissible continuation. The original fastest clock on a particular infinite labelling could still converge. Proving it requires information about those labels or direct control of their induced distances.
