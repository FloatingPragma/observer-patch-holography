# Does a measured native collar charge scale as the fourth power of radius?

Two correlated written words give a nonzero fourth-order charge on a family of shrinking native clock regions. The spatial extent of their preparation controls where that charge can be detected. With independent preparation bits in the 20 ancestor cells, distant regions have zero mixed charge, while two nearby, disjoint regions sharing an ancestor retain a measurable mixed charge. The two word-energy sums keep their original joint distribution. [Primary protocol](PROTOCOL.md) · [Finite correlation range protocol](ANCESTOR_PROTOCOL.md)

## Can these same records set the acceleration scale without rotation data?

The per-cut theory uses a number n of independent gravitating cuts per proper length, a variance of anomalous mass per cut V(Mᵦ)=c Mᵦ, and the Newton coupling G. In SI units n has units of m⁻¹ and c has units of kg, so G n² c has units of acceleration. The native collar read supplies dimensionless log probabilities, whose variances are quoted in nats². The native energy meter supplies seam exchange units. An independent ordinary baryonic mass calibration and a conversion from the modular defect to gravitating mass are both necessary to measure c.

A retrospective sharp-energy comparison tests whether these particular native fields can determine a per-cut variance slope. Use the reversed-tag microscopic modular reference calibrated in this experiment and all eligible outcomes in independent archived word cohorts. Restrict the two regional word energy sums to (32,32) or (65,65) in exchange units. Within either bin, the two allowed orientations for each word have identical relabelled probabilities and connected modular scores at the population reference. The exact variances of the connected defect are **0.00690305, 0.00800701 and 0.00900036 nats²** for reversed, fresh and shared histories, respectively. Thus changing the tags changes the variance at exactly fixed measured energy. Changing the matched total stored energy from 64 to 130 leaves the three population variances unchanged. Counts give 0.00690293, 0.00800780 and 0.00900175 nats² at (32,32), with single-cohort sampling standard errors of 0.00000060–0.00000069 conditional on the frozen calibration. These cuts give no positive linear slope of this variance against this energy that transfers between the two sharp bins and tag histories. Local word energy and ordinary baryonic rest mass are distinct readouts.

The radial cut count also admits a direct native proxy check. Count distinct routed radial bins containing an actual written word. In an annulus of nₛ out of N stations the expected number of admitted marks is 2nₛ/N. The count of their distinct radii can fall below it by at most nₛ(nₛ−1)/(N(N−1)), the probability that both distinct marks enter. On the finest board the resulting density is between **0.15194 and 0.15201** occupied radii per routed home-range unit in (r/2,r]; in the adjacent doubled annulus it is between **0.30140 and 0.30194**. These ranges describe the actual two-word population under the specified sampling law. They do not identify the nested gravitating cuts or their density per proper metre. No physical acceleration scale is recoverable from these inputs alone. ### Which metric do these clocks determine at large scales?

Consider any continuation with the same protected cell template, three distinct accessible gateways, and the face adjacency of the uniform midpoint subdivision of an icosahedron. Set \(n=2^j\), so \(N=20n^2\) stations. Write \(M_x(r)\) for a closed ball's population, \(H_x\) for the greatest station distance from \(x\), and \(D=3\) for the template diameter after removing port 4. All following estimates are independent of the gateway labels and the ball centre. The auxiliary triangles serve to count graph paths.

* Each external step crosses one microtriangle edge. Realize the twenty faces as a regular polyhedron with microtriangle side one. Centroids of adjacent triangles are at distance at most one. On each face all reached triangles fit inside a disk of area at most \(\pi(r+1)^2\). Each has area \(\sqrt3/4\). Hence \(M_x(r)\le200(r+1)^2\). [Upper counting bound](../proper_ball_ruler/README.md)
* For \(D\le r\le n\), choose the largest dyadic \(s\le 1+(r-D)/(3(D+1))\). A side-\(s\) ancestor containing the starting microtriangle contains \(s^2\) stations. A generic segment between interiors stays in that ancestor and crosses at most \(s-1\) grid lines in each of three directions. A dual path of \(k\) crossings lifts to a protected route of length at most \(D+(D+1)k\), including the endpoint-to-gateway steps. Thus all those stations lie inside the ball and \(M_x(r)\ge r^2/[36(D+1)^2]\).
* The upper bound at \(H_x\) gives \(N\le800H_x^2\). Any two macrofaces join through at most twenty faces. Crossing each in at most \(3(n-1)\) dual steps and joining successive faces by one more step gives \(H_x\le60(D+1)n\). Thus \(H_x\) is comparable to \(n\). On every refinement with \(r\to\infty\) and \(\ell=r/H_x\to0\), these estimates eventually apply and \(M_x(r)/N\ge\alpha\ell^2\), with \(\alpha=1/460800\). For example \(r=\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor\) has these two limits. The upper estimate also gives \(M_x(r)/N=O(\ell^2)\).

There is a metric test independent of any assignment of volume weights. For points separated by distance at least \(s\) in a ball of radius \(r\), the balls of radius \(\lfloor s/3\rfloor\) about them are disjoint and lie in the ball of radius \(r+\lfloor s/3\rfloor\). The two population bounds give at most \(C_1(r/s)^2\) such points for \(D\ll s\le r\le n\), with a fixed constant \(C_1\). In a smooth three-dimensional proper ball a small coordinate cube contains at least \(c_1(r/s)^3\) points at separation \(s\). A reconstruction dense up to \(o(r)\) with upper and lower distance ratios bounded independently of level would transfer that packing to the station ball, up to fixed constants. Take \(s/r\) fixed and sufficiently small before refining. The bounds contradict each other. Single-station clock positions therefore cannot fill an open three-dimensional proper ball with that distance calibration. Positions encoded in correlations or in cumulative port records use different distances.

### Does the signed source discrepancy survive refinement?

Let \(P_-,P_0,P_+\) be the independently specified reversed, fresh and shared tag laws on the two words. All entries are positive, \(P_0\) is the product of their common marginals, and \(P_+-P_0=P_0-P_-\). With the reversed preparation as the population reference, the shared-minus-fresh expectation of the connected log score conditional on joint admission is
\[
 c=\sum(P_0-P_-)\log(P_0/P_-)
   =D_{\rm KL}(P_0\Vert P_-)+D_{\rm KL}(P_-\Vert P_0)>0.
\]
Term by term, \((a-b)\log(a/b)\ge(a-b)^2/\max(a,b)\) by the mean value theorem. Summing the exact rational word probabilities gives \(c\ge101/33696\), with \(c=0.0033051275331\) nats. Zero or one admitted word contributes zero contrast. This calculation uses the specified reference. The product reference yields zero contrast. The complete joint histogram of the two ordinary word-energy sums agrees between preparations. This checks the measured energies; identifying the full matter stress is a separate physical step.

For two distinct uniformly placed words the full-ball charge is exactly \(cM(M-1)/[N(N-1)]\). At \(M\ge2\) it is at least \(c(M/N)^2/2\). For independent tags on the twenty ancestor groups with the ancestor-conditional reversed reference, the numerator is instead \(\sum_g M_g(M_g-1)\). Cauchy--Schwarz bounds this below by \(M^2/20-M\ge M^2/40\) once \(M\ge40\). Both preparations consequently have charge of order \(\ell^4\) in these complete balls, with a uniform strictly positive lower coefficient. The clock records at each placement, including the random boundary exit times, have exactly the same distribution in the two arms. Every area or volume computed solely from those records has zero contrast. At any fixed positive coupling and with the measured ordinary contribution held fixed, the signed residual \((\Delta A+\kappa\Delta K/(2\pi))/(\kappa\ell^4)\) is at least \(c\alpha^2/(4\pi)\) for global tags, or \(c\alpha^2/(80\pi)\) for ancestor tags, at all sufficiently fine levels. Linear interpolation of the shared and fresh preparations gives the same discrepancy per unit first variation. The finite annulus readings above and this all-level closed-ball statement have different regions and evidence. The latter follows from incidence and the exact population law. Its radius is in common routed units. A uniformly bounded conversion to a larger proper radius preserves a positive lower bound after rescaling by that conversion's fourth power.
