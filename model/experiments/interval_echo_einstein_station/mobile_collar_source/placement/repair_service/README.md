## The closed sharp write protects a local matter record

Orient an edge from its lower-address owner to its higher-address owner and denote its binary endpoint loads by $a,b$. On initially blank memory the native write records $d=a-b$ and coin $c$. For unequal loads it publishes $c,1-c$ at the endpoints. Define $f(\mathrm{blank})=f(c,0)=0$ and $f(c,d)=(1+d)/2-c$ for $d=\pm1$. The binary loads and six written coin/difference labels together with blank give an invariant 28-state subspace of the full 99-state seam. Across every nonzero echo matrix element in that subspace, $a+f$ and $b-f$ remain fixed. The check includes encounters with already written memories and transitions back to blank. It follows that $Q_i=n_i+\sum_{e\ni i}\epsilon_{ie} f_e$ is conserved at every owner, with incidence $+1$ at an edge's first end and $-1$ at its second. Measuring in this sharp basis preserves the same relation. [Exact dyadic transition tables and native columns](local.py) · [Full-seam and routed-rail checks](test_service.py)

For the initially full half-patch $S$ and all-blank collars, its lost load is exactly the oriented sum of $f$ on its original boundary. It can lose at most $B=|\partial S|$ units regardless of the appointment count. With $N$ owners the difference between mean inner and outer occupation therefore stays at least $1-4B/N$. The 320-owner patch has 32 cut edges and a permanent lower bound **0.60**. This theorem concerns the echo and its sharp record. ## Exchange removes the stationary dependence on the initial patch

Let $W$ and $V$ be the probability kernels for the sharp echo and the separately read half exchange on the same physical pair. Both are symmetric and stochastic. Integer arithmetic gives $W(W-I)(2W+I)=0$ and $V(V-I)(2V-I)=0$ on all 28 sharp states. The half contact is computed at native contrast eight, where the contrast phase completes an integer number of rotations. Removing reversal leaves an idle half contact at this appointment. An admitted fraction $p$ of half contacts gives the kernel $(1-p)I+pV$. For every $p>0$ it fixes precisely the vectors fixed by $V$. Each of these real symmetric contractions preserves a vector's Euclidean norm only in its fixed space. Hence every stationary vector of an entire matching cycle is fixed by each individual serviced kernel. The same argument for any eigenvalue of modulus one forces that eigenvalue to be one. On this finite stochastic system each closed class therefore converges to its uniform distribution. Equivalently, its stationary classes are generated by the union of the local transition supports, even when different physical seams occur in a fixed cyclic order. On blank 01 or 10 the half contact can perform either a stay or a swap, leaving the collar blank. Such swaps on a connected board connect every all-blank assembly with the same number of loads. At half filling the exact symmetry sending $n$ to $1-n$ and $(c,d)$ to $(1-c,-d)$ maps the initial all-blank word to another in the same class. This symmetry makes the stationary mean of each $n$ one half and of each $d$ and $f$ zero. Since a stationary class has positive weight on blank configurations with a given bridge straddling unequal loads, that bridge has a positive steady rate of new unit-contrast writes. In a retained bank the positive birth and return rates balance. An unsigned stationary record can depend on the physical location of the edge. The placement of the *initial* all-blank matter at fixed inventory cannot affect it. This statement covers positive exchange admission on every serviced edge and repeated complete sweeps of this finite, measured instrument.

A separately maintained control restores the originally prescribed sharp matter rail at each owner after every matching. The same native write still makes a fair swap on each unlike input. If a unit crossed its original boundary, the controller uses one inside supply rail and one outside sink rail, exchanging each via the calibrated native π swap on physical local ports 8 and 9. It retains all spent rails and replaced memories. A cycle needs a binomial number of corrections with $B$ trials and probability one half. Thus maintaining the 20-, 80- and 320-owner boundaries requires **3, 8 and 16 units on average** delivered from the inside reservoir and the same number removed to outside storage every cycle. On 320 owners this produces 32 fresh contrast births while keeping 32 such heads at the cut. It also exports the previous 32 unit heads per cycle after the first.
