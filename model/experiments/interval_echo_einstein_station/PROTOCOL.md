# A local entropy change at a fixed cut

Test the regional first-law and area-response ingredients of G4. The Einstein theorem requires a physical area measured independently of the entropy, at fixed proper volume. The finite experiment measures the state on both sides of all incident boundary seams and the entropy accessible inside one echosahedron. The service address, durations and launch probabilities are supplied. On each 20-, 80- and 320-cell federation, use cell zero and its first external contact in carrier order. Call its endpoints A inside and C outside. Choose the first strictly interior neighbour B of A and the first strictly interior spare D adjacent to B. Initially exactly one double occupies A, B or C with respective probabilities 4p/5, 4(1−p)/5 and 1/5. All other ports and all memories start sharp and blank. Use the registered 99-state seam generator at κ = 8. Apply A–B for π/2 and A–C for π/3. All remaining seams are protected through this window.

Measure all three cut seams including both endpoint cells’ **boundary ports**, together with their boundary seam memories. The rest of those cells belongs to the respective interiors. Calculate the entire reduced state on these boundary registers while varying p. Compare it with the same cut when the first splitter is bypassed. For the regional measurement use only cell zero. Measure the double on A and B separately with a neighbouring empty rail. On fresh preparations also interrogate A after a half contact on A–B, and after a π/65 contact on B–D followed by that half contact. The extra local contact rotates the phase of the B component relative to A. With the four settings recover both diagonal populations and both real components of the coherence. Retain every quiet outcome. Verify the inversion against tracing out the other cells. All data before these alternative terminal measurements refer to the same state.

Use p = 3/4 for the modular reference and p = 1/2 as a separate symmetry control. Compute the Shannon entropy of the zero/one-double regional centre, the conditional entropy of the occupied block, and the regional von Neumann entropy, in nats. Use the reference operator −log ρ on its fixed support. Compare the limiting slope from independent entropy differences at steps 1/128, 1/256, 1/512 and 1/1024 to the expectation derivative of this fixed operator. Audit all three board embeddings. Their repeated local incidence is one design.

For finite readout use p = 3/4 ± 1/16 and 1/2 ± 1/16 on the 20-cell board. At each setting draw 32 independent blocks of 16,384 binary readings with NumPy PCG64 seed 2026112110. Estimate each entropy slope from the pooled records and its uncertainty by paired delete-one-block jackknife. Report whether the interval of four standard errors contains zero. Keep the exact finite-step forecasts and every detector block. Compare against occupancy-only readout. Report a tomography or positivity failure rather than repairing an estimate by projection.
