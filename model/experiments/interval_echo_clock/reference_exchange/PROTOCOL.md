# Exchange of differently prepared hand references

Two patches prepare independent phase ensembles on their own two clock rails. The supplied input ensembles are the sharp and heralded preparations of the modular-reference experiment. An overlap communication service brings matching rails to native contacts along a specified path. A positive exchange decision swaps both rails. The repair coin is read on a native fair contact. Two consecutive positives request an exchange on a quarter-probability service. One positive requests the exchange on a fair service. Keep every decision and include preparation failures. For the reference of an owner who reads only the prepared flag, number flag and terminal analyzer, pool the exchange words. Also retain the two-owner joint counts for each word.

Use adjacent cells on the 20-cell board and two paths on a native cell cycle. On the direct path measure the no-service and one-quarter-service reference contrasts and the settled reference after a fair service. Use these readings to predict two receivers after four quarter services and after one final fair service on the other path. The two local third-rail analyzers give p = 3/8 + x/4 on an X-aligned reference. Require each held hit fraction to fall within 0.012 of its forecast. Resolve the joint distribution against two independently randomized sources of which reference each owner received. Replace the heralded input by a sharp input to test dependence on the conserved mean. Replace the random full exchanges by two coherent half-contacts to test whether the two different interactions share the same conditional reference. Read the local one-double number flag before the phase detector and retain both load-bunching outcomes.

On separate sharp probes compare a quarter-turned terminal read after eight accepted ticks with the modular phase inferred from the direct fair-service reference and the held fair-service reference. Use the in-phase terminal read there for sensitivity to phase near pi/2. Enrol 2^19 launches per bank. The 0.012 frequency tolerance is the resolution of this finite comparison. These path choices and readout choices are supplied. This is an exploratory simulator study.
