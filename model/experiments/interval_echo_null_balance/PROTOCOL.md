# Predicting a cell’s matter-energy uptake from two gate responses

Test the finite matter-energy part of G3 on the same local two-load source and current pulse as the registered Interval Echo port instrument. Use cell zero of the 20-cell spherical federation and open its first two external links in native port order. The two neighbours and the central cell run their 30 internal seams. The remaining contacts and reference services are gated closed, as in the earlier selective bridge experiments. Define the central patch energy as the sum of the 30 internal blank-memory seam generators. Keep the two link energies separately from the three cell energies. Use native exposure units.

Prepare the registered inversion-odd star at local port one. Apply the registered current of strength ±0.41 at ports 0 and 9 on separate preparations. Observe all four releases at native times 1, 4 and 16. No coefficients are fitted. For each opening independently interrupt free evolution on fresh copies and apply only that gate at the 19 equally spaced positive exposure angles around its native period, including zero. Read the central seam energy in each copy through the local occupancy and mixed-seam interference outcomes. Extract its instantaneous energy slope by exact trigonometric interpolation at zero. The native binary seam has eigenvalues 0, 8 and 9, so no transition frequency exceeds nine.

Sum both separately determined gate responses. Compare with the slope inferred from independent central energy readings after *uninterrupted* full evolution at each appointment plus and minus 0.0001 and 0.00005. Retain the residual and the effect of halving the step. Check against the commutator for all prepared states, the native 99-state contact columns, and conservation of the complete cells-plus-links energy. In a control, discard the second gate response. In a separate closed apparatus both gates remain disabled throughout. Report absolute discrepancies, the RMS signal over all 12 appointments and the exposure range. All reported local probabilities are exact finite-ensemble predictions. Optical, modular and gravitational normalizations are external to this test.

## Regional energy on the free federation

Open all 630 contacts and the native degree references of the level-zero sphere. Split every external contact energy equally between its two owners. Use three regions: cell zero, its neighbour at the first external seam, and their union. Take the registered positive port-zero and negative port-nine launches at exposures 3/8 and 9/8. Predict each uninterrupted regional energy derivative from separate 19-angle interventions on the external seams crossing its boundary. For each seam take half the difference of the responses of its two owners' internal energy and references. Compare with a symmetric difference of complete regional energy reads at offsets ±10⁻⁴ and ±5×10⁻⁵. The union has its own energy meter with its common seam counted in full. Report every address and both owners, omit a boundary current as a sensitivity control, and repeat the free derivative using only internal energy and references to check the effect of shared storage. Verify the Ward operator identity on every binary pair column of the sphere. Numerical appointment comparisons use exact finite probabilities from fresh preparations; the service exposure and accounting convention are supplied.
