# Accumulated energy from finite observer preparations

Use the same 20-cell sphere, central patch, odd-star source, two opened gateways and positive port-nine current. Select the fresh interval [1.125, 1.375] in native exposure units. On independent preparations measure the central energy at the endpoints. Independently measure the two gateway responses at each of six Gauss–Legendre appointments inside the interval. Apply the same 19-angle response reconstruction per gateway. Retain the local energy record at every angle and at both endpoints. Compare with analytic finite model values. Double the quadrature order in a noiseless control to measure quadrature sensitivity.

For each 31-setting energy reading, draw 524,288 independent preparations per setting, including all quiet outcomes. The diagonal word identifies the occupied subset of the 12 central ports, which can hold zero, one or two units. An antisymmetric output identifies a click on one chosen seam after a phase and half exchange. Each analyzer uses a fresh ensemble. Sample with PCG64 seed 114702 on a fixed stream in this order: two direct endpoint energies, then chronological six-point nodes, native gate order and angle order. Treat every panel as independent. Estimate the standard error from the observed sample variance at each setting and propagate the registered linear coefficients in quadrature. Report the total difference, both measured flows, the two-gateway residual and the residual dropping the second gateway, each with uncertainty. Report whether a zero effect is excluded by four standard errors. No observed sign, degree of agreement, or control discrimination is required for reporting.
