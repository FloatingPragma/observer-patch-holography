# Thermal contact between finite material bodies

Assign one guarded two-port dimer per owner on the 20-cell federation. Bodies A, B and C contain one, two and three distinct owners respectively. Each dimer contains one double and a vacancy. All other ports used for temporary routing are restored after each contact appointment. Keep the bound native gap and the local energy decoder from the parent experiment. In a contact between any two bodies offer identity, a π free flight of any participating dimer, or transposition of both data rails between any one dimer of each body. Draw enough independent fair native bits to index this list and assign unused tags to idle. Record every tag and also report results summed over tags. Use a separate decoder check, independent launches of each body and fresh paired runs. The thermostat is absent during the contact.

First derive the fixed-point criterion of this single channel for every faithful independent pair of initially arbitrary finite density matrices on the specified dimer registers, including correlations within either body. Identify the parameter measured by a local energy ratio and check transitivity. For ordinary positive temperatures the upper population is less than one half; check what happens at equal populations and inversion. Distinguish exchange of energy measured on single trajectories from mean heat flow and stationarity of the entire state.

Use the previous one-appointment filter on A and C at matched settings. On B use the previously measured fixed-address repair with four attempts per dimer, starting at the lower mode. Record preparation refusals. Enroll exactly 262144 original launches for each body calibration and 524288 for each contact bank. Carry out A:B, B:C and independent held A:C experiments. Prepare C hot (zero filter appointments) and cold (three) on additional A:C banks. Contrast two equal-mean B preparations by copying the first B sign to the second on a fair flag after successful independent repair. This changes its correlations while preserving each marginal. In B:A compare the original and final probability of both B records being upper; in the exchange-only sub-bank also compare the swapped sign with B's other sign. Park the material exchange or replace its kinetic term by zero at the same π appointment. As a phase-sensitive foil put all incident dimers in sharp physical rail A (a coherent superposition of the two energies). Check its local return following contact with and without the independent free-flight phase words. Preserve all original denominators. Compare probabilities or changes with a single simultaneous 99% allowance, including the uncertainty of the held temperature calibrations. No fitted temperature should be required to classify a changing control.
