## Selecting a positive energy with a bounded number of records

Consider a source with a variable mixture of two preparations. Each completed signed word draws its own mixture flag. A controller starts from a fixed state and reads at most $n$ independent words in a preparation. Its other operations have fixed probabilities. Write $Z,E,O$ for the joint probabilities of acceptance in the zero, even unit and odd unit modes. The exchange-normalized energy is a source-independent positive number $k$ if $Z/E=(E/O)^k$ on an open mixture interval with varying parity odds. The full quadratic spectrum is required only at the stationary operating preparation.

The number of words bounds this energy. Each joint probability is a polynomial in the mixture fraction of degree at most $n$, by expanding over the source flags and padding an early stop with unused flags. Put $R=E/O$ and $S=Z/E$. The rational identity $S'/S=kR'/R$ implies $k=p/q$ in lowest terms because the orders of $S$ and $R$ at a zero or pole of $R$ are integers in that ratio. Choose integers $a,b$ such that $aq+bp=1$. From $S^q=R^p$, the reduced rational function $u/v=R^aS^b$ has $R=(u/v)^q$ and $S=(u/v)^p$. Coprimality then gives

$$
(Z,E,O)=h\,(u^{p+q},\ u^q v^p,\ v^{p+q})
$$

for a polynomial $h$. At least one of $u,v$ has positive degree, so $p+q\le n$. This argument allows arbitrary acceptance rules on the words, correlated outputs and recorded failures. Conditioning all three outputs by a common positive acceptance probability preserves the odds.
