# Canonical repair by measured overlap ballots

Each owner stores a sharp Boolean output and one eligibility bit in separate balanced rail pairs. On the selected loop-free graph a canonical change at one site must cure every disagreement touching that site. The strict Interval Echo pointer compares two extremal rails, records whether they differ, then restores the queried rails by its inverse. Both owners of every edge make this measurement independently. Each owner lights its ballot exactly when it has at least one incident edge and every incident comparison reports a mismatch.

A covering scan reads the stored ballots and grants the first positive report. The grantee checks its guard and swaps the data pair. Its incident edges now agree. Withdrawing any lit ballots at that owner and its immediate neighbors restores the exact ballot map everywhere. Empty scans still invoke a site and incur a charge. The [finite proof](../../../../../Lean/ObserverPatchHolography/Execution/MeasuredRepairBallots.lean) identifies these operations with canonical single-site repair on every finite loop-free Boolean equality graph. At every reducible word the next attempt makes a change, including runs started at a later scheduler index. Hence waste is zero, pathwise weak fairness holds, and a budget equal to the initial number of broken edges suffices to reach a normal record. On the cubic board each genuine change removes three mismatches. The charge counts an invocation. The receipt separately counts all routed pointer probes and exchanges for service and audit.

The native trials use both consultation orders on every small word and twelve sphere preparations. In all 458 invocations, the 346 equality stutters occurred after the last possible change. Four matched controls per trial bring the total to 600 runs. Blind cyclic offers wasted 226 attempts at reducible words. Withholding neighbor withdrawals produced 48 stale grants, including 17 while another site could repair. Parking the local comparison silenced eligibility. In the parked arm an independent standard supplies the matched trial budget. The zero-budget control leaves every initially enabled source unrepaired. The final reader at the far owner rereads all outputs and latches independently. Native service consumed 9,253 pointer probes and 234,392 elementary exchanges. The separate audit consumed 5,056 probes and 109,952 exchanges. All sharp outcomes come from a deterministic reduction calibrated against the full local echo and its inverse.

**Counting terminal records.** Let the initially enabled vertices induce a graph. A productive grant removes that vertex and all its neighbors from eligibility. Complete orders therefore flip exactly the vertices of a maximal independent set. Conversely, putting the members of any maximal independent set first realizes its terminal word. Two orders can give different words exactly when initially enabled neighbors share an edge: grow a maximal independent set from either endpoint. On path input `0010` opposite orders give `0000` and `0011`. On the full sphere only two quiescent words agree on all edges. Twenty quiescent words still have 18 broken edges. The full-board counts exhaust Boolean words using the calibrated comparison rule and are independently checked over complement pairs. They are separate from the 24 routed full-board trials.

**Which inputs can reach complete agreement?** For a fixed target bit on a graph without isolated vertices, every initially opposite site must flip. Those sites must form an independent set: the two ends of an initially equal edge can never be the first to change. Conversely, if the opposite sites are independent, each one is enabled and they can all change in any order when given priority. The sphere has 5,828 independent subsets. Its pentagons prevent both color classes from being independent, so exactly 11,656 inputs admit a unanimous endpoint under some service order. A particular unanimous target is forced precisely when its opposite sites are independent and no site already carrying the target is initially enabled. If an enabled target site moves first, its change is permanent. Otherwise the required changes commute. Exactly 7,736 sphere words guarantee unanimity, 3,920 admit it only for some orders, and the remaining 1,036,920 can never reach it. The corresponding reachability counts are 14 of 16 on the path and 22 of 32 on the pentagon. This post-protocol consequence was checked by enumerating all possible small-graph terminal words and by independent-set recursion on the sphere.
