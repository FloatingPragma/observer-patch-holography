# The same correlated material bath selects a state and its local transitions

Observers can expose four binary ports on a connected eight-port path of Interval Echo material carrying four unit loads. The complete visible word is denoted by x. A second deployment of the same path supplies its reference. On this apparatus the native transfer and the number of inaccessible reservoir arrangements determine a strictly positive, correlated law R on all sixteen words. Conditioning it on a source constraint predicts the entire state. Conditioning the **same** law on three fixed ports predicts a new bit on the fourth port. All 32 tests of the latter prediction passed, including each initial value. [Source protocol](PROTOCOL.md) · [Transition protocol](TRANSITION.md)

A fixed-duration version now sends **every** admissible input to the unique Gibbs projection at its initial parity frequency. It works for every frequency from zero to one and carries out both local conditional repairs at fixed appointments. Observers can change the input moment while using the same reference and repair settings. All five complete 70-by-70 kernels were verified, and 42 forward banks tested both skewed starts across the three boards. [Fixed-time proof and results](FIXED_TIME.md) · [Native implementation](gibbs.py)

On this same collar the forward and reverse address schedules generate measurable fluctuations. Across three independently calibrated ensembles and two held trajectory cohorts, all 64 feasible path odds agree with the finite theorem. In the forward cohort 16,271 of 65,536 histories have negative entropy production. [Experiment, proof and controls](FLUCTUATIONS.md)

## Material source and equilibrium reader

During a sweep, contact each successive pair of neighbours for half a native exchange period, with the other contacts and degree services shuttered. Read the two occupations after each contact. Loads agree or there are two possible orderings. On unequal binary words the registered blank seam Hamiltonian has a common diagonal and off-diagonal entries −1/2. Its evolution at exposure π/2 and a sharp read therefore leave either ordering with probability 1/2, for both input orders. The program extracts these probabilities from the full 99-state seam and checks absence of memory leakage. Equal loads are unaffected. A protected π exchange routes a selected neighbouring port when needed. The graphs and deployed path coordinates come from the registered 20-, 80- and 320-cell boards. The experiment supplies contact shutters, initial sharps, clocks, independent native-coin preparations, a classical controller and sharp terminal reads. Exact probabilities refer to the declared Born instrument. [Material calculation](measure.py)

For the reference, all seven path contacts take part. Each attempted exchange has a symmetric doubly stochastic transition matrix. Their ordered product preserves the uniform law on the seventy words with four units. Any adjacent exchange can occur and all other exchanges can hold in a sweep. The chain is irreducible and has a positive self-loop. This establishes a unique attracting equilibrium without inserting any target probability into its evolution. The four hidden ports have exactly as many completions as the inventory allows. Hence

\[
 R(x)=\binom{4}{4-|x|}/70.
\]

For the source, park the contact between sites 1 and 2, numbering from 0. The two left ports conserve their total K. Three independent fair native bits determine the preparation. Two furnish left-port digits, and the third selects whether the second digit copies the first. Thus K has proportions (3/8, 1/4, 3/8). Hidden loads maintain total inventory four for every preparation. The same native sweeps on the components of sizes two and six converge to the uniform law on each of their joint inventory fibres. Consequently the exposed state P agrees with R conditional on K. In fact, for the single measured statistic F=1 when K=1 and zero otherwise,

\[
 P(x)=\tfrac74 R(x)\,4^{-F(x)},\qquad E_PF=\tfrac14.
\]

A controller may vary the frequency of opposite left bits through an independently calibrated native transfer on a sharp unequal pair. If this frequency is s and the first bit is fair, p(K)=( (1−s)/2, s, (1−s)/2 ). For 0<s<1 the same balance gives the one-parameter family proportional to R(x) exp(λF(x)), with exp(λ)=3s/[4(1−s)]. The counts here use the frozen s=1/4 preparation. The protected components preserve the full K census during evolution. Starts with a different ratio of the two even sectors need not approach P even if they agree on the odd-event frequency.

## Every competitor on the observed cover

Consider all probability laws Q on the sixteen words satisfying just E_Q F=1/4. They may have arbitrary correlations and support. Since log(P/R) is an affine function of F, its Q and P expectations agree. Expanding the divergence gives

\[
 D(Q\Vert R)= D(P\Vert R)+D(Q\Vert P).
\]

The last term is nonnegative with equality exactly at P. This independently supplies the moment, faithful reference and exponential factor entering the finite information-projection theorem. [Finite Gibbs projection](../../../../Lean/InformationProjection/PathGibbs.lean)

One observer keeps the first three sites; another keeps the last three. Both receive the same terminal K flag. Their overlaps agree, and their pooled joint word separates the entire visible simplex. Collect exactly one reading per window and one pooled reading per trial. This fixes equal positive weights. Because every read retains F, its marginal log-density ratio is the same affine function of F. The identity above holds **term by term** under marginalization. Any positive weighted sum is minimized, and including the complete pooled word gives a unique visible joint law. The underlying bath microstate need not be inferred.

For a coarse reading first keep only the counts on the two exposed pairs. Then resolve the two exposed bath ports. Finally resolve the left ports. Forgetting a finer label carries P and R to the corresponding coarse optima. Every admissible coarse law lifts to an admissible fine one by using the conditional law of R in each coarse fibre, since F is already fixed there. The same argument works for any finite chain of this record refinement retaining F. These are refinements of the observable menu on each board. Matching the same protected local experiment on three spherical resolutions supplies no unrestricted geometric or dynamical refinement theorem.

## A finite stopping rule reads the exact law

Each routine half-contact plus read can equivalently use a fresh fair bit, assigning an unlike pair to 01 or 10 and retaining like pairs. This is a physical implementation of the same sharp kernel using a shared classical coin, a sharp read and a conditional native SWAP. Fan the coin out to copies prepared in each admissible starting word. Beginning at the final appointment, prepend an earlier sweep of fresh coins and compose its maps with the already stored later ones. Stop when the resulting map sends **all** allowed initial words to the same terminal word. The program tracks only the exact images. It generates neither the reference table nor a desired terminal probability inside this reader.

An all-left set of sweep coins, repeated eight times, packs every connected component to its left edge. The same argument applies to a component with its other rails fixed. Such a finite block has positive probability, so checking disjoint earlier blocks gives almost-sure termination and a finite mean tape length. For correctness, imagine an arbitrary large fixed past horizon with an independent stationary start and apply the same coins to it. This gives a stationary final state. If the prepended maps have coalesced by that horizon their output equals this stationary sample, irrespective of which such start was used. The probability of failing this comparison tends to zero. Thus each returned sharp record has exactly the stationary law. Histograms of all stopping depths are retained in the receipts. The reference law and the full state probability enter the independent comparison only.

Ordinary sweeps from the packed source have a different finite-time meaning. All probabilities after a fixed finite number of fair updates are dyadic, whereas this reference contains 1/70 and the conditioned state contains 3/20. No fixed finite number of sweeps from the enrolled dyadic preparations gives either complete table exactly. At 32 source sweeps the total variation distance to P is 0.00008825 and at 64 it is about 0.00000000886. These are calculations from the native forward kernel; ordinary forward counts at each held wait were also retained. [Count banks](results.json)

## Independent predictions, conditional repairs and their boundary

Let the two exposed bath ports report whether their bits agree. Predicted agreement is 9/20. An independent-bit alternative matching even the individual bath frequencies at every K predicts 13/24. With 16,384 starts per board, the coupled reader gave respectively **7,497, 7,430 and 7,391 agreements**. All sixteen full-word bins on all three boards agree with the native forecast within the simultaneous 99% allowance of 0.01932. The independent-port prediction fails on every board. The reference table is independently acquired. Leaving the cut open gives R and disabling the exchange leaves the input packed. These controls reject the conditioned forecast at 64 sweeps. [Independent tests](test_bath.py)

A second transaction freezes the complete ordered word u at the other three exposed ports. It couples the active port to the same four hidden ports, retaining the total inventory. At equilibrium each of those five positions has conditional unit probability (4−|u|)/5. For every allowed visible output y this is exactly R(y) divided by the mass of the whole visible fibre, and also P(y) divided by the P mass of that fibre. The active service therefore realizes weighted conditional resampling by the same reference as the state projection. Equivalently it minimizes the transition-row divergence among **all** viable distributions supported on the full fixed-u fibre. A second choice of the active port is obtained by an adjacent SWAP followed by its inverse. The native left-pair half contact likewise resamples its ordering conditional on its total and the two exposed bath bits. These three published transactions can be composed and repeated on the same material. Each preserves both R and P because each is their common conditional law given its held datum. Each updated bath bit has both outputs with positive probability, and the left ordering can swap wherever it is unequal. Their cyclic product is therefore irreducible with a positive hold probability within every K class. On the prescribed mixture of K classes it converges to P. This gives a four-bit repair kernel whose local transitions and state selection use the same reference, independently of how the hidden bath was arranged at the beginning of a transaction. This five-site transaction holds every declared conditioned bit and erases dependence on the previous value of the refreshed bit. We tested all eight complete words, both active ports and both possible old values. The largest absolute discrepancy across the 32 banks was 0.00767.
