# A two-repair fluctuation measurement

Use the fixed-duration, four-unit, eight-port material path on the 20-owner board. Expose the first four bits only. For a refresh of visible port j, interchange sites j and 3 with adjacent swaps, shuffle sites 3 through 7 completely using the previously specified five-site insertion calendar and undo the address interchange. Publish the four-bit word at the start and after each complete refresh. A route or gate outcome inside an appointment remains internal. The same material R preparation is available through the eight-site shuffle.

Prepare P using the registered fixed-duration Gibbs source with odd selector exposure π/3 and the packed inputs 60 and 29. P, R and Q each get a fresh calibration bank. Q is generated by preparing another P and servicing port 0 then port 2, discarding both intermediate reads. No shot is seeded from a forecast table. For the forward experiment record three words following that same address order from a new P. For the reverse use another generated Q and offer 2 before 0. On a third independently drawn Q retain the forward address order as a control. Run an equilibrium control by starting at R, and a changed-contact control by parking the exchange term on the two public repairs after the same native P preparation. Permit no rejected launches. Use 65,536 launches per calibration or trajectory bank; retain all 16 word or 4,096 trajectory bins with implicit zeros. Use independent random draws with fixed separate seeds.

Before counting, the predicted odds multiplier assigned to a history x,m,y is (P(x)/R(x))/(Q(y)/R(y)). Compute each marginal prediction independently of the forward and reverse history samples from the inventory count and the native full-fibre rule. Test every exact path, including zero support, the integral identity, the relative-entropy loss and both signs. Compare forward frequency to reversed frequency times that multiplier for every distinct multiplier and for all feasible triples. Test raw marginals against the three separate calibration cohorts. Repeat with a flat visible reference, which drops hidden-port multiplicity. A held control with the reverse service in the wrong order must alter a registered path event. Equilibrium predicts zero production, and parking predicts no changes. Calibrate a familywise 99% Hoeffding half-width for up to 1,024 tested scalar sample means, with each statistic rescaled to its known range. Compare two independently measured frequencies with their two separate half-widths. Keep stochastic scatter apart from exact rational identities. Units of entropy production are natural logarithms of dimensionless record probabilities.

For a comparison using only detector counts, also form simultaneous exact binomial intervals on 48 calibration frequencies and on all 64 forward and 64 reversed path frequencies. Compare the independent ranges for the endpoint/reference odds and for the forward/reverse frequency ratio on each path. Report how many forward histories have a negative sign already resolved by the independent calibration. Split the family error ceiling equally between the Hoeffding and exact-binomial families. For the flat-reference foil aggregate all predicted positive residuals into one signed statistic before inspecting the counts.
