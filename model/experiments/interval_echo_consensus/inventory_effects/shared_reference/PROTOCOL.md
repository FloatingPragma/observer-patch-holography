# Comparing one split material load with zero, one or two shared reference loads

On the twenty-cell Interval Echo board, prepare a signal from one sharp unit and split it equally on a blank contact. Independently prepare zero, one or two reference units the same way on disjoint contacts. Route one rail of each pair to cell 0 and its partner to cell 13. Apply a private phase of zero or π to the signal rail at cell 0. These are the two equally likely source settings. No site receives the source key. All contacts outside the addressed services are parked. The labels, source timing, detectors and classical feedback are supplied. This experiment tests local readout of a relative phase between different local loads. The registered quantum response supplies the probabilities.

In the enrolled mode order (signal, first reference, second reference), Alice mixes her m=1,2,3 rails by a discrete Fourier transform lifted by adjacent number-preserving rotations. She reads her whole sharp pattern. Publish those bits classically with a fixed-load pointer. Bob uses the received bits to set his analyzer on the remaining m rails. The two possible phase branches, computed from an ideal equal splitter and Alice's Fourier minors, prescribe his positive and negative basis vectors: add or subtract the separately normalized amplitudes from initial signal-at-A and signal-at-B. Their orthogonal complement is retained. Where only one branch can have contributed, assign plus at Alice weight zero and minus at full weight. Publish a binary guess on every start, giving plus to any extra complement outcome. A reader that fails to receive a valid report must record a refusal and counts as incorrect.

Freeze the native, exchange-only (κ=0), and signal-path-read controls on the two-reference apparatus. The path-read control sharply reads which signal rail was occupied after routing and retains that bit. Also run the native zero- and one-reference experiments. In order test native L=0,1,2, path-read L=2, exchange-only L=2; within each enumerate phase 0 then π. Use 131072 simulated starts per bank from PCG64 seed 26032615 and the existing integer-grid sampler. Count every Alice word, retained path bit, Bob word and guess, including both occupations at a station and any empty detector. Keep the marginal Alice histogram.

Predict the equal-prior success fractions (1+D)/2, with D=0, 1/2, 1/√2 at L=0,1,2. Both controls should give 1/2. A guarantee for all independent reference states of fixed total L bounds their usable distinguishability by cos(π/(L+2)) when local instruments conserve each station's total including its apparatus. A closed finite charged reference cannot give sharp distinction of all phase-sensitive effects. A separation by a setting-dependent transport or by coherent feedback carrying charge phase would fall outside this bound. Test every bank against a simultaneous two-sided 99% counting bound, including equality of Alice's marginals across the hidden signs. Retain a mismatch as a measured failure, and treat missing banks as missing. The three input resources were chosen to span the first cases of that bound.
