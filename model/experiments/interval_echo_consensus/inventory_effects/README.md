## A prediction from record symmetry

A second procedure reads Y and three independently prepared native fair bits on owners 1, 2 and 3. At Y+, three of the eight bit words give each H record. At Y−, one word gives each. The other bit words give the complementary record. In the symmetry calibration we give the second Y output an additional quarter phase. The two sharp input states are then respectively even and odd eigenrays of a literal native SWAP of the two output words. Relabelling covariance, equality of probabilities for the same prepared ray, sharp support, and completeness force equal probabilities for the two output words. This derivation uses no squared-amplitude sampler. The specified blank tie has fair signs, and the three coins are independently prepared. Counting their 16 equally weighted joint alternatives predicts **4, 4, 8** for the three records on either sharp source.

The independent simulation gave 733,305, 733,666 and 1,465,765 records. Inserting an additional native SWAP gave 732,946, 732,812 and 1,466,978. Omitting the balancing rotation gave 820,543, 820,829 and 1,291,364. The law's committed quantum instrument supplies the simulated draws used to compare with this symmetry prediction. Its independent content is the forecast from relabelling, sharp support, fair bits and causally independent preparation. ## Replay

Every bank draws from integer weights on a 2⁻⁴⁰ grid. All bins except the most probable are rounded, the most probable takes the complement, and each launch compares one PCG64 integer below 2⁴⁰ with the cumulative weights. With the 3×10⁻¹² norm budget of a read and its fair-bit product, the complement moves by less than 3×10⁻¹¹. The compiler sends phases within roundoff of a multiple of 2π to zero and arguments within roundoff of −π to π, and the two rank-one held effects keep an exact zero eigenvalue.
