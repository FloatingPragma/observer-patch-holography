# What the two measurement cohorts establish

## Exact sharp history underlying the sampler

The twelve independent preparations use the routed 02 stocks of the [affine chart](../../chart.py). Tracing the companion stock leaves a diagonal binary marginal for each work port, with ternary outcomes 0 and 2 and a zero middle-bin probability. This gives the complete product law of the initial data words. In the [native sharp meter](../../apparatus.py) the two readings against 0 and 2 give Lüders projectors on load 0, 1 or 2. A repair on (a,b) first reads a, then applies the physical seam dwell. The decision controller does not receive this intermediate result.

For a sharp pair x and its swap Sx, the native pulse has nonzero entries only on x and Sx. For unlike 0 and 2 the exchange probability is p=sin²(t/2), since the contrast diagonal is the same for both orders. Equal words remain equal. For an arbitrary diagonal terminal effect D, including any spectators, its pullback after observing pre-read r is Pᵣ U† D U Pᵣ. Its only possible off-diagonal entry joins x to Sx, whose first-port values differ when x≠Sx. Hence that entry vanishes. The resulting diagonal entry is the indicator of the observed r times (1−p)D(x)+pD(Sx). Equal words give D(x). Backward induction over a finite word gives the probability of each complete pre-read history and terminal bin. The sampled sharp trajectories implement these quantum effects exactly; they do not postulate that the underlying coherent material has classical hidden loads. The independent test constructs all conditional 16-by-16 density matrices on the active four-port system for repeated and overlapping seams. It agrees with the trajectory calculation on every terminal word. The mean marginal after a native π/2 offer is the arithmetic average at both endpoints. At π/3 the incoming weights at a are 3/4 and 1/4.

Both the prepared chart and the sharp trajectories include all twelve data ports. The other eight are spectators during the registered program. Each consumed cohort releases the terminal histogram and retains the joint sharp transcript for audit. Only the terminal totals are used by the supplied controller. A new decision cannot depend on an unobserved source coordinate, a previous individual's intermediate read, or a retained copy's state. If preparations share random disturbances or are incapable of independent repetition, the conditional product law below requires a separate certificate.

## A common adaptive word and its cost

At launch, independent cohorts are allocated to the successive possible inspections. Each still-live copy physically receives every instruction on the realized common prefix. At step j, the controller consumes one cohort and chooses the continuation from its terminal interval. Other cohorts' random preparations and native outcome tapes are independent of that consumed cohort before conditioning on the common word. This suffices even though the selected word depends on earlier data. Conditional on any reached controller history, the next fresh cohort therefore supplies N independent bounded readings with the correct mean for the selected word. Two disjoint terminal cohorts receive exactly the same complete word. No individual outcome is used to prune survivors. On an early stop all remaining cohorts keep their physical prefix and are counted. No recovery of an inspected copy and no cloning enter this argument.

For D decision nodes along a reached route with H=D+2 reserved cohorts, one seam before each decision and one before the two terminal reads costs at most N[H+(H−1)+⋯+2] copy-level offers. Earlier stops reduce the count; this receipt gives every actual instruction and receiving cohort index. More general finite read trees may reserve their maximal inspection depth, the sum of the instructions applied to each remaining cohort, and an explicit storage and publication budget. The implementation caps retained audit history at eight offers along a path and encodes a terminal signed score plus N in 17 places. A resource check precedes source mounting. Supported registers and seams are those of the 12-port chart. A sense or repair outside its physical layout requires a different implementation. Nonstationary source or preparation error must fit the independently established allowance.

## One event covering decisions, abstention and publication

Let μ be the declared ensemble mean at a reached read. A calibrated material reading X lies in [−1,1], with its actual mean within γ of μ. Conditional on the reached controller history, the probability that |sample mean−μ| exceeds r=√(2 log(2K/δ)/N)+γ is at most δ/K by the two-sided Hoeffding inequality for independent bounded variables. Summing over the at most K potentially visited inspections gives simultaneous coverage at least 1−δ. Unvisited looks do not spend extra error probability. For the present simulation the analytic chart and native matrices have their exact mean actions. The extra 10⁻⁹ allows more than the chart and gate floating-point checks accumulated over three exchanges; it is not an estimate of hardware noise. In a chart of physical width W the sampling term becomes W√(log(2K/δ)/(2N)).

On the covered event the true μ lies in each clipped interval. Every accepted sign is correct and a true zero always abstains. If |μ|>2r, every reached interval resolves its correct sign. At the terminal step the true common mean then lies in the nonempty intersection. Requiring both intervals independently to exclude zero allows a conservative abstention even when an intersection alone would resolve a sign. Acceptance near a boundary is not guaranteed. The [Lean result](Publication.lean) proves containment, soundness and the 2r sufficient separation for two bounded reads, conditional on the individual coverage event. The confidence probability follows from the preceding conditional concentration argument.

The terminal native words store the actual signed scores and one of three orthogonal decisions. The public receiver obtains the numerical interval from the pair of integer words, N and r when the sign flags authorize it.
