# Occupation-count action protocol

Use ports 2 and 3 of one owner for the two positions of a sharp double and port 4 as its empty comparison rail. An echo, a read of agreement between ports 3 and 4, and the inverse echo publish the bit and restore its sharp rails. The only active flight seam is (2,3). Compare the registered Hamiltonian with exchange suppressed while all port incidences, pointer operations, contrast, and appointments are preserved.

A half contact followed by the binary read prepares a fair initial law for the individual chain. Apply the same subsequent duration on either input, with cosine of its angle equal to 1/3. This design setting makes an exact comparison to the symmetric binary chain in the variational theorem. Use pi/3 as an independent held duration. Resolve the two native columns and both branches of the pointer before pooling any launches. Read four consecutive times in a fair-initialized bank of 262,144 five-bit histories, including its initial bit.

Independently prepare banks of 1, 8, 16, 32 and 64 records. Stratify the initial sharp preparations by zero, half (even sizes only), or all of the copies on the second rail. The positions after one contact are read individually. An observer publishes the number on the second rail. For each bank retain the complete histogram, including zero-count categories. Use 262,144 independent banks at each setting. Batch independent identical Bernoulli reads by their binomial counts. The predicted count law is the convolution of the two independently derived single-input laws.

For a check on fitted curvature reserve the 64-copy input-zero bank at the specified duration. Compare the quarter and half output bins against the per-copy corner action. A velocity-quadratic correction fixes the ratio of their corrections to 3/4. Assess also the infinite-copy prediction analytically. Use a separate common-draw alternative with the same single-rail marginals to test the independence of the repetitions. Every endpoint read is retained. No timing, stiffness or shape is selected from these banks.
