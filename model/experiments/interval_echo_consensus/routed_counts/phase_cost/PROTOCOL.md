# Calibrating a quadratic load reading on twelve ports

## Frozen before acquisition

PR-117 already describes a squared-load score and a proposed physical readback cost. This comparison determines which physical quantity two readers can assign that score to. The registered Interval Echo law has contrast coefficient eight and a private reference on degree-five ports. Earlier sharp census and one-load phase experiments informed this choice. [Census and strict repair](../README.md) · [Independent reference phase](../../../interval_echo_gauge/scalar_grammar/README.md) · [Counting mechanism and alternative costs](../../../../../code/a5_closure/record_counting_mechanism_certificate.py)

Use the 20-cell board. Source owners 0 and 13 send to each other in independent runs. For each target choose the first adjacent pair inside the receiver with a private first endpoint, in lexicographic order. Start every other cell empty. Route the sampled source address to the first target port by full blank exchanges on the fixed spanning tree. The paths, degree check, addressing, private exposure and local final occupation meter are declared. Each acquired word has twelve ternary loads summing to twelve. Prepare six doubles followed by six vacancies on the source. Offer successive complementary pairs for strict conversion to two singles, on fresh unused chords off the spanning tree, returning the other loads to their original addresses. Take every prefix of the six offers; also offer two equal loads in a separate refusal control. Track event identifiers, offered endpoints and both signed load changes reported at each successful transaction. An all-single word prepared directly is the independent endpoint.

Each of the twelve addresses is enrolled equally on fresh copies. A half exchange splits the selected load against the empty second port. Expose the private reference of the first port, undo the splitter with the complementary positive dwell, then read the second port. Keep its full ternary read, including zero. The primary decoder uses only the fraction of nonempty second-port readings pooled over the twelve addresses. Commission bank A at an exposure π/κ and bank B at π/(4κ), with separate unit and empty standards. Under the native term the unit clicks in A and clicks with probability a in B, a obtained from the standard. A double is dark in A and bright in B. Define its quadratic reading in single-load reference units as twelve times [A + 4(B − a A)]. The decoder receives neither a preparation word nor its repair count. Check a third exposure 3π/(8κ) on each prefix against the reconstructed single/double fractions. All control dwells remain frozen.

Use 65,536 original trials per enrolled address and appointment; aggregate counts and obtain simultaneous 99% conservative errors from independent bounded trials. Retain post-transport reports for both owners. Pass if both records cover the independently reconstructed quadratic sum on every prefix, adjacent native repairs separate by two within simultaneous errors, and all third-appointment forecasts pass. Challenge the apparatus with the same private exposure and a linear load energy, also with the contrast coefficient removed everywhere. The predictions need to discriminate both controls from the quadratic law on a word with doubles; the all-single calibration cannot do so on its own. Report every disagreement. Separately count the number of contact activations and the private dwell required per read at a fixed address. Test directly whether that resource changes between the six-double and all-single words. The readings do not presume that a phase accumulation is the minimum time, energy dissipated, or number of gates needed to read a port.
