# Consensus on the material load census

On every initially blank, connected Interval Echo screen with enough spare seams, a routed strict-repair service reaches the minimum possible quadratic load at the given total load. Every ordering of successful repairs gives the same number of vacancies, doubles and published marks. This holds for **all ternary occupations**, with no fixed particle number. Offers may compete for either endpoint. An arbitrary all-open native flight can occur between completed transactions. The positions and signs of the written marks can distinguish the schedules. ## What the observer measures

Let \(N\) be the number of material ports. The observer reads the counts \(v,d\) of ports holding zero and two loads, respectively. The other \(N-v-d\) ports each hold one. Let \(m\) count the occupied collars. A local blank/occupied guard or the initial count plus the published strict-write successes supplies this third reading. The detector can read the material and the flags at the terminal visit. Calibration copies supply the initial readings without making an intermediate position measurement on a coherent flight.

The independent target is equalization at fixed material load \(L=N+d-v\). From the ternary loads the quadratic cost is \(Q=\sum_i n_i^2=L+2d\). Its minimum over every word of that load is \(Q_{\min}=L+2\max(0,L-N)\). The gap is precisely \(2\min(v,d)\). A terminal census with a vacancy and a double fails this target, even if no presently adjacent seam can see the two together. These are finite record units.

## Which transactions the native material implements

Reserve a connected spanning tree of blank native seams. Write onto any available chord. On the 20-, 80- and 320-cell screens the trees route between all 240, 960 and 3,840 ports. They leave 391, 1,561 and 6,241 eligible write sites. Since a commit consumes one vacancy and one double, at most half the number of ports need a fresh site, even from the heaviest finite preparation. Previously occupied chord memories of arbitrary internal state are also allowed if there are enough blank chords. More generally the tree may be chosen anew before each offer, provided the blank graph remains connected and the chosen write edge lies on a cycle. The initial blank cycle surplus must cover \(\min(v,d)\).

A fully prewritten screen shows why the initial memory condition matters. Prepare one vacancy and one double at equal overall load to the all-unit material. Every strict request reads an occupied collar, even after an arbitrary native flight. The full 99-state checks give an invariant occupied flag and zero strict-hit operator on every occupied column. The material retains a quadratic excess of two. This is an exact obstruction if one extends the input domain to all possible prewritten collars while allowing only flight and strict writes.

On an isolated blank seam the native term is \((I-S)/2+8(n_a-n_b)^2\), where \(S\) exchanges the two ports. The two parts commute. At exposure \(\pi\) the first factor exchanges the loads and the second gives phase one for all nine inputs. Other contacts and degree-reference services are parked **during these routing pulses**. A forward path of swaps and a return along all but the final edge exchanges the two distant endpoints and restores every intermediate slot. Two such transpositions bring the offered ports to the chord. Their definition tracks the displaced second slot even if it started at the first chord endpoint. The material on those paths may be occupied and coherently entangled with other registers. Each transfer is a permutation on the entire load space.

The chord service uses the registered echo, the public magnitude-two and outgoing-unit-pair test, and the occupied flag. Its quiet branch undoes the echo. A hit changes 20 or 02 to 11, leaves the signed information in the collar, and publishes success. Undo the **routing permutation** on either branch. On the remaining material this returns every unoffered slot to its original address. The two possible input orientations have orthogonal successful records even if their signs remain unread. The controller supplies routing, guard isolation, the availability mask, and finite requests. The load inputs and native strict response determine whether the requested pair succeeds. ## The finite theorem

Each accepted write on the census has precisely the reduction

\[
(v+1,d+1,m)\longrightarrow(v,d,m+1).
\]

A vacancy and a double exist whenever the material is above its minimum. Routing a chosen pair can visit them on every connected board in the stated domain. Hence a census is normal exactly when it achieves the independent minimum. Every accepted step decreases \(\min(v,d)\) by one. The protected values \((v+m,d+m)\) are preserved and uniquely determine the terminal census at fixed \(N\). Indeed, the final mark count is the smaller of these two values. Every competing accepted fork projects to the same next census, so all its descendants can reach the same normal form. The Lean proof instantiates the existing observation-relative endpoint theorem and checks completeness, descent, the local diamond, existence and cross-source uniqueness for unbounded natural counts. The native certificate supplies the physical relation used by that proof. [Lean source](Census.lean) · [Observation-relative endpoint theorem](../../../../Lean/ObservableNormalForms/ObservableNormalForms/ObserverConfluence.lean)

A complete scan of unordered port pairs normalizes with certainty if it runs uninterrupted. After inverse routing, a quiet probe has its diagonal discrepancy projector removed from the source. A strict hit changes only the two selected slots into unit loads. A pair once quiet can never become a new discrepancy in the rest of that scan. Thus at its end every input word has reached the minimum. Within a homogeneous census sector this also holds for coherent inputs: every nonzero history has reduced exactly the same number of discrepancies. A scan can stop after its first success, its pair order can change on every subsequent scan, and unrestricted native flights may intervene between those scans. Equally, all individual successful steps decrease the rank when flights occur between offers. Progress then requires a schedule that actually reaches a discrepancy. Repeating a quiet offer forever can stall.

For a coherent mixture across census sectors, individual conditional outcomes update the observer's information. The schedule-independent statement concerns the **complete** terminal measurement including the no-write outcome. Its effect for census \(z\) equals the sum of all initial census projectors whose normal value is \(z\). On each such projector the event is certain and on the others impossible. For a positive effect bounded by the identity this also forces the off-diagonal terms to vanish. Taking a partial trace gives the same joint terminal census and untouched reference for every normalizing schedule. The result concerns probabilities for the declared instrument. It permits different complete material states, full address logs, and relative phases between witnesses of equal census.

## Records and controls

Each native sharp run on the three federations visits all 66 pairs of twelve enrolled source ports, with different eligible chords and opposite offer orders. Five load words include asymmetric, balanced and already-normal inputs. The twelve sites are embedded in an otherwise unit-filled screen. In the five-vacancy, five-double preparation every native run wrote five times and finished with all unit loads. The addressed exchange-disabled control wrote zero times for these same offers at each size. On the 320-owner board the forward order wrote at offers 5, 16, 26, 35 and 43; the reverse order wrote at offers 24, 32, 41, 51 and 62, counting from one. The receipt retains all refusals and the partially completed census after ten offers. The remaining, unenrolled board pairs are provably quiet on the native terminal words. Their number is reported separately from executed offers. [Sharp trajectories](measure.py)

A separate prospective control loaded **all 240 ports** independently and preoccupied eleven chords in known memory states. Both complete scans executed every one of their 28,680 offers. The two orders wrote 74 further marks, finished with 85, and reduced the quadratic cost from 372 to its independent minimum of 224. The exchange-disabled runs made zero new marks and retained cost 372. The pair order was fixed before reading the loads. [Dense preparation](dense.py) · [All accepted and refused offers](dense.json)

The coherent simulation uses the full 240-port federation. One launch component has all unit loads and the reference in its plus state. Two equal-weight tagged components have a vacancy at port 0 and a double at port 12 or 13, with relative phase \(i\) and orthogonal reference bits. This joint preparation and the reference readout are supplied. The components have the same total load. Vacancy and double propagate together for a quarter exchange unit in the entire 57,360-word ordered two-defect sector. A program independently pulls both opposite full scans' strict detectors back through the physical routing paths and their earlier quiet events. All 28,680 possible successful pair records and the no-mark event are archived for both outcomes of the reference, using \(2^{20}\) simulated launches per bank. The changed-flight control suppresses exchange during the freely timed interval; both scan instruments remain functional.
