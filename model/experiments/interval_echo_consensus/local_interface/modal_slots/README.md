One 320-cell Interval Echo board carries the two registered pairs of modal record alphabets. A common thirteen-label register is the first slot in both pairs. Its partner has fourteen labels in one pair and thirteen in the other. The material encoding realizes all **2,366** triple labels, even though the first pair's two 96-node record supports share 25 nodes. Publishing one observer's material outcome destroys its interference and preserves the other observer's interference. ## What the observers recorded

The scheduled replay visits the exact 32 joint path positions. For the designated pair the 31 steps comprise nine with neither label changing, four for each of the one-sided changes, and fourteen changing both. For the disjoint pair the corresponding counts are nine, four changing only the right, five changing only the left, and thirteen changing both. These counts test the source-to-reader mapping on the programmed path. The address requests supply the walk.

Independent cohorts tested phase. For the first and last labels a native half exchange prepares two amplitudes. Invert the material encoding and apply the same half exchange: leaving the packet unread gives the opposite label on all 65,536 launches for each observer. Publishing all packet occupations at the intervening appointment gave 32,532, 32,869 and 32,751 opposite labels respectively. Separate paired banks published only the first observer's packets. In 86/88, 32,766 left outcomes were first and 32,770 were last; all 65,536 right outcomes were last. In 86/247 these counts were 32,615 and 32,921, again with every opposite-slot transfer intact. A completely read superposition thus loses contrast even on the disjoint pair. For observer 88 that tuple lies outside its alphabet. All 65,536 invalid records enter the denominator.
