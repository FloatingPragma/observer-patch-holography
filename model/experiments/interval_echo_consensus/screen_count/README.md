# When do cell records multiply?

Two simulated receiving stations read all twelve ternary loads of each enrolled cell and report its parity. With one boundary seam parked, a twelve-cell region gave **2,048** possible words. Opening that seam for half a swap gave **4,096**. Every eleven-cell marginal is exactly the uniform product in both experiments. The same comparison on six and nine cells gave 32 versus 64 and 256 versus 512. The possible words follow from the native transfer amplitudes and the decoded meter responses. All possible words occurred in each 262,144-launch bank. [Frozen comparison](PROTOCOL.md) · [Sharp receiver records and counts](records.json.gz) · [Count-only results](summary.json)

Each internal bridge starts with one unit shared between its physical ends. Distinct bridges have disjoint endpoints. In the blank-memory single-load sector the native exchange transfers the unit with probability sin²(t/2). The first exterior bridge has its unit outside the region. At transfer times zero, π/3, π/2 and π it contributes an interior unit with probabilities 0, 1/4, 1/2 and 1. The input inventory of the combined region and reservoir is fixed. Both heads transport each queried digit to their own zero and double references using native exchanges, verify the two bright/dark flags, and return the transport. Sharp records can be read twice. Acquisition contracts these calibrated measurements on the disjoint prepared dimers. The clock, isolated contacts, region boundary, loads and binary read menu are specified. ### Which comparisons distinguish the four boundary settings?

Changing an internal bridge orientation toggles precisely its two owner bits. A path therefore toggles its endpoints while cancelling all changes in between. Leaf-first choices on a spanning tree realize every requested change of even total parity. A disconnected region requires even change in each connected component. Conversely, every allowed internal move preserves those component totals. Each reachable word has equally many orientation preimages, since adding any one preimage of a difference bijects its fibers. [Incidence and deletion theorem](Product.lean)

In a connected region of n cells, deleting any owner gives a bijection from a fixed total-parity class to all 2^(n−1) words on the other owners. All smaller projections therefore agree, even if the boundary class is mixed in any proportion. In the twelve-cell bank, 262,144, 196,709, 130,908 and zero reports respectively had odd parity. The read on the exterior port distinguished the two classes in every registered arm. At a quarter transfer probability the number of possible whole words is still 4,096, while the Shannon entropy is 11.811278 bits. At half transfer it is 12 bits. Parking a separating internal bridge splits the twelve-cell region into sizes one and eleven and reduces its closed support to 1,024 words. Deleting exchange from all active links gives one word. A missing reference merges two sharp levels at its own calibration. A cycle flip on the larger regions changes observed rail records at the same public cell word. The tests reconstruct all joint counts from the rail banks. Of 218,616 registered bin comparisons, the largest discrepancy used 0.807 of its simultaneous 99% allowance. [Acceptance rule](READBACK.md) · [Independent readback](readback.py)

On an unswitched region all interior seams can also run at once. With one unit among the 72 ports the six-cell screen has at most six parity words. With two units it has sixteen. A shortest configuration path contributes with the same sign as every other shortest path, so all legal configurations have positive amplitude at common generic times. For C cells and K mobile binary loads, a word with r odd cells can occur exactly when r has the parity of K and r is at most min(C, K, 12C−K). Summing the binomial coefficients over those r gives the generic count. At time eight the finite banks resolved five words in either six-cell sector. The analytic support count needs no assumption of uniformly likely records. [Simultaneous free flight](free_inventory/README.md)

### How many messages can a later observer recover?

On a separately enrolled eight-cell tree, the closed boundary gives 128 raw parity words. An unannounced native continuation can leave the source alone or swap one of seven internal bridges. A decoder receives only the eight cell parities. The possible receipts for each source therefore contain eight distinct words. At most 16 disjoint eight-word sets fit in one sector. The measured tree coordinates realize the binary Hamming code of length seven. Its 16 codewords attain that bound. With both boundary classes available there are 32 messages. A source which half-splits every active bridge and then corrects the syndrome of its actual material record prepares all codewords without an intended output label. All 2,048 sharp source/continuation combinations are deposited. Passing the unresolved-label mixture as one joint kernel to the exact checkpoint evaluator verifies every edge of its confusability graph. The separate deterministic calibration runs alone supply the individual transfer columns. A two-swap control produces a terminal report attributed to the wrong codeword by the one-swap decoder. The continuation menu matters even at the same enrollment. When an arbitrary finite sequence of the seven errors is allowed and its labels stay hidden, the measured generators give 128 reachable permutations. Every source can reach every word of its boundary class. Zero-error capacity becomes one message in a fixed class, or two across both. Adding the boundary swap generates 256 permutations and leaves just one message across the combined classes. Giving the receiver the exchange log makes every permutation invertible and recovers the source. Equivalently the registered evaluator permits a different decoder for each member of an indexed, known channel family. For that experiment every individual permutation has 128 distinguishable inputs in one class, or 256 across both. The smaller counts require a single decoder without the label. The deposited closure includes a representative path for each permutation and its action on every possible input. Closure under the generators certifies all lengths. The hidden-label model was also realized as one joint physical channel. Half-swaps on independent auxiliary dimers at owners 8 and 10 produce the selector bits. The measured optima are 32, two and one messages. Parking the selector gives 256. Providing the selector record to the terminal decoder also gives 256.
