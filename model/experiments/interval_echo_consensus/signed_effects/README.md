# A common instrument for both signs of one material defect

A vacancy and an excess in a unit-filled Interval Echo cell can be addressed independently by **one sequence of the same native settings**. No controller needs to know the incoming sign. The ideal protected control group on an \(m\)-port connected region is \(U(m)\times U(m)\), including the two unobservable sector phases when the signs are measured separately. On a cell this gives a common measurement for every compatible effect pair of the complex algebra \(M_4\oplus M_4\). The terminal frequency rule can be characterized by an elementary local pointer condition. A retained charge count determines the one remaining central weight. ## Two independent controls from one sequence

Write \(s=-1,+1\) for a single vacancy or excess relative to a port background of one unit, and \(E_p=|p\rangle\langle p|\). Keep all seams blank. Open one contact or one degree-five reference at a time, parking the others. For the addressed edge \(ij\) the Hamiltonian in **both** sectors is
\[
H_{ij}=\tfrac{17}{2}(E_i+E_j)-\tfrac12 X_{ij},\quad X_{ij}=|i\rangle\langle j|+|j\rangle\langle i|.
\]
The spectator word \((1,1)\) has zero energy. The native reference on port \(i\) has \(D_i=8I+q_sE_i\), where \(q_s=8[(1+s)^2-1]\) is \(-8\) for a vacancy and \(24\) for an excess. The complete 99-state column reductions and reference law are checked. If a desired port has degree six, conjugate the physical degree-five reference by native \(\pi\)-swaps along a path inside the region. Such swaps exchange the two positions with phase \(+1\) on either sign. This uses a connected region that contains at least one degree-five reference. [Native gates and rational commutator identities](measure.py)

With \(2H_{ij}\) as the edge generator, its second and fourth commutators with \(D_i\) give \(-q_s^2 X_{ij}\) and \(-q_s^4 X_{ij}\) on sector \(s\). The coefficient determinant is \(18{,}874{,}368\). Linear combinations therefore isolate the edge exchange in either sign alone. One more commutator supplies the antisymmetric edge generator, and their bracket supplies the diagonal difference. Along a connected graph these generators span each \(\mathfrak{su}(m)\) independently. Finally the sector traces of \(H_{ij}\) are \((17,17)\) and those of \(D_i\) are \((8m-8,8m+24)\), which span both central directions. The control group is the full connected product. To see exact reachability, the linear span of conjugates of primitive generators by available control words is invariant under their adjoints and therefore contains the generated Lie algebra. Finite products of the conjugate one-parameter gates in a spanning basis have full differential at the identity, so their image contains an open neighborhood. A connected group is generated by any such neighborhood. Both primitive generators have integer spectra, allowing negative durations to be replayed with positive waits. Durations and independently protected windows are supplied controls.

For compatible \(E_s,F_s\) on four input rails in each sector, stack \(\sqrt{E_s},\sqrt{F_s},\sqrt{I-E_s-F_s}\) into a twelve-by-four isometry. Extend each isometry to a twelve-port unitary. Simultaneous controllability realizes both with one program. Grouping the exhaustive output address into three four-port sets implements the requested common instrument, including unrelated noncommuting effects in the two signs. This is the sign-preserving algebra. The specified controls conserve total load and cannot detect a phase between different total-load sectors. ## Which probability assumption suffices?

Take a definite sign and \(m\ge3\). Assume a complete terminal position read with one click, certainty on sharp inputs, implementation-independent probabilities for the same prepared ray, and covariance under relabelling the address. Require that any protected unitary on all the other rails leaves the click probability at a spared rail unchanged. In the cell the punctured graph remains connected and has available degree references, so those complementary unitaries are constructible. Preservation of the actual *record probabilities* under such a disjoint operation is the operational locality assumption. The Hamiltonian specifies the gates. Within Interval Echo's quantum instrument law, the local position projector commutes with the disjoint gates and verifies this assumption.

On a pure input \(\psi\), unitaries on the other rails act transitively on complementary vectors of any fixed norm. Together with global-phase invariance and relabelling this makes the probability at rail \(i\) a common function \(f(|\psi_i|^2)\). Sharp calibration gives \(f(0)=0,f(1)=1\). Compare the normalized three-coordinate configurations \((x,y,1-x-y)\) and \((x+y,0,1-x-y)\) in squared magnitudes. Completeness gives \(f(x)+f(y)=f(x+y)\) on the entire unit interval where the sum is allowed. Positivity makes \(f\) monotone; rational bounds now give \(f(x)=x\) without a continuity assumption. Rotating the terminal basis or using the three-block implementation gives the corresponding pure-state Born probabilities for every compatible event. Classical mixing of actual runs extends this result to their density mixtures.

This proof uses neither squared-amplitude sampling as a premise nor agreement on a finite list of fitted effects. It uses the stated pointer conditions on all admissible pure preparations. Their necessity can be tested. A normalized fourth-power sampler agrees with every sharp calibration and all equal-amplitude splittings. Apply a balanced exchange only between the two spectator rails of a preparation with squares \((1/2,1/2,0)\). The new squares are \((1/2,1/4,1/4)\). That sampler changes the **untouched** first-port probability from \(1/2\) to \(2/3\). Each individual sampler context still has a normalized, additive distribution.

For a public mixture with vacancy fraction \(t\), conditioning and complete counts give the probability \(t\,p_-+(1-t)p_+\) exactly. Without any central source flag or affine mixing contract, the sign-conditioned data leave a freedom: one can use \(w(t)=t^2/[t^2+(1-t)^2]\) as the central weight for an uncalibrated label \(t\). The alternative agrees at 0, 1 and \(1/2\); it gives genuine additive event probabilities in each fixed preparation. It changes the mapping from a preparation label to the operational state. At a publicly counted 1-in-4 vacancy source it instead predicts the wrong signed source frequency. This example separates cross-context additivity from the calibration of a mixture. The declared model already fixes both when its quantum state and pointer probabilities are supplied.

A three-pulse receiver tests these distinctions directly. On ports 0 and 1 apply the contact for \(\pi/2\), the degree reference at 0 for \(\pi/12\), then the contact for \(3\pi/2\) to invert the splitter. Starting the charge at 0, occupation at 1 has probability \(\sin^2(q_s\pi/24)\). With the same controls, 49,299 of 65,536 vacancy and zero of 196,608 excess simulated starts arrived. Every start has both a public sign and an address. The exact combined probability is \(3/16\); ignoring the actual sign tally and taking \(w(1/4)=1/10\) would instead give \(3/40\). The code uses the committed sampler for these synthetic counts. [Complete raw bins and seed](results.json) · [Independent rank, selector, and mutation tests](test_signed_effects.py)

The 288-dimensional driven unitary group is a control group on the full signed cell. This constrains any claim of twelve-direction exhaustion that includes this protected menu and these carriers. The twelve registered compressed port currents have a separate physical menu.
