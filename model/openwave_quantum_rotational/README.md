# Quantum angular momentum of a localized charged state

A finite native matter construction coupled to quantized Maxwell modes has
an interacting charged doublet with exact total spin one half. Its complete
records join localized position, rest energy, current and field angular
momentum on one source. One electron calibration supplies conditional GeV
values, and independent photon caps retain their common unit. The rotational
carrier, trap and coupling are declared; a physical electron and continuum
interpretation require additional matching. See the [proof](PROOF.md) and
[replayable records](results.json).

Run `python3 -m model.openwave_quantum_rotational --write` or `--check`.
