## bare native source

**Source:** complete all-seam C/E on H66 or H78; source-only charges.

**Preparation and readout:** native complete occupation/current records.

**Propagation and energy:** no nontrivial charge supplied by this classification.

**Topology:** no protected source-only reducing charge in this domain.

**Boundary:** history, coherent memory, recovery, partial seam support and refinement are outside this domain.

[Owning proof or construction](../../model/completion/defect_algebra.py).

## ordered exchange charge

**Source:** H66 with a coherent orientation bit; complete ordered-cover repair.

**Preparation and readout:** coherent memory preparation; complete charge pointer; local qutrit realization retains the same Q.

**Propagation and energy:** exact pair-configuration transport; no relativistic rest-energy map.

**Topology:** Z2 character of the ordered exchange cover.

**Boundary:** sharp charge is not strictly localized with reference exterior; local nonsharp excess is available; memory dephasing destroys protection.

[Owning proof or construction](../../model/defect_protection/README.md).

## selected spin charge

**Source:** actual eight-site selected spin cube, J=1, B=1/4; inherited two-flavor repair.

**Preparation and readout:** complete native local-Z followed by one X pointer prepares coherent Q=+/-1 branches; separate ideal 256-outcome X audit resolves charge weights and dephases within sectors.

**Propagation and energy:** positive finite same-H_B energy; contrast bound through ten repairs for T<=1/16 under the stated source scaling.

**Topology:** global internal U1 symmetry, not a winding number or identified electric charge.

**Boundary:** no nonidentity proper-support Pauli charge; coherent controls can change parity; no permanent localization or particle pole.

[Owning proof or construction](../../model/defect_localization_readout/PROOF.md).

## single occupancy spin memory

**Source:** eight-site one-particle-per-site spin code with complete C/E repair.

**Preparation and readout:** declared spin control and detector services.

**Propagation and energy:** exchange evolution acts nontrivially within the code.

**Topology:** no topological label supplied by identity repair.

**Boundary:** repair protection of every state does not make every state an energy eigenstate or a distinct species.

[Owning proof or construction](../../model/defect_localization_readout/PROOF.md).

## joint positive field canonical sector

**Source:** protected spin interaction H_B=J sum(S_x.S_y-1/4)-B sum epsilon_x S_x^1; supplied p=n^-2 and B=J n^-5; positive-B ground and retained H_B clock.

**Preparation and readout:** finite positive-field ground preparation, local marginal modulus, source spin-rotation observables and complete native local Pauli histories with all pointer and failure records.

**Propagation and energy:** continuous even-envelope translations, positive generator K_B/(Jp), and finite propagation cone; no sharp dispersion or rest mass.

**Topology:** internal source spin rotation, no spatial winding or identified electromagnetic charge.

**Boundary:** B(n) is a supplied preparation prescription; unread repair does not select it; equality to the ordered zero-field history limit, physical Lorentz spin, sharp poles and scattering are unproved.

[Owning proof or construction](../../model/spin_joint_field_limit/PROOF.md).

## common hidden spin factor

**Source:** same six-site Weyl/contact field and complete charged-block repair.

**Preparation and readout:** complete ideal joined histories with arbitrary spin/reference correlations.

**Propagation and energy:** multiplicity evolves and localized carrier leaks; repair need not conserve field energy.

**Topology:** internal spin factor, no topological boundary invariant.

**Boundary:** global factor preservation does not identify localized matter, Lorentz spin or a physical spin apparatus.

[Owning proof or construction](../../model/common_spin_protection/README.md).

## cube eta range

**Source:** original eight-site Weyl/contact field with the stated four-dimer repair.

**Preparation and readout:** source-prepared eta reference and complete local response detector.

**Propagation and energy:** same interacting action intertwines with an energy shift U; one probe has eight inelastic lines plus elastic weight.

**Topology:** eta symmetry representation, no spatial winding invariant.

**Boundary:** eight response lines are not eight species; protection uses the complete outside outcome for this repair domain.

[Owning proof or construction](../../model/cube_eta_protection/README.md).

## compact opposite texture cores

**Source:** prescribed two-core texture in the declared cubic two-spin field.

**Preparation and readout:** declared texture and transported band controls; autonomous source formation unproved.

**Propagation and energy:** uniform finite texture/vacuum-energy bounds and controlled band transport.

**Topology:** genuine shell winding of the prescribed order-parameter field.

**Boundary:** winding does not force zero modes, four generations or physical rest masses; stability needs the stated dynamics.

[Owning proof or construction](../../model/compact_defect_pair/PROOF.md).
