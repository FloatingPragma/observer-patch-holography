# Lepton and hadron mass records on one source

One declared192-mode native action contains the fitted strong sector,
three analyzer charged-lepton masses, three calibrated Dirac neutral
masses and an actual semileptonic current. Exact local current bounds prove
positivity, a unique finite vacuum and the full charged threshold equal
to the electron unit. Gaussian current contractions give coupled baryon
energy-window bounds without enumerating the lepton Fock space.

The source retains all families, conjugates and preparation failures.
Its neutral tensor is calibrated, its baryon energy windows are not
physical poles, and purely leptonic contact terms and photons are absent.

Run python3 -m model.openwave_common_mass_source --write or --check.
