The full-Haar pure-$SU(2)$ source has a volume-independent gap at sufficiently
strong coupling and fixed spatial spacing. On periodic cubes, group three
outgoing links into each local Hilbert space and distribute the electric
Casimir over four-site stars. Each normalized local classical term has a unique
Haar-product vacuum and gap at least one. The exact local perturbation norm is
$64/(g^2)^2$, independently of volume. These premises admit Yarotsky's
infinite-local-space stability theorem. Gauge invariance places the unique
ground in the Gauss sector, where restriction preserves the gap. The theorem's
smallness constants have not been evaluated, so the numerical $g^2=8$ member is
not certified by this argument. No spatial-continuum limit, GeV mass or Clay
solution is asserted.
