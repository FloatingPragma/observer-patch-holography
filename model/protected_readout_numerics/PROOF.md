# Complete protected-readout numerical contract

## Three distinct comparisons

The original mathematical program is the literal six-level port-control word,
proper-code load/export, finite field schedule and complete C/E instruments.
Its exact source certificate bounds the control and angle approximation to an
ideal circuit. The ideal circuit uses exact hopping, contact, FSWAP and pointer
gates at the emitted Strang schedule. Floating evaluation is a third object.
A binary64 residual is not a theorem that a physical output is zero.

`packet.py`, its numerical admission, the complete retained raw JSON and the
original NPZ are unchanged. `contract.json` authenticates those bytes and the
original producer. Two named parent-receipt metadata transitions are enumerated
with both hashes; all other original source pins and the exact source scope
must match. This does not accept executable or physical-program changes.

## Exact protected template

Let T=(eta+)² from the complete 120-dimensional N2 space into the N6 space.
The actual 24 hopping terms occur in 12 consecutive spin pairs on physical
bonds. The two even CAR terms in each pair have disjoint mode support and
commute. Exact Gaussian-integer checks show each bond sum commutes with eta+.
The complete contact D=sum_x n_up(x)n_down(x) satisfies [D,eta+]=eta+.
Consequently every paired hopping factor intertwines T without a shift, and
exp(-it D) T=T exp[-it(D_N2+2)]. Forward and reversed schedules both satisfy
these identities. Routing FSWAPs conjugate the same CAR factors and undo their
routes. The actual tape is checked against the inherited source-word binding.

Write eta+=A+q, where q creates the origin pair. Since q²=0,

```
(eta+)³|0> = 6 R_integer,
(eta+)²(eta+-6q)|0> = 6 F R_integer.
```

Both pointer arms therefore belong to im(eta+). The exact radical repair
columns satisfy eta_minus V=0, hence V† im(eta+)=0. Every terminal native Kraus
map vanishes there and Q=I-VV† is the identity. This proves exact zero terminal
weight for the ideal emitted circuit, independently of its Strang error
relative to exp(-itH).

For the literal finite-control program, let epsilon be the inherited complete
preparation error plus pointer error plus both field-word source errors. The
complete-instrument telescoping bound gives total terminal repair weight at
most epsilon², with epsilon²<7.318e-21. The Strang error is absent from this
particular bound because the ideal Strang circuit itself is protected.
Floating backend error is not included in that mathematical source bound.

## Full interval output

`interval_replay.py` evaluates every paired term on all 120 N2 parent states.
It uses the inherited integer-only, outward 160-bit sine/cosine and square-root
kernel. It includes the full exp(-2it) contact scalar, both pointer arms, the
exact eta² integer lift, both final analyzers and all 131,072 output rows.
The scalar phase and zero padding are checked through the full complex state.
There is no probability renormalization or postselection.

The reference normalization is T/(6 sqrt112) before final pointer analysis.
Each field half-hop angle is 1/2048; each complete contact step has angle 1/64.
There are 64 complete steps. Exact interval arithmetic, rather than another
floating eigensolver, encloses the target. Each raw final-state array must lie
within the operation-derived Euclidean radius below. The retained arrays have
errors below 3.581e-11; this is a property of those bytes, not an accuracy promise
for every backend.

## Arithmetic and admission

Every local source success matrix is compared entrywise with exact interval
gates. Full six-level primitive matrices are independently compared with the
proper-code exponentials proved by the source's exact control identities.
The resulting Frobenius bound also bounds operator norm. No expm accuracy or
unitarity residual is assumed. A backend is admitted only when each primitive,
local field/pointer block and repair embedding error is at most 2^-46, and its
actual copied input is within 2^-40 of the exact reference. These are explicit
numerical-service accuracy requirements, not empirical or hardware claims.

For unit roundoff u=2^-53, gamma_k=ku/(1-ku), ordinary binary64 complex dot
products of length d satisfy a conservative gamma_(8d) absolute-product bound.
For a d-dimensional local block with exact-target error delta, the state-step
error coefficient is at most

```
beta = delta + gamma_(8d) sqrt(d) (1+delta).
```

If E bounds the incoming state error, E' <= (1+beta)E+beta. With S=sum beta<1,
E_final <= (E_initial+S)/(1-S). The fixed budget counts 26,624 two-bit field
blocks, pointer preparation and analysis, and all ten complete outside repairs.
The sparse embedding has 64 nonzeros per column and one per row. Its two
products and subtraction receive their own explicit gamma bounds; the exact
outside projector is a contraction. Fewer than 2^80 real operations occur in
this finite state path. A total 2^-900 absolute allowance covers underflow,
even when individual underflows are bounded by the smallest normal value.
No overflow is possible for the stated finite norm and matrix bounds.

These formulas yield the successful full-register state radius E in
`admitted_budget()`. Keeping every orthogonal failure branch gives the further
complete-history bound sqrt(E²+4*681283*p_export+120*p_terminal), below2e-8.
The factor four is a conservative count of export slots per pulse; exact
padding zeros and the actual narrower slot counts are checked separately. Each terminal
probability is bounded from its compressed amplitude, including sparse-dot,
division and norm-sum arithmetic. Every complete export entry is bounded by
four times the squared primitive cap plus the underflow allowance. A local
prefix has at most 256 pulses; its norm-growth and reduction factors are below
that conservative factor four. Every failure table, its sum, all labels and
its exact padding zeros are retained. Structurally zero native maps and final
export complements must remain exactly zero. Complete masses and pointer
probabilities are checked against their own full arrays and the ideal interval
law, rather than compared as reproducible platform-independent raw decimals.

Only the explicitly listed numerical fields use these enclosures. All other
fields use the original comparator, including exact bounds, tolerances, source

reported complete-output diagnostic is recomputed from its own array using

This contract validates the declared numerical experiment. It supplies no
