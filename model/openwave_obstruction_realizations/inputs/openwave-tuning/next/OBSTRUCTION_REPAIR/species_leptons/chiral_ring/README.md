# Two-cell interacting wall ring

Both spatial momenta and all eight CAR modes enter this compact-gauge
vacuum calculation. The electric energy obeys Gauss law for every
occupation and charge reference. The vacuum favours holonomies near
the critical wall region; all particle and hole currents are retained.
Its response differs from the one-cell result, so spatial refinement
cannot be omitted when assessing mass protection.

See [the proof](PROOF.md), [results](RESULT.json), and the
[independent review](EXACT_REVIEW.json). The finite reference transitions
are not physical lepton poles.
