# Two-cell wall ring with exact electric Gauss law

Use L=2 spatial cells, S=2 regulator slices, one physical spin and all
8 CAR modes. Include both spatial momenta. Keep the fractional-service
clock h=1/8, Wilson r=1, electric coefficient1/4, and the declared fifth
and boundary-mass operators. The two mass cases are zero and the
actual432 electron coefficient. The one-dimensional ring has no spatial
plaquette and no inserted magnetic pinning potential or vacuum subtraction.

Gauge-fix a spanning tree. Its interior link is identity and the closing
link is the compact holonomy. In sector N the static references are
−S at each cell, with an additional charge −(N−LS) at cell zero. Thus
q0=n0+S−N, q1=−q0. The complete Gauss basis has
`E0=ell+q0, E1=ell`, with every occupation and integer ell. Interior
hopping changes E0 through its occupation update; closing hopping also
changes ell. The electric energy is `[(ell+q0)^2+ell^2]/4`.

Execute flux cutoffs K=4,8,16. Retain every number sector. Complete
spectra are required at K4/8; K16 may use full-space sparse eigenpairs
and linear boundary resolvents, with their narrower numerical scope
stated. Retain all vacuum occupation/flux probabilities. Charged probes
change the reference at cell zero and use the fixed tree dressing.
Compare actual holonomy, boundary response and wall leakage to the
one-cell control. No holonomy or mass parameter is selected from its
outcome, and no finite-volume reference transition is called a physical
lepton pole.
