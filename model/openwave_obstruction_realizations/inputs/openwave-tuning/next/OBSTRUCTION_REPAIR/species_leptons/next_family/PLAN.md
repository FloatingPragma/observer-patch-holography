# Independent charged and neutral source frames

Frozen before this package's calculations. Fable's `ROW_10_symmetry`
enumerates residual-subgroup pairs; this package does not repeat that menu.
Its completed ANGLES comparison has no three-angle pass among the listed
complete patterns. No member of that menu is selected here.

The charged coefficient is the actual 432-state register's positive
circulant effect, embedded by the inherited outward third-turn of face
(4,5,11) in the native geometric triplet. Its eigenvalues are ordered by
the existing electron/muon/tau mass assignment. The neutral coefficient
uses the independent positive five-state native-memory Y, already present
in the uncalibrated oscillation source. This is an explicitly new join;
the two source eigenframes are not forced equal.

Three fixed comparisons are made, all retained:

1. Neutral Y with no phase service. This tests whether distinct real source
   dynamics suffice. The charged conjugate-character assignment may impose
   a stronger equal-row obstruction.
2. Neutral V*YV†, using the existing predeclared ascending port word
   (2,3,10), phase -i. No word or phase is chosen by the observed angles.
3. The closed two-generator loop V3 V2 V3† V2†, also with phase -i. This
   is the commutator of the two native port operations, chosen to test
   oriented loop transport rather than an open pulse sequence. The inverse
   loop is a CP/orientation control, not another candidate selected by fit.

The neutral mass eigenvalues remain those of Y in all three. Every result,
including failed angle or hierarchy comparisons, is retained. No search
over words, coefficients, permutations, or comparison tolerances is used
to select a winner. Neutrals use increasing source mass order.

The original charged balance and phase retain their retrospective ancestry.
Architecture need not be unique to be tested. A prediction that fails is
not repaired by relabelling it or by fitting its architecture afterwards.
