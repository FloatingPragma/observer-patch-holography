# Full wall-Fock vacuum in a compact loop control

Use the frozen finite-wall Hamiltonian with one physical spin, one unit
charge, S=2,4, all 2S CAR modes, a compact loop holonomy theta, and
electric energy E^2/4. The spatial matter symbol is
`alpha_z sin(theta)+beta(1−cos(theta))` on every slice. The fifth hopping,
boundary mass and h=1/8 clock are exactly those of `chiral_service`.
The two bare masses are zero and the actual432 electron coefficient.
No scalar shift, critical mass or magnetic pinning potential is inserted.
Flux cutoffs K=4,8,16 retain all records and boundary transport defects.

For every particle-number sector N=0 through 2S compute its lowest
energies. A static reference of charge −N imposes the one-vertex loop
Gauss constraint. Reference energy is zero by declaration. A fermion
insertion also shifts that reference, so its complete probe is neutral.
Compare all sectors before naming a vacuum. Keep complete particle and
hole boundary spectral weights and their CAR sum rule.

Compute the boundary Green matrix at zero frequency, its chirality-flip
part and actual dynamical wall mixing. Report holonomy moments, finite
flux leakage and an analytic discarded-flux Schur bound. The one-loop,
one-spin model has no physical three-dimensional particle interpretation;
a charged reference transition is not an observed lepton pole. Each
bare-mass case has its own source vacuum. A common three-family vacuum
requires a separate tensor-product matter construction.
