## 2. Two exact local restrictions

At a fixed cell and fixed weak/colour charge, the old quark has only two
physical spin coordinates. If its three spatial α matrices are the Pauli
matrices, any Hermitian2×2 β has the form b0I+Σbiσi. Solving
{β,σi}=0 for all three i gives b0=b1=b2=b3=0 exactly. It cannot also obey
β²=I. The script checks this algebra symbolically. This rules out a direct
local3+1 massive Dirac representation on those same two coordinates.
It does not exclude a staggered continuum reconstruction or an enlarged
carrier; each would require its own position/spin/current map.

Similarly, a unit-charge one-particle Dirac carrier QD=I has no nonzero
charge-preserving injection W from a quark spin/flavour carrier with
Qq=diag(2/3,2/3,−1/3,−1/3): QD W=W Qq implies W(I−Qq)=0, and I−Qq is
invertible. An amended fractional-charge field source can avoid this
restriction. Such an amendment changes its Gauss/route-current dictionary
and has not been supplied by the existing unit-charge source.

Neither restriction is a no-go theorem for OPH or for relativistic quarks.

proves isometry, orthogonality, and u+u+†+u−u−†=I4 by exact polynomial
reduction under E²=m²+|p|². No negative band is discarded from completeness.
