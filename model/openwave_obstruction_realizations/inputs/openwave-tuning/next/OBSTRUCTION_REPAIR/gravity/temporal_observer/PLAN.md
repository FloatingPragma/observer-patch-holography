# Complete-band tilted-observer test, before execution

Use the actual continuum quartic source H(k)=alpha k+beta R³k⁴ at zero
transverse momentum, retaining all four spinor components and both energy
bands. No nonlinear redefinition of translation momentum is made.

1. Weighted-energy boost K={x,H}/2 has
   [H,K]=−i[p+4R⁶p⁶p]. Measure the exact IR defect rather than infer full
   Lorentz covariance from the existing low-mode modular comparison.
2. Actual field samples on t=u x have Fourier map kappa=k−u sE(k).
   Enumerate EVERY real source-momentum root, its band s, projector and
   positive Jacobian weight. Retain all polynomial roots, marking complex
   momenta outside the source's real Fourier Hilbert space.
3. Compute the actual CAR Gram matrix and occupied covariance before and
   after Gram normalization. Compare only afterwards to local Dirac
   normalization. Record the missing branch multiplicity explicitly; do not
   discard UV roots or introduce negative Pauli-Villars weights.

At kappa approaching zero, the analytic prediction is two extra real roots
at |k|R=(u^−2−1)^(1/6), Gram4/3 times the Dirac result and normalized
four-component covariance eigenvalues1/2±sqrt(10)/8. These are diagnostic
predictions from the source equations, not acceptance inputs or target data.

Passing the algebra test establishes a preferred-foliation obstruction for
the complete source. It does not rule out an approximate IR gravitational
theory, but its extra preferred-frame terms and observer records must then
be derived. A local lapse equation, common entropy equilibrium and physical
clock/G join remain separate obligations.
