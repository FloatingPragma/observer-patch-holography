# Positive spectral flattening: declared construction and gates

This additive branch follows the failed full boost transfer in `../temporal_observer/`. It uses a continuous metric/source, not removal or counting of cells. A finite Fourier grid is an executable approximation.

At flat reference metric let D = alpha dot p, beta anticommute with D, R > 0, and define

H_flat = (D + beta R^3 D^4) / sqrt(1 + R^6 |D|^6).

All positive and negative bands remain. H_flat^2 = |p|^2 exactly. Its negative projector equals the finite-entropy quartic source's projector, so its equal-time regional entropy is unchanged. A smooth momentum-dependent unitary V rotates the massless Dirac Hamiltonian into H_flat. This is a new source architecture; it is not a derivation from native OPH events.

Acceptance gates declared before executing this packet:

1. Verify Hermiticity, unitarity, band/projector equality and the complete Poincare Lie algebra on its common smooth one-particle core. Retain the momentum-dependent connection in the boost.
2. Check a positive constant-mass extension retaining the high-momentum projector decay. A fixed massless rotation applied to an ordinary mass term is a different extension and must not be silently substituted.
3. Construct the actual tilted-plane CAR Gram matrix from every real physical band and its Jacobian. Check positivity, completeness and the regional state after canonical normalization. Compare that Gram matrix with the ordinary local Dirac normalization.
4. Test propagation of the original finite-entropy CAR regions outside the metric light cone. Report operator-norm leakage as well as averaged leakage, with spatial resolution and distance controls.
5. If tractable, compare the actual planar entropy coefficients measured by distinct tilted observers and cut orientations. Equality of the Poincare algebra alone does not satisfy this gate.

The fully transported Dirac net V chi_B V^dagger has the original Dirac regional spectrum in the transported vacuum; it therefore does not retain the continuum finite entropy proved for the original chi_B algebra. These two regional nets are never identified here. Exact static nesting of chi_B regions also does not prove their cross-frame geometric locality.

Even a successful observer component would leave an entropy-based joint state/geometry selection law, all-observer equilibrium, a native event realization, interacting charge/chiral compatibility, and a physical gravitational coefficient in the same matter clock to be established. No Poisson equation or lapse relation is installed as an update.
