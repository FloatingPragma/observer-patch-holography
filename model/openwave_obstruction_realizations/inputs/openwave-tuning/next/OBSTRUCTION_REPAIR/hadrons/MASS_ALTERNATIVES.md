# Absolute mass alternatives tested

The original normalized source and two concrete alternatives have now been
kept distinct. No source is adjusted to the proton target.

1. The normalized positive composite source has m_e=1, t=1/8, U=1 and J=1/4.
   Its colour-singlet endpoint trial gives E_B<=57/16. A stronger string
   changes neither this zero-span trial nor its 1.821 MeV upper bound.
2. The archived positive baryon-current square U sum B_c² removes that
   zero-span obstruction without changing the vacuum or colourless electron.
   This uses the existing native current, not a baryon spectral projector.
   Tying its coefficient A to the existing colour coefficient U adds no
   separately fitted parameter. The complete three-quark bound is
   U+51/16 <= E_B <= U+57/16. With U=1 this gives 2.140–2.331 MeV. With the
   previously declared strong-source value U=2048 it gives 1.04815–1.04835
   GeV. Both miss. The independent ancestry of U=2048 is not established by
   this audit, and it cannot be used as an unexplained prediction of scale.
3. Keeping the raw electron analyzer coefficient instead of normalizing it
   to one creates a genuinely different interaction-to-mass ratio. The
   actual 432-state analyzer has e_e≈6.03008e-5; the separate 3600-address
   analyzer has e_e≈7.53757e-5. Their traces differ by 5/4, so their identical
   lepton ratios do not permit substitution. The common electron anchor
   would give units about 8.47417 and 6.77936 GeV respectively. However, the
   unchanged colourful-spin term has a universal three-quark floor 3J/4
   with J=1/4. Even before kinetic and colour contributions, the proton-like
   energy exceeds 1.590 GeV or 1.272 GeV respectively. Propagating the
   vectorlike up/down ratios does not remove this exclusion.

The density mechanism is constructive but does not select its strength.
Every positive U preserves the same empty colourful vacuum and Gauss
constraint, so neither condition selects one physical mass. A scalar
reference shift cancels between particle and vacuum; a common clock rescale
cancels in the baryon/electron ratio. A new interacting vacuum could change
that ratio, but it must be constructed rather than inferred from changing
the energy origin.

The raw-mass join does not prove that the positive-Laplacian quark action is
the interacting counterpart of the native Dirac/Higgs source. Its explicit
conditional exclusion is useful: even granting that common-clock
identification, the old J term is too costly. A viable continuation must
replace or derive the spin interaction and its dressed-vacuum effect on the
same source, retaining the electron and full field complements. Supplying a
new small J to hit the target would only calibrate another coefficient.

Exact scripts: `density_mass.py` and `raw_mass_scale.py`. Their result files
retain both successes in removing an old obstruction and failed physical
comparisons. The first enumerates every three-quark occupation pattern on
2–5 cells and checks the full rational two-cell matrix. The second uses the
actual frozen rational analyzer intervals, not the unnormalized display
amplitudes or an extra squaring.
