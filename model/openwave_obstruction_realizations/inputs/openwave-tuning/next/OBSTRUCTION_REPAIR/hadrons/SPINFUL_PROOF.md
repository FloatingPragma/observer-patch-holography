# Physical-spin currents on the two-cell source

This is a new Hamiltonian on two spatial cells, two physical-spin states
and three colours. Its 4096-dimensional CAR carrier has the same dimension
as the four-site spinless construction, but no equivalence is claimed.
The hopping now connects equal spins across the two cells; the electric
term is the Casimir of the whole left cell; the mass signs are ++-- in
the orbital order (left up,left down,right up,right down).

The complete SU(3) singlet sectors have dimensions 1,20,50,20,1 at
N=0,3,6,9,12. Physical spin is an actual current,

    S+ = sum_cell,color c_(cell,up,color)†c_(cell,down,color),
    2Sz = N_up-N_down.

The exact CAR matrices obey [2Sz,S+]=2S+ and [S+,S-]=2Sz. Each newly
constructed Hamiltonian component commutes with those currents. Exact
joint kernels of S+ and 2Sz-2S within the complete colour-singlet carrier
give all highest-weight multiplicities:

| N | Physical spin S and highest-weight multiplicity |
|---|---|
| 0,12 | 0:1 |
| 3,9 | 1/2:2; 3/2:4 |
| 6 | 0:10; 1:6; 2:3; 3:1 |

Multiplication by 2S+1 exhausts every original colour-singlet dimension.
Every source and spin sector is retained. Rational generalized inertia
certifies each lowest band, with the small computational mass-rounding
error added outward. The actual global vacuum is N=6, S=0 at all three
inherited kappa values. No spin label is assigned from a matching number.

The B=-1 and B=+1 intervals coincide. The lowest baryon multiplet is spin
3/2, so treating it as a proton would fail the actual spin measurement.
The spin-half branches also miss the physical mass scale. No coefficient
was tuned after these comparisons.

Every integer highest-weight three-quark column is also turned into its
literal CAR creation polynomial. Dividing by its coefficient l1 norm makes
it a contraction on the entire Fock space. All six polynomial labels,
every target spectral outcome and the complementary failure are retained.
Their actual source-ground records have nonzero numerical residues in the
appropriate spin sector. An external baryon reservoir loses one occupancy
on creation success; source and reservoir baryon number are balanced.
This is a complete operator instrument, not an executed native M6
preparation/decay. The residues remain diagnostics, separate from exact
energy intervals.

The failure is informative. With one flavour, the local colour epsilon
requires a symmetric three-spin factor, hence spin 3/2. A spin-half state
requires a different orbital or flavour symmetry. The next natural source
change is to include an actual weak-flavour doublet and its currents. It
must carry its own vacuum, complete sectors and mass calculation; simply
relabelling these states or fitting a negative spin penalty is not a repair.
