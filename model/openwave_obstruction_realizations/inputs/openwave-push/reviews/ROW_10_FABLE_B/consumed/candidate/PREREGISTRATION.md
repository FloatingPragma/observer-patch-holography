# Row 10, construction `row10-carrier-residual-symmetry-fable-v1`: rules fixed before the runs

Fixed before any pattern, angle or distance of this directory was computed. The stamp at the foot is the output
of `date` at the moment the file was closed; `s1_enumerate.py` prints the SHA256 of this file in its log.
Anything decided later is marked "fixed after the run" where it is used.

## 1. Objects

- **Group.** I: the rotation group of the regular icosahedron, 60 matrices of size 3 × 3 with entries in Q(√5),
  generated by the cyclic coordinate permutation, diag(−1, −1, 1) and
  (1/2)[[1, −φ, 1/φ], [φ, 1/φ, −1], [1/φ, 1, φ]], φ = (1 + √5)/2. Verified in the run: closure at 60 elements,
  g gᵀ = 1 and det g = 1 exactly, element orders 1, 2, 3, 5 in numbers 1, 15, 20, 24, the 12 vertices
  (0, ±1, ±φ) and cyclic images permuted.
- **Representations.** 3: the matrices themselves. 3′: the same group elements with √5 → −√5 in every entry.
  No other representation has three states (the remaining irreducible dimensions are 1, 4, 5).
- **Subgroups.** All subgroups are computed by closure from the 60 elements (expected census 1, 15, 10, 5, 6,
  10, 6, 5, 1 for orders 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 60; a different census stops the run).
- **Basis fixed by a subgroup.** An abelian subgroup A whose three characters on the representation are
  pairwise distinct fixes three rank-one projectors P_ψ = (1/|A|) Σ_a conj(ψ(a)) ρ(a). These are the cyclic
  groups of order 3 (10) and 5 (6) and the Klein four-groups (5). A cyclic group of order 2 has characters
  (+, −, −): it fixes one line and leaves a plane undetermined. A non-abelian subgroup has an irreducible
  constituent of dimension 2 or 3: an operator that commutes with it has equal levels there, and no basis
  is fixed.
- **Pattern.** For an ordered pair (G_e, G_ν): M_ij = Tr(P_i Q_j), P of G_e, Q of G_ν: the matrix of squared
  moduli of the overlaps of the two eigenbases. Computed exactly in Q(ζ₁₅) by two routes (matrix products of
  the projectors; the character sum (1/|A||B|) Σ conj(ψ(a)) conj(ψ′(b)) χ(ab)) and compared exactly, and by a
  third route in floating point (numerical eigenvectors of random invariant Hermitian operators).
- **Identification.** Two patterns are the same if and only if one is obtained from the other by a
  permutation of rows and a permutation of columns (36 relabellings). Transposition and the conjugation
  √5 → −√5 are not identifications; they are reported as relations between patterns.
- **Multiplicity.** The number of ordered pairs of subgroups that give the pattern; besides it the number of
  orbits of such pairs under simultaneous conjugation by the 60 rotations and the order of the subgroup the
  pair generates.

## 2. Menus (the enumeration rule)

| Menu | G_e | G_ν | Ordered pairs | Role |

| H | the 10 cyclic groups of order 3 | the 5 Klein groups, the 6 cyclic groups of order 5, the 10 cyclic groups of order 3 | 210 | the hypothesis of the task: the charged basis is that of a face third-turn. The look-elsewhere number of the construction is counted on H |
| X | H and the 6 cyclic groups of order 5 | as H | 336 | completeness |
| A | X and the 5 Klein groups | as H | 441 | closure: every basis-fixing subgroup on both sides. Not asked by the task; reported |
| C2 lane | the 10 cyclic groups of order 3 (and order 5 beside) | the 15 cyclic groups of order 2 | 150 (240) | one fixed column; not parameter-free. Reported, not part of the menu count |

Each menu is evaluated on 3 and on 3′. G_e = G_ν is a member (the pattern of a permutation matrix).

**Labelled candidates.** A pattern with an assignment of its rows to (e, μ, τ) and its columns to (1, 2, 3):
36 per pattern. Mass order is not predicted by the construction (masses are inputs), so every column
assignment is admitted. The menu size is counted three ways: distinct patterns; distinct labelled moduli
matrices; distinct triples (sin²θ13, sin²θ12, sin²θ23).

## 3. Angles

Standard parametrization: sin²θ13 = M_e3; sin²θ12 = M_e2/(1 − M_e3); sin²θ23 = M_μ3/(1 − M_e3); for M_e3 = 1
the two others are undefined and the candidate is counted as outside every tolerance.
J² = M_e1 M_e2 M_μ1 M_μ2 − R², R = (M_e3 M_μ3 − M_e1 M_μ1 − M_e2 M_μ2)/2, computed exactly. The sign of J and
cos δ are computed from the complex overlap matrix of the labelled eigenvectors in floating point; cos δ from
M_μ1 = s12² c23² + c12² s23² s13² + 2 s12 c12 s23 c23 s13 cos δ where all five factors are non-zero. The
phase is reported and is not part of any gate. Majorana phases are not addressed.

## 5. Distances, tolerances, ranking

For a labelled candidate with x = (sin²θ12, sin²θ13, sin²θ23):
- pull_k = (x_k − best_k)/σ_k with the upper error above the best fit and the lower error below;
  χ² = Σ pull_k².
- **T3** ("within 3 standard deviations on all three angles"): |pull_k| ≤ 3 for the three k. **T3r**: every
  x_k inside the published 3σ range (differs from T3 for θ23, whose profile has two octants).
- **T10, T30** ("within 10 %, 30 % on each angle"): |x_k/best_k − 1| ≤ 0.10, 0.30 for the three sin². **T10a,
  T30a**: the same for the three angles in degrees.
- **Ranking.** Primary: χ². Secondary: D = max_k |θ_k − θ_k,best| in degrees. The "best assignment" of a
  pattern is the one of smallest χ²; the one of smallest D is given beside it when different. The "nearest
  pattern" is named in both rankings.
- **Null for the look-elsewhere number.** 2 × 10⁶ unitary matrices drawn from the Haar measure (numpy
  generator seed 20261114, QR with phase correction), rows (e, μ, τ), columns (1, 2, 3): the share p_χ with
  χ² at or below the nearest candidate's and the share p_D with D at or below the nearest candidate's;
  reported with N × p for N = the number of distinct triples of menu H.

## 6. Selection search (what counts)

Searched in the theory repository checkout fd461f1d, directories `model/` and `paper/` (and `claims/`,
`flagship/` for the same terms): "neutral register", "neutrino", "Majorana", "Klein", "frame",
`native_flavor_orbit`, `native_flavour_transport`, `neutrino_mass_response`, and beside them "half-turn",
"two-fold", "fifth-turn", "third-turn", "PMNS", "tri-bimaximal", "golden".
- **S1**: a statement that names a subgroup of the carrier's rotation group, or a set of lines of the carrier,
  as the symmetry or the basis of the neutral states. Then its pattern is the construction's prediction,
  whatever the distance.
- **S2**: a neutral construction whose family frame is that of an enumerated subgroup without naming it. Same
  consequence.
- **S3**: neither. Then the nearest pattern is a selection, of log₂(N) bits for N distinct triples of menu H
  (and of menus X, A beside).

met", with the nearest candidate's pulls, its tolerances, and the remaining obligation.

## 8. Limits declared in advance

- The construction is kinematic: a representation of the carrier's symmetry group and two residual subgroups.
  It contains no dynamics that produces the residual subgroups. Masses are inputs in every run.
- The identification of the register's three states with the eigen-lines of the face third-turn on 3 (corner
  ↦ position vector, equivariant for the face stabilizer) is a declared identification.
- The oscillation run uses the standard vacuum evolution with the declared masses; it is an executed
  consequence of the pattern, not a record on a common source of the group.
