# Coulomb energy from the native charged sector

The quadratic-field construction gives an exact charge-separation energy
on its complete pinned native charge sector. The unknown local charged
mass cancels, so calculating that mass is unnecessary for this observable.
All link states and flux-loop degeneracy are retained. The computed Green
potential gives an attractive inverse-square static force at resolved
distances. Pinning and the field-energy coefficient are explicit inputs;
free particle motion and the physical fine-structure constant require
separate derivations.

Run `python3 -m model.openwave_pinned_coulomb --write` or `--check`.
The [proof](PROOF.md) gives the complete instrument and minimum identity.
