# Which remote ranges are determined by the local work outcomes?

The bridge and triangle counts and the failure of linear area transfer are known. This secondary analysis uses their simultaneous probability intervals without another selection of runs. A radar side is a mean of bounded return times, including timeouts. Its mean on a probabilistic mixture of source stocks is exactly the corresponding mixture of its means. Heron's area generally lacks this property.

For each of the eight survey arms use every directed range and its three reciprocal averages. Use the ordinary pair, the paired-record intervention and both independently formed/product pairs, taking every selection of three of the four. For each triplet take the determinant of the two bridge-outcome probability contrasts and the mean range contrast. A nonzero interval certifies that some two physical source mixtures have identical reflection-symmetric contact outcome distributions and different remote ranges. There is then no function of those local outcomes alone that recovers the reported range on these mixtures. Repeat the inference with source changes that keep both single-head marginals fixed, reporting whether this stronger statement is resolved.

For three differences write s and a for their symmetric and antisymmetric changes and t for the clock-range changes. Use unscaled endpoint differences for the mixture construction. Write c=s cross a. If det(s,a,t) is nonzero, mix the positive and negative endpoints of each contrast with weights |c_i|/sum |c_i|, reversing that pair if c_i is negative. The exact (population) mixtures have the two identical bridge weights, and their range difference is det(s,a,t)/sum |c_i|. Interval bounds on this quantity and the preparation weights quantify the witness. No fitted calibration or approximation to a small derivative enters this identity. The exact balancing prescription uses the population bridge probabilities. Counts bracket these weights; independent preparation of that exact balance would need the model values or additional feedback. A third work outcome is the complement of the other two. Operators sensing the direction of motion, internal memories or other contacts are outside this claim. The inference concerns a charge-selected material range.
