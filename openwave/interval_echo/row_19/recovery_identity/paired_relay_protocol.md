# Two receiving records and a clocked survey

Single-head transport and the single-head charge-even release results are known. Test whether a second receiving record makes the originating modular signal independently readable, and whether a clocked survey predicts that signal under the ordinary calibration. The dimension argument below motivates the design. No four-head transfers or survey results for this design have been inspected.

Use the 80-cell preparation, its original two sender edges, formation port and arrival-order graph. Among outward three-port rails inside a cell one owner hop from either sending owner, select the first lexicographic path at minimum starting radius, then the first such path at a different receiving owner. Rank by (radius, first, second, third port). Write an even head in coin zero on the first edge in each receiving path. The board is prepared with both receivers present simultaneously. An excess or a vacancy travels from the same formation port for eight exchange times. Discard the courier and both sending heads, with no terminal selection. Keep the full joint state of the two receiving heads, including their coherences. The sign of the courier belongs to the public preparation record.

Use the 18 orthonormal two-head Pauli correlation perturbations within the two parity sectors. Each preserves the individual sending heads, parity weights and full ordinary parked-energy distribution. Compute the linear map from these perturbations to the 15 nonconstant joint Pauli readings after each courier. Report all singular values, including the unresolved directions. Also examine the maps keeping individual receiver marginals only and the two courier signs together. A single chosen courier has at least three exactly invisible source directions by dimension. For a physical example use a unit vector in the numerical null space for the excess courier, selected by projecting the established fixed-reference modular score onto it. Treat singular values below 1e-12 absolute or 1e-10 of the largest as numerically unresolved. Distinguish the exact dimension statement from a tolerance in computed examples. Use the established physical preparations for ordinary and held comparisons and freely formed paired/product stocks. Test the numerical null direction against the second courier sign. Counting and law-level inversion have different precision requirements.

Use a separate uniform product source P with even and odd weights 3/4 and 1/4. Construct R=P+hD on the selected fixed-marginal unit direction D. Choose a fixed safe h=1/64. Read the source contrast of log(P)−log(R) on P and R using parity-resolved joint Pauli settings. Their difference is the sum of two relative entropies. Check positivity and existence of native product-atom preparations for both. Make a positive calibration by independently reading the source. On fresh propagated copies measure all 15 joint Pauli expectations of each typed courier, with no kept-out paths or outcomes. Include the unchanged product control. Bound the trace distance by the measured Pauli differences, including counting uncertainty, to cover any experiment on one receiving pair with fresh independent apparatus. Use 2^66 synthetic trials per setting with seed 191203 for receiver/source counts.

For the geometric test use repeated exchange-clock appointments on two fresh charge probes separately. Survey all ordered pairs of the three near-side ports on the first receiving path, with both written receiving heads present and the sending contacts returned to blank. At each half exchange time monitor for first arrival at the destination. Once there, monitor for return to launch. Use the complete round trip time histogram (timeout at appointment 12); divide its capped mean elapsed time by two for a radar length. Average forward and reverse lengths, then compute the Euclidean comparison area from the three sides when the triangle inequalities hold. This is a radar observable in a common calibrated material clock. Derive its slope for the ordinary and held comparison from independent endpoint counts. The modular forecast comes from the source alone. Include all conditional choices, failures and a blank receiver-state control; targets and sign weights may not be adjusted. Use seed 191204 and 2^74 synthetic trials per setting. Compute simultaneous concentration bounds on each empirical distribution and propagate to the area ratio. The survey can report unresolved ordinary calibration. A successful scalar comparison needs both charge probes consistent with the independent source interval. A proper-area identification needs further evidence beyond this finite survey.
