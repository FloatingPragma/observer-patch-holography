## The operator and its sign

Let $R=\bigoplus_b R_b$ be the faithful two-head reference. The reversal parity $b$ labels two four-dimensional quantum blocks, each with weight $w_b=\mathrm{Tr}R_b$. Write $P_b=R_{b,L}\otimes R_{b,R}/w_b$ for the product of its two unnormalized marginals. The connected modular score used in the original source selection is
$$
L=K_R-K_P=\log P-\log R.
$$

Natural logarithms give nats. The two logarithms have a commutator of operator norm 0.07801, so a single logarithm of a matrix quotient would lose the stated operator convention. The mean of $L$ on $R$ is $-D(R\Vert P)=-0.110893$; on $P$ it is $D(P\Vert R)=+0.120212$. These are relative entropies with their respective signs. The eigenvalues of $L$ span −0.3780 to +0.6429 in the even sector and −0.4049 to +0.7265 in the odd sector. All eight eigenvalues are separated from zero by at least 0.3701 at the working precision. The literal $L$ cannot vanish on a nonempty bulk subspace of this head space. A shifted operator would require a physically fixed bulk and its normalization.
