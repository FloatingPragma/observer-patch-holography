## The proposed physical explanation

OPH proposes that observers on overlapping patches can agree on all their local frequencies while finding additional correlations when they compare records. For an observed pair \(i,j\) and a previously calibrated reference \(q\), the observer assigns the modular cost \(-\log q_{ij}\). The change in its expectation is measured in nats. The hypothesis is that a regional part of this change contributes stress, so ordinary matter and clocks respond to it at ordinary gravitational coupling. This would account for an apparent extra attraction without introducing a new particle species. Converting nats to gravitational mass requires a physical identification of the source.
