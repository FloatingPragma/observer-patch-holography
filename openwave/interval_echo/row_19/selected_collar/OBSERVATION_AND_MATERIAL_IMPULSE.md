## Allowing an ordinary distribution and a second force

We calibrated the ordinary write separately for six service addresses, each at both launch signs. Let \(c_{jh}\) be the ordinary increment for instrument \(j\) on history \(h\), and \(s_j\) the sparse increment. An ordinary write assigned freely across those histories has \(s_j=\sum_h w_h c_{jh}\) with \(w_h\ge0\). We allowed unbounded coefficients, even though a physical lottery has a finite budget. The twelve equally weighted controls agree with separate ordinary measurements on all six material instruments and all thirty clock routes at every window. These histories change the physical interrogation of the same marked memory. They do not sample every possible spatial distribution of cosmological matter.
