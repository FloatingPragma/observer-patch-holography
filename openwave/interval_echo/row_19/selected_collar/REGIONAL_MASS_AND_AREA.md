# What can observers prove about ordinary mass, contraction and extra gravity?

**A finite survey observes contraction under an ordinary energy write. A material transport experiment also shows why observer agreement alone cannot force that contraction.** The two results concern different operational geometries. The first gives an independently tested ordinary energy-to-area prediction. The second follows an identifiable object into a region and challenges the proposed implication directly.
