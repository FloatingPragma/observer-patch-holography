# Can the two observers calibrate the connected modular charge themselves?

Use the prepared 80-cell equilibrium and the two source-selected preparations with fixed one-head marginals and parked parity energy. Their ideal charge difference and the earlier simulated read with supplied operator weights are known. Measure the equilibrium independently, withhold its matrix, temperature, Pauli weights and preparation coordinates from the decoder, and obtain the full connected modular operator from the parity-tagged head coincidences. Test the difference of this operator's means on the two source preparations. Use its full operator, including any local components, so an unknown small marginal change is covered by the inference.

Use nine combinations of X, Y and Z on the two heads. Each setting records both signs and the parity, including all outcomes. For each source cohort and its independent reference cohort repeat the comparison at 2^24 and 2^38 simulated draws per setting. Include a null in which each parity block of the reference is replaced by the conditional product of its own head marginals, keeping the source endpoints fixed. The inferred connected charge for that reference should include zero. Count all settings in one 99% simultaneous confidence statement. Report the precision cost and both attainable and unresolved signs. These are synthetic banks from the candidate's calibrated measurement menu. A physical laboratory must identify an implementation of its preparations and readers.
