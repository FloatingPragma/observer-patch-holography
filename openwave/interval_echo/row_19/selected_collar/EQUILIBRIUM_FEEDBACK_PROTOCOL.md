# Can reacting to an actual midpoint report maintain the ordinary entropy equilibrium?

A lottery over flights uses no information about the memory in the incoming copy. Give the controller a physical record instead. Launch from one of the six enrolled senders with either sign, wait one through twelve half-ticks, and interrogate whether the probe occupies the sender. On a click and on a miss allow independently selected further flight durations from zero through twelve half-ticks. Stopping immediately is allowed. Keep both branch probabilities and both head outcomes. Enroll the 24,336 deterministic rules and every randomized mixture of them. As a further test allow the choice of rule to depend on the incoming, exactly conserved parity seen by a nondemolition parity reader. The four-anchor ordinary reference and its error budget stay fixed.

Measure subnormalized end-head records jointly with the midpoint report. A rule's unconditional output is the sum of its click branch at its chosen duration and its miss branch at the other chosen duration. A stochastic duration conditional on a branch is a convex mixture of deterministic rules. Use this identity to cover the family without assuming that the click and miss subensembles are individually normalized preparations. Test for a separating measured observable, or for an interior ball that survives the full simultaneous statistical error, the native propagation allowance and the displacement to the exact entropy equilibrium. A parity-dependent choice requires the drift to vanish in each conserved block. A central-value solution alone is inconclusive.

A measured ordinary reference that could be sustained this way would supply a concrete starting point for an omission experiment. Holding the initial reference fixed for one round is distinct from attracting nearby inputs back to it. This test purchases no new mass interaction, inserts no replacement heads, and gives the controller no area, impulse or galaxy targets. The ordinary reference was known when the experiment was designed.
