# Which correlation fails to reach a regional observer?

Use the existing six-source acquisitions, station enrollment and fixed choices of ordinary, rotation and joint interventions. Add one fresh independent control. For each parity, independently prepare a left and right head from the equilibrium conditional marginals and use the equilibrium parity frequency. Pass that control through the same native pulse instrument, on every route. Compare the input reference with the product it would have under this procedure. Use a fresh seed and the same number of trials per setting. Keep the raw counts and the timeout at its actual frequency.

Test the differential of relative entropy lost under the regional observation channel. Its two references are the correlated incoming equilibrium and this incoming product. Both references undergo the identical channel. The regional observer holds the public route and either the two returned heads with their parity or a timeout. Include the scalar timeout contribution. Use the incoming log product minus log equilibrium convention and the same sign at the output. Predict the signed deficit (incoming score less transmitted score) on the held source by calibrating on the ordinary intervention and right-head rotation. Measure the absolute loss of distinguishability as well. Repeat with the simpler classical record of just route and arrival time, including every deterministic grouping of the successful ticks and any publicly labeled mixture. Test compatibility for every quartet and whether one positive area coefficient can serve them all. Report uncertainty in both references and interventions. A positive value of the loss itself follows from data processing. Only its measured response to source intervention can test stationarity.

If a linear combination of the two-head source and the transmission correction is proposed, the coefficients have to work across independently surveyed regions. Test this with a bounded nonzero minor of the measured stationarity residuals. A singular or unresolved minor is inconclusive. Record separately any exclusion requiring the calibrated gain to have its ordinary positive sign.
