# Ordinary calibration of the two marked-force readings

The material probes give oppositely signed raw four-tick marked-force contrasts on the paired source. Each probe can also measure its own response to the ordinary parked-energy write. Ask whether a single positive ordinary-energy equivalent reproduces both readings. Divide the held force contrast by its own independently measured ordinary contrast and multiply by the calibrated ordinary energy increment. The denominator is required to exclude zero. The affine measurement law makes this test exact for a lottery of ordinary writes. A different spatial source distribution or a distinct force law can give a different comparison.

Repeat both marked-force measurements with a separate random seed at 2^76 simulated starts per instrument setting. Count plus, minus and missed terminal appointments for both probe loads. Propagate counting intervals and the calibration of the parked energy. Compare the inferred energies with the finite prediction from joint head counts. Keep both outcomes if either force sign remains unresolved. This tests mass equivalence on this four-tick force, which is one term of the material momentum balance.
