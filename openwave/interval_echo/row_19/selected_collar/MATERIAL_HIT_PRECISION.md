# Resolving a material reading with terminal port records

The two-sign material reader at its declared exposure did not resolve the predicted contrast on any of the eighteen outputs. In a separate acquisition retain which selected port or port pair contains the probe after four ticks. The original method assigns a random sign on an empty measurement. Instead record zero on a miss and the original signed Pauli reading on a hit. For a term measuring the X or Y interference of ports i and j, the square of the signed observable is the projector onto those two ports. For a diagonal term it is the projector onto that port alone. Weight the term selection by the absolute native Pauli coefficient and retain the same mean and scale for each charge and both parities, including the idle probability from a smaller parity-specific scale. Measure the probabilities of plus, minus and zero using independent simulated launches at the same exposure as the two-sign experiment. Retain all channels. Use empirical upper bounds on the hit probability to bound each signed mean with Bernstein's inequality across all arms. Recheck at higher exposure if a declared contrast lies below the observed noise. The original two-sign result remains in the record.
