## Evidence needed for filing

The adopted clock's sharp routing, fair draws and coherent double-rail hand give the same record laws at every half-integer strength. Positive strengths 1/2 and 8 also have identical vacuum fringes, yet their original home probabilities at exposure 37 are 0.41682 and 0.98027. A forecast using only these records has worst expected absolute error above 0.2817. At strength zero any initial density matrix on the thirty enrolled home seams has home probability at most 0.898509 at exposure one. Taken together, these tests exclude a coupling selection based on that clock and a rescue of the 94% pair forecast through coherent launch shaping. The same pin supports a different finite persistence experiment: an observer can prepare a single-load interference mode that stays on four ports for every real strength. It uses a different state and reader. The two-load classification also finds exactly confined modes on certain larger cells at −1/2, with shared-seam probability 1/2. Disclose the tried strengths and laws, and show the losing forecasts. Running the archived κ = 8 operator on another launch cannot resolve this choice.
