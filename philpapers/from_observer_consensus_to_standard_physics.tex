\begin{abstract}
Can operational consistency among finite observers reconstruct the
mathematical structures that quantum theory, spacetime geometry, and gauge
physics ordinarily postulate separately? Observer Patch Holography (OPH)
studies this question on a finite architecture: observer patches with local
state, boundaries, durable records, authorized overlaps, and repair maps,
governed by three axioms fixing the architecture, the naturality of shared
meaning, and conditional maximum randomness of everything agreement leaves
free. We present the strongest results of the program in a single chain, with
every result labeled by its evidence class: proved finite theorem,
machine-certified finite witness, conditional reconstruction theorem, or open
physical producer. Terminating confluent repair yields protected public
records; the completed central record surface carries Born probabilities,
L\"uders conditioning, and the Tsirelson bound as finite theorems. The
oriented spherical support carries
$\mathrm{Conf}^{+}(S^{2})\cong\mathrm{SO}^{+}(3,1)$ with a three-dimensional
space of observer frames. A typed composition theorem assembles modular flow,
null translations, stress tomography, and entropy stationarity into the
Einstein relation, with every physical antecedent displayed. Deterministic
simulations measure a held-out Lorentzian event form of inertia $(1,3)$ across
a support-adjusted scaling path with a same-size control that degrades to
$(2,2)$. Twelve-port icosahedral incidence with target-blind readback yields
the coefficient algebra
$\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)$, the global form
$S(U(3)\times U(2))\cong(\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times
\mathrm{U}(1))/\mathbb Z_{6}$, and a fifteen-state one-generation
representation witness with exact anomaly arithmetic. Interval certificates,
a positive-chamber Koide theorem whose conditional $72$-eV tau enclosure
carries a registered falsification protocol, and finite de Sitter capacity
identities complete the quantitative surface. A
formal library exceeding one thousand machine-checked declarations,
executable receipts, and adversarial negative controls accompany every
claim. We state
precisely which attachments remain open, why the separation of evidence
classes is itself a methodological contribution, and how each conditional
component can fail.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and claim boundary}
\label{sec:intro}

Physical theory ordinarily postulates its deepest structures separately.
Quantum theory posits a Hilbert space and the Born rule; relativity posits a
Lorentzian manifold; the Standard Model posits a gauge group and matter
representations. Reconstruction programs ask whether some of these
postulates are theorems of something smaller. This paper presents one such
program, Observer Patch Holography (OPH). Its primitive is a finite society
of observers that keep records, compare them where they overlap, and repair
disagreement.

The central question is operational:

\begin{quote}
\emph{Can operational consistency among finite observers reconstruct the
mathematical structures ordinarily postulated separately in quantum theory,
spacetime geometry, and gauge physics?}
\end{quote}

Our answer is a structured partial yes, and the structure is the point. Some
reconstructions are finite theorems with machine-checked proofs. Some are
exact computations with executable certificates. Some are conditional
compositions whose physical antecedents are stated as named premises. Some
require physical producers that are open obligations. This paper never mixes
those classes. Every result below carries one of four labels:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}cl@{}}
\toprule
\evT & proved finite theorem (formal or exact-arithmetic proof)\\
\evW & machine-certified finite witness (executable certificate, controls)\\
\evC & conditional reconstruction theorem (exact, with named premises)\\
\evO & open physical producer (required attachment, no construction on record)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The discipline matters because reconstruction programs fail most often by
silently promoting an interpretation into a theorem. We regard the
maintained separation, enforced by fail-closed machine-readable receipts,
as a contribution of the program independent of any single result.

\begin{boundary}
This paper claims no completed derivation of particle masses, no source-only
determination of the fine-structure constant, no completed cosmology, and no
claim that formal verification establishes physical truth. Quantitative
surfaces that consume measured values are labeled diagnostics and are never
counted as predictions. Section~\ref{sec:empirical} lists the decisive open
gates.
\end{boundary}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.6em]

\section{The primitive finite architecture}
\label{sec:architecture}

\begin{definition}[Observer patch]
An observer patch is a finite object with local state, an observable boundary
map, durable records, readback, and a set of authorized repair moves. A
patch sees a fragment of the world: its own state and the boundary data of
its authorized overlaps.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Overlap and repair]
For patches $x_i$, $x_j$ with an authorized overlap $e=(i,j)$, both induced
boundary records must agree for the pair to hold public data. A repair move
changes local state while preserving protected readout. A configuration is a
normal form when no authorized repair applies.
\end{definition}

The carrier realization used throughout is the twelve-port echosahedral
architecture of A1: each carrier's boundary packet is combinatorially the
oriented icosahedron boundary $(P,E,F,o)$ with $|P|=12$, $|E|=30$,
$|F|=20$; carriers federate through typed seam algebras with coherent
triple-overlap cocycles into a nerve carrying a degree-one bridge to the
oriented spherical support. This is the declared source
architecture. We do not claim a uniqueness theorem selecting it among all
possible carriers; where results depend on it, the dependence is displayed.

Two exact features of this architecture do heavy work later. First, the
proper rotation group of the icosahedral packet is the alternating group
$A_{5}$, with sixty rotations acting on the twelve ports; the real
port-coefficient space decomposes as
\begin{equation}
P_{12}\;\cong_{A_5}\;\mathbf 1\oplus\mathbf 3\oplus\mathbf 3'\oplus\mathbf 5,
\label{eq:bands}
\end{equation}
an exact multiplicity-free decomposition with certified projectors
(\evT). Second, record keeping is integer valued: a write appends $+1$, a
retraction appends $-1$, and readback sums atomic events per port; a
conservative repair transfers one unit across a seam whenever the oriented
mismatch has magnitude at least two. With $V(N)=\sum_i N_i^{2}$, each such
repair strictly decreases $V$ by $2(d-1)$ for mismatch $d\ge 2$, so repair
terminates (\evT). Divisibility of total load by twelve is a necessary
consensus condition, settled constructively on the declared packet by an
explicit witness schedule (\evW).

\section{The fixed-point consensus theorem}
\label{sec:consensus}

The world, in this program, is a fixed point:
\begin{equation}
T(\mathfrak U)=\mathfrak U,
\end{equation}
where $T$ is the composite repair-and-agreement dynamics. The mathematical
content is that the fixed point is well defined and schedule independent on
the declared carrier class.

\begin{theorem}[Consensus normal form; \evT/\evW]
\label{thm:confluence}
On a terminating, frustration-free carrier with a confluent accepted repair
relation, every repair schedule reaches the same quotient normal form. On
the reference twelve-port carrier, exact code exhausts all six declared
overlap schedules and returns the identical normal form; termination follows
from the exact quadratic descent of Section~\ref{sec:architecture}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{boundary}
Confluence is part of the carrier contract, not a free consequence of
locality: arbitrary local repair does not imply it, and countermodels with
non-confluent repair are retained in the formal library. The theorem is
finite; no continuum limit is asserted.
\end{boundary}

Three structural properties accompany the theorem on the declared class:
gauge compatibility (repair commutes with local relabeling), idempotence of
the completed dynamics, and refinement compatibility where proved, each with
its formal statement and, where the hypothesis fails, its countermodel. The
value of the fixed point is not metaphysical: it is the object on which
records become public, and every later structure is read off it.

The architecture sits close to distributed agreement in the tradition of
Lamport, Shostak, and Pease \cite{lamport1982}: patches play the role of
protocol nodes, overlap repair of a quorum vote, and the completed fixed
point of the decided state. A component paper of the stack proves safety and
liveness for a Byzantine-tolerant reading of repair under explicit quorum
and partial-synchrony assumptions; the impossibility boundary of Fischer,
Lynch, and Paterson \cite{flp1985} is the reason those assumptions are named
rather than left implicit.

\section{Observable normal forms and finite quantum probability}
\label{sec:quantum}

Once a compare, write, and verify slice has completed, its accessible events
generate a finite central record algebra. The following identities are
finite theorems on that surface, developed in an audited event-algebra
library of more than one hundred declarations (\evT).

\begin{theorem}[Finite quantum record arithmetic; \evT]
\label{thm:born}
Let $P_E$ be the projector of a completed public event $E$ and $\rho$ the
normalized state of the completed record surface. Then the probability and
update rules
\begin{equation}
\Pr(E)=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho P_E),
\qquad
\rho\,|\,E=\frac{P_E\,\rho\,P_E}{\operatorname{Tr}(\rho P_E)}
\end{equation}
hold, and for the finite bipartite correlation surface the
Clauser--Horne--Shimony--Holt parameter obeys
\begin{equation}
|S_{\mathrm{CHSH}}|\le 2\sqrt 2 .
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

The first identity is the Born rule on this finite surface; the second is
L\"uders conditioning; the third is the Tsirelson bound \cite{tsirelson1980}
for the Clauser--Horne--Shimony--Holt combination \cite{chsh1969} at fixed
cutoff.
Compatibility structure for commuting events follows the standard finite
operational pattern within the same library.

\begin{boundary}
This is finite operational quantum structure derived from public
observability. It is not a continuum interacting quantum field theory, and
no claim of that kind is made anywhere in this paper. Its role in the chain
is specific: the probability and update rules that later physical branches
require are supplied by the consensus architecture rather than postulated.
\end{boundary}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item a sorry-free Lean library exceeding one thousand theorem and lemma
declarations, whose count deliberately includes premise boundaries and
countermodels, because exposing assumptions is the purpose of the
formalization;
\item exact-arithmetic code receipts: integer and rational computations,
interval certificates with outward rounding, and $\mathbb F_{2}$ rank
computations, each emitting a canonical hashed payload;
\item simulation receipts pinning source commit, configuration, seeds,
grids, tolerances, and output hashes, with deterministic reserialization
distinguished from fresh source replay;
\item fail-closed verifiers that recompute verdicts from clause vectors and
refuse receipts whose stored verdict disagrees, so a truth flag can never be
asserted by a caller;
\item adversarial negative controls: every certificate ships with mutations
that must be detected, and a control that cannot fail is treated as a defect
of the certificate rather than as support;
\item no-target-ancestry rules: machine-readable ledgers classify every
quantitative row as source result, conditional composition, diagnostic, or
rejected candidate, and forbid promotion by prose, issue state, or numerical
proximity.
\end{itemize}

The repository, receipts, schemas, and rebuild instructions are public
\cite{ophrepo,ophsim}. The verification stack does not prove OPH physically
true; it proves that the stated theorems hold and that the stated boundaries
are the ones actually enforced.

\section{Empirical meaning, falsifiability, and open obligations}
\label{sec:empirical}

The program maintains a preregistration architecture: custody-bound target
definitions, kill bands, precision floors, and decision rules fixed before
comparison, with falsifications retained at the same prominence as
confirmations \cite{ophregister}. Standing registered items include the
conditional tau window above and a ringdown comb stance re-tested against
each sufficiently loud gravitational-wave ringdown. Candidate hypotheses
that failed their predeclared comparisons are retained in a negative ledger
and contribute nothing to the case made here.

Rather than a list of numerical coincidences, the program's empirical future
rests on three decisive gates:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \textbf{Physical realization of the common refinement tower.} The
Einstein composition of Theorem~\ref{thm:einstein} needs one source-derived
tower satisfying its premises jointly; the signature instrument and its
controls measure progress toward exactly this object.
\item \textbf{A source-only current and continuum producer.} The gauge and
matter results of Section~\ref{sec:gauge} await laboratory current
attachment and a continuum operator limit; identifiability audits
delimit which attachments the registered source interface cannot supply, so
this gate requires source-visible observables beyond that interface rather
than more certificates.
\item \textbf{One preregistered quantitative forecast not used in
architecture selection.} A machine-readable prediction contract with sealed
comparison custody exists; the first candidate that becomes source visible
under it will be scored exactly once, and a complete negative inventory is
itself an acceptable published outcome.
\end{enumerate}

Each gate can fail, and the second one can fail provably. The program
treats a rigorous non-identifiability theorem as a result of equal standing
with a positive construction, because it tells the theory where additional
observable structure must come from.

\section{Discussion and comparison}
\label{sec:discussion}

The components of OPH have recognizable neighbors. Operational
reconstructions of quantum theory derive Hilbert-space structure from
information-theoretic postulates; OPH's Section~\ref{sec:quantum} differs by
deriving the finite record calculus from a consensus fixed point rather
than from axioms about state spaces. Algebraic and modular approaches to
quantum gravity use Tomita--Takesaki flow as intrinsic time; OPH's Einstein
branch consumes modular structure the same way but insists on a finite,
producer-typed route to the premises. Holographic and tensor-network
programs relate bulk geometry to boundary entanglement; OPH's screen is a
boundary object throughout, and its capacity identities are finite-algebra
statements rather than large-$N$ limits. Thermodynamic derivations of the
Einstein equation from entropy stationarity are the closest relatives of
Theorem~\ref{thm:einstein}; the program's contribution there is the typed
decomposition into separately certifiable premises with executable
instruments. Distributed-computation views of physical consensus,
quantum error correction, and relational interpretations each anticipate one
ingredient; none, to our knowledge, combines finite observer consistency,
confluent repair with formal certificates, measured signature instruments
with mechanism controls, and an exact finite route to the Standard Model
coefficient algebra and global form.

The novelty claim is therefore compositional. No single ingredient is
unprecedented; the maintained chain from three observer axioms to the
labeled structures above, under a verification and falsification discipline
that a hostile auditor can execute, is the contribution offered for
evaluation.

Two methodological positions are worth stating for a philosophy-of-physics
readership. First, the program treats \emph{evidence-class separation} as
part of the theory's content: a reconstruction whose conditional joints are
hidden is a different, weaker object than the same mathematics with its
joints displayed, and machine-enforced receipts make the display
non-optional. Second, the program treats \emph{observer consistency as
prior to state}: A3 selects states from agreement constraints, reversing
the usual order in which states are fundamental and intersubjective
agreement is emergent. Whether that reversal ultimately earns the physics
is exactly what the open gates of Section~\ref{sec:empirical} will decide.

\subsection*{What this paper does not claim}

For maximal clarity: no completed prediction of the fine-structure
constant; no derivation of the particle mass spectrum; no completed
cosmology or dark-sector claim; no claim that the simulator outputs our
universe; no claim that formal verification establishes physical truth; no
probabilistic certainty language. Downstream research programs exploring
such directions exist within the project and are deliberately excluded from
the case made here.
