# Appendix D: Run It Yourself

The prologue complained that the universe ships with no source code and no documentation. The executable construction in the public repository supplies both. Its law couples matter, electric and magnetic fields and local registers that respond to their total energy. Every site has its own volume, capacity and memory. Reducing active capacity preserves the inactive hardware memory, and disconnected parts evolve independently. Every interaction is specified before evolution begins. The simulated observers do not have to invent or install that law while it is running. Its native repair law is a calibrated intermediate test, and a big search for the correct native repair law, the simple law that governs the interactions of observers in the early Universe, is ongoing.

Start from the [public repository](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography) and follow its model guide. It gives installation instructions, the exact verification command and the complete instrument for each finite experiment. The replay checks every retained outcome, including zero-probability and failure outcomes. A detector's work is counted separately from the energy-conserving evolution that precedes its readout.

There are two different kinds of execution to distinguish. Exact algebra checks the complete matter and field operator, retaining every fermionic occupation in its definition. Applying that operator to selected native basis states keeps every resulting output. A complete coherent measurement detects an initial phase response of that source. Small, fully enumerated experiments propagate their declared finite states and show magnetic-loop motion and a distant clock's response when neutral matter changes at its source. Their small carriers are specified separately; they do not stand in for an arbitrary state of the full interacting model.

The book also uses independent finite examples: the observer's repair records, the interference left by a measurement and the correlations between event clocks. Their calculations can be replayed from their own construction guides. They establish what follows from their stated rules and preparations. Passing those checks does not transfer their clocks, spectra or geometric interpretations to a different Hamiltonian.

The physical question is whether the common construction produces the records of our world. That requires its actual interacting states, measured particle assignments, a common geometry and physical units. The mathematical gravity theorem gives an implication with precise regional and refinement conditions. A finite register that responds to energy is evidence about a mechanism; it does not by itself satisfy all those conditions.
