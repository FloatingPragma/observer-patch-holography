# Appendix D: Run It Yourself

The prologue complained that the universe ships with no source code and no documentation. The public repository supplies both for the executable model: the OPH law, its exact specification and a simulator that runs it. The law moves each constituent by reflected routing: at every address it reflects the equal-slot part of eight amplitudes and sends each slot one step along its axis. Two constituents that meet in matched slots step together as a pair, and the relative phase of one constituent's two rails is the pair's clock. A head-on encounter forms such a pair through a reversible capture that sends a marker away on the junction's own wire, and the newborn pair opens its hop over 128 updates, then hops fully. The law is specified before evolution begins, and the simulated observers do not have to invent or install it while it is running. Under it the field of a single constituent obeys the Klein–Gordon equation in three space dimensions, moving clocks keep proper time, two constituents form a pair that holds together, and that matter obeys the relativistic wave relation.

Start from the [public repository](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography) with Python 3.11 or newer. Two commands do the work. `python3 -m model` checks each exact method of the simulator against the literal update, and `python3 -m simulator run all` recomputes every experiment on the law and writes an offline replay page that shows them. The [law page](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/tree/main/model) lists what is proven about the law and what its simulated observers measure.

There are two kinds of check to distinguish. An exact check compares a closed formula with the literal update: the field of one constituent, the invariance of a bound pair and the wave relation of formed matter. A measured result comes from records the simulated observers hold, read by their own detectors and clocks. Every forecast is sealed before the records are read, and a reading outside its registered band counts against the law.

The book also uses independent finite examples: the observer's repair records, the interference left by a measurement and the correlations between event clocks. Their calculations can be replayed from their own construction guides. They establish what follows from their stated rules and preparations. Passing those checks does not transfer their clocks, spectra or geometric interpretations to a different Hamiltonian.

The physical question is whether the common construction produces the records of our world. That requires its actual interacting states, measured particle assignments, a common geometry and physical units. The mathematical gravity theorem gives an implication with precise regional and refinement conditions. A finite register that responds to energy is evidence about a mechanism; it does not by itself satisfy all those conditions.
