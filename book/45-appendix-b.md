# Appendix B: Glossary

Michael Faraday distrusted the word pole. It had been attached to the two ends of a battery for decades and it smuggled in a theory, that something sits at each end attracting and repelling across a distance, which was the account of electrochemistry he believed was wrong. What he wanted was a pair of words naming where the current went in and where it came out, committed to nothing else.

So in the spring of 1834 he wrote to William Whewell at Trinity College, Cambridge, who had the Greek. Candidates went back and forth between them: eisode, exode, zetode, dexiode, skaiode, orthode, anthode. On 5 May, Whewell came back with anode and cathode, on the ground that short words get adopted and elaborate ones do not, and threw in ion, anion and cation while he was about it. Faraday replied ten days later to say he had taken all of them, and that his colleagues' objections subsided the moment he mentioned whose idea they were.

Anode and cathode name a direction and a doorway. They say nothing whatever about what is traveling, which is why they outlived the theory they were coined for. The entries below try for the same restraint.

**Action.** A single number scored over a whole history, one contribution per step from start to finish. The history the world runs is the one where nudging an interior reading leaves that total alone.

**Algebra of observables.** Every question an observer can put, together with the three things you may do to questions: ask one after another, scale one, add two.

**Alice and Bob.** Two observers with separate views and no access to each other's insides, on hand whenever a claim has to survive being checked by somebody else. Charlie joins when two parties are not enough to make the trouble visible.

**Alignment score.** How much of what an observer holds points the way its field does. Whatever fails to point that way is the repair load, so the two are one reading taken from opposite ends.

**Antimatter.** For every list of matter states there is a mirror list with every charge reversed. The two are one object, and a world built on either gets the same physics with the labels swapped.

**Area law.** An entropy or capacity contribution proportional to the area of a region's boundary in a stated regime. A black-hole horizon is the best-known example: twice the radius buys four times the area. Thermal entropy can scale with volume, so the quantity and state being counted matter.

**Bit.** The answer to one well-chosen yes-or-no question, and the unit anything measured in questions is counted in. Erasing one releases heat, and how much depends on nothing about the device, only on the temperature around it.

**Carrier.** The finite wiring of the model: twelve ports at the corners of a twenty-sided solid, thirty seams joining them, twenty triangular faces. The structural numbers of the physics are read off that arrangement.

**Causal diamond.** Everything after one event and before another: two cones joined at the rim. Count the completed records inside one and the count approaches its volume, and the fourth root of a ratio of two such counts is a ratio of durations.

**Causal set.** A partial order in which any two related events have only finitely many others between them. The 1987 proposal is that spacetime, looked at finely, is one of these, with the order supplying the light cones and the number of elements supplying volume. The observers' records form one.

**Center.** What commutes with everything else in sight. In an algebra of questions it is where records live, which is why the public world looks classical; in a group of moves it is the moves nobody inside can detect.

**Charge.** A number a closed loop of seams hands back to whoever walks it. Renaming conventions cannot reach it, because a round trip through your own conventions returns you to them, and repair cannot flush it out, because repair reaches one seam at a time.

**Checkpoint.** The four things that have to travel for an interrupted observer to be the same observer when it resumes: its records, its accessible state, its interfaces, and the law saying which moves it may make.

**Chirality.** Handedness. A pattern with a heading and a spin either turns the way a right-handed screw advances or turns the other way; a mirror swaps the two cases, and the world treats them differently.

**Collar.** The thin band of patches wrapped around a region's edge, with the region inside it and everything else beyond. When a collar screens, the inside can be rebuilt from the collar alone, and whatever the outside learns about the inside crosses that band.

**Commensurability.** Whether two observers' readings answer each other at all. The rule saying which of your readings answers which of mine sits on the seam between us.

**Commit.** An accepted update, with its reads, its writes and the record it leaves. Midpoint averaging is many-to-one on the two readings it updates, and the record keeps what the readings forget. A count of commits orders events, and seconds come from a clock laid against the count.

**Commutator.** What you get by doing two operations in both orders and subtracting. Zero means the two questions can be answered together, and anything else is the amount by which they cannot.

**Consistency.** A description is consistent when it does not require two incompatible things at once. An inconsistent one buys nothing, because a ledger saying the account holds both nothing and a hundred pounds justifies any withdrawal you like.

**Decoherence.** The usual word for what happens to alternatives once a record has been written and the comparisons between them stop being available. The mechanism is the record.

**Defect.** A small core the transport between carriers cannot be smoothed across. The records around it have settled, and a loop drawn around it carries a twist that no local repair can undo. Repair can move a defect, two defects can meet and cancel, and nothing else removes one. Stable defects are the particles of matter, and chapter six's leftover of one is the simplest.

**Density matrix.** The description left behind when somebody is cut out of the picture: odds for every question you can put, and nothing left of what only the far side could have detected. Two different accounts of the whole can leave the same one behind, and no operation on your side tells them apart.

**Dependency cone.** The events or patches that can affect a readout. Dependence crosses at most one seam per repair event, so a round in which every seam is repaired once widens the cone by at most one seam, and a sweep along a path carries a chain several seams, one event per seam.

**Derived.** Obtained by arithmetic from the structure, with nothing measured fed in to produce it. A number fitted to a measurement and then read back out has been derived from the measurement rather than from the structure.

**Direction.** The one-way character of time: the loss inside a single repair, where seven arrangements of a seam collapse into one and quantum coherence drains away for good. It is separate from causal order, which says what depends on what, and from flow, which runs both ways.

**Direction split.** The fork at every repair: spend your own capacity on a private update, or spend the capacity shared out on the overlap. Some outcomes are reachable only the second way.

**Duality.** Wave and particle are one thing at two depths of record. Between commits there is a spreading pattern with a phase whose questions refuse to commute; at a commit there is a record, and records hold ordinary numbers.

**Effective description.** An account that works over a stated range, takes its constants from measurement, and can point to where it will break. Every working piece of engineering physics is one.

**Eigenvalue.** A grid of weights usually swings a direction round to point somewhere else. Where it merely stretches or shrinks one, that direction is the grid's own, and the factor is its eigenvalue.

**Energy.** The quantity that generates a region's clock evolution. When the region is thermal, its own modular flow is that evolution rescaled by the inverse temperature, so the generator is minus the logarithm of the state times the temperature, up to an additive constant. A particle's rest energy is the excess maintenance of its core, read on the same clock.

**Entanglement.** Content living in the relation between two halves of a thing rather than in either half. Cut a definite whole in two, look at one side alone, and what comes back is vague by exactly the amount the cut severed.

**Entropy.** How many yes-or-no questions it would take to fix which arrangement you are holding, given what you know about it. It is a reading of the description rather than of the object, and the word disorder has no place in it.

**Equivalence principle.** What a thing costs to hold together sets both how hard it is to push and how hard gravity pulls on it, so the two readings never come apart. The MICROSCOPE satellite spent five months of free fall trying to catch a titanium cylinder and a platinum one falling at different rates, and reached one part in a thousand million million without catching them.

**Excess maintenance.** The work repair spends holding a defect's core inside settled surroundings, over and above what the same region costs without it. Read on the region's clock it is the particle's rest energy, and divided by the square of the speed of light it is the mass.

**Exclusion principle.** Two identical matter particles cannot occupy the same state. Exchanging two identical particles is the same loop as turning one of them once, the turn returns a sign, and the sign makes the doubled amplitude vanish. Matter stacks into shells and solids because of it, while a laser piles any number of photons into one mode.

**Exported harm.** The mismatch an actor leaves for other observers to carry, having declined to repair it. A cost written onto a seam the actor does not share never appears on the actor's own readings. It appears on the readings of whoever holds the other side.

**Fiber.** All the consistent states of the world carrying one given public reading. An empty fiber makes the reading impossible, a fiber holding one state makes the reading enough to fix the world, and a fiber holding many means something besides the reading has to choose.

**Field.** The word names two objects. One is a value assigned at every place, like the temperature through a room or the wind over an ocean; the other is a net of algebras, which is the sense the Standard Model is written in. Both are descriptions observers fit to their records once the records agree. The records come first, and the field is the compressed account that predicts them.

**Fine-structure constant.** The dimensionless strength of electromagnetic interaction, near 1/137 at low energy. The same-cell loop, with the weak and strong forces taking their shared width of the cell, gives inverse coupling 137.035660, two and a half parts per million from the laboratory value. Wissner-Gross's entangled-pair expression gives 137.035999189566 and returns the same grain to seven decimal places.

**Fixed point.** An object x left unchanged by a specified map F: F(x)=x. The object may be an instantaneous state, a reduced record, a probability law or a complete history. A fixed complete history can contain changing local states; a stationary law can support changing realizations. Fixedness alone establishes neither attraction nor uniqueness.

**Flow.** A parameterized family of transformations. Modular flow belongs to an algebra and a state, leaves the state's odds where they were, and has a bare number for its parameter. An observer's clock steps it once at every event at the observer's ports.

**Force.** A steepness in the potential. A single unit of source dropped on the twelve ports lowers the reading eleven sixtieths over the first hop, three sixtieths over the second and one sixtieth over the third, and a pattern held in a slope like that comes back each cycle a little further down it.

**Formal system.** Marks you may write, a starting arrangement of them, and moves that take one arrangement to the next. Nothing in it means anything, which is why the rules run identically in the hands of somebody who knows nothing about the subject.

**Free parameter.** A number a theory does not fix, which somebody measures and types in. The minimal Standard Model carries nineteen, closer to twenty-six once neutrinos have masses, plus Newton's constant and the cosmological constant.

**Frustration.** A loop of constraints that no assignment satisfies, however long you wait. Three coins in three rooms, each pair recording that it differs, is the smallest one, and a triangular magnet is the same arrangement in a material.

**Gauge.** A symmetry whose convention is picked afresh at every place rather than once for the world. Twelve observers each choosing for themselves make twelve of them, and what settles between them is the relation rather than the values.

**Generation.** One complete list of matter states, fifteen of them, ten from picking two slots out of five and five from picking four. The world runs three copies and no more. Fewer than three leaves matter and antimatter with no physical difference between them, more than five and three eighths costs the strong force its weakening at short range, and the cheapest band of the carrier able to carry a face's threefold turn has exactly three directions, one per family.

**Geodesic.** Where you end up by going straight ahead and never turning. On a floor it draws a straight line; on a globe, the flight path from London to Tokyo, up over the Arctic.

**Graph.** Dots and the lines joining them, a dot per observer and a line wherever two of them have compared records. A walk that returns to its starting dot without retracing is a cycle, and the cycles are where the trouble lives.

**Group.** A collection of changes that is closed, so that two of them give a third from the same collection, and that holds a do-nothing move and an undo for every move. The eight ways of picking a square up and putting it down are one.

**Higgs field.** The settled reference read as a field: the background every particle's core is maintained against. It points in the two weak slots and its magnitude is the stiffness of the reference, about 246 billion electron-volts. The three weak carriers have to drag it and acquire mass; the photon leaves it alone and stays massless. A ripple in its magnitude is the Higgs boson.

**Hilbert space.** The arrows an algebra of questions acts on, given their lengths and angles by the state. Physics courses start there and the order runs the other way, because what an observer holds is a list of questions.

**Holonomy.** The difference a round trip makes: set off round a loop, come back, and see what changed. Round a triangle of coin records it is one bit saying whether the coins can be set at all; on twelve observers it is a charge.

**Homeostasis.** Keeping a bounded set of internal readings where they were against a world that keeps pushing them off, at the observer's own expense. Walter Cannon gave the steady states of the body's fluids that name in 1926.

**Incident clock.** A counter that advances once whenever a repair meets a region, idle visits included. Normalized by how often its region is touched, every region's count converges to one parameter, and the change from one tick to the next is positive, so the clock can be read at any fraction of a tick. A clock that counts only changes has a mode that flips sign at every tick and cannot.

**Inertia.** What it costs to change a pattern's drift, proportional to what the pattern costs to hold together and to nothing else about it. Redirecting it means renegotiating every seam on its boundary instead of reconfirming them.

**Inside-only clause.** A relabeling counts only if the system can carry it out on itself. It reads like bookkeeping and it kills grand unification, because a symmetry acting from within forces a center, and simple groups have none.

**Interval.** The quantity two observers who disagree about distances and durations both compute the same value for. Its sign sorts every pair of events into three classes: reachable by something slower than light, reachable only by light, reachable by nothing.

**Invariant.** Whatever a given change of description leaves the same. Naming it comes first and the symmetry second: a rotation preserves length, and without length there is nothing for the rotation to be a symmetry of.

**Lag record.** A comparison of a field with itself some number of repairs later. Lag records give space its third direction: weighting the longer lags more heavily carries the screen's field inward, the deeper the coordinate the longer the lags, as the exponential of minus the depth times the square root of the screen operator.

**Lagrangian.** A function of configurations and their rates, or a field density, whose action gives equations of motion. It compresses dynamics, and it comes last: every symbol on the Standard Model's line has an account in the structure before the line is written. Different lines can compress the same histories, so the line is a summary and the histories are the evidence.

**Leaky abstraction.** An account that lets you get on with the work while the machinery stays out of sight, good until the machinery does something the account has no words for. Joel Spolsky wrote the rule down in 2002, and the everyday container picture of space and time leaks in at least six places.

**Mass.** A particle's rest energy divided by the square of the speed of light: the excess maintenance its core costs per tick, read on the region's clock. In the relativistic description it is the invariant in \(m^2c^4=E^2-c^2p^2\). A core's mass falls exponentially with the number of ticks it persists between repairs, which is why masses span many orders of magnitude while charges come in whole sixths.

**Memory law.** The rule for what a continuing observer keeps. Each repair's record enters a small working buffer, older records leave it for an exported archive, and the observer chooses where to look next from what the buffer holds. Archive and buffer together reconstruct the whole history.

**Mismatch.** The disagreement on a seam: what is left when one reading, passed through the seam's dictionary, is held against the other. It is zero when the two answer each other and positive when they do not. Every seam can read zero while a loop carries a residue, which is chapter six's leftover of one.

**Modular clock.** The flow a region's own state determines on the region's questions, with a bare number for its parameter. It differs for different states, leaves records where they are, and coincides with the region's clock evolution exactly when the state is thermal, the inverse temperature being the exchange rate.

**Momentum.** What a pattern carries as it drifts across the seam network, conserved because the laws do not care where you are. Newton listed it second among his definitions and called it the quantity of motion.

**Native observer.** The smallest observer the model runs: one twelve-port carrier, the repair, the record each repair writes and the memory law, with a controller that picks the next seam from the records it holds. Wiring that choice to the records moves the average final load at one port from one sixth to 35/264, which is what it means for a reading to make a difference.

**Natural selection.** Variation, heredity and differential persistence running together. The list closes at three, and nothing on it decides anything. At the bottom it runs on patterns under one static rule: what persists under repair is physics, from conserved totals to stable defects. Self-repairing patterns built from those persist and copy, and the same arithmetic runs on them.

**Net of algebras.** An assignment to every region of the questions answerable inside it, where a region contained in another carries the shorter list and two regions too far apart to signal carry lists that commute. This is what quantum field theory means by a field.

**No-cloning.** An unknown quantum state cannot be copied. Anything written down as a record can be copied freely and as often as you like, which is the half of the theorem most treatments leave out.

**Noether's procedure.** The machine that turns a continuous symmetry of the scored line into a conserved quantity, one for one. Shifting in space gives momentum, shifting in time gives energy.

**Normal form.** A state with no move left to make. When repair terminates and is confluent, every order of repairs from a given start reaches the same one. A patch with nothing to do knows that about itself and nothing about the rest.

**Observer.** A bounded system that reads its boundary, keeps records, acts on what it reads and carries all of it forward: an algebra of questions bounded by its interface, a record algebra at its center, an interface that behaves the same way every time, and a state fixing the odds. A thermostat qualifies. Being an observer takes no mind.

**Orbit.** Every place the moves in a group can send one thing. The four corners of a square make one orbit under its eight moves, the four edge midpoints make another, and nothing carries a corner into the second set.

**Order.** Precedence between events: one comes before another when a chain of certified reads runs from the first to the second. Events with no chain between them have no order, and numbering them anyway creates none.

**Ordering fraction.** Of all the pairs of events inside a causal diamond, the share in which one could have influenced the other. It is a half with one direction of space, eight thirty-fifths with two and a tenth with three, which makes it a dimension meter that needs no ruler.

**Overlap rule.** Two accounts have to match where both parties look, and nowhere else. Alice's brown table and Bob's waxy one never have to be reconciled.

**Packet.** What appears at a port: one reading drawn from a finite list, available to whatever sits on the far side. It is what a patch's state looks like from outside at one interface rather than the state itself.

**Particle.** A stable topological defect of the transport between carriers: a small core whose surrounding records have settled, with a loop around it carrying a twist no local repair can undo. Its charges are the twist and its mass is what the core costs to maintain. A particle of matter also holds a two-setting pointer that a full turn negates, which is its spin. It is carried from seam to seam with its charge intact and read at a commit as one count at one place.

**Patch.** A bounded local system with its own state, its ports and a printed menu of moves: read, compare, choose, write, expose, verify. A patch carries no coordinates; positions come out of what patches record about each other.

**Pinching.** Sorting every question into the alternatives a record distinguishes, keeping the parts that fall inside one of them and deleting every comparison that only meant something across two. This is what writing a record does to an observer's algebra, and decoherence is the usual name for the result.

**Port.** An interface through which part of a patch's state is visible to a neighbor. A carrier has twelve, five seams meet at each, and chapter fourteen shows that no other count closes.

**Probability.** Numbers between zero and one on the questions an observer can ask, adding up across every complete list of exclusive alternatives. On quantum questions, blunt ones included, every such assignment is the trace against one density matrix.

**Projector.** A sharp yes-or-no question: an operation that, applied twice, does what it did once. Blunt questions answer yes with a weight, and the sharp ones are the special case whose weights are zero and one.

**Quotient.** Sweeping up everything that counts as the same into one bundle and working with the bundles. The twelve hours on a clock face are the whole numbers with twelve counting as nothing.

**Rank.** The number of independent directions in a table of comparisons. The carrier's slow band has rank three, which is the count of directions in space, and the plane perpendicular to a light ray has rank two, which is why light has two polarizations.

**Record.** A write into the center of an observer's algebra, where everything commutes. It reads the same the second time, copies to a neighbor without loss, and stays put under the flow until another write moves it.

**Record law.** What a repair writes down when it acts: how much load crossed the seam, and possibly the coin, the parity of the seam total or the full count at both ends. It fixes which quantum coherences survive the repair. Experiments that prepare definite loads and read only how many units crossed give the same statistics under every record law. Interference experiments tell the laws apart. One, between an empty seam and a waiting unit, separates the coin record from the parity record; a second, between an empty seam and a full one, separates the parity record from the full count; and nine such measurements fix all nine numbers of the family.

**Record map.** The map from the full state of the world to the part that survives comparison: every seam entry and nothing private to either end. Its fibers are what the records leave unresolved.

**Reference.** The settled state that repair maintains everything against: the least informative state compatible with what observers can check. A particle is a core that differs from the reference, its mass is what holding that difference costs, and read as a field the reference is the Higgs field.

**Refinement.** Describing something at ever finer resolution with no completed infinite object at the end, the way 3, 3.1, 3.14 describes pi and nobody ever holds pi. Smooth space is a limit of that kind. Carriers joined into ever finer spheres have a smooth limit of their own, and it lives on a two-dimensional screen. The smooth version is a limit nobody occupies.

**Relative entropy.** How many extra questions per state your odds cost you against a reference. Pass both through the same process, classical or quantum, and it can only fall, which makes it the quantity the second law is about.

**Repair.** The one move a seam makes: both readings to their midpoint, with the total left where it was; on whole-number loads the two ends go to the floor and ceiling of their mean, and a fair coin places the odd unit. Its quantum form also writes a record, and the record law says what. Repair only runs downhill.

**Repair load.** How much of an observer's state fails to lie along the field its neighbors have written on its boundary. It cannot go below zero and it reaches zero exactly when the two agree, which is the reading a tuner is listening for when the beat disappears.

**Repair tick.** One repair event. Ticks put the history in order and carry no duration: stretch every tick by the same factor and no record law changes. Durations come from clocks that count ticks, and the conversion to seconds is one number fixed in a metrology laboratory.

**Running coupling.** A force strength is one number only if nothing surrounds the charge. Screening puts more of the charge in view the closer an experiment gets, so every strength quoted carries a distance with it.

**Schedule independence.** Every order of repairs that keeps going until nothing is left to do lands, from the same start and the same boundary data, on the same settled state. The routes there differ, and so do their lengths.

**Screen.** Every reading on every port an observer has facing outward. Nobody looks at it, it points nowhere, and its size is counted in readings rather than measured in meters. Carriers joined into a sphere make a screen whose smooth limit is two-dimensional, the surface a hologram is written on.

**Seam.** Two ports routed to each other, which makes it the one place a disagreement can show up at all. Twelve observers wired into a twenty-sided solid have thirty.

**Selector.** What settles an ambiguous fiber, where every record in existence is already satisfied and repair has nothing left to bite on. Which repair law gets run by the observers capable of running one is a selector.

**Spin.** A two-outcome measurement with a direction attached. Aim the magnet however you like and the atom answers with one of exactly two deflections, and the aim is the only thing you choose. Every quantum of matter holds one such direction, a pointer with two settings, and a full turn negates it. Gauss's law cancels the signs on its slots, its transport and the reference, so the world comes back with one minus sign for each quark and lepton, which is spin one half. A twist with no quantum in it, a ripple and the reference come back unchanged, with whole-number spin. Since exchanging two identical particles is the same loop as turning one of them once, identical fermions anticommute and exclude each other.

**Static rule.** The repair, its record law and its memory law, which do not change anywhere in the history. What changes is the history. Every law of physics is a pattern the static rule cannot erase: conserved totals, the slow band, loop residues and settled records.

**Superposition.** A system described in terms of questions it was not prepared for, carrying one number for each answer. The numbers are amplitudes, two of them can arrive with opposite signs, and a dark band is where that happens.

**Symmetry.** A change of description that leaves a named invariant where it was. Finding one deletes a part of your description as unphysical, so it subtracts from the world rather than adding to it.

**Temperature.** The exchange rate between energy and questions, in joules per bit. Three hundred kelvin sets that rate at 2.87 joules divided by ten to the twenty-first, for each yes-or-no question a record settles.

**Tsirelson ceiling.** The quantum ceiling on the four-question score, 2 times the square root of 2, a winning fraction of about 85.36 percent. Answer sheets, and every written record with its answers fixed on each side, top out at 2, or 75 percent. A world that only forbade signalling would allow scores above the ceiling; non-commuting questions and a state put it at 2√2 exactly.

**Verifier.** Anything that runs the check on the exposed readings and settles whether a patch's claim about its own work holds. The inside of the patch never enters it, because the check is arithmetic, and arithmetic is indifferent to whose readings it gets.

**Wave.** A disturbance that keeps its phase as it travels. Repair by itself only smooths; a wave needs two quantities feeding each other, and the clock that steps it is a memory advanced once at every touch. Its first response arrives at one speed for every observer and every pair of regions, which is the speed of light.

**Zero-error capacity.** The rate at which messages can be sent with no chance of confusion. On the ring of five symbols the largest one-use code has two messages; used many times over, the ring carries the square root of five messages per use, or log base two of that in bits.

**Zombie.** A philosophical hypothetical: a physical duplicate without experience. The identity account of experience that chapter forty takes leaves no room for one.
