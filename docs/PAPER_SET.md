# The OPH paper set

The papers form one tower over the executable model. Each layer states a
hypothesis, proves finite theorems under displayed premises, and hands a
conclusion upward only after the next layer proves that premise on the same
model process. The common target is the
[executable-model hypothesis](OPH_EXECUTABLE_HYPOTHESIS.md): one simple local
repair and memory law on the carrier architecture whose continuing observers
have the observed 3+1-dimensional quantum-field description and a common
classical-gravity limit, with stable matter as localized topological defects
of native overlap transport. The [completion program](COMPLETION_PROGRAM.md)
orders the open joins and their stopping rules; the [basis](BASIS.md) states
the closure hypothesis behind it, and the [model directory](../model/README.md)
holds the executable construction. Each paper gives its own construction, proofs and physical identification
conditions. The shared dependency map belongs here; detailed proofs belong
in the specialist paper that owns the result.

## Where each layer stands

- **Established on stated domains:** the finite repair, normal-form and
  holonomy-frustration theorems; the forced classical seam rule and the

  near 63 source-energy units; populated regional charge and native spin
  increments with retained pair energy; a selected energy-moment bound
  `M2 <= 4000000/a`; and nonzero finite energy-current response with the
  applied-work term in its Ward identity. The transition's original sector
  weight vanishes under refinement. Physical charged thresholds or particle
  branches, relativistic dispersion and GeV masses remain open. The
  [common dependency map](../model/particle_joint_reconstruction/DEPENDENCIES.md)
  binds these results to the same source, preparation, reference and clock.
- **Conditional on declared inputs:** the smooth 1+3 scalar description
  (declared radial extension), the candidate modular clocks (declared
  reference and controls), the one-generation matter representation and
  Higgs channels (declared spin lift and exterior module), the cosmological
  branches.
- **Open joins:** a physical clock and energy normalization; a native defect
  sector with a protecting invariant, its maintenance energy and transport;
  an interacting chiral 3+1 field description from native records; a
  responsive geometry realizing the Einstein premises; independent
  prospective tests.
- **Negative results on the tested law:** the native double-load defect is
  transient; the static radial product fails the null-balance comparison;
  passive repair has no wave sector; the tested uncalibrated family and mass
  laws miss the charged hierarchy; all twelve comparison values of the
  target-informed mass branch lie outside their printed intervals; no nontrivial source-only charge commutes
  with every complete C/E branch; real native operations cannot implement the
  nontrivial modular action of the constructed reference on its full support
  algebra; no measured particle spectrum is derived.

## How the papers share results

One paper owns each result and gives its full statement and proof. Another
paper states what it consumes, in words and formulas sufficient for its own
argument, and cites the owner. Every paper reads on its own, so a citation
never replaces a definition its argument needs. Values quoted in two papers
agree exactly. The flagship is the exception by design: it states the strongest
theorems of the whole tower with short proofs and cites their owners for the
rest.

## How the papers change

- Model results enter the [model directory](../model/README.md) first, with
  their own claim rows owned by the protocol records there. The construction
  paper states them in its next revision.
- Library papers receive errata, correctness fixes and links to the premise
  map. New results go to the model or to a companion paper.
- The flagship carries the strongest derivations within its 60-page ceiling:
  a stronger result replaces a weaker one.
- A new paper release follows the owner's approval of a recorded model
  milestone. Deposited releases retain their original bytes.
