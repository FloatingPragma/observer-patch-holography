# The OPH paper set

The main candidate law of the executable model is [Interval Echo](../model/candidate_laws/interval_echo/README.md).
It runs the contrast-memory law of the reference candidate
[A-contrast-memory-spherical-v1](../model/candidate_laws/contrast_memory_spherical_v1/README.md), which reproduces
particle stability on its observers' agreed records, and gives each observer patch a travelling clock whose closing filter implements the special-relativistic reunion rate on the calibrated relay. A further candidate law on the same carrier, the
scalar channel, holds an object that carries a prepared clock. The executable-model paper lists the finite
theorems whose premises the outputs of the model supply. The
papers state the theory around it, the law, what its observers read, the
applicable mathematical theorems and the physical identification conditions.
A theorem transfers between constructions only through its actual hypotheses.

## Specialist results

The [additional papers](../extra/README.md) contain finite event-algebra,
normal-form, capacity, coupling, family and pure-gauge results. The
[cosmology papers](../cosmology/README.md) state their physical source,
state, scale and comparison assumptions. These results do not establish
cosmology, a pure Yang–Mills gap or a physical coupling for the executable
model merely by sharing terminology.

The results of the executable model are reproduced in
[model/candidate_laws/contrast_memory_spherical_v1](../model/candidate_laws/contrast_memory_spherical_v1/README.md). Its tests compare
distributions of admissible observer records; the carrier is hardware of the
computation and no physical space, and its field theorem approximates admissible readouts on a supplied radial interval with a supplied hyperbolic evolution.

## Mathematical and physical evidence

A paper states the theorem or calculated consequence in a form readable
without the repository. It cites the owning proof or executable artifact
for reproduction. The same quantity has the same definition, source and
units wherever it appears. Supplied coefficients and target-informed
calibration do not become independent predictions when quoted in another
paper.

General quantum, CAR, complete-record and conditional Einstein results are
reused under their actual hypotheses. Source-dependent vacua, propagation,
regional stress, geometry and clock comparisons require calculations on the
executable model or an explicit controlled map.

## Reproduction

The [reproduction guide](../REPRODUCE.md) gives model, proof and publication
commands. The [paper manifest](../paper/paper_release_manifest.json) binds
the maintained sources and rendered release PDFs; the book gives a separate
standalone introduction. A successful build verifies the artifact and its
layout, not the physical identification of its mathematical construction.
