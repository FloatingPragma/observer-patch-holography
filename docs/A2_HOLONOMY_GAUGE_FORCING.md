## The exact Lie-type theorem

Let

\[
V=\mathbb R^{12}
\cong_{A_5}
\mathbf 1\oplus\mathbf 3\oplus\mathbf 3'\oplus\mathbf 5
\]

be the real port module of the oriented carrier. Suppose its complete
reversible response supplies a finite-dimensional unitary space \(H\) and an
injective real-linear derivative

\[
D:V\longrightarrow\mathfrak u(H)
\]

whose image \(\mathfrak g=D(V)\) is closed under commutators. Suppose every
proper carrier automorphism has a closed overlap transport whose projective
implementer acts on the same current by

\[
\operatorname{Ad}_{U_a}D(v)=D(a\cdot v),
\]

and whose implementer lies in the connected group generated by
\(\exp(\mathfrak g)\), modulo a unitary that centralizes
\(\mathfrak g\) pointwise. Then

\[
\mathfrak g
\cong
\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3).
\]

This conclusion fixes the abstract compact Lie-algebra type. It does not fix
couplings, a preferred matrix representation, the allocation of the two
Galois triplets between the simple factors, spectator representations, the
global quotient, matter content, or a laboratory current.

## Proof

Injectivity gives \(\dim\mathfrak g=12\). A finite-dimensional Lie subalgebra
of \(\mathfrak u(H)\) is compact and reductive. The proper carrier action on
\(V\) is transitive, so its fixed subspace has dimension one. Equivariance
and injectivity of \(D\) give the same fixed dimension on
\(\mathfrak g\). If \(U_a=g_ac_a\), the factor \(c_a\) centralizes
\(\mathfrak g\), so
\(\operatorname{Ad}_{U_a}|_{\mathfrak g}
=\operatorname{Ad}_{g_a}|_{\mathfrak g}\). The carrier action on
\(\mathfrak g\) is therefore inner. The chosen path lifts need not form a
homomorphic section. Covariance through the injective map \(D\) still gives
a well-defined homomorphism from the proper carrier group to
\(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathfrak g)\), and every value is inner. Inner
automorphisms fix the centre pointwise, hence

\[
\dim Z(\mathfrak g)\leq 1.
\]

If the centre were zero, compact-simple classification through dimension
twelve would leave

\[
\mathfrak g\cong\mathfrak{su}(2)^4.
\]

Inner automorphisms of this product do not permute the four simple factors.
The action of \(A_5\) on each factor is trivial or faithful because \(A_5\)
is simple. A trivial factor has fixed dimension three. A faithful
icosahedral factor has fixed dimension zero. The full fixed dimension would
therefore be one of \(0,3,6,9,12\), contradicting the carrier value one.

The centre consequently has dimension one and the semisimple part has
dimension eleven. Compact-simple classification gives the unique split
\(11=3+8\), which proves the result.
