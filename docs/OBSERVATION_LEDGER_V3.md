## Adequacy rungs

- **Formal precursor**: an exact mathematical representation or helper exists, but no theorem attaches it to the observer architecture or a physical readout. It cannot by itself satisfy a physics target.
- **Structural**: the architecture provably carries the law; an exact theorem from the three axioms plus named register rows, machine-checked where the statement permits.
- **Emergent**: simulated observers' records exhibit the law; a preregistered, target-clean instrument run at laptop scale with pinned receipts.
- **Predictive**: a pre-comparison frozen instrument owned by the relevant physics lane binds the row to future data with registered kill bands.

A structural claim whose premises are unregistered is invalid, an emergent claim without a preregistered instrument is invalid, and a predictive claim without its exact pre-comparison frozen target is invalid.

## Status labels

- **attained**: the rung's contract is met inside the stated boundary, with committed receipts.
- **partial**: committed receipts cover part of the row's contract; the boundary column states what is owed.
- **owed**: no qualifying receipt is committed; the owning lane carries the obligation.

Statuses are conservative: a conditional result is conditional, a declared premise is declared, and postdictions are never predictions. Predictive rows name their related FZ register targets; an attained predictive row requires the targets specified by its fixed row-level contract to be frozen or locked.
