It is not itself evidence: a promotion requires the owning lane's receipts and survives independent audit before landing here.

## Adequacy rungs

- **Structural**: the architecture provably carries the law; an exact theorem from the three axioms plus named register rows, machine-checked where the statement permits.
- **Emergent**: simulated observers' records exhibit the law; a preregistered, target-clean instrument run at laptop scale with pinned receipts.
- **Predictive**: a frozen instrument binds the row to future data with registered kill bands, under the custody rules of the standing lane.

A structural claim whose premises are unregistered is invalid, an emergent claim without a preregistered instrument is invalid, and a predictive claim without custody is invalid.

## Status labels

- **attained**: the rung's contract is met inside the stated boundary, with committed receipts.
- **partial**: committed receipts cover part of the row's contract; the boundary column states what is owed.
- **owed**: no qualifying receipt is committed; the owning lane carries the obligation.

Statuses are conservative: a conditional result is conditional, a declared premise is declared, and postdictions are never predictions.
