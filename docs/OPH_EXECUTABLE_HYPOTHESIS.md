The official executable realization is [CCF-LT-2](../model/SPEC.md), a common
finite matter, field and response construction. The hypothesis is that its
compatible observer records, on a justified refinement and physical
interpretation, reproduce the observable physics of our Universe. This is a
physical acceptance target, not a consequence of declaring a Hamiltonian.
The [machine-readable obligations](../claims/executable_model_hypothesis.json)
carry the same distinction into the scientific registers.

The model is built, and the theory around it is complete. Its current native
repair law is a calibrated intermediate test. The correct native repair law,
the simple law that governs the interactions of observers in the early
Universe, is missing, and the search for it is ongoing. The physical
comparisons below are the tests that law has to pass on the common source.

## Closure and a specified construction

The overall OPH hypothesis is closure: a complete history is both a timeless
self-referential structure and its internal computation. Observers recover
and realize their world's specification inside that history, including its
realizing hardware. Space, time and matter describe compatible observations.
The [basis](BASIS.md) and [interpretation contract](INTERPRETATION_AND_TERMS.md)
state the proposal and its adopted mathematical premises.

The creator may specify the carrier, full Hamiltonian, preparations,
references, instruments and finite resources. Simulated observers need not
invent or install the laws. Neither uniqueness nor derivation of architectural
choices is a prerequisite for execution. The resulting records must satisfy
their physical comparisons on the same source and with all calibration
inputs visible.

## Gravity through the retained theorem

The matched-regional theorem consumes ten entropy/area/stress observations
in one orthonormal frame. Its finite residual bound propagates three stated
errors, with common positive area coefficient and radius. The same regional
data must enter all three estimates. A source-specific continuum attachment
requires errors negligible relative to the fourth power of the shrinking
proper radius, convergent tensor data and a common nondegenerate Lorentz
metric. Independent conservation and a common vacuum reference give the
corresponding constant-coupling and cosmological-reference conclusions.

The local-capacity law supplies finite energy coupling, a refining
angular/radial carrier, constant-coframe and shift mechanical derivatives,
and a faithful finite Gauss/Gibbs reference. These interfaces do not identify
a common physical stress, modular kernel, area or Lorentz metric limit.
The finite response alone is neither an Einstein equation nor a proof that
scalar microscopic variables exclude emergent tensor geometry.

## Scope of physical quantities

Construction units, laboratory seconds, physical rest masses and measured
couplings are distinct attachments. An input value named speed, field
coefficient, reference energy or vacuum strength does not derive physical
`c`, the fine-structure constant, Newton's constant or the cosmological
constant. A finite matrix element is not an on-shell decay rate. A bounded
quantum lapse is not exactly eliminated by continuous static minimization.

The intended photon-containing physical theory has no strictly positive
global vacuum gap. Sector gaps, charged thresholds and pure Yang–Mills
statements retain their separate hypotheses and limits. The official
finite model's empirical identification depends on its source-specific
observations and controlled physical comparison.
