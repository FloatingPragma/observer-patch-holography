## Overall OPH hypothesis and the concrete candidate

OPH's overall hypothesis is closure: one complete history is both a timeless
self-referential structure and its actual internal computation. Observers
recover and realize their world's specification inside that same history,
including the realizing hardware. Space, time and matter are descriptions
of the compatible observations. The [basis](BASIS.md) and
[interpretation contract](INTERPRETATION_AND_TERMS.md) state this proposal.

**The executable-model hypothesis is that a simple, fully specified local
repair and memory law on the proposed carrier architecture admits continuing
observers whose compatible records and interventions have the observed
3+1-dimensional quantum-field description and a common classical-gravity
limit. The preferred matter mechanism is localized topological defects of
native overlap transport, with rest energy derived from their excess
maintenance dynamics in the same physical clock and energy dictionary.**

One source process must meet all parts of this claim. It is not enough for
different supplied actions to reproduce its separate words. The twelve-port
architecture, its refinement/composition, complete C/E record instruments,
memory law, scheduler, preparations and available controls must be stated
before testing. Supplied coherence, protected codes, radial directions,
fields, clocks or couplings remain premises until realized by this process.
The [construction contract](../model/CONSTRUCTION.md#completion-target-and-acceptance-criteria)
specifies how complete observable laws are compared.

This is a stronger candidate hypothesis than closure alone. Closure does not
imply that the current C/E implementation, every admissible repair law, or
every initial state yields Standard Model physics. A failed specified law
rejects that law and its tested interpretation; it rejects the wider OPH
proposal only if the rejected choice is proved necessary and exclusive.
Changing the law, memory, state or decoder defines a new candidate and keeps
its empirical ancestry visible.

## Modular clocks and energy

Modular flow is fixed by an algebra and a faithful reference state. Its
parameter is dimensionless; its relation to local physical duration and
energy requires a derived clock and normalization. A tracial finite reference
has trivial modular flow. Passive dissipative repair is not automatically
that flow, and a compiled modular unitary does not establish that its
Hamiltonian or units were selected by native repair.

The target is mutually comparable local observer clocks, not a preferred
universal clock. Preparation, modular action, continuing measurements and
their failures must be available within the declared process, with memory,
controls, delays and resource costs accounted. A stationary reference need
not freeze individual histories. Neither stationarity of that reference nor
entropy increase under repair alone supplies gravitational entropy
stationarity.

## Particles, defects and the spectrum

The preferred initial target is **stable massive matter as localized,
topologically protected native defects**. Exhibit the actual sector and
boundary invariant under admitted repairs, its reference regime and localized
core, and how charge is transported. The same law must determine allowed
creation, fusion, annihilation and decay. Unstable particles require
metastable or internal defect excitations with calculated decay channels;
they cannot all be declared stable. Massless radiation and gauge response
must also arise from this construction, with explicit maps to its transport
or collective excitations. No universal defect identification is established.

The proposed mass is the defect's **excess rest energy over the reference**,
read in the same clock and energy dictionary that controls its propagation
and interactions. In a justified local relativistic regime this must give
the same `E_rest = m c^2`, with one common signal speed. Repair attempts,
host runtime, entropy production and maintenance cost are not interchangeable
energy observables. The maintenance-to-energy map must be derived, including
kinetic normalization and any required regulator/reference subtraction;
fitting a conversion per species would not test the hypothesis.

The target includes the observed chiral species, families, charges,
spin/statistics, interaction strengths, masses and mixing in compatible
schemes. The immediate charged-spectrum benchmark is nine running charged
Yukawas and four independent CKM parameters. Neutrino observations and their
mass/mixing mechanism remain further obligations; the minimal massless-
neutrino Standard Model is not the full observed target. Quark masses require
renormalization/matching and confinement observables; unstable masses require
resonance conventions; infrared charged sectors need their stated treatment.
"Exact spectrum" means a definite source law with controlled physical
predictions, tested within experimental and theoretical uncertainties, not
exact equality to rounded reference numbers.

**There is no strictly positive global vacuum mass gap in the intended
infinite-volume theory with a massless photon.** Sector gaps and massive
thresholds must name their state, sector and limit. A positive repair
relaxation gap or finite-cavity level spacing does not prove a physical mass
gap. A separate pure Yang--Mills gap target must retain its own hypotheses
and must not be conflated with the full photon-containing theory; see the
[Clay problem statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf).

The target has an unbroken electromagnetic sector and a massless photon.
The color gauge theory has no explicit gauge-breaking gluon mass term;
confinement and its gauge-invariant spectrum must be established rather than
asserting observable free massless gluons. A quantum-gravity extension could
seek a massless spin-two graviton in a suitable weak-field regime. A graviton
is not a Standard Model particle, and the classical Einstein reconstruction
does not prove that quantum excitation. See the
[PDG Standard Model review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-standard-model.pdf)
and [QCD review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-qcd.pdf).

## Realize the Einstein premises, then use the theorem

The target is a recovered common matter/geometry/clock description that
actually satisfies the reconstruction hypotheses. On one refinement tower,
the construction must provide:

1. An effective Lorentzian event/geometry domain with the required locality,
   differentiability, causal and count/volume limits.
2. Geometrically normalized local cap modular flow whose directional charges
   define the same matter stress tensor used in propagation and interactions.
3. Its Ward conservation identity, independently of defining stress from the
   desired Einstein equation.
4. The edge-center entropy split and area normalization, the modular first
   law, and generalized-entropy stationarity under the specified local
   geometric/state variations at fixed proper volume.
5. Controlled small-ball remainders, sufficient observer/null directions and
   compatible coarse-graining.
6. Universal coupling, a common vacuum/reference and a scale calibration
   giving one positive Newton constant.

These are the flagship's G1--G6 realization obligations. With them realized,
the established null-balance and tensor-completion implications yield the
Einstein equation up to a cosmological constant, under their stated domain
and conservation hypotheses. At finite resolution, stationarity and balance
errors must propagate into a controlled gravitational error budget.

A stationary Markov distribution, an entropy-maximizing density matrix or a
stationary conditional record entropy is not by itself the required
fixed-proper-volume generalized-entropy variation. The current native
entropy controls and the geometric theorem cannot be joined by reusing the
word stationarity. Defect maintenance energy must enter this same stress and
modular dictionary if it is also to gravitate. This common realization,
rather than a new proof of the conditional tensor identity, is the next
gravitational construction problem.

## What the present work is testing next

Construct a genuine localized native sector and its invariant without
disabling admitted repairs; test persistence and transport, then derive its
same-process energy, kinetics and observer readout. Extend that process to a
controlled interacting chiral description. In parallel, construct a
responsive geometry and the joint modular/stress/entropy variation consumed
by the Einstein theorem. Retain negative controls: the transient collision
sector, corrected memory without an established defect-energy map, and the
static radial geometry that cannot balance its admitted scalar stress.

These results would support the executable hypothesis only when their actual
state, law, access, clock and refinement maps compose. More benchmark
eigenvalues or larger carriers alone do not close that join.
