# Claim Contract for Public Web Surfaces

Binding rules for every statement about OPH theory content on public web
routes. The contract derives from `claims/axiom_registry.yaml` and
`docs/AXIOM_REFERENCE.md`; where it and they disagree, they win.

## 1. Permitted statements and their required premises

Each permitted statement class is listed with the premises a page must
carry when it makes the statement. A statement without its premises is a
forbidden promotion.

| Permitted statement | Required premises on the page |
|---|---|
| OPH rests on exactly three axioms (A1, A2, A3). | None; unconditional. Any surface that states or counts the basis gives exactly three and pairs each informal sentence with its formal statement or a citation to the axiom reference. |
| The carrier boundary is combinatorially the icosahedron boundary; its orientation-preserving incidence automorphism group is the alternating group on five letters. | States that the group fact is a theorem about A1 carriers, proved from incidence; the group is never called an axiom. |
| Public records glue to finite compatible global sections with a commutative event algebra. | States the gluing theorem's premises; the physical-universe capacity attachment stays labeled as an open interface. |
| Lorentz kinematics: the conformal group of the spherical support is the proper orthochronous Lorentz group; 3+1 events; hyperbolic observer space. | Conditional on the typed global-support branch; the page names that branch dependence. |
| The Einstein equation follows from entropy stationarity. | Conditional composition theorem; the page states that named interface premises are consumed and links or lists them. Never unconditional. |
| The finite chain from boundary incidence yields the gauge algebra su(3)+su(2)+u(1) and the Z6 global structure. | Exact inside the named carrier realization under declared premises (full-rank physical current with inner A5 action; conjugate matter-projector pair). The page states that no matter-content selection rule enters and that laboratory identification is a separate pending attachment. |
| One exact 15-state chiral generation witness exists (rank-15), with hypercharge and anomaly balance and the Z6 kernel. | Exact inside the named realization and its declared exterior-module menu; menu completeness open. |
| The generation count lies in the conditional window three to five; the value three is a declared completion. | The window and the completion status are stated together; family attachment is labeled open. |
| Quantitative fixed-point certificates: each declared map has exactly one fixed point on its stated domain. | Arithmetic exactness on the stated domain only; physical transport, endpoint, and identification premises are labeled open or pending. |
| Nonimplication statements (the axioms do not force X). | Placed with their countermodel per section 3. |

## 2. Forbidden promotions

Each row names a promotion that never appears on a public surface, in any
wording, and the compliant replacement.

| Forbidden promotion | Compliant replacement |
|---|---|
| Unconditional Einstein claim ("OPH derives the Einstein equation" with no premise clause). | "Under its named interface premises, entropy stationarity yields the Einstein equation; every clause is instrumented and the premises are catalogued." |
| Three generations from the axioms ("OPH predicts/derives three generations"). | "One exact generation witness exists inside the named realization; the count is open inside the window three to five, and three is a declared completion." |
| One-Higgs uniqueness. | Withdrawn as an economy-based selection. "The witness carries invariant scalar lines; scalar existence and multiplicity are an open interface." |
| Extra-sector completeness ("no extra light sectors exist"). | Withdrawn as an economy-based selection. "Source completeness for extra light sectors is an open interface; sterile extensions remain countermodels." |
| Recovery from the axioms ("the axioms give recovery/recoverability"). | "Collar recovery is a catalogued interface with countermodels; the identity-channel countermodel shows the axioms do not force it." |
| Any economy or minimality argument presented as physics selection. | "Finite order definitions are diagnostics; they select no physics." |
| Untyped consumption ("the axioms give X" without naming A1/A2/A3 and any interfaces). | Name the exact axiom dependency and any registry interfaces consumed. |

## 3. Placement of countermodels

A page that states a nonimplication places the countermodel in the same
section as the claim it limits, at the same heading level, before any
forward pointer. The five canonical countermodel families (global
extension, confluence, exponential-family closure, collar recovery,
matter selection) are summarized in the learning handoff and are quoted
or condensed from there. Countermodels are described as permanent
controls; they are never framed as defects, gaps to be papered over, or
temporary states.

## 4. Public wording rules for the seven status classes

Every theory claim on a public surface resolves to one status class and
uses that class's wording shape. Mixed scopes never share one label.

| Status class | Public wording shape | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| `axiom_forced` | Plain indicative; may say "theorem" and "every model of the axioms". | "In every model of the axioms, the carrier boundary symmetry group is the alternating group on five letters." |
| `exact_named_realization` | "Exact inside the named realization" plus the declared premises; never "the axioms select it". | "Inside the named carrier realization, the chain constructs su(3)+su(2)+u(1) exactly, under its declared premises." |
| `discovery_only` | "Observed in simulation; no coverage theorem." Never "predicted" or "derived". | "This pattern appears in simulation runs; a coverage theorem is open." |
| `conditional_open_interface` | "Under the named premises ..." with the premises listed or linked; the open part named. | "Under the collar recovery and entropy-split premises, stationarity yields the field equation; those premises are open interfaces." |
| `independence_limited` | The claim plus its delimiting countermodel in the same breath. | "Alignment holds only under added premises; the Bell-pair countermodel excludes the unconditional form." |
| `physical_identification` | "Identified with" plus attachment state (pending, supported, rejected). Never presented as derivation. | "The horizon-area reading is a physical identification with attachment pending." |
| `withdrawn` | Stated as withdrawn when mentioned at all; never silently deleted where readers may hold the old claim. | "The economy-based generation count is withdrawn; the surviving exact results keep the scope their remaining premises supply." |

## 5. Style constraints

All web copy follows `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md`: state-only language with no
narrative of research evolution, no em-dashes, no "not X, but Y" sentence
shapes, no moral-character adjectives for surfaces, and no internal
identifiers, receipt names, issue numbers, or repository paths in
teaching copy. The axiom names A1, A2, A3, the group name A5, and the
seven status-class words are public vocabulary.
