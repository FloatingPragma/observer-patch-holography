## What the dispositions mean

### remove

A remove row is consumed as a declared premise while its derivation from the three axioms remains open. The row leaves the register only through a proved source derivation; every composition citing it is conditional on it, and any bounded no-go attached to the row records exactly what the current axioms fail to supply.

### axiomatize

An axiomatize row is a candidate for promotion into the basis. Promotion is a recorded register decision with rationale and a basis-wide dependency and countermodel review, never a silent edit; the row keeps its source-selection no-gos as the evidence of what the current axioms do not supply, and a promoted row remains on the register with its decision record.

### import

An import row is a flagged external input that stays on the register: classical external mathematics, pinned empirical data packets, and the two numerical inputs P and N. An import is never presented as a derivation; replacing an import with a source-side result is a recorded disposition change.

Register rows keep the source-selection no-gos proved against them as permanent evidence of what the current axioms do not supply. The register is the anti-cheating surface of the V3 program.
