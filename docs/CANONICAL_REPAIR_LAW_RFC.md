# Canonical repair law RFC

## Status

This document specifies a proposed strengthening of A1 and A2. It is not part
of the three-axiom basis. Adoption requires the proof and refinement gates
listed below.

The proposed A1-R and A2-R clauses are substantive basis amendments, not
clarifications of consequences that follow from the current wording. They
exclude models that satisfy the present A1-A3 basis. Keeping them inside A1
and A2 would preserve a three-axiom count, but adoption would still require a
basis-wide dependency and countermodel audit.

The scalar finite branch has a positive exact result. The thirty primitive
seam constraints, their sixty directed integer completions, the unique
generator ray, the exact conditional mean, and the complete nonnegative
total-twelve closure class have executable certificates. The full accessible
algebra, path-law premise, refinement semigroup, protected-sector choice, and
physical attachment remain outside that result. Lean proves the signed-sector
minimum-shell formula and the one-, two-, and three-seam progress arithmetic;
almost-sure shell hitting still consumes the proposed free path law.

Lean also proves the finite-word selection implication for an arbitrary
nonempty finite primitive alphabet. If A1-R has supplied the complete,
quotient-deduplicated event identities, A2-R has proved that every finite word
law is feasible, and A3 minimizes Kullback-Leibler divergence to source
counting measure, the selected law is the uniform product law. The proof uses
Gibbs' equality case, covers every word length, proves prefix consistency, and
fixes the mass of every declared subset by its number of actual event
identities. This determines orbit masses after a separate orbit classification.
It does not prove the completeness, orbit-classification, or temporal-freedom
premises.

The proposal is designed to derive a unique dimensionless repair generator at
each certified finite regulator after a common repair algebra, a faithful
selected state, and a fair schedule have been certified. It does not introduce
a fourth axiom, and it has not been adopted.

## Motivation

A1 exposes repair instruments, A2 defines agreement of interpreted data, and
A3 selects a least-informative state or transition distribution inside a fixed
feasible space. The canonical axiom reference does not require the A1 repair
grammar to be complete and does not define a completed A2 reconciliation.
Consequently, several inequivalent repair policies satisfy the basis.

The proposed clauses close those two gaps. A1 fixes the complete primitive
move grammar and types the data carried through repair. A2 fixes the
operational meaning of a completed repair. The A3-selected faithful state
then determines the state-preserving conditional expectations. On a single
transitive move orbit, presentation invariance and normalization fix the
schedule. Several primitive orbits retain relative weights unless the source
reference counts the complete quotient-visible event identities. With that
counting reference, their relative masses are fixed by their actual
cardinalities. A grammar that exposes only orbit names or leaves incidence
multiplicity unresolved still retains a weighting choice.

## Proposed A1-R clause: complete primitive repair grammar

**Informal statement.** The repair interface contains all and only the
primitive observer-visible reconciliation problems generated by the patch,
seam, higher-overlap, record, and checkpoint interfaces. Each problem
identifies the data that must agree, the committed data that must survive, and
its transport under recharting and refinement. Hidden labels, duplicate
presentations, measured targets, and unregistered repair moves do not enter
the grammar.

**Formal statement.** At each finite regulator \(r\), A1 supplies one finite
compatible-section repair algebra or module

\[
\mathcal W_r^{\mathrm{rep}},
\]

together with a finite presentation groupoid \(\mathsf M_r\) of primitive
repair diagrams. In the algebraic branch,
\(\mathcal W_r^{\mathrm{rep}}\) is constructed from compatible sections of
the declared observer cover. It is an operational quotient-visible repair
workspace, not a microscopic global state algebra and not a global-state
extension premise.

The repair workspace carries four separately typed objects:

\[
(\mathcal D_r^{\mathrm{work}},\mathcal P_r,
\mathfrak C_r,\mathsf{Chk}_r).
\]

1. \(\mathcal D_r^{\mathrm{work}}\) is the repairable working data. Its values
   may change under reconciliation.
2. \(\mathcal P_r\subseteq\mathcal W_r^{\mathrm{rep}}\) is the protected
   committed-record subalgebra. Every accepted repair fixes it pointwise.
   Checkpoint storage is not folded into this subalgebra.
3. \(\mathfrak C_r\subseteq(\mathcal W_r^{\mathrm{rep}})^*\) is the declared
   family of conserved linear functionals. Conservation means
   \(c\circ\Phi=c\), not that the working observables contributing to \(c\)
   are fixed pointwise. The scalar total \(\sum_i x_i\) belongs here.
4. \(\mathsf{Chk}_r\) is an external checkpoint and record-writing instrument
   with save, restore, append, and continuation maps. The reduced repair
   channel does not determine this instrument.

Each \(m\in\mathsf M_r\) additionally carries a local repair domain, its A2
equalizer, and lift data

\[
(\mathcal W_{r,m}^{\mathrm{loc}},
 \mathcal B_{r,m}^{\mathrm{loc}},
 \lambda_{r,m}).
\]

5. \(\mathcal B_{r,m}^{\mathrm{loc}}\) is defined before any repair map as
   the equalizer of the relevant restriction, translation, interpretation,
   and higher-overlap maps.
6. The lift data place the local equalizer in the common workspace before a
   repair map is chosen; call the resulting subalgebra
   \(\widehat{\mathcal B}_{r,m}\). Once A2-R identifies the local
   expectation, the same data and the compatible restriction squares must
   produce an endomorphism
   \[
   \widehat E_{r,m}:\mathcal W_r^{\mathrm{rep}}
   \longrightarrow\mathcal W_r^{\mathrm{rep}}.
   \]
   Its range must equal \(\widehat{\mathcal B}_{r,m}\). Intersections, convex
   sums, kernels, and spectra are formed only after every primitive map has
   been lifted to this one common repair workspace.
7. The joint equalizer
   \[
   \mathcal B_r^{\mathrm{agr}}
   =\bigcap_{m\in\mathsf M_r}\widehat{\mathcal B}_{r,m}
   \]
   equals the complete A2 agreement algebra on the declared observer cover.
8. Every A2-visible repairable mismatch factors through the grammar.
   A diagram is atomic when its rational constraint row space is not generated
   by diagrams with strictly smaller visible write support. Isomorphic copies
   of one primitive diagram form one move type unless their multiplicity is
   supplied by actual A1 incidence.
9. Presentation equivalences permute \(\mathsf M_r\), preserve the common
   repair workspace, working-data type, protected algebra, conserved
   functionals, equalizers, and checkpoint instrument, and introduce no
   weights.
10. Refinement supplies a source-defined move map
    \[
    \theta_{sr}:\mathsf M_s\longrightarrow
    \mathsf M_r\sqcup\{\bot\},
    \]
    where \(\bot\) denotes a fine repair invisible at the coarse regulator.
11. The primitive event alphabet counts seam-readback and reconciliation
    attempts, including attempts whose completion is a wait. Unless a
    quotient-visible record constraint says otherwise, every letter remains
    enabled after every finite history. Successful or committed repairs are
    not substituted for this attempt alphabet.

A1-R fixes the move space and its targets. It does not fix a channel,
relaxation coefficient, or physical clock. A transitive atomic move orbit
does fix the invariant normalized schedule once A3 is applied.

## Proposed A2-R clause: canonical completed reconciliation

**Informal statement.** A completed primitive repair keeps exactly the meaning
on which observers agree, leaves committed records and checkpoint
continuation intact, and carries no additional choice about the discarded
mismatch.

**Formal statement.** Let \(\omega_r\) be a faithful state on
\(\mathcal W_r^{\mathrm{rep}}\), obtained from the compatible A3-selected
local family by the declared compatible-section construction. Nonfaithful
states are outside the theorem stated in this RFC. For each primitive diagram
\(m\), the lifted agreement algebra is required to be invariant under the
modular flow of the selected state:

\[
\sigma_t^{\omega_r}(\widehat{\mathcal B}_{r,m})
=\widehat{\mathcal B}_{r,m}.
\]

A completed repair is a unital completely positive map

\[
\Phi_{r,m}:\mathcal W_r^{\mathrm{rep}}
\longrightarrow\mathcal W_r^{\mathrm{rep}}
\]

such that

\[
\operatorname{Ran}\Phi_{r,m}=\widehat{\mathcal B}_{r,m},\qquad
\Phi_{r,m}|_{\widehat{\mathcal B}_{r,m}}=\operatorname{id},\qquad
\omega_r\circ\Phi_{r,m}=\omega_r.
\]

The map fixes \(\mathcal P_r\) pointwise and obeys

\[
c\circ\Phi_{r,m}=c
\qquad(c\in\mathfrak C_r).
\]

The external instrument \(\mathsf{Chk}_r\) is carried separately. Save,
restore, record append, and continuation diagrams must commute with the
reduced repair channel. These instrument properties require their own
receipt. They do not follow from conditional-expectation recognition. A
restored working state remains subject to subsequent repair.

The lifted primitive maps agree on common restrictions by construction. This
gluing condition is required before they are summed. It does not assert a
microscopic global state extension.

A2-R also requires temporal-constraint completeness. Every observer-visible
constraint on finite repair histories factors through the declared history
grammar. An IID conclusion is available only when this theorem finds no
state-dependent event deletion, next-event restriction, or cross-position
coupling. Absence from a finite test battery is not a completeness theorem.

The word “completed” is load-bearing. Partial maps

\[
(1-\alpha)I+\alpha\Phi_{r,m}
\]

retain an arbitrary \(\alpha\). A primitive tick must mean complete local
reconciliation if the relaxation strength is to be removed from the
dimensionless law.

## Derived finite law

For a faithful finite-dimensional state \(\omega\) on the common repair
algebra and a modular-invariant lifted agreement algebra
\(\widehat{\mathcal B}\), the A2-R conditions identify the unique
\(\omega\)-preserving conditional expectation

\[
E_{\widehat{\mathcal B}}^{\omega}:
\mathcal W^{\mathrm{rep}}\longrightarrow\widehat{\mathcal B}.
\]

In the GNS inner product

\[
\langle x,y\rangle_\omega=\omega(x^*y),
\]

this map is the orthogonal projection onto
\(\widehat{\mathcal B}\). No theorem for a nonfaithful state is claimed here.

The unequal weights of reducible central blocks come from the A3-selected
state. They cannot be replaced by an unproved dimension trace.

A3 applies separately to the fixed primitive-move simplex:

\[
\mu_r=
\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{\mu\in\mathcal K_r^{\mathrm{move}}}
D(\mu\Vert\nu_r).
\]

The expected one-tick repair and its positive dimensionless generator are

\[
R_r=\sum_{m\in\mathsf M_r}\mu_r(m)\widehat E_{r,m}^{\omega},
\qquad
L_r=I-R_r
=\sum_m\mu_r(m)(I-\widehat E_{r,m}^{\omega}).
\]

The selected ontic state \(\omega_r\) and the schedule optimizer \(\mu_r\)
are distinct A3 objects.

The schedule reference is constructed from quotient-visible A1 incidence and
must be invariant under every admissible presentation equivalence. If the
move automorphism group is transitive and normalization is the only schedule
constraint, invariance forces

\[
\mu_r(m)=\frac1{|\mathsf M_r|}.
\]

No separately adjustable uniform weight remains on a single orbit. The
twelve-port scalar grammar gives \(1/30\) on the thirty seam constraints.
For the integer lift, the unresolved odd-total tie has two
presentation-related nearest completions. A3 assigns \(1/2\) to that tie,
equivalently \(1/60\) to each directed seam completion. If the move grammar
contains several atomic orbits, the complete quotient-visible event set and
its source counting reference give each actual primitive event one unit.
Orbit mass is therefore its event count divided by the total event count.
This conclusion does not apply when the grammar has classified only orbit
types or has not settled which incidence copies are distinct events.

The same conclusion can hold on event strings under the proposed history
clauses. If A1-R proves that the length-\(k\) attempt space is the full word
space \(\mathsf M_r^k\) with source counting reference, and A2-R proves that
the complete temporal grammar imposes no coupling, A3's information
projection gives weight \(|\mathsf M_r|^{-k}\) to each word. These laws are
prefix-consistent and define the IID process. Canonical A3 alone does not
imply Markovity. Uniform one-event marginals are insufficient because a
repeat-the-first-event schedule has the same marginal and a different
two-event kernel.

`Lean/ObserverPatchHolography/RepairWordSchedule.lean` proves this implication
with the Kullback-Leibler divergence. It derives the uniform law from global
minimality on the full PMF simplex, its product factorization, exact subset
and orbit masses, invariance under event relabeling, and consecutive-length
prefix consistency. No geometric transitivity hypothesis is used.

Each primitive expectation is GNS self-adjoint. Therefore

\[
\langle x,L_rx\rangle_{\omega_r}
=\sum_m\mu_r(m)
\left\|(I-\widehat E_{r,m}^{\omega})x\right\|_{\omega_r}^{2},
\]

and

\[
\ker L_r
=\bigcap_{\{m:\mu_r(m)>0\}}\widehat{\mathcal B}_{r,m}.
\]

Only a fair, full-support schedule with
\(\mu_r(m)>0\) for every \(m\in\mathsf M_r\) makes this kernel equal to the
full joint agreement algebra \(\mathcal B_r^{\mathrm{agr}}\). At a finite
regulator, the semigroup \(e^{-tL_r}\) is unital, completely positive,
state-preserving, and converges on observables to the GNS projection onto the
positive-support fixed algebra.

This gives uniqueness of the asymptotic observable reconciliation map under
the stated finite assumptions. It supplies no uniqueness theorem for states
fixed by the dual channel. Even when the observable fixed algebra equals the
protected subalgebra, uniqueness of a dual stationary state in a protected
sector requires a separate primitivity or dual-irreducibility theorem.

## Scalar twelve-port specialization

The scalar A1 mismatch row on a seam \(e=\{i,j\}\) is
\(C_e x=x_i-x_j\). It has minimal two-port write support. A triangular-face
agreement row space has rank two and is generated by two of its three seam
rows. Faces therefore supply coherence checks rather than a second scalar
primitive orbit. On all 66 unordered port pairs, the proper carrier action
has three orbits: 30 seams, 30 distance-two pairs, and six antipodal pairs.
A1 incidence selects the seam orbit. This atomic decomposition removes an
edge-versus-face rate selector on the scalar branch. It does not prove the
same completeness statement for a noncommutative repair algebra.

For one seam \(e=\{i,j\}\) and
\(b_e=e_i-e_j\), a real-linear update that changes only the two endpoints,
preserves their sum, and lands in the equalizer \(x_i=x_j\) is uniquely

\[
E_e=I-\frac12 b_e b_e^{\mathsf T}.
\]

It replaces both endpoint readings by their arithmetic mean. Uniform
scheduling on the thirty seams gives

\[
R=I-\frac1{60}L_{\mathrm{ico}},
\qquad
L_{\mathrm{rep}}=\frac1{60}L_{\mathrm{ico}}.

The scalar integer lift has a sharp boundary. For an even endpoint total, its

## Refinement gate

Finite uniqueness does not imply cross-refinement naturality. For a coarse
channel \(Q_{sr}\), an exact sufficient gate is

\[
Q_{sr}\widehat E_{s,m}
=\widehat E_{r,\theta_{sr}(m)}Q_{sr}
\]

for visible moves and

\[
Q_{sr}\widehat E_{s,m}=Q_{sr}
\]

when \(\theta_{sr}(m)=\bot\).

If the schedule weights satisfy

\[
\sum_{\theta_{sr}(m)=n}\mu_s(m)
=a_{sr}\mu_r(n),
\]

then

\[
Q_{sr}L_s=a_{sr}L_rQ_{sr}.
\]

The scale factors must satisfy the cocycle law

\[
a_{tr}=a_{ts}a_{sr}.
\]

Write \(\Delta_r\) for the raw graph Laplacian, \(J\) for midpoint
interpolation, and \(Q\) for inherited-vertex readback. The current geodesic
tower satisfies the exact first-order identity

\[
Q\Delta_fJ=\frac12\Delta_c.
\]

This raw identity must not be confused with the per-total-attempt repair
generator

\[
K_r=\frac{\Delta_r}{2|E_r|}.
\]

Midpoint refinement quadruples the seam count, so the corresponding exact
identity is

\[
QK_fJ=\frac18K_c.
\]

The per-total-attempt generator fails strong intertwining and the second-order
semigroup condition. Neither first-order identity supplies refinement
naturality of the repair semigroup. A renormalized refinement theorem or a
different source-justified coarse channel is required.

## Circularity controls

The amendment is admissible only if all of the following checks pass.

1. Each local equalizer is defined from A2 data before its repair map is
   constructed, and every local map is lifted to the common repair workspace
   before intersections or sums are formed.
2. The move groupoid is generated from A1 data-access diagrams rather than
   from moves that produce preferred physics.
3. The state \(\omega_r\) is selected from a pre-repair constraint grammar.
4. The schedule reference is constructed from quotient-visible A1 incidence
   and contains no measured spectrum or target. On a single transitive atomic
   orbit, presentation invariance and normalization force uniformity. Several
   atomic orbits require a source-derived relative weighting theorem.
5. Modular invariance is tested for every primitive agreement algebra.
6. Duplicate presentations are quotiented out before weights are assigned.
7. Uniform scheduling is claimed only on a proved transitive orbit. IID
   scheduling additionally requires the all-word free-composition theorem,
   temporal-constraint completeness, and prefix consistency. Finite
   event-string tests alone do not discharge this premise.
8. The observable fixed algebra is computed using only positive-support
   moves. Equality with the full joint equalizer requires fair full support,
   and no dual stationary-state uniqueness is inferred from it.
9. Refinement compatibility is proved through exact commuting squares.
10. The dimensionless generator is distinguished from a physical clock.
11. The operational integer-transfer mechanism is not identified with the
    conditional-expectation channel without a macro-repair bridge.
12. A reduced channel is not identified with an individual random history or
    a unique record-writing instrument.

## Self-reference and state selection

The conditional expectation depends on the A3-selected state
\(\omega_r\). This dependence is target-free when \(\omega_r\) is computed
from the A1 and A2 grammar before any repair channel is constructed. It also
means that the repair generator does not create additional physical
information absent from that grammar. It dynamically realizes the same
least-informative compatible state.

A stronger timeless closure would treat the state and repair law as a coupled
fixed-point problem:

\[
\omega_r
=\operatorname{A3State}(A1_r,A2_r,L_r),
\qquad
L_r
=\operatorname{CanonicalRepair}(\omega_r,A1_r,A2_r).
\]

The framework may claim selection by self-reference only after existence and
uniqueness of this pair are proved, modulo protected record sectors and a
common clock scale. Multiple solutions would leave a genuine selector open.
No measured property of the desired solution may enter either map.

That coupled pair still lives inside one fixed A1 model. Selection of a whole
observer universe requires a stronger A2 self-readback closure. Fix a
protected-record sector \(p\). Let
\(\mathsf{Model}_{r,p}^{\mathrm{vis}}\) be the source-defined category of
quotient-visible finite models in that sector, with objects

\[
\mathfrak X_r=(\mathfrak N_r,\omega_r,L_r),
\]

where \(\mathfrak N_r\) includes the compatible-section repair workspace,
complete primitive grammar, equalizers, records, conserved functionals,
checkpoint interface, and refinement data. A source-defined reconstruction
must factor through a separately typed public evidence space:

\[
\operatorname{PublicReadback}_{r,p}:
\mathsf{Model}_{r,p}^{\mathrm{vis}}\longrightarrow\mathsf{Evidence}_{r,p},
\qquad
\operatorname{Reconstruct}_{r,p}:
\mathsf{Evidence}_{r,p}\longrightarrow
\mathsf{Model}_{r,p}^{\mathrm{vis}}.
\]

The first map is fixed by the A1 public readback interfaces. The second is
fixed from source-side interpretation rules before solutions or laboratory
data are inspected. Their composite

\[
\operatorname{SelfReadRepair}_{r,p}
=\operatorname{Reconstruct}_{r,p}
\circ\operatorname{PublicReadback}_{r,p}
\]

would:

1. read the public operational data produced by \(\mathfrak X_r\);
2. reconstruct its A1 grammar and A2 equalizers without hidden labels;
3. recompute the A3 state and schedule;
4. recompute the canonical repair generator; and
5. return the quotient-visible observer model encoded by that same readback.

The full self-reading closure equation is

\[
\mathfrak X_r
\simeq
\operatorname{SelfReadRepair}_{r,p}(\mathfrak X_r),
\]

where \(\simeq\) is generated by the declared A1 presentation and gauge
equivalences and by one common positive rescaling of \(L_r\). It does not
identify different protected-record sectors. This belongs conceptually in
A2: the observers' interpretation of the rule that repairs their data must
agree with the rule that generated the public repair evidence. It does not
ask A3 to compare unrelated ontology classes.

The equation is useful only if both typed maps are constructed independently
from the declared interfaces.
Allowing reconstruction to access hidden model data, defining it to return
its input, importing a desired spectrum, or choosing its decoder after
observing laboratory values would make the closure empty. A
universe-selection theorem requires one equivalence class of solutions in
each declared protected sector across the complete A1 model class, compatible
solutions across refinement, and a separate physical readout attachment.
Unconditional selection also requires a source-derived choice or uniqueness
of the protected sector. Those requirements are open. The finite scalar
certificate establishes none of them.

## Consequence for the universe-selection claim

If adopted and discharged, the proposed clauses remove arbitrary local
reconciliation maps and, on a single transitive primitive orbit, arbitrary
seam schedules from a fixed observer model. They do not select the complete
physical universe by themselves. A1 still describes a class of observer nets, A3
selects inside a fixed model, protected records label fixed sectors, the
refinement limit is unresolved, and physical field and readout maps remain
separate.

The strongest supported statement is:

> A1-R fixes a common repair workspace and the complete observer-visible move
> grammar. A2-R fixes completed reconciliation on that workspace. Given a
> faithful A3-selected state, source-derived move incidence, and fair full
> support, the three clauses determine a dimensionless agreement-repair
> generator at each certified finite regulator whenever the primitive moves
> form one transitive orbit. More generally, on a complete quotient-visible
> event alphabet, source counting plus full temporal freedom fixes the
> schedule without a transitivity premise and assigns each orbit the mass
> determined by its number of event identities. This does not select a unique
> physical universe.

If inequivalent repair laws survive after every proof obligation above is
closed, their remaining choice is a genuine additional physical premise. That
is the point at which a fourth axiom would become justified.
