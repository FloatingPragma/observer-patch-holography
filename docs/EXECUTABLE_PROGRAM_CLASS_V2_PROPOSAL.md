# Proposed executable class and observer acceptance

The [V2 contract](../model/completion/program_class_v2.json) defines the adopted
class for CP-00 while preserving the full physical targets of the completion
program. It is marked `ADOPTED_AFTER_REVIEW`. Its
[checker](../model/completion/program_class_v2.py) verifies the bound review,
interpreter membership and theorem assignments without accepting additional
physical gates.

The intended acceptance is limited but substantive: define which constructions
are being tested, exhibit one complete continuing observer in that class, and
bind the theorem map to it. It establishes none of the five EMH physical
obligations. In particular, a source-word realization of a supplied field
Hamiltonian is a service construction; the native selection, species,
continuum and gravity claims require their own evidence.

## What the proposal admits

The class preserves three source interfaces:

1. The twelve-port integer total-two `H78` carrier and its invariant binary
   `H66` shell, including the existing bounded endpoint-weight family.
2. The actual native binary federation with its internal and intercell seams,
   at declared growth level and global total occupation. The full occupation
   tensor product is the ambient carrier; each admitted state and algebra
   uses its declared fixed global number sector. Regional partial traces
   retain all resulting local number sectors. Cross-global-number coherences
   are outside this interpreter, and a tensor product of per-cell total-two
   sectors is not invariant under the joining repairs.
3. Independent finite products of admitted observers, with identities on
   untouched factors. Coupling through a proper-code source service requires
   an explicitly bound encoding; an arbitrary supplied native isometry is
   not an interpreter primitive.

The endpoint law and C/E records are unchanged. Existing fair binary
federation operations are admitted separately from the bounded biased
single-carrier family; no nonuniform federation stationarity theorem is
assumed. The native [binary step](../model/federated_observer/source.py)
executes the original two-endpoint rule on finite occupation tuples and
leaves spectators unchanged. This preserves actual intercell load transfer,
rather than replacing it with independent local fixed-number cells.

Coherent words use only the existing six-level processor generators and
proper-code load/export interface. Their pulse angles retain the exact
binary64 values as rational strings; there is no silent rounding to a new
angle grid. Preparation and every ancillary, record, continuation, timeout
and recovery service are supplied or executed with explicit resource counts.
The native and proper-code register interfaces remain distinct until a
particular source encoding is verified.

The [typed interpreter](../model/native_program/interpreter.py) supplies the
admission and execution interface. Program description length, repeated
attempt counts, literal word length, archive size and source pulses have
fixed ceilings. An evaluator stop preserves its input and emits a stop
outcome. Declared larger source levels are not called excluded merely because
their dense evaluation is unavailable.

The refinement clauses deliberately retain two different constructions:
quantum tensor restriction by partial trace, and the existing commutative
cell-parent coordinate lift/mean. The
[native nonlumpability witness](../model/federated_observer/refinement.py)
prevents identifying the latter with exact quantum or dynamical refinement.
A continuum construction must prove a compatible state/operation/clock tower
with errors tending to zero. Declaring the family does not prove that limit.

## Fixed physical target and exposure rules

The contract retains all 71 rows of the observation ledger, all five EMH
obligations, the nine charged running Yukawas and four CKM parameters, neutrino
mass/mixing obligations, and the same-process Einstein G1--G6 target. It binds
the existing particle, conditional quark and NuFIT source fixtures and all
prior observation, forecast and physical-identification registers. The
predecessor commit and tree identify the retained earlier evidence.

The original observation and physical-identification registers are preserved
as [immutable exposure snapshots](../model/completion/historical/exposure/provenance.json).
Their original class hashes are verified directly. Live ledger commentary may
evolve, but the live observation identities and targets must match the frozen
71-row inventory. All other exposure pins and all interpreter and review-source
bindings retain exact live-file verification.

Every current physical target is exposed. V2 enables retrospective consistency
comparisons and explicitly disables novel-prediction claims. A prospective
campaign needs a successor contract specifying its actual unexposed data and
scoring rule before validation. This does not convert older retrospective
comparisons or calibrated constants into independent predictions.

The exact interval rule has three separate outcomes. A complete predicted
interval contained in the frozen comparison interval passes that marginal
compatibility diagnostic. Disjoint intervals give a candidate discrepancy.
Other cases are inconclusive. Missing physical identification, complete error
accounting or independent output ancestry also gives an inconclusive result.
Marginal intervals with differing stated confidence levels are not multiplied
into a joint confidence statement. Full empirical acceptance requires its
additional joint uncertainty and matching certificate.

V2 adds no physical dimensional anchor. A candidate can report dimensionless
outputs while deriving a common clock/energy dictionary. Existing dimensional
benchmarks keep their supplied anchors and their conditional status. A later
physical calibration measurement, its identified source transition and its
covariance must be fixed before comparison; adding that calibration or changing
the dataset requires a successor version. Separate mass scales per species
are forbidden. Every P/N ancestor keeps its source-derived, calibrated,
imported or target-exposed classification.

The limits on program size, source requests and search epochs are research
boundaries, not fundamental physical constants. Reaching one emits a resource
stop. Excluding a tested implementation does not exclude every admitted
construction, and no finite search exhaustion is a whole-program no-go.

## CP-01: the actual finite premises

CP-01 asks for a continuing native observer with accounted services. It does
not ask that its observer witness solve CP-06's full spatial field theory.
Its same-process requirement means that each invoked conclusion must apply
to this observer's actual state, algebra, operations and records; separate
examples cannot be combined by using the same terminology.

The proposed witness is the continuing canonical `H66` observer. Its retained
record acquisition, preparation and readout use one source throughout. The
receipt counts 31,458,883 native attempts and separately accounts the supplied
literal coherent realization, including its 15,938,607,203,238 pulse upper
bound. Counting these words does not claim that the acquisition replay
executed trillions of coherent pulses. The observer's emitted records, FIFO
and archive have separate costs.

are retained. Calling finite trace linearity a discharge of PR-03 would be
incorrect.

preparation rule, record protocol and complete run output. The native-only
diagonal-input grammar cannot prepare the coherent apparatus; V2 explicitly
admits counted coherent services. Neither choice supplies an authenticated

whose conclusion authenticates such an external producer. The canonical

The CP-13 map must identify this distinction explicitly. CP-01 owns the
finite observer-instance proof and its service boundary. Any later claim of
source-selected Born valuation, passive coherent apparatus or physical
producer authentication requires its canonical obligation to be proved
before invocation. It cannot inherit acceptance from the finite observer.
Keeping the broader row registered is compatible with accepting a finite
construction that declares its representation and services; silently calling
the broader row proved is not.

## Original acceptance checklist and adoption decision

| Original criterion | Required review evidence |
| --- | --- |
| CP-00: distinguish fixed laws, implementations and bounded alternatives | Check the explicit carrier union, local biased bounds, fair federation branch, literal source primitive set and complete-outcome rules against interpreter admission. |
| CP-00: observables, targets, calibrations, ancestry, uncertainty, budgets and thresholds | Check every contract section against the bound physical hypothesis and all exposure registers; verify that undefined matching or uncertainty yields inconclusive results and cannot be bypassed. |
| CP-00: actual source interface and resource boundary | Replay the typed source interpreter and the actual continuing observer member; verify large-level resource stops and the distinction between supplied service and physical selection. |
| CP-00: failures/exposure and versioning | Check the predecessor tree, bound registers, unchanged frozen campaigns and successor-version rule. |
| CP-01: frozen source, state, controls, scheduler and memory | Confirm that the continuing observer's literal schedule or declared constructor is a member of the exact adopted grammar, rather than relying on a description or test count. |
| CP-01: coherent, ancillary, initialization, retention, feedback, timeout and recovery services | Recompute resource costs and retain every complement and stop; supplied costs are allowed by the issue contract, hidden services are not. |
| CP-01: full outputs and growth bounds | Check arbitrary-reference and complete-record semantics, checkpoint continuation, overlap restrictions and the stated resource/growth boundary. |
| CP-01: each assigned theorem premise | Review the finite instance table above against actual source arguments and tests; reject promotion of the global PR-03/PR-65 selection and custody obligations. |
| CP-01: original q=3 history | V2 proposes `NOT_ADOPTED` for the original #1077 scalar recipe, whose historical status remains `UNEXECUTED_NOT_OBSTRUCTED`. This retires a particular prototype obligation, not the field or internal-closure targets. |
| CP-13: actual statements, maps, relevance and cleanup | Review the canonical ownership map, finite attachments and this class/observer interface; retain counterexamples and future physical premises, and remove the excluded inherited-product clock join from the active route. |

The acceptance proposal is therefore conditional on concrete independent
review, not on a presumption that this text completes the gates. The
checker reports missing interpreter bindings and missing independent review;
it refuses adoption while either is absent. The root program registry and
issue statuses are separate changes made only after that review.
