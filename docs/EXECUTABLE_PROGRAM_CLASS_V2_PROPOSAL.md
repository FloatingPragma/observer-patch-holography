A physical limit requires a compatible state, operation and clock family

Supplied coherent services are counted resources, not a derivation of passive
apparatus or external producer authentication. Native attempts, modular
parameters and physical duration remain distinct. Finite component results
do not identify physical particles, establish a continuum field theory, or
supply Einstein-branch data.

The interval comparison rule distinguishes marginal compatibility, disjoint
intervals and inconclusive cases. Missing physical identification, uncertainty
accounting or independent ancestry remains inconclusive. Target-exposed and
calibrated inputs do not become independent predictions; differing marginal
confidence levels do not define a joint confidence statement.
