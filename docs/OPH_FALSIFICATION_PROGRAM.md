# OPH Falsification Program

The [executable-model hypothesis](OPH_EXECUTABLE_HYPOTHESIS.md) states the
target: the agreed records of the observers of
[A-contrast-memory-spherical-v1](../model/contrast_memory_spherical_v1/README.md), whose contrast-memory law
is a candidate native repair law, reproduce the observable physics of our
Universe. Its tests compare distributions of admissible observer records with
what observers in our world record; a difference between microscopic state
encodings is no failure of the model. The broad obstruction search #1078 is
open. A whole-program exclusion requires a fixed quantified class and coverage
of every admitted realization. The mature claim/branch falsifiers below keep
their existing scope and custody.

## Scope

This program covers completed, non-cosmological OPH claim surfaces with stable
hypotheses and comparison objects. It contains no frozen-target registry,
preregistration queue, forward scorecard, or aggregate hit count.

The parallel [programme-wide obstruction search (#1078)](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/issues/1078)
investigates inconsistency of the adopted basis, impossibility of a common
physical realization, limits on explanatory unification and empirical
exclusion of a fixed formulation. It pins the tested premises and admissible
interpretations. This broader research question does not extend any falsifier
below beyond its stated claim or branch; changing the target requires an
explicit revision and preserves the original verdict.

The eligible sources are:

  [*Reality as a Consensus Protocol*](../paper/reality_as_consensus_protocol.pdf);

  and the D7--D9 claims in the
  [Standard Model gauge paper](../paper/deriving_standard_model_gauge_structure_from_observer_overlap_consistency.pdf);
- the exact gauge, charge, color, generation, and declared-action carrier
  claims in
  [*Particles from Observer Consistency: Defect Classes, Statistics and the Bound Objects of an Executable Observer*](../paper/deriving_the_particle_zoo_from_observer_consistency.pdf);
- the finite carrier and screen-sieve theorems in
  [*Twelve-Port Icosahedral Carriers and Native Seam Repair*](../paper/screen_microphysics_and_observer_synchronization.pdf);

A claim belongs here only when its statement is complete, its hypotheses are
explicit, the falsifier addresses the same object, and no open map or
measurement convention can absorb the result.

## Ineligible Surfaces

The following surfaces are not part of this falsification program because the
physical map, source derivation, comparison object, or paper-level closure is not complete:

- all cosmology: capacity, dark energy, inflation replacement, scalar tilt,
  CMB spectra, low-$\ell$, birefringence, $H_0$, $S_8$, growth, dark abundance,
  cosmological neutrino rows, and structure formation;
- the repair-charge condensate dark-sector continuation, including galaxy
  acceleration scales, Solar-System response, lensing, clusters, abundance,
  and laboratory force claims;
- quantitative fine-structure and hadronic transport continuations whose
  physical readout or source selector is incomplete;
- black-hole spectroscopy, entropy, Page curves, and ringdown templates;
- coherent-matter, anti-gravity, hardware enrichment and meaning-layer continuations, and the archived cognition and string-vacuum papers;
- Yang--Mills mass-gap claims whose continuum, reflection-positivity,
  transfer/intertwiner, or nontriviality receipts are open.

Diagnostic comparisons, simulator outputs, known-data checks, and engineering
benchmarks carry no falsification verdict.

Two exact no-go receipts delimit replacement searches. In the real
five-dimensional traceless-symmetric family representation, threefold and
fivefold fixed points have a double eigenvalue; the twofold fixed locus has
two parameters after scale. Symmetry alone is nonpredictive, so a numerical
spectrum requires a screen-derived invariant potential. Separately, the 31
real three-dimensional icosahedral residual axes have no
$13.0029^\circ$ acute angle; their smallest nonzero angle is
$20.9052^\circ$. This excludes direct equality between the Cabibbo angle and
one of those axis angles. Spinorial representations, higher-order breaking,
additional dynamics, and general overlap models lie outside that result.

## Operational Use

Each proposed falsifier must identify the claim row, the owner paper, and the
receipt set used to establish its premises. The machine-readable row mapping is
[`claims/falsification_matrix.csv`](../claims/falsification_matrix.csv), and
the current claim status is in
[`claims/claim_registry.yaml`](../claims/claim_registry.yaml). Open physical
maps are tracked on the open physics issues
[#1147](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/issues/1147),
[#1134](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/issues/1134) and
[#1078](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/issues/1078). A result on an
ineligible surface is evidence for or against that continuation only; it is not
silently promoted into this program.

## Decision Rule

A falsifier binds only when every hypothesis of the named claim is verified
and the result addresses the same mathematical or physical observable in the
same convention. Missing hypotheses yield no verdict. Counterexamples remain
scoped to the stated claim and do not transfer to independent theorem rows.

Current work ownership and unclosed maps are recorded on the open physics
issues [#1147](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/issues/1147),
[#1134](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/issues/1134)
and [#1078](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/issues/1078).
Scientific claim boundaries are recorded in the owner papers and the
machine-readable claim registry.
