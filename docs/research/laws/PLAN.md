# Plan for one executable observer law

**Question.** Can one frozen local specification generate histories whose
inhabitants measure a three-dimensional space, one causal speed shared by all
the signals they can use, clocks that keep proper time and a changing cosmic
scale, while the same specification reproduces at least two further
observables of the [observation ledger](../../registers/OBSERVATION_LEDGER_V3.md)
completely? The finite results below give starting points and tests that can
eliminate candidates. The [journal](JOURNAL.md) records what each step found.

## What carries forward

**Theorems.** The source-record causal-count theorem of the
[flagship paper](../../../flagship/from_observer_consensus_to_standard_physics.tex#L2103-L2142)
gives a positive reference for flat 3+1D causal order with a specified
population, read radius and previous-layer dependencies. For fixed interior
timelike diamonds its normalized event counts converge to spacetime volume.
The one-cell
[rank-three theorem](../../../flagship/from_observer_consensus_to_standard_physics.tex#L1185-L1235)
concerns a different object, the slow-response band of the twelve-port
conservative carrier. The
[effective-field proposition](../../../flagship/from_observer_consensus_to_standard_physics.tex#L1984-L2031)
gives a smooth static 3+1D Lorentzian description of a fixed finite menu of
admissible observations under its own hypotheses. An ordinary repair carrier
needs a physical measurement performed by an observer before any of these
theorems describes what that observer reads.

**Reference programs.** A shared colour source carries replayable winding
and binding programs. Its prepared bodies, ring profiles and calibrations are
inputs. Small search examples come from finite work on other laws. A finite
reversible matter model retained pairs and converted signed matter on one
Hamiltonian. A local detector on two rotor laws distinguished
three-direction from layer-confined propagation, and thermal tests on the
same laws exposed numerical and vacuum-dependent failures. Exact searches
found two admissible three-harmonic interactions and identified the minimum
memory in a bounded reversible repair problem. These results motivate the
search grammar. No component result transfers to a law by assembly or
ancestry.

**Step 1.** The reflected-routing law reproduces the long-wave moving-clock
ratio of special relativity and has a proved flat 3+1D Klein–Gordon limit in
a fixed field sector. It is the positive control for the one-body tests below.
[Journal, step 1](JOURNAL.md#step-1-clocks-and-spacetime-from-observer-records)

**Step 2.** On the same apparatus an inhabitant who can invoke the addressed
relay sends every freshly chosen bit ahead of the smooth scalar cone, and a
fixed marker carries every bit through its launch time. The scalar cone of
that apparatus is no universal causal limit for such inhabitants.
[Journal, step 2](JOURNAL.md#step-2-a-race-between-the-addressed-relay-and-the-free-wave)

## Communication access

A candidate that claims one causal speed accounts for which inhabitants can
trigger calibration, routing and collection after a fresh choice. Every
send, trigger and return operation after the start has a named owner, and
every operation an inhabitant can trigger or delay counts among its signals,
including the timing of an otherwise fixed operation. Restricting the
analyst's data after an inhabitant has received a message removes no channel.
An apparatus that supplies permission to send is classified explicitly, and
an autonomous law has to show whether its inhabitants can gain that
permission through their own dynamics.

## What constitutes a candidate

Freeze the local alphabet and state space, the preparation and its
distribution, the exchanges or instruments, the scheduler, the memory
including its reset costs, the ownership and delivery of records, and the
programs observers apply to those records. Hash these together with the test
versions. Different preparations are allowed when the same law can execute
them. A changed transition, controller or memory channel makes a separate
candidate. Record every supplied symmetry, clock calibration, reference and
free scale.

Observers use their own records and the copies they actually receive. The
implementation uses port incidence and scheduling to execute a law. The
geometry reader gets neither the incidence data nor the global simulation
step. An observer's clock can be as simple as a repeating or countable local
process. It earns use as a range standard by surviving a calibration and a
change of experiment.

A geometric reading on a carrier separates three sources of signals and
clocks: (a) those ordinary repair supplies, (b) finite emitter and reflector
operations added for the experiment, and (c) planted controls with known
dimension. Include a silent reflector, a changed signal strength, an
intervention on relay activity, clock drift or stoppage, interchanged
endpoints and a geometric ambiguity control. With six endpoints a generic
four-dimensional fit is nearly saturated, so withhold a baseline or repeat
measurements in a way that exposes ambiguous completions. A complete matrix
reconstructed by an analyst is a different result from a matrix delivered to
a single observer.

## The next experiment

Take a bounded autonomous candidate that assigns an owner to every send,
trigger and return operation after the start. Give a sender a fresh local bit
chosen after the start event and let it try to encode the bit through every
available interface, including the timing of an otherwise fixed operation and
the triggering or delaying of an ordinary calibration message. A distant
observer identifies the bit and returns an authenticated receipt. Race every
channel found against an independently timed wave along the same baseline,
with deadlines and silent controls fixed in advance. Carry a second,
independently built clock on the same routes and seal the predictions of the
scalar metric made with the first signal and clock before opening it. Rerun
the wave and clock benchmarks of step 1 on any revision of the law or its
access rules. A reproducible faster message or a discrepant independently
calibrated clock identifies the law or access rule that has to change.
Agreement on held-out routes gives one spacetime description two independent
kinds of records to predict.

## How to compute candidates

1. Enumerate bounded **whole observer programs** in increasing complexity. A
   transition kernel alone cannot say who can send a fast nonce, who holds a
   returned clock or what happens when memory fills. Bound the neighbourhood,
   local state, memory capacity, transition count, coefficient domain and
   history length. Keep the measured one-body reflection law as a positive
   control and a simple routing law as a measurement control. Seed one branch
   with the finite signed matter, neutral and flux source and another with
   the colour law. Start the coherent branch with finitely many declared
   phases, including the identity and the tested symmetric perturbation. In
   an interacting branch add number-conserving local encounter operations on
   a finite alphabet. Add branches with different microscopic mechanisms so
   that one successful architecture does not define the search space.
2. Remove equivalent candidates before simulation. Canonicalize relabelings
   that preserve the law, the owners, the instruments and the record
   distributions. Use exact arithmetic, or SAT/SMT over a finite encoding,
   for admissible transitions and instrument branches, conservation where
   required, ownership, repeat-service memory, reset behaviour and schedule
   counterexamples. On the coherent branch verify unitarity and derive the
   one-body dispersion where it is available. Archive a proof or a minimal
   counterexample for each pruning rule. Exhaust small histories, including
   alternate legal schedules. A solver that reports exhaustion speaks for the
   specified grammar, bounds and histories.
3. Run survivors against held-out receiver observations. Vary size, starts,
   timing, numerical step and lawful alternative interactions. Save the
   smallest discriminating failure as a regression. Predeclare every
   mandatory gate separately, so that a good fit on one cannot hide a failure
   on another.
4. Choose experiments on which survivors disagree. Candidates that agree on
   every one-particle record stay together until such an experiment exists.
   To resolve an encounter phase, prepare two coherent alternatives in which
   the same two excitations meet a different number of times and recombine
   them at a local detector. The relative phase shifts the fringe as long as
   the paths stay indistinguishable to the detector. A separated-particle
   control tests propagation alone. Forecast the fringe positions before the
   counts are opened.
5. Spend computation on discriminating measurements. Order expensive runs by
   the expected difference in obtainable records relative to uncertainty and
   cost. An evolutionary or learned search can propose the next program. Keep
   a parallel lane of exact bounded enumeration with a coverage ledger. Store
   passes, contradictions, missing receipts, statistical ambiguity and
   timeouts separately.

**A cheap one-body ranking.** Measure $R$, the returned-to-rest beat ratio,
and $v$, the envelope speed, with different releases, and measure $c$ with a
third signal. Candidates whose $R^2+(v/c)^2$ approaches one under long-wave
refinement deserve the more exacting tests. A code that inserts this relation
into its clock update supplies the effect and cannot establish its
emergence.

## How far a result transfers within a family

Use one operational question and one scoring rule across families. Implement
the emitter, reflector, local clocks and receipt delivery with operations
available to each family. A vacancy pulse is one family's implementation of a
signal. A coherent exchange law can use a different physical preparation and
return the same four local timestamps or an explicit missing receipt.

Screen each family first on a small representative and a lawful alternative.
A representative's pass or failure applies to that specification and
preparation. A measurement extends to other members only with a proof that
they preserve the joint record distribution for the enrolled interventions,
or with a quantitative bound on its change strong enough to preserve the
particular verdict. The bound includes clock response, permitted schedules,
losses and missing records. A pure relabelling that also maps owners and
readouts preserves the result. A change to a memory reset, a relay or a clock
can change it. Use exact constraints to prune, reserve the full echo suite for
distinct survivors, and replay the coupled geometry and observable tests on
the exact frozen candidate claimed.

## When a family is retired

Retire a bounded family when every member contradicts a required,
independently readable observation. Expand it for a stated physical or
implementation reason and rerun the earlier failures. A contradiction that
follows from indispensable OPH premises across all admissible realizations
would warrant changing those premises. Small discriminating experiments come
before larger populations.
