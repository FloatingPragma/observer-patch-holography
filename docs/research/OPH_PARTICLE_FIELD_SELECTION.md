## Declared architecture and derived consequences

The construction contract permits a carrier, repair law, quantum control,
preparation and regulator to be specified. Their uniqueness is not a
prerequisite for calculating complete source-generated records. A transfer
claim between two constructions requires an explicit map of their actual
state, operators, clock and retained outputs. Architecture choices also do
not erase empirical calibration ancestry.

The [field guide](OPH_FIELD_RECONSTRUCTION.md) distinguishes the native
chiral source, collective block extensions, classical defect simulation,
prepared Dirac viewer and primary Wilson family. This page states which
choices carry the proofs. The [native join](OPH_NATIVE_PARTICLE_MASS_JOIN.md)
defines the spectral observables; the [mass guide](OPH_PARTICLE_MASS_RESPONSE.md)
specifies ratios, units and tested mass maps. None of these source programs
provides a source-derived Standard Model GeV mass.

models. Its physical field, species and comparison nodes require their stated

## Physical preparation and reference selection

For the fixed chiral source, all gauge sectors of the physical carrier enter
the ground comparison. A cyclic subspace reached from one seed is not by
itself a global-ground proof. The full local and lattice bounds, the
[charged-sector exclusion](../../model/coherent_defect_wave/chiral_joint/interacting_charged_response/PROOF.md)
and [physical preparation](../../model/coherent_defect_wave/chiral_joint/interacting_ground_preparation/PROOF.md)

Preparation failure is a physical outcome. A filter from the unrestricted
maximally mixed carrier can leave a nonphysical failure branch, whereas the
declared vacuum/algebraic protocol preserves Gauss on every branch. A charged
pump preserves joint source/reference Gauss, not source-only zero charge at
the reference site. The reference energy `4*N_ref` is part of the enlarged
Hamiltonian; merely declaring its vacuum would not prove that enlarged ground.

The transport experiment's P0 localization is an explicit intervention after
ground preparation. It must remain in its success/failure law. Its odd packet
is not described as untouched evolution from the stationary interacting
ground. Its energy and arrival settings share that same preparation while
measuring different, potentially noncommuting effects.

For coupled neutral blocks, the number-conserving architecture has an exact
product ground with a scalable preparation error. Quadrature pairing has a
different finite-capacity reference. Its full-source variational comparison
gives a selected symmetric ground and an explicit error relative to a Gaussian
reference, without a uniqueness or uniform finite-N gap theorem. A stationary
comparison state cannot be replaced by a convenient free packet without
retaining the preparation and comparison error.

The primary Wilson polynomial state, an exact Gibbs state, the ordered-spin
ground and a corrected-memory stationary state retain distinct specifications.
On a supported finite density rho, `-log(rho)` defines a modular operator.
Identifying its parameter with H time requires the actual state/H relation;
stationarity alone does not give `rho proportional exp(-beta*H)`.

## Which selection tests constrain a mass model

Complete finite response can reject a specified mechanism without excluding
other admitted architectures. The invariant shared-feature candidate, a
chosen projector-feedback law and a corrected-memory cycle each have their
own mass map and input ancestry. The
[hierarchy diagnosis](OPH_PARTICLE_MASS_RESPONSE.md#hierarchy-diagnosis)
retains those mathematical restrictions.

The three characters of a neutral C3 register do not supply three
independently occupiable fermion families. Those require the declared
`F(K tensor C3)` algebra, rather than `F(K) tensor C3`. Nor does an XOR
probability alone determine a coherent phase. A controller can declare that
coupling and prove its consequence, while retaining the controller and clock
as architecture inputs.

Cooling, dissipation and coherent Hamiltonian filtering have different
channels. For a rank-three projector P,
`Phi(rho)=P rho P+tr((I-P)rho)P/3` has dual
`Phi*(B)=P B P+tr(PB)(I-P)/3`. It is not the compression `B -> PBP` and
can erase the phases needed by a proposed triplet field. Equal populations
also do not fix equal quantum records or reference states.

The field-selection task is therefore a concrete one: fix the actual H,
parameter rule, prepared reference, current-preserving probe and clock;
derive its complete response; then test the physical comparison under one
mass convention. A target-selected parameter is a calibrated input even if
the inverse decoder sees only records. Failure of one law is not an error
bar that can be absorbed by an unspecified self-energy correction.

## Minimal construction choices at the charged boundary

The fixed source proves positive arrival and strong low-bin single-charge
localization. Its first unresolved observable is the energy-resolved
spatial measure with the retained cloud outcome. If the intended target is
a Coulomb charged particle, declare the electric/magnetic source that is
intended to support it and establish its own coupled physical ground and
charged dressing. The measured charge in the finite proof is native
hypercharge; an unbroken electromagnetic generator and its coupling require
their actual electroweak construction.

The exact variational identity for a quadratic electric comparison is
`min_(D E=rho) a_E||E||^2/2 = a_E rho^T(DD^T)^+rho/2` for neutral rho on
a connected graph. Its distributed-flux cost differs from counting nonblank
links. A physical inverse-distance limit, quantum transverse modes and the
coupled charged threshold are additional calculations on that changed H.
The existing finite Green and Whitney packages retain their own geometry,
reference and clock until a source transfer is proved.

For the neutral block route, the decisive interacting-limit requirement is
a nonzero connected scattering response under an explicit common refinement.
For a Standard Model comparison, the decisive identification is a populated
physical current/spin channel with a controlled threshold or resonance and
an independent unit scale. These are precise source calculations, not a
requirement to prove the uniqueness of the chosen architecture.
