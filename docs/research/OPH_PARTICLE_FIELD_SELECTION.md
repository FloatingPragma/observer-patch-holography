prerequisite for calculating complete source-generated records. A transfer

defines the spectral observables; the [mass guide](OPH_PARTICLE_MASS_RESPONSE.md)
specifies ratios, units and tested mass maps. None of these source programs
provides a source-derived Standard Model GeV mass.

## Which selection tests constrain a mass model

Complete finite response can reject a specified mechanism without excluding
other admitted architectures. The invariant shared-feature candidate, a
chosen projector-feedback law and a corrected-memory cycle each have their
own mass map and input ancestry. The
[hierarchy diagnosis](OPH_PARTICLE_MASS_RESPONSE.md#hierarchy-diagnosis)
retains those mathematical restrictions.

The three characters of a neutral C3 register do not supply three
independently occupiable fermion families. Those require the declared
`F(K tensor C3)` algebra, rather than `F(K) tensor C3`. Nor does an XOR
probability alone determine a coherent phase. A controller can declare that
coupling and prove its consequence, while retaining the controller and clock
as architecture inputs.

Cooling, dissipation and coherent Hamiltonian filtering have different
channels. For a rank-three projector P,
`Phi(rho)=P rho P+tr((I-P)rho)P/3` has dual
`Phi*(B)=P B P+tr(PB)(I-P)/3`. It is not the compression `B -> PBP` and
can erase the phases needed by a proposed triplet field. Equal populations
also do not fix equal quantum records or reference states.

The field-selection task is therefore a concrete one: fix the actual H,
parameter rule, prepared reference, current-preserving probe and clock;
derive its complete response; then test the physical comparison under one
mass convention. A target-selected parameter is a calibrated input even if
the inverse decoder sees only records. Failure of one law is not an error
bar that can be absorbed by an unspecified self-energy correction.
