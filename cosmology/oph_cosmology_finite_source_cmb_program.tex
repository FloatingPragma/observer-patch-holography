\begin{abstract}
A supplied layered event population represents a spatially flat expanding
background through its count measure: the scale factor contributes a fourth
power per comoving cell and conformal tick. Count ratios recover redshift
for congruent constant-profile windows, or in a shrinking-window limit.
We connect this exact construction to conditional primordial source models.
A specified collar release fixes a screen amplitude; a specified generator
density fixes the tilt; physical dilation or complete radial cross-covariances
can determine a three-dimensional spectrum. Native load records provide
absolute covariance controls but no geometric curvature identification.
The source, stress, clock, radial observation and photon-transfer maps must
therefore be supplied on the same physical process. A numerical value of
the pixel parameter does not provide those maps. Retrospective cosmological
comparisons test the declared branches, not a completed source-derived
cosmology.

\end{abstract}


\section{Finite-Source Interface}

Let \(X_r\) denote a finite observer-screen state at refinement level \(r\).

\begin{equation}
\mathsf S_r(X_r)
=\bigl(g_{\mu\nu},T_{\mu\nu},\mathcal I,\mathcal B\bigr)_r,
\label{eq:source-map}
\end{equation}
where \(T_{\mu\nu}\) is conserved, \(\mathcal I\) contains gauge-invariant
initial data, and \(\mathcal B\) states the background and boundary conditions.
The map must be defined without using the observed sky quantities that its
outputs are compared with.

The complete one-shell covariance determines the three-dimensional correlation
only for chord lengths \(0\le s\le2R_\star\). The resulting radial kernel is
infinite-dimensional and persists under positivity. Two sufficient routes
for a source-derived lift are a scale-natural embedding that transports
refinement to physical log-wavenumber dilation and complete radial
cross-covariances supporting spherical-Hankel tomography. Each route has its
own hypotheses. The physical source map must also specify
the radial measure, null-space treatment, clock, normalization, and non-fitting
forward residual. No finite source construction satisfying these conditions is
supplied here. Its source-dependency graph must be finite and acyclic.
This graph is a scientific prerequisite graph among stress, clock, scale,
radial, transfer, and likelihood objects. It is distinct from the semantic
read-from order of the consensus layer. If its nodes are instantiated by
semantic commits, a declared mapping and complete certified read supports can
make the generated informational order verify their execution dependencies.
The provenance theorem alone derives neither these scientific nodes nor their
prerequisite edges.
The rank-three source Gram metric is a finite readout of one carrier's slow
response, not an extended space; on the refining source screens the
rank-three band leaves bounded-energy port modes at order \(2^{-l}\), and
native dependence crosses at most one seam per ordered repair event, which
fixes no laboratory speed or continuum light
cone~\cite{ophmodel,ophcontinuum,ophcausality}. A cone-selection theorem
forces a Lorentzian cone under operational icosahedral and boost covariance
of histories, interventions and clock readouts, and the conservative
Fibonacci-record family realizes a flat \(1+3\) causal-order, count-volume
and count-clock limit for a specified population and read law, with

The following finite construction realizes a supplied spatially flat

\subsection{Native records and geometric identification}
\label{subsec:native-cosmology-source-boundary}
\label{subsec:native-geometric-source-identification}

\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(z(t),z(0))=\kappa e^{-|t|L/2}\Pi,
 \quad \kappa=\frac{r(p-r)}{p(p-1)},\quad
 \Pi=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/p.
\]
Here \(t\) counts unit-rate seam attempts. A normalized eigenmode of the
ordinary average \(T^{-1}\int_0^Tz(t)\,dt\) has variance
\(2\kappa(u-1+e^{-u})/u^2\), \(u=\lambda T/2\); the fluctuation record
\(T^{-1/2}\int_0^Tz(t)\,dt\) instead has limiting covariance
\(4\kappa L^+\). Incident pre-attempt records, including null attempts,
have the same leading term with correction bounded by
\(3\kappa/d_{\min}\)~\cite{ophmicro}.

On refining \(240\cdot4^l\)-port screens at one-third filling, uniform
fixed-number preparation and independent fair seam attempts give joint

\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(z_\tau(f),z_u(g))
 =\langle f,e^{-|\tau-u|A}g\rangle.

\subsection{Exact Fourier control for the golden source population}
The deterministic Cartesian population
\(x_b=\mathcal L(\{b_1\varphi\},\{b_2\varphi\},\{b_3\varphi\})\),
\(0\le b_i<q\), has an exact fixed-cube Fourier response. Here
\(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), \(\mathcal L\) is the declared cube size,
and the integer mode \(n\) has wave vector \(2\pi n/\mathcal L\).
Its normalized amplitude factors as
\[
 F_q(n)=q^{-3}\sum_b e^{2\pi i n\cdot x_b/\mathcal L}
       =\prod_{j=1}^3 a_q(n_j),\qquad a_q(0)=1,
\]
\[
 a_q(m)=e^{\pi i m\varphi(q-1)}
       \frac{\sin(\pi mq\varphi)}{q\sin(\pi m\varphi)}
       \quad(m\ne0).
\]
This follows by separating the three finite sums, using integer-mode
periodicity to remove fractional parts, and evaluating each geometric
series. For \(q=F_j\), the Fibonacci identity
\(\varphi F_j-F_{j+1}=(-1)^{j+1}\varphi^{-j}\), with
\(F_j\varphi^{-j}\to1/\sqrt5\), gives for fixed \(n\)
with \(h\) nonzero coordinates
\[
 q^{4h}|F_q(n)|^2\longrightarrow
 \prod_{n_a\ne0}\frac{(\pi n_a)^2}{5\sin^2(\pi n_a\varphi)}.
\]
The denominator is nonzero by irrationality. The small-angle limit of
each numerator proves the rate. It concerns fixed integer modes in a
fixed cube. It is not an infinite-volume hyperuniformity result or a
primordial tilt. Finite directional power ratios can be large while both
powers vanish; some finite modes remain above the independent-uniform
structure-factor reference. The exact formula, independent small-cloud
checks and the retained numerical receipt are

amplitude is selected by this supplied coordinate population. The Lean

analytical, not a claimed formal limit theorem.

\section{Dark-Sector Interface}

The dark-matter paper proposes anomalous modular charge on overlap collars.
For a conserved total charge \(Q_A\) in a comoving region of physical volume
\(V_0a^3\), its homogeneous density satisfies
\begin{equation}
\rho_A=\frac{Q_A}{V_0a^3}\propto a^{-3}.
\label{eq:dust-branch}
\end{equation}
Under the separate homogeneous continuity equation with no exchange, this
dilution is the pressureless background law. Deep-regime scale covariance
and quadrature composition characterize the static spherical acceleration
law and baryonic Tully--Fisher scaling.

Equation~\eqref{eq:dust-branch} does not determine the abundance, initial
perturbations, relativistic stress, gravitational slip, sound speed, nonlinear
evolution, or coupling to the visible sector. A cosmological use requires a
covariant completion that supplies these quantities from one common source and
obeys the full energy-momentum ledger. That completion is not supplied here.

\section{Primordial source and conditional comparisons}
\label{sec:public-comparison}
The source must provide conserved stress, a common clock and release
surface, scalar/vector/tensor initial data, radial observation and physical
scale, and controlled refinement. Neither the value of \(P\) nor a finite
gauge-current algebra determines these objects. In particular, a conditional
particle/memory fixed point does not derive the separate cosmological
generator density \(P/24\).

On the declared density branch, \(n_s=1-P_\star/48\) gives
\(0.96602\) at the comparison pixel \(P_\star=1.6309682094\), which is
located from the measured fine-structure constant. The continuous survival
cocycle alone admits every exponent, so this numerical density is a
substantive premise. At that value the retrospective position in the
ACT DR6, Planck, lensing and DESI 2024 BAO joint posterior is the lower
\(0.612\) percent tail; the same value is within one standard deviation of
Planck 2018 and the two cited SPT combinations. These are seen-data
comparisons under baseline \(\Lambda\)CDM, not likelihood ratios or a
selection of the physical source~\cite{act2025,ophpublicdata}.
The complete comparisons, including background tensions, are given in the
likelihood companion~\cite{ophcontracts}.

A single-clock Gaussian source has no independent source isocurvature or
connected higher Gaussian cumulants, and a fixed dilation exponent has
zero running. Tensor zero needs an extra condition: no tensor forcing alone
permits homogeneous solutions. For example,
\(h_k''+2h_k'/\tau+k^2h_k=0\) admits
\(h_k=\sin(k\tau)/(k\tau)\). Vanishing initial amplitude and velocity,
together with zero forcing, select the zero linear solution. A finite
observational upper bound excludes only alternatives above that bound;
single-field slow roll has no universal positive tensor floor.

\section{Transfer Interface}
\label{sec:transfer-interface}

Given a completed source map, the transfer calculation must declare the
background species, equations of state, collision terms, recombination model,
initial modes, gauge convention, numerical tolerances, and observable
projection. The finite particle comparison computes no particle abundance or
early-Universe population distribution, so those background species densities are
imported~\cite{ophparticles}. An equation of state needs its own strain response:
identical fixed-volume records admit \(H_\alpha(V)=Q(V/V_0)^{-\alpha}\) with
\(p/\rho=\alpha\), and an additive energy offset changes \(p/\rho\) while leaving
pressure unchanged~\cite{ophobservers}.
Standard Einstein--Boltzmann software can test this interface with
imported inputs. Such a run tests the solver path rather than an OPH source
theorem.

The minimum transfer outputs are
\begin{equation}
\bigl(C_\ell^{TT},C_\ell^{TE},C_\ell^{EE},C_L^{\phi\phi},
P_m(k,z),H(z),D_A(z),f\sigma_8(z)\bigr).
\end{equation}
All outputs must arise from one compatible background and perturbation system.

\section{Likelihood Interface}

A data comparison must state the datasets, masks, covariances, nuisance
parameters, priors, and combination rule. Overlapping samples and shared
calibrations must be represented in the covariance model. Visual overlays,
diagonal residual sums, and comparisons made after selecting a formula are

The cosmic microwave background (CMB), galaxy, and background comparisons are
diagnostic. These computations supply neither a prospectively fixed
cosmological comparison nor a falsification verdict.
