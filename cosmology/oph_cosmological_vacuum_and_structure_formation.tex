\begin{abstract}
A finite quotient, a reference measure and an action determine an observer
ensemble. A vacuum interpretation needs additional reversible Euclidean
data and compatible refinement. We give the corresponding finite transfer
construction and separate it from stationary repair and from supplied
Hamiltonian ground states. Native density records have a reflection-positive
Gaussian reconstruction generated by the screen operator \(A\), while the
full return reference approaches the normalized trace. An active free field
has vacuum covariance \((2\sqrt B)^{-1}\) under its declared preparation;
its flat massless spectrum grows as \(k^2\) per logarithmic interval and
therefore is not a scale-invariant primordial curvature spectrum.
Structure formation requires an expanding background, a curvature readout,
and transfer dynamics on the same state. The capacity relation
\(\Lambda\ell_P^2=3\pi/N\) is a conditional horizon-entropy dictionary,
not an independent selection of the cosmological constant.

\end{abstract}


\section{Ensemble, vacuum and background}
A probability law on a finite physical quotient is distinct from a
Hamiltonian vacuum and from cosmological initial data. The native source
screen has a positive continuum operator of spectral dimension two;
its construction supplies no expanding background or physical
screen-to-volume map~\cite{ophmodel,ophspine,ophcontinuum}.

If the capacity \(N\) is identified with de Sitter horizon entropy in
nats, then
\[
 \Lambda\ell_P^2=\frac{3\pi}{N}.
\]
This is the entropy relation read backwards; it determines \(\Lambda\)
only after \(N\) and the area interpretation are supplied. For a separately
conserved vacuum component with \(\rho\propto N^{-1}\), the continuity
equation gives \(w=-1+\tfrac13d\log N/d\log a\). Constant \(N\) gives
\(w=-1\); nondecreasing \(N\) gives \(w\ge-1\). Neither relation chooses
\(N\). This \(w\) is the effective-fluid quantity of the continuity equation:
consensus about identical fixed-volume records cannot select an unobserved strain
derivative, since \(H_\alpha(V)=Q(V/V_0)^{-\alpha}\) gives \(p/\rho=\alpha\). Three
geometrically spaced energy readings identify the response within a family with
\(\alpha\ge0\), which excludes \(w=-1\) at positive energy, and an additive energy
offset changes \(p/\rho\) while leaving pressure unchanged~\cite{ophobservers}.
Native regional entropy grows with port count, whereas mutual
information per crossing seam vanishes, so the whole-screen horizon reading
needs a region map~\cite{ophentropy}. Target-informed capacity formulas
and their retrospective data comparisons belong to the likelihood
companion~\cite{ophcontracts}. A smooth Einstein interpretation also needs
the common-stress and tensor-curvature or small-ball identification
premises~\cite{ophEinstein}.

\section{Matter and States on a Supplied Spatial Domain}

The Whitney construction on the fixed tetrahedral cone gives a controlled
matter continuum in a specified sector of one charged scalar action. For
\(m^2>0\), \(g\geq0\), a supplied
\(u\in C^2([0,T];H^2(\Omega))\) solving the real cubic Klein--Gordon
equation with natural Neumann boundary, and Ritz-projected initial data,
uniform conforming refinements give
\[
 \sup_{0\leq t\leq T}
 \bigl(\|u_n-u\|_{H^1}+\|\dot u_n-u_t\|_{L^2}\bigr)
 \leq C_T n^{-1}.
\]
The real, zero-current solutions are invariant solutions of the full
charged action. The proof uses a fixed shape-regular refinement family,
the conserved nonlinear energy and a Ritz error estimate, as proved in
\emph{Observers Are All You Need}~\cite{ophobservers}.

On the original finite mesh with nonzero coupling \(e\), eliminating
mean-zero gauge directions gives a
complete kinetic metric \(\gamma\) on
\(\mathcal Q\cong\mathbb R^{30}\times\mathbb C^{13}\). With supplied
\(\hbar>0\) and Laplace--Beltrami ordering, the neutral subspace of
\(L^2(\mathcal Q,d\mathrm{vol}_\gamma)\) has a unique nonnegative
self-adjoint Hamiltonian and unitary dynamics. Normalized Gaussian
half-density states lie in its operator domain and have explicit initial
matter moments~\cite{ophobservers}. These
are states of a finite spatial discretization, not a derived cosmological
ground state or a continuum quantum field limit.

For the same autonomous action, the Jacobi duration
\[
 d\tau=\sqrt{\frac{G_q(dq,dq)}{2(E-V(q))}}
\]
recovers action time on regular nonturning solutions at supplied energy
\(E\), using the full coupled kinetic metric or its gauge
reduction~\cite{ophobservers}. Its
configuration-based reconstruction supplies an internal model clock.
A cosmological interpretation additionally requires observer-selected
geometry, physical preparation and units, and a compatible expanding
background. Those attachments are separate from these matter and state
theorems.

The golden source population of the spacetime construction carries a second
matter and state construction on the same population that the causal order
uses. Its positive source-metric scalar action has a finite-time continuum
limit with an explicit detector error bound, a canonical quantization whose
quadratic Hamiltonian has a unique normalized ground state, the original
action vacuum, and a bounded probability comparison between the split
evolution of that vacuum with a coherent displacement and the continuous
evolution of the same finite action, with all sixty-four modes retained at
\(q=5\). The joint spatial and temporal refinement of one compact detector is
certified at \(q=233\), with total error below \(0.0501\) against a resolved
response above \(0.1377\). This is a vacuum state of a finite scalar action on
the source population, with the population, action and tick supplied. It is
neither a reflection-positive Euclidean vacuum of a cosmological release law
nor a source-instantiated triple \((J_r,V_r,a_{t,r})\) in the sense of the
next section, and it carries no expanding background.

On the same family a spatially flat expanding background is a record
density: the scale factor enters only as the weight \(a^4\) per comoving
cell and conformal tick, and the redshift is the fourth root of a
record-count ratio of congruent comoving windows with constant scale factor
within each, or in the shrinking-window limit of a continuous profile. The
finite-source
cosmology paper proves these statements with their Lean
module~\cite{ophcmb}. The record-production profile is a supplied datum; the
vacuum and fluctuation conditions below concern the field on that background
and do not select the profile.

\section{Finite Quotient Ensemble Theorem}

Let \(Q_r=\Sigma_r/\Gamma_r\) be a finite physical quotient and
\(n_r:Q_r\to N_r\) a retraction onto settled states. Then
\((n_r)_\#^2=(n_r)_\#\), and every law supported on \(N_r\) is fixed.
Settlement alone chooses no ensemble. A declared positive base weight
\(m_r\) and action \(S_r\), finite somewhere, instead give
\[
 \mu_r(q)=\frac{m_r(q)e^{-S_r(q)}}{Z_r},\qquad
 Z_r=\sum_qm_r(q)e^{-S_r(q)}>0.
\]
A prior pushed through \(n_r\) agrees with this law only when its
normal-form fibre sums equal \(\mu_r\). Uniform sampling of representatives
weights quotient classes by their multiplicities; a conditional lift must
instead use \(\widetilde\mu(x)=\mu(\pi x)\kappa(x\mid\pi x)\), with
unit conditional mass on each fibre.

\begin{theorem}[Finite selection and refinement]
For quotient observables \(F_a\) with a feasible full-support expectation
constraint, relative entropy maximization yields
\(\mu(q)\propto m(q)e^{-\sum_a\theta_aF_a(q)}\).
For a faithful quantum reference \(\sigma\), the analogous state is
\(\rho\propto\exp(\log\sigma-\sum_a\theta_aF_a)\).
On a proper joint support \(P\), compress \(\log\sigma\) before
exponentiating; \(\log(P\sigma P)\) cannot replace that compression
without a commutation hypothesis.

For compatible coarse maps \(c_{sr}:Q_s\to Q_r\), exact refinement
consistency is equivalent to
\[
 \sum_{c_{sr}(q')=q}m_s(q')e^{-S_s(q')}
 =\alpha_{sr}m_r(q)e^{-S_r(q)},\qquad \alpha_{sr}>0.
\]
One-step total-variation defects \(\delta_{k+1,k}\) accumulate by at most
\(\sum_{k=r}^{n-1}\delta_{k+1,k}\). For a bounded observable \(O\),
sampling error \(\epsilon_{\rm samp}\) and total refinement error
\(\epsilon_{\rm ref}\) give expectation error at most
\(2\|O\|_\infty(\epsilon_{\rm samp}+\epsilon_{\rm ref})\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The multiplier equations and strict concavity give the entropy maximizers.
Normalizing the fibre sums proves the equivalence; contractivity of pushforward
in total variation and the triangle inequality give the accumulated bound.
The expectation estimate is the dual bound for total variation.
\end{proof}

A tracially pointed gauge quotient additionally fixes representation
multiplicities: if the chosen sector is
\(\bigoplus_\alpha V_\alpha\otimes M_\alpha\), its central weights are
\(d_\alpha m_\alpha/\sum_\beta d_\beta m_\beta\). These weights are
invariant under trace-preserving intertwiners and inert trivial ancillas,
not arbitrary changes of representation multiplicity. A representative
Markov kernel descends only if its transition sums to each quotient fibre
are independent of the input representative. An asymmetric proposal \(R\) uses acceptance
\(\min\{1,w(q')R(q',q)/[w(q)R(q,q')]\}\), with
\(w=me^{-S}\), to sample the specified law. If a coarse conditional
free energy differs from the claimed exponential family by at most
\(\varepsilon\) uniformly, normalization bounds its total-variation
defect by \(\tanh\varepsilon\).

For \(L_{\rm rep}=\sum_Cc_C(I-E_C)\), let
\(\mathcal F=\bigcap_C\operatorname{Ran}E_C\). If \(c_C\ge\gamma_*>0\),
\[
 L_{\rm rep}\ge\gamma_*\kappa(I-P_{\mathcal F}),\qquad
 \kappa=\inf_{0\ne f\perp\mathcal F}
 \frac{\sum_C\|(I-E_C)f\|^2}{\|f\|^2}>0
\]
when \(\mathcal F^\perp\ne0\). Finite-dimensional compactness proves
positivity. Replacing \(\mathcal F\) by constants needs ergodicity;
a bound uniform in refinement is separate. The expectations
contain a specified reference law, and multiplying all rates changes the clock and
gap without selecting that law.

\begin{theorem}[Finite Euclidean vacuum]
Supply \((Q_r,m_r^0,J_r,V_r,a_{t,r})\), with \(m_r^0>0\), a connected
graph of symmetric nonnegative conductances \(J_r\), a real potential
\(V_r\) and \(a_{t,r}>0\). On \(L^2(Q_r,m_r^0)\),
\[
 (H_r^Ef)(q)=\frac1{m_r^0(q)}\sum_{q'}J_r(q,q')[f(q)-f(q')]+V_r(q)f(q)
\]
has a unique positive normalized ground state \(\Omega_r\). Its law is
\(\mu_r^{\rm vac}=|\Omega_r|^2m_r^0\); conjugating
\(e^{-a_{t,r}(H_r^E-E_{0,r})}\) by \(\Omega_r\) gives a stochastic
kernel reversible for this law.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The symmetric conductance form is nonnegative and the finite potential
is bounded below. Connectedness makes the finite Feynman--Kac semigroup
positivity improving. Perron--Frobenius gives the ground state; its
eigenvector equation proves the row-sum identity for the conjugated kernel.
Self-adjointness proves detailed balance.
\end{proof}
The Euclidean data must be selected independently of the desired vacuum
law. Otherwise a positive transfer operator can be constructed around any
faithful target distribution. A continuum vacuum further requires
reflection positivity or an equivalent reconstruction and compatible
transfer refinement.

\paragraph{Native source screens.}
On the native source screens the actual density law has the
reflection-positive reconstruction that a continuum interpretation requires,
along one common refining subsequence; it supplies no coherent preparation,

supply~\cite{ophcontinuum}.

\section{Vacuum Claim Boundary}
\label{sec:vacuum-claim-boundary}

A simulated field is classified as seed noise, repair jitter, or a conventional
reference ensemble unless it satisfies the finite quotient ensemble
conditions. An OPH-native vacuum requires source data of the form
\[
\mathfrak S_r^E=(Q_r,m_r^0,J_r,V_r,a_{t,r})
\]
with \(J_r\), \(V_r\), and \(a_{t,r}\) derived from OPH source structure rather than from a target
law or a desired visual spectrum. Directed accepted repair moves cannot be
reused as reversible Euclidean conductances. The native seam rule differs:
each unread repair has the law of a fair identity-or-endpoint swap, so its
configuration transfer \(K\) is symmetric and positive, and the actual native
density law has a reflection-positive Gaussian reconstruction. The
obstruction on the native source lies elsewhere: the reconstructed
one-particle generator is the
continuum operator \(A\), not \(\sqrt A\), and the full configuration return
reference built from \(K\) approaches the normalized trace at fixed modular
parameter and gives the density-wave sector vanishing weight, so it
identifies no quantum vacuum~\cite{ophtimefields}.

\subsection{Vacuum and initial-state readouts on the effective product}

The active product construction has a different, conditional state contract.
With its positive spatial operator \(B\), canonical fields and supplied
inverse temperature \(b\), its ordered free-field two-point function is
\[
 C_b(t)=\frac{\coth(b\sqrt B/2)\cos(t\sqrt B)
                   -i\sin(t\sqrt B)}{2\sqrt B}.
\]
The limit \(b\to\infty\) gives the static ground-state covariance
\((2\sqrt B)^{-1}\); finite \(b\) changes fluctuations while preserving the
same commutator and response. These are joint observer-readout predictions
of the declared free-field family, not the passive native density law or
the interacting field's covariance~\cite{ophtimefields}.
The radial apparatus, state preparation and physical clock map remain inputs.

On a finite algebra with two invariant sectors, restricted thermal
equilibrium leaves a central sector weight:
\[
 \rho_w=w\frac{e^{-bH_+}}{Z_+}\oplus
        (1-w)\frac{e^{-bH_-}}{Z_-},\qquad 0<w<1.
\]
Every such state has the same restricted modular flow. Cooling each block
does not select \(w\). The full Gibbs preparation additionally chooses
\(w=Z_+/(Z_++Z_-)\); its global ground-state limit is therefore a stronger
state choice. Admitted record statistics can test sector populations,
but do not identify an unobserved purification.

A concrete interacting preparation is available on the 90 localized CAR
modes of the common defect/current construction. Its Hamiltonian
\(H=H_{\rm pair}+\lambda(N_W-45)^2\), \(\lambda\ge0\),
preserves parity and has a faithful finite Gibbs state
\(\rho_\beta=e^{-\beta H}/Z\) for \(\beta>0\). A global spectral reset
prepares this state. For \(H_s=H+sX\) at fixed \(\beta\), let
\(E_s=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_sH_s)\) and
\(S_s=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_s\log\rho_s)\). Derivatives at \(s=0\) obey
\[
 \rho_\beta'=-\beta\int_0^1\rho_\beta^t
 (X-\langle X\rangle)\rho_\beta^{1-t}\,dt,
 \qquad S'=\beta(E'-\langle X\rangle).
\]
This is a chosen preparation on the same interacting Hamiltonian, not a
cosmological vacuum or a geometric stress variation. It does not select
sector weights from native repair, an expanding background or a physical
clock~\cite{ophinteracting}.

A direct cosmological identification has a discriminating control.
In the flat massless limit, with \(\hbar=c=1\) and
\(\langle\phi_{\mathbf k}\phi_{\mathbf k'}\rangle
=(2\pi)^3\delta(\mathbf k+\mathbf k')P_\phi(k)\), the static vacuum gives
\[
 P_\phi(k)=\frac1{2k},\qquad
 \Delta_\phi^2(k)=\frac{k^3P_\phi(k)}{2\pi^2}
                  =\frac{k^2}{4\pi^2}.
\]
Thus a scale-independent identification of this field with primordial
curvature does not reproduce the nearly scale-invariant curvature spectrum
inferred from CMB data~\cite{planckinflation}. This flat-limit control is
not a Fourier-spectrum claim for the bounded native radial product.
An expanding evolution and an operational curvature map must supply the
transfer before the state can predict cosmological observations. The
initial-state target is consequently a law for accessible earlier-state
correlations and their later records, not an assumed observable absolute
beginning of the Universe.

\section{From a state to structure formation}
The state constructions above do not fix a primordial curvature field.
A complete cosmological model must supply the source Euclidean data or an
equivalent physical preparation, the expanding geometry and clock, and the
map from its accessible field to conserved stress and curvature. A screen
covariance alone leaves an infinite-dimensional radial null space;
physical source dilation or complete radial cross-covariances can provide
uniqueness under their additional hypotheses~\cite{ophcmb}. The resulting
initial data must then enter one compatible matter, photon and metric
transfer system and a stated observational likelihood. Quotient invariance,
stationarity of a sampler or visually coherent trajectories establishes none
of these physical identifications by itself.
