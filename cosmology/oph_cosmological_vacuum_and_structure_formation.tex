\begin{abstract}
We specify finite quotient ensembles and transfer conditions for
Observer-Patch Holography (OPH) cosmology. A declared quotient-intrinsic action
and base measure determine a finite state, while reversible Euclidean data,
reflection positivity and compatible refinement support its vacuum
interpretation. On a supplied Whitney action, controlled real-field
trajectories and a neutral interacting Hilbert space provide explicit matter
and state constructions, and we state the further source and transfer
conditions that connect such objects to primordial seeds, observer-facing
worldlines and large-scale structure observables. The source screens of the
executable observer construction test these conditions: their native
density law has a reflection-positive reconstruction whose generator is the
native continuum operator, and the full return reference approaches the
normalized trace, so no quantum vacuum is identified. On the sourced causal
order the expansion history is a record density. The capacity dictionary
\(\Lambda\ell_P^2=3\pi/N\), the de Sitter horizon entropy read backwards,
returns, as diagnostics of two target-informed
closure candidates, the cosmological constant \(+0.62\) and \(+0.38\)
percent above the Planck central value, while the candidates sit \(-2.64\)
and \(-2.81\) standard deviations below the DESI DR2 display of the same
coordinate; under a Gaussian moment diagnostic the fixed-capacity equation
of state \(w=-1\) sits \(2.6\) to \(3.9\) standard deviations from the
DESI DR2 posterior means.
\end{abstract}

\section*{This paper's hypothesis and place in the tower}

This paper sits at layer seven and develops the vacuum and structure-seed
branch. Its hypothesis is that the cosmological vacuum is the refinement
limit of a finite quotient ensemble whose reversible Euclidean data come
from the source itself, that structure grows from the fluctuations of that
vacuum through an expanding background, and that the vacuum term of the
background is fixed by a screen capacity through \(\Lambda\ell_P^2=3\pi/N\).

The finite quotient ensemble theorems and the matter and state constructions
on a supplied spatial domain are proved on their stated domains; the flat
massless vacuum control and the sector-weight freedom of restricted thermal
states are exact. The vacuum interpretation, the seed bridge and the
capacity reading are conditional: the conductances, potential and slab
thickness, the expanding background, the physical clock and the region map
behind an area reading of \(N\) are premises.

For the branch to apply, the executable model must derive the Euclidean
source data \((J_r,V_r,a_{t,r})\) from one native process, prepare a coherent
state on it, and supply the expanding background, clock calibration and
screen-to-volume transfer. The native construction constrains this join: its
unread repair is a fair swap, so its density law has a reflection-positive
reconstruction, but the reconstructed one-particle generator is the
continuum operator \(A\) rather than \(\sqrt A\), and the full return
reference approaches the normalized trace, so no quantum vacuum is
identified (Section~\ref{sec:vacuum-claim-boundary}). The branch is refuted
by a proof that no admissible source data give a reflection-positive vacuum
with a relativistic one-particle generator; its fixed-capacity background is
refuted if the frozen equation-of-state protocol excludes
\((w_0,w_a)=(-1,0)\) on its eligible data.

\section{Matter and States on a Supplied Spatial Domain}

The Whitney construction on the fixed tetrahedral cone gives a controlled
matter continuum in a specified sector of one charged scalar action. For
\(m^2>0\), \(g\geq0\), a supplied
\(u\in C^2([0,T];H^2(\Omega))\) solving the real cubic Klein--Gordon
equation with natural Neumann boundary, and Ritz-projected initial data,
uniform conforming refinements give
\[
 \sup_{0\leq t\leq T}
 \bigl(\|u_n-u\|_{H^1}+\|\dot u_n-u_t\|_{L^2}\bigr)
 \leq C_T n^{-1}.
\]
The real, zero-current solutions are invariant solutions of the full
charged action. The proof uses a fixed shape-regular refinement family,
the conserved nonlinear energy and a Ritz error estimate, as proved in
\emph{Observers Are All You Need}~\cite{ophobservers}.

On the original finite mesh with nonzero coupling \(e\), eliminating
mean-zero gauge directions gives a
complete kinetic metric \(\gamma\) on
\(\mathcal Q\cong\mathbb R^{30}\times\mathbb C^{13}\). With supplied
\(\hbar>0\) and Laplace--Beltrami ordering, the neutral subspace of
\(L^2(\mathcal Q,d\mathrm{vol}_\gamma)\) has a unique nonnegative
self-adjoint Hamiltonian and unitary dynamics. Normalized Gaussian
half-density states lie in its operator domain and have explicit initial
matter moments~\cite{ophobservers}. These
are states of a finite spatial discretization, not a derived cosmological
ground state or a continuum quantum field limit.

For the same autonomous action, the Jacobi duration
\[
 d\tau=\sqrt{\frac{G_q(dq,dq)}{2(E-V(q))}}
\]
recovers action time on regular nonturning solutions at supplied energy
\(E\), using the full coupled kinetic metric or its gauge
reduction~\cite{ophobservers}. Its
configuration-based reconstruction supplies an internal model clock.
A cosmological interpretation additionally requires observer-selected
geometry, physical preparation and units, and a compatible expanding

The golden source population of the spacetime construction carries a second

\section{Vacuum Claim Boundary}
\label{sec:vacuum-claim-boundary}

A simulated field is classified as seed noise, repair jitter, or a conventional
reference ensemble unless it satisfies the finite quotient ensemble
conditions. An OPH-native vacuum requires source data of the form
\[
\mathfrak S_r^E=(Q_r,m_r^0,J_r,V_r,a_{t,r})
\]
with \(J_r\), \(V_r\), and \(a_{t,r}\) derived from OPH source structure rather than from a target
law or a desired visual spectrum. Directed accepted repair moves cannot be
reused as reversible Euclidean conductances. The native seam rule differs:
each unread repair has the law of a fair identity-or-endpoint swap, so its
configuration transfer \(K\) is symmetric and positive, and the actual native

continuum operator \(A\), not \(\sqrt A\), and the full configuration return
reference built from \(K\) approaches the normalized trace at fixed modular
parameter and gives the density-wave sector vanishing weight, so it
identifies no quantum vacuum~\cite{ophtimefields}.

\subsection{Vacuum and initial-state readouts on the effective product}

The active product construction has a different, conditional state contract.
With its positive spatial operator \(B\), canonical fields and supplied
inverse temperature \(b\), its ordered free-field two-point function is
\[
 C_b(t)=\frac{\coth(b\sqrt B/2)\cos(t\sqrt B)
                   -i\sin(t\sqrt B)}{2\sqrt B}.
\]
The limit \(b\to\infty\) gives the static ground-state covariance
\((2\sqrt B)^{-1}\); finite \(b\) changes fluctuations while preserving the
same commutator and response. These are joint observer-readout predictions
of the declared free-field family, not the passive native density law or
the interacting field's covariance~\cite{ophtimefields}.
The radial apparatus, state preparation and physical clock map remain inputs.

On the finite interacting work algebra
\(\mathcal A=M_8(\mathbb C)\oplus M_8(\mathbb C)\), restricted thermal
equilibrium leaves a central sector weight:
\[
 \rho_w=w\frac{e^{-bH_+}}{Z_+}\oplus
        (1-w)\frac{e^{-bH_-}}{Z_-},\qquad 0<w<1.
\]
Every such state has the same restricted modular flow. Cooling each block
does not select \(w\). The full Gibbs preparation additionally chooses
\(w=Z_+/(Z_++Z_-)\); its global ground-state limit is therefore a stronger
state choice. Admitted record statistics can test sector populations,
but do not identify an unobserved purification~\cite{ophproduct}.

A direct cosmological identification has a discriminating control.
In the flat massless limit, with \(\hbar=c=1\) and
\(\langle\phi_{\mathbf k}\phi_{\mathbf k'}\rangle
=(2\pi)^3\delta(\mathbf k+\mathbf k')P_\phi(k)\), the static vacuum gives
\[
 P_\phi(k)=\frac1{2k},\qquad
 \Delta_\phi^2(k)=\frac{k^3P_\phi(k)}{2\pi^2}
                  =\frac{k^2}{4\pi^2}.
\]
Thus a scale-independent identification of this field with primordial
curvature does not reproduce the nearly scale-invariant curvature spectrum
inferred from CMB data~\cite{planckinflation}. This flat-limit control is
not a Fourier-spectrum claim for the bounded native radial product.
An expanding evolution and an operational curvature map must supply the
transfer before the state can predict cosmological observations. The
initial-state target is consequently a law for accessible earlier-state
correlations and their later records, not an assumed observable absolute
beginning of the Universe.

\section{Unsupplied Premises}

The cosmological source construction does not supply the reversible
conductances \(J_r\), potential \(V_r\), or slab thickness \(a_{t,r}\)
required above. A source-instantiated reflection-positive vacuum, or an
equivalent reconstruction on this cosmological branch, is not supplied.
The bridge from a source-derived vacuum to primordial growth seeds
without cosmic microwave background or large-scale structure data is not
supplied. A physical proto-object or worldline requires conditions distinct
from those used for production particle claims.
