\begin{abstract}
Observer-screen synchronization is a conditional alternative to an inflaton
model. Its mathematical ingredients are a finite source ensemble, a
geometric screen covariance and a physical radial lift. We derive the
Gaussian screen spectrum and its amplitude from specified source data,
show how an infinitesimal collar density would give
\(n_s=1-P/48\), and state the conditions for a unique radial power law.
The survival-cocycle form does not select that density. On a supplied spatially flat
background, expansion is represented by a fourth-power record-density
weight; the source law does not select its profile. Horizon coherence,
flat-sector selection, a hot radiation state and a common physical clock
are independent premises. A finite scheduled material exchange selects a homogeneous ensemble at
fixed total from every sharp preparation. On 1,310,720 cells a deliberately
uneven half-board retains 97.69 percent of its mean contrast after 512
service rounds. The equilibrium bounded records admit a stationary extension
with no distinguished first round.

\end{abstract}

\label{def:oph-screen-conformal-precision}

 C_\ell^q=\frac{A_q}{\Lambda_\ell(\theta)},\qquad

This follows from \((1+a)/(1+b)\) with

\section{Physical content of an inflation-free branch}
A replacement for inflation must explain horizon-scale coherence, flat-sector
selection, a nearly scale-invariant geometric spectrum, its amplitude and
phase, and a hot radiation state on one common clock. The covariance
calculation establishes the spectrum only after its source data are supplied.
It proves none of the other requirements.

A modular flow supplies a dimensionless state-dependent parameter, not an
absolute cosmological duration. A dark-sector
continuation must likewise supply its abundance, covariant stress, exchange
and perturbation dynamics; a static acceleration law supplies none of those
objects.

\paragraph{Uniformity from any fixed material total.}
There is an all-time prediction for this instrument. On a finite connected
board with $N$ cells and $M$ doubles, the distribution at full schedule
cycles converges from every sharp preparation to the uniform distribution
on the $\binom{N}{M}$ placements. Each contact is a mixture of two
permutations and preserves that distribution. Over one cycle, every edge
has positive probability of being the sole exchange and there is positive
probability of no exchange. Edge transpositions on a connected graph
generate all placements with the same total. The finite cycle chain is
therefore irreducible and aperiodic. The limiting distribution also
holds at each intermediate schedule phase because every contact preserves it.
Writing $p=M/N$, every cell then has mean $p$ and distinct cells have
covariance $-p(1-p)/(N-1)$. This follows by sampling two distinct sites
without replacement. The law selects a homogeneous material ensemble
conditional on the conserved total and sufficient service. Individual
readings continue to fluctuate. A proposal that this equilibrium preceded
the radiation era requires a physical release criterion and a comparison
of the available mixing duration with that release.
