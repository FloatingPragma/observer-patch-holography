\begin{abstract}
Observer-Patch Holography (OPH) cosmology replaces the inflaton with finite observer-screen
synchronization. Flatness, horizon coherence, near scale invariance, hot source
release, and acoustic-transfer inputs are treated as jobs for the finite screen
state rather than for a new scalar field.

The conditional source theorem fixes the screen amplitude from a primitive
collar release law and the tilt from an edge-center generator. The radial
result proves one-shell non-identifiability and supplies physical dilation and
cross-covariance tomography as conditional uniqueness routes. A physical
cosmological prediction requires one finite source instantiation, transfer
calculation, and likelihood; simulator outputs without those receipts are
diagnostics.
\end{abstract}
