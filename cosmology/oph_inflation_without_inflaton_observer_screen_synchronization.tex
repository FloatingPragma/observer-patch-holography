\begin{abstract}
For a supplied layered event population whose comoving read law generates
the same finite order for every scale-factor profile, the expansion history
is a record density per comoving cell and conformal tick, and the redshift
between two epochs is the fourth root of a record-count ratio for congruent
comoving windows with constant scale factor within each, or in the
shrinking-window limit of a continuous profile. These statements are exact
and machine-checked; native repair selects neither the population nor its
profile. On this footing we state observer-screen synchronization as a candidate
inflation-free branch of Observer-Patch Holography (OPH) and list the premises
it must supply; none of its replacements for the jobs of inflation is
supplied here. A conditional source theorem fixes a screen amplitude from a primitive collar
release law and a spectral tilt \(n_s=1-P_\star/48\) from an edge-center
generator once its full-collar generator density is supplied; physical
source dilation and radial cross-covariance tomography give two sufficient
routes to a unique three-dimensional spectrum, and vanishing spatial
curvature holonomy identifies a flat FLRW branch without selecting it. The
generator density is a declared branch input, so the tilt is a declared map
evaluated at a target-informed pixel. At the comparison pixel
located from the measured fine-structure constant the tilt is \(0.96602\),
\(0.27\sigma\) above Planck 2018 and at the lower \(0.612\) percent position
of the joint posterior of ACT DR6 with Planck, lensing and DESI 2024 baryon
acoustic oscillations, a retrospective tension on data that precede the
registration.
\end{abstract}

\section*{This paper's hypothesis and place in the tower}

This paper sits at layer seven and develops the primordial branch without an
inflaton. Its hypothesis is that observer-screen synchronization does the
logical jobs of inflation: expansion is a record density on the sourced
causal order, spatial flatness is zero clock-slice curvature holonomy,
horizon-scale coherence is same-boundary repair, the nearly scale-invariant
spectrum is a geometric screen spectrum with edge-center tilt
\(n_s=1-P_\star/48\), the hot start is a MaxEnt release, and the acoustic
sky follows from ordinary Boltzmann transfer.

The unconditional finite results are the record-density form of a
spatially flat expanding background on a supplied layered population, the
finite quotient ensemble theorems, and the cocycle freedom that leaves the
tilt exponent unselected. The screen amplitude, the tilt, the two radial
uniqueness routes and the flatness identification are conditional theorems:
each holds once its displayed premise is supplied, and no finite source
supplies the full-collar generator density \(P_\star/24\), the primitive
collar release law, the record-production profile or a horizon-coherence
mechanism. The FLRW chart, the population and its profile are premises.

For the branch to apply, the executable model must emit from one native
process the geometric collar-volume field, the generator density and
orientation-half identity, a physical clock calibrating the release, a
radial observation algebra that lifts the two-dimensional screen to a
three-dimensional curvature field, and the record-production law. The
native construction constrains this join. Its continuum generator is not
identified with the round Laplacian, its reference state identifies no
quantum vacuum, and under a bounded form-congruent identification its
spatial covariance forces \(\theta=0\), so the tilt needs an ingredient
beyond the native density field (see the paragraph on the native source
after the screen-spectrum theorems). The
branch is refuted if the frozen tilt protocol excludes \(n_s=0.96602\) on
its eligible data, if nonzero running or primordial isocurvature is detected
beyond the measurement errors, or if no admissible native ingredient can
supply a nonzero tilt.
