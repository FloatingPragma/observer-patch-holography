\begin{abstract}
Observer-screen synchronization is a conditional alternative to an inflaton
model. Its mathematical ingredients are a finite source ensemble, a
geometric screen covariance and a physical radial lift. We derive the
Gaussian screen spectrum and its amplitude from specified source data,
show how an infinitesimal collar density would give
\(n_s=1-P/48\), and state the conditions for a unique radial power law.
The survival-cocycle form does not select that density. Native load
covariance has spectral dimension two and cannot carry the proposed tilt
under a bounded energy identification. On a supplied spatially flat
background, expansion is represented by a fourth-power record-density
weight; the source law does not select its profile. Horizon coherence,
flat-sector selection, a hot radiation state and a common physical clock
are independent premises. The construction therefore specifies a testable
conditional spectrum without establishing an inflation-replacement
cosmology.

\end{abstract}




\section{Source ensemble and screen covariance}
\label{sec:screen-spectrum-theorems}
A finite physical quotient \(Q_r\), positive base weight \(m_r\), and
intrinsic action \(S_r\) define
\(\mu_r(q)=Z_r^{-1}m_r(q)e^{-S_r(q)}\). Settlement only retracts onto
normal forms and fixes every law supported there; it does not select
\(m_r\) or \(S_r\). Compatible coarse maps must push \(\mu_s\) to
\(\mu_r\), and a physical vacuum needs reversible transfer data and a
reconstruction theorem~\cite{ophvacuum}.

\begin{definition}[Geometric scalar and precision]
\label{def:oph-screen-scalar}
For a positive collar-volume readout \(J_{X,r}\) and independently
specified reference \(\bar J_{X,r}\), set
\[
 q_r=(I-\Pi_{<2,r})\frac13\log(J_{X,r}/\bar J_{X,r}),\qquad
 \Pi_{<2,r}=B_r(B_r^{\mathsf T}M_rB_r)^{-1}B_r^{\mathsf T}M_r,
\]
where \(M_r\) is the positive screen quadrature and \(B_r\) spans its
certified monopole/dipole space. Let \(K_r\) be positive and self-adjoint
in this quadrature on \(V_r=\operatorname{im}(I-\Pi_{<2,r})\).
Its normalization and the source release energy \(E_{q,r}^{\rm src}\)
are specified independently of the desired spectrum.
\end{definition}
A chart change satisfying \(U^{\mathsf T}M'_rU=M_r\),
\(q'_{0,r}=Uq_{0,r}\), \(B'_r=UB_r\) gives
\(\Pi'_{<2,r}U=U\Pi_{<2,r}\) by substitution. Patch count alone is not
a quadrature or angular-resolution certificate.

\begin{theorem}[Screen covariance]
\label{thm:oph-screen-maxent}
For \(d_r=\dim V_r\), the entropy-maximizing continuous law with zero
mean and expected energy
\(\tfrac12\mathbb E\langle q,K_rq\rangle=E_{q,r}^{\rm src}>0\)
is Gaussian with covariance \(A_{q,r}K_r^{-1}\), where
\(A_{q,r}=2E_{q,r}^{\rm src}/d_r\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In an \(M_r\)-orthonormal eigenbasis, the multiplier equation gives
\(\exp[-\beta\sum_i\kappa_i a_i^2/2]\). Each mode contributes
\(1/(2\beta)\) to the energy. Strict entropy concavity gives uniqueness.
\end{proof}
\label{prop:oph-screen-amplitude-nogo}
Every \(A>0\) defines such a Gaussian; MaxEnt does not choose the energy.
One source prescription is a primitive collar law
\(\nu_r^{\rm rel}\propto m_r^0\exp(-\sum_a\lambda_aF_a)\), whose
constraints are specified before measuring the screen energy. It fixes
\(E_{q,r}^{\rm src}=\tfrac12\mathbb E_{\nu_r^{\rm rel}}
\langle q_r,K_rq_r\rangle\) only after that law is supplied.

\subsection{Conformal precision and controlled refinement}
\label{def:oph-screen-conformal-precision}
On a round spherical continuum, choose, for \(-2<\theta<4\),
\[
 K_\theta Y_{\ell m}=\Lambda_\ell(\theta)Y_{\ell m},\qquad
 \Lambda_\ell(\theta)=
 \frac{\Gamma(\ell+2+\theta/2)}{\Gamma(\ell-\theta/2)},\quad \ell\ge2.
\]
Then \(K_0=-\Delta_{S^2}\), and the Gaussian law gives
\[
 C_\ell^q=\frac{A_q}{\Lambda_\ell(\theta)},\qquad
 \frac{\ell(\ell+1)C_\ell^q}{2\pi}\sim\frac{A_q}{2\pi}\ell^{-\theta}.
\]
A finite functional-calculus realization uses
\(K_{\theta,r}=\Gamma(B_r+3/2+\theta/2)/
\Gamma(B_r-1/2-\theta/2)\), \(B_r=(L_r+I/4)^{1/2}\), on a specified
positive spectral branch; positivity of this finite operator is an explicit
hypothesis. A sufficient condition is
\(\sqrt{\lambda+1/4}>(1+\theta)/2\) for every retained eigenvalue
\(\lambda\) of \(L_r\), which places both gamma arguments on the positive
real axis. Positivity of \(L_r\) and its continuum limit alone do not
imply this finite spectral condition. If on a fixed angular band its eigenvalues and amplitude have
relative errors \(\epsilon_K<1\) and \(\epsilon_A\), respectively, then
\[
 \left|\frac{C_{\ell m,r}^q}{A_q/\Lambda_\ell}-1\right|
 \le\frac{\epsilon_A+\epsilon_K}{1-\epsilon_K}.
\]
This follows from \((1+a)/(1+b)\) with
\(|a|\le\epsilon_A\), \(|b|\le\epsilon_K\). The round operator and its
normalization are premises, not consequences of finite icosahedral symmetry.

\subsection{Generator density and radial spectrum}
A positive continuous covariance-survival cocycle has
\(u(s+t)=u(s)u(t)\), \(u(0)=1\), hence \(u(s)=e^{-\theta s}\).
An independently supplied full-collar derivative
\(-u'_{\rm full}(0)=P_\star/24\) and a weighted orientation-half identity
\(-u'_q(0)=-u'_{\rm full}(0)/2\) therefore give
\[
 \theta=P_\star/48,\qquad n_s=1-P_\star/48,\qquad
 \kappa_{\rm rep}^{\rm edge}=\frac{P_\star}{48(P_\star-\varphi)}.
\]
The last coordinate requires \(P_\star\ne\varphi\). A finite survival
step has exponent \(-\log u(\log b)/\log b\); its probability cannot
replace the derivative. Every real \(\theta\) satisfies the cocycle
identity, and every nonnegative \(\theta\) is a decay cocycle. The density
\(P_\star/24\) is therefore an independent premise.

A physical dilation intertwiner, source stress, common clock, freezeout and
translation/rotation covariance can transfer this law to the curvature
covariance. On the common compact-\(\log k\) domain, assume
\(D_s^{-1}C_\zeta D_s=e^{-\theta s}C_\zeta\), where
\((D_sf)(k)=f(e^{-s}k)\). A positive measurable radial multiplier then
has \(\Delta_\zeta^2(k)=A_\zeta(k/k_\star)^{-\theta}\) almost everywhere.
Indeed \(e^{\theta t}\Delta_\zeta^2(e^t)\) is translation-invariant in
\(t\); measurability makes it constant almost everywhere. A global bounded
covariance norm is not assumed for a nonzero tilt.

For \(q=Z_\star\Pi_{\ell\ge2}\zeta(R_\star\hat n)\),
\(Z_\star\ne0\), the spherical-Bessel integral gives
\[
 C_\ell^q=4\pi Z_\star^2\int d\log k\,
 \Delta_\zeta^2(k)j_\ell^2(kR_\star),\qquad
 A_\zeta=\frac{A_q}{\pi^{3/2}Z_\star^2(k_\star R_\star)^\theta}
 \frac{\Gamma(3/2+\theta/2)}{\Gamma(1+\theta/2)}.
\]
The stated range \(-2<\theta<4\) ensures convergence for \(\ell\ge2\).
A shell alone determines correlation only for chords up to \(2R_\star\),
leaving an infinite-dimensional radial kernel even under positivity.
Complete radial cross-covariances supply a separate tomography route.
For a finite radial matrix \(C=Ap\), the null space has dimension
\(N_k-\operatorname{rank}A\).

For a normalized nonnegative radial window with mean \(R_\star\) and
variance \(\sigma_R^2\), Taylor's theorem gives
\(\delta_\ell(k)=\tfrac12 k^2\sigma_R^2
\sup_{r\in\operatorname{supp}W}|j_\ell''(kr)|\) and
\[
 |C_{\ell,W}^q-C_{\ell,\rm shell}^q|
 \le4\pi Z_\star^2\int d\log k\,\Delta_\zeta^2(k)
 [2\delta_\ell(k)+\delta_\ell(k)^2].
\]
This requires a finite right-hand side. A source spectrum must control the
radial null space, window error, finite refinement and forward residual
before photon transfer can assign it an observed sky.

\paragraph{Native source and the screen premises.}
The conformal precision above takes the round
continuum limit \(-\Delta_{S^2}\) as a premise. The native repair law
supplies a continuum generator \(A\) on the screen whose energy lies between

source-only vacuum law selecting its release amplitude: its full configuration
reference approaches the normalized trace, and its continuous wave readout
has the classical displacement covariance \(A^{-1}\), not the vacuum
covariance \((2\sqrt A)^{-1}\)~\cite{ophtimefields}. Under any bounded
form-congruent identification of the shell precision \(K_\theta\) with the
native long-average field, the min-max argument of the boundary-trace
test~\cite{ophcontinuum} matches the eigenvalue growth \(n^{1+\theta/2}\)
of \(K_\theta\) against the linear growth of \(\lambda_n(A)\) and forces
\(\theta=0\), so the native field's spatial covariance cannot carry the
edge-center tilt, which requires a separate ingredient.

\section{Physical content of an inflation-free branch}
A replacement for inflation must explain horizon-scale coherence, flat-sector
selection, a nearly scale-invariant geometric spectrum, its amplitude and
phase, and a hot radiation state on one common clock. The covariance
calculation establishes the spectrum only after its source data are supplied.
It proves none of the other requirements. In particular, the lowest native
screen eigenvalue is bounded below only in the rescaled attempt parameter
\(\tau=\mathrm{attempts}/(1260\cdot16^l)\); this gives no
refinement-uniform physical low-wavenumber coherence rate.

A modular flow supplies a dimensionless state-dependent parameter, not an
absolute cosmological duration. Active coherent services can implement
modular steps and relative event-clock laws, but do not synchronize an
entire physical screen by themselves~\cite{ophmodel}. A dark-sector
continuation must likewise supply its abundance, covariant stress, exchange
and perturbation dynamics; a static acceleration law supplies none of those
objects.

\section{Expansion as Record Density}
\label{sec:expansion-record-density}

The spacetime construction supplies a flat causal-order and count-volume
limit for the conservative golden-record population and the retrospective
count clock. The source-cosmology companion gives the exact finite count identities
for a spatially flat expanding background. The profile law specifying
records per comoving cell and conformal tick is supplied; these count
identities do not derive the Friedmann equation. Conformal time \(\eta\), the layer index
\(j\) and the durations \(\sigma_j\Delta\) called physical ticks are
coordinates of the supplied FLRW chart; none is a calibrated observer clock.

For a positive profile \(a(\eta)\), the metric
\(ds^2=a^2(c^2d\eta^2-|dx|^2)\) has the flat comoving causal order.
Scaling a layer's site positions and read radius by the same \(a_j\)
preserves every read relation. With constant physical event density \(\rho\),
uniform comoving cell volume \(v\) and conformal tick \(\Delta\),
\[
 M_a(I)=\rho\Delta v\sum_{(j,s)\in I}a_j^4,
 \qquad a_{\min}^4M_1(I)\le M_a(I)\le a_{\max}^4M_1(I).
\]
The fourth root of a ratio therefore reads the corresponding scale ratio
for congruent constant-profile windows. For shrinking diamonds and continuous
profiles it converges to the proper-time ratio; fixed finite diamonds can
retain a bias. For example, \(a(\eta)=1+\eta\), with durations \(1\)
and \(1/2\), gives mass ratio \(1516288/44451\), while the fourth power
of the proper-time ratio is \((12/5)^4\). These differ by
\(25944064/27781875\).

For emission and reception windows of equal comoving shape, the same
constant-profile or shrinking-window condition gives
\[
 1+z=\frac{a_0}{a_e}=\left[\frac{M_a(I_0)}{M_a(I_e)}\right]^{1/4}.
\] A de Sitter profile
\(a=-1/(H\eta)\) gives record density proportional to \((H\eta)^{-4}\).
The profile is supplied; these identities derive neither the Friedmann
equation nor a physical clock. Their proofs and exact finite controls are
in the source-cosmology companion~\cite{ophcmb}.

\section{Flatness Boundary}

The flatness branch is split into identification and selection. On a clocked
Friedmann--Lema\^itre--Robertson--Walker (FLRW) continuation
with \(u_a=-N\nabla_a\chi\), \(h_{ab}=g_{ab}+u_au_b\), and
\(K(\tau)=\kappa/a(\tau)^2\), vanishing area-normalized small-loop holonomy of
the spatial Levi--Civita connection identifies \(K=0\) and hence
\(\kappa=0\). It does not select that sector
from the raw FLRW fiber. A numerical calculation must distinguish exact
selection derived from the finite screen, a flat branch conditioned on an
explicit premise, and a branch for which no selection theorem is supplied. Screen and collar defect
decay is a repair diagnostic rather than a spatial curvature holonomy.
Curvature damping supplies only an approximate bound on \(|K|\) or
\(|\Omega_K|\).

\section{Conditional value and observation}
At the comparison pixel \(P_\star=1.6309682094\), located using the
measured fine-structure constant, the declared density gives
\(n_s=0.96602\). The diagnostic clock choice
\(\theta=e(P_\star-\varphi)\) gives \(0.96484\). Neither the survival
cocycle nor a particle/memory fixed point selects the cosmological density
or this clock choice. The first value lies \(0.27\sigma\) above Planck
2018 and at the lower \(0.612\) percent position of the ACT DR6, Planck,
lensing and DESI 2024 BAO joint posterior; the alternative lies at
\(0.200\) percent. These are retrospective comparisons under
\(\Lambda\)CDM, not likelihood ratios~\cite{act2025,ophpublicdata}.

The fixed dilation exponent gives zero running. A Gaussian single-clock
source has no independent isocurvature mode. Zero linear tensors requires
both zero initial tensor amplitude and velocity and zero forcing; absence
of a scalar-induced tensor source alone leaves homogeneous solutions.
A tensor upper limit can exclude only models above its sensitivity, because
slow-roll inflation has no universal positive tensor floor. Physical
comparison additionally needs the curvature map, acoustic transfer,
recombination, foregrounds and covariance on the same branch.

\section{Unsupplied Premises}
\label{sec:unsupplied}

The same-boundary or low-\(k\) coherence branch has no quotient-language
derivation. A finite source emitting the geometric collar-volume field
\[
q_r=\Pi_{\ge2,r}(1/3)\log(J_{X,r}/\bar J_{X,r})
\]
is not constructed by the stated source data. The same source must supply the primitive collar release
law and pooled release energy that fix \(A_q\), together with the full-collar
infinitesimal reserve generator and orientation-half condition used by the
conditional \(P_\star/48\) theorem. Its source-dependency graph must contain
compatible stress, clock, freezeout, physical-scale, radial-window, null-space,
and forward-residual evidence; this scientific prerequisite graph is
distinct from the semantic read-from order of the consensus
layer~\cite{ophcmb}. The resulting quantities are the inputs to the cosmic
microwave background transfer and likelihood calculations.

The rank-three source Gram metric is a finite readout of one carrier's slow
response, not an extended space; on the refining source screens the
rank-three band leaves bounded-energy port modes at order \(2^{-l}\), and the
native continuum generator has heat-trace spectral dimension two on the
two-dimensional source~\cite{ophmodel,ophcontinuum}. Native dependence
crosses at most one seam per ordered repair event, a bound in graph hops
with no laboratory speed or continuum light cone~\cite{ophcausality}. The
flat \(1+3\) count clock of the spacetime construction uses a specified
population and read law~\cite{ophEinstein}. An inflationary realization
therefore requires the same events and clock to support physical expansion,
stress, freezeout and scale, together with the radial, transfer and
likelihood conditions above; smooth Einstein promotion requires same-family
tensor-curvature convergence or the continuum small-ball/null-balance
identification.
