\begin{abstract}
On the layered source family of the spacetime construction, with specified
population and read law, the number of unordered read pairs crossing a
surface per layer obeys an area law with exact limiting coefficient
\((\pi/4)n^2a^4\) per unit area for site density \(n\) and read radius
\(a\), and declaring one nat of cut entropy per crossing read fixes the
Planck-area dictionary \(n^2a^4=1/(\pi\ell_P^2)\). This declared count is
distinct from native regional entropy, which on the two-dimensional source
screen carries no nonzero coefficient times boundary-seam count. Around it
we formulate a finite information ledger for observer-patch models using
machine-checked dynamics and record channels. A continuous one-parameter
star-automorphism group on a finite-dimensional private algebra acts by
blockwise unitary conjugation; for a supplied projective partition, the
public record channel is the exact uniform mixture of independent sign
unitaries; classical relative-entropy contraction and finite fluctuation
identities supply complementary thermodynamic inputs; and a declared
rich-fibre adapter constructs noncommutative regional algebras with
commuting disjoint blocks and unique restriction gluing. The
support-aware entropy identity for pinching is a known theorem that the
library has not formalized; we state its proposed thermality and
information-relocation consequences as finite conjectures. Together these
results and conjectures make the black-hole information question precise
within the supplied finite model.
\end{abstract}

\section*{This paper's hypothesis and place in the tower}

This paper sits at layer seven and develops the black-hole branch as a
finite record ledger. Its hypothesis is that black-hole information is
relocated, never destroyed: the ambient evolution of a private algebra is
unitary, the thermal-looking public record is produced by an explicit unital
channel, and the information missing from the public record is the relative entropy \(\KL{\rho}{\mathcal E(\rho)}\), carried by
coherences that no observer access cut exposes.

Statements labeled \emph{Theorem} are machine-checked in the Lean library at
the cited modules. Statements labeled \emph{Known theorem} are standard
finite-dimensional results that the library has not formalized. Statements
labeled \emph{Conjecture} are unproved, and nothing here claims them. The
crossing-read area law holds on a supplied layered population with a
declared one-nat-per-read count. The phrase ``black hole'' names the shape
of the question: the model has no continuum horizon, gravitational collapse,
Hawking flux or evaporation endpoint, and no observable prediction follows
from the finite ledger.

For the branch to apply, the executable model must supply on one process a
faithful continuum realization of the finite source-causal carrier, a
calibrated identification of native event clocks with proper
duration~\cite{ophmodel}, Einstein-branch geometry and stress, and a record
channel derived from its native record laws; any observable additionally
needs a prospectively fixed measurement contract under immutable custody.
The native construction constrains this join. Its record-discarded repair
channel attenuates coherence and is not unitary on the carrier, while each
finite observation history becomes unitary once its record environment is
kept (Section~\ref{sec:targets}); its executed record law is not idempotent,
so the ledger's pinching is not derived from it~\cite{ophrecordlaws}; and its
regional entropy carries no boundary-seam coefficient on the two-dimensional
source screen~\cite{ophentropy}, so the area law is a declared count rather
than a native entropy law. Its continuum operator is a positive two-dimensional spatial operator
with no radial domain or quantum vacuum~\cite{ophcontinuum,ophtimefields},
and its causal statement is a bound of one seam per ordered repair event
with no laboratory calibration~\cite{ophcausality}.
The branch is refuted by an access cut of the specified net that exposes
the off-block coherences, by a failure of the generalized-second-law-shaped
monotone with a unital record channel, or by a proof that the native record
laws admit no channel whose missing information is relocated rather than
destroyed.

\section{The question in finite form}

The black-hole information problem sets two commitments against each
other: microscopic dynamics that preserves information, and a
macroscopic record that looks exactly thermal. In the observer-patch
model both commitments are formal objects, and the question becomes
finite: given a unitary ambient dynamics on a finite private algebra,
and given that what any observer can hold is a public record
produced by a specific channel, account exactly for the information that
the record does not carry.

The model supplies sharper versions of both sides than a generic quantum
system would.

First, within the supplied pointwise-continuous star-automorphism class,
unitarity is not an additional assumption. The Stone-converse packet
proves that every pointwise-continuous one-parameter star-automorphism
group of a unital finite-dimensional private star subalgebra is blockwise
unitary conjugation generated by one self-adjoint Hamiltonian per block.
The theorem makes no claim about dynamics outside that class.

Second, the record channel is not a generic decoherence map. For every
projective partition, the publicization pinching is exactly the uniform
average of the $2^{k}$ independently signed block reflections. The
channel that produces the public record is therefore an explicit uniform
mixture of unitaries, with the mixing unitaries named. It is unital; entropy
nondecrease for this channel is part of Known theorem~\ref{tgt:core}. This
pinching is the publicization of a supplied partition. The native record
laws of the executable observer form one convex family classified by five
coherence multipliers, in which exactly two members are idempotent and the
executed law C is not; the ledger's channel is not derived from that
family~\cite{ophrecordlaws}.

The conjectured ledger combines these two facts with the exact thermodynamic
theorems. It would identify the information absent from the public record with
observer-inaccessible coherences of the ambient state through the formula in
Known theorem~\ref{tgt:core}. The executable observer computes this missing
information exactly for its own records. Its complete classical tape and
final load register recover every initial load population but not an
arbitrary unknown quantum state; the missing information is coherence
between different committed histories. The optimal entanglement fidelity
over all decoders is \(D^{-2}\sum_h w_h\,\operatorname{rank}(P_h)^2\), which
equals \(1301/2028\) for the full-carrier two-event C observer and
\(3329/6084\) for E~\cite{ophrecovery}.

\section{Machine-checked inputs}\label{sec:checked-inputs}

Each input below is a machine-checked result in the repository, cited by
module. The first two are standard finite-dimensional facts: the first is
the Skolem--Noether theorem combined with Stone's theorem on each Wedderburn
block, and the second is character orthogonality on the group of diagonal
signs. They enter as checked inputs, not as new results. Their joint consequence and the precise conjectures are stated in
Section~\ref{sec:targets}.

\begin{theorem}[Finite Stone packet]
Every continuous one-parameter group of star automorphisms of a unital,
finite-dimensional private star subalgebra containing the ambient identity
acts by blockwise unitary conjugation. On each Wedderburn block it is generated
by a fixed self-adjoint Hamiltonian, unique up to a real scalar.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Publicization is a uniform mixture of sign unitaries]
For every supplied $k$-block projective partition, the pinching map is
the uniform average of the $2^{k}$ diagonal sign unitaries
(Lean module \texttt{EventAlgebra.RecordMajorization}~\cite{ophlean});
averaging only the two
global signs does not pinch, so the independence of the signs is
load-bearing.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Exact data processing and second law]
Every stochastic kernel preserving a faithful stationary reference
contracts Kullback--Leibler divergence to that reference
(\texttt{kl\_push\_le}), the conditional heat bath obeys the second law
(\texttt{heatBath\_secondLaw}). For finite real-valued distributions and
kernels, the integral fluctuation identity holds under its positivity,
normalization and stationary-reference hypotheses; the pointwise Crooks
identity additionally assumes detailed balance.
The proofs are in the Lean modules
\texttt{Thermodynamics.FiniteConditionalRepair} and
\texttt{Thermodynamics.FluctuationTheorems}~\cite{ophlean}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Support boundary for entropy architecture]
The totalized continuous-functional-calculus logarithm sends every
projection to zero, so the raw finite trace formula for relative entropy
assigns the value zero to an orthogonal pair of density projections whose
support inclusion fails
(\texttt{EventAlgebra.SpectralEntropyBoundary}~\cite{ophlean}). A support-aware
extended-value layer is therefore mandatory, not stylistic.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional rich-fibre regional packet]
The locally pinned fresh-seed source run supplies split-fibre labels at four
pairwise-disjoint observer windows. A declared finite adapter constructs a
genuinely noncommutative block algebra with a nonunital two-by-two
designated-fibre matrix corner at every observer region, elementwise
commutation across distinct constructed blocks, computed four-window coverage
of the declared top algebra, and unique restriction gluing with its
reconstruction corollary
(\texttt{QFT.RichFibreWitness},
\texttt{QFT.RichFibreRegionalNet}~\cite{ophlean}).
That source run does not produce the matrix units, state, regional
restrictions or repair of this adapter, and the corner is neither a tensor
factor nor a
\texttt{TensorSplitReceipt}. A source-attached regional-factor theorem requires an operator
algebra and a justified factor or local-channel adapter.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Finite KMS identity, degenerate inheritance boundary]
The finite algebraic KMS identity holds for Gibbs states of
support-graded flows, and the truncation-8 nets inherit KMS
structure only degenerately
(\texttt{QFT.StructuralInheritance}~\cite{ophlean}).
The nondegenerate continuation on the rich-fibre net is
Conjecture~\ref{tgt:kms}.
\end{theorem}

Because the native full configuration reference has a tracial limit at
fixed modular parameter, its limiting modular automorphism is the identity,
and it supplies no nondegenerate KMS state of this
kind~\cite{ophtimefields}.

\section{Finite conjectures}
\label{sec:targets}

Throughout, $\rho$ is a finite density matrix on the ambient algebra,
$\mathcal E$ the pinching of a supplied projective partition, $\Ent$ the
support-aware von Neumann entropy, and $\KL{\cdot}{\cdot}$ the
support-aware relative entropy. The statements of this section add finite
hypotheses to the preceding theorems and do not follow from them. Two statements of this section are known theorems that the
library has not formalized; they are labeled accordingly.

\begin{known}[Support-aware entropy core]
\label{tgt:core}
For the pinching $\mathcal E$ of a projective partition,
\[
  \Ent(\mathcal E(\rho))-\Ent(\rho)=\KL{\rho}{\mathcal E(\rho)}\ge 0 ,
\]
with equality exactly at the public fixed states, where
($\mathcal E(\rho)=\rho$), and the sign-unitary mixture is
entropy-nondecreasing. This core is the subset of the
spectral-information chain required by the ledger; the more general
majorization, maximum-entropy, and H-theorem scope is not used here.
\end{known}

\begin{proof}[Standard proof]
The pinching is the uniform average of $2^k$ sign conjugations, so
$\mathcal E(\rho)\ge2^{-k}\rho$ and the support of $\rho$ lies in that of
$\mathcal E(\rho)$; the relative entropy is finite. The operator
$\log\mathcal E(\rho)$ is block diagonal, and $\mathcal E$ is a
trace-preserving conditional expectation onto block-diagonal operators, so
$\operatorname{Tr}\rho\log\mathcal E(\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}\mathcal E(\rho)
\log\mathcal E(\rho)$. Hence
$\Ent(\mathcal E(\rho))-\Ent(\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}\rho\log\rho
-\operatorname{Tr}\rho\log\mathcal E(\rho)=\KL{\rho}{\mathcal E(\rho)}$,
which is nonnegative with equality exactly when $\rho=\mathcal E(\rho)$ by
Klein's inequality. What remains open is the support-aware extended-value
formalization in the library, which the support boundary theorem above makes
mandatory.
\end{proof}

\begin{known}[Finite no-loss statement]
\label{tgt:noloss}
Let the specified ambient dynamics be a unitary dilation of repair that
retains its record environment. The joint state of system and records
evolves by the forced unitary conjugation of the Stone packet, and ordinary
entropy of that joint state is preserved; when both arguments co-evolve under the same
unitary, each explicitly named jointly unitarily invariant quantity is
also preserved (including support-aware relative entropy and trace
distance when those quantities are defined). The statement quantifies over the specified
dynamics class; no toy evolution is substituted and no arbitrary
coordinate-dependent divergence is covered.
\end{known}

This is unitary invariance of the von Neumann entropy and of every jointly
unitarily invariant quantity; its content for the ledger lies in the premise
that the specified dynamics is a unitary dilation that retains its records.

The record-discarded native repair channel is not of this kind: it strictly
attenuates a coherent mode, which rules out unitary conjugation on the
carrier, and under independent seam attempts the unread binary
fixed-number state converges to its tracial entropy maximum. Each finite
native observation history has an exact unitary representation once its
record environment is kept, with a dimensionless Hermitian action \(A_0\)
whose exponential \(\exp(-iA_0)\) is that unitary
representation~\cite{ophmodel,ophnativeaction,ophmixing}.

\begin{conjecture}[Ledger identity and relocation]
\label{tgt:ledger}
For the specified record channel, the public entropy gain equals
$\KL{\rho}{\mathcal E(\rho)}$; the quantity is nonnegative, vanishes
exactly at the public fixed states, and is carried entirely by the off-block
coherences that no access cut of the specified net exposes. Information
is relocated, never destroyed, and the relocation is exactly quantified.
\end{conjecture}

The equality and its sign are Known theorem~\ref{tgt:core}; the open part is
the statement that no access cut of the specified net exposes the off-block
coherences.

\begin{conjecture}[Generalized-second-law-shaped monotone]
The composition of the cap first law with Kullback--Leibler contraction
yields one exact monotone combining record entropy and the capacity
ledger under repair, with finite constants and a negative control
exhibiting failure of the monotone when unitality of the record channel
is dropped.
\end{conjecture}

A classical sector-level instance follows from the joint counting identity
\(S_{\rm count}(p,d)=H(p)+\sum_ip_i\log d_i=\log M-\KL{p}{d/M}\): a repair
kernel on sectors that preserves \(d/M\) contracts the divergence by
\texttt{kl\_push\_le}, so \(S_{\rm count}\) does not decrease, while a reset
kernel with stationary law \((1/4,3/4)\) on two equal sectors does not
preserve \(d/M\) and lowers \(S_{\rm count}\) from its maximum~\cite{ophentropy}.
The conjecture concerns the quantum record channel and the cap first law.

\begin{conjecture}[Complementarity receipts]
There is a composition of the generic operational access-cut interface, the
supplied rich-fibre data, and a justified operator/local-channel adapter
that forms a two-observer ledger in which each accessible algebra determines its own
record entropy, the declared block-commutation theorem supplies an algebraic
locality receipt, and a glued top observable is uniquely reconstructed within
the declared restriction system. No physical no-signalling or
refinement-natural interpretation follows before that attachment.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Nondegenerate finite KMS receipt]
\label{tgt:kms}
The modular data of a restricted state on one genuinely noncommutative
rich-fibre region satisfies the finite KMS identity at a capacity-linked
inverse temperature, nondegenerately. This receipt supplies a finite KMS
input but does not establish structural QFT thermality.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Finite Page-crossover inequality]
For a bipartition of the specified finite net, the exact $2^{k}$ sign-unitary
average obeys a finite inequality that locates the crossover of
accessible-record information without Haar integration.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Conditional finite implication]
Known theorems~\ref{tgt:core} and~\ref{tgt:noloss} with Conjectures~\ref{tgt:ledger}--\ref{tgt:kms} would imply simultaneous ambient
unitarity and a thermal public record, with the inaccessible information equal
to the computable functional $\KL{\rho}{\mathcal E(\rho)}$ and localized in
observer-inaccessible coherences. The implication supplies no physical
black-hole attachment. A source-selected order embedding with calibrated
density (a causal-set faithful embedding), manifoldlikeness, and a
calibrated identification of native event clocks with proper time are
additional hypotheses. Any smooth Einstein reading
also requires same-family tensor-curvature convergence or the independent
continuum small-ball/null-balance identification.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{Declared cut entropy and native regional entropy.}
The population, its read radius and the layer read law of the preceding
section are specified inputs, and the cut entropy is a declared count on
read links; the seam-count wording of that section refers to these read
links, not to native repair seams. It is distinct from native regional
entropy. On a source screen of \(N=240\cdot4^l\) ports, a complete refining
base-face patch with \(12\cdot4^l\) ports has \(3\cdot2^l\) crossing seams;
its stationary reduced entropy grows in proportion to \(4^l\), while the
return reference's mutual information is at most
\(\log(12\cdot4^l+1)+2025/(64N)\), which per crossing seam tends to zero.
Neither native regional quantity carries a nonzero universal coefficient
times boundary-seam count on that two-dimensional source~\cite{ophentropy}.
A reading of a whole screen's cell count as the area of a three-dimensional
region's boundary is not excluded, and that region map is a premise. The
Lean statements of the preceding section are in the module
\texttt{Geometry.CrossingReadAreaLaw}~\cite{ophlean}.

\section{Relation to standard approaches}

The Page analysis, the Hayden--Preskill protocol, and island computations
concern continuum or holographic systems with gravitational dynamics. The
finite ledger formulates their information question using separately proved
unitary dynamics and a specified record channel. Its proposed entropy and
thermality composition is conditional on
Known theorems~\ref{tgt:core} and~\ref{tgt:noloss} with Conjectures~\ref{tgt:ledger}--\ref{tgt:kms}. Within the supplied automorphism
class, the Stone converse gives unitary implementation; the public channel
is the explicit sign-unitary mixture. A physical black-hole interpretation
additionally requires the spacetime, horizon and stress attachments stated
above.

\section{Observables discipline}

The finite ledger implies no observable black-hole signature. A proposed
observable claim derived from the ledger, such as
saturation-regime relaxation structure or record-capacity signatures in
compact-object transients, requires a prospectively fixed clock, frame, scale,
and readout contract under immutable custody before any data comparison.

A conditional instrument template is defined for the integer-\(k\) ringdown
transition comb. It is not preregistered or frozen and therefore supports no
observable claim; no ringdown value is frozen in the prediction
register~\cite{ophladder}. The transition rule is an imported
continuation premise, not a source-derived consequence of the finite ledger.
For an emission it assumes positive integer record dimensions satisfying
\(d_{\rm before}=k d_{\rm after}\), so \(k\) must divide the initial
dimension. The signed black-hole entropy change is
\(\ln d_{\rm after}-\ln d_{\rm before}=-\ln k\); the positive entropy loss
entering the emitted-energy formula is \(\ln k\). For positive remnant mass
and subextremal spin \(|\chi|<1\), conditional on that rule,
candidate features above the rotation line sit at
\(x_k=\ln k/(8\pi)\), with
\(x=(GM/(c^3 g(\chi)))(\omega-m\Omega_H)\) and
\(g(\chi)=2\sqrt{1-\chi^2}/(1+\sqrt{1-\chi^2})\). Their
offset-subtracted ratios equal \(\ln k_a/\ln k_b\) under a common scale and
offset for a denominator tooth \(k_b\ge2\), whose offset-subtracted value is
proved nonzero. At \(|\chi|=1\), \(g(\chi)=0\): the candidate teeth collapse
to the rotation line, and the rescaled coordinate and these ratios are undefined.
Observed frequencies must use the detector-frame remnant mass
\(M_{\rm det}=(1+z)M_{\rm source}\) in both terms; the ratio is redshift-free
only with this consistent frame convention. The tooth relation uses the
leading small-transition Kerr first law; finite-step background corrections
are not derived by this template.

The declared linewidth-to-spacing ratio is
\(64\pi^2p_0/(a g(\chi)^2\ln k)\), which is mass-independent but
spin-dependent; a constant Page coefficient is not controlled near extremal
spin. The declared \((k-1)/k\) KMS factor is a within-line net-response
factor, not a cross-\(k\) transition probability or prior. The algebraic
invariance, divisibility and entropy-sign boundary,
ladder bracketings, producer, independent byte-identical verifier, and tests
are target-blind. No event likelihood has been evaluated. Published posterior
samples alone are neither a strain likelihood nor a model evidence. This template is not anchorable until a source-derived transition, common detector
likelihood or sufficient likelihood product, detector-frame nuisance model,
normalized integer prior or explicit finite submodel, event selection, trials,
greybody normalization, no-comb reference, and convergence rule are fixed.
The computed \(2\le k\le12\) ladder is only a finite candidate submodel;
excluding it would not reject the unrestricted integer family.
