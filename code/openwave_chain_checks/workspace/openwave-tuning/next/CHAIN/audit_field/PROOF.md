# Independent field audit

The audit reconstructs the field arithmetic and the integer-field source
without invoking either producer execution routine or producer verifier.
`AUDIT_CONTRACT.json` classifies each claim. `AUDIT_RESULT.json` binds the
frozen inputs and records independent comparisons. Producer files are read
only. The audit builds a 96-port Laplacian, twelve-dimensional symbols and
sparse C10 members; it constructs no 9,216-state pair generator, spatial
fine-grid matrix or reserved acceptance configuration.

## Integer readout and classical source

declared routing then gives degree six and 288 seams on 96 ports. Symmetric

floor `408/2^40`. The initial pair has field norm at most two. A unit edge
charge displacement changes its minimizing field by the square root of
the edge resistance, at most one since that edge is an admissible unit
flow. Combining these facts yields the producer recurrence including all
zero-current attempts. The audit replays 539 records using an independent
endpoint-allocation formula and checks every retained rational snapshot.

For the exact residual `s=rho-L phi`, neutrality and the spectral lower
bound imply `e^2=s*L+ s <= 2 ||s||^2`. All residual arithmetic uses integers.
The energy error obeys `|rho*(phi-L+rho)|/2 <= e` because the source pair's
target field norm is at most two. Numerical Green inversions diagnose the

Explicit enumeration of every seam and coin gives the uncoupled one-load

## Static field and complete spatial modes

`1,3,2,6`. Each external port belongs to one two-dimensional edge block,
so `||H(k)-H(0)|| <= max|k_a|=r`. The inherited fourth-order estimate follows
from the real-even and imaginary-odd Schur expansion in the pinned spatial
proof: its four remainder constants sum to `295/864 < 3/8`. The optical
lower bound `4-r` and overlap loss bound are applied to the whole
eleven-dimensional optical subspace.

Writing `q=|k|^2/8`, `R4=3r^4/8`, `beta=4-r` and
`d=r^2/(4-r-r^2/2)^2`, spectral resolution gives

    |u*H^-1u-1/q| <= R4/[q(q-R4)] + d/(q-R4) + d/beta.

The inverse block identity, `QHQ >= beta Q` and `||QHu|| <= r` give

    ||u*H^-1Q|| <= r/[beta(q-R4)].

These statements retain acoustic and optical contributions. For a signed
localized pair, `|u*rhohat| <= 2/sqrt(12)` and `||Q rhohat|| <= 2`.
The normalized inverse Fourier factor and potential scaling give
`Phi_h=h^2 u*H^-1rhohat/sqrt(12)` and limit
`Phi=(2/3) sum(rhohat)/|xi|^2`. The resulting potential error is the
producer bound, with the safe rational `3/5 > 1/sqrt(3)`. Its cross term
is O(h); explicit averaging over internal ports removes it and gives
O(h^2). Averaging is a distinct source comparison.

half-trace distance and all terminal outcome laws; using a cap of one at

    TV <= min(1, 2 exp(-lambda T)
