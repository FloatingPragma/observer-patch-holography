# INS-03 v2 authenticated binding (design, unimplemented)

This document designs the custody half of the INS-03 v2 gate: the
authenticated binding that would tie a primitive transcript to an owner
key, a frozen semantics, a configuration, and outcomes. Nothing in it is
implemented. No owner key exists, no producer exists, no run exists, and
no signature has ever been made or verified under this design. The
verification hook it names is present in
`transcript_replay_verifier.py` as `verify_authenticated_binding` and is
UNIMPLEMENTED, failing closed with
`PRODUCER_AUTHENTICATION_UNIMPLEMENTED` on every invocation.

Governing records: the deep audit
`plan/audits/RER_POST_R2020_ACTUAL_COMMITS_DEEP_AUDIT_2026-08-24.md`
(finding F3 and completion item 1), the plan section V3.26 of
`plan/COMPLETION_PLAN_V3.md`, the v1 contract
`code/phase_instrument_export/VALIDATOR_CONTRACT.md`, and the companion
`PRIMITIVE_GENERATION_SEMANTICS.md` in this directory.

## A. Why a self-digest cannot substitute

The audit's ruling is exact and this design is built around it:
conformance certifies structure only; production, provenance, and custody
claims require authenticated generation semantics, transcripts, and
independent replay, and until those exist the verdict vocabulary must say
so.

A self-digest fails as custody for a structural reason, not an
implementation gap. The `transcript_digest_sha256`,
`custody_digest_sha256`, and `preparation_content_sha256` fields are
digests computed over content by whoever wrote the content. Any party
holding the bytes recomputes them after any edit, so a matching
self-digest establishes only that the document is internally consistent
under its declared serialization. It binds the content to no agent, no
key, no time, and no execution: it cannot distinguish a producer's output
from an editor's fabrication, and the v2 test
`test_self_digest_refresh_authenticates_nothing` executes exactly that
distinction. The same holds for self-declared module hashes, commits, run
identifiers, and runtime logs, which the v1 contract refuses as
authentication. What a self-digest is good for is content addressing: a
stable name for one byte stream, used by this design as the thing signed,
never as the signature.

## B. Key custody

* One signing keypair, generated by the owner of instrument lane
  [#737](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/issues/737)
  in a recorded generation event: algorithm, parameters, public key, and
  generation statement registered as freeze artifacts under the
  register's `{path, sha256}` artifact rule.
* The private key never enters any repository, transcript, export,
  receipt, or log. Its storage medium and access policy are declared in
  the generation statement.
* The public key is the verification anchor: the verifier accepts only
  signatures checking against the registered public key at the registered
  algorithm. A key rotation or revocation is a new registration event; a
  signature under a revoked key verifies nothing.
* The candidate algorithm is Ed25519 over the payload of section C; the
  algorithm choice is a declared choice that binds at the freeze event,
  not here.

## C. Signature payload

The signature covers the canonical serialization (the
`json.dumps(obj, sort_keys=True, indent=2, ensure_ascii=False)` convention
of the v1 module, UTF-8, no trailing line break) of the binding body:

```json
{
  "domain": "oph.ins03.authenticated_binding.v2_proposed",
  "semantics_digest_sha256": "<digest of the frozen semantics document>",
  "verifier_digest_sha256": "<digest of the frozen verifier module>",
  "transcript_digest_sha256": "<the transcript's self-digest>",
  "configuration_digest_sha256": "<digest of the run configuration>",
  "outcomes_digest_sha256": "<digest of the public outcome records>"
}
```

* `semantics_digest_sha256` pins the exact frozen
  `PRIMITIVE_GENERATION_SEMANTICS.md`; a signature over an unfrozen or
  different semantics binds nothing under this design.
* `verifier_digest_sha256` pins the replay verifier the owner froze
  alongside the semantics.
* `transcript_digest_sha256` names the transcript by content; the
  signature converts that self-digest from a name into a commitment by the
  key holder.
* `configuration_digest_sha256` covers the run configuration: producer
  module hashes, simulator and RER commit pins, the input inventory, the
  excluded-input set, and the environment record, in the custody shape of
  design section E of `plan/INS03_SOURCE_BOUND_PHASE_INSTRUMENT_DESIGN.md`.
* `outcomes_digest_sha256` covers the public outcome record manifest of
  design section C.2 (b), recorded before analysis.
* The `domain` string separates this signature from every other use of
  the key.

The transcript carries the result in its reserved
`authenticated_binding` field: the binding body, the algorithm name, the
public-key identifier, and the signature bytes in a declared encoding.

## D. The verification hook

`verify_authenticated_binding(node, where, errors)` in
`transcript_replay_verifier.py` is the named hook this design would
implement. Its implemented behavior would be: recompute every digest of
the binding body from the referenced artifacts independently, resolve the
public key from the register, verify the signature over the canonical
body, and fail closed with a named error on any mismatch, unknown key,
revoked key, or unregistered algorithm. Its shipped behavior is: fail
closed with `PRODUCER_AUTHENTICATION_UNIMPLEMENTED` on every invocation,
because no key exists and no registration exists to verify against. The
hook never degrades to a warning; a producer claim without a verifiable
signature is a failure, not a caveat.

## E. What evidential weight requires

Under the corrected INS-03 v2 gate, a producer transcript carries
evidential weight only under the conjunction of all of the following, each
necessary and none sufficient alone:

1. **Owner-frozen semantics.** `PRIMITIVE_GENERATION_SEMANTICS.md` and the
   replay verifier are frozen by the owner with registered digests
   (section H of that document). An unfrozen grammar can be edited to fit
   any output.
2. **Authenticated signature.** A signature under the registered owner key
   over the section C binding body, verified by the implemented hook. An
   unsigned or self-digested transcript is unattributed bytes.
3. **Independent replay.** The transcript replays and verifies under an
   implementation that did not produce it, from the committed starting
   objects, with every intermediate digest and the final export matching.
   A signature over an unreplayable transcript authenticates a claim, not
   a derivation.
4. **Target-blind deletion and mutation controls.** The controls of design
   section D.5 pass: deleting the claimed source operation from the
   transcript removes the claimed effect or forces
   `SOURCE_PRODUCER_MISSING`, and mutating target labels does not change
   what the frozen semantics generates.

Even that full conjunction certifies production under the frozen grammar,
that is, that the named key holder committed to this transcript and that
the transcript derives its export. It does not by itself discharge PR-65:
the decision rule of design section C, its preparation and provenance
gates, and its public-outcome custody run on top of this binding, and the
discrimination gate can still return `SOURCE_PRODUCER_MISSING` on a
record-diagonal preparation.

## What is not proved here

No part of this design is implemented: no key exists, no signature
exists, no producer exists, no run exists, and no freeze event exists.
The hook fails closed on every invocation, so no transcript can reach a
producer or custody verdict through this repository. PR-64 and PR-65 are
open, public outcomes are open, and OL-C5 stays `partial`. Nothing here
is source production, nothing arms an instrument, draws a seed, freezes a
rule, or scores a comparison, and nothing here supports any physical
claim of the OPH program.
