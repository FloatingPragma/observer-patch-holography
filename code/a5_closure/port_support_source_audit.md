## Findings and remedies

**HIGH — no relative morphism in the admitted geometric source data.**
`DERIVATION.md` §1 gives each carrier its own local twelve-port boundary;
§3 makes local chart holonomy distinct from federation carriers. The support
construction marks vertex zero by fibre multiplicity and a process link,
but supplies no port at that host and no ordered local tangent frame.
Remedy: produce one primitive law relating the local port object to support
incidence, with its change-of-presentation action, before testing its degree.

**HIGH — endpoint rounding cannot carry the naive fixed A5 action.** The
implemented convention uses `max(edge)`. Under the standard lifted action
on subdivision vertices, 59 nonidentity seed rotations fail its square.
More generally, for each seed edge e={a,b}, the certificate emits an
order-two proper rotation fixing e and swapping a,b. It fixes the midpoint
m_e. An equivariant rule c(m_e) in {a,b} would have to fix its chosen endpoint
under that rotation, which is impossible. This proves a no-go **only for
endpoint-valued midpoint rounding with that action**. Transporting the
entire declared coarsening as presentation data is consistent. Geometric
realization with genuine midpoints also survives. Remedy: state which
category and maps the sought attachment uses; do not call recomputed max
intrinsically natural.

**MEDIUM — historical pins drift.** Two historical attachment pins fail on
current bytes: DERIVATION.md and m1_source_realization/receipt.json. The
former changes the wording at its old lines 414–416 concerning additional
repair-law conditions; it does not introduce a relative attachment. The
latter changes operational/numerical evidence and source custody. Its full
`evidence.topology` is exactly unchanged. The frozen hashes were not edited.
Remedy: retain `BASELINE_PIN_DRIFT`, replay at the matching historical
commit, and audit live topology separately, as done here.

**MEDIUM — continuous theorem acceptance required.** The [proof proposed
for acceptance](continuous_equivariant_degree_proof.md) supplies the relative
homotopies with a strict normalization bound, a finite-dimensional transverse
perturbation on the free source, and the same-element normalizer comparison
in both faithful rotation representations. The cyclic lemma covers arbitrary
continuous maps, including nonisolated initial fibres and critical poles.
Its reconstruction and finite geometric controls do not constitute acceptance
by a distinct mathematical reviewer. Remedy: obtain that acceptance before
promoting the theorem gate. No replay of an original external proof packet
is claimed; the proposed argument is self-contained.

## Refinement boundary and finite squares

The live source reconstructs support pairs (vertices,faces)
`(12,20),(42,80),(162,320),(642,1280)` and nerve carrier counts
`13,44,165,646`. At each stage `b_r s_r=id` and `(b_r)_*z_r=[S_r]` exactly.
With q_r:N_(r+1)→N_r and c_r:S_(r+1)→S_r the actual coarsenings,
the finite checks establish

    b_r q_r = c_r b_(r+1),
    q_r s_(r+1) = s_r c_r,
    (q_r)_* z_(r+1) = z_r.

All arrows here are fine to coarse. These are support/nerve squares, not
the missing local square `q_r h_(r+1)=h_r u_r`. No K_r local incidence tower,
u_r or selected h_r has been constructed by this audit. ScreenCarrierMapCandidate
proves scaling of barycentric coordinates inside the same parent face and
explicitly leaves a committed refined complex open. Copying the support
tower onto K and choosing p by whether it commutes with max would add exactly
the presentation matching under investigation. The unique identity among
commuting permutations is therefore not a source-clean selector.

For the displayed marked generator, midpoint 18 represents edge {1,3}; its
lift is midpoint 17 of {1,2}. The square gives

    c(p1(18)) = c(17) = 2,
    p0(c(18)) = p0(3) = 1.

The exact edge identities and values are checked by the independent incidence
reconstruction. No geometric embedding coordinates are used to select this
falsifier.

The comparison `radial_± ~ iota_r |b_r| h_r` still lacks a source-selected
h_r, common oriented target and proof of equivariance/homotopy. Equal seed
triangles and abstract degree-one cycles supply none of those. No infinite
tower theorem follows from checking four levels. Even an accepted continuous
degree floor would give only a conditional twin exclusion after these
premises and would identify at most a representation class, not a unique
physical frame or observer.
