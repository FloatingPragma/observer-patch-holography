# B15 matter class freeze v1 (issue #706)

Mirror of `matter_class_freeze_v1.json`. The JSON is the binding artifact; on
any divergence the JSON governs. Validate with

    python3 code/b15_matter_freeze/validate_matter_class_freeze.py

## Role and claim boundary

This document fixes the B15 source grammar, candidate class, budget,
observables, and decision rule before any comparison, under the plan's
no-privilege protocol (freeze everything, then compare). It performs no
selection, evaluates no candidate against laboratory data, identifies no
laboratory matter multiplet, and closes no deliverable of issue #706 beyond
the freeze itself. Every count below is replayed by the validator from the
frozen structural data alone. `selection_performed` is recorded as false.

## Source packet

The B14 certificate (`code/b14_jacobi/b14_compact_locus.certificate.json`)
classifies the compact real locus of the fourteen-parameter A5-equivariant
Jacobi variety on the twelve-port carrier with sector decomposition 1+3+3+5.
The locus is the union of exactly three families:

| family | description | generic algebra |
|---|---|---|
| P | closed plane, `t_pp_to_p` and `t_mm_to_m` free, other channels zero; compact at every point | so(3)+so(3)+R^6 |
| F | `t_pp_to_p=a`, `t_ff_to_p=b`, `t_pf_to_f=-sqrt(5)a`, `t_mm_to_m=e`, others zero; compact for a nonzero, a\*b<0 | su(3)+so(3)+R |
| G | mirror of F under the swap of the two three-dimensional sectors; compact for a nonzero, a\*b>0 | su(3)+so(3)+R |

The bracket lives on the carrier Q^12, so the carrier in its adjoint action is
the single source-supplied module.

## Grammar and budget

A candidate is a pair (S, M): S a stratum of the classified compact locus and
M a nonempty Z/2-graded finite-dimensional complex module of the complexified
semisimple part of the bracket at S, with zero abelian charges, total
dimension at most 12, counted up to graded isomorphism and the global parity
flip. The module theory depends only on the stratum's bracket type. Points
inside one stratum carry isomorphic brackets; the choice of the point is owned
by the issue #705 source response.

The dimension cap 12 is the declared structural budget under the plan's
equal-budget clause: it equals the dimension of the twelve-port source
carrier, the only scale the packet supplies. Changing the cap is a grammar
change that requires a fresh freeze version before any evaluation. The
degree-wise exterior tower of the carrier is the natural second budget rung;
any comparison there requires its own frozen enumeration before evaluation.

## Constraints and justification chain

| id | status | constraint | justification |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | imposed | total complex dimension at most 12 | declared budget; carrier dimension from the stage-1 basis pin and certificate premise MP1 |
| C2 | imposed | complex modules of the complexified bracket | declared grammar item; complete for the frozen invariants, real type stays an observable |
| C3 | imposed | abelian charges in the source-certified charge lattice, which is {0} | every certified sample has central sectors acting trivially; the packet certifies no abelian period, B16 (#707) owns it and is open |
| C4 | imposed | semisimple weights in the integral weight lattice of the certified bracket | intrinsic to the certified algebra; widest lattice the packet defines, adds no assumption |
| C5 | recorded | carrier-lattice refinement: weights in the sublattice generated by the carrier weights (the root lattice: odd A1 dimensions, triality-zero A2 labels) | equivalent to realizability over the group generated by exponentiating the certified generators on the carrier; group and holonomy promotion are open in #705, so this stays a flagged subclass with recorded counts |
| C6 | imposed | Z/2 chirality grading, trivial grading admissible, identified under the global parity flip | declared grammar item matching the #706 chirality deliverable; excludes no module |
| C7 | recorded | exact anomaly functionals: A2 cubic anomaly A(p,q) = (p-q)(p+2q+3)(2p+q+3)dim(p,q)/60 with A(1,0)=1, zero on A1; abelian and mixed functionals vanish by C3; graded sum and the global Z/2 functional frozen as observables | no source receipt derives a finite anomaly obstruction, so vanishing is a recorded subclass with counts, never a membership constraint |
| C8 | imposed | the zero module is excluded | structural nondegeneracy |

Constraints considered and left out, with the class kept larger as a result:
faithfulness (inert and duplicated sectors are named #706 controls; kernel
pattern stays an observable), anomaly vanishing (C7), carrier-lattice
integrality (C5), self-duality and reality (observable only),
irreducibility (modules are arbitrary direct sums under the budget), and any
condition phrased in laboratory quantum numbers (barred).

## Observables

Selection may consult: decomposition multiplicities; total and graded
dimensions; weight multiplicities under a fixed Cartan; kernel pattern per
simple summand; endomorphism algebra dimension; Frobenius-Schur indicators and
invariant-form symmetry type (Clifford and Spin compatibility data); graded
and ungraded anomaly sums; the global Z/2 functional; behavior under the F/G
mirror swap and the plane-family automorphisms; occurrence multiplicities in
exterior powers of the carrier (degree 0 through 12) and in carrier tensor
powers within the same budget; the inherited A5-equivariant structure.

Barred as selection inputs: lepton masses, quark masses, mixing angles,
laboratory quantum numbers (electric charge, weak isospin, hypercharge, color
assignments), experimental family and generation counts, Standard Model
multiplet tables, any laboratory dataset and any quantity computed from one.

## Decision rule

Source selection is a deterministic rule whose inputs are limited to the
pinned B14 source receipts (with receipts produced by the open #705 physical
continuation from the same registered source) and the frozen observables
evaluated on the frozen candidate list. The rule evaluates every candidate
under the identical budget and outputs either exactly one pair, a bracket
point of the classified locus together with one candidate module, or
NO-SELECTION. NO-SELECTION is a valid negative exit of B15 and is recorded as
a result; failure to select never licenses weakening the grammar, raising the
budget, unbarring an observable, or re-ranking candidates after an evaluation
output has been seen. Any post-evaluation change to grammar, budget,
constraints, observables, or rule voids the run and requires a fresh freeze
version with a distinct filename and hash. A hard-coded candidate that matches
a known target proves nonemptiness of the class and never constitutes
selection.

## Validator

`validate_matter_class_freeze.py` replays the full enumeration from the frozen
structural data and compares every admissible irrep list and every count,
verifies every pinned file hash fail-closed, checks the mirror strata share
one bracket type, checks the negative-exit declaration, the
`selection_performed` flag, and the barred observable list, and spot-checks
the frozen anomaly normalization on five irreps. Exit code 0 with
`FREEZE VALID` on success; any mismatch prints `FREEZE INVALID` and exits
nonzero.
