# Exact candidate contract

Candidate: `A-paired-charge+moment` (N+), proposed single publication member.
The machine-readable CONTRACT.json fixes its finite inputs and interfaces.

## Law and source domain

The two native sources X,Y each contain binary loads on the same twelve ports,
with total two. An admitted initial pair is
`x=delta_p+delta_s`, `y=delta_r+delta_s`, with p,r,s distinct. There are1320 such
ordered preparations. The native endpoint is the pinned integer function:

    j=(x_a-x_b-(2c-1)(abs(x_a-x_b) mod2))/2,
    x'_a=x_a-j, x'_b=x_b+j.

The code uses integer floor division. Binary inputs make the displayed quotient
integral. The sources have separate current-only restoring censuses. Their
coin draws are fresh from one sequential IID stream; “independent censuses”
means separate controllers/records, with neither given its private initial
label. It does not mean that a second PRNG object is secretly required.

After both successful restorations, each body attempt uses the same seam e and
same placement coin c on X and Y. Seams are uniform among the30 existing seams;
coins are fair. Both local currents are recorded. No branch of the native body
selects a different repair law according to the requested row. The row label
chooses a disclosed observation protocol only.

## Memory and readout

Observers hold their addressed census/restoration records, decoded initial
configurations, complete addressed body records `(e,c,jX,jY)`, and failure flags.
The comparator reads `q=x-y`, `k=jX-jY`, and accumulates F. The incidence matrix B
has +1 at the smaller endpoint and -1 at the larger endpoint, so
`q_next=q-B_e k` and `BF=q_initial-q_terminal`. The physical charge dictionary
is submitted for adjudication; it is not part of this algebraic identity.

The same records admit the finite vector readout
`delta=k(v_b-v_a)`. Across the complete one-attempt law, m is its mean and C its
centered second moment. The v table is the source packet's exact reindexing of
the finite Lean current quotient. This establishes the record map and does not
identify a seam with a physical translation or select a physical clock.

## Two allowed experiments

`charge_response`: all1320 initial preparations with the two frozen seed
families. Censuses use cap12 per addressed probe or restoration edge. Continue
the common native body until the publicly reconstructed configurations agree,
or2048 attempts. Retain every timeout. Compare the stopped flux class modulo
oriented face relations with its independently read source. The original
exact branch/reward and integer-topology certificates are part of this task.

`initial_moments`: the120 antipodal preparations `(p,antipode(p),s)` with the
frozen seed sequence. Use the identical census/restoration. On each successful
read preparation compute its full60-branch first-attempt instrument. These
branches are separate alternatives, not60 sequential events or copies of an
unknown quantum state. A physical sampling version would prepare fresh sources
and draw an addressed branch from the same distribution. The exact instrument
calculation is the finite numerical test actually performed here.

Both experimental memories and caps are finite. The66-state binary restoration
graph has a simple route of at most65 edges; a conservative census bound is
`11*12+65*12=912` native calls per source. Thus a charge trial has at most5920
native calls and at most3872 stored census/body tuples; the branch calculation
for one restored moment input has at most1944 native calls. These loose bounds
count apparatus work, not physical time. The executed receipts give exact counts.

The two experiments concern the same prepared source class and continuation.
They do not form one sequential nondestructive measurement of all observables:
after charge synchronization q is zero, and its later relative-current moments
are zero. Row7 uses a fresh measured preparation. Free persistence, electron
identity and the higher particle rows are separate tests.

## Controls and ancestry

The original frozen, common-swap and rational-half controls belong to the charge
task. The frozen/fixed-seam and off-axis controls belong to the moment task.
They are explicitly changed laws or plants; their outcomes are never counted
as native successes. This proposal adds compatibility checks without changing
the successful native law, its preparation domain, or original gates.

`A-paired-charge` is the charge ancestor. `A-paired-charge+moment` adds the finite
vector/tensor decoder to that process. Frozen receipts keep those labels. The
single proposed new pin contains the common contract, both permitted protocols
and both source packets. This is a design and reproducibility result. It does
not itself choose the column's pin, confirm G7, or award either row.
