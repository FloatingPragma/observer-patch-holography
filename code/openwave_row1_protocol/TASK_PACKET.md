## Decision and interpretation

A passing task establishes the finite carrier/current-derived spatial charge and complete native apparatus on the declared domain. The maintainer separately decides whether that mechanism meets the physical-charge interpretation of criterion 1, and records any supplied architecture in the cell's method link. The claim is relative charge n−G. No electric-unit calibration, universal integer decoding from bare modulo-six memory, extension to coherent CAR dynamics or award for another row follows.

The integer event alphabet and whole-unit preservation are properties of the declared source law. Continuity alone also holds for fractional currents, and closed-map integer degree holds for integer tapes that no native trajectory produces. The phase map is an observer's encoding of the current class. Independent opposing reviews therefore support at most a partial physical interpretation; agreement on the finite identities is not agreement to a validated cell. This packet preserves that interpretation dispute for platform adjudication.

Two independent methods and their mutation outcomes must accompany the acceptance execution. The existing development reviews are transparent prior evidence; they do not replace prospective task registration or independent platform reproduction.
