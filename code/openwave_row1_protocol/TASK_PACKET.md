## Prospective independent acceptance scope

After candidate identity and these gates are accepted, an independent reviewer chooses and records source seam sequences of lengths four through eight, initial one-load ports and regional masks before reading their output. Those choices are not executed by this packet. The reviewer runs both coin branches at every source step, retains every complete source history, and checks the meter as a finite stopping policy with exact grouped success and discovery/routing/measurement timeout laws. An initial pointer port is an input column, not an inferred distribution. Run all twelve columns. Both signs and zero regional degree must occur across the declared suite.

The observer is restricted to the known preparation contract and emitted currents. Diagnostic terminal loads can be removed from its input before meter execution. No expected winding, final occupation or receipt value may be supplied as a meter command. Source coordinates and region labels are declared architecture, not fitted physical data.

The static finite criterion does not require a particle mass, spin or force law. Those observables require their own source-bound tasks.
