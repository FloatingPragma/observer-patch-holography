# Same-group review of the native occupation theorem

No mathematical blocker found in sections1–8 of the bound source. The review checks the finite covariance remainder, analytic Gaussian and higher-moment limit, native auxiliary-coin completion, rooted Green identity and finite Dirichlet scalar reflection positivity. The source pin and protocol expansion remain explicit. This is a written-proof review, not an execution review or another-group G6 pass.

The reflection expansion is justified by an integrable absolute majorant: for each side Laplacian block A, xᵀAx >= |x|ᵀA|x| because the off-diagonal entries are nonpositive. After replacing the crossing product by its absolute value, the resulting integrand is bounded by the full positive Dirichlet Gaussian evaluated on absolute coordinates. This is a useful explicit intermediate step, with no change to the result.
