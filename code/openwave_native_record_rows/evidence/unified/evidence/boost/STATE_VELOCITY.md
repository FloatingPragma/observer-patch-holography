# Velocity and state accounting

The rest packet's radial momentum amplitude has zero mean spatial momentum.
Its mean four-momentum is `(Ebar,0)`, with finite `Ebar>m`. In the unitary
boosted state it is `(gamma Ebar,gamma v Ebar,0,0)`. The center of energy
therefore travels at velocity v by the integrated stress conservation law.
This is a state-velocity statement independent of the finite-width intensity
contraction calculation. The latter retains its spreading correction.

The boost changes the energy expectation by `(gamma-1)Ebar`. A physical
apparatus preparing that state must supply and retain this work. The current
packet derives the reconstructed state/observable functional from the common
source's two- and four-point moments. It does not claim a zero-work kick, an
executed state-preparation service or a detector record obtained by directly
moving primitive ports at v.

This supplement was written after the numerical width analysis. It changes
no source, analytic input, numerical threshold or output.
