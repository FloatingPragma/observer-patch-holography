# Method note

Native branch counts determine L_internal and hence lambda. The common
source moment theorem determines covariance(p_E²+lambda)^-1. Positive
reflection reconstructs L²(d³p/(2E)); invariant mass-shell measure fixes
unitary boosts. PROOF.md equations(1)-(4) derive the boosted envelope and
its Parseval RMS widths. check_boost.py evaluates those exact finite
integrals for all registered inputs and retains every result.

The source-word covariance and its limit are supplied by bound theorem and
actual-record artifacts listed in SOURCE_PINS.json. We do not replace
native evolution with a field Hamiltonian. The36 checks compare quadrature
orders and a1% narrow-packet gate; the analytic proof establishes the limit.

Scope: reconstructed free-field states and intensity functionals; no executed
boost preparation, interacting charged object, finite-lattice Lorentz group,
other-group G6 or platform acceptance.
