# Curvature from the changing volume of a freely falling cohort

Use only the existing three-arm radar tapes and independently read scalar stress. At each radar appointment estimate the infinitesimal comoving volume in reference-cell units by the product of the three simultaneous normalized round trips. Center-difference volume twice in proper time and measure the three directional expansion rates from the individual arms. For the orthogonal, freely falling, nonrotating cohort read the timelike Ricci contraction as minus the fractional second derivative of the volume plus twice the sum of pairwise products of the rates. Independently compare with κ(ρ+Σp)/2−Λ using the mass spectrum and frozen ground/x calibration. Include the y, z, even-x and later x appointments at all three spacings, and a field propagating on vacuum geometry. At the finest spacing require a maximum held error below 0.0001 in box units.

Check contraction in flat spacetime by placing three inertial mirrors on orthogonal axes with inward coordinate velocity of magnitude equal to the initial arm spacing, setting the light speed to one. Seen from the inertial origin, their separation per unit spacing is 1−t. An echo centered on t has half-duration equal to the separation at reflection. Record and quantize the send/receive times and apply the identical analysis. Require decreasing volume and a curvature estimate approaching zero with decreasing step. Archive uncorrected estimates to expose the effect of initial expansion and shear. Compare the three arms through one time direction only.
