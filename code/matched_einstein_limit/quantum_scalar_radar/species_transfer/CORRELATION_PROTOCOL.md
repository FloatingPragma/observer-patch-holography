# Can two quantum states with identical local field records source distinguishable geometry?

The ordinary two-species gravity-transfer experiment fixes the single-field gravity calibration and radar and area readers. This additional comparison uses two canonically normalized real scalar fields A and B with equal diagonal mass squared 0.7, comoving wave number one in all three directions, and homogeneous bilinear interaction energy density 0.3 ⟨AB⟩. The two orthogonal combinations (A±B)/√2 propagate with squared masses 1.0 and 0.4. All twelve standing-wave modes and their zero-point energies are retained. This quadratic interaction can be diagonalized exactly.

At the common central proper-clock mark 0.60 take unit scale factors and the three original shear offsets. Define all mode preparations using one flat reference frequency √1.7. In each x-directed sine/cosine pair put one reference quantum in the plus combination and leave the minus combination empty, or reverse the occupied combination and leave the plus empty. The other axes are empty in both preparations. In the physical A,B basis the x-directed one-quantum states are (|1,0⟩±|0,1⟩)/√2. Each preparation therefore has the same complete reduced state of A and of B at the central mark. Their cross-field quadrature covariance changes sign. Propagate the constraint-compatible classical ADM geometry and the exact Gaussian canonical maps of the quadratic quantum Hamiltonian to both sides of that mark. Also compare a zero-interaction Hamiltonian with the same preparations, shape, shear offsets, readout instruments and frozen calibration.

Read the two fields' marginal q and p quadratures independently along each axis and read the q quadratures of (A+B)/√2 and (A−B)/√2 on separate copies. Their moment difference divided by two estimates the joint covariance. Use 2²⁷ independent samples at each setting, the standard quadrature bins and seed 298903. Calibrate the diagonal squared mass from the original A-only spectroscopy. Fix the off-diagonal mass coefficient from separate flat spectra for the plus and minus combinations before any curvature read. Use no gravitational data for this spectral calibration, no two-field data for the gravitational coupling, and no retuning at later epochs. Reconstruct energy and three pressures from the marginal moment records, the measured cross potential and observed lengths. Compare the two preparations' changes in radar G₀₀ and three spatial Einstein components at clock marks 0.60, 0.85 and 1.10, on the three original radar spacings. Use both the complete reader and a reader blind to the cross moment. The latter is an instrument that measures the two fields separately. Measure G₀₀ from the independent equal-proper-volume rim/interior instruments at 0.60, on the three original ball radii. All other curvature comparisons use radar alone.

At the central mark the largest local marginal moment difference must be below 0.002 across the enrolled A or B quadratures. At the finest spacing require an absolute radar tensor-contrast discrepancy below 0.0002 at all three held times for the complete reader. On the interacting central preparation require |ΔG₀₀| above 0.001, the marginal-only temporal prediction to miss by over 0.001, and the independent area contrast at radius 0.1 to agree with the complete prediction to within 0.0002. The zero-interaction generator must give identical radar ticks for the two states; the finest-area contrast must be below 0.00001. Include the full refinement sequence and the null contractions Δ(G₀₀+Gᵢᵢ) at the central mark. These provide an independent check of the pure-potential stress signature, whose null contractions vanish at identical marginal state and metric.
