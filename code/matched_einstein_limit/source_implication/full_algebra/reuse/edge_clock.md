# What one native radar edge identifies

On the 80-cell survey, a single directed route and a single probe charge suffice to reconstruct the normalized stored head state from first-relay and completed-return timestamps. This holds on each of the twelve directed routes. Unknown parity, both coin marginals, their correlations and head entropy are all accessible from the same probability record. The observer uses independent preparations and retains every launch in the denominator. [Rational native calibration and interval bounds](edge_clock_history.py) · [Per-route certificates and worked affine matrices](edge_clock_history.json)

## Two timestamps

Start at station $i$, interrogate the relay $j$ at appointments separated by $1/2$ unit of native time, and after its first hit interrogate the original station for the return. For an original launch define $a$ as its first relay appointment and $t$ as its first completed-return appointment, counted from launch. The branch amplitude for this event is $V_{a,t}=F^{ji}_{t-a}F^{ij}_a$, where $F$ denotes the monitored first-hit amplitude on the specified leg. Thus its absolute probability is $\operatorname{Tr}(V_{a,t}^\dagger V_{a,t}\rho)$. The population at a timeout contributes zero to each specified success bin. Whether the source would be disturbed by another measurement on the same individual is immaterial to this independent-preparation protocol.

With the charge specified, the 36 events $1\le a<t\le9$ have rank **31** for each of the two charges on every directed edge. On the physical route $38\to1$, station indices $2\to1$, the inverse gain from the Euclidean probability errors to Euclidean head-coordinate errors is below $1.969\times10^9$ for a vacancy and $1.031\times10^{11}$ for excess. These bounds quantify the possible amplification of counting noise. The estimate uses the affine coordinates of the full-clock proof and no supplied source marginals. Therefore any independently calibrated Hermitian head observable can be inferred on that single route. The observable's calibration supplies its physical meaning. [Coordinate definitions and continuity estimates](CLOCK_HISTORY.md) · [Charge-specific ranks and conditioning](edge_clock_history.json)

Retaining the first-relay hit through an unfinished return advances single-charge identification to tick 8 on all twelve routes. The 28 paired completed-return events and eight first-relay marginals then have rank 31. Tick 7 supplies at most $21+7=28$ independent probabilities, so tick 8 is the earliest possible for this 31-parameter family using these two timestamps. [Earliest single-charge certificates](edge_clock_history.json)

The complete through-tick-12 record can even identify the head when the observer has **no charge tag and no prior charge probability**. Allow the stored head to differ arbitrarily between the two probe classes. Write its unnormalized charge blocks as $X_q=p_q I_8/8+\sum_k x_{qk}D_k$, where $p_- = 1/2-z$, $p_+ = 1/2+z$ and the 31 traceless $D_k$ are the enrolled head directions. The absolute observed probability vector is
$$
y=(b_-+b_+)/2+A_-x_-+A_+x_++(b_+-b_-)z .
$$
Here $A_q,b_q$ are the two known per-charge affine calibrations. At each of the twelve directed routes the 66 events $1\le a<t\le12$ have rank **63** on the normalized joint state. A single charge-unobserved calibration therefore determines both unnormalized blocks, including when one weight vanishes. Summing the blocks recovers the marginal stored head. The gains bound sensitivity to noise. On $38\to1$ the joint inverse gain is below $1.451\times10^{11}$. This record counts a completed return on the final tick as a success. [Single-route calibration including absolute offsets](edge_clock_history.json)

The observer can finish **one tick before the cap** by retaining the relay log even when the probe has yet to return. Use the 55 completed $(a,t)$ events up to tick 11 together with the 11 unconditional first-relay probabilities $\Pr(a)$ up to tick 11. These events may be derived from disjoint histories by adding the completed branches back to the corresponding relay-without-return counts. Their 66-row joint map again has rank 63 on **every** directed route, including both charge probabilities and all physical head coordinates. The inverse gain on $38\to1$ falls below $8.647\times10^{10}$. Through tick 10 there are only 45 completed branches and 10 first-relay probabilities. The complementary probability follows by normalization, so tick 11 is the earliest possible identification of this 63-parameter family using these two timestamps. [Pre-cap joint certificates](edge_clock_history.json)

For a replay on $38\to1$, divide the two **native_affine_clock** matrices and their **offsets** by **affine_denominator** ($8\cdot2^{100}$). The first 36 rows of each matrix are the through-tick-9 completed returns. The 66 rows use the ordered $(a,t)$ pairs in **event_labels**. For the strictly pre-cap menu append the eleven **relay_matrix** rows and **relay_offsets** to the first 55 completed rows; they are ordered by first-relay tick 1 through 11. Their operator errors are the last eleven **event_effect_error_upper** entries. The first 66 entries of **event_effect_error_upper** bound the operator errors on these events. An absolute offset error is bounded by the corresponding operator error and each slope error by twice that value.

The verifier bounds each rounded first-hit amplitude using the native integer-generator kernel. For a product of two rounded first hits, with approximants $\bar F_a,\bar F_b$ and operator error bounds $e_a,e_b$, its amplitude error is at most $e_a\|\bar F_b\|+e_b\|\bar F_a\|+e_a e_b+8/2^{100}$. For amplitude error $u$ the event-effect error is at most $(2\|\bar V\|+u)u+8/2^{100}$. Integer squared Frobenius norms bound the two displayed operator norms. The program quantizes a proposed left inverse $L$ and evaluates $I-L\bar A$ by exact integer multiplication. For $d$ affine parameters it adds an operator-error allowance of $2\sqrt d\sum_k e_k\|L_{:k}\|_2$, using the appropriate effect error for each event. A total defect $v<1$ proves full column rank and gives inverse gain $\|L\|_F/(1-v)$. Speeds, metric lengths and field equations play no role in this rank proof. ## What survives after discarding the relay appointment

Now keep only the **completed return time before the cap**, treating tick 12 and later completions as the complementary outcome. There are ten possible nontrivial timestamps, $t=2,\ldots,11$, on each directed route. Tick 1 is impossible. Dropping the relay time sums distinct event effects over $a$. For each of the six undirected edges in the four-station survey the following ranks hold on the 31-dimensional normalized head.

| Information retained | Rank for either charge or assignment | Joint rank of the two maps | Dimension of common linear readings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Forward direction alone | 10 | 20 | 0 |
| Reverse direction alone | 10 | 20 | 0 |
| Directions averaged with equal weights | 10 | 20 | 0 |
| Both direction labels, same charge in both directions | 20 | 31 | 9 |
| Both direction labels, opposite charge assignments $(−,+)$ and $(+,−)$ | 20 | 31 | 9 |

A common reading may use different calibrated functions on the two charge records and must assign the same value to every eligible source. If $A$ and $B$ are the corresponding linear parts, the common linear covectors form $\operatorname{row}(A)\cap\operatorname{row}(B)$ of dimension $r_A+r_B-r_{[A;B]}$. On a connected convex open set of sources the same quotient characterizes arbitrary common readings. A shared reading is locally invariant along both kernels, hence along their sum by moving in consecutive short steps inside the open set. Subdivision along a line proves constancy on each slice parallel to their sum. In particular a zero-dimensional entry forces every common reading to be constant on that set. The native preparation table contains an open set of full affine dimension. [Native preparation interior](../PROOF.md) · [Exact rank certificates](edge_clock.json)

If the two directions can independently select vacancy or excess, a common reading has to work on all four assignments. Write $A,C$ for the two forward row spaces and $B,D$ for the two reverse row spaces. The certified triple ranks are $\dim(A+B+D)=\dim(C+B+D)=30$. The ten-dimensional summands in either triple sum directly. Thus $(A+B)\cap(A+D)=A$ and $(C+B)\cap(C+D)=C$. The forward joint rank proves $A\cap C=0$. Every shared scalar is consequently constant in the interior for this record. With a fixed common charge across directions the nine-dimensional result applies. [All six edge checks](edge_clock.py)

The middle edge $1\leftrightarrow38$ illustrates why machine precision alone gives the wrong count. The forward and reverse tick-2 probability rows differ in at least one coefficient by more than $3.69\times10^{-25}$ for vacancy and $5.39\times10^{-25}$ for excess. This asymmetry lies below the ordinary calibration enclosure. For the middle edge alone the verifier computes the half-step $\exp(-iH/4)$ with the integer generator and a degree-104 Taylor polynomial using exact integer powers. The reverse propagator block is the transpose of the forward block because $H$ is real symmetric. Its projected operator error is at most $e=(43/4)^{105}/105!$ by the integral remainder for a skew-Hermitian exponential. After multiplying the reverse-minus-forward tick-2 row by $2^{76}$, its entry error is bounded by $4\cdot2^{76}[(1+e)^4-1]+1/(8\cdot2^{100})<10^{-28}$. Replacing one redundant-looking row with this rescaled difference preserves the exact rank. The reported transformed singular-value bound on this edge refers to the rescaled rows. The raw difference has Euclidean norm below $6.82\times10^{-25}$ or $1.06\times10^{-24}$ respectively. [High-precision verifier and raw bounds](edge_clock.json)
