# A two-head measurement detects the deficit of uncontrolled radar reuse

The original 80-cell survey has two different volume-matched preparations. One maximizes \(F=S+\eta A\) on the enrolled head algebra. The other is the attracting head ensemble obtained by uniformly choosing the original radar routes and probe charges, keeping both heads after every cycle and retaining every timeout. At the same calibrated tetrahedral volume, the unconstrained head output of the repeated survey has **at least 0.015834 nats less \(F\)**. Measuring one binary joint coin product on each preparation suffices to bound the deficit.
