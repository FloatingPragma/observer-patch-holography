## Physical interpretation and unresolved discriminator

Only one energy scale is admitted: assigning511keV to the selected positive
phase. The phase clock then predicts pair composition and a second phase band.
No laboratory number is used to choose seam weights, filter length or Fourier
resolution. The quantities tested here are operational quasienergies on a
specified finite clock, with actual empty-cell transfer. All integer-word
records are invariant under theta -> theta+2pi*k. The compiler, transfer and
pair test do not distinguish these branches. Principal positive phase is an
explicit bounded-band convention, not a proof of unrestricted physical energy,
proper time, inertia, or an electron mass. A continuous/source-resolved clock
or a further work/dispersion experiment must discriminate this alias before
calling the packet a physical rest-energy solution. The old logarithmic pair
excess and its80 lower modes retain their original disposition.
