# What the new finite tests prove

## The old tensor return is insensitive to an equivariant law change

For antipodal q=e_p-e_anti(p), the native event contributes each incident
current vector with probability1/60 per relevant coin. The move-always law
doubles every nonzero first-current probability. If m and C are native mean
and covariance, its moments are m'=2m and C'=2C-2mm^T. Thus m parallel to the
source axis and C uniaxial imply precisely the same facts for the alternative.
The half-turn reverses both means and preserves both covariances. The exact
coefficients become parallel8(3-phi)/45 and transverse2(phi+2)/15. This is
the missing symmetry-preserving control. Changing the magnitudes does not
make the rotation return law-specific.

## Continued native repair does not exactly identify antipodal states

Each of the133 basis source states has sixty event alternatives. Vacuum has
sixty self transitions. Every nonzero signed pair has fifty self transitions
and ten outgoing alternatives, including absorption when adjacent. Therefore
the complete transition count matrix has diagonal entries strictly greater
than the sums of the off-diagonal row entries. It is nonsingular: if Mx=0
and |x_i| is maximal and nonzero, the diagonal term exceeds the sum of all
other terms in row i, a contradiction. Every finite power of P=M/60 is also
nonsingular. Hence distinct initial endpoint probability rows, including
antipodes, remain distinct at every finite time. This uses complete source
outcomes, reconstructed from the current tape, not an even decoder.

On the connected finite graph either signed unit can follow a selected path
to the other with a finite positive-probability event word. Contact absorbs.
Taking the minimum success probability and maximum path length over finitely
many states bounds the unabsorbed tail geometrically. Thus eventual equality
of future endpoint laws is equality to vacuum for every orientation. All
directional information disappears. Retaining the full past tape preserves
even more distinctions and cannot repair this route.

The eigenmode rates and finite total variations in RECEIPT.json are numerical
diagnostics, not an exact comparison theorem at every intermediate time.
They show no registered approximate-identification window: axis information
decays faster than sign information in the tested preparation pair. At128
attempts the sign TV is0.00441733 and explicitly projected axis TV5.84323e-6.
The exact nonsingularity/absorption statements do not exclude other state
spaces, record-fed controllers, clocks, source memories or observables.

## Ordinary port rotations do not generate the requested central sign

The executed72-degree proper cell rotation is a permutation r with r^5=I.
Its declared action on every signed source basis state is the induced ordinary
permutation U_r. Thus U_r^5=I on the entire linear source space, including
arbitrary signed/complex amplitudes, and U_r^10=I. The ideal two-path analyzer
reads minus probability(1-Re overlap)/2. It reads0 after the full ordinary
turn and1 for a deliberately planted relative minus. No native analyzer
implementation is inferred from this mathematical detector calculation.

This is not a theorem that positive-coefficient maps can never produce a
relative minus on a sign-changing optical state. The constructive alternative
in berry/ explicitly does so through actual transfer products around a
parameter path. Its supplied memory and rotation interface remain disclosed.
