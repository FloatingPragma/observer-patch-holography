# Method and execution accounting

The target is a three-position one-unit quantum source. There are two separate
one-unit marker registers on the same3M-site subdivided triangle graph, a
two-arm interference register, fresh coherent choice/coin/current flags and
an auxiliary vacuum reference. Native floor/ceil endpoint repair acts on
addressed target or marker seams. The comparator swaps every addressed seam.
All choices, memory, path analyzers, coordinate embedding, local routing and
eight-attempt marker cap are declared candidate architecture.

At a marker slot vM+k, M-k fair tickets route to star(v) and k to star(v+1),
with two equally likely source seams at each star. For M4/M8 these ticket
choices are ordinary finite fair-bit registers; the same selector is used
under both laws. Full-triangle preparation/filter stages declare a uniform
three-way selector, as in the ancestor. Target ticket, body coin, addressed-seam
workspace and current remain coherent during the target interaction. The
seam workspace is reversibly uncomputed using the retained path, classically
decoded marker position and coherent ticket before the eraser is applied.
No copied seam or address is discarded. Target choice/coin/current flags are
then coherently transformed into the complete eraser basis and that output
is read. Marker currents and the INITIAL warmup's fine records are committed;
they are not confused with the main target's coherent records. Every choice/
coin/current branch and every orthogonal eraser failure belongs to the full
instrument, including its conditional surviving source state.

The observers receive marker addresses, coins and currents, recorded herald
and failure flags, and the final path port. The scalar fringe comes from path
probabilities. Target vectors/densities in receipts are labelled simulator
audits, not privileged inputs to the decoder. The original target position
basis is supplied; its accepted contrast is process-selected from arbitrary
admitted input, with a proved nonzero success bound.

PILOT_PREREG precedes the fixed128-case pilot. CONFIRM_PREREG binds the full
source/current/outcome check after explicitly disclosing those pilot values.
ERROR_PREREG binds the new finite-error challenge before its execution.
No file's timestamp substitutes for those ledger entries. Independent
verification scripts and the small headline are reproductions of known
results, not new prospective experiments. Source tables are generated by
literal endpoint enumeration; products are exact rational propagation of
those tables. No sampled trajectories or experimental hardware data occur.
