## What this does not establish

This implements and checks a common **source instrument interface**, together
with its two observation modes and event embeddings. It does not compile
coherent preparation, the analyzer/eraser, or a universal record controller
from integer records. Those supplied services are the same open resources
identified by the row-7 packet. It is not evidence that a classical random coin
can be unread retroactively. It does not turn row-2 modular costs into a sharp
physical rest energy or a parameter loop into a physical spatial rotation.
Upstream pin selection and external review remain separate decisions.
