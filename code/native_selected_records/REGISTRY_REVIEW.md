## Mathematical check

For unit selection, convex integer endpoint repair preserves the initial finite
load box and total. The squared-load potential falls on a contrast of at least
two. The maximum-transport path argument supplies a positive-probability finite
word producing such a fall from every unbalanced state. The stated uniform IID
scheduler supplies a uniform block probability; finite descent gives almost-sure
entry. For a public bound B and N=12B vacuum-reservoir construction, |Q|≤N
then gives absolute0/+1 or0/−1 levels. Uniformly filled two-load reservoirs
violate that preparation hypothesis and supply no counterexample to it.

The two-current identity, earlier-probe incidence correction and spanning-tree
integration recover contrasts. An initial zero datum fixes the common offset
through the complete body tape even if its port fills. No hidden state needs
to enter that observer. In the selected class, a mixed edge exchanges with
probability one half. The conditional rate D/(2m), symmetry of the k-subset
transition matrix and stationary rate k(n-k)/(n(n-1)) follow on the stated
uniform graph schedule. Integrality itself is explicitly uncredited.

For the localized mode, write an orthogonal mean-zero vector as core m*1+z,
with sum z=0 and equal leaf coordinates -Nm/2. Its norm has m coefficient
beta=N(N+2)/2. With epsilon=1/[2(N+2)], the lower-bound m coefficient divided
by beta is w+3w/(2N)=w+3v. The z coefficient is at least
c*mu-2w(2N+3)(N-1)/N. For v>0 and the stated strict inequality both exceed
w+2v. Together with the explicit leaf eigenvector and connectedness, this gives
the unique lowest nonconstant mode. The conclusion is stronger than a numerical
ordering check and does not identify it as physical rest energy.

## Required hypothesis precision

1. State n≥2 and m≥1 for the unit/transport theorem, since uniform seam selection
   and k(n-k)/(n(n-1)) otherwise have undefined singleton cases.
2. The unit result's scheduler is IID uniform over edges with fair independent
   coins. The dictionary phrase “positive seam schedule” alone is too broad for
   D/(2m), the explicit waiting-time bound and the stated statistical test.
   Fixed positive nonuniform weights require the corresponding weighted formula.
3. Retain v>0 and mu>0 explicitly in the owner proof, paper and canonical
   leaf-mode statement, as in the copied supporting proof. At v=0, w=0 and the
   leaves disconnect; the claimed strict uniqueness does not follow. These
   positivity assumptions are part of the declared weighted source.

These precision requests apply to the bound wording. The executed graphs and
supporting leaf proof satisfy them. They do not refute those finite instances.
