## 6. Interface and scope

This theorem concerns the native event-count/mean-transfer clock, including
its exact source spectrum and finite flagged implementation. It does not
say an unread quantum source preserves a compact ray. It does not derive
unitary evolution or physical rest energy merely by naming a decay rate.
The coherent continuation U_L and its actual phase/energy reader are a
separate declared mode that must be implemented and tested on the selected
source sector. The new source-clock certificate can support that mode;
it cannot replace it. No physical proper-time normalization, full row
