# Controls

- The fixed-count clock N=1 uses the same actual native source columns
  and yields pair factor104/105 versus squared single(209/210)², a difference
  of1/44100. Poisson counting changes the complete event-count distribution;
  it does not insert a compensating pair phase.
- The same count decoder on a HOLD auxiliary sees movement bits0 and
  returns N=0 in all16 retained checks. The native clock instead permits
  and records zero, one and multiple body events per tick.
- The analytic HOLD-source law has T=I and generator0 with an active
  Poisson clock. This identifies the role of source dynamics; it is not
  counted as an executed native call or independent experimental discovery.
- Every unresolved16-bit word and every count/address cap remains a
  separate outcome. The finite implemented clock is not normalized into
  an exact Poisson law after dropping those outcomes.
- The rare-event clocks retain their finite nonadditivity. Their convergence
  is a theorem with an explicit bound, not a renamed finite success.

Integer alphabets, the declared clock unit, elementary exponential algebra
and a subsequent coherence/energy interpretation are not credited as
new physical outcomes. The mean-transfer generator is not confused with
the full unread quantum channel.
