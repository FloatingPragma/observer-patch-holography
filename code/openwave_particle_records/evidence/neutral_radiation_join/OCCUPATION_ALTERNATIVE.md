# Separately assessed local occupation intervention

Analytic design note only. This alternative is not executed, not consumed by
the characteristic-field result, and not an energy-conservation claim.

The native occupation Gaussian theorem and row29 field reconstruction use a
different source: one unit on a finite connected graph, P=I-L/(2W), with a
collective long-block decoder. A single initial kick changes only a transient
occupation sum at fixed graph. Its contribution to the normalized fluctuation
vanishes as n^(-1/2); naming the kick a radiation creator would not repair this.

A local law perturbation can have a nonzero limiting response. For one unit
edge a--b of weight1, change the native moving-coin probability on that edge
to 1/2+εj when the unit is at a and 1/2-εj when it is at b. Other seams and
non-endpoint states retain the fair coin. The complete coin record is kept;
this is an explicitly changed scheduler/instrument, not the original fair law.
The prior actual contact current j may control its sign. For sufficiently small
|ε| the finite chain remains irreducible and has analytic stationary π_ε.

Writing d=e_b-e_a and π_0=1/M, exact stationarity gives

    L π_ε = 2εj(π_ε(a)+π_ε(b)) d,
    (d/dε)π_ε |_(ε=0) = (4j/M)L^+d.

Consequently, for ε=κ/sqrt(n), the occupation fluctuation normalized as in the
native theorem has limiting mean

    2κj/sqrt(MW) L^+d.

At fixed graph the triangular perturbation retains the unperturbed limiting
covariance; a uniform analytic spectral-gap argument supplies this statement.
A uniform initial law changes the finite transient but not this limiting mean.
Four explicitly coupled channels and a local curl could turn suitable biases
into a field-strength mean. A divergence-free physical source would require a
specified conserved multi-channel current, for example an explicitly declared
closed loop; a lone arbitrary vector-potential bias does not supply that.

The coefficient κ, graph, localization, finite n and apparatus controls must
be frozen. The source intervention requires a continued weak bias, not a single
free contact event. Relative entropy of its coin law can be computed, but
relative entropy is not automatically physical work or emitted energy. No
common energy operator or conservation equation currently relates this bias
to the removed pair. This is a concrete route for a later production/readout
experiment, with that missing bridge exposed, not a result used to award row8.
