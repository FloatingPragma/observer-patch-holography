archives, then failed while serializing NumPy int64 position labels in its
receipt. AMENDED_RUN.log and evidence/ remain unchanged. This successor adds
only NumPy-scalar conversion to JSON, writes fresh archives under
evidence_serialized/, and binds its receipt to this preregistration. No
source operation, observable, tolerance, time menu or control changes.

The new headline is headline_serialized.py; original headline variants remain
historical reproductions of their respective failures.
