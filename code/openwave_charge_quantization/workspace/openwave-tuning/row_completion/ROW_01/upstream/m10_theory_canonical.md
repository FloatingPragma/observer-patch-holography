## 2. Arena

A holographic screen of twelve-port icosahedral carriers. Each carrier holds integer
loads on its twelve ports. Neighbouring carriers share seam links, and a six-class
flux register records the protected classes. The port symmetry is the icosahedral
group A5, with its binary cover for spinors.

The native screen has spectral dimension two. The 3+1 field constructions use a
declared radial extension. The substrate is not a field and not a dynamical
medium, and the design is declared: no derivation and no uniqueness is claimed for
the carrier, its wiring or its program.

## 3. Dynamics

Tier 2 in the terms of [`ONBOARDING_MODELS.md`](../../../../ONBOARDING_MODELS.md):
evolution rules written directly, with no action behind them. The native law is a
local repair and memory law, run by the native observers. There is no Lagrangian
and no field equation.

The row constructions are a different object. They are finite quantum Hamiltonians
on declared cubes, chains and two-cell blocks, among them a Wilson plaquette
action, a lattice Dirac Hamiltonian and a Maxwell complex, evolved exactly or with a
contractive fourth-order step. Being tier 2, the column demonstrates conservation
per source instead of asserting it.

## 5. The hypothesis, and the five obligations the author sets

The overall hypothesis is closure: one complete history is both a timeless
self-referential structure and its own internal computation, written
`R(D(U)) ≅ U`. The executable-model hypothesis is narrower: a simple, fully
specified local repair and memory law on the carrier admits continuing observers
whose compatible records have the observed 3+1-dimensional quantum-field
description and a common classical-gravity limit.

The author's hypothesis page requires one source process to meet every part of
that claim, and lists five obligations with their status. All five read "not
established" at the pin.

| ID | Required on the same construction (condensed) | Status at the pin (the author's) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EMH-1 | Observer-accessible algebras, modular clocks, and the physical energy normalization | Not established |
| EMH-2 | An interacting 3+1-dimensional effective quantum field theory with the observed matter and gauge content | Not established |
| EMH-3 | The same native defects identified through charge, spin, propagation, interactions and energy, with the measured spectrum | Not established |
| EMH-4 | The Einstein reconstruction premises realized on the same matter, state, clocks and geometry | Not established |
| EMH-5 | Complete observation laws validated against independent measurements, with input ancestry declared | Not established |

The author also states the scope of a failure: a failed specified law rejects
that law and its tested interpretation, and rejects the wider proposal only if
the rejected choice is proved necessary and exclusive.

## 8. Consumption rules: how this column is scored

These are the rules of the admission reply
([discussioncomment-19595538](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/discussions/712#discussioncomment-19595538)),
which answer the application's second question.

| Rule | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| A declared or installed source is inherited | A row whose construction runs on a source that is declared, not derived from the carrier, stays at 🚧. The precedent cells are M9's installed Standard Model rows and M8's imported Einstein equations, in [`MODELS.md`](../../../../MODELS.md) |
| What moves a row | Its observable computed on the carrier's own process in-platform. With something still supplied, that is a case for ⚠️ with the caveat in the cell. With the source derived from the carrier, it meets the bar for ✅ set in the [#482 answer](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/issues/482#issuecomment-5428725010) |
| A calibrated source is a calibration | It is labelled as one and earns no credit for the values it was fitted to ([`ONBOARDING_MODELS.md`](../../../../ONBOARDING_MODELS.md) § 1.1). Its held-out failures go in the cell as results |
| Strength clauses | The Coulomb, Newton and cosmology rows credit a strength only when it follows from the model's own mechanism, never fitted or supplied (the rows' own simplest tests) |
| Who decides a cell | The maintainers, at review, one cell at a time, from the artifact ([`dev_docs/PR_REVIEW_STANDARDS.md`](../../../../dev_docs/PR_REVIEW_STANDARDS.md) § 7) |
| The author's map is not the platform's score | Statuses on the author's own site or papers are the author's estimates. The platform's claims are the cells in `MODELS.md` and the scripts behind them |
