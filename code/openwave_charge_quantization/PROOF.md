# Current, spatial degree and native readout

The source is the integer endpoint-repair law on the twelve-port carrier.
For an oriented seam e, its emitted current j gives n'=n-jB_e and F'=F+je.
Starting at the known reference G with F=0 yields BF=G-n for every finite
native history. The local dual-edge map exp(2 pi i F_e t) has shared endpoint
values and regional winding sum(BF). Exact integral minors establish
ker_Z(B)=im_Z(A), so the spatial homotopy class depends on the current
divergence and has no fractional sectors. The local circle map is a declared
interpretation, not uniquely selected by the abstract lattice.

For disjoint daughter contours, degrees are integers and add. Equal halves
of a primitive unit would require 2d=1 or 2d=-1 with integer d. Native double
loads can split into two whole units. Fractional ensemble means are allowed;
they are distinct from sharp half-charge outcomes.

The closing instrument visits every native seam with a contrast read.
Faithful closing currents preserve the discrepancy between actual and
reconstructed loads. Matching contrasts make that discrepancy constant on
the connected graph; equal total load makes it zero. The reported charge
refers to the post-sweep state. With total load one or two, every regional
degree is in [-2,2], where modulo-six counters are injective. The native
pointer resets an unknown one-load carrier and records signed movement
around a six-edge cycle. Timeouts retain their unconditional probabilities.
Its public interface validates the whole tape before decoding and ignores
supplied terminal-state metadata.

The static criterion is satisfied on the specified construction and bounded
observation domain. Known true G, the integer source alphabet, the local
circle interpretation and faithful closing records are explicit premises.

unrestricted modulo-six decoding. Physical charge units, mass, spin and a
transfer to another dynamics are outside this result. Internal scientific
