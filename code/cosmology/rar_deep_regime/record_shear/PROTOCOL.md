# A shape test for shear sourced by transported records

Take the disclosed finite cubic mark walk and the leading positive quadratic record response obtained by weakening the enrolled correlations. Generalize from the central source to an arbitrary set of injections and any positive semidefinite quadratic form of the stationary visit fields with fixed coefficients. Verify the full three-dimensional discrete sum-of-squares identity, including outside projections of the injectors. Compare the sign on a cell with an active injection, an absorbing walk and a change of constitutive sign. Derive the corresponding continuum inequality for the azimuthally averaged excess surface density in an annulus free of projected injection. Assume an ordinary, weak-field, thin lens whose excess density is proportional to this response and whose baryons are compact inside the annulus. A common positive constant is allowed; no scale, source map, edge, inclination or lens amplitude may be fitted to the data.

Use the previously compared six bins wholly above the isolation threshold in each of Brouwer et al.'s primary KiDS-bright and GAMA isolated-lens profiles. Their overall amplitudes and prior profile residuals were known. No signs of the three-bin contrasts were inspected for this design. Use the released tangential excess surface density and multiplicative correction, the full released covariance, and the cross channel as a signed instrumental control. Test every increasing triple of the six bins, keeping all 20. At the reported acceleration-bin centres, each middle value must lie above the straight chord joining the other two under the hypothesis. Use the one-sided Gaussian upper tail of the chord excess and a Bonferroni upper bound for the within-survey family. Do not multiply the two partly overlapping datasets. Check robustness first to a common distribution of fractional positions in all logarithmic bins and separately to arbitrary positive within-bin weights of a common curve, using the minimum and maximum chord weights. Describe the common-population, projection, bin weighting and source-free conditions and give both active and changed-law outcomes. Use the published covariance as a sampling model for this retrospective comparison. No physical radius scale or galaxy count is inferred from counting screen cells.
