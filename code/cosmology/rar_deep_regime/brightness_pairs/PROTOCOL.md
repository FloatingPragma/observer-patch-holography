# Local stellar light at matched baryonic force

This is a retrospective use of the deposited SPARC data. Freeze the selection below before extracting the observed velocities of any selected pairs. Use quality flags 1 or 2 and catalogue inclination of at least 30 degrees. Use the tabulated disk and bulge surface brightnesses added in solar luminosities per square parsec at 3.6 µm. Require both brightnesses positive and one at least twice the other. Use the existing stellar mass-to-light ratios 0.5 (disk) and 0.7 (bulge) and the released signed gas force. Require positive baryonic acceleration, with the larger no more than 1.10 times the smaller. The primary sample requires both accelerations below 1.2×10⁻¹¹ m/s². A separately labeled wider sample requires both below 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s²; the samples need not be independent.

For each galaxy and cut, take the pair with the greatest brightness ratio. Break any ties by the smaller baryonic-acceleration ratio, followed by the earlier pair of table line numbers. Use no velocity, velocity uncertainty, gas fraction cut, fitted acceleration scale or distribution of residuals in selection. Retain all eligible galaxies and report the number omitted at each cut. Evaluate faint/bright observed acceleration from the squared speed and inverse radius ratios. Compare it with the whole common-amplitude square-root shape range. Report signs nominally and allowing each observed speed its published error in either direction, separately at one and two quoted errors. Do not drop unresolved cases. No aggregate significance level is assigned. One pair contributes one sign per galaxy at each cut. Publish all selected radii and inputs so the reader can examine gas dominance, outer-ring selection, brightness quantization, and relative uncertainties.

## Sensitivity of the eight lower ratios

Keep the eight primary pairs whose upper two-error orbital ratio lies below the lower shape bound at the nominal masses. They were identified before this sensitivity protocol. Keep the annular identities, brightnesses, signed gas forces, radii and speed boxes fixed. Vary the stellar disk and bulge mass-to-light ratios independently over the closed rectangle [0.4,0.6] and [0.56,0.84], allowing each galaxy its own calibration but applying it to both its annuli. The widths are sensitivity choices, without assigned probabilities. At every corner check positivity of both baryonic forces, the original deep and matching cuts, and the smallest attainable baryonic ratio. Report also the minimum arbitrary fractional force change required to reach the orbital ratio bound, and how many conflicts persist for *any* positive forces satisfying the original ten-percent match. This audit conditions on the original pair selection; it does not reselect the most dissimilar annuli at each mass.
