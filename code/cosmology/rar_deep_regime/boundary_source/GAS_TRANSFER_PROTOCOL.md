# Gas boundaries outside the motivating pair

The orbital results of the ten-galaxy all-baryon comparison and detailed maps of NGC 3521 / NGC 3198 are already known. The close numerically matching two-gas-only image ratio on that pair motivated this new test. Before calculating any radial gas-only predictor, fix a single calibration galaxy, NGC 3198, the Cepheid galaxy with the longer orbital baseline in the pair. Its apparent distance agrees with both the SPARC and the direct-distance analyses. The other pair member contributes no calibration and no held score.

Use the original 0.8 kpc Leroy aperture, measured H I and any detected H₂; preserve every non-detection and original signed outer excess. Both calibration and eight outside galaxies use the same radial source and SPARC geometry with the original molecular correction. Calibrate Y=kS once on NGC 3198 separately for gas-only and for the all-baryon comparison. Compare every other originally enrolled galaxy except NGC 3521. Report every signed residual, median and RMS total-speed and excess errors, multiplicative discrepancy in excess and largest misses by km/s. Repeat with atomic-only and with the finite source factor, keeping each calibration confined to NGC 3198. The earlier orbital data are reused and these eight are external only to this motivating pair. No revision to sample or gain based on any of the eight.
