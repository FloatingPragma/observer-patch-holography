# Resolved baryon boundaries and exterior excess speed

These choices were recorded after inspecting Leroy et al.'s available surface-density columns and SPARC catalogue inclinations, distances, quality flags and radius coverage, before computing excess speeds or examining rotation values for this analysis. SPARC has already been used extensively in this project. The holdout is local to this source estimator.

- Match the published Leroy et al. 2008 Table 7 radial stellar, atomic and molecular surface-density profiles to SPARC by galaxy ID, including the Holmberg II alias. Enrol every matched galaxy of SPARC quality 1 or 2, inclination at least 30 degrees, whose outermost SPARC ring reaches the final tabulated baryonic source radius and at least three Leroy stellar scale lengths. Use the outermost ring once per galaxy.
- Use tabulated stellar and measured gas densities in their published face-on geometry. Linearly interpolate missing atomic densities within the tabulation with nearest-endpoint extrapolation, as long as at least one exists. Count detected molecular densities and assign zero to unpublished or undetected molecular bins. Interpolate internal stellar gaps in the same way as atomic gas. Convert source radii to the catalogue distance. Take midpoint ring edges, the inner edge at zero and the final radius half a spacing beyond the last centre. Average the tabulated radial fields by area onto 0.8 kpc intervals, retaining the final partial interval. Use the actual resulting outer edge in the aperture check. Include an explicit fall to zero at the source aperture. Report catalogue atomic mass coverage, molecular availability, sensitivity to 1.6 kpc intervals and omission of molecular gas, and the unknown exterior gas problem.
- Use one shared threshold with uniform measure over surface density from 0 to 10,000 solar masses per square parsec and one shared cell scale. Radial jump strength is circumference times absolute change in total baryonic surface density. Integrate observed annular masses separately. The primary conditional exterior force law gives a positive speed-squared contribution proportional to the total jump strength S. Compare a finite-source continuation F in which normalised jump strengths weight spherical emitting shells, u(s) is the sum of their weights divided by max(s,shell radius), and F is the radial average of (s u(s)) squared out to the observed outer ring. Also evaluate square-root enclosed baryonic mass and the exponential-disk proxy M/Rd with and without F where appropriate. This choice of force map and the deprojection of a disk into shells are additional hypotheses.
- The target is the squared observed rotation speed minus the squared SPARC baryonic contribution at the chosen ring, using stellar mass-to-light ratios 0.5 (disk) and 0.7 (bulge) and the signed SPARC atomic-gas force. In the primary calculation also subtract G times measured enclosed molecular mass divided by radius as an exterior spherical correction. Repeat without this correction. Retain signs of all excesses. Investigate inclination and distance errors and catalogue rotation uncertainties for the matched pair.
- Rank galaxies by measured source-aperture mass (ID breaks ties). Even zero-based ranks calibrate one nonnegative gain per law by the median target/predictor; odd ranks constitute the holdout. Report signed predictions and velocity residuals on held galaxies. Compare excess-speed-squared dependence on surface density at fixed mass using log(S/sqrt(M)) and log(Y/sqrt(M)), reporting the number eligible for this positive-target diagnostic. Form a gain-free matched pair from all enrolled galaxies with measured masses within a factor of two, choosing the largest contrast in S/sqrt(M) before target access. Include comparison with sqrt(M), both sides' actual radii and input sensitivities. These checks reuse the same galaxies.
- Distinguish a finite optical survey aperture from the whole galaxy, radial averages from 2D clumps, and common-threshold loads from independent occupancies. There is no prescription here for unresolved transverse structure, missing baryons or peculiar velocities. Do not adjust threshold, gain, distance, sample selection or gas conversion using a held orbital target.
