# External transfer to six gas-rich ultra-diffuse galaxies

Use all six objects of Table 1 of Mancera Piña et al. (2019, ApJL 883 L33). This is a retrospective challenge sample selected from a publication reporting slow rotation. It is external to the SPARC calibration. Check the publications for follow-up source geometry and for the assumptions under which the published circular speeds can represent asymptotic speeds.

Freeze the normalization and its reported conditional 95% training-galaxy bootstrap range from the existing calibration on 74 SPARC galaxies. For every tabulated baryonic mass calculate the limiting speed (G Mbar a0)^(1/4), apply no fit to the targets, and retain the observed circular speed, uncertainties and inclination. Derive the effective acceleration scale Vcirc^4/(G Mbar) and a speed ratio for each object. Treat the finite-radius use of a limiting-speed relation as an explicit physical extrapolation. Positive baryonic attraction would raise the circular-speed prediction if the compact anomalous contribution were present in full.

Make a conservative box comparison from the lower training-scale endpoint, mass minus its stated one-error value, and observed circular speed plus its stated one-error value. Repeat with two stated errors in mass and speed. Report the number still discrepant. These boxes do not have joint confidence coverage. Report the inclination that would be needed at the measured mass and central normalization, using Vcirc sin(i) as a proxy for the projected velocity; the source corrects Vcirc for gas pressure support. If an independent higher-resolution observation reports a better constrained inclination for one object, test its outer circular speed and calibrated geometry separately rather than counting that galaxy twice. Preserve provenance for each observation. No sample-prevalence or full-likelihood claim.
