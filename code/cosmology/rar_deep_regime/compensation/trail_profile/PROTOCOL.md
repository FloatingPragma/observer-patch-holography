# A radius-ratio test for a finite trail

Compare the three actually prepared trails and their frozen shuffled-direction controls against the four All sample mass bins in Mistele et al. Table 1. The reported profiles and their outer mass requirements are known. Use the first published radius of at least 50 kpc and the last within 300 kpc as the primary pair, and all other inner rows up to 150 kpc as sensitivity checks. Apply the spherical weak-gravity mass-to-rotation correspondence with a common native unit of mass and ordinary stock 50.5 inside both radii. Also test a reduced fraction of baryons inside the inner radius. Allow any constant positive physical hop length for each datum. Take an upper envelope over every possible length, using the actual stock at release and the all-time prefix bounds; include a control retaining the same number of records with a different placement. Treat a ceiling derived with the lens radii as a necessary condition rather than an independently inferred length. Preserve marginal statistical and systematic errors, do not assume radial covariance, and identify the differing lens weights.
