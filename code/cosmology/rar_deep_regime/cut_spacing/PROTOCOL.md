# A finite cut count in rotation and shear data

Test the arithmetic progression in the RER dark-sector paper. At one galaxy draw a single phase uniformly on [0,1) and set N(r)=floor(r/s+phase) for every ring. The anomalous squared speed from the local-baryonic-force extension of the deep law is multiplied by s N(r)/r. The smooth arm is s=0. A common spacing in kpc and independent phases across galaxies are comparison assumptions. The theorem for a compact exterior does not determine the force of an extended disk. No phase is inferred from a held galaxy.

Use the source-pinned SPARC tables. Enrol quality 1 or 2, inclination at least 30 degrees, positive radius, positive tabulated speed uncertainty, and positive catalogue baryonic acceleration below 1.2e-11 m/s². Use the existing disk and bulge mass-to-light ratios and signed-component convention. Retain speeds irrespective of their fractional error or signed excess. Use the existing five galaxy-name folds. At each spacing in 0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 12 and 16 kpc, fit one acceleration scale and two dispersion parameters on the training galaxies. Choose the spacing by training joint marginal log likelihood. Compare the predictive log density for whole held galaxies against the smooth model. Report each fold, the pooled difference, and a paired fixed-prediction galaxy-bootstrap interval. These are retrospective splits on a known dataset.

Condition on catalogue geometry and baryonic forces. In linear speed units the covariance at conditional mean m has diagonal independent contribution e_i² + w² m_i² plus a common contribution b² m_i m_j, where e_i is the published speed uncertainty. The two scatter parameters describe residual variation and are shared across galaxies. Integrate one common phase per galaxy exactly by the radial thresholds. Include covariance determinants. For the full SPARC fit, transfer both arms to all five fiducial published rings of AGC 114905 and to the six wholly isolated bins in each KiDS-bright and GAMA stack with their published covariance. Report the external predictive speed distribution at the outer ring and the complete curve log density. Compute a necessary spacing bound from the outer speed plus two plotted errors, conditional on the fitted scale and baryonic force. Treat the two lens samples separately. No shear, ultra-diffuse-galaxy velocity or per-target phase is used to fit the common scale or spacing. The tensions in these external data were known when choosing the test.

After seeing the primary spacing selection, compare a smooth mean with a fractional independent speed variance w²+c²/r² in addition to the original common scatter. Refit on each original training fold. This control tests whether allowing small-radius variation alone accounts for the predictive gain. Also integrate the held profile over the reported distance error with a lognormal positive distance distribution of catalogue mean and catalogue standard deviation. For a distance multiplier d, the physical radius and squared baryonic speed both scale by d. Use the same multiplier at every ring. Report this last transfer as a sensitivity at frozen training parameters because some common variance can also absorb distance uncertainty.
