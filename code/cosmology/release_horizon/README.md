# A material release front and thermal correlations

On a 320-cell Interval Echo screen, an earlier release produces correlated local energy differences at two edges whose scheduled causal pasts are disjoint over the two sweeps following a clock reset. The exact covariance is 6.41026 × 10⁻⁷ in squared native energy gaps. A common bit broadcast before the reset produces a spatially uniform response with zero covariance of these differences. The [protocol](protocol.json) identifies the root, clocks, preparations and pair selections. The [joint counts and exact distributions](results.json) include all originally prepared receivers, including those that miss a release.

## What a receiver does

Each 12-port cell carries five one-load dimers and two vacant helpers. The first two rails encode a shared bit X. The next two encode a private pending flag D. The other three dimers hold a thermal probe, its partner and a selector that also serves as a local clock. Isolated native π exposures exchange the two endpoint loads on every blank-memory column with the same phase. Walking forward along a path and back along all but its final edge transposes the endpoints and restores every intermediate port. These routes move the desired roles to the detector path (0,1,7,6) and back. A native echo distinguishes a sharp load from a vacancy against a vacant reference and restores the input on its measured branch. The [gate and seam checks](relay.py) use the registered Interval Echo generator, the sharp pointer and the energy decoder.

A receiver listens at an external seam with a vacant helper. Its neighbor offers the active rail of X if its flag is clear, or a spare vacancy if its flag is raised. The receiver reads what crossed, returns it, restores the local routes, and posts its own X if it saw a load. The private flag stays local. For each oriented gateway and every pair of register words, the checks verify both full allocations after the consultation. Cells acknowledge published records by clearing their flags after the last matching of the sweep. Cutting only the external exchange keeps local clocks running and leaves the notice at its root. Clearing the flag immediately upon receipt lets some notices travel several links per sweep.

The scheduler permits idle, a flip of X, or a clear of D at each logical commit. The complete four-input support is exactly the set of moves that keep the defect count from increasing and change at most one logical bit. This gives a pathwise unit write bound and hence a unit bound on the expected sum of private and shared write costs for every row. A local change selector with time 2 asin(sqrt(p)) realizes probability p. Sequential conditional selections therefore implement any probability row on the allowed moves, including deterministic rows. The code checks a three-output row with probabilities 1/5, 4/15 and 8/15. Charging both a post and an acknowledgement to a single commit would cost two. Raising a clear flag in an admitted step would increase mismatch. The [Lean proof](../../../Lean/ObserverPatchHolography/Provenance/ReleaseRelay.lean) maps the menu to the existing admissible-scheduler type in both directions. These four clauses concern the declared logical write budget. They assign no one-pulse or energy budget to the routing, measurements or controller.

## What sets the range

Clocks begin in aligned sharp states. A fresh π/3 exposure gives a measured marker change with probability 1/4 on either previous rail. The controller restores the old position after that measurement and makes one deterministic π stroke, which the owner reads. At each sweep every thermal probe with an active contact gets its own offer before the notice front advances. The first change of a separate marker at the root triggers publication at the end of sweep T, followed by a separate acknowledgement. The declared exposures, contact order and preparations are supplied interventions.

For a ready root the set reached in one sweep is exactly the previous set together with its neighbors. The formal sweep theorem iterates this relation. The producer also propagates public and private bits separately over all directed seams and compares every arrival with an independent graph-distance calculation. The root eccentricities on the 20-, 80- and 320-cell boards are 5, 11 and 23 sweeps. For a triggered root the notice arrives at time T+d, where d is distance to the root. The full circuit can permit faster dependencies than this notice. Propagating the union of the two endpoint input sets through each scheduled matching gives a conservative past for every reading. Disjoint sets exclude shared post-reset causes under the independent-product preparation and local independent offers. Overlap alone gives no numerical covariance.

The thermal dimers have energy levels 32 and 33. Independent native rotations and sharp reads prepare upper populations c=1/5 and w=1/3. Inverting the energy decoder on the measured rail prepares the corresponding eigenstate. Every start enters the record. A successful local offer swaps both rails of the probe and its partner and conserves their joint energy at contact endpoints. At the fixed deadline H the probe reports its energy, including if the notice misses. The conditional upper probability after n offers is

    m(n) = (c+w)/2 + (c−w)2^(−n)/2 = 4/15 − 2^(−n)/15.

On the release bank n=min(T+d,H) and T is geometric with parameter 1/4 and support starting at one. Conditional on T all probes are independent. Thus pair covariance is the covariance of their two conditional means over the trigger history, including the tail T>H. If d≥H−1 the site's energy distribution is independent of T. At d=H−1 the first possible notice can be read at H even though it has no effect on the energy recorded then.

A single earlier trigger also gives an upper bound at every deadline. For independent trigger copies T and T′, covariance is half the expectation of the product of the two sites’ mean differences between those copies. Both differences have the same sign. Capping either response at m(H) can only shrink the product. For an uncapped trigger, E[2⁻ᵀ]=1/5 and E[4⁻ᵀ]=1/13. Hence a pair at distances i and j has covariance at most (4/24375) 2⁻⁽ⁱ⁺ʲ⁾, with equality in the unlimited-time limit. Since i+j is at least the graph separation, this gives exponential attenuation with separation regardless of how early the root is armed. In that limit the shared-trigger contribution to the entire covariance matrix has rank one, with radial vector entries 2⁻ⁱ. Independent read noise adds a diagonal matrix. Finite deadlines clip each response separately, so the exact rank-one statement applies to the unlimited-time common-trigger component. The bound concerns native links at fixed instrument settings.

## What survives a local difference

The earlier-release bank arms the same trigger and starts thermal contacts one root eccentricity before the comparison origin. The comparison clock returns to zero without disturbing the source, stored energies or notice front. The fixed reading at elapsed age H uses total exposure D+H from arming, where D is that board's root eccentricity. A separate preparation broadcasts one fair bit from a ready root over D sweeps with all thermal dimers parked. At reset the receivers use their local latches to swap or keep their thermal partners, park the relay, and start H independent offers. The shared-bit conditional means are 4/15 ± 2⁻ᴴ/15. The contribution of the common bit to the covariance matrix is the all-ones matrix times 4⁻ᴴ/225. Removing the spatial mean annihilates this contribution. Local detector noise persists. Independent local bits reproduce both single-probe distributions, and cutting the preparation seams makes every nonroot site start cold.

For a measurement insensitive to a common offset, take the difference of the two captured energies on a neighboring pair of cells, ordered from smaller to larger root distance. Take a second edge whose post-reset input cells are disjoint from the entire first edge's input cells. Compare the two records after capture. The [contrast producer](contrast.py) picks the smallest sum of inner root distances among eligible edge pairs and then breaks ties by address. ## Data and reproduction

The [producer](horizon.py) computes rational probabilities and separately evolves integer cohorts through thermal contacts. For each trigger age, cohort counts start from the independent probe and reservoir energies. At each service a fresh binomial count splits every live joint word. Accepted counts go to the word with the two relevant bits exchanged. The four-probe banks retain all 16 energy outputs across 2⁴⁸ nominal starts per setting. The two-probe banks retain 4 × 4 joint tables across 2⁴⁰ nominal starts per setting. Cohort counts sample the independent-trajectory distribution without allocating an observer for every nominal start. The board sizes are 20, 80 and 320 material owners.

In the pair tables each axis is ordered as no notice/lower, no notice/upper, notice/lower, notice/upper. The earlier release counts hits from its arming origin. The bit-preparation controls measure energy without a post-reset notice service and use the first two categories. The four-probe output index contains one energy bit per listed cell, first cell least significant. For each bank, counts sum to its original trial count. Exact between-trigger spatial power removes the ensemble mean at each cell and the spatial monopole of the conditional-mean response, excluding the root. It excludes the independent thermal sampling noise.

The two distinct families of 99% bounds each use Hoeffding's inequality and a union bound over all single and product moments over the declared boards, times, selections and modes. Each pair moment takes values in [0,1]. Each contrast or product of contrasts is in [−1,1]. If each moment has error at most b, the covariance half-width is b(1+|mean A|+|mean B|)+b² using the two observed means. Exact probabilities and the four-pair expansion of contrast covariance are checked independently by the [tests](test_horizon.py). Seeded counts were produced with NumPy 2.3.5.
