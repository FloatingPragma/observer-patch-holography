# Angular transport of a screen pattern

A detector on the registered 320-owner Interval Echo carrier reads a positive and a negative angular preparation separately. One preparation illuminates the twelve vertex directions of a cell uniformly, the other its twenty face normals. Their difference has zero monopole and its first nonzero spherical multipole is degree six. Free transport preserves the measured degree-six contrast. One transverse polarization projection erases it. A fair bypass gives half the initial contrast per original launch and three fifths per accepted detection. The frame and encounter frequency are supplied.

## Six accepted port steps

The [route compiler](rays.py) uses the native port labeling and triangular incidence of the cell. Its vertex coordinates are signed permutations of (0,1,φ). The relation φ² = φ+1 permits the finite chart homomorphism φ ↦ 3 modulo 5. This maps a port to a displacement of a 125-owner periodic array. The smaller port in each antipodal pair selects the positive step. All sixty proper cell rotations act on the reduced displacements. A face-normal setting uses the sum of its three vertex displacements. The angular frame uses the real unit vectors before reduction. The quotient supplies endpoints; it gives neither a Euclidean flight distance nor a common travel time to the two orbits.

An independent controller accepts the requested port by a sharp read across a material seam. Directed cycles of full native π exchanges move a three-rail payload and restore every borrowed port. The compiler follows distinct labels on all 3,840 physical ports. Each accepted word sends every polarization component to the same interpreted owner with its phase preserved. The property extends by linearity to arbitrary internal states and to translation of the full position probability distribution. Terminal calibration uses local and neighboring readers on every owner. All fifteen ordered-pair comparisons agree on all 125 addresses. Conjugating one step by a transposition of two owners keeps it reversible but makes a selected pair disagree at eight addresses. The [Lean proof](../../../../Lean/ObserverPatchHolography/Provenance/ScreenAngularTransfer.lean) instantiates the surjective endpoint-descent theorem on this finite position group. Faithfulness of the infinite six-generator lattice and physical cosmic geometry require separate identifications.

## The angular instrument

Each incident ray starts at the inverse image of a common scattering station. An outgoing ray ends at one of 32 distinct detector addresses. The triangular settings comprise three signed port moves. A single load on three local rails carries the polarization amplitudes. Exchange and parked relative-phase gates implement real rotations from the local transverse basis to the cell frame. A normal-rail echo flags a missed projection and leaves both surviving transverse components coherent. An outgoing detector measures those components in the linear, diagonal or circular basis. Four incident preparations span the transverse density matrix. All matrix columns and the rejected readings are included in the [polarimeter](polarimeter.py) and its [data](detectors.npz).

With mutually incoherent, equally weighted incident transverse states the successful fraction for an outgoing proposal is (1+μ²)/2, where μ is the measured frame's direction cosine. Each orbit has second moment I/3 and the isotropic fourth moment. Five fair bits select the outgoing aperture. The survival per encounter is 2/3 for each incident direction. The source score H₆(n) is the mean of P₆(v·n) over the twelve vertex poles. Its values are 11/25 on the vertex orbit and −11/45 on the face orbit. The orbit difference has higher even multipoles as well. Both source preparations have the same mass and second moment, so every outgoing polarization probability agrees after one collision. The conditional quadratic-direction contrast retains one tenth of its initial value. For an incident P₂ modulation around v, the emitted linear Stokes amplitude per proposal is (1−μ²)/10 with zero circular signal. The normalized angular dependence matches the finite transverse projection in the [transport paper](../../../../cosmology/oph_boltzmann_transport_derivation.pdf).

At fair bypass, 5/6 of launches yield detections. The raw degree-six gain is 1/2 and the accepted gain is 3/5. The corresponding quadrupole gains are 8/15 and 16/25. For the one-collision bank, two polarization strata of 65,536 starts for each incoming/outgoing pair give a raw degree-six gain of −0.00003635 with half-width 0.00014951. Independent multinomial cohorts count both transverse results and the rejected result. Hoeffding bounds allocate 0.005 error probability to the common band for all 2,048 hit probabilities and 0.005 to the weighted gain. Their simultaneous coverage is at least 99 percent for simulation error.

## A second encounter

A secondary measurement propagates the surviving polarization to the named intermediate station and applies a second analyzer. Each intermediate station has 32 distinct final detectors in its own translated frame. The intermediate direction is retained when combining the data. The total surviving fraction is 4/9. The accepted intensity quadrupole after two encounters is 7/100 of its initial value. Applying one of four equally weighted native polarization rotations before the second encounter randomizes the surviving transverse state and gives 1/100 with the same survival. A reconstruction from the first-encounter polarization tomography independently recovers both two-encounter kernels. Thus iteration of the scalar intensity kernel alone loses a measurable part of the second response.

These are dimensionless angular probabilities of a specified material analyzer. An identification with physical Thomson scattering also needs the photon and electron sectors, calibrated optical depth and interaction rate. A prediction for the observed CMB additionally needs a source history, line-of-sight transfer and an independently fixed sky frame.

## Replay and reuse

The [frozen protocol](protocol.json), [driver](measure.py), [records](records.json) and [independent checks](test_angular_transfer.py) specify the complete experiment. In the NPZ archive, `tomography[s,t,p,a,r]` stores the probability times 10⁹, rounded to the nearest integer. Indices are incident direction, outgoing direction, incident polarization (H,V,D,R), analysis basis (linear, diagonal, circular) and outcome (first transverse, second transverse, rejected). `counts[s,t,p,r]` stores integer outcomes for the H and V input banks in the linear basis. The two `second_kernels` use retained and randomized polarization in that order, with the same probability scale and original-launch denominator. `second_stations[j,k]` identifies the final owner for intermediate direction j and final direction k. The JSON lists directions, endpoint witnesses, gains and confidence bounds. Reproduce the native data from the repository root.

```sh
export OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 VECLIB_MAXIMUM_THREADS=1
.venv/bin/python code/cosmology/release_horizon/angular_transfer/measure.py
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q code/cosmology/release_horizon/angular_transfer
(cd Lean && LEAN_NUM_THREADS=1 lake build +ObserverPatchHolography.Provenance.ScreenAngularTransfer:olean)
```
