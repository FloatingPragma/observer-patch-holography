# A reversible slab from sharp material records

On each cell a guarded pair of material ports holds a single double. The observer reads which rail is occupied after each addressed π/3 exposure, with the sign of the rail recovered by an echo against a vacant reference. Other contacts are parked. For either input the probability to retain its rail is 3/4. The same probabilities result from a fair enable on a half exchange. The law and aperture are fixed before any sampling. The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [transfer and ancestry results](results.json), [per-owner word counts](counts.npz) and [release distribution](release.json) provide the reusable inputs. Both producers run locally with a single numerical thread.

## The transfer and its refinement

In the two blank-memory columns for (2,0) and (0,2), the native generator is exactly 32 I + (I−X)/2, where X exchanges the rails. This follows by restricting the registered blank-seam identity. Its invariant sector has gap one. Squaring the entries of the unitary at π/3 gives the sharp transfer K=(3I+X)/4. Its sign eigenvalue is 1/2. Thus the unique positive Euclidean exposure with this transition and this gap is log 2. The receipt checks both columns in the full 99-state source, its leaked norm, the pointer and the independent matrix exponential. The unmonitored probability of changing rail over two such real exposures is 3/4. Two sharp appointments give 3/8. A full exchange has a negative sign eigenvalue, which precludes any positive Euclidean exponential for that kernel. Parking the contacts removes connectivity. Removing the exchange term from the generator while retaining the contrast, exposure and pointer also gives an identity transition and zero energy gap. Each initial rail word then persists indefinitely.

For N owners let Q be their 2^N sharp rail words and use the normalized counting weight m(x)=2^−N. The energy generator is the sum of the isolated dimer terms. Each hypercube edge x to x with one bit flipped therefore has conductance m(x)/2. In absolute native energy units the potential is the constant 32N and the lowest energy is 32N. Equivalently one can use zero potential throughout in measured gap units relative to the ground. The graph is connected. The normalized ground wavefunction in L²(m) is the constant one, its law is m, and its Euclidean transition is K tensorized N times. For a product of k distinct rail signs the eigenvalue is 2^−k. Reversibility follows by symmetry. For histories reflected about a site, conditional independence of past and future at the central state gives a squared conditional expectation. For reflection about a temporal bond the quadratic form is an inner product with this positive transfer. These proofs hold for arbitrary finite cylinder histories of this source.

Each parent enrolls one distinguished child rail at subdivision. Conditional on *any* complete fine input, summing out every hidden rail gives the coarse kernel. Restrictions compose on nested enrollments. They preserve m, the ground, all centered-energy modes on the retained bits, and all observed joint distributions across any number of appointments. ## An observer-triggered release

Start four independent rail instruments in the sharp zero state. The first switch of the first instrument releases a parked double into a supplied material route. At each subsequent appointment each active receiver advances its local sharp instrument once. The controller implements the within-cell swaps and crosses one external seam before the next receipt. A receiver checks the tag against a vacant reference, undoes its check to preserve the tag, and captures its local word on first arrival. The route lists the actual ports for each of the three boards. Only the data dimers evolve between interventions. Tag pickup, local reads, routing and externally supplied relative cadence are apparatus. The three ports receive successive reports of a common event, at different local read epochs.

If T is the root's first change then Pr(T=t)=(1/4)(3/4)^(t−1). The captured sign at delay d has mean 2^(−T−d) conditional on T. The three captures are conditionally independent. Hence the covariance of their conditional means is rank one and the covariance between distinct signs i,j is 2^(−d_i−d_j) Var(2^−T). The full covariance also includes the conditional sign variances on its diagonal. With delays 1, 2 and 3 the cross-covariances are 3/650, 3/1300 and 3/2600. Their joint third centered moment is 51/754000. With independent fair initial receiver rails the same common release gives zero cross-covariance. Independent release triggers also give independent outputs. Cutting the route leaves three explicit misses. The exact capped calculation keeps all eight sharp output words by trigger appointment and the no-trigger outcome after 16 root reads. The three-independent-trigger control keeps each separate timeout. None of the records identifies a radiation species or an early-universe freezeout.

## An energy read on the same release

At each arrival use the existing guarded-dimer energy decoder immediately after the sharp orientation read. It distinguishes the symmetric and antisymmetric eigenmodes, with native energies 32 and 33. Either sharp rail has squared overlap one half with each mode. At every trigger age and for every measured rail word, the eight joint energy words are equiprobable. On the original launch denominator each has probability (1−(3/4)^16)/8, plus the common timeout. Further filter appointments give the same law on either input rail, with first-leak probabilities 2^(−j−1) at j=1,…,b and terminal ground and excited probabilities 1/2 and 2^(−b−1). These comparisons use the registered native gate matrices. Conditioning on successful release gives zero pairwise energy covariance at every delay although the orientation covariances are positive. Equal mode populations characterize this two-level readout. Identifying a plasma temperature requires an energy-exchange and species measurement on the cosmological source.

The stationary rail histories have consistent finite-window laws in both temporal directions. The probability of no switch over the preceding n appointments is (3/4)^n, which tends to zero. At any specified appointment there is almost surely an earlier change, and the same holds at all integer appointments by countability. The stationary process thus has no first change anywhere. The deployed release first arms the root instrument and subsequently waits for its first change. A cosmological model needs a physical event playing the role of arming.
