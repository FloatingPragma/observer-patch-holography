# How far beyond the observed baryonic origins would the source have to reach?

The constant-rate Poisson transfers miss the mapped curves. Remove the constant-rate assumption. At the same catalogue parameters, allow every galaxy to place arbitrary positive spherical gravitational source anywhere inside the last edge of its Leroy baryon map, with unlimited amplitude. Using the ordinary Newtonian G, derive the allowed enclosed source mass at each observed SPARC ring. Test existence by monotonicity and the equality of all exterior masses. Where two rings outside the map require additional positive enclosed mass, report its minimum and the minimum displacement from any mapped origin. This exterior increase also constrains signed stocks because it bounds their net added mass. An upper speed already below the baryon-only value is a separate contradiction for positive sources. The final mapped edge is an observational boundary.

For the explicit transfer that the positive gravitating stock follows the bound ordinary modes of a permanent mark, use the finite native uniform-in-size localization bound. For each mass-increase pair outside the map, combine its minimum required positive mass with the conditional cosmic inventory ceiling. Assume each native hop covers at most one common physical length; take the largest resulting minimum hop length. Test both the measured ordinary occupation and its degree-weighted closed-neighborhood field. Report outward-rounded bounds. These are conditions on this identification, stocking cap, and projection, not a measurement of an OPH length.
