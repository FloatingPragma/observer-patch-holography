# Does one calibrated pair-abundance law predict other galaxies?

The NGC 2841 shell challenge is known. Enroll every ring of the existing ten-galaxy matched SPARC, Leroy and THINGS sample. Take the nominal 0.8 kpc face-on cell and its four face neighbors, with the exposure proportional to the product of their ordinary surface densities at fixed depth. Predict two distinct source maps: atomic gas with helium over the full radio image, and that same image plus the measured Leroy stellar and molecular annuli over their published coverage. The observed images set the radial distributions. Leave the previously adopted disk baryonic velocities and spherical continuation fixed.

Enroll the existing leave-one-galaxy-out least-squares normalization, giving each of the other nine galaxies total weight one when calibrating squared speeds. Apply exactly one gain to every ring of the withheld galaxy. Also report how many whole curves could fit even with an arbitrary nonnegative gain per galaxy, and the baryon-only hit count and median speed error. Report all ten galaxies, total points, two-error coverage and median absolute error. The fitted common dimensional conversion is a diagnostic grant. Any proposed OPH derivation of it remains independently necessary.
