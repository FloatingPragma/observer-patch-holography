# Does isolated ordinary material select the galactic radial field?

Use the site counts of the uniform in-phase ordinary preparation in the longest registered isolated-medium time window, T=65536. Freeze that native source before comparing with any orbital data. Subtract the blank closed-neighborhood field from the written one using the measured ordinary gain, and sum over each graph-distance shell about the seam. Include the central shell and every signed exterior shell. The exterior theorem relates gradients of this field to released probe probability, with a separately measurable current-derivative term. Its magnitude per cut per arm at uniform age is at most twice the quadrature norm divided by T. Assigning minus the resulting field as a specific gravitational potential on independent mapped baryonic origins is the transfer under test.
