# Can the detected outer disk predict the shape of the extra attraction?

The integrated optical inventory and the outer-ring mass requirement are known. Use the independent THINGS natural-weighted atomic-gas image of NGC 2841 and Zhang et al.'s approximate stellar-to-gas surface-density ratio of three between 20 and 50 kpc. Use this nominal ratio only where that paper can measure it. Grant a positive spherical extra mass proportional to these local annular baryons. Allow arbitrary positive pre-existing extra mass inside 20 kpc. The ordinary baryonic rotation contribution stays at the catalogue assignment. No global cosmic budget is required.

Reacquire the previously verified radio image on the same 14.1 Mpc chart. Use the published fixed deprojection, positive-flux pixels and 0.4 kpc grid of the existing image pipeline. Sum cell centers in each radial aperture and archive the enclosed atomic-plus-helium masses at the observed SPARC radii, including the 20 kpc origin. Use 20–35 kpc to determine all compatible nonnegative values of the interior mass and of a common extra mass per unit of combined outer stars and gas. Predict the mass band at every remaining measured ring through 50 kpc with those same parameters. Test individual and simultaneous coverage at twice each quoted orbital speed error, and report incompatible pairs or combinations. This is a test of the nominal baryon-tracing shape. Neither the shape nor an empirical gain inferred from inner orbits constitutes an independent OPH charge calibration. The warp, photometric conversion, beam, and fixed disk orientation remain systematics.
