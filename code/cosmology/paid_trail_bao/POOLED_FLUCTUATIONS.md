# A second cosmological measurement after fixing the mean

Use the homogeneous 0.8 kpc opportunity cube and ordinary-equivalent source law from the mass-ratio comparison. Grant the size of each communicating pool inferred from the Planck mean cold-to-baryonic ratio. Test an additional observable at that same size. Place equal cubic pools on a stationary grid with a uniformly translated origin. Draw the source opportunities independently within and between pools and allocate their extra ordinary mass uniformly inside each active pool. Treat this as a present-day linear gravitating matter contrast with a uniform remaining matter background. Use the Planck total physical matter density for the fractional normalization. These physical assumptions make a prediction for the root-mean-square total contrast in a spherical top-hat of radius eight inverse-h Mpc. Compare with Planck's base-model sigma-eight and retain the results for every image capacity and tracer.

Compute separately the probability of an active pool, its conditional population variance and the top-hat overlap. Bound the variance using the mean and the probability of an empty pool alone. Evaluate sensitivity throughout rectangular one- and two-quoted-error ranges in h, the Planck baryonic, cold and total matter densities, and the empirical-rate BBN baryon density (combine its two reported errors in quadrature). The Planck parameters are correlated. These rectangles give sensitivity bounds and supply no joint likelihood.
