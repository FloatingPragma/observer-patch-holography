# What does the measured stellar disk outside 30 kpc supply in NGC 2841?

Zhang et al. trace the optical disk to about 70 kpc and give the integrated stellar mass beyond about 30 kpc as (1.4 ± 0.2) × 10⁸ solar masses at 14.1 Mpc. Use the same adopted SPARC distance as the prior rotation-curve test. Convert angular support linearly and photometric mass quadratically with distance. Within the optical aperture, use every SPARC ring beyond the nominal start of the warped disk. Compare every ordered pair to the largest allowed inner and smallest allowed outer spherical extra mass from the frozen baryonic speed and the published speed errors at twice their quoted values. Report the pair with the greatest required increase and all individual intervals. Also report the first and last eligible pair to make the selection explicit.

Give the proposed local source every star in that outer inventory, at the central estimate plus twice the quoted error. Calculate the minimum extra gravitating mass per solar mass of that stellar population. As a broader inventory control, add the entire positive-flux THINGS atomic-plus-helium mass and the entire measured HERACLES molecular-plus-helium mass, including material inside the inner radius. These are generous assignments of the detected inventories, conditional on the adopted reductions. No per-galaxy cosmic mass cap enters. The comparison bounds a local conversion model. It does not identify modular charge from photometry or require a fixed stock per baryon in OPH. The radii, stellar mass, warp, and systematic limits are described in [Zhang et al., §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.02583).
