# Exterior mass inferred from a compact passage-time source

Use the original four work intervals for NGC 2841 at the previously chosen 10.25 kpc per hop. Grant an arbitrary potential conversion and test the profile measured by the double-load clock against all four intervals. Treat a different matter coupling or length identification as a different transfer.

Also test the sign of exterior mass growth without choosing a hop length or clock amplitude. Outside every changed contact the unit-current voltage is harmonic. For a positive sum of squared such voltages, the graph Laplacian (degree minus adjacency) applied to the sum is nonpositive off the sources. It sums to zero on the whole closed chart. At the potential-well sign the implied enclosed mass must therefore be nonnegative and nonincreasing on nested regions containing every changed endpoint. The opposite gain gives nonpositive enclosed mass. This test assumes the graph flux measures the same enclosed spherical mass as extra circular speed squared times radius. Ordinary gas and stars enter through the SPARC baryonic reduction used in the preceding comparisons.

Enroll every galaxy in the existing fixed reduction with at least three measured rings at or beyond half its last tabulated radius. Assume every changed contact lies inside the first tested ring. Use each quoted speed interval with the same two-error and baryonic conventions as the prior comparison. Test existence of any nonnegative, nonincreasing enclosed mass within *all* the intervals. Report negative upper endpoints separately from growth conflicts. For a growth conflict give the strongest pair of inner and outer measurements and the minimum additional mass required between them at this reduction. Include all qualifying and excluded galaxies. Use both the pre-existing ten imaged systems and the 118 selected bulgeless systems, counting overlaps separately. Use half the last eligible radius in the corresponding reduction. For the bulgeless sample also bound the same pairwise mass-growth witness over a shared distance and inclination within two catalog errors, stellar normalization 0.3 to 0.8 and gas normalization 0.7 to 1.5. Preserve every positive physical distance and inclination in those ranges. This second count is a lower bound on exclusions, because a single pair must survive the whole box. The sample and outer-half cut specify a conditional spatial hypothesis; extended active sources in those annuli would change its premise. Catalog values were examined in earlier research. ## Distributed sources on the observed images

The compact-source results above are known before testing this extension. Place independent copies of the measured clock profile at the retained 0.8 kpc image-cell centers in each of the ten mapped galaxies. Use the same 2200 eligibility, local filling, atomic and mapped baryonic tracers, seven capacities, image crop and disk geometry as the existing closed-inventory experiment. At every seed put the normalized native flux at radius zero and its negative shell increments at their native hop radii with uniform solid angle. This isotropic projection and linear addition of independent contributions are stipulated. All seed weights come from the images before any velocity comparison.

Compare hop lengths of 0.2, 0.4, 0.8, 1.6, 3.2, 6.4, 10.25, 12.8, 25.6 and 51.2 kpc. At each measured radius compute the enclosed signed mass by exact spherical cap fractions. Allow any real common conversion to the extra squared speed. Test all 369 speed intervals and also compute the largest count any single conversion can meet simultaneously. Give the number of complete curves that admit even their own conversion. A gain inferred from the speeds is a diagnostic fit, and this finite menu cannot exclude other transporting dynamics or every length. Validate the integration by checking unity of the central weight, exact compensation, and recovery of the original enclosed imaged source with the compensating shells at infinity.
