# Does pressure solve the cosmological abundance problem for this measured source?

Test the specific continuation in which the closed stock is a separately conserved, homogeneous effective stress in the ordinary Einstein equations. Allow arbitrary isotropic pressure, including sign changes. Price the conserved energy on the ordinary spectroscopic calibration and grant either sign up to the existing closed-inventory bound. Repeat granting the bound for populations made entirely of accepted stock. Baryons dilute as dust. Integrate the active gravitational density over the entire scale-factor interval between the lowest and highest radial BAO bins. Compare with the Planck cold-to-baryon benchmark. Keep the density evolution explicit.

For an independent check against observed expansion records, use the two endpoint radial distances from the DESI DR2 covariance table, fitting no expansion rate or constant vacuum energy. In a flat Friedmann background subtract the largest allowed baryon, small massive-neutrino and radiation contributions. Allow arbitrary pressure histories at all intermediate times and opposite extra-energy signs at the two endpoints. Give each radial measurement a simultaneous 99% Gaussian Bonferroni interval and vary the absolute drag ruler over 125–165 Mpc, the baryon density over 0.02163–0.02273, and the ordinary neutrino dust contribution over 0–0.0007 in physical density units. Display the curvature coefficient required if flatness is relaxed. The ruler and baryon ranges are sensitivity choices. Treat the calibrated BAO comparison as conditional on these ranges and ordinary background assumptions.
