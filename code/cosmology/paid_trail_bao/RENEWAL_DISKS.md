# Predict a clock potential from images at two annuli

Use the ten enrolled H I and stellar images, both existing tracer choices and all seven capacities. In each 0.8 kpc cell, take the four ordinary owner occupancies as independent Bernoulli draws at the imaged filling. Read the middle-load frequencies of each full native stationary inventory. The specified repair changes only the accepted part of the two-double inventory, by its independently measured displacement at fixed mean. Average predicted attempts per completed tick over equal-area addresses, then infer the potential from the logarithm of the resulting clock rate. For the bare ordinary calibration use the same filling before the writes, with ordinary loads zero and two. Compare the shell of cell centers ending at the measured radius nearest half the last radius to the last shell. Use only centers between the first and last measured radii. If a designated shell contains no centers, use the nearest populated shell in radius. Compute both ordinary and excess potential differences from the measured radial curves on precisely those same centers, taking the speed-squared reductions as piecewise linear in log radius.

Fix a positive gain first from each galaxy’s bare ordinary clock and its independently reduced baryonic potential difference. Also calibrate on the baryonic reductions of the other nine galaxies and predict the held ordinary and excess differences. Neither uses the observed excess for calibration. Record wrong-sign or degenerate ordinary calibrations. Separately allow the most favorable common positive gain for the dark component, to diagnose its radial shape. These image populations, finite read settings and source law constitute the forecast. The interpretation of the bare or repaired clock as a universal gravitational lapse is under test.
