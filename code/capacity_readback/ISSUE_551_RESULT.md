# Issue 551: Bounded Generation-Register Countermodel

## Result

The target-clean generation-register constructor gives exact capacity
formulas at every positive rung. The reversible identity, copy-collapse, and
two-class completions have different slack-zero sets:

| Completion | Exact capacity | Slack-zero set |
|---|---:|---|
| reversible identity | `24 k` | every positive rung |
| copy collapse | `24` | rung one |
| two-class cap | `24 min(k,2)` | rungs one and two |

These formulas prove nonidentifiability for the declared base-agreement,
positivity, and carrier-bound completion class. Lean proves their all-rung
arithmetic. This result does not establish nonidentifiability for the complete
A1--A3 capacity source class.

The executable lift constructs terminal-fiber, observer/interface, public
section, history, continuation, joint-kernel, A2, A3, extension, refinement,
and sewing data on audit rungs one through six. The three arithmetic
completions pass the transported controls on that sample. Hidden spectator
multiplicity fails the sampled A3 state-determinacy control, and parity
oscillation fails the sampled A2 extension square and no-new-confusability
control.

Finite sampled control checks do not prove universal source-contract
membership. The packet therefore carries the verdict
`BOUNDED_GENERATION_REGISTER_COUNTERMODEL__UNIVERSAL_MEMBERSHIP_OPEN`, and
direct N carries `NOT_EVALUABLE_INCOMPLETE_CAPACITY_SOURCE_ANTECEDENT`.
