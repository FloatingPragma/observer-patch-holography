# Primary mathematical/field-theory references used by the specification

- Gambino and Grassi, *The Nielsen identities of the SM and the definition of mass*, Phys. Rev. D 62 (2000), arXiv:hep-ph/9907254.
- B\'elanger et al., *Implementation of the Non-Linear Gauge into GRACE*, arXiv:hep-ph/9907406.
- Dittmaier and Rzehak, *Electroweak renormalization based on gauge-invariant vacuum expectation values of non-linear Higgs representations*, arXiv:2203.07236.
- Denner et al., *Renormalization of mixing angles*, arXiv:1705.06053 (FJ tadpole-scheme discussion and field-reparametrization equivalence).
- Kraus, *Renormalization of the Electroweak Standard Model to All Orders*, arXiv:hep-th/9709154.
- Grassi, Hurth and Steinhauser, *Practical Algebraic Renormalization*, arXiv:hep-ph/9907426.

These references supply standard conditional mathematics. They do not instantiate the OPH source packet or certify a particular implementation.
