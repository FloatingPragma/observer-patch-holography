\begin{abstract}
Observer-Patch Holography models an observer as a finite self-reading patch
with local state, boundary ports, readback records, and repair moves. This
paper gives two gauge-reconstruction routes. The categorical route
reconstructs a compact group from overlap transport on a declared bosonic
sector category. The finite route starts with twelve ports and their
icosahedral incidence. Complete reversible port response and endogenous
observer transport force the compact Lie-algebra type
\(\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)\).
Exact finite calculations derive the carrier module and inverse-port
response. Compact classification and the fixed-space argument exclude the
remaining compact alternative. A conditional matrix realization supplies
the familiar current blocks. An exterior-algebra
witness contains one fifteen-state generation with exact Standard Model
charges and anomaly cancellation. Its common central kernel is
\(\mathbb Z_6\). The physical matrix current, matter action, and global
quotient require same-source response, character-completeness, and
loop-to-kernel receipts. Couplings, scalar attachment, family attachment,
and laboratory-current identification are work in progress.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Two reconstruction routes}
\label{thm:echosahedral-source-selector}
\label{ass:matter-typing}
\label{thm:global-form-boundary}

The first route is structural:
\[
\begin{split}
\text{overlap gluing and transport receipts}
&\Longrightarrow
\text{rigid symmetric sector category}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{Aut}_{\otimes}(\mathcal F).
\end{split}
\]
It reconstructs a compact group on the declared bosonic branch. It does not
select the Standard Model without a realized sector witness.

The second route is finite and carrier-specific:
\label{thm:current-algebra}
\[
\begin{split}
&\text{twelve-port incidence and complete reversible response}\\
&\Longrightarrow\text{internal proper-carrier holonomy}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)\\
&\Longrightarrow
\text{conditional Standard Model matter image}.
\end{split}
\]
This arrow combines three restrictions. Incidence supplies the
twelve-dimensional \(A_5\)-module and its single fixed line. Axiom~1 supplies
the faithful compact commutator-closed response on that module. Axiom~2 makes
the proper carrier action inner on the same response. Incidence without the
response clauses leaves many equivariant laws, while response without
endogenous transport leaves the centreless compact branch available. Their
intersection removes that branch.

The finite argument constructs its compact current inside the Axiom~1 unitary
response space; an ambient continuous gauge group is absent from its premises.
Write \(\mathfrak g=D(\mathbb R^{12})\subset\mathfrak u(H)\). Faithfulness gives
\(\dim\mathfrak g=12\), and commutator closure makes \(\mathfrak g\) compact
reductive. The transitive action of the alternating group on five letters,
\(A_5\), gives \(\dim\mathfrak g^{A_5}=1\). Axiom~2 implements that action
internally on the same response, so it fixes the centre pointwise and
\(\dim Z(\mathfrak g)\leq1\). A centreless compact algebra of dimension twelve
would be \(\mathfrak{su}(2)^4\). Its factorwise inner \(A_5\) action has fixed
dimension zero or three on each factor and cannot have a one-dimensional fixed
space. The centre therefore has dimension one, while the semisimple part has
dimension eleven. Compact-simple classification gives the unique split
\(11=3+8\), which proves
\[
\mathfrak g\cong
\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3).
\]
The carrier identity \(10J=A^3-4A^2-5A+10I\) and target-blind readback derive
\(R=-J\). They do not construct the current derivative. The Lie-type theorem
uses the complete-response clauses of A1 and the endogenous-transport clauses
of A2. A descent repair law enters only when a branch asks for settled public
records. It is not an undeclared premise of the Lie-type theorem. The released
matrix current is an exact conditional witness.
Generation, scalar, global-form, and extra-sector statements carry their own
premises.
