\begin{abstract}
Gauge structure is proposed to be the transport of overlap data between
observer patches. On twelve ports with icosahedral incidence, a faithful
compact commutator-closed response whose proper rechartings act by inner
automorphisms of that same response has exactly one compact Lie type,
\(\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)\).
Faithfulness fixes the dimension twelve, the single fixed line of the
transitive \(A_5\) action bounds the centre by one, the fixed-space count
excludes \(\mathfrak{su}(2)^4\), and the classification of compact simple
Lie algebras, a declared input, leaves the split \(11=3+8\). The dimension
arithmetic and the one-dimensional fixed subspace are machine checked; the
centre bound, the fixed-space exclusion and the classification enter Lean as
declared hypotheses.

A conditional matrix realization supplies the current blocks; its declared
exterior module is the Georgi--Glashow \(\mathbf{10}\oplus\bar{\mathbf 5}\)
generation with exact Standard Model charges and anomaly cancellation. The
record law's covariance under independent port phases, read on the protected
code, generates exactly
\((\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm U(1))/\mathbb Z_6\). Its
order-six central subgroup generated by \(e^{2\pi iY}\) is the flux alphabet of the
protected defects, whose class equals \(6Y\bmod 6\)
and identifies left- and right-handed quarks, leptons with the Higgs, and neutral
fields. The seam cost around a plaquette gives equal bare couplings at the cell, with
\(\sin^2\theta_W=3/8\) there. In the window \(3\le N_g\le5\), the exact
Laplacian costs \(5-\sqrt5<6<5+\sqrt5\) select the rank-three band.
\end{abstract}

\section*{This paper's hypothesis and place in the tower}
\label{sec:gauge-hypothesis}

This paper is the gauge half of layer~5. Its hypothesis is that gauge
structure is the transport of overlap data between observer patches: on the

\emph{What is proved.} The Lie-type theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:current-algebra}) is a finite implication from the
response clauses of premise A1, the endogenous-transport clause of premise
A2 and the classification of compact simple Lie algebras. The categorical
route proves a fixed-cutoff bosonic sector category unconditionally and a
compact Tannaka group on any refinement tail that carries the stated
receipt; a Doplicher--Roberts field net requires further declared
hypotheses. The matrix current, spin lift, trace redistribution, exterior
matter module, band selection, bracket discriminator and local action are
conditional realizations under displayed declarations.

\emph{Open joins.} The executable model must realize the complete response
of A1 and the endogenous transport of A2 from its own repair and memory law,
with the port generators, commutators and implementers reconstructed from
ordered source histories. The tested native exchange response gives
seventeen currents and fails A1; a specified coherent control law gives the
twelve closed currents on a declared control menu
(Section~\ref{sec:executable-current}). Physical spin and statistics, a
physical Weyl field, the global quotient, the family attachment and all
couplings are further joins. The matter hypothesis, that stable massive
matter is a localized topological defect of native overlap transport whose

\emph{Refutation.} The complete accessible current of a specified executable
law, if it is not a faithful closed twelve-dimensional response with inner
carrier action, excludes that law's realization of the branch. A proof that
no admissible local repair and memory law on the carrier supplies the A1 and
A2 response clauses would remove this gauge branch. The finite failure
conditions are listed in Section~\ref{sec:gauge-failure-conditions}.

\section{The finite-current Lie-type theorem}
\label{sec:lie-type}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-current fixed-space theorem]
\label{thm:current-algebra}\label{thm:finite-current-fixed-space}
Let \(\mathfrak g=D(\mathbb R^{12})\subset\mathfrak u(H)\) be the image of
the complete reversible twelve-port response of premise A1, and let the
proper carrier rotations act on it by inner automorphisms of the same
response, as premise A2 requires. Then
\[
\mathfrak g\cong
\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3).
\]
The implication assumes no ambient continuous gauge group and is
independent of the categorical refinement receipt.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Faithfulness gives \(\dim\mathfrak g=12\). Commutator closure inside
\(\mathfrak u(H)\) makes \(\mathfrak g\) compact reductive, so
\(\mathfrak g=Z(\mathfrak g)\oplus[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]\). The
transitive action of the alternating group \(A_5\) on the twelve ports gives
\(\dim\mathfrak g^{A_5}=1\). Inner automorphisms fix the centre pointwise,
so \(Z(\mathfrak g)\subset\mathfrak g^{A_5}\) and
\(\dim Z(\mathfrak g)\le1\). If the centre vanished, \(\mathfrak g\) would be
semisimple of dimension twelve; the compact simple algebras of dimension at
most twelve have dimensions \(3\), \(8\) and \(10\)~\cite{knapp2002}, so
\(\mathfrak g\cong\mathfrak{su}(2)^4\). The inner action preserves each
simple factor of \(\mathfrak{su}(2)^4\); simplicity of \(A_5\) makes each
restricted action trivial or faithful, with fixed dimension three or zero.
No sum of such terms equals one, so this branch is excluded. Hence
\(\dim Z(\mathfrak g)=1\), the semisimple part has dimension eleven, and
compact-simple classification gives the unique split \(11=3+8\).
\end{proof}

The twelve ports are premise content of A1, so the port count fixes the
dimension twelve by declaration; the content of the
theorem is the exclusion of \(\mathfrak{su}(2)^4\) and a one-dimensional
centre. The dimension arithmetic, the one-dimensional fixed subspace and the
dimension-six exclusion below are machine checked in the accompanying Lean
development~\cite{ophrepo}. The centre bound, the \(\mathfrak{su}(2)^{4}\)
fixed-space step and the compact-Lie classification enter Lean as declared hypotheses, and no
source current or full Lie-algebra isomorphism is constructed there. The
formal interface keeps the inputs separate: an injective port-response map
supplies dimension twelve; an inner carrier action places the centre inside
the one-dimensional fixed space; the fixed-space exclusion supplies a nonzero
centre; and compact-simple classification supplies the allowed simple
dimensions \(3,8,10\). The final Lean selection theorem takes the last three
constraints as hypotheses and proves the split. An observable relative phase
alone establishes neither centrality nor trivial carrier action on the whole
centre. The trivial carrier action on the centre is a sufficient premise for
this Lie type; the proof supplies no independent physical origin for it.

\section{The categorical route}
\label{sec:categorical-route}

\subsection{Transport from overlap gluing}

At a fixed regulator the overlap rechartings of a connected cut \(\Sigma\)
form a compact unitary transition system. We use chronological multiplication
of comparison maps: \(U_{ij}\) maps components in chart \(j\) to components
in chart \(i\), so an overlap path \(p=(i_0\to\cdots\to i_m)\) has
\(U_p=U_{i_0i_1}\cdots U_{i_{m-1}i_m}\). Thus \(U_p\) compares the terminal
chart to the initial chart; forward transport of column components is
\(U_p^\dagger\). If \(p\ast q\) means first following \(p\), then \(q\),
the conventions are \(U_{p\ast q}=U_pU_q\) and
\(U_{\overline p}=U_p^{-1}\) for the reversed path \(\overline p\).
Gauge-as-gluing, the tree and loop
lemmas and the transportability theorem are proved in the spacetime
paper~\cite{ophEinstein}. The gauge route consumes the following exact
criterion.

On the central-defect branch, identify the overlap centres with one fixed
abelian unitary coefficient group \(Z_\Sigma\) on which overlap transport acts
trivially. With \(U_{ji}=U_{ij}^{-1}\), define the implementer-level
multiplier
\[
\Omega_{ijk}=U_{ij}U_{jk}U_{ki}=z_{ijk}\in Z_\Sigma,
\]
equivalently \(U_{ij}U_{jk}=z_{ijk}U_{ik}\). The multiplier is defined at the
implementer level: \(\mathrm{Ad}(z_{ijk})\) is the identity and does not
record the defect.

\begin{theorem}[Central defect strictification and residual holonomy]
\label{thm:central-defect-strictification}
The family \(\{z_{ijk}\}\) is an untwisted \v{C}ech 2-cocycle with
coefficients in \(Z_\Sigma\), and its class
\([z]\in\check H^2(N_\Sigma,Z_\Sigma)\) is gauge invariant. A central edge
rephasing by a cochain in \(C^1(N_\Sigma,Z_\Sigma)\) removes every triangle
defect if and only if \([z]=0\). After such a rephasing, let
\(U^{\mathrm{str}}\) be the resulting edge \(1\)-cocycle and
\(\mathcal C_\alpha\) the full transported charge block, including carrier
and multiplicity data. Loop-coherent endpoint-only transport exists on that
block if and only if, in addition,
\[
\operatorname{Hol}_{\alpha,U^{\mathrm{str}}}([\gamma])
=U^{\mathrm{str}}_\gamma\big|_{\mathcal C_\alpha}
=\mathbf 1_{\mathcal C_\alpha}
\qquad
\text{for every }[\gamma]\in\pi_1(|N_\Sigma|,i_*).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Conjugation by \(\Omega_{ijk}\) fixes every triple-overlap observable, so the
multiplier is central there; the fixed-coefficient and trivial-action
hypotheses place it in \(Z_\Sigma\). Comparing \((U_{ij}U_{jk})U_{kl}\) with
\(U_{ij}(U_{jk}U_{kl})\) on a quadruple overlap gives
\(z_{jkl}z_{ikl}^{-1}z_{ijl}z_{ijk}^{-1}=1\). A vertex-frame change
\(U_{ij}\mapsto V_iU_{ij}V_j^\dagger\) conjugates \(z_{ijk}\) and leaves a
central multiplier unchanged. A central edge rephasing
\(U_{ij}\mapsto a_{ij}^{-1}U_{ij}\) replaces \(z\) by the corresponding
coboundary, so \([z]=0\) is equivalent to removing all triangle multipliers.
For two paths \(p,p'\) with common endpoints, the residual comparison
\(U_pU_{p'}^{-1}\) is the holonomy of the closed loop
\(p\ast\overline{p'}\), based at their common initial chart. It is the
identity for every pair exactly when the displayed holonomy representation
is trivial. Conversely, strict
path independence makes every triangle comparison and every represented loop
action trivial.
\end{proof}

If overlap transport acts nontrivially on a local system of centres, the
comparison carries the transported factor and requires the twisted \v{C}ech
differential. On the genuinely noncentral branch the coefficient data is a
crossed module \(H_\Sigma\to G_\Sigma\). The vanishing \(o^{(2)}_\Sigma=0\)
strictifies the higher associator, after which at least one allowed strict
\(G_\Sigma\)-valued representative must have trivial represented holonomy. The
full orbit \(q_\Sigma\) need not determine a unique ordinary \(H^1\) class
and is not itself the strictification test. A nonzero \(o^{(2)}_\Sigma\)
remains a higher-gauge sector, and an orbit whose every strict representative
has residual loop action lies outside the ordinary path-independent sector.
The class \([z]\) is structurally analogous to an effective 't~Hooft anomaly
class; a map between them requires a separate anomaly-descent construction,
so \([z]=0\) is used here only as the internal strictifiability condition.
The overlap obstruction calculus classifies and routes sectors; it does not
select the Standard Model.

\subsection{The fixed-cutoff sector category}

At cutoff \(r\), choose one common stagewise strict edge cocycle
\(U^{\mathrm{str}}_r\) from the allowed representatives, and retain only seeds
on which this representative acts trivially around every closed overlap loop.
Sectors requiring incompatible strictifications define alternative
fixed-stage categories; they are not unioned before tensor closure.

\begin{definition}[Fixed-cutoff tensor-generated sector category]
Let \(S_r\) be the set of visible collar carriers \(W_{\alpha,r}\) with trivial
holonomy under the common \(U^{\mathrm{str}}_r\). Define
\[
\mathsf{Sect}^{\mathrm{bos}}_r
=\bigl\langle\mathbf 1,S_r,S_r^*\bigr\rangle_{
\oplus,\otimes,\mathrm{subobjects},\mathrm{isomorphism}}
\subset\operatorname{Rep}_{\mathrm{fd}}(\widehat K_{\Sigma,r}),
\]
the replete full additive Karoubi rigid tensor subcategory generated by the
finitely many visible carriers. Its simple objects are the irreducible
summands of finite tensor words in seeds and conjugates; there may be
infinitely many of them although every object and Hom space is
finite-dimensional.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-cutoff bosonic sector category]
At every fixed regulator cutoff, on the ordinary or central-defect
zero-obstruction branch and in the bosonic internal-gauge sector of the
\(3+1\)-dimensional effective regime, \(\mathsf{Sect}^{\mathrm{bos}}_r\) is a
semisimple rigid symmetric \(C^*\)-tensor category with one monoidally
coherent path-independent transport. The carrier-forgetful functor
\(F_r:\mathsf{Sect}^{\mathrm{bos}}_r\to\mathsf{Hilb}_{\mathrm{fd}}\) is
faithful, \(^*\)-preserving and symmetric strong monoidal.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Complete reducibility of finite-dimensional representations of the compact
group gives semisimplicity and subobjects; the operator adjoint and norm give
the \(C^*\)-structure; conjugate representations give duals; the flip gives
the bosonic symmetry. Around a closed loop \(U^{\mathrm{str}}_r\) acts as the
identity on every retained seed, hence on direct sums, tensor products,
conjugates and invariant subobjects. Collar concatenation realizes tensor
words whenever the finite tensor-realization receipt is supplied.
\end{proof}

Fermionic signs, spinorial matter and chirality are not part of this bosonic
category; they belong to the matter lift of Section~\ref{sec:matter}.

\subsection{Refinement and compact-group reconstruction}

\begin{definition}[Compact-gauge refinement receipt]
On a cofinal tail, the receipt supplies: a surjectivity certificate for each
finite-state restriction \(\rho_{sr}:Q_s\to Q_r\); multiplicities
\(m_{\beta\alpha}^{rs}\) and unitaries \(V_\beta^{rs}\) with
\(d_{\beta,s}=\sum_\alpha m_{\beta\alpha}^{rs}d_{\alpha,r}\), defining the
injective block embedding
\[
\bigl(\jmath_{rs}(a)\bigr)_\beta
=V_\beta^{rs}\left[\bigoplus_\alpha
\bigl(a_\alpha\otimes\mathbf 1_{m_{\beta\alpha}^{rs}}\bigr)\right](V_\beta^{rs})^*
\]
of the collar algebras, coherent under composition; continuous surjections
\(\pi_{sr}^{\Sigma}:\widehat K_{\Sigma,s}\twoheadrightarrow\widehat K_{\Sigma,r}\)
with \(\pi_{tr}^{\Sigma}=\pi_{sr}^{\Sigma}\circ\pi_{ts}^{\Sigma}\); one common
strict representative at each stage, intertwined by the pullbacks; and
compatible finite concatenated-collar realizations of every tensor word and
invariant subobject projector. The finite-state consensus maps alone imply
none of these data.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Refinement functor and fibre descent]
On a tail carrying the receipt, \(U_{rs}:=(\pi_{sr}^{\Sigma})^*:
\mathsf{Sect}^{\mathrm{bos}}_r\to\mathsf{Sect}^{\mathrm{bos}}_s\) is fully
faithful, symmetric strong monoidal and \(^*\)-preserving; it preserves the
unit, duals, irreducibles and the full zero-obstruction criterion, with
\(U_{st}U_{rs}=U_{rt}\) and \(F_sU_{rs}=F_r\) on carrier spaces.
