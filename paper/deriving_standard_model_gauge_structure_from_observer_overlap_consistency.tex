\begin{abstract}
Observer-Patch Holography models an observer as a finite self-reading patch
with local state, boundary ports, readback records, and repair moves. This
paper gives two gauge-reconstruction routes. The categorical route
reconstructs a compact group from overlap transport on a declared bosonic
sector category. The finite route starts with twelve ports and their
icosahedral incidence. Complete reversible port response and endogenous
observer transport force the compact Lie-algebra type
\(\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)\).
Exact finite calculations derive the carrier module and inverse-port
response. Compact classification and the fixed-space argument exclude the
remaining compact alternative. A conditional matrix realization supplies
the familiar current blocks. An exterior-algebra
witness contains one fifteen-state generation with exact Standard Model
charges and anomaly cancellation. Its common central kernel is
\(\mathbb Z_6\). Among single complete faithful bands in the conditional
window \(3\le N_g\le5\), the exact Laplacian costs
\(5-\sqrt5<6<5+\sqrt5\) select the rank-three band. The physical matrix
current, matter action, and global
quotient require same-source response, character-completeness, and
loop-to-kernel receipts. The compact locus of the equivariant Jacobi
variety is classified into three certified bracket families with exact
carrier-selected global forms, and over those brackets the Killing kinetic
term is forced block-diagonal with one coupling ray per simple factor and
is the derived action of its own finite source dynamics. Coupling scales,
scalar attachment, family attachment,
and laboratory-current identification are premises outside these theorems.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Two reconstruction routes}
\label{thm:echosahedral-source-selector}
\label{ass:matter-typing}
\label{thm:global-form-boundary}

The first route is structural:
\[
\begin{split}
\text{overlap gluing and transport receipts}
&\Longrightarrow
\text{rigid symmetric sector category}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{Aut}_{\otimes}(\mathcal F).
\end{split}
\]
It reconstructs a compact group on the declared bosonic branch. It does not
select the Standard Model without a realized sector witness.

The second route is finite and carrier-specific:
\label{thm:current-algebra}
\[
\begin{split}
&\text{twelve-port incidence and complete reversible response}\\
&\Longrightarrow\text{internal proper-carrier holonomy}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)\\
&\Longrightarrow
\text{conditional Standard Model matter image}.
\end{split}
\]
This arrow combines three restrictions. Incidence supplies the
twelve-dimensional \(A_5\)-module and its single fixed line. Axiom~1 supplies
the faithful compact commutator-closed response on that module. Axiom~2 makes
the proper carrier action inner on the same response. Incidence without the
response clauses leaves many equivariant laws, while response without
endogenous transport leaves the centreless compact branch available. Their
intersection removes that branch.

The finite argument constructs its compact current inside the Axiom~1 unitary
response space; an ambient continuous gauge group is absent from its premises.
Write \(\mathfrak g=D(\mathbb R^{12})\subset\mathfrak u(H)\). Faithfulness gives
\(\dim\mathfrak g=12\), and commutator closure makes \(\mathfrak g\) compact
reductive. The transitive action of the alternating group on five letters,
\(A_5\), gives \(\dim\mathfrak g^{A_5}=1\). Axiom~2 implements that action
internally on the same response, so it fixes the centre pointwise and
\(\dim Z(\mathfrak g)\leq1\). A centreless compact algebra of dimension twelve
would be \(\mathfrak{su}(2)^4\). Its factorwise inner \(A_5\) action has fixed
dimension zero or three on each factor and cannot have a one-dimensional fixed
space. The centre therefore has dimension one, while the semisimple part has
dimension eleven. Compact-simple classification gives the unique split
\(11=3+8\), which proves
\[
\mathfrak g\cong
\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3).
\]
The carrier identity \(10J=A^3-4A^2-5A+10I\) and target-blind readback derive
\(R=-J\). They do not construct the current derivative. The Lie-type theorem
uses the complete-response clauses of A1 and the endogenous-transport clauses
of A2. A descent repair law enters only when a branch asks for settled public
records. It is not an undeclared premise of the Lie-type theorem. The
finite Lie-type classification is machine checked in the project's Lean
development. The registered recurrence generates the exact commutative
algebra \(\operatorname{span}\{I,A,A^2,A^3\}\). It contains neither twelve
independent current generators nor a nonzero bracket, and its only proper
recharting is the identity. This result is bounded to the registered
single-recurrence packet. The released matrix current is an exact conditional
witness. The target-free diagonal phase lift has twelve independent first
derivatives and an abelian bracket. Adding the registered connected adjacency
tangent generates \(\mathfrak u(12)\), with derived rank \(143\). This exact
control rejects that direct lift and leaves non-diagonal source responses in
play. Conditional on the canonical oriented carrier, the complete target-free
space \(\operatorname{Hom}_{A_5}(\Lambda^2\mathbb Q^{12},\mathbb Q^{12})\)
has dimension fourteen and an exact rational Reynolds basis. It is a search
space only; no bracket is selected. Substitution of the general bracket into
Jacobi gives 2,640 coordinate equations in 44 target orbits and an exact
coefficient-row rank of 38. A channel change over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\)
splits that rowspace into eleven fixed-line derivation-weight products and a
rank-27 residual system. The residual equations apply on every weight flat.
A three-parameter derivation-only Jacobi family is exact and supplies no
compactness or source selection. The compact real locus of that
fourteen-parameter equivariant variety is now classified, conditional on
three named textbook lemmas and exactly over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\), into
three certified bracket families, and for each family the coroot lattices,
integral carrier weights, and carrier-selected global form are computed
exactly; the selected forms are adjoint with zero abelian charge lattice, so
the abelian sector is undetermined at carrier level and stands exposed
rather than repaired. Over those certified brackets the Killing kinetic
term is forced: it decomposes block-diagonally over the sector projectors
with exactly zero cross entries, the invariant-form space of each simple
factor is one-dimensional, so the kinetic sector carries exactly one
coupling ray per simple factor, and the certified kinetic form is the
derived action of its own finite Gibbs dynamics, unique up to an additive
constant and a multiplier rescaling, with the coupling scale identified
with the multiplier gauge. Enumeration of the noncompact components,
source selection of a bracket, same-current holonomy, and the coupling
scales themselves are undischarged \cite{ophfpe,ophrepo}.
Generation, scalar, global-form, and extra-sector statements carry their own
premises.
