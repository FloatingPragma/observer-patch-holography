\begin{abstract}
Observer-Patch Holography models an observer as a finite self-reading patch
with local state, boundary ports, readback records, and repair moves. This
paper gives two gauge-reconstruction routes with distinct proof boundaries. The categorical route
starts from overlap transport and reconstructs a compact group through a
receipt-certified bosonic sector category and its fiber functor. The finite
carrier route derives an inverse-port response from icosahedral incidence and
target-blind readback, then constructs the current algebra
\(\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)\).
An exhaustive scan of all 1024 subsets of the exterior module selects the
rank-15 matter object: exactly one unordered conjugate pair is chiral and
anomaly free, and the fermionic-parity grading is an output of the scan
rather than a declared contract. A measured transport artifact supplies the
non-split double cover of the deck action, whose central element is the
unique source implementation of that grading, so the Spin/odd-Weyl typing
is forced at finite source-model scope. Anomaly balance fixes the Standard
Model charges up to conjugation and the realized tensors have common kernel
\(\mathbb Z_6\), with maximal faithful matter image
\((\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1))/\mathbb Z_6\).
Local tensors alone also admit the cover and the \(\mathbb Z_2\) and
\(\mathbb Z_3\) quotients; the selection is carried by the measured
flux-sector menu of the carrier federation, which realized matter transports
single-valuedly and which matches exactly the \(\mathbb Z_6\) quotient.
Scalar existence and multiplicity, four-dimensional instanton sectors and theta
periodicity, laboratory-current attachment, and identification of the finite
current with the categorical group require separate evidence.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Two reconstruction routes}
\label{thm:echosahedral-source-selector}
\label{ass:matter-typing}
\label{thm:global-form-boundary}

The first route is structural:
\[
\begin{split}
\text{overlap gluing and transport receipts}
&\Longrightarrow
\text{rigid symmetric sector category}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{Aut}_{\otimes}(\mathcal F).
\end{split}
\]
It reconstructs a compact group on the declared bosonic branch. It does not
select the Standard Model without a realized sector witness.

The second route is finite and carrier-specific:
\label{thm:current-algebra}
\[
\begin{split}
&\text{icosahedral incidence}
\Longrightarrow 10J=A^3-4A^2-5A+10I
\Longrightarrow R=-J\\
&\Longrightarrow
\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)
\Longrightarrow
\text{conditional Standard Model matter image}.
\end{split}
\]
The response signs, equivariant lift, anomaly balance, charge lattice, and
common tensor kernel are independently recomputed by released certificates.
This gauge implication uses the incidence, response, and matter premises
alone. Generation, scalar, and extra-sector statements carry their own
separately marked declarations.
