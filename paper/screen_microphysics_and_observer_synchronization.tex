\begin{abstract}
This paper develops the carrier layer of Observer-Patch Holography (OPH):
the hypothesis that federations of twelve-port icosahedral carriers,
repaired by the native seam law, are the substrate on which observers,
clocks and geometry execute. On one carrier, oriented incidence and an
integer atom-counting grammar with normalized readback cost give twelve
unit port lines with exact gap two, the inverse-port pairing, the proper
action of \(A_5\) and an exact rank-three Gram frame. The classical seam
rule follows from four stated requirements, its quantum record laws form
one convex family classified by five coherence multipliers, and the
slowest band of the uniform native repair mean is the normalized
rank-three Gram band. Any finite-range cosine kinetics invariant under the
sixty proper icosahedral rotations is isotropic through angular rank five
at every order in the cell size; its first directional term is a multiple
of the unique rank-six invariant, which has an exact sixty-two-point Morse
census and enters at order \(a^4k^6\) whenever the weighted sixth moment is
nonzero. On the thirty seams we prove the exact integer current image,
finite continuity, a rational Coulomb Green function, local face-curvature
and step-scaled Maxwell-shaped actions with sharp stability threshold
\(h^2(3+\sqrt5)<4\), and a continuum limit of the complete seam symbol on a
supplied Euclidean domain. Stationary native repair has an exact covariance
and record-power law. Central records carry the Born--L\"uders identities
for a declared algebra-state representation, and checkpoint data fix an
observer's future law. Federations of \(20\), \(80\) and \(320\) carriers
serve larger domains and refinement; their cross-carrier routing is a
declared convention, and the existing port-ancestry map does not preserve
seam adjacency. The carrier's oriented face complex has vanishing first
cohomology. The distinct two-load configuration complex nevertheless has
$\mathbb Z/2$ exchange topology, and a specified coherent ordering memory
protects its two quantum characters. This finite transport sector does not
yet supply the localized overlap defect required for physical matter.
\end{abstract}

\section*{This paper's hypothesis and place in the tower}
\label{sec:hypothesis-tower}

This paper is layer~2 of the tower: the carrier and its repair law. Its
hypothesis is the following.

\begin{quote}\itshape
The substrate is a federation of twelve-port icosahedral carriers whose
thirty internal seams and routed cross-carrier ports are repaired by the
native C/E seam law. One carrier supports the local observer, clock and
geometry constructions; federations of \(20\), \(80\) and \(320\) carriers
on the nested icosahedral screens serve larger domains and refinement.
\end{quote}

\paragraph{What is proved.}
Unconditional finite theorems on the declared carrier: the
counting-and-incidence selector
(Theorem~\ref{thm:echosahedral-source-selector-microphysics}); the
characterization of the classical seam rule and the five-multiplier record
family (Section~\ref{subsec:native-seam-rule}); the rank-three slow band of
the uniform repair mean (Proposition~\ref{prop:native-rank-three-band});
angular-rank isotropy and the rank-six residue
(Section~\ref{sec:angular-rank-isotropy}); the exact seam incidence, Green,
action and stability results (Section~\ref{sec:seam-electromagnetism}); the
stationary repair covariance (Section~\ref{sec:native-repair-histories});
and the central-record and checkpoint theorems
(Section~\ref{sec:records-checkpoints}). Conditional implications: the
continuum Maxwell limit takes a supplied Euclidean domain and reversible
dynamics; the dispersion coefficients and photon threshold take physical
position, clock and sector attachments; the edge-center scalar register
(Appendix~\ref{app:edge-center-scalar-register}) takes completeness and
reserve premises.

\paragraph{Open joins.}
The twelve-port architecture, the seam schedule and the record-law
covariances are premises; closure does not select them. Cross-carrier
routing on the federations is a supplied convention, and a record-derived
refinement-natural routing that preserves seam adjacency is unconstructed
(Section~\ref{sec:federations-screens}). The rank-three band is a
dimensionless response metric on twelve ports; physical position, an
extended three-dimensional space, a physical clock and an energy unit
require further maps.

\paragraph{What this layer feeds upward.}
Layer~3 executes observers on these carriers~\cite{ophmodel}; layer~4 reads
their clocks and the screen generator; layer~5 places stable matter in
localized defects of overlap transport. That transport must live on the
seams and routed ports of this layer. The seam graph has nineteen
independent holonomies, but the oriented face complex has vanishing first
cohomology and native load contrasts are exact, so a protected native
defect needs transport data that the repair law must supply
(Section~\ref{subsec:carrier-transport-defects}).

\paragraph{Refutation.}
The carrier branch fails if an observable depends on hidden carrier
coordinates after the full visible signature is fixed
(Claim~\ref{claim:implementation-invariance}), if no admissible routing of
the federations is refinement-natural and adjacency preserving, or if the
repair laws on this architecture admit no localized defect sector protected
under admitted repairs. The tested native double-load candidate is
transient, and the existing port-ancestry map fails adjacency; both are
facts about the tested law.

\section{Observer patches and their carriers}
\label{sec:observer-patch-semantics}

An observer patch is first an operational algebraic object,
\[
\mathsf O_i=
\left(
\mathcal A_i,\rho_i,\mathcal R_i,
\{(\mathcal I_e,\pi_{i,e})\}_{e\ni i},
\mathcal U_i,\mathrm{Chk}_i
\right).
\]
Here \(\mathcal A_i\) is the finite or regulated accessible algebra, \(\rho_i\) is the accessible
state, \(\mathcal R_i\subseteq Z(\mathcal A_i)\) is the exact central record algebra or a declared
approximate record algebra, \(\mathcal I_e\) is an overlap-visible interface algebra,
\(\pi_{i,e}:\mathcal A_i\to\mathcal I_e\) is the visible restriction map, \(\mathcal U_i\) is the
allowed family of update and repair instruments, and \(\mathrm{Chk}_i\) is checkpoint data
sufficient for the target reconstruction problem.

\begin{definition}[Abstract observer patch]
An abstract observer patch is the tuple \(\mathsf O_i\) above, considered up to isomorphism of
accessible algebras, record statistics, visible restrictions, repair instruments, and checkpoint
continuation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Support patch]
A support patch is a geometric chart for an abstract observer patch on a branch with reconstructed
geometry. Examples are a cap \(P_i\subset S^2\), a collar, or a causal diamond.
The support patch supplies chart data for modular flow, entropy variation, and overlap comparison.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Carrier patch]
A carrier patch is a physical or digital implementation satisfying
\(\operatorname{Realize}_{\varepsilon}(H_i)\to\mathsf O_i\):
the implementation induces the declared accessible algebra, visible interface
statistics, record readout, repair instruments, and checkpoint continuation within error
\(\varepsilon\).
\end{definition}

The observer identity lives in the quotient data exposed by \(\mathsf O_i\).
The tuple does not impose equal primitive observer size: fixed-cutoff OPH
requires bounded accessible algebras and declared interfaces, and an
observer-supporting object may be a variable finite federation of carriers.
A theorem selecting isomorphic fixed-capacity elementary carriers would need
homogeneous cellulation, isomorphic local algebras, equivariant overlaps,
refinement preservation of the cell type, and a proof that one cell or a
fixed block of cells is the primitive carrier.

\begin{claim}[Presentation invariance and carrier sensitivity]\label{claim:implementation-invariance}
Two carrier patches are physically equivalent for the declared OPH observables when a
quotient-visible isomorphism preserves their accessible algebras and states, interface algebras,
record statistics, repair maps, checkpoint continuation, response laws, and refinement lineage.
On the twelve-port branch it must also preserve the oriented incidence and its
antipodal pairing. A material, coordinate, or wiring change satisfying this contract is silent. A
change of the preserved carrier signature is outside the equivalence claim and may change the
physical branch.
\end{claim}

The claim is theorem-level on a branch that supplies the required quotient
isomorphism and error bound. It fails if a predicted observable depends on
hidden carrier coordinates after the full visible signature has been held
fixed. It says nothing about a cube and an icosahedron, whose visible
incidence structures differ. OPH is therefore presentation-invariant and
carrier-sensitive: port incidence, orientation, response, repair, clock and
refinement data can change the physical branch.

\begin{definition}[Operational observer test]\label{def:operational-observer-test}
A candidate physical system realizes an observer patch when it supplies a bounded accessible
interface, durable re-readable records, self-read or internal state estimation, record-conditioned
future behavior, boundary prediction better than shuffled-record controls, and checkpoint
continuation within a declared error.
\end{definition}

The formal finite receipt implements seven corresponding algebraic clauses at
one regulator. Two operational observers have unequal owner-region records
whose typed restrictions agree on a nonzero common section accessible to both;
a one-corner mutation breaks the agreement. This is a bounded
overlap-readable observer receipt, without a physical instrument, source
realization, refinement naturality, or higher-overlap coherence.

\begin{definition}[Federated patch carrier]
\label{def:federated-patch-carrier}
A fixed-cutoff federated patch carrier is a tuple
\[
\mathfrak F=(V,E,\{\mathcal A_i\}_{i\in V},\{\mathcal I_e\}_{e\in E},
\{\pi_{i,e}\},\{\mathcal R_i\},\{\mathcal U_i\}),
\]
where \(V\) is a finite set of patches, \(E\) is a finite overlap graph,
\(\mathcal A_i\) is the local finite algebra of patch \(i\), \(\mathcal I_e\) is the finite
interface algebra on overlap \(e=\{i,j\}\), \(\pi_{i,e}:\mathcal A_i\to\mathcal I_e\) is the
declared visible restriction, \(\mathcal R_i\subseteq Z(\mathcal A_i)\) is the patch record
algebra, and \(\mathcal U_i\) is the allowed local update and repair interface.
The carrier is observer-facing when every physical claim is made through the
visible restrictions, record algebras and quotient-local observables; hidden
coordinates may exist but are not directly physical.
\end{definition}

The smallest instance is one twelve-port carrier. In the executable observer
construction a single carrier supports overlapping observer regions, native
repair and local event clocks, and joined carriers serve larger regions,
distant interventions and refinement~\cite{ophmodel}. A fixed-cutoff
simulator keeps three operators on the physical quotient \(Q_r\) distinct:
the strict-descent accepted-repair normalizer \(\operatorname{Rep}_r\), a
separately declared equilibrium or Euclidean relaxation \(e^{-tL_r^{\rm eq}}\),
and the reversible modular automorphism group \(\sigma_t^\rho\). If an
accepted move lowers a well-founded repair measure, its reverse cannot be
another accepted descending step, so the accepted relation cannot serve as a
reversible equilibrium law. A common local proposal menu may feed all three
constructions without identifying them.

\subsection{The echosahedral carrier and its selector}

\begin{definition}[Echosahedral carrier]
\label{def:echosahedral-carrier}
An \emph{echosahedral carrier}, or echosahedron, is a finite patch object
\[
\mathcal E=(\mathcal A,\{P_a\}_{a=0}^{11},\{\rho_a\}_{a=0}^{11},
\mathcal R,\mathcal M,\mathcal U,\mathcal G_{\mathcal E}),
\]
where \(\mathcal A\) is the internal finite algebra, \(P_0,\ldots,P_{11}\) are
twelve labeled overlap ports, \(\rho_a:\mathcal A\to\mathcal I_a\) are port
readout maps, \(\mathcal R\subseteq Z(\mathcal A)\) is the observer-accessible
record algebra, \(\mathcal M\) is a finite mismatch-score family on exposed
port packets, \(\mathcal U\) is a finite family of local update and repair
instruments, and \(\mathcal G_{\mathcal E}\) is a declared finite symmetry
group of the port arrangement. Its ports are the vertices of an oriented
icosahedron and its thirty internal seams are the edges.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Certified echosahedral counting realization]
\label{def:certified-echosahedral-lineage}
A certified local lineage augments the quotient-visible echosahedral patch by:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item twelve primitive orthogonal port-center atoms \(e_{r,p}\), each of
normalized trace \(1/12\);
\item the oriented \(12\)-vertex, \(30\)-edge, \(20\)-face triangular
incidence packet, with degree-five vertices and five-cycle links;
\item a separately declared integer atom-counting grammar with total-charge
fiber \(\mathcal Q_r=\{q\in\mathbb Z^{12}:\sum_pq_p=12\}\), and the
normalized central-readback Hilbert--Schmidt cost
\(H_r(q)=\sum_pq_p^2\);
\item an append-only twelve-register event packet in which writes and
retractions carry signs \(+1\) and \(-1\), together with conservative
whole-unit seam repairs and minimum-move settling cost.  An accepted repair
orients a seam from \(i\) to \(j\) only when
\(d=q_i-q_j\ge2\), then replaces
\((q_i,q_j)\) by \((q_i-1,q_j+1)\); uphill and equalizing-reversal moves are
not repair steps;
\item every refinement carries an orientation-preserving incidence

\item a source manifest that rejects Standard Model, product-adjoint, gauge,
coupling, particle, measured-target, and fitted-coordinate fields, is closed
to undeclared fields, and encodes trace fractions exactly.
\end{enumerate}
The signed-event packet realizes integer loads and its separate dynamic
settling cost. The total-charge fiber, quadratic cost normalization, and

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Echosahedral counting-and-incidence selector theorem]
\label{thm:echosahedral-source-selector-microphysics}
On every lineage of
Definition~\ref{def:certified-echosahedral-lineage}, the declared realization packet
canonically determines:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item the twelve coefficient lines \(\mathbb R e_{r,p}\) and the unique
all-one defect allocation, with \(H_{\min}=12\), next floor \(14\), and exact
gap \(2\);
\item integer loads generated by the signed atomic events, termination of
every accepted settling path by strict descent of
\(V(q)=\sum_pq_p^2\), and a minimum settling-move cost natural under every
carrier rotation and declared refinement;
\item the unique fixed-point-free inverse pairing by graph distance three and
the resulting six axes;
\item the full incidence group \(A_5\times C_2\) and its
orientation-preserving subgroup \(A_5\);
\item the exact incidence Gram matrix
\[
G_{pq}\in\{1,1/\sqrt5,-1/\sqrt5,-1\}
\quad\text{at distances }0,1,2,3,
\]
with
\[
G^2=4G,\qquad \operatorname{tr}G=12,\qquad \operatorname{rank}G=3,
\]
whose oriented factorization is the regular six-axis icosahedral frame up to
\(SO(3)\); and
\item refinement naturality and arbitrary simultaneous port-relabeling

\end{enumerate}
Thus this realization gives the exact twelve-line unit allocation and gap,
integer settling dynamics, inverse pairing, proper \(A_5\) action, and
rank-three Gram frame stated above.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Primitive central atoms give the intrinsic line family.  On the total-charge
fiber,
\[
H(q)=12+\sum_p(q_p-1)^2,
\]
so the all-one allocation is unique and the least nonzero integral zero-sum
deviation has squared norm two. A seam repair with oriented mismatch
\(d\geq2\) changes the load square by \(-2(d-1)\), which proves termination.
Every declared rotation and refinement is an isomorphism of the repair graph
and therefore preserves shortest-path cost. The explicit full-pile witness
settles in eighteen moves. The exact incidence distance profile is
\((1,5,5,1)\), giving a unique distance-three involution.  Enumeration gives
\(120\) incidence automorphisms and \(60\) orientation-preserving ones;
conjugation on the five Klein-four subgroups identifies the latter faithfully
with all even permutations of five objects, hence with \(A_5\).  Exact
multiplication in \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\) gives \(G^2=4G\), and the trace fixes
rank three.  Every declared refinement or complete relabeling preserves the
source formulas.  The exact implementation and countermodels are carried by the echosahedral
selector certificate and its accompanying analysis.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The native seam rule and its record laws}
\label{subsec:native-seam-rule}

The executable observer construction~\cite{ophmodel} fixes one repair law on
this carrier. For an oriented seam \(e=(a,b)\) with loads \(x_a,x_b\) and a
fair coin \(c\in\{0,1\}\), set \(d=x_a-x_b\) and
\[
j=\tfrac12\bigl[d-(2c-1)(|d|\bmod2)\bigr],\qquad
x\longmapsto x+j\,(\delta_b-\delta_a).
\]
The endpoints become the floor and the ceiling of their mean; when their
total is odd the coin decides which endpoint receives the extra unit. The
rule conserves the total and never increases \(\sum_px_p^2\). At difference
one it waits or swaps, so the balanced binary shell keeps moving instead of
settling pathwise. Accepted whole-unit repairs at difference at least two
are the strict-descent moves of
Theorem~\ref{thm:echosahedral-source-selector-microphysics}.

\begin{proposition}[The classical seam rule is characterized]
\label{prop:classical-seam-rule}
Let a stochastic seam rule (i) conserve the seam total, (ii) end at a
configuration minimizing \(x_a^2+x_b^2\), (iii) break any remaining tie by a
random choice independent of the loads, and (iv) be symmetric under exchange
of the two endpoints. Then it is the rule above with a fair coin.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By (i) and (ii) the outputs are the floor and ceiling of the mean. An even
total has one output. An odd total has two, and by (iii) endpoint \(a\)
receives the ceiling with a constant probability \(q\). Exchange of the
endpoints maps \(q\) to \(1-q\), so (iv) gives \(q=1/2\).
\end{proof}

Each requirement does work. Without (ii), partial and idle moves are
admitted. Without (iii), the rule that gives the extra unit to the larger
endpoint satisfies the others and freezes every binary configuration. On the
executed total-two sector the seam difference is at most two, so there the
requirements fix only the tie-break coin. On classical diagonal states of
the binary shell the rule is the lazy symmetric simple exclusion process with
two particles; the twelve collision states of the \(78\)-configuration
carrier are transient.

A quantum seam event may also keep or destroy coherence between
configurations that move the same way. Require of a one-seam channel seam
locality, the exact classical law, local number-phase covariance
(commutation with \(\exp(i\sum_pt_pN_p)\) for every real \(t\)) and
endpoint-swap covariance; both covariances are declared premises. Every
admissible channel then has the form
\[
\Phi(|x\rangle\langle x'|)=\sum_jG_j\bigl(\kappa(x),\kappa(x')\bigr)\,
|x+jD\rangle\langle x'+jD|,
\]
where \(\kappa(x)=(x_a,x_b)\) is the seam key, \(D\) moves one load from
\(a\) to \(b\), and each \(G_j\) is positive semidefinite with diagonal equal
to the classical probability of current \(j\). On the total-two carrier these
laws form one convex family, classified exactly by five coherence
multipliers \(k,g,h,m,n\), with \(|k|\le1\), \(|g|,|h|,|n|\le1/\sqrt2\),
\(h=g\) when \(k=1\), and \(m\) real with \(|m|\le1/2\)~\cite{ophmodel}. The
executed law C records coin and current (\(k=1\), \(g=1/2\)); the comparison
law E also records the seam parity (\(k=1\), \(g=0\)) and is idempotent.
Forgetting a label afterwards does not undo its dephasing, so C is not a
classical coarse-graining of E. Under a common definite-current record
interface all members share the classical current laws, and one coherent
interference experiment reads one coherence multiplier directly.

\begin{proposition}[The uniform repair mean selects the rank-three Gram band]
\label{prop:native-rank-three-band}
In a balanced binary sector under independent unit-rate seam attempts, the
native rule acts on centered loads with mean generator \(-L/2\), where \(L=5I-A\) is the
Laplacian of the thirty internal seams
(Theorem~\ref{thm:native-stationary-history}). The positive eigenvalues of
\(L\) are \(5-\sqrt5,6,5+\sqrt5\), with multiplicities \(3,5,3\). The slowest
band, eigenvalue \(5-\sqrt5\), is the range of the incidence Gram matrix
\(G\) of Theorem~\ref{thm:echosahedral-source-selector-microphysics}, and its
orthogonal projector is \(G/4\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write \(G=UU^{\mathsf T}\), whose rows \(u_p\) are the unit port directions of
the oriented frame; adjacent ports have \(u_p\cdot u_q=1/\sqrt5\). The
order-five rotation about \(u_p\) permutes the five neighbours of \(p\), so
their sum is parallel to \(u_p\) with component \(5/\sqrt5=\sqrt5\). Hence
\(AU=\sqrt5\,U\) and \(LU=(5-\sqrt5)U\). The range of \(G\) has dimension
three, the multiplicity of \(5-\sqrt5\), and \(G^2=4G\) makes \(G/4\) its
orthogonal projector.
\end{proof}

The band is a dimensionless response geometry on twelve ports. Native
records reconstruct it from currents and clock fluctuations, and all thirty
seams form its shortest shell~\cite{ophmodel}. It is distinct from the
spectral dimension two of the refining screen generator
(Section~\ref{subsec:native-transport-evidence}), and its rank three does not
derive three physical spatial dimensions.

\subsection{An alternative variational selector}

\begin{theorem}[Alternative variational icosahedral screen sieve]\label{thm:icosahedral-screen-sieve}
Assume the fixed-cutoff OPH screen branch is represented by a quantum-link
triangulation of \(S^2\), with finite Hilbert spaces on links, Gauss constraints
at vertices, boundary-gauge-invariant physical algebras, and a refinement-stable
quotient ensemble locally six-valent away from curvature defects. Let
\[
q_v=6-\deg(v).
\]
Assume integer charges, a feasible twelve-unit configuration, and an additive
cost with \(h(0)=0\), \(h(1)>0\), \(h(k)\ge h(1)|k|\), strictly for
\(|k|\ge2\). Let edge-center collars expose each unit defect as one central
port, with a fixed-point-free inverse-port involution giving six axes \(u_i\).
Assume the minimum-cost locus contains a six-axis configuration with
\(F_1=2I_3\) and \(F_2=(4/5)I_5\).  If the source selector first minimizes the
additive cost and, among those minimizers, maximizes
\[
 \det F_1\det F_2,\qquad
 F_1=\sum_i u_iu_i^{\mathsf T},\quad
 F_2=\sum_i|Q_i\rangle\langle Q_i|,\quad
 Q_i=u_iu_i^{\mathsf T}-I_3/3,
\]
then the screen has twelve unit ports on the regular icosahedral orbit. An
equal-weight additive invariant load \(X\) is read locally as \(X/12\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For a triangulated sphere with \(V\) vertices, \(E\) edges, and \(F\) faces,
Euler and triangular incidence give
\[
V-E+F=2,\qquad 3F=2E,\qquad E=3V-6.
\]
Therefore
\[
\sum_v q_v
=
\sum_v(6-\deg(v))
=6V-2E
=12.
\]
Thus \(\sum_vq_v=12\). The cost satisfies
\[
 \sum_vh(q_v)\ge h(1)\sum_v|q_v|\ge12h(1).
\]
Equality requires nonnegative unit charges, and feasibility supplies the
twelve-unit minimum.  The selector's first stage therefore returns exactly
twelve unit defects. The collar rule turns them into twelve central ports.
For six axes, \(\operatorname{tr}F_1=6\) and
\(\operatorname{tr}F_2=4\). Determinant AM--GM gives
\(\det F_1\le2^3\) and \(\det F_2\le(4/5)^5\), with equality exactly at
\(F_1=2I_3\) and \(F_2=(4/5)I_5\).  The assumed feasible isotropic frame
attains both bounds, so the selector's second stage does too. The six
equal-norm \(Q_i\) then form a
regular simplex in \(\operatorname{Sym}_0(3)\), so
\((u_i\cdot u_j)^2=1/5\) for \(i\ne j\). The associated Seidel matrix satisfies
\(S^2=5I_6\); after switching one vertex is isolated and the remaining
negative-edge graph is a five-cycle. This is the unique switching class and
gives the six axes of a regular icosahedron, whose proper
rotation group \(A_5\) is transitive on the twelve ports. Equal-weight
additivity gives the local read \(X/12\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Scope of the variational selector]
The identification \(X=\log(N/\pi)\) of the load is imported from the
companion capacity relation. The D-optimal vector/quadrupole functional is a
branch premise; a strictly completely monotonic pair cost is another
sufficient selector by universal optimality. Neither is needed on the
certified lineage of
Theorem~\ref{thm:echosahedral-source-selector-microphysics}. Here

\(\pi_uq_{u\to v}^{\rm eq}=\pi_vq_{v\to u}^{\rm eq}\).
\end{enumerate}
The rates belong to the reversible equilibrium layer. They are not rates of the accepted
strict-descent relation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Condition \textup{(ii)} telescopes around every cycle. Under \textup{(i)}, fix a root and define
\(K_x\) by summing \(a\) along any path from the root to \(x\); zero cycle affinity gives path
independence. Exponentiating \(K_v-K_u=a_{u\to v}\) gives detailed balance, and taking logarithms
of detailed balance gives \textup{(ii)}.
\end{proof}

The potential \(K\) is a classical Gibbs potential. On the commutative state
algebra its modular automorphism is trivial, so rate reconstruction creates
no nontrivial modular clock. The finite potential-theory core, with
condition \textup{(i)} read over closed walks, is machine-checked in the Lean
thermodynamics library (\texttt{KolmogorovCriterion})~\cite{ophrepo}; the
source-derived rates and the channel realization are outside that check.

\begin{theorem}[Exact modular coarse graining]
\label{thm:exact-modular-coarse-graining}
Let \(c:X_s\to X_r\) be a surjective coarse-graining map between finite sets and let
\(\mu_s(x)>0\).  The pushed-forward law and its modular potential are
\[
 \mu_r(y)=\sum_{c(x)=y}\mu_s(x),
 \qquad
 K_r(y)=-\log\!\left(\sum_{c(x)=y}e^{-K_s(x)}\right),
 \qquad K_s=-\log\mu_s .
\]
Thus modular potentials coarse-grain by a log-sum-exp rule, up to the common additive
normalization convention.  More generally, if
\(\mu_s(x)=Z_s^{-1}m_s(x)e^{-S_s(x)}\) and \(m_r(y)>0\) is the declared coarse reference
weight, then
\[
 S_r^{\rm eff}(y)
 =-\log\!\left(\sum_{c(x)=y}m_s(x)e^{-S_s(x)}\right)+\log m_r(y)
\]
gives the exact pushed-forward law.  For a finite quantum channel \(\Phi_{sr}\), the corresponding
statement is \(\rho_r=\Phi_{sr}(\rho_s)\) and \(K_r=-\log\rho_r\) whenever the output is faithful.
An arithmetic average of \(K_s\) is not the coarse modular potential in general.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Both classical formulas follow by substituting \(\mu_s=e^{-K_s}\) or
\(\mu_s=Z_s^{-1}m_se^{-S_s}\) into the definition of the pushforward.  The quantum formula is
the definition of the modular Hamiltonian of the pushed-forward faithful state.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A faithful equilibrium law does not select its generator]
\label{prop:generator-underdetermination}
Let \(G=(X,E)\) be a connected finite graph and let \(\mu_x>0\).  For every collection of
positive symmetric edge conductances \(c_{xy}=c_{yx}\),
\[
 q_{x\to y}=\frac{c_{xy}}{\mu_x}
\]
defines a continuous-time Markov generator that is reversible with stationary law \(\mu\).
Distinct conductance families give distinct generators with the same state.  A geometry,
MaxEnt state, or repair normal form therefore selects no equilibrium dynamics unless an additional
rate or conductance rule is supplied.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The identity \(\mu_xq_{x\to y}=c_{xy}=\mu_yq_{y\to x}\) is detailed balance.  The diagonal
entries are fixed by row-sum zero.  Varying any conductance varies the generator while preserving
the same stationary law.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Weighted graph Hodge split and entropy production]
\label{thm:weighted-hodge-entropy}
Choose an orientation of a connected finite graph, positive vertex and edge weights, and let
\(D\) be the vertex-to-edge coboundary.  Every real edge one-cochain has the unique orthogonal
decomposition
\[
 a=DK+h,
 \qquad D^{*}h=0,
\]
with \(K\) unique up to an additive constant.  For an irreducible stationary Markov law \(\pi\)
whose transition graph is \(E\), assume the rate support is bidirectional:
\(q_{x\to y}>0\) and \(q_{y\to x}>0\) for every \(\{x,y\}\in E\).  Define on each chosen edge
\[
 J_{xy}=\pi_xq_{x\to y}-\pi_yq_{y\to x},
 \qquad
 F_{xy}=\log\frac{\pi_xq_{x\to y}}{\pi_yq_{y\to x}} .
\]
Then the steady entropy-production rate is
\[
 \sigma=\sum_{\{x,y\}\in E}J_{xy}F_{xy}\ge 0,
\]
and it vanishes exactly when detailed balance holds on every edge.  The bare logarithmic rate
ratio omits the stationary weights and is therefore not, by itself, the thermodynamic force.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Finite-dimensional orthogonal projection gives the Hodge split, since
\((\operatorname{im}D)^{\perp}=\ker D^{*}\).  Each entropy-production summand has the form
\((A-B)\log(A/B)\ge0\) for positive \(A,B\).  Equality holds exactly when \(A=B\) edge by edge.
\end{proof}

receipt \(1-P/24\le\lambda_{\rm collar}\le1\). The coefficient is order one
on every reading; its exact value is reading dependent, and the presence
reading is the one this branch's receipt semantics supply.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[Uniform product-thickening branch]
\label{def:uniform-product-thickening-branch}
The branch consists of a product collar algebra
\(\mathcal A^{\rm thick}_{r,C}\cong\mathcal E_{r,C}\otimes\mathcal T_r(Y)\),
a product trace \(\tau^{\mathcal E}_{q,r,C}\otimes\tau^Y_r\), the reserve
pullback \(Z^{\rm thick}_{6,r,C}=Z_{6,r,C}\otimes1_Y\), the disintegration
\(\Pi^{\rm scal,thick}_{r,C}=\sum_y\Pi^{\rm scal}_{r,C}(y)\otimes p_y\) with
\(\Pi^{\rm scal}_{r,C}(y)\in\mathcal E_{r,C}\), and slice-wise scalar-reserve
unbiasedness,
\(\tau^{\mathcal E}_{q,r,C}(\Pi^{\rm scal}_{r,C}(y)Z_{6,r,C})/
\tau^{\mathcal E}_{q,r,C}(\Pi^{\rm scal}_{r,C}(y))=\tau^{\mathcal E}_{q,r,C}(Z_{6,r,C})=P/24\).
The last clause is essential: product algebra and trace do not force the
scalar-active sampler to be reserve-unbiased.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exact uniform product-thickening coefficient]
\label{thm:exact-uniform-product-thickening-coefficient}
On the uniform product-thickening branch, under the presence reading,
\(\lambda_{\rm collar}=1-P/24=0.9320428293392126\ldots\) at \(P_C\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Unbiasedness gives \(\epsilon_{\mathbb Z_6}(y)=P/24\) on every scalar-active
slice, and \(w\) is normalized.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Exact value licensing]
\label{rem:exact-value-gate}
\label{sec:local-scalar-receipt-ledger}
The exact value \(1-P/24\) is licensed by the scalar-weighted \(P/24\)
receipt under the presence reading. Without that receipt the theorem-grade
coefficient is \(1-\int dy\,w(y)\epsilon_{\mathbb Z_6}(y)\), and under the
mean-count reading only the Markov band \(1-P/24\le\lambda_{\rm collar}\le1\)
is available. No survival coefficient uses \(24/P\).
\end{remark}
