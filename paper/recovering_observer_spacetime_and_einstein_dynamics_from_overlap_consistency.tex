\begin{abstract}
Observer-Patch Holography starts from finite patches that record, read shared
boundaries, and repair disagreement. This paper derives its spacetime and
gravity chain. On a declared twelve-port branch, the normalized
repair-response metric has an exact rank-three quotient. The antipodal load
readout reaches every signed six-coordinate record, while conservative seam
currents span its even-sum submodule. Both are dense in the same abstract
continuous Euclidean completion under the response metric. Physical position,
scale, and overlap gluing enter only as premises. A separate incidence limit gives a
round celestial two-sphere; modular covariance gives Lorentz kinematics and
the three-dimensional observer-frame space. Record germs produce a
four-dimensional Lorentzian event manifold under named
population, separation, response-chart, cone, and causal conditions. A
machine-checked finite soldering contract isolates the algebraic part: an
actual coincidence setoid and invariant readback give a unique descended
readback. Separately, one affine Lorentz cocycle and a supplied chart-coordinate
family satisfying its overlap law induce displacement, celestial, frame, and
local-rest-space covariance. The formal contract does not identify that chart
family with the quotient-descended readback. It retains the source, population,
certified-separation, open-chart, physical-cone, refinement, causal, and clock receipts.
A target-clean source supplies an exact finite causal complex, atlas, seam
transport, and local operator domain. It does not construct the continuum
manifold.
Positive null translations supply directional charges, and finite null
tomography reconstructs a tensor when its linearity relations hold.  The local,
covariant, conserved stress reading additionally requires same-source
null-stress matching, locality/covariance, and a weak Ward balance.  A declared
fixed-cap generalized-entropy stationarity interface, together with the proved
finite MaxEnt identities, gives the Einstein equation only under the remaining
small-ball, common-scaling, universal-coupling, vacuum-reference, and independent
scale-readout premises. Fixed-screen identities determine the
capacity-transfer law and de Sitter shock sign. The physical reading requires
one refinement tower carrying every continuum condition.
\end{abstract}
