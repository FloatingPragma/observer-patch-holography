\begin{abstract}
Observer-Patch Holography starts with finite patches that keep records, read
their shared boundaries, and repair disagreement. This paper gives the full
spacetime and gravity chain. A receipt-selected incidence limit produces the
round celestial two-sphere and its cap geometry. Modular covariance gives
Lorentz kinematics and the three-dimensional space of observer frames.
Record germs produce a four-dimensional Lorentzian event manifold only under
the stated population, separation, response-chart, cone, and causal receipts.
A target-clean finite source inhabits an exact causal, atlas, seam-transport,
and local operator domain. One local chart has Euclidean inertia and the cone
margin is not positive, so this finite result does not construct the continuum
event manifold.
Positive null translations and modular charges reconstruct a local conserved
stress tensor. A fixed-cap generalized-entropy identity then gives the
Einstein equation under one common scaling family, universal coupling, a
vacuum reference, and independent scale readouts. The finite incidence and
algebraic identities are source-derived; construction of a physical tower
carrying every continuum receipt is work in progress.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Derivation map}

The complete implication chain is
\[
\begin{split}
&\text{finite observer records and repaired quotient normal forms}\\
&\quad\Longrightarrow\text{receipt-selected round }S^2\text{ and cap normals}\\
&\quad\Longrightarrow\text{geometric modular flow, Lorentz action, and }H^3
   \text{ observer frames}\\
&\quad\Longrightarrow\text{conditional four-dimensional event manifold}\\
&\quad\Longrightarrow\text{positive translations and local conserved }
   \langle T_{ab}\rangle\\
&\quad\Longrightarrow
G_{ab}+\Lambda g_{ab}=8\pi G\langle T_{ab}\rangle .
\end{split}
\]
Each arrow below states its own finite, analytic, scaling, or physical
antecedents. The celestial \(S^2\) and the frame hyperboloid do not by
themselves populate a spacetime. The event-manifold and Einstein conclusions
therefore remain conditional on their named receipts.

The finite source realization sits below the event-manifold arrow. It supplies
one exact causal complex, a closed observer-neighborhood cover with affine
wiring transitions, a seam-sign layer, and a local operator domain. An
isolated rerun of the same producers reproduces canonical receipt content. The
finite object lacks the positive cone margin,
uniform local Lorentz signature, open-chart topology, and continuum
identifications required by that arrow.
