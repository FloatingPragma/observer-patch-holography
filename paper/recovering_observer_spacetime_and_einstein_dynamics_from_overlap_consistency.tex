\begin{abstract}
This paper develops two layers of Observer Patch Holography: observer
clocks, causal order and spatial geometry read from the same repair process,
and Einstein dynamics as the coarse-scale response of that process. Normalized
repair response on the twelve-port carrier gives a rank-three metric quotient
and, with an adjoined scalar line, a $1+3$ Lorentz carrier. Conservative
Fibonacci record populations with complete reads inside a shrinking radius and
a declared model clock have causal orders and normalized counts converging to
flat $1+3$ spacetime; the ordering fraction of interior causal diamonds tends to
$1/10$, and interval counts reconstruct proper-time ratios. Boost invariance
selects the light cone of the declared quadratic form among closed convex
pointed cones, and finite null tomography reconstructs a symmetric tensor from
null projections. Null balance on nine design directions, with Ward and Bianchi
identities, gives the Einstein equation up to a cosmological constant, and
the small-ball entanglement-equilibrium calculation supplies the required
tensor response under six
realization premises. The proved centered Weyl vacuum supplies a conserved
Lorentz stress tensor, its geometric double-cone modular generator and a
finite vacuum-relative first law. For the exact free lattice source, compact
state perturbations and finite quadratic stress readouts converge to the same
leading stress-response matrix with explicit error bounds, closing a specified
free-sector matter-response premise. Its join with an area law, entropy
stationarity and responsive geometry on one refinement tower remains to be
constructed; the static radial geometry of the executable construction fails
null balance for its own scalar excitation.
\end{abstract}

\section*{This paper's hypothesis and place in the tower}

This paper sits at layers 4 and 6 of the tower. Its hypothesis is that the

\begin{theorem}[Conditional cap modular normalization]
\label{thm:conditional-cap-modular}
Let one cofinal refinement tower have a common support-visible geometric

refinement, and modular action is readable as a map of cap supports.

automorphism statement on the limit algebra; a literal
$K_C=2\pi B_C+Z_C$ representation requires an additional type-I
realization, with $Z_C$ central.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof outline]
Controlled Markov replacement confines the finite modular defect to the
collar, with the faithful-collar comparison controlling its matrix elements
on fixed local observables. Projective compatibility and mixed-GNS
convergence define one limiting cap pair independently of a chosen
comparison sequence. Support readability turns its modular action into a
cap-support flow. Oriented cross-ratio rigidity identifies this flow with
the cap-preserving conformal one up to parameter scale; the independent
KMS comparison fixes that scale to $2\pi$. None of these steps identifies
the full algebra with a type-I factor or derives the certificate from the
unchanged native repair law.
\end{proof}

\section{Null balance and the Einstein tensor}
\label{sec:einstein-tensor}

The tensor theorem isolates the precise content of the gravitational

\begin{theorem}[Null tomography]
\leavevmode\par\raggedright
Let $V$ be four-dimensional with Lorentz form
$\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$. A symmetric form $X$ vanishes on every
null square exactly when $X=\phi\eta$. Nine null directions suffice to
recover $X$ modulo this line: the six $(1,\pm e_i)$ and

$\|x\|_\infty\leq(2+\sqrt3)\|q\|_\infty$ in its registered coordinates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On the unit sphere, $X(1,n;1,n)=X_{00}+2X_{0i}n_i+X_{ij}n_in_j$.
Opposite directions remove the mixed temporal entries. The six axis
values fix the spatial diagonal modulo the metric, and the three
remaining sign choices fix the spatial off-diagonal entries. Thus the
kernel consists exactly of the metric line. Solving this finite system
gives the stated decoder bound; its determinant and identities are also
checked in the finite theorem library \cite{ophrepo}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Einstein equation from a common null balance]
Let $(M,g)$ be a connected time-oriented $C^3$ Lorentzian four-manifold and
$T$ a symmetric $C^1$ tensor with $\nabla^aT_{ab}=0$. Fix a single
$\kappa=8\pi G_N>0$. If
\[
 G_{ab}k^ak^b=\kappa T_{ab}k^ak^b
\]
for every null $k$ at every point, then there is a constant $\Lambda$ such
that $G_{ab}+\Lambda g_{ab}=\kappa T_{ab}$.
Balance on the nine directions in any orthonormal frame suffices.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Null tomography gives $G-\kappa T=\phi g$ pointwise. The contracted Bianchi
identity, the independently supplied Ward identity and metric compatibility
give $\nabla\phi=0$. Connectedness makes $\phi$ constant; set
$\Lambda=-\phi$. Its value requires one reference condition.
\end{proof}

\end{proof}

The $255$ matrices $E_{ii}-E_{15,15}$, $E_{ij}+E_{ji}$ and $i(E_{ij}-E_{ji})$
each have operator norm one, trace norm two and squared Hilbert--Schmidt norm
two, and their Gram determinant is $16\cdot2^{240}$. They span every trace-zero
Hermitian regional variation, including number-changing and noncommuting ones,
and every bound holds on the parity-preserving subspace of dimension $127$ and
the number-preserving subspace of dimension $69$. For one of these directions
the symmetric estimator has error below $4\times10^{-9}+(1600/3)h^2$ on
$0<h\leq1/40$. The abort block is held fixed and contributes zero to entropy
differences. The variations are field-marginal lifts at fixed success
probability, without a record-resolved lift or an executed native intervention,
and the plaquette is a source site region rather than a geometric diamond, so
these identities supply neither responsive area nor fixed-proper-volume
stationarity.

\subsection{A complete native action of the regional modular polynomial}
\label{sec:native-regional-modular}

The same prepared region admits an executed modular clock. In the convention
$\alpha_s(A)=\sigma^{is}A\sigma^{-is}$ the state unitary is $W_s=e^{isK}$. The
polynomial has an exact real Pauli decomposition $P_8=\sum_jb_jP_j$ with $39$
nonzero terms, its scalar identity term among them, and $B=\sum_j|b_j|<0.051$.
It commutes with regional number and parity. For $h=s/n$, let $S(h)$ execute
each factor $e^{ihb_jP_j/2}$ in listed order and then in reverse order, every
factor being the source compiler's literal word of basis changes, parity
controls, one phase rotation and uncomputation.

\begin{proposition}[Native regional modular actuator]
\label{prop:native-regional-modular}
For real $s$ and integer $n\geq1$,
\[
 \|e^{isP_8}-e^{-isa}W_s\|\leq|s|E,\qquad
 \|S(s/n)^n-e^{isP_8}\|\leq\frac{B^3|s|^3}{3n^2}.
\]
At $s=1/2$ and $n=32$ the native word has $37{,}568$ pulses. It defines a
complete map with successful output $W$ and first-failure outputs $F_f$,
$W^*W+\sum_fF_f^*F_f=I$. Up to the scalar phase, which cancels from every
channel, its half-diamond distance from conjugation by $W_{1/2}$ is below
$6.4\times10^{-9}$ on all inputs, including the prepared columns, the four
exterior field bits and an arbitrary untouched reference.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Anti-Hermitian Duhamel and $\|K-P_8-aI\|\leq E$ give the first bound. The
palindrome obeys $S(-h)=S(h)^{-1}$ and $S'(0)=iP_8$, hence $S''(0)=S'(0)^2$, so
$S(h)$ and $e^{ihP_8}$ agree through second order. All factors are unitary, and
the multinomial rule bounds both third derivatives by $B^3$. Taylor's remainder
gives $B^3|h|^3/3$ per step and unitary telescoping gives the second bound. For
each pulse with emitted generator $G_e$ and ideal proper-code generator $T_e$,
$\|e^{G_e}-e^{T_e}\|\leq\|G_e-T_e\|$, with the outward enclosure taken over all
six processor inputs. Isometric telescoping bounds the literal word, with every
failure output retained, by the sum $d_{\rm lit}$ of those errors. The total
$d_{\rm lit}+B^3|s|^3/(3n^2)+|s|E$ has exact rational value near
$6.385\times10^{-9}$: $5.34\times10^{-12}$ for the literal word,
$5.388\times10^{-9}$ for the product and $9.92\times10^{-10}$ for the bridge to
the prepared logarithm.
\end{proof}

All sixteen regional input columns are executed. Continuing the preparation is
exact channel composition: each unnormalized success column becomes

record bits, every earlier abort output and all first-failure labels retained
and no source reset in between. The target $W_s$ preserves $\sigma$, so the
same bound controls regional stationarity. On the full plaquette a regional
unitary can change correlations and need not preserve $\Sigma$, so it does not
intertwine the regional modular group with the global one, in accord with

services are the admitted simulator specification and $s$ is dimensionless, so

\subsection{Finite modular covariance and compressed responses}
\label{sec:finite-modular-covariance}

Geometric modular covariance requires a comparison beyond the existence
of a finite modular clock. The following finite identity states a necessary
boundary of that comparison, independently of the repair scheduler.

\begin{proposition}[Finite modular dilation and its compression boundary]
\label{prop:finite-modular-dilation}
Let $G,P$ be Hermitian matrices on a finite Hilbert space, let
$U(s)=e^{isG}$, and let $\kappa\in\mathbb R$. Then
\[
 \|i[G,P]-\kappa P\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 =\|[G,P]\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2+\kappa^2\|P\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2,
 \qquad
 \|U(s)PU(s)^*-e^{\kappa s}P\|
 \geq |e^{\kappa s}-1|\,\|P\|.
\]
For an orthogonal projection $Q$, write $G_Q=QGQ$, $P_Q=QPQ$ and
$B_Q=i(QG(1-Q)PQ-QP(1-Q)GQ)$. Then
\[
 Q\,i[G,P]Q=i[G_Q,P_Q]+B_Q,
 \qquad
 \|Q(i[G,P]-\kappa P)Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}+\|B_Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}
 \geq |\kappa|\,\|P_Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Cyclicity gives $\operatorname{Tr}(P\,i[G,P])=0$, so the cross term
in the squared Hilbert--Schmidt norm vanishes. Unitary invariance of
the operator norm and the reverse triangle inequality give the
finite-parameter bound. Inserting $Q+(1-Q)$ into each product gives
the compressed identity. Apply the first identity to the compressed
matrices, then the triangle inequality, for the last bound.
\end{proof}

For the native faithful reference with per-configuration core weights
$(2/225,1/36,2/45)$, take $G=\log\sigma$; thus $s$ is the dimensionless
modular parameter. Choose configurations $a,b$ with core occupation zero
and two, respectively. On their two-state corner declare
\[
 P=\begin{pmatrix}1&i/2\\-i/2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 Q=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|,\qquad
 |\psi\rangle=(|a\rangle+|b\rangle)/\sqrt2,
\]
and extend $P$ by zero. It is positive, with nonzero eigenvalues
$1/2,3/2$. For $\kappa=\log(5)/2$, the projected first derivative
is exactly $\kappa P_Q$, supplied entirely by $B_Q$; the internal
compressed commutator is zero. Nevertheless
\[
 \langle\psi|U(s)PU(s)^*|\psi\rangle
 =1+\tfrac12\sin(s\log5)\ne e^{s\log(5)/2}
\]
in general. At $s=1/\log5$ the two values are approximately $1.420735$
and $1.648721$. The declared probe is not identified with physical null
momentum.

Consequently a fixed finite Hilbert algebra cannot supply nonzero
global modular dilation. A spectral projection of $G$ has $B_Q=0$ and
retains the same obstruction. Restricted continuum comparisons must
specify states, observable domains and finite parameter windows, retain
the compression boundary, and control the actual responses on one
refining family. Unbounded continuum generators are not excluded by
these bounded-operator statements.
