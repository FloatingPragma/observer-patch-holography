\begin{abstract}
This paper develops two layers of Observer Patch Holography: observer
clocks, causal order and spatial geometry read from the same repair process,
and Einstein dynamics as the coarse-scale response of that process. Normalized
repair response on the twelve-port carrier gives a rank-three metric quotient
and, with an adjoined scalar line, a $1+3$ Lorentz carrier. Conservative
Fibonacci record populations with complete reads inside a shrinking radius and
a declared model clock have causal orders and normalized counts converging to
flat $1+3$ spacetime; the ordering fraction of interior causal diamonds tends to
$1/10$, and interval counts reconstruct proper-time ratios. Boost invariance
selects the light cone of the declared quadratic form among closed convex
pointed cones, and finite null tomography reconstructs a symmetric tensor from
null projections. Null balance on nine design directions, with Ward and Bianchi
identities, gives the Einstein equation up to a cosmological constant, and
Jacobson's entanglement-equilibrium argument supplies null balance under six
realization premises. The proved centered Weyl vacuum supplies a conserved
Lorentz stress tensor, its geometric double-cone modular generator and a
finite vacuum-relative first law. For the exact free lattice source, compact
state perturbations and finite quadratic stress readouts converge to the same
leading stress-response matrix with explicit error bounds, closing a specified
free-sector matter-response premise. Its join with an area law, entropy
stationarity and responsive geometry on one refinement tower remains to be
constructed; the static radial geometry of the executable construction fails
null balance for its own scalar excitation.
\end{abstract}

\section*{This paper's hypothesis and place in the tower}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{This paper's hypothesis and place in the tower}

This paper sits at layers 4 and 6 of the tower. Its hypothesis is that the
clocks, causal order and spatial geometry an observer reads are produced by the
same native repair process that runs the executable observer~\cite{ophmodel},
and that Einstein dynamics is the coarse-scale response of that process to its
own matter.

The paper proves finite and analytic theorems under displayed premises. The
rank-three source Gram quotient and its $1+3$ Lorentz carrier are exact. On
supplied conservative Fibonacci populations with a complete local read law and
model clock, the causal order and counts converge to flat $1+3$ spacetime and a
count clock reads proper-time ratios. Cap modular covariance gives Lorentz
kinematics on the support-visible geometric subnet. The gravitational theorem
is an implication. Null balance on nine design directions, together with Ward
and Bianchi identities, gives $G_{ab}+\Lambda g_{ab}=8\pi G\langle T_{ab}\rangle$
(Figure~\ref{fig:einstein-chain}); the null-balance step follows from the
following realization premises on one refinement tower:
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
\item[(G1)] a Lorentzian event and geometry domain with the required
  locality, differentiability, causal and count/volume limits;
\item[(G2)] geometrically normalized cap modular flow whose directional charges
  define the same matter stress tensor used in propagation and interactions;
\item[(G3)] its Ward identity, independent of the Einstein equation;
\item[(G4)] the edge-center entropy split and area normalization, the modular
  first law, and generalized-entropy stationarity at fixed proper volume;
\item[(G5)] controlled small-ball remainders and sufficient observer and null
  directions;
\item[(G6)] universal coupling, a common vacuum reference and one positive
  Newton constant.
\end{enumerate}

Realizing (G1)--(G6) on one refinement tower of the executable model is the
open join of this paper. The following facts bound it. The static radial product, the
one geometry tested on the executable construction, has vanishing radial null
Einstein contractions while an admitted scalar excitation has positive relative
null stress, so it cannot balance both; a responsive geometry is required.
Stationary Markov or record entropy is not the fixed-volume
generalized-entropy variation that (G4) requires
(Section~\ref{sec:continuing-record-entropy}). The native screen generator $A$
has spectral dimension two, and the $1+3$ scalar description uses a declared
radial extension. The Fibonacci population, read law and model clock of the
causal limit are premises until the native process produces them.

A proof that no responsive geometry on the native process balances the null
stress of its own admitted excitations, or that its cap modular flow or record
entropy cannot meet (G2) or (G4) on any refinement tower, would refute this
gravitational branch. A refinement tower on which every component premise
holds while the composed Einstein relation fails would contradict the stated
implication.

\subsection{Entropy stationarity from a continuing native record}
\label{sec:continuing-record-entropy}

The native population interface admits an entropy calculation after
record-adaptive repairs, without assuming an unread stationary continuation.
Fix the binary total-two carrier and a faithful reference with weight $w_q$
per configuration of initial core charge $q=n_0+n_1$. The core pair
observable is $c=n_0n_1$. Let $b$ be a complete native tape with bounded
stopping length $T_b$, and let $d_b$ count its compatible endpoints.
Native branch inversion identifies each endpoint with an initial state.
Assume the tape resolves $q$, and that any retained flag has probability
$\beta>0$ independent of the initial state.

\begin{proposition}[Counted native-record entropy envelope]
For an initial population $p$, let $p_B$ be its tape law conditional on that
flag. Define
\[
 F(p)=H(p_B)+\mathbb E_p\log d_B
       -\mathbb E_p T_B\log2-\log\beta.
\]
Writing $\sigma$ for the reference population, one has
\[
 F(p)-S(\sigma)
 =h_1\bigl(\langle q\rangle_p-\langle q\rangle_\sigma\bigr)
  +h_2\bigl(\langle c\rangle_p-\langle c\rangle_\sigma\bigr)
  -D(p_B\Vert\sigma_B),
\]
where $h_1=\log(w_0/w_1)$ and $h_2=\log(w_1^2/(w_0w_2))$.
Hence the fixed-moment first variation vanishes at $\sigma$, and its
Hessian is the negative semidefinite Fisher form of the tape response.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Injectivity and the retained fair coins give
$\sigma_B(b)=d_b w_{q(b)}2^{-T_b}/\beta$.
Substituting this law in $D(p_B\Vert\sigma_B)$ leaves
$-\sum_b p_B(b)\log w_{q(b)}$.
State-independent flag probability makes its initial-state marginal $p$.
Finally $-\log w_q=-\log w_0+h_1q+h_2c$.
The second variation of relative entropy is the Fisher form; directions
invisible to this record map can remain flat.
\end{proof}

The charge-resolution hypothesis has a native readout. For E, the contrasts on
seams $(0,1)$ and $(0,5)$ determine the three binary occupations at the start
of that census: a common all-one offset is impossible at total load two.
Retained prefix currents then recover the initial core, including after
crossing repairs. No success conditioning is needed. For C, waiting for both
coin values on each census seam gives a bounded service with state-independent
success; all timeouts stay in the complete instrument. Unresolved timeout
fibres have counted-entropy excess bounded by
$(1-\beta)\log(w_{\max}/w_{\min})$. The expected coin cost must vary with $p$
when stopping depends on records. Cap completions are marginals of the same
conditional endpoint measure, so their shared predictions agree without
independently resetting the source.

This is a population-record entropy theorem. It identifies neither the
completion count nor the coin-cost subtraction with gravitational area, nor
the core moments with physical volume or stress. Its stationarity is a
fixed-moment record stationarity, not the fixed-proper-volume
generalized-entropy variation of (G4); joining the two requires an area,
volume and stress attachment on the same process.

\subsection{Finite modular covariance and compressed responses}
\label{sec:finite-modular-covariance}

Geometric modular covariance requires a comparison beyond the existence
of a finite modular clock. The following finite identity states a necessary
boundary of that comparison, independently of the repair scheduler.

\begin{proposition}[Finite modular dilation and its compression boundary]
\label{prop:finite-modular-dilation}
Let $G,P$ be Hermitian matrices on a finite Hilbert space, let
$U(s)=e^{isG}$, and let $\kappa\in\mathbb R$. Then
\[
 \|i[G,P]-\kappa P\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 =\|[G,P]\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2+\kappa^2\|P\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2,
 \qquad
 \|U(s)PU(s)^*-e^{\kappa s}P\|
 \geq |e^{\kappa s}-1|\,\|P\|.
\]
For an orthogonal projection $Q$, write $G_Q=QGQ$, $P_Q=QPQ$ and
$B_Q=i(QG(1-Q)PQ-QP(1-Q)GQ)$. Then
\[
 Q\,i[G,P]Q=i[G_Q,P_Q]+B_Q,
 \qquad
 \|Q(i[G,P]-\kappa P)Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}+\|B_Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}
 \geq |\kappa|\,\|P_Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Cyclicity gives $\operatorname{Tr}(P\,i[G,P])=0$, so the cross term
in the squared Hilbert--Schmidt norm vanishes. Unitary invariance of
the operator norm and the reverse triangle inequality give the
finite-parameter bound. Inserting $Q+(1-Q)$ into each product gives
the compressed identity. Apply the first identity to the compressed
matrices, then the triangle inequality, for the last bound.
\end{proof}

For the native faithful reference with per-configuration core weights
$(2/225,1/36,2/45)$, take $G=\log\sigma$; thus $s$ is the dimensionless
modular parameter. Choose configurations $a,b$ with core occupation zero
and two, respectively. On their two-state corner declare
\[
 P=\begin{pmatrix}1&i/2\\-i/2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 Q=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|,\qquad
 |\psi\rangle=(|a\rangle+|b\rangle)/\sqrt2,
\]
and extend $P$ by zero. It is positive, with nonzero eigenvalues
$1/2,3/2$. For $\kappa=\log(5)/2$, the projected first derivative
is exactly $\kappa P_Q$, supplied entirely by $B_Q$; the internal
compressed commutator is zero. Nevertheless
\[
 \langle\psi|U(s)PU(s)^*|\psi\rangle
 =1+\tfrac12\sin(s\log5)\ne e^{s\log(5)/2}
\]
in general. At $s=1/\log5$ the two values are approximately $1.420735$
and $1.648721$. The declared probe is not identified with physical null
momentum.

Consequently a fixed finite Hilbert algebra cannot supply nonzero
global modular dilation. A spectral projection of $G$ has $B_Q=0$ and
retains the same obstruction. Restricted continuum comparisons must
specify states, observable domains and finite parameter windows, retain
the compression boundary, and control the actual responses on one
refining family. Unbounded continuum generators are not excluded by
these bounded-operator statements.
