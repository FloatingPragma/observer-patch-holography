Observer-Patch Holography starts with finite patches that keep records, read
their shared boundaries, and repair disagreement. This paper gives the full
spacetime and gravity chain. On a declared twelve-port branch, the normalized
repair-response metric has an exact rank-three quotient, and the cumulative

separate receipt-selected incidence limit produces the round celestial
two-sphere and its cap geometry. Modular covariance gives Lorentz kinematics
and the three-dimensional space of observer frames.
Record germs produce a four-dimensional Lorentzian event manifold only under
the stated population, separation, response-chart, cone, and causal receipts.
A target-clean finite source inhabits an exact causal, atlas, seam-transport,
and local operator domain. One local chart has Euclidean inertia and the cone
margin is not positive, so this finite result does not construct the continuum
event manifold.
Positive null translations and modular charges reconstruct a local conserved
stress tensor. A fixed-cap generalized-entropy identity then gives the
Einstein equation under one common scaling family, universal coupling, a
vacuum reference, and independent scale readouts. Exact fixed-screen
identities supply the capacity-transfer law
\(\Delta S_{\mathrm{gen}}^{\max}=\log(1-f)\), the pure de Sitter shock
identity \(\mu^{2}=d-2\), and the definite shock-sign Hessian split. The
finite incidence and algebraic identities are source-derived. A physical
tower carrying every continuum receipt is a premise of the physical reading.
\end{abstract}
