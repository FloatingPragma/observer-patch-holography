\begin{abstract}
Observer Patch Holography proposes that particles are reproducible features
of the records of a specified observer, repair and memory process. We
construct a finite link between that proposal's gauge and particle sectors:
the same localized fermion modes carry the twelve native current operators
and a pixel-dependent charged-lepton mass matrix. A complete memory process
realizes the matrix with a controlled weak-step limit. Its feedback into a
declared electromagnetic kernel has certified conditional fixed points;
a minimal field census with one specified scale law is instead inconsistent.
A compact opposite-winding texture has finite excess-energy bounds and four
isolated modes with controlled infinite-volume errors. Its same-band quartic
extension gives exact interacting spectral readouts; local guarded repair
preserves both charges and admits controlled transport of the band.
The compact splitting changes the interacting ground sector and its poles.
The finite gauge
theorems constrain the internal representation; they do not identify physical
particles or fix all couplings. The resulting construction is a precise
conditional particle model, without a complete observed spectrum, an
interacting chiral continuum, or a common Einstein limit.
\end{abstract}

\section{One source process and its particle interpretation}
\label{sec:particle-hypothesis-place}

Observer Patch Holography (OPH) proposes a self-referential complete history:
observers infer the specification of their world from shared records and
realize it through operations within that same history. The executable
version specifies a carrier Hilbert space, accessible algebras, repair
instruments, a scheduler, memory, feedback and preparation services

\[

where $\Phi_{e,r}$ is the operation on seam $e$ with retained outcome $r$,

joint and conditional laws of actual preparations, interventions and detector

\begin{hypothesis}[Matter as localized defects]
\label{hyp:defect}
Stable massive matter admits a localized excitation of a continuing source
reference, with an invariant preserved by the admitted dynamics. Its creation,
transport, interactions and detection are operations of the same source.
Its total excess energy and propagation use one energy and clock dictionary.
Unstable species may be metastable or internal excitations; massless radiation
need not carry a defect invariant.
\end{hypothesis}

This hypothesis does not require a distinct conserved topological charge for
every mass eigenstate. Flavor mixing and decay can connect internal states of
a defect family. A protected label cannot change at an admitted vertex without
another field or record carrying the compensating charge. A proposed complete
particle catalogue must account for those vertices as well as masses.

\subsection{Complete response and the compact Lie algebra}

The carrier is the oriented icosahedral boundary, with twelve ports, thirty
seams and twenty faces. Its real port module under the proper rotation group

\[

\]
The following is the finite input to the particle construction. Assume a
faithful real-linear response map
$D:V_{12}\to\mathfrak u(\mathcal K)$ whose image is the complete connected
response algebra and is closed under commutators. Assume proper carrier
rotations act on that image through the same connected inner response group,
with operationally silent centralizer factors allowed. Then the compact
classification gives
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak g\simeq\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)
                         \oplus\mathfrak{su}(3).
 \label{eq:finite-gauge-type}
\end{equation}

The short dimension argument explains the strength of the assumptions.
A compact Lie algebra is the sum of its centre and its semisimple part.
Inner rotations fix the centre, so the one-dimensional invariant subspace
bounds its dimension by one. A twelve-dimensional centre-free compact algebra
would be $\mathfrak{su}(2)^4$ under the small-dimension classification.
Inner $A_5$ actions preserve each factor; the fixed subspace in each
three-dimensional factor has dimension zero or three. Their sum cannot have
fixed dimension one. The centre therefore has dimension one, leaving the
unique semisimple dimension split $11=3+8$ \cite{ophGauge}.

Twelve ports alone do not imply~\eqref{eq:finite-gauge-type}. Dropping
completeness, bracket closure, faithfulness or the inner-action condition
changes the problem. In particular, a diagonal port response can be abelian,
and allowing further connected directions can produce a larger Lie algebra.
The finite result also does not identify carrier rechartings with physical
spatial rotations of a relativistic scattering theory.

 Q_p=\tfrac12v_pv_p^T-\tfrac16\|v_p\|^2 I_3+\tfrac1{12}I_3.
\]
The twelve matrices have rank twelve as response directions; their brackets

Coherent control, encoding and record-assisted recovery belong to this

\subsection{Particle observables and comparison conventions}

\[

A visible eigenvalue requires nonzero residue in the actual detector channel.
The physical dictionary must transport $O$, the state, the Hamiltonian,
current charges and the clock together. Localized wavefunctions have position

transform of a pinned mode is not a moving-defect dispersion experiment.

In a justified relativistic regime a stable massive excitation has a common
rest-energy and propagation relation. Setting the numerical speed of light
fixes units only. The same source must establish the relative normalization

separate statements. Color confinement prevents reading a free color-mode

Comparison uses mass ratios in one stated running or pole convention,
physical mixing observables, widths and interaction strengths. Running
charged masses and mixing data must share scale and scheme
\cite{antusch2025running,flag2024}. Quark and unstable-particle conventions
cannot be replaced by arbitrary ratios of finite-cavity levels.
Parameters chosen after exposure to these quantities carry that provenance;
independent tests use data or consequences not used in the choice.
The finite source spectra reported here have no certified running-to-pole,
confinement, or laboratory-clock map.

\subsection{Interaction, chiral continuum and responsive geometry}

The quartic result gives a genuine connected interaction on a fixed localized

anomaly sums are necessary representation checks; a chiral lattice measure

The same matter energy and stress must then enter the geometric response.

The finite Gibbs first law proved for the interacting localized Hamiltonian
is a useful common-operator identity. It does not supply a geometric area
term, the small-ball remainder, the Ward/Bianchi map or a universal Newton
normalization. Einstein dynamics cannot be inferred by joining unrelated
matter and entropy calculations.
