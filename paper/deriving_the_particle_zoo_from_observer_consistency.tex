 \operatorname{TV}(\hbox{complete records})
 \leq\min\left\{1,2\sum_{n\geq r}

\]
At $J=\kappa=1$, $K=48$, $K_0=0$, $T=1/2$, this is below $0.000248607$ uniformly
for the five charges. It controls the stated finite-time preparation and complete
observations, not an unbounded-flux ground state without a tail bound.

For the source Euclidean overlap projector $P(\theta)$ on a local admissible

\[
 V^\dagger(-\partial_\theta^2)V=-(\partial_\theta+A)^2+\Phi,\qquad
 A=V^\dagger V',\qquad
 \Phi=V'^\dagger(I-P)V'=V'^\dagger V'+A^2\succeq0.

 0\leq a_j-\lambda_j(H_\Lambda)

The differential scalar $\Phi$ is positive; the finite-grid difference from a polar-link
