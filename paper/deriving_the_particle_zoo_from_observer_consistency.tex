\begin{abstract}
In Observer Patch Holography a particle is a persistent object that observers
read on the records they agree on, with charges and statistics as properties
of those records. On a declared five-level code, a protected face-flux
register has an exact dictionary to Standard Model centre classes,
$k=6Y\bmod6$ with multiplicities $(4,6,6,4,6,6)$. Under declared
spin-doublet, Gauss-sector and antisymmetric-occupation premises exactly one
grading agrees with the $2\pi$ rotation and makes every code pattern a
fermion. A census of classes determines an aggregate exactly when its weights
are constant on classes; individual species need further labels. The
executable model of the theory supplies bound objects that persist in its
observers' agreed records. Under the seam interaction of the contrast-memory
law a pair of loads on one seam is read as a pair on some
seam with probability at least $1-40/(2\kappa-6)^2$ at every reading, for
every coupling $\kappa>3$ on every federation of complete cells; the pair is
read on other seams, the law without the agreement energy loses it, and a
face object persists as well. The main candidate law, Interval Echo, runs
this interaction at $\kappa=8$, adds clocks that observers carry, and
inherits these objects. Under the second candidate law, the scalar
channel, the excitation of a cage of four seams is kept for every interval
and read from counts by a local detector.
\end{abstract}

\section{The particle question}
\label{sec:particle-hypothesis-place}

Observer Patch Holography (OPH) proposes a self-referential complete history:
observers infer the specification of their world from shared records and
realize it through operations within that same history. The executable
formulation specifies a carrier, accessible algebras, repair
instruments, a scheduler, memory, feedback and preparation services
\cite{ophobservers,ophmodel}. These are permitted design choices. Operational
agreement concerns accepted shared data; state selection and endogenous
implementation of response transformations are additional stated principles,
recorded with the exact theorem premises in the common registry \cite{ophbasis}.

The executable model of the theory runs candidate repair and memory laws on
one fixed carrier, the spherical federation of twelve-port icosahedral cells
and its refinements \cite{ophmodel}. One cell is one observer, its twelve
ports are its interfaces, and larger observers are finite patches of cells.
The main candidate law, Interval Echo, takes as its elementary interaction
the seam interaction of the reference candidate, the contrast-memory law
A-contrast-memory-spherical-v1, at coupling $\kappa=8$, and adds a clock that
a finite observer patch carries; the scalar channel is the second candidate
law. A particle assertion about the model concerns the
joint and conditional laws of the agreed records of its observers, under
its declared preparations and complete instruments.
A test compares what those observers read from their own records and agree
on with what observers in our world record; the state of the computation is
a tool of the calculation and contains no physical space
(Section~\ref{sec:cms-test}). The class and statistics theorems of
Section~\ref{sec:defect-particle-classes} consume a declared five-level code
with a spin doublet, which the model does not produce, and they retain their
domains. The response theorem of Section~\ref{sec:finite-supply} consumes a
complete response of the twelve ports. The contrast-memory law gives twelve
closed directions for one load under one declared port service, and Interval
Echo gives twelve for one load and for the bound pair under a
rotation-selected port service; completeness of the physical control menu is
a separate premise (Section~\ref{sec:cms-response}).
A shared label does not identify the states, currents or clocks of these
theorems with those of the model, and Section~\ref{sec:exclusions} proves
exact obstructions to such transfers.

\begin{hypothesis}[Matter as localized defects]
\label{hyp:defect}
Stable massive matter admits a localized excitation of a continuing source
reference, with an invariant preserved by the admitted dynamics. Its creation,
transport, interactions and detection are operations of the same source.
Its total excess energy and propagation use one energy and clock dictionary.
Unstable species may be metastable or internal excitations; massless radiation
need not carry a defect invariant.
\end{hypothesis}

The hypothesis does not ask for a distinct conserved topological charge on every
mass eigenstate. Flavor mixing and decay can connect internal states of a defect
family, and a protected label cannot change at an admitted vertex without another
field or record carrying the compensating charge. Section~\ref{sec:flavor-labels}
gives the exact constraint. Under the contrast-memory seam interaction, which
Interval Echo uses, the conserved invariant of an object is its number of
loads, and the excitation of a cage is conserved under the scalar channel; in
both cases persistence is read on records
(Sections~\ref{sec:cms-objects} and~\ref{sec:cms-cage}).

\section{Internal quantum numbers}
\label{sec:finite-supply}

\subsection{Complete response and the compact Lie algebra}

The carrier is the oriented icosahedral boundary, with twelve ports, thirty
seams and twenty faces. Its real port module under the proper rotation group
$A_5$ decomposes as
\[
 V_{12}=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf3'\oplus\mathbf5,
 \qquad \dim V_{12}^{A_5}=1.
\]
Assume a faithful real-linear response map
$D:V_{12}\to\mathfrak u(\mathcal K)$ whose image is the complete connected
response algebra and is closed under commutators, with proper carrier rotations
acting through the same connected inner response group up to operationally
silent centralizer factors. The gauge paper \cite{ophGauge} proves that the
compact classification then gives
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak g\simeq\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)
                         \oplus\mathfrak{su}(3),
 \label{eq:finite-gauge-type}
\end{equation}
because inner rotations fix a centre of dimension at most one and a centre-free
$\mathfrak{su}(2)^4$ cannot have a one-dimensional fixed space, which leaves the
semisimple split $11=3+8$. It also constructs an explicit current law on the
protected native carrier realizing $\mathfrak u(3)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(3)$.
Twelve ports alone do not imply~\eqref{eq:finite-gauge-type}, and carrier
rechartings are distinct from physical spatial rotations of a relativistic
scattering theory. This paper consumes the result and its twelve current
matrices. Section~\ref{sec:cms-response} reports what the candidate laws
of the executable model give for the same premise.

 M_{15}=\Lambda^2\mathbb C^5\oplus\Lambda^4\mathbb C^5,
 \qquad \dim M_{15}=10+5=15,
\]
the familiar $\mathbf{10}\oplus\overline{\mathbf5}$ branching
\cite{georgiglashow1974}. The current lift preserves all brackets and has

color/weak Casimir readouts give the following all-left convention.

generations, and equivalent triplet representations on spatial, color and family
factors do not identify those factors.

Exchange records have a separate finite route. On the native ordered-cover
transport, let $p_h(x)$ be the permutation parity accumulated by branch $h$. A
coherent ordering bit gives
\[
 K_h=\frac1{\sqrt2}\sum_{x\in D_h}|f_h(x)\rangle\langle x|
             \otimes X^{p_h(x)},\qquad Q=I\otimes X,
 \qquad [K_h,Q]=0.
\]
An odd exchange loop acts as $X$ on the bit, and the corresponding colored
fermionic lift acts as $-\mathrm{SWAP}$. The sectors survive complete
record-adaptive native schedules in this specified memory design; dephasing or
resetting the bit changes that conclusion. These are exchange-history sectors
on their stated domain.

\subsection{The conditional field action}
\label{sec:conditional-field-action}

Choose one complex weak doublet $H$ of hypercharge $1/2$, canonical positive
kinetic forms, physical Weyl statistics and a Lorentz-covariant local field
interpretation in $3+1$ dimensions. Restrict the polynomial action to operator
dimension at most four, modulo integration by parts. The invariant grammar
then gives the gauge kinetic terms, scalar kinetic term and potential,
fermion kinetic terms and exactly three charged Yukawa lines. Independent
gauge couplings, topological theta terms and an additive vacuum-energy
constant are also free. In the four-component convention the charged Yukawa
part is
\[
 \mathcal L_Y=-\overline Q_L\widetilde H Y_u u_R
             -\overline Q_L H Y_d d_R
             -\overline L_L H Y_e e_R+\mathrm{h.c.}
\]
Each $Y_a$ is a complex $3\times3$ matrix. The native invariant-line computation
solves all twelve current equations for both Higgs components and gives one
line for each allowed origin $(6Y_1,6Y_2)=(-4,1),(1,2),(-3,6)$.
Tensoring these lines with the gauge-trivial family factor leaves exactly the
three matrix coefficients \cite{ophGauge,pwweinberg1967model,pwpeskin1995introduction}.

\subsection{Fermion operators and gauge links}

The internal permutation $P_{15}$ lifts to
$P_{90}=I_2\otimes P_{15}\otimes I_3$ on the configured spin, internal and
family factors. Its exterior lift obeys, on every occupation sector,
\[
 \Gamma(P_{90})a^\dagger(f)\Gamma(P_{90})^*=a^\dagger(P_{90}f),
 \qquad[d\Gamma(T_A),a^\dagger(f)]=a^\dagger(T_Af).
\]
Thus the same twelve currents act on the full odd-CAR field; auxiliary
even occupation masks do not supply its statistics. Particles carry the
internal representation, whereas holes carry its conjugate:
$\operatorname{Tr}_{15}Y^5=5/9$ changes sign under conjugation. This fifth
trace distinguishes representations; it is not an anomaly coefficient.

There is also a finite interacting gauge attachment. On the specified
sixty-state links, the hopping
$\sum c_x^\dagger(i c\sigma_j/2)U_{xy}c_y+\mathrm{h.c.}$ commutes with
every full-Fock local Gauss generator. The same fifteen-state matrices have
zero linear and symmetric cubic anomaly tensors, counting the internal
Weyl representation once rather than its two spinor rows. These are exact
finite identities. The quantum-link raising operators are nilpotent and
cannot be replaced by identity backgrounds to import the free dispersion.
A compatible gauge state, continuum scaling and mirror treatment are
additional premises for an interacting chiral field interpretation.

\subsection{Flavor frames and conserved defect labels}
\label{sec:flavor-labels}

The canonical family-frame freedom is $\U(3)^5$, and it classifies every
same-current, canonical-kinetic family embedding. It preserves the charged
singular spectra and basis-invariant mixing data. In particular
$V_{\rm CKM}=U_{uL}^\dagger U_{dL}$ is determined by the same left-handed
weak current, and relabeling families cannot generate physical CP violation.
A real symmetric coefficient class has
$\operatorname{tr}[Y_uY_u^\dagger,Y_dY_d^\dagger]^3=0$
\cite{pwjarlskog1985commutator}.

Conserved additive defect labels must respect the same vertices. If the
charged-current mixing graph is connected, the relation
\[
 q(W^-)-q(e_\alpha)+q(\nu_i)=0
\]
for every nonzero entry forces one common neutrino label $N$ and one common
charged-lepton label. A massive Majorana field adds $2N=0$, which in an integer
winding group implies $N=0$. If a massless neutrino is excluded from defect
assignments and has label zero, connected mixing forces the other neutrino labels
to zero as well. Distinct protected winding numbers therefore cannot simply be
assigned to the three mixed mass eigenstates. Internal excitations, nonadditive
invariants and charged compensating vertices require their own source
construction.

\section{What records and symmetries exclude}
\label{sec:exclusions}

Each statement below is exact on its stated domain and removes a tempting route
from finite records to a particle reading. Each obstruction concerns the
specified construction it is stated for; it is not an exclusion of other admitted
OPH designs.

\subsection{Coherent phases and family averaging}

\begin{proposition}[Classical records select no coherent phase]
For a finite instrument with Kraus operators $K_{ra}$ and arbitrary real $\theta_{ra}$,
replacing $K_{ra}$ by $e^{i\theta_{ra}}K_{ra}$ leaves every outcome map, every repeated
composition and every record-dependent feedback experiment unchanged, on the full matrix
space and with arbitrary entangled references. A well-defined function of the instrument
maps therefore cannot depend on these phases.
\end{proposition}

A coherent sum $\sum_{ra}c_{ra}K_{ra}$ thus does not follow from the instrument alone, and
setting a probability equal to an angle in radians is an additional rule. A
source-selected Stinespring isometry with an accessible coherent reference can carry the
missing information, and such a reference is outside this exclusion. The private seam
memory of A-contrast-memory-spherical-v1 is a coherent reference of this kind: its phase acts on the
loads, and no record of it is committed (Section~\ref{sec:cms-stability}).

\begin{proposition}[Real cyclic averaging forces a repeated eigenvalue]
For a real regular three-cycle $R$ and $P=(I+R+R^2)/3$,
\[
\tfrac13\sum_{j=0}^{2}R^jX(R^T)^j=aP+b(I-P),\qquad X=X^T\in M_3(\mathbb R).
\]
\end{proposition}

The two-dimensional rotation plane forces the repeat, so cyclic averaging of a real
symmetric kernel cannot select three distinct family values. An equivariant construction
from real inputs has real outputs, and a nonreal chiral response needs an input or selection
rule supplying the oriented complex structure.

\subsection{Global symmetry identities}

Cyclic trace gives $\operatorname{Tr}([K,P]H)=\operatorname{Tr}(K[P,H])$. If
$[P,H]=0$, $[K,P]=iH$ and $H$ is Hermitian, then $\operatorname{Tr}(H^2)=0$ and $H=0$.
Exact global finite translation and boost identities therefore require a different
domain from a nonzero relativistic Hamiltonian; controlled sectors and infinite limits
carry their own error bounds. In OPH such identities belong to the effective
descriptions of agreed records (Section~\ref{sec:cms-test}), and the finite law of the
executable model is not asked to carry them.

\subsection{Comparing record laws}

Finite tests can compare whole record laws without enumerating histories.

\begin{proposition}[Sufficient criterion for equal record laws]
Let $A_y,B_y$ be nonnegative outcome matrices of two finite classical instruments with
column-stochastic sums, and $C$ a nonnegative column-stochastic map. If $CA_y=B_yC$ for every record
$y$ and $Cp=q$, then all finite joint record laws coincide. With
$\epsilon_0=\|Cp-q\|_1$ and $\delta=\max_j\sum_{y,i}|(CA_y-B_yC)_{ij}|$, the record total-variation
distance after $n$ steps is at most $\min(1,(\epsilon_0+n\delta)/2)$.
\end{proposition}

Induction gives the exact case, and a telescoping sum of $\ell_1$ contractions gives the approximate
one. Equality of unread channels is insufficient: the one-state instruments $A_0=1,A_1=0$ and
$B_0=0,B_1=1$ have the same unread channel and disjoint records. Determinism supplies neither the
intertwiner nor a compatible initialization. The criterion compares record laws and
ignores the state encodings that produce them, which is the form a test of the executable
model takes (Section~\ref{sec:cms-test}).

\section{What a particle reading of the model requires}
\label{sec:particle-joins}

At finite size the spectral measure of a prepared operator $O$ is
\[
 d\mu_O(\omega)=\sum_n|\langle n|O|\Omega\rangle|^2
 \delta\!\left(\omega-(E_n-E_\Omega)\right)d\omega.
\]
A visible eigenvalue requires nonzero residue in the actual detector channel, and the
physical dictionary must transport $O$, the state, the Hamiltonian, current charges
and the clock together. In the executable model the levels of a law are audit on the spectrum;
what a test uses is the probability of agreed records under the complete reader of
Section~\ref{sec:cms-objects}.

A particle reading of the model composes three steps on one design:
\[
\begin{split}
 &(\text{same law, preparations, observer clocks, complete instruments})\\
 &\quad\longrightarrow
 (\text{object records, port responses, merging and splitting records})\\
 &\quad\longrightarrow
 (\text{an effective field description of the agreed records}).
\end{split}
\]
The first row is supplied by the contrast-memory seam interaction, which Interval Echo
inherits, for the pair and the face object, with their local emitter, their complete reader
and their event programs, and by the second candidate law for the excitation of a cage,
with a local detector. The second row needs the class of an object as a record
and a complete port-control menu acting on that object.
The written-field extension supplies two signs relative to a filled
reference and a material probe that responds to a remote source.
Its four-face doublet can cross a gateway with the load, and exchange
interference distinguishes the native dynamics from a material fermion
control. The exact addressed exchange leaves those orbital labels at
their original sites. Identifying the full branch as an electron requires
spin carried through exchange, an intrinsic magnetic response and a
rest energy tied to an independent physical clock on the same branch
(Section~\ref{sec:cms-signed-exchange}). The third
row is the proposition on smooth effective field descriptions of the flagship
\cite{flagship}, together with the rank-three spatial carrier and the causal and count
limits of the spacetime paper \cite{ophEinstein}. Of their premises the model supplies the
carrier with its rank-three band and observers whose clocks are compared through shared
readings; in Interval Echo a moving observer patch carries its clock, and at reunion the
observers read its relativistic slowing from their own records. The model does not produce
the readouts in the Weyl operators of
a free field on a radial cylinder that the proposition consumes, or the authenticated event
order and refinement family that the limits consume \cite{ophmodel}. These are composition
requirements on one permitted design; uniqueness among all possible simulator designs is
not a prerequisite.
