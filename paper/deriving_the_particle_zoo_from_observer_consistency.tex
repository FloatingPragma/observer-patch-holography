 C_B(n)=\cos(nh),\qquad C_D(n)=0,\qquad
 C_{\rm tr}(n)=\tfrac23\cos(nh).
\]
The same transfer frequency therefore has a nonzero or a zero measured residue depending on the
supplied state and detector, with the first measurement operation included.

Density records also cannot fix absolute field frequencies. Adjoin a reference line and extend each
branch by $V_j(\delta)=1\oplus e^{-i\delta h}U_j$. All complete record probabilities and conditional
density matrices confined to the original response sector are independent of $\delta$, while
coherences with the added reference acquire that phase, $F_\delta=e^{-i\delta h}F_0$.

A coherent family potential need not be a scalar mass. For the supplied Dirac symbol
$\alpha_i=Z\otimes\sigma_i\otimes I$, a potential $I_4\otimes A$ has zero projection onto the
spin-invariant terms anticommuting with all $\alpha_i$. For Hermitian $A,M$ and
$H_h(k)=\alpha\cdot k+X\otimes I_2\otimes M+hI_4\otimes A$,
\[
\operatorname{Tr}[(H_h(k)^2-|k|^2I)^2]-\operatorname{Tr}[H_h(0)^4]
=16h^2|k|^2\operatorname{Tr}(A^2),
\]
so a nonzero potential in this truncated Hamiltonian is no collection of constant relativistic
masses. Finally, cyclic trace gives $\operatorname{Tr}([K,P]H)=\operatorname{Tr}(K[P,H])$. If
$[P,H]=0$, $[K,P]=iH$ and $H$ is Hermitian, then $\operatorname{Tr}(H^2)=0$ and $H=0$. Exact global
finite translation and boost identities therefore require a different domain from a nonzero
relativistic Hamiltonian; controlled sectors and infinite limits carry their own error bounds.

\subsection{Comparing capacities}

Finite tests can compare whole record laws without enumerating histories.

\begin{proposition}[Sufficient criterion for equal record laws]
Let $A_y,B_y$ be nonnegative outcome matrices of two finite classical instruments with
column-stochastic sums, and $C$ a nonnegative column-stochastic map. If $CA_y=B_yC$ for every record
$y$ and $Cp=q$, then all finite joint record laws coincide. With
$\epsilon_0=\|Cp-q\|_1$ and $\delta=\max_j\sum_{y,i}|(CA_y-B_yC)_{ij}|$, the record total-variation
distance after $n$ steps is at most $\min(1,(\epsilon_0+n\delta)/2)$.
\end{proposition}

Induction gives the exact case, and a telescoping sum of $\ell_1$ contractions gives the approximate
one. Equality of unread channels is insufficient: the one-state instruments $A_0=1,A_1=0$ and
$B_0=0,B_1=1$ have the same unread channel and disjoint records. Determinism supplies neither the
intertwiner nor a compatible initialization.
