\begin{abstract}
The matter hypothesis of Observer Patch Holography (OPH) identifies stable
massive matter with localized topological defects of native overlap
transport, whose excess maintenance energy is read in the same clock as
propagation. A finite construction realizes protected transport-history
sectors under a specified coherent memory law. The native two-load
configuration complex has exchange group $\mathbb Z/2$; its ordered-cover
lift preserves two sharp quantum sectors. Giving each occupied port an
independent internal state makes the odd loop act as $-\mathrm{SWAP}$,
with exact CAR signs and complete source instruments. This construction
protects a memory-carried charge but does not localize a physical
particle core.

An exact basis map joins the retained fifteen-state matter module to a
specified chiral action. Its calibrated charged Yukawas and mixing remain
inputs. Complete CAR source factors implement finite spatial propagation,
particle-changing Higgs operations and both coherent and separately
flagged collision holds. A joint experiment uses one calibrated clock and
retains all field sectors, native records and quantum references. It
exhibits a measurable difference between the two collision policies.

Negative controls delimit these results. The unextended double-load
candidate survives $n$ uniform seam attempts with probability $(5/6)^n$;
its collision sector is not stable. Fixed finite mass dictionaries fail
the charged hierarchy. A separate moving Weyl/contact bound pair is
topologically trivial and fails its same-speed rest/kinetic mass test.
The remaining physical task is to realize localized defects, their
interactions and measured spectrum in one chiral $3+1$ theory with the
required massive sectors and gapless electromagnetic field.
\end{abstract}

law. A protected sector whose excess maintenance quantity is not one energy
with the common signal speed of its propagation rejects the maintenance
reading of mass. A frozen defect dictionary that fails the matched spectrum
rejects that law and dictionary.

\section{The defect hypothesis}
\label{sec:defect-hypothesis}

OPH models observations through finite accessible algebras, retained records
and local repair. The executable observer construction supplies explicit
carriers, source operators, instruments and a native repair law
\cite{ophmodel,ophrepairlaw}, and the observer layer supplies the record and
measurement theorems on which a detector reading rests \cite{ophobservers}. A particle in this setting is a statement about
a continuing observable response: preparations, currents and detector
records must exhibit one excitation, with its quantum numbers, propagation
and interactions, in a specified state and clock regime. A finite internal
basis vector, memory level, channel decay rate or agreement record does not
have that meaning by itself, and no fundamental particle object behind the
observers' statistics is assumed.

\subsection{Statement}

\begin{hypothesis}[Localized defects of native overlap transport]
\label{hyp:defect}
Stable massive matter is a localized defect of the native overlap and
transport structure, in the following sense.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(\roman*)]
\item \emph{Core and reference.} There is a reference regime of the
native process and a bounded core outside which the records approach that
regime.
\item \emph{Protecting invariant.} The transport around the core carries a
loop holonomy, a nontrivial invariant relative to the reference, that
admitted local repairs cannot erase. The obstruction is to trivializing the
transport relative to the reference; observers agree on every measured
event.
\item \emph{Transport.} Moving the core transports the invariant.
\item \emph{One law.} Creation, annihilation, fusion and decay follow from
the same native law. Freezing selected seams or supplying punctures to
protect an assigned label changes the law and does not count.
\item \emph{Rest energy.} The rest mass is the excess rest energy of the
defect over the reference, produced by its excess maintenance dynamics and
read in the same clock and energy dictionary as its propagation and
interactions. In a justified local relativistic regime this gives
\(E_{\rm rest}=mc^2\) with one common signal speed \(c\), the speed of the
massless response of the same construction. No conversion factor is fitted
per species.
\item \emph{Unstable species.} Unstable particles are metastable or
internal excitations of defects, with decay channels and widths derived
from the same law.
\item \emph{Radiation.} Massless radiation and the gauge response arise from
the same construction, as transport or collective excitations of the
overlap structure.
\item \emph{Identity.} Species, charges, spin and statistics, and families
are properties of defect classes and of their transport.
\end{enumerate}
\end{hypothesis}

The hypothesis is the matter clause of the executable-model hypothesis
stated above. It specifies a mechanism and its observable consequences. It
is not implied by mass--energy equivalence: a real free scalar field has a
contractible field target and trivial vacuum topology, yet its one-particle
states have relativistic massive dispersion \cite{tongQFT}. A dispersion
relation alone therefore does not reveal a topological sector. Whether an
effective particle is microscopically a defect is a question about the
source realization.

The hypothesis names parameters explicitly. Native attempt counts, host
runtime, solver counters and modular parameters are distinct from calibrated
observer clocks, and repair counts, entropy production and resource use are
distinct from energy. The maintenance-to-energy map in clause~(v) must be
derived, including kinetic normalization and any reference or regulator
subtraction. Different schedules or implementations can change resource
counts without changing the claimed physical response; an energy must be
invariant under such changes.

\section{What the finite theorems supply}
\label{sec:finite-supply}

\subsection{Frustration at fixed holonomy}

The mathematical seed of the hypothesis is the cycle-obstruction theorem of
the consensus layer \cite{ophconsensus}. Let \(A\) be an abelian group,
\(G=(V,E)\) a finite connected graph with oriented edges, \(b_e\in A\) an
edge offset and \(w_e>0\) an edge weight. The affine overlap equations
\(x_v-x_u=b_e\) have a global solution exactly when every cycle holonomy
\(\sum_{e\in C}\varepsilon_C(e)b_e\) vanishes.

\begin{theorem}[Persistent frustration at fixed holonomy {\cite{ophconsensus}}]
\label{thm:fixed-holonomy-frustration}
Define the mismatch cost
\[
 \Phi_b(x)=\sum_{e:u\to v}w_e\,\mathbf 1[x_v-x_u\neq b_e],
 \qquad x:V\to A.
\]
If some cycle holonomy of \(b\) is nonzero, then
\(\min_x\Phi_b(x)>0\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The cost takes values among the finite subset sums of the positive weights,
so its minimum is attained. A zero minimum would solve every edge equation,
contradicting the cycle criterion.
\end{proof}

Repairs of the vertex assignments \(x\) cannot remove this frustration while
\(b\) is fixed, and it persists under changes of \(b\) inside its cohomology
class. The theorem lacks four things that the hypothesis needs. It has no
update rule for \(b\); a physical update that changes the compatibility data
outside its class can remove the obstruction. It does not localize the
frustration in a bounded core. It does not move it. Its residual
\(\min_x\Phi_b\) is a cost of the whole compatibility problem, not an
excitation energy above a reference ground state of its own sector.

On a two-dimensional complex the holonomy and its localization are related
by an elementary identity. Let \(K\) be a finite oriented two-complex, \(b\)
an \(A\)-valued one-cochain and \(F=\delta b\) its face curvature,
\(F(f)=b(\partial f)\).

\begin{lemma}[Loop holonomy is enclosed curvature]
\label{lem:enclosed-curvature}
For every two-chain \(c\), the holonomy of \(b\) around \(\partial c\)
equals \(F(c)\). If \(H_1(K;A)=0\), every closed edge loop is such a
boundary, so every loop holonomy is the curvature it encloses. If \(K\) is a
closed oriented surface with coherently oriented faces, then
\(\sum_fF(f)=0\). If \(b=\delta n\) is an exact difference, then \(F=0\) and
every loop holonomy vanishes.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The first statement is the definition of the coboundary. A one-cycle with
vanishing homology class bounds a two-chain. The sum of all coherently
oriented faces of a closed surface has zero boundary. Finally,
\(\delta\delta n=0\).
\end{proof}

On a closed surface with vanishing first homology, a nonzero holonomy around
a bounded core therefore requires nonzero curvature inside the core, and the
total curvature vanishes: cores come with compensating curvature elsewhere.
For a nonabelian structure group the holonomy of a based loop is defined up
to conjugation, and its conjugacy class is the invariant that a defect
charge would carry.

\subsection{A quantitative frustration instance}

Frustration produces a strictly positive gap on the executable carrier. Let
\(D_\sigma\) be the sign-twisted seam derivative on the connected
observer-visible finite local domain, and let
\(H_\sigma=D_\sigma^\ast D_\sigma\) use the declared unit-counting measure.

\begin{proposition}[Exact finite source-Hamiltonian gap]
\label{prop:finite-source-hamiltonian-gap}
The signed-incidence formula makes \(H_\sigma\) integer symmetric and
positive semidefinite. A kernel vector would supply a sign-consistent
coloring on each connected component. The declared reversing seam transport
frustrates every triangle, so the connected complex has no such coloring and
the signed incidence rank is \(8{,}662\). Therefore \(\ker H_\sigma=0\). With
maximum degree \(12\), the determinant bound gives
\[
  \lambda_{\min}(H_\sigma)\geq 24^{-8661}>0 .
\]
A sparse numerical refinement gives
\(\lambda_{\min}=0.11753670336118684\) with relative residual
\(1.49\times10^{-14}\).
\end{proposition}

The determinant floor follows because the positive integer determinant is
at least one and every eigenvalue is at most 24. The decimal is measured,
with a residual check and no interval enclosure. This is the
\(\mathbb Z_2\) analogue of Theorem~\ref{thm:fixed-holonomy-frustration}
with frustration on every face. It is a global gap of a declared operator.
It does not select a localized defect, a physical field fiber or a mass.

\begin{proposition}[Collision decay for quantum inputs]
\label{prop:collision-decay}
Let \(\{K_h\}\) be the complete uniform C or E instrument on the 78-state
domain, \(K_h=M_h/\sqrt{60}\) over the thirty seams, two coins and retained

\[
 \sum_hK_h^\dagger P_{\rm coll}K_h=\frac56P_{\rm coll}
\]
for the projector \(P_{\rm coll}\) onto the twelve double-load
configurations. For every state \(\rho\) on the domain and an untouched
reference,
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}\bigl[(P_{\rm coll}\otimes I)(\Phi^n\otimes\mathrm{id})
 (\rho)\bigr]
 =\Bigl(\frac56\Bigr)^n
 \operatorname{Tr}\bigl[(P_{\rm coll}\otimes I)\rho\bigr],
\]
where \(\Phi\) is the unread one-attempt channel. No persistent quantum subspace is supported wholly on the
collisions under this
unread evolution.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The Heisenberg identity is verified exactly on all retained branches; its
effect matrix is diagonal with entries \(50/60\) on collisions, and no branch
feeds a binary state into a collision. The dual channel is linear, so
\(\Phi^{*n}(P_{\rm coll})=(5/6)^nP_{\rm coll}\), and tensoring with the
identity on the reference preserves the identity. A persistent
collision-supported subspace would keep its collision weight equal to one.
\end{proof}

These results reject the proposed autonomous configuration defect and the
tested automatic cost-to-energy identifications: attempt counts, the
quadratic load potential and the edge-disagreement cost. They reject
quantum persistence wholly within the collision subspace. They do not
exclude other quantum, complete-history or federated defects, another
repair law, a common energy offset, or the general hypothesis. A replacement
candidate must identify its actual source sector and allowed dynamics before
any mass comparison.

\subsection{The actively corrected code}

The actively corrected matching code is a different persistent object. It
uses six fixed nonedge matching words; coherent recovery preserves its
logical information, and actual port rotations act on it. Without recovery,
one uniform-seam C or E attempt takes the normalized code projector out of
the code with probability \(31/108\) or \(55/108\), respectively. This is a
one-step escape from that preparation, not an all-state lifetime. With the
declared recorded recovery, the logical state and its references are
preserved and the complete record law has the product form
\[
 \rho\longmapsto\rho\otimes p(\mathrm{records}).
\]

\begin{proposition}[Record costs cannot split logical states]
\label{prop:record-cost-split}
For a channel of this product form and any observable \(c\) of the records
alone, the expectation \(\operatorname{Tr}[(I\otimes c)(\rho\otimes p)]
=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho)\,\mathbb E_p[c]\) is independent of the logical
state \(\rho\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The trace factorizes over the tensor product.
\end{proof}

The exact receipt checks all 720 raw branches. The one-port cost, each
native edge cost and the quadratic load potential compress on the code to
\(I_6/6\), \(I_6/3\) and \(I_6\). A fixed cost of seams or records alone
therefore cannot give different masses to different logical states. A
common energy offset is not excluded, and no physical offset is derived.
Persistence here is active recovery selected by existing records, not
autonomous protection, and the code carries no exhibited topological charge.
Its fixed matching table and the separately derived equivariant code table
must not be identified for a nonscalar observable or a new interaction.

\section{Mass from maintenance}
\label{sec:mass-maintenance}

\subsection{Requirements}

A rest mass can be the excess rest energy of a localized excitation over the
reference state in a regime where energy, clock and spatial dispersion are
defined. To identify it with repair maintenance, the source must derive how
the excitation changes an intrinsic action or energy readout, or the same
clocked response; subtract the reference contribution; and account for
cutoff, volume and renormalization dependence. Propagation and interactions
are then tested with that same identification. Attenuation can govern
lifetime or decoherence instead of inertial mass, and the response
calculation must decide which.

Counting alone cannot supply the scale.

\begin{proposition}[Count-rate no-go]
An oriented register with twenty-four slots, a twenty-four-stage automaton,
or a decomposition into twenty-four formal update steps determines no
physical frequency, Hamiltonian gap or mass scale.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Rescaling every physical generator by \(H\mapsto\lambda H\), \(\lambda>0\),
preserves the register, transition graph, grading and settled quotient while
multiplying all generator gaps and rates by \(\lambda\).
\end{proof}

The number twenty-four plays no role in the argument, and a repair
iteration counter is likewise not an operational clock. A physical rate
requires a source-derived clock instrument, a calibrated comparison line and
a residual bound. Once such a line exists, a scale follows exactly.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional determinant-clock attachment]
Let \(L_{24}\) be a one-dimensional source-derived operational clock line
with a distinguished calibrated positive gap vector \(\ell_{24}\) of norm
\(\Delta_{24}\). Suppose there is a refinement-natural, quotient-visible
norm-preserving line isomorphism
\(\Theta_e:L_{24}^{\otimes3}\to\det M_e\) to the physical charged-lepton
determinant line, with the element compatibility
\(\Theta_e(\ell_{24}^{\otimes3})=\det(M_e)\). Then
\(g_e:=|\det M_e|^{1/3}=\Delta_{24}\). If the element-compatible map
rescales norms by a separately derived factor \(\kappa>0\), then
\(g_e=\kappa^{1/3}\Delta_{24}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The distinguished tensor-cube vector has norm \(\Delta_{24}^3\). Element
compatibility and norm preservation give \(|\det M_e|=\Delta_{24}^3\).
\end{proof}

Writing a norm-preserving line isomorphism without the distinguished-vector
equality constructs neither the clock line nor the attachment. Data that
fix only centered logarithms leave the scale free.

\begin{theorem}[Absolute-scale underdetermination]
Any readout factoring only through a centered log triple
\((\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)\), \(\sum_i\ell_i=0\), and its difference invariants
leaves the common scale \(g>0\) of \(m_i=g\,e^{\ell_i}\) free.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For every \(c\in\mathbb R\), \(e^c\operatorname{diag}(e^{\ell_i})\) has the
same centered logarithms and differences, determinant \(e^{3c}\), and masses
scaled by \(e^c\).
\end{proof}

A rest energy appears to a comoving observer as a clock rate. On a declared
Lorentz module and mass shell, free plane waves persist under the declared
translation flow, and their phase along their own frame has proper-time
derivative \(m\). For \(m>0\) the internal-clock periods are
\((2\pi/m)\mathbb Z\); the zero-mass clock is constant and has no fundamental
period. This is conditional kinematics, without a source-selected mass or
clock, and it states the target that a maintenance rate must meet: the same
\(m\) must appear in the internal clock, in the dispersion and in the
kinetic normalization.

Kinetic and mass terms must come from the same source. For a four-component
Dirac convention with positive kinetic metrics,
\[
 Y_{s,\mathrm{can}}=Z_L^{-1/2}Y_sZ_R^{-1/2}/\sqrt{Z_H};
\]
a literal all-left Weyl bilinear has a transpose on the left normalization
factor. A record matrix can change a coordinate without changing the mass:
\(Z_L=Z_R=R>0\) and \(Y_s=g_sR\) give \(Y_{s,\mathrm{can}}=g_sI/\sqrt{Z_H}\)
and \(\det(pR-hg_sR)=\det(R)(p-hg_s)^3\). A hierarchical Gram matrix by itself
need not produce a hierarchical physical vertex. Eliminating an invertible
heavy block also changes the Higgs derivative,
\[
 \partial_H(A-BD^{-1}C)=A_H-B_HD^{-1}C-BD^{-1}C_H+BD^{-1}D_HD^{-1}C .
\]

Repair contraction and coherent propagation are different dynamics. Passive
stationary fixed-reference schedules have an exact rank-two Schur response
obstruction, including eight zero crossing weights among the 22 seams, and
positive whitening preserves the null direction. The uncorrected recorded
66-state channel has no invariant fifteen-state work code in the tested
class. Its ten-mode response conditioned on an incident event has operator
norm \(\lambda_C=7/10\) or \(\lambda_E=2/5\).

\begin{proposition}[Contraction has no direct unitary intertwiner]
\label{prop:no-intertwiner}
Let \(R\) have operator norm \(\lambda<1\), let \(U\) be unitary and let \(J\)
be any linear map. Then
