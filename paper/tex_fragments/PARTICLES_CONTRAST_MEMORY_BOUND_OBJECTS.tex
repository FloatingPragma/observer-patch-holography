\section{The bound objects of the executable model}
\label{sec:cms-objects}

The executable model of the theory runs candidate repair and memory laws on one fixed
carrier and is specified with its proofs in the executable-model paper \cite{ophmodel}.
Its main candidate law, Interval Echo, takes the seam interaction of the reference
candidate, the contrast-memory law A-contrast-memory-spherical-v1, at coupling $\kappa=8$
as its elementary interaction and adds a clock that a finite observer patch carries; the
scalar channel is the second candidate law. A travelling patch keeps a hand, an elapsed
count and the number of its own repair opportunities since its last verified handoff. At
reunion with a home patch the ratio of the two elapsed counts matches the relativistic
proper-time forecast that the observers compute from their own light-pulse and arrival
records, a slowing of $3.2$ to $4.4$ percent, within about one part in a thousand on the
tested journeys. The closing filter of the clock is calibrated on the reunion ratio of a
supplied reciprocal relay journey \cite{ophmodel}. This section reads the bound objects of
the contrast-memory seam interaction as particle candidates, and Interval Echo inherits
them together with the binding theorem. The section states what they are, which
observable about them the model reproduces, how their response compares with the
complete-response premise of Section~\ref{sec:finite-supply}, and how they could meet the
class map of Section~\ref{sec:defect-particle-classes}. Statements about the
specification, the binding theorem, the emitter and the reader are proved in
\cite{ophmodel} and are quoted here.

\subsection{What a test of the model is}
\label{sec:cms-test}

A test of the model compares distributions of admissible observer records with what
observers in our world record. The state of the simulation does not contain the physical
Universe or its spacetime. Ports, seams, cells and federations are hardware of the
computation; they form no physical space, and no test asks that the carrier be a
three-dimensional lattice. Physical space, time, fields and particles are effective
descriptions that the observers inside the computation use for the records they agree
on. A difference between microscopic state encodings is therefore no failure of the
model, and a resemblance between them is no success.

The bridge is proved in the theory. Observers who inhabit the simulation can describe
their experiences as relativistic $3+1$-dimensional continuum spacetimes: for every
finite admissible menu of observer readouts, a smooth $(1+3)$-dimensional Lorentzian
field theory reproduces their complete predictions within any tolerance, on the premises
of the proposition on smooth
effective field descriptions of the flagship \cite{flagship}, and the spacetime paper
gives the rank-three spatial carrier and the causal and count limits \cite{ophEinstein}.
What the executable model has to supply is the premises of those theorems, with its own
local clocks and records. It does not have to rebuild three dimensions inside its state.
The executable-model paper lists the finite theorems whose premises its outputs supply as
they stand \cite{ophmodel}.
Every object below is read on seams and faces by observers through their ports; none of
them sits or moves in space.

State vectors, levels of the law, the evolution parameter and positions in a sequence of
events are tools of the calculation. Statements that use them are marked as audit. No
common clock is assumed: each port counts the events of its own seams, and that count is
a clock of the observer that owns the port.

\subsection{Carrier, loads and the law}

A cell is one twelve-port icosahedral carrier, the echosahedron, with $12$ ports, $30$
seams and $20$ faces \cite{ophmicro}; one cell is one observer, and its twelve ports are
its interfaces. A federation joins complete cells by external
seams that form a partial matching on the ports; in the spherical federations every
cell has three external ports, so a port has five or six seams. The federations used
below have $20$, $80$ and $320$ cells. Every port holds a load $n_v\in\{0,1,2\}$, and
every seam has a private memory of eleven states, coherent working memory held by the
owners of its two ports, which is no public record. On every seam the owners write the
native seam event into the memory, apply a phase that depends on the recorded contrast
of the two loads and reverse the write. On blank memory this echo acts on the loads
exactly as
\begin{equation}
 H_\kappa=\frac12\sum_e\bigl(I-\mathrm{SWAP}_e\bigr)+\kappa C,\qquad
 C=6\sum_v n_v^2-2\sum_{e=\{a,b\}}n_an_b,
 \label{eq:cms-law}
\end{equation}
for every real $\kappa$, and it keeps a blank memory blank \cite{ophmodel}. The coupling
$\kappa$ is one dimensionless number, the agreement energy in units of the exchange. A
seam whose two ports both hold a load is an agreed seam and lowers $C$ by $2$. The law
conserves the number of loads and their multiset. On loads $0$ and $1$ the seam term is
the ferromagnetic XXZ interaction with anisotropy $1+2\kappa$, and the binding below is
of the droplet kind known for that interaction
\cite{nachtergaele2001,fischbacher2018}. The model claims no interaction of its own;
its content is the realization of the law by a private memory, the constants on this
carrier with loads up to two, and the tests on agreed records.

\subsection{The pair on a seam}

A pair is two loads on the two ends of one seam (Figure~\ref{fig:cms-cell}a). A single
load persists by conservation of the number of loads, so the pair is the smallest object
whose persistence is an outcome of the law. Let $P$ project on the span of the pairs on a
seam in the sector of two loads and $Q=I-P$, which contains every separated pair and
every doubled load. On every federation of complete cells with a matching of external
seams, the binding theorem of \cite{ophmodel} gives
\[
 PH_\kappa P\le(10\kappa+6)P,\qquad QH_\kappa Q\ge12\kappa\,Q,\qquad
 \|QH_\kappa P\|\le\sqrt{10},
\]
and for $\kappa>3$, for every state that starts in $P$ and every value of the evolution
parameter,
\begin{equation}
 \Pr(\text{no pair on a seam})\le\frac{40}{(2\kappa-6)^2}.
 \label{eq:cms-binding}
\end{equation}
The bound does not depend on the number of cells. The proof combines ordered-block
min-max with the Sylvester equation for ordered spectra. On the $20$-, $80$- and $320$-cell carriers, exact upper bounds on the
exchange block and coupling give $17.35/(2\kappa-5.701)^2$ for
$2\kappa>5.701$.

\paragraph{Emitter and reader.}
A local emitter with one fuel state at a seam writes the pair with a stated error in
norm; at $\kappa=128$ and emitter coupling $64$ the probability of no pair, a doubled load
or fuel is at most $64009/25600000\approx0.0025004$ at the first read after any chosen free interval, for any emitter seam of the spherical family. Every
seam has two ends, and at each end the observer that owns the port copies the predicate
``both ends hold one load'' into a private pointer; a courier carries one copy, and a
record is admitted only when both copies are valid and agree. The complete outcome set is one record per seam and one
outcome ``no pair''; fuel left in the emitter counts as ``no pair''. The seam memory stays
private and unread, the seam records are committed at the reading, and each reading uses
a fresh run, so reading never holds the object in place \cite{ophmodel}.

\paragraph{A pair and a clock formed by one seam write.}
There is a different preparation in which the clock and the binding interact.
Start with a sharp double load at one end of seam $(0,1)$. The native echo
leaves one unit on each end and records signed contrast $+2$ with an equal
superposition of the two coins. Starting at the other end gives the same
loads and contrast $-2$. To prepare the second quadrature the apparatus
isolates the seam for an inverse half exchange before the write. Leaving
the degree references active during this pulse detunes the two doubles by
$4\kappa$ and bounds the transfer probability by $1/(1+16\kappa^2)$.
Two isolated one-load dimers in a distant
cell measure elapsed phase. Vacuum writes isolate their contacts before launch.
Every contact acts during flight. At reception the inverse tag echo maps the
two signs back to two distinct double-load outcomes when both ends are
occupied. All other pair positions give a quiet outcome.

In the sign-parity basis let $s=0,1$ denote even and odd. In the coin state
prepared by the write, the tagged term has energy $4\kappa+s$ except on
endpoint loads $(1,1)$, where the echo maps it to blank memory of zero
generator. Let $|p\rangle$ be the sharp two-load word on the tag,
$P=|p\rangle\langle p|$ and $Q=I-P$.
With the common $12\kappa$ and dimer energies removed, the exterior pair
generator is
\[
 H_s=K-\kappa(\ell_a+\ell_b+2A_{ab})+(4\kappa+s)Q .
\]
Here $(a,b)$ is the occupied word, $K$ is its binary exchange generator
on the active bonds, $A_{ab}$ is their adjacency and $\ell_a$ counts the
bonds removed from load exchange at $a$. The original degree references
continue to act. On each of the three released federations,
$\langle p|K|p\rangle=9/2$, $\|QK|p\rangle\|=3/2$, and the maximum contrast saving
$\ell_a+\ell_b+2A_{ab}$ off the tag is at most three.
For $\kappa=8$ the complementary block lies at least $19.5+s$ above the
tag diagonal. Interlacing gives exactly one low eigenvalue $E_s$.
Its eigenvector has tag weight at least
$w_s=[1+(1.5/(19.5+s))^2]^{-1}$ by the complementary resolvent.
The tag return probability is therefore at least $(2w_s-1)^2$ at every
exposure, which exceeds $0.9766$ for either parity.

Interpolating $s$ between zero and one gives
$dE_s/ds=\langle Q\rangle_s$. The clock line $E_1-E_0$ is generated by
excursions from the tag. In the strong positive contrast limit the
complementary resolvent is $\kappa^{-1}D^{-1}+O(\kappa^{-2})$, where
$D_{ab}=6-\ell_a-\ell_b-2A_{ab}$ off the tag. Four of the nine one-hop
exits have $D=3$ and five have $D=5$, all with exchange amplitude $-1/2$.
Thus
\[
 \lim_{\kappa\to+\infty}\kappa^2(E_1-E_0)
 =\tfrac14(4/9+5/25)=29/180 .
\]
At the candidate coupling the line is $0.00260641$ per reference-clock
radian. On 20 cells, joint receiver records at 97 appointments give the
simultaneous 99 percent interval $[0.002214,0.002981]$ with both source
quadratures and every quiet reading retained. Restoring continuous ticking
on the occupied tag returns the reference rate. The preparation, control

\path{model/experiments/interval_echo_encounter/dwell.py}.

The clock is pinned. The range of the native write is invariant under
contrast reversal and the contrast square. Conjugation of the memory
