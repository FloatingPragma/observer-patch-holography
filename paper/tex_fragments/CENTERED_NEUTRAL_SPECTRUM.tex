\paragraph{The centered cube's complete finite gap and neutral spectrum.}
For the same centered source $K=H-N/2$, $c=1/8$, $U=1$, exact
spin/eta and signed-translation reductions cover all $4900$ balanced
states and all $12870$ states with $N=8$.  Every block has a complete
integer eigenbasis proposal admitted by rational Gram and residual
bounds.  The isolated first excitation has hidden spin one, eta zero,
spatial character $(1,1,1)$, and multiplicity three.  Its gap satisfies
\[
  0.015021254176\leq\Delta_{\rm finite}\leq0.015021254177.
\]
The proved separation greater than $1/8$ in every other charge sector
makes this the gap of the entire $65536$-state source.  This is a
quantitative finite neutral-gap closure, not a uniform continuum gap.

The local probes $S^z_x$ and $\rho_x=n_x-1$ select $(S,\eta)=(1,0)$
and $(0,1)$ respectively.  Their highest/lowest component calculations
use $S^+_x$ and $c_{x\downarrow}c_{x\uparrow}$, with neutral spectral
weights respectively one half and twice those component weights.
The conserved total-spin and total-eta channels vanish exactly.
Each remaining $136$-dimensional local candidate space has one exactly
dark eigenline at $K=-2$: an integer eigenvector's probe pullback is
also an energy-$-2$ vector, hence orthogonal to the actual ground.
Every other weight is certified strictly positive, giving $135$ lines
in each measure without an amplitude cutoff.  All local weights and
spatial residue matrices are retained.

Each fixed spin component and the density probe reach $357$ dimensions;
the three spin components together with density reach $1428$
orthogonal neutral dimensions.  The other $11441$ excited neutral
states are invisible to these specified probes.  Current divergences
are also resolved by the exact continuity equations:
\[
 \dot\rho_x=-\operatorname{div}j_x,\qquad
 \dot S^z_x=-\operatorname{div}j^S_x,\qquad
 w_{\operatorname{div}j}(E)=E^2w_\rho(E).
\]
This statement does not resolve independent transverse currents.
The local sum rule $\langle(S^z)^2\rangle+\langle\rho^2\rangle/4=1/4$
provides an additional exact check.  These are finite source energies
and hidden-spin multiplets, not relativistic masses or an empirical
particle identification.  A nonzero fixed-volume gap does not replace
the required control of the interacting massive continuum limit.
