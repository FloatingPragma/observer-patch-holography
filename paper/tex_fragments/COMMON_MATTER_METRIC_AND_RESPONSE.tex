\section{A common matter metric and its gravitational response}
\label{sec:common-matter-metric-response}

This section connects the conditional scalar description of a radial
product with the gravitational layer of the tower. The description supplies
a concrete dictionary for comparing matter and geometry, and the same
dictionary yields the static-product control that any responsive geometry
must overcome. Write its four-dimensional coefficient density and metric as
\[
 C=\mu\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,a),\qquad
 g=\sqrt{-\det C}\,C^{-1}
   =N^2\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,a^{-1}),\qquad
 N^2=\mu\sqrt{\det a},
\]
where \(a\) is a positive two-dimensional screen tensor and \(\mu>0\).
These identities use the complete action density, including its measure.
For measurable coefficients, smooth representatives are understood with the
finite-menu approximation of
Proposition~\ref{prop:smooth-effective-descriptions} and its smearing
transport.

\subsection{Common principal symbols and finite field operators}

Choose an orthonormal coframe \(e\) with \(g=e^t\eta e\). Transporting
Clifford matrices through its inverse gives
\[
 \{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu}I,\qquad
 (\gamma^\mu p_\mu)^2=(g^{\mu\nu}p_\mu p_\nu)I.
\]
The scalar wave symbol, both Weyl principal determinants and the Maxwell
symbol therefore have the same characteristic cone. The Maxwell gauge
direction is retained, leaving two physical polarizations on a nonzero
null covector. A change of clock coordinate rescales all field generators
and the reference inverse temperature together, preserving \(\beta H\).
This pointwise dictionary does not select the fields' dynamics or chirality.

For a finite incidence factor \(C_0\) on the declared angular--radial product,
\[
 D=\begin{pmatrix}0&C_0^\dagger\\C_0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 D^2=\operatorname{diag}(C_0^\dagger C_0,C_0C_0^\dagger).
\]
The node block is the scalar spatial operator \(B=C_0^\dagger C_0\).
CAR second quantization gives a conditional fermion construction with node
propagation \(\cos(t\sqrt B)\) and node-to-edge propagation
\(-iC_0\sin(t\sqrt B)/\sqrt B\). The full finite construction retains its
extra edge modes.

The smooth product admits a spin structure, but scalar radial Dirichlet
conditions do not by themselves define a self-adjoint first-order spinor
operator. A reflecting extension squares to a Neumann component
and a Dirichlet component. Spin-connection and curvature terms also enter:
a curved spinor square is not a componentwise scalar Laplacian. These
domain and connection hypotheses are explicit premises.

\subsection{Area, volume and the entropy variation}

The same product metric gives exact induced measures
\[
 dA=\mu\,d^2x,\qquad dV=\mu N\,dr\,d^2x.
\]
Thus the scalar action measure supplies a whole-screen area dictionary.
Fixed counting measure alone does not fix physical three-volume. Radial
slices in this product have equal area and are not concentric small
geodesic spheres.

A constrained maximum-entropy completion and its joint counting first law
hold on their finite algebraic variation domains. They do not identify fixed
cap marginal or fixed total dimension with fixed proper volume.

The family
\[
 \mu(z)=\mu_0e^{-2z/3},\qquad a(z)=e^{2z}a_0
\]
keeps proper volume fixed on the declared radial coordinate interval and
decreases its boundary area. At fixed model-coordinate inverse temperature,
the cavity thermal entropy also decreases. Hence the trial functional
\(S_{\rm thermal}+A/(4G_N)\) is not stationary for positive \(G_N\) on
these variations. This is a specified thermal-cavity comparison; it is not
an identification with local vacuum entanglement or a proof that repair
generates those variations.

The distinction between observable algebras matters as well. For a
symmetric mixture of \(\pm\sqrt\epsilon\) displacements, the
unread work density has positive first-order entropy and energy derivatives
at fixed reference Hamiltonian. The retained sign-plus-work density has
zero entropy derivative. Both outcomes and the purification are retained.
A gravitational entropy identification must specify which region algebra
and which records enter its bulk term.

\subsection{A fixed-product obstruction and a necessary dynamic response}

\begin{proposition}[Radial null response of the static product]
\label{prop:static-product}
For a smooth metric
\[
 g=f(x,y)\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,h(x,y)),\qquad f>0,
\]
with positive screen metric \(h\), both radial null contractions of its
Einstein tensor vanish. With the Gaussian reference and other sources fixed,
the same geometry cannot satisfy the Einstein balances for that reference
and every coherent scalar excitation at positive gravitational coupling.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The \((t,r)\) factor has Lorentz symmetry and all metric coefficients are
independent of \(t,r\). Its Ricci components obey
\(R_{tt}+R_{rr}=0\) and \(R_{tr}=0\), also verified by direct differentiation
for arbitrary smooth \(f,h\). For \(k=\partial_t\pm\partial_r\),
\(g(k,k)=0\), so trace and cosmological metric terms vanish and
\(G(k,k)=0\). The restored angular-constant scalar sector admits
\[
 \phi=A\sin(\pi r/L)\cos(\pi t/L).
\]
It obeys the product wave equation and the declared radial Dirichlet
condition. Its action-derived coherent relative stress satisfies
\[
 \Delta T(k,k)=(k\phi)^2
   =\frac{A^2\pi^2}{2L^2}>0
 \quad\text{at }t=0,\ r=L/4.
\]
Subtracting the reference and excited Einstein equations contradicts
\(\Delta G(k,k)=0\). This uses relative stress and supplies no absolute
vacuum-energy prescription.
\end{proof}

A coherent amplitude changes stress quadratically; this argument is not
a claimed failure of the linear entropy first law. A symmetric mixture of
opposite displacements supplies a first-order state direction, with its
observable algebra specified separately. Independently changing compensating
sources and geometries outside the static product class are outside the
proposition.

For the smooth spherical extension
\[
 ds^2=-2du\,dv+R(u,v)^2d\Omega^2,
\]
direct curvature calculation gives \(G_{uu}=-2R_{uu}/R\). The null Einstein
balance consequently requires
\[
 R_{uu}=-4\pi G_NR T_{uu},\qquad
 \theta_u=-\frac{\theta^2}{2}-8\pi G_NT_{uu},\qquad
 \theta=\partial_u\log(4\pi R^2).
\]
Here \(u\) is affine along the declared null curves. These are necessary
area-response equations in this symmetry class, not a selected repair law
or a solution of the full Einstein system. The alternative finite
nine-null theorem also requires its independent balances; a pointwise
tetrad map alone does not supply them.

library of the public repository. These identities establish no
