\paragraph{The actual regional modular limit of the free source.}
For $U=0$, $c=v/a$, and a fixed ball $D=B_R$, the new proof in
\path{model/lattice_modular_limit/README.md} strengthens the lattice stress
readouts to the regional modular flow itself. Let $E_a$ select the finitely
many lattice sites with centers in $D$, and let $\Pi_a$ be the full source
filled-band projector. The exact finite regional covariance is
$A_a=E_a\Pi_aE_a$ on $H_a=E_a\ell^2$. Its continuum counterpart is
\[
 C=\bigoplus_{b\in\{0,1\}^3}\chi_D\Pi_b\chi_D,
 \qquad H=\bigoplus_b L^2(D;\mathbb C^2).
\]
All eight valleys are retained. The comparison map
$T_af=E_a\sum_bM_bJ_af_b$ has norm at most $\sqrt8$, is asymptotically
isometric on each fixed vector, and satisfies
\[
 R_a(f):=A_aT_af-T_aCf\longrightarrow0.
\]
The proof combines the preceding gapless projector estimate with a
boundary-cell layer of volume at most $16\pi R^2a$. On a collared smooth
probe its additional error is at most
$8R\sqrt a\,\|\Pi_bf\|_\infty$; density then covers all of $H$.
No exact orthogonality of the finite-spacing valley images is assumed.

Both regional covariances have no endpoint eigenvectors. A compactly
supported continuum pure-band spinor would have an entire Fourier
transform satisfying a square-root band equation; continuation around
one simple complex branch point forces it to vanish. The lattice version
uses the trigonometric-polynomial transform and the branch polynomial
\[
 z^4-(2+4s)z^2+1,\qquad
 \operatorname{disc}=4096s^2(s+1)^2>0,
 \quad s=\sin^2k_2+\sin^2k_3>0.
\]
Thus $\ker C=\ker(1-C)=0$ and $0<A_a<1$. These assertions supply no
uniform gap away from zero and one.

Put $k(x)=\log((1-x)/x)$ and $w(x)=x(1-x)$. For every $g\in H$ set
$f=w(C)g$ and $f_a=w(A_a)T_ag$. The range of $w(C)$ is a graph core for
$k(C)$, and
\[
 \|f_a-T_af\|\leq\|R_a(g)\|+\|R_a(Cg)\|.
\]
The functions $w(x)k(x)$ and $w(x)e^{-itk(x)}$ extend continuously as
zero at both endpoints. Polynomial functional calculus consequently gives
\[
 k(A_a)f_a-T_ak(C)f\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad e^{-itk(A_a)}T_af-T_ae^{-itk(C)}f\longrightarrow0.
\]
The second limit extends to every fixed $f\in H$ and is uniform on
compact intervals of modular time. Explicitly, since
\[
 \frac{d}{dx}\bigl(w(x)e^{-itk(x)}\bigr)
   =(1-2x+it)e^{-itk(x)},
\]
its Lipschitz constant is at most $1+T$ for $|t|\leq T$. Degree-$N$
Bernstein approximation gives the core-flow error bound
\[
 \frac{3(1+T)\|g\|}{\sqrt N}
 +\frac{N3^N}{4}\max_{0\leq j<N}\|R_a(C^jg)\|.
\]
This finite-residual bound does not presume uniform smoothness of every
sharply restricted covariance iterate.

For $k_\delta(x)=\log((1-x+\delta)/(x+\delta))$,
\[
 w(x)|k_\delta(x)-k(x)|\leq\delta.
\]
It follows that any $\delta(a)\to0$ is admissible on the matched core,
with extra generator error at most $3\delta\|g\|$ and extra flow error
at most $3T\delta\|g\|$. Density extends the flow assertion to every
fixed probe. Embedded zero-complement modes are excluded from the
physical logarithm; for a cell isometry $W$ and continuous $F$ the correct
identity includes $F(0)(I-WW^*)$ in $F(WAW^*)$.

The faithful finite regional state is exactly
$\rho_a=Z_a^{-1}\exp[-d\Gamma(k(A_a))]$. In the antilinear annihilator
convention its modular action is
\[
 \sigma_t^a(c_a(f))=c_a(e^{-itk(A_a)}f).
\]
The same covariance formula describes the continuum quasifree modular
group by Araki's KMS construction (Publ.~RIMS \textbf{6}, 385--442,
Theorem~3). Thus the comparison is the norm difference between
$c_a(e^{-itk(A_a)}T_af)$ and $c_a(T_ae^{-itk(C)}f)$ in the same source
algebra. All fixed finite CAR words and their state correlations converge.

There is also a finite relative-entropy readout. For a normalized nonzero
core vector $f$, use $U_\theta=\exp[i\theta c^*(f)c(f)]$ and the matching
normalized $f_a$. Then
\[
 D(\omega\circ\operatorname{Ad}U_\theta^*\|\omega)
 =2(1-\cos\theta)
 \bigl(\langle C\rangle_f\langle k(C)\rangle_f
                 -\langle Ck(C)\rangle_f\bigr)<\infty.
\]
The corresponding exact finite-density relative entropies converge to
this value. The proof uses the relative Tomita identity and Wick's rule,
not convergence of divergent absolute entropies.

This closes a fixed-region free-sector modular refinement premise.
It does not assert convergence of unmodified bare logarithms on every
vector, operator-norm convergence of the full modular groups, a
shrinking-ball area or chart limit, nonzero-$U$ regional convergence,
native modular gate execution, Newton normalization, or generalized-
entropy stationarity.
