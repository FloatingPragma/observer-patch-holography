\paragraph{Spatial channels and normalized spin scaling.}
The source frame fixes
$S_x^a=(-1)^{\sum_j x_j-x_a}s_x^a$ on the full onsite CAR space.
For an affine proper cubic action $x=Ry+t$, the hidden spin transforms
by $Q_tR$, where
$Q_t=\operatorname{diag}((-1)^{t_2+t_3},(-1)^{t_1+t_3},(-1)^{t_1+t_2})$.
The staggered order transforms by
$\operatorname{diag}((-1)^{t_1},(-1)^{t_2},(-1)^{t_3})R$.
An axis-1 ordered state therefore preserves physical translations
$2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z$.
Its two hidden near-zero transverse components occupy distinct
residual-zone neighborhoods of $(0,0,\pi)$ and $(0,\pi,0)$.
They are exchanged by the site-centered quarter turn about axis~1.

Residual internal rotations and the combined odd-translation/spin-flip
symmetry make the transverse spectral matrix scalar on a momentum
packet disjoint from its $Q=(\pi,\pi,\pi)$ translate.
The circular channels have charges $+1,-1$, equal nonzero weights,
and are orthogonal. For a quarter-turn-invariant envelope they have
cubic characters $-i,+i$; each circular channel occupies a momentum
star, rather than one translation eigenchannel.
For $m=1/1000$ and a normalized envelope in $p/2<|k|<p$, the inherited
positive-energy filter gives
\[
 \frac{m^2p}{96}\le\|\Psi_{\pm,p}\|^2\le\frac p4,\qquad
 \operatorname{supp}\mu_{\Psi_{\pm,p}}
 \subset\left(\frac{mJp}{16},\frac{64Jp}{m^2}\right).
\]
After normalizing by this weight and rescaling $(E,k)$ by $(Jp,p)$,
the recentered real-channel joint measures are probabilities on
a fixed compact positive-energy annulus. They have nonzero
subsequential limits, and the two channels have the same recentered
measure. Uniqueness, a sharp dispersion surface and scattering are
not inferred.
The natural spectral rescaling by $1/p$ likewise has nonzero finite
subsequential mass, between $m^2/96$ and $1/4$.

The source comparison remains controlled after this normalization.
For $p=1/n$, truncate space and time at $n^2$ and $n^2/J$, and use
a source box of radius $128n^3$ with
$c=J|E|^4$, $U=J|E|^8$.
Exact cube integration bounds the packet operator by $K n^{3/2}$;
the normalization cost is consequently $O(n^4)$.
The complete-record source error is $O(n^{-7})$, the normalized
source error is $O(n^{-3})$, and smooth truncation contributes
$O(n^{-4})$. The boundary exponent is at most $-19n^3$ for $n\ge2$.
This comparison uses restrictions of the selected infinite spin ground;
an effective finite-cost preparation rate for those restrictions is
not assumed. The theorem constructs spatially resolved scaling
readouts of the fixed-spacing spin sector. It does not identify
relativistic helicity, measured particles, or a continuum field algebra.
