\paragraph{Regional modular data on the full particle ground.}

The invariant finite reference $\rho_G=P_G/5$ supplies regional
modular data on the same ground used for the particle spectrum.
In the hidden spin frame, its highest-weight vector is the Slater
determinant of four negative spatial orbitals, with projector
$P_-=(I-A/\sqrt3)/2$. Averaging a common spin rotation yields $\rho_G$.
For a region $R$, let $\lambda_j$ be the eigenvalues of the spatial
compression $(P_-)_R$. The Slater Schmidt decomposition and the spin
average give, for every occupied natural-orbital subset $I$,
\begin{equation}
 w_I=\prod_{j\in I}\lambda_j\prod_{j\notin I}(1-\lambda_j),
 \qquad
 \rho_{R,I}=\frac{w_I}{|I|+1}P_{\mathrm{sym},I}.
\end{equation}
Here $P_{\mathrm{sym},I}$ projects onto the symmetric spin space of
dimension $|I|+1$; all other states have zero weight. Indeed phase
integration deletes unequal spin counts, and the remaining diagonal
integral is
$\binom{k}{l}\int_0^1t^{k-l}(1-t)^l\,dt=1/(k+1)$.
Thus, retaining the complete kernel,
\begin{equation}
 S(\rho_R)=\sum_j h_2(\lambda_j)+\sum_I w_I\log(|I|+1).
\end{equation}
For $0<\lambda_j<1$ the modular generator on the positive support is
\begin{equation}
 K_R=-\sum_j\log(1-\lambda_j)I
 +\sum_j\log\frac{1-\lambda_j}{\lambda_j}N_j+\log(N_R+1).
\end{equation}
Endpoint occupations are handled as fixed blocks, without taking
logarithms of zero. This is a support identity, not a full-CAR
faithfulness claim.

Every adjacent two-site region has positive spectrum
\begin{equation}
 \left\{\frac16\ [1],\quad
 \frac{2-\sqrt3}{12}\ [2],\quad
 \frac{2+\sqrt3}{12}\ [2],\quad
 \frac1{18}\ [3]\right\},
\end{equation}
where brackets denote multiplicities. Its eight other eigenvalues are
zero. The support consists of the vacuum, the four one-particle states,
and the three two-particle triplet states. With $H_R$ the actual internal
regional Hamiltonian, whose contact term vanishes on this support,
\begin{equation}
 K_R=\log6\,I+\beta_R H_R+\log(N_R+1),
 \qquad \beta_R=16\log(2+\sqrt3).
\end{equation}
Exact signed CAR traces of all five source ground vectors verify this
identity on all twelve cube bonds; the sixteen nonadjacent pairs give
$K_R=\log4\,I+\log(N_R+1)$ instead. The same local spin-frame change
conjugates the density, support and Hamiltonian back to the original
source coordinates.

For every Hermitian trace-zero variation $\delta$ within the positive
support, including noncommuting variations,
\begin{equation}
 DS_{\rho_R}(\delta)=\beta_R\operatorname{Tr}(\delta H_R)
                  +\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\delta\log(N_R+1)\bigr).
\end{equation}
The second term is central on the supported number-preserving algebra,
not on the full CAR algebra. Since
$\lambda_{\min}^+=(2-\sqrt3)/12>1/48$, for
$\delta=\sigma-\rho_R$ with $\sigma$ supported there and
$0\le\epsilon<1$,
\begin{equation}
 0\le D(\rho_R+\epsilon\delta\Vert\rho_R)
 \le\frac{24\epsilon^2\|\delta\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2}{1-\epsilon}.
\end{equation}
In contrast, a density $\sigma_\perp$ in the orthogonal kernel
obeys the full-state identity
$S((1-\epsilon)\rho_R+\epsilon\sigma_\perp)
=(1-\epsilon)S(\rho_R)+\epsilon S(\sigma_\perp)+h_2(\epsilon)$,
whose derivative diverges at zero. The kernel is therefore retained in
the operational domain; no supported first law is applied to such a
disturbance. These are same-ground finite modular and entropy inputs.
Identifying their central term with a geometric edge, their energy
pairing with a diamond charge, or their coefficient with a physical
clock requires the existing Einstein theorem's common geometric
and scaling premises.
