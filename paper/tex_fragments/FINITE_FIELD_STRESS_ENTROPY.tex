\label{subsec:finite-field-stress-entropy}

This field attachment uses the same supplied Weyl/contact CAR

$H=d\Gamma[-(1/8)\sigma\cdot\sin k]+\sum_x n_{x0}n_{x1}$.
On the open cube of 27 sites, its complete two-fermion space has dimension
1,431. Assign half of each Hermitian hopping bond to either endpoint and
the contact energy to its site. Then
\[
 h_x=UD_x+\tfrac12\sum_{b\ni x}H_b,\qquad
 \sum_xh_x=H,\qquad J_{x\to y}=i[h_x,h_y],\qquad
 \frac{dh_x}{dt}+\sum_yJ_{x\to y}=0.
\]
Here $t$ is Hamiltonian model time with the supplied convention $\hbar=1$.
Hermiticity and antisymmetry of the currents follow directly. Summing the
identity over a region cancels all internal currents and gives its complete
outward flux. Overlapping bond stars produce range-two currents; omitting
them fails conservation. Exact Gaussian-integer matrices verify all 351
site pairs and 27 local equations, and deleting $U$ changes 54 currents.
This is interaction energy accounting, not a number current renamed as
