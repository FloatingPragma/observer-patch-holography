\subsection{Finite regional restrictions of an entropy reference}
\label{subsec:finite-field-stress-entropy}

A regional entropy attachment needs a specified regional algebra and a
specified way of restricting a global reference to it. The following exact
statements fix two such choices and their limits.

On the fixed-$N$ direct sum
$\bigoplus_n(\mathcal H_A^n\otimes\mathcal H_{A^c}^{N-n})$, a faithful
density admits a preserving unital completely positive retraction onto
$\bigoplus_n(\mathcal B(\mathcal H_A^n)\otimes I)$ exactly when it has
blocks $r_n\otimes\tau_n$, with $\operatorname{Tr}\tau_n=1$.
Multiplicative-domain identities remove cross-sector blocks and force
this conditional product form. For a Gibbs density $e^{-\beta H}/Z$ with
$\beta\ne0$ this requires block-additive $H$ and hence $[H,N_A]=0$. A
Hamiltonian with a nonzero one-body hopping entry across the region boundary
has $[N_A,H]\ne0$, so no dense
Gibbs logarithm is needed to exclude that exact retraction or its exact
generator intertwining. This does not exclude partial traces,
reduced modular clocks, approximate maps or different accessible domains.
Where nested algebras do admit expectations preserving one faithful
reference, uniqueness supplies their tower law. Independently selected
regional references do not supply it.

A weaker observation interface does compose without factorization. For a
CPTP state restriction $T_{BS}$ and one faithful reference with
$\rho_B=T_{BS}(\rho_S)$, define
\[
 E_{BS}(X)=\rho_B^{-1/2}
 T_{BS}(\rho_S^{1/2}X\rho_S^{1/2})\rho_B^{-1/2}.
\]
Its explicit Kraus matrices make it unital and completely positive, and
$\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_BE_{BS}X)=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_SX)$.
For nested restrictions and their same reference reductions, cancellation
of the intermediate square roots gives $E_{AB}E_{BS}=E_{AS}$.
This finite Petz-transpose construction preserves complete POVMs and
arbitrary references. It generally neither fixes the embedded local
algebra nor intertwines modular flows; its reference-dependent effects
are not thereby implemented local detectors. It supplies a
compatible observation tower while leaving those stronger clock and access
requirements explicit.

These constructions supply an exact quantum entropy map and its regional
restrictions. They do not inhabit the Einstein premise bundle. Needed are a common symmetric Lorentz stress tensor, its
geometric Ward identities and null charges, modular diamonds and their
edge split, fixed-proper-volume area variation, Bianchi/geometry data and
a controlled common refinement with physical normalization. In particular,
finite Gibbs stationarity is not generalized-entropy stationarity at fixed
proper volume. These finite attachments close only the obligations on
those declared algebras.
