The summary is deliberately negative: the constructed net does not
inherit quantum-field-theoretic structure.  Of the seven targets, five
are not statable against the committed carriers --- four blocked on
structure that exists neither in the development nor in the pinned
Mathlib, one gated by design on separate finite-reconstruction packets
--- one has a refutable restricted surrogate (bijective localized
endomorphisms are inner), and one
carries a finite provable core whose net-level reading is degenerate
and whose public-level reading is refuted.  Two downstream gates
follow.  The Einstein-branch readout completion may consume the finite
Gibbs-KMS identity as finite-block algebra, but its modular readout
cannot be carried by a public flow (no nontrivial one exists), its
stress readout receives nothing (the stress row is a typed exit), and
no statement here supports ``the net is thermal''.  Particle-sector
promotion receives exactly two finite receipts --- the Gibbs-KMS core and
innerness of the restricted bijective localized-endomorphism class --- and one
constraint: no candidate may infer a charge or flavor quantum number from that
restricted automorphism surrogate. Non-bijective transportable endomorphisms
and full DHR reconstruction are unavailable; the spin-statistics, pole, and
continuum receipts that promotion additionally requires are typed exits or
absent structures here, not available results.

Natural powers nevertheless give one exact architecture constraint on the
scattering exit: in every Hausdorff topological group, $g^n\to a$ forces
$g=1$ (\path{Lean/QFT/FiniteUnitaryScatteringNoGo.lean}).  Hence a nontrivial exact
unitary update at fixed finite cutoff cannot itself settle to an ordinary
matrix-topology large-time limit.  The same module proves the scope control
$(g^n)^{-1}g^n=1$: relative evolution can converge even when the individual
powers do not.  Any fixed-cutoff direct-power construction that uses $U^n$
itself as its asymptotic carrier must therefore do something beyond full
convergence of that sequence.  At fixed finite dimension, merely passing to
full weak-operator convergence does not evade the obstruction because the
standard finite-dimensional operator topologies coincide.  Routes not
excluded here include comparison dynamics, selected projected scalar or
observable readouts, an infinite-dimensional weak limit after a continuum or
infinite-volume passage, or open-system evolution.  A finite-time operational
or recurrence-aware readout is another route not excluded here.  The theorem
proves no S-matrix, asymptotic state, optical theorem, cross section, or
interacting continuum limit, and the interacting-QFT/RG/scattering construction remains unresolved.
