\subsection{What a fixed-geometry repair history identifies}
\label{subsec:native-geometric-source-identification}

The repair carrier has locally readable port loads, seam attempts and records.
Its supplied cell chart and incidence are not updated by a load repair.
This distinguishes two possible source constructions: reading the geometry
present in that implementation, and attaching a proposed volume law
to its load history.

\begin{proposition}[Frozen geometric readout]
Let a state be \((\Gamma,x)\), and suppose every allowed repair has the form
\((\Gamma,x)\mapsto(\Gamma,F_e(x,\xi))\). For any positive cell-volume
readout \(V_i(\Gamma)\), prepared reference
\(\bar V_i=V_i(\Gamma)\), and finite repair history,
\[
 q_i=\frac13\log\frac{V_i(\Gamma)}{\bar V_i}=0.
\]
Every unequal-position covariance and every linear coarse/fine covariance of
this field is zero, under every load preparation, schedule and recording
clock. The conclusion applies separately at every refinement level.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Induction over the history preserves the first component. Its readout equals
the reference pointwise, so the log contrast vanishes before any expectation
or coarse graining is taken.
\end{proof}

This proposition concerns perturbations of the supplied geometry. It says
neither that the background geometry is flat nor that all possible observer
dynamics have zero curvature. It also does not prohibit an operational metric
reconstructed by a new constitutive rule from load-dependent probes. Such a
rule would add the missing metric-volume interface.

On the native geodesic carrier, a concrete chart diagnostic is the positive
cone volume \(V_i=|\det(v_{i0},v_{i1},v_{i2})|/6\) of a triangular cell.
It is an imported Euclidean chart volume, not a measured physical collar
volume. The finite controls apply the production integer repair to both a
uniform half-filled preparation and a clustered half-filled preparation at
\(20\) and \(80\) cells, with \(240\) and \(960\) ports. All \(64\)
attempts in each control are retained and independently replayed. Rotating
the chart preserves these volumes; scaling all coordinates by two multiplies
them by eight. Both operations leave the log contrast with the matched
reference zero. Comparing absolute cell sizes at different refinements does
not turn this background change into a stochastic perturbation.

\begin{proposition}[Equal local variance does not identify source shape]
Consider the isolated twelve-port carrier's internal icosahedral Laplacian \(L\),
\(\Pi=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/12\), and native stationary
fixed-occupancy load \(z\). Both \(z\) and the local seam drive \(Lz\)
are locally computable, mean-zero, and equivariant under carrier rotations.
After separately normalizing their covariance traces to one, they give
\[
 C_{\rm load}=\frac{\Pi}{11},\qquad
 C_{\rm drive}=\frac{L^2}{360}.
\]
Both diagonals are \(1/12\). At antipodal ports their covariances are
\(-1/132\) and \(0\), respectively; at adjacent ports they are
\(-1/132\) and \(-1/45\). Thus locality, carrier symmetry, mean removal
and equal one-point variance leave separated-position power unselected.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Native stationarity gives \(\operatorname{Cov}(z)=\kappa\Pi\).
Since \(L\mathbf1=0\), the drive covariance is \(\kappa L^2\).
The graph is five-regular, so \((L^2)_{ii}=30\) and
\(\operatorname{tr}(L^2)=360\). Adjacent vertices have two common
neighbors, yielding \((L^2)_{ij}=-10+2=-8\); antipodal vertices have
neither an edge nor a common neighbor, yielding zero. Division by the
respective traces proves the statement. Equivariance follows because every
carrier automorphism commutes with \(L\).
\end{proof}

This is the exact internal seam graph used by native repair and by its
isolated-carrier control; inter-carrier seams are excluded from this
twelve-port calculation. Its covariance matrices are not asserted to be
the full coupled federation covariances. The filling, stationary reference
and gains used to normalize the traces are declared inputs. The normalization
is a diagnostic comparison, with no observed spectrum used to choose either gain. Exact enumeration retains both the one-raised
and six-raised stationary preparations, with \(12\) and \(924\)
configurations and \(\kappa=1/12\) and \(3/11\). It checks all covariance
entries, including two disjoint antipodal two-port blocks and their
fine/coarse cross covariance within that carrier. This block comparison
is not a cosmological separation or a continuum refinement limit. For a
centered current-state readout,
\(q=Bz+g\) with \(B=\kappa^{-1}\mathbb E[qz^{\mathsf T}]\),
the same calculation retains the absolute density component and residual.
The local mismatch readout
\(\sum_{j\sim i}(n_i-n_j)^2\), centered under its preparation, has
\(B=0\) and a nonzero residual at half filling: complementing every load
leaves this readout invariant while reversing \(z\). Nonlinearity therefore
supplies an additional load observable, but by itself no geometric meaning.

The exact record controls use \(P=I+G/30\), with the continuous
generator \(G\) at unit attempt rate per seam: one tick is a uniformly selected
seam attempt with a fair endpoint coin. For \(m=1,2,4\) retained readings
and \(c=1,2\) attempts between readings, they check
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}\!\left(\frac1m\sum_{a=0}^{m-1}q(X_{ca})\right)
 =\frac{mC_0+\sum_{a=1}^{m-1}(m-a)(C_{ca}+C_{ca}^{\mathsf T})}{m^2},
 \qquad C_t=\mathbb E[q(X_0)q(X_t)^{\mathsf T}].
\]
These altered clocks change record covariances and leave the supplied
geometry invariant. They are finite exact checks, not a physical time
calibration or an asymptotic refinement experiment.

For any of these load observables \(u_i\), one may attach positive labels
\(J_i/\bar J_i=\exp(3u_i)\), giving a formal log-volume contrast \(u_i\)
without changing a single native load transition. These are unselected
extensions, not certified physical models. Even gain calibration cannot
choose between the distinct covariance shapes above. A nontrivial geometric
source needs an actual metric or collar-volume update, its relation to native
records, an independently specified reference slice, and a physical
calibration. The fixed-geometry construction supplies none of those by
relabelling a load fluctuation. Nonstationary preparations, spatial memory or
new couplings are possible mechanisms, but each must expose that interface.

Reconstructing a chart solely from the exact native linear response does
not introduce a stochastic geometry update: the fair repair law has
\(Gx=-Lx/2\) with the same prescribed \(L\). Any fixed reconstruction
from this operator is independent of the realized load preparation. A
state-dependent metric extracted from other response or fluctuation data
would require its own observable and constitutive identification.
