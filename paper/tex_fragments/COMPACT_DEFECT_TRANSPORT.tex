\subsection{Protected texture records and controlled band transport}
\label{sec:compact-defect-transport}

A declared local repair law protects the compact pair's two winding charges.
Let $f_x$ be its unnormalized reference texture and fix the zero cores and
constant exterior. At every noncore site retain a rational vector $v_x$ with
$\lVert v_x-f_x\rVert\le\eta\lVert f_x\rVert$, $0<\eta<1/4$, using
$v_x/\lVert v_x\rVert$ in the actual Pauli potential. A one-vertex proposal
is accepted only if it satisfies this budget and has nonnegative scalar
product with the old vector and every other vertex of every incident shell
triangle. Shared-face data are checked on both shells. Rejected proposals
remain recorded identity operations.

\begin{proposition}[Guarded winding and uniform isolated band]
Every admitted finite history preserves the two shell degrees. Its spatial
Hamiltonian has exactly four isolated modes with $|E|\le r=b^2/(M-4)$ and
complementary spectrum outside $(-g,g)$, $g=M-2$, where $b^2=7/4$ for adjacent
unit shells and $b^2=3/2$ for separated shells. For two admitted band
projectors $\Pi_v,\Pi_w$,
\[
 \lVert\Pi_w-\Pi_v\rVert\le\beta
 =\frac{4M\eta c}{d^2},\qquad c=\frac{g+r}{2},\quad d=\frac{g-r}{2}.
\]
The statements are uniform in the exterior volume.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
During an accepted replacement, every interpolated shell vector is a convex
combination of nonzero vectors with pairwise nonnegative scalar products.
Its squared norm is at least $\sum_i t_i^2\lVert v_i\rVert^2>0$, so
normalization gives a shell homotopy. Every noncore onsite eigenvalue stays
$\pm M$ and the four core coordinates stay zero; the compact-source Schur
proof therefore gives the same band count and localization bounds.
Normalization gives $\lVert H_w-H_v\rVert\le4M\eta$. On $|z|=c$ both
resolvents have norm at most $1/d$. Their identity and the contour formula
for the two projectors give the displayed bound.
\end{proof}

For $\beta<1$, define
\[
 S=\Pi_w\Pi_v+(1-\Pi_w)(1-\Pi_v),\qquad
 U=S(S^*S)^{-1/2},\qquad S^*S=1-(\Pi_w-\Pi_v)^2.
\]
Since $S\Pi_v=\Pi_wS$ and the positive factor commutes with $\Pi_v$,
$U$ is unitary and $U\Pi_vU^*=\Pi_w$. The literal particle-hole relation
$H_vC=-C\overline{H_v}$ implies $\Pi_vC=C\overline{\Pi_v}$, and the polar
formula gives $UC=C\overline U$, hence $UCU^T=C$. Thus $U\otimes I_{45}$
transports all twelve uniform native currents and the same pairing tensor
$C\otimes\overline K$. For $W'=UW$, the pairing matrix
$W'^*C\overline{W'}$ is unchanged, while $A_w=W'^*H_wW'$ retains the actual
changed spatial energies. Second quantization also transports
$N_v=d\Gamma(\Pi_v\otimes I_{45})$ and $\lambda(N_v-90)^2$.

The explicit source uses $M=64$, $\eta=1/512$ and four equiprobable proposals
at each record: two opposite transverse changes at the shared-face center,
one forbidden zero and one forbidden core change. Its three texture states
have transition rows $(1/2,1/4,1/4)$, $(0,3/4,1/4)$ and $(0,1/4,3/4)$.
All twelve state/proposal records are retained. Each branch applies its
texture transition and $\sqrt{q_r}\,\Gamma(U_r)$, with identity control for
a repeated texture, so the complete instrument preserves trace. The band
is transported exactly at record endpoints. Omitting $U_r$ instead gives
leakage probability at most $\min(1,n^2\beta^2)$ after $n$ quenches and
intervening source evolution; the numerical controls exhibit nonzero leakage.

A nearest-neighbor snake through the $N=2(2L+1)^3$ spatial-spin coordinates
compiles each $U$ by adjacent Givens rotations: at most $N(N-1)/2$ two-mode
gates and $N$ onsite phases. The complete $N=686$ controls replay 234,955
two-mode gates per nontrivial texture pair. Addressed quadratic pulses,
their timing and drift cancellation are supplied services; this is not a
compilation into unchanged C/E repair. Intermediate pulses need not preserve
the band, and the controller supplies the endpoint energy change, bounded
by $2r$ on the one-body band. Erasing the guards permits zero-degree textures
with the same four-mode count. The result establishes controlled protection
and transport on this law, without autonomous core motion, local Gauss
dressing, physical species identification or relativistic dispersion.
