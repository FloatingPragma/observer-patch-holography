\section{Finite selection tests for particle readout}
\label{sec:finite-particle-selection-tests}

Several supplied field controls admit exact tests against the finite source.
The following calculations retain the stated source, complete records and
operator domains. They determine which coefficients follow from those data
and which require an additional selection law. No measured particle mass
enters their construction. Transport to another carrier requires an
identification preserving its state, currents and clock; equality of
spectra alone supplies no such identification.

For the coherent three-cycle $R$, write
$C=aI+rb(e^{i\theta}R+e^{-i\theta}R^2)$ and $Y=C^2/3$, where
$a=\sqrt5/6$, $b=a/\sqrt2$ and $0\le r\le1$.
The positive-root chamber is $|\theta|\le\pi/12$.
The \href{https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/main/model/particle_calibration_selection/PROOF_PHASE_ATTENUATION.md}{exact source calculation}
gives
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}Y=\frac5{36}(1+r^2),\qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}Y^2=\frac{25}{3888}+\frac{25r^2}{648}
 +\frac{25r^4}{2592}+\frac{25\sqrt2\,r^3}{1944}\cos3\theta.
\]
With a declared neutral Higgs number $h>0$, the charged free-CAR sea energy
at kinetic norm $p$ is $-2\sum_j\sqrt{p^2+h^2y_j^2}$, where $y_j$ are the
eigenvalues of $Y$. At $p=0$ it equals $-2h\operatorname{Tr}Y$, independent
of phase. Convexity in each root proves that external energy minimization
sets $r=1$ for every positive-weighted finite momentum set. Rational bounds
at $p=1/256$, $h=1$ prove an interior phase minimum exists; changing $p$
can reverse a phase comparison. The momentum prescription is supplied.

The actual finite quantum scalar has vacuum and two doublet states.
For its gauge-invariant pair-creation vertex $W(Y)$, the conserved
$N_F-2N_H=0$ sector has dimension $1+2\binom{18}{2}=307$.
The vacuum and $W(Y)$ applied to it span an invariant two-dimensional
subspace; the scalar cutoff forbids another creation step.
With common number energies and $d=2\epsilon_F+\epsilon_H$, its levels are
\[
 E_\pm=\frac{d\pm\sqrt{d^2+16\operatorname{Tr}Y^2}}2;
 \qquad 305\text{ retained dark levels have energy }d.
\]
The lower level is the whole-sector ground. Minimization selects
$\theta=0,r=1$, whose action ratios are $1:1:(34+24\sqrt2)$.
This sector calculation excludes spatial hopping and supplies neither the
global interacting ground nor a Higgs expectation value. A dynamical
phase register requires its own mixing or preparation law and common
energy reference.

The \href{https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/main/model/particle_calibration_selection/PROOF_CAPACITY_RESPONSE.md}{finite record-budget response}
uses the literal reset memory, event probabilities $p_C=1/12,p_E=1/6$
and declared C-enabled coherent pulses. Its first-derivative coefficient
$Y_n$ has trace $1/12$, rank two at one attempt and rank three at every
$n\ge2$. Exact invariant bounds give, for ordered positive eigenvalues,
\[
 \frac{\lambda_2(Y_n)}{\lambda_1(Y_n)}<\frac{1171058}{6633}<177,
 \qquad
 \frac{\lambda_3(Y_n)}{\lambda_1(Y_n)}<\frac{8616}{11}<784.
\]
The complete direct archive has $24^n$ positive histories and capacity
$n\log24$ nats. Varying this budget cannot reproduce observed lepton ratios
by a common linear mass conversion. These coefficients are derivatives of
a noisy finite response; no interacting mass identification follows.
Pixel capacity and archive capacity require a separate attachment.

An independent \href{https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/main/model/particle_calibration_selection/PROOF_CLOCK_UNITS.md}{record-clock calculation}
uses all sixty seam/coin outcomes on the binary total-two carrier.
Its transition $\mathsf P$, occupation matrix $B$ and graph Laplacian $L$
satisfy $\mathsf PB=B(I-L/60)$. Consequently the occupation correlations
decay at exact rates $e_\lambda=-\log(1-\lambda/60)$ for
$\lambda=5-\sqrt5,6,5+\sqrt5$. Common attempt blocking and common energy
conversion cancel from their ratios. These are record-relaxation rates
and reconstructed $-\log\mathsf P$ gaps; the charged action has no proved
identification with them, and GeV units require an energy reference.

The \href{https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/main/model/particle_calibration_selection/PROOF_FAMILIES_CHIRALITY.md}{finite band and canonical dressing}
give two further operator connections. Uniform sampling of the twelve

on the full 36-state pair. Its strictly least positive eigenvalue is
$(5-\sqrt5)/3$, of rank three, with both zero-cost states retained.
This identifies the declared cooling band without a dimension window;
selection of that controller and its physical family attachment are extra
premises.

For two CAR modes and every level of the 60-state quantum link, let
$S=c_x^\dagger Uc_y+c_y^\dagger U^\dagger c_x$ and
$W=e^{i\pi S/4}$. The operators $b_i=Wc_iW^\dagger$ satisfy exact CAR
and retain all endpoint gauge transformations. Their canonical
one-particle projector has rank 60, including 59 dark directions beyond the
rank-one edge projector. Its sign construction satisfies the
Ginsparg--Wilson identity but has 118 dark zero columns; it supplies no
relativistic kinetic regulator. A local interacting chiral limit requires
a selected kinetic and complement Hamiltonian, compatible quantum electric
transport, uniform locality bounds and a positive physical state on the
same clock.
