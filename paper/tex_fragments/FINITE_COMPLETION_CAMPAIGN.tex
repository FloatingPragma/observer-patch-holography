\paragraph{Other finite interfaces.}
The full-CAR locality bounds used above control dynamics at fixed
lattice spacing; a chiral spatial continuum remains a further limit.
For partition expectations $E_c$ and positive rates,
\[
 L=\sum_c\gamma_c(E_c-I),\qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}(X^*LX)=-\sum_c\gamma_c\|X-E_cX\|_{\rm HS}^2
\]
identifies $\ker L$ with the intersection of the partition commutants,
without assuming commuting pinchings or independent no-cancellation.
Applying this theorem requires the stated generator. Equivariant rational
chain maps and homotopies on the supplied finite support tower are homology
maps, not quantum channels or a dynamical refinement limit.

\paragraph{What the negative controls exclude.}
Several finite benchmarks constrain possible identifications.
A supplied Weyl/contact action has an isolated moving bound-pair band,
complete contact residues and an optical identity, but its positive
contact section makes the band topologically trivial. Its certified
curvature fails the rest/kinetic mass relation with the same speed
parameter. Contact interactions also couple proper Weyl momentum pockets
to their complements. The lattice scattering theorem, finite thermal
positivity and KMS identities do not remove those obstructions or supply
physical asymptotic detectors, a chiral continuum or calibrated cross sections.

The centered repulsive dimer gives a sharper family-wide exclusion.
For $t=c/2>0$, $U\ge0$, $\mu=U/2$ and $d=\sqrt{U^2+16t^2}$, its two
charge-one doublets have gaps $g_\pm=d/2\pm t$, hence
\[
 1<g_+/g_-\le3.
\]
No common positive linear conversion yields the charged-lepton hierarchy
or three distinct same-charge families there. Allowing another chemical
potential can evade the ratio bound but does not create a third doublet.
The fixed complete charged-frequency catalogue also fails its broader
lepton-ratio comparison. The periodic four-site extension fails its
declared $E(k)^2=m^2+c^2\sin^2k$ benchmark at $c=1/8$; a two-parameter
fit to two distinct momentum values does not rescue that test.
These exclude stated finite dictionaries, not every larger action or a
renormalized continuum limit. Electron/positron masses set by one common
measured calibration, and retrospective parameter comparisons, provide
no independent mass prediction.

Finite current response also has a precise scope. An open-bond phase is
pure gauge and measures no magnetic moment; the contact kernel is not
a Coulomb kernel. On the complete periodic ring, a genuine loop phase
has gauge-invariant current and total curvature,
\[
 E_0'=\langle\partial_\phi H\rangle,\qquad
 E_0''=\langle\partial_\phi^2H\rangle
 -2\sum_{n\ne0}\frac{|\langle n|\partial_\phi H|0\rangle|^2}{E_n-E_0},
\]
under the isolated-ground hypothesis. Degenerate free and atomic controls
lie outside that formula. The calculation retains all $256$ Fock states
but does not identify a photon or physical Lorentz spin.

Clock and memory exclusions are similarly conditional. A frozen
real-operation grammar has a normalized trace-distance lower bound
$1/2$ for a specified nontrivial modular action of its nontracial
reference. Finite independent copies of the original native reference
also fail the required field modular-gap ratios. These do not exclude
the explicitly enlarged clock and control laws above. The earlier
pair/dark-reference encoding covers a fixed corner, not the whole spatial
field. Finite stationary observer references concern source/buffer
marginals at the stated repair rate and on their recurrent support;
delayed variants need their own construction, and growing exported
histories are not made stationary by these marginal references. Actively
corrected coherent memories can have flat logical energy and a fixed
core, so memory lifetime alone does not establish a moving massive
particle. Properties proved on distinct carriers require an explicit
intertwiner before they can be combined.

\paragraph{Physical target and remaining obligations.}
The target is an interacting quantum field theory with gapless photons
and experimentally matched massive sectors, not a positive global mass
gap. A gapless photon rules out such a global gap. An exact
nontrivial positive-energy conformal theory in a flat-space vacuum
representation with unbroken unitary dilations also cannot have a
positive flat-space vacuum mass gap: for a spectral value
$E>0$ and proposed gap $g>0$, scale covariance also puts $g/2$ in the
spectrum. This does not exclude a gap in scaling dimensions or discrete
finite-radius cylinder levels; the cylinder radius supplies an energy
scale. Conformal behavior may describe a critical or short-distance
regime, but a deformation or symmetry-breaking mechanism responsible for
massive flat-space sectors must then be specified and implemented.

The finite results leave concrete physical joins. A localized defect must
carry the protected charge and the same measured field observables.
Spatial refinement must control the additional lattice modes and yield
the required Lorentz propagation, while keeping the interactions and
reference state. The electromagnetic field and gauge--Higgs interactions
must be dynamical, with charge constraints, detector coupling and their
infrared behavior treated on that same theory. Massive excitations need
a common energy and clock normalization, including the relation
$E''(0)=c^2/E_0$ for a relativistic branch with $E_0>0$, together with the
appropriate stress and proper-time relations. The neutrino sector and
other measured particle properties require their own matching tests.
Neither a successful finite source compilation nor a calibrated spectrum
alone discharges these obligations.
