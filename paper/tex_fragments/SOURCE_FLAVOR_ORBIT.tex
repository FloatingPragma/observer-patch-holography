\section{Flavor from a specified current-dependent memory}
\label{sec:source-flavor-orbit}

Reading a complete repair and memory specification fixes its predictions,
including predictions that disagree with particle observations. Consider the
declared module
\(M=\Lambda^2\mathbb C^5\oplus\Lambda^4\mathbb C^5\), its twelve native
current generators \(G_a\), and a gauge-trivial family factor
\(F=\mathbb C^3\). The three families, spin/exterior realization, canonical
kinetic form \(I_{45}\), and neutral Higgs background \(H=(0,1)\) are inputs.
They do not follow from a defect multiplicity. The calculation below uses no
empirical Yukawa coefficients; this runtime fact makes no claim about
target-independent design history.

\begin{proposition}[Complete family orbit]
\label{prop:source-flavor-orbit}
Every unitary on \(M\otimes F\) preserving the same twelve currents and
canonical kinetic form belongs to \(\mathrm U(3)^5\), with one common
family unitary on the two components of the quark weak doublet.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The primitive charge, an exact linear combination of the native currents,
has eigenvalues \((-4,-3,1,2,6)\) with multiplicities \((3,2,6,3,1)\).
Any commuting operator preserves these five blocks. The complete
commutator equations within them have ranks \((8,3,35,8,0)\) in
\((9,4,36,9,1)\) complex unknowns. Each kernel consists precisely of the
identity. Therefore
\begin{equation}
 \{G_a\}'=\mathbb C^5,\qquad
 \{G_a\otimes I_3\}'=\bigoplus_{q=-4,-3,1,2,6}M_3(\mathbb C).
 \label{eq:source-flavor-commutant}
\end{equation}
Taking the unitary elements proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

Let \(S_q\) be these five unitaries. In the all-left Weyl convention the
symmetric pairing kernel transforms as \(K\mapsto S^tKS\); its directed
Higgs block contains \(\overline Y\). Consequently
\begin{equation}
 Y_u'=S_1^\dagger Y_u\overline S_{-4},\qquad
 Y_d'=S_1^\dagger Y_d\overline S_2,\qquad
 Y_e'=S_{-3}^\dagger Y_e\overline S_6.
 \label{eq:source-flavor-transform}
\end{equation}
Each singular spectrum is invariant. Writing \(H_f=Y_fY_f^\dagger\),
both quark matrices undergo the same similarity by \(S_1\), preserving
their spectral-projector overlaps and \(\operatorname{tr}[H_u,H_d]^3\).
A specified law with \(Y_u=Y_d=Y_e\) cannot acquire distinct hierarchies
or physical quark mixing through these embeddings. The complete Higgs
invariant spaces nevertheless permit three arbitrary complex matrices;
current covariance alone does not select their 27 complex entries.

\subsection{Stationary coefficients from complete records}
\label{subsec:source-flavor-memory}

\[
 M_0=\cos(\theta\sqrt{\mathcal C}),\qquad
 M_a=iF_a\frac{\sin(\theta\sqrt{\mathcal C})}{\sqrt{\mathcal C}}.
\]
Their Gram sum is the identity. The recorded jump/no-jump grouping keeps
both outcomes. On the invariant left and right Higgs domains its effects
are scalar, so the two-leg probabilities factorize even for entangled
inputs. Combining them with the complete native repair records gives the

\begin{equation}
 p=\frac1{12}\sin^2(\theta\sqrt{c_L}),\qquad
 q=\frac1{6}\sin^2(\theta\sqrt{c_R}),\qquad
 (c_L,c_R)=
 \begin{cases}
 (25/8,4),&u,\\

 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:source-flavor-gates}

The angle, independent current legs, coherent feedback and reset rule are

Each feedback pulse uses \(X\otimes P_j\), where \(X\) is the Pauli flip
of a two-state auxiliary register.

The twelve memory ports reset to port 2. Set \(a=p(1-q)\), \(b=pq\) and
\(D=1-a^2+ab\). Within the recurrent ports, the nonreset transitions are

and \(9\leftrightarrow10\) with probability \(a\). All other weight

has reset probability at least \(1-p>0\), hence a unique stationary law:
\begin{equation}
 \pi_2=\frac{1-a}{D},\quad \pi_3=a\pi_2,\quad
 \pi_{10}=\frac{ab\pi_2}{1-a^2},\quad \pi_9=a\pi_{10}.
 \label{eq:source-flavor-stationary}
\end{equation}
All other stationary weights vanish. To specify the geometric projectors,
put \(A=(5-\sqrt5)/10\), \(B=(5+\sqrt5)/10\), and
\[
 v_2=(\sqrt A,\sqrt B,0)^t,\quad
 v_3=(\sqrt A,-\sqrt B,0)^t,\quad
 v_{10}=(\sqrt B,0,\sqrt A)^t.
\]
Then \(P_j=v_jv_j^t\) and \(P_9=P_{10}\). The unconditional stationary
coefficient is exactly
\begin{equation}
 Y(p,q)=\frac{(1-a)P_2+a(1-a)P_3+abP_{10}}{D}.
 \label{eq:source-flavor-Y}
\end{equation}

The four pulse duties are unconditional, C-event, E-event and joint event.
Their coefficients are

For \(0<p,q<1\), the traces and ranks are

\subsection{Exact histories and finite-memory noise}
\label{subsec:source-flavor-histories}

\begin{proposition}[History grading and unread-channel obstruction]
\label{prop:source-flavor-histories}
Every retained feedback history commutes with \(X\otimes I_3\) and is fixed
by the antiunitary \(\Theta=(Z\otimes I_3)K\), where \(K\) is conjugation
in the declared source basis. If its spectrum excludes \(-1\), its
principal Hermitian generator has the exact form
\begin{equation}
 H=I_2\otimes iA+X\otimes S,\qquad
 A^t=-A,\quad S^t=S,\quad A,S\text{ real}.
 \label{eq:source-flavor-history-grading}
\end{equation}
For \(0<p,q<1\), nonidentity pulse step
\(\epsilon\notin2\pi\mathbb Z\), and every finite \(N\ge2\), the unread
feedback channel from any independent initial memory preparation is
nonunitary, for all four duties.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Each pulse \(U_j=e^{-i\epsilon X\otimes P_j}\) obeys the two symmetries
because \(P_j\) is real and \(ZXZ=-X\). Products preserve both identities
in their original order. The principal logarithm consequently commutes
with \(X\) and satisfies \(\Theta H\Theta^{-1}=-H\), giving
\eqref{eq:source-flavor-history-grading}. The sufficient condition
\(\sum_j|\epsilon_j|<2\) follows from \(\|U-I\|<2\).

For the second assertion use the normalized Choi state
\(J_N=\sum_h w_h|U_h\rangle\!\rangle\langle\!\langle U_h|/6\)
of the complete history sum. Distinct active projectors satisfy
\(\operatorname{tr}(P_iP_j)=1/5\), so the normalized trace overlaps are
\(t_0=(2+\cos\epsilon)/3\) for idle versus active and
\(t_1=(7+8\cos\epsilon)/15\) for distinct active pulses.
For unconditional duty, compare all C-events false with the history whose
only C-event is at the final step, with its E-event false. The common
prefix resets to port 2, and the final pulses are \(U_2,U_3\). For the
other duties use an all-inactive prefix and one final active event. Their
positive pairwise contributions to the full Choi purity defect give
\begin{equation}
 1-\operatorname{tr}J_N^2\ge
 \begin{cases}
 2a(1-p)^{2N-1}(1-t_1^2),&\text{unconditional},\\
 2d_D[(1-p)(1-q)]^{2N-1}(1-t_0^2),&D=C,E,CE,
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:source-flavor-finite-noise}
\end{equation}
where \(d_C=a,d_E=(1-p)q,d_{CE}=b\). These terms are strictly positive;
no history is conditioned away.
\end{proof}

If \(\delta=1-\operatorname{tr}J_N^2\), the distance from every unitary
channel in the unhalved diamond norm is at least
\(2(1-\sqrt{1-\delta})\ge\delta\). This follows by testing a normalized
maximally entangled input and bounding every pure-state overlap of its
output by \(\lambda_{\max}(J_N)\le\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}J_N^2}\).
The finite-history lower bounds decay with \(N\); they do not assert an
asymptotic noise floor or contradict the weak-pulse estimate above.

For one stationary step the exact defect is
\(2w_0(1-w_0)(1-t_0^2)+[(1-w_0)^2-w_2^2-w_3^2-w_{10}^2](1-t_1^2)\),
where \(w_0\) is the idle weight and \(w_j\) sums every record applying
\(P_j\). This provides rationally enclosed witnesses for all three
channels and four duties. The full classical-memory/quantum-feedback channel has
operator-space dimension \(432\), but its two forbidden output memory
ports give rank at most \(360\). It therefore has no matrix logarithm.
Restricting to recurrent ports changes the domain and leaves the
eigenvalue \(-a\) on identity feedback blocks, excluding the standard
principal logarithm on the negative-axis cut.

The diagonal term \(iA\) in
\eqref{eq:source-flavor-history-grading} need not vanish after averaging
histories. For the Hilbert--Schmidt projection of the complete two-step
channel onto Hamiltonian commutators, at \(p=1/12,q=1/6\),
\(\cos\epsilon=99/101\), and \(\sin\epsilon=20/101\), exact multiplication
gives \(A_{02}=1656589(\sqrt5-1)/899081864640>0\). This projection is a
coherent diagnostic, not an exact channel generator.
The graded off-diagonal coefficient \(S\) remains real, but in the
\(X\) eigenbasis the block \(S+iA\) can be complex Hermitian. For example
\(K_u=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,3)\) and
\(K_d=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1&i\\1&2&1\\-i&1&3\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)
both occur in the containing graded class and satisfy
\(\operatorname{tr}[K_uK_u^\dagger,K_dK_d^\dagger]^3=-34560i\).
This is a counterexample to a universal CP inference from that symmetry;
it is not a claim that these blocks are reached by the controller or are
physical Yukawa matrices. The auxiliary antiunitary is not identified
with physical CP. Its covariance extends to the reduced memory and
feedback channel, whereas the complex fine-current output legs require
a separately specified antiunitary map.
