\section{Certified defect spectra and their conserved currents}
\label{sec:certified-defect-currents}

Consider the complete fermion Fock space on the open cube
$\Lambda_R=\{-R,\ldots,R\}^3$, $R\geq1$, with $V=|\Lambda_R|$ and two
modes per site.  With canonical anticommutation relations, define
\begin{align}
 H&=\sum_{x,y}c_x^\dagger h_{xy}c_y+\mathcal W,
 &\mathcal W&=U\sum_x(n_{x\uparrow}-\tfrac12)
                         (n_{x\downarrow}-\tfrac12),\label{eq:cdc-source}\\
 h_{x,x+e_j}&=\frac{iv}{2a}\sigma_j,
 &h_{xx}&=m\sum_j(-1)^{x_j}u_j(x)\sigma_j,\qquad h_{00}=0.
 \nonumber
\end{align}
Reverse hopping blocks are adjoints; other blocks vanish.
Here $a,v,m,U>0$ are supplied parameters.  The unit direction $u(x)$ is
radial, or is a specified sphere map of degree $k\neq0$ obtained from
stereographic $w\mapsto w^{|k|}$ and the appropriate target orientation.
This defines a Hamiltonian with a pinned mass texture; it assumes neither
a formation mechanism nor an identification of winding with particle charge.

\begin{proposition}[Complete finite spectral certificate]
\label{prop:cdc-spectral-certificate}
Let $h_q=h_q^\dagger$ approximate the full $2V$-dimensional matrix $h$,
let $Q$ be square, and let $\mathcal L=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,
\ldots,\lambda_{2V})$ have ordered real entries.  Suppose
\[
 \|h-h_q\|\leq s,\quad
 \|Q^\dagger Q-I\|\leq\gamma<1,\quad
 \|h_qQ-Q\mathcal L\|\leq r,\quad \|\mathcal L\|\leq L.
\]
Every ordered eigenvalue of $h$ lies in $[\lambda_j-\delta,\lambda_j+\delta]$,
where
\begin{equation}
 \delta=s+\frac{r+2L(1+\gamma)\gamma}{1-\gamma}.
 \label{eq:cdc-spectral-error}
\end{equation}
If $u(-x)=-u(x)$ and exactly two such intervals contain zero, then
$\ker h$ has dimension two and is inversion-even.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $G=Q^\dagger Q$ and $Q_0=QG^{-1/2}$.  This is a square unitary matrix.
The identity
$h_qQ_0-Q_0\mathcal L=(h_qQ-Q\mathcal L)G^{-1/2}
+Q[\mathcal L,G^{-1/2}]$, together with
$\|G^{-1/2}\|\leq(1-\gamma)^{-1}$,
$\|G^{-1/2}-I\|\leq\gamma/(1-\gamma)$ and $\|Q\|\leq1+\gamma$,
gives \eqref{eq:cdc-spectral-error}.  Weyl's inequality then includes every
eigenvalue and multiplicity.  Spatial inversion $\mathcal R$ anticommutes
with $h$.  Its trace is two, from the two origin modes, so rank-nullity gives
$\dim\ker h|_{\mathcal R=+1}-\dim\ker h|_{\mathcal R=-1}=2$.
The two zero-containing intervals exhaust this lower bound.
\end{proof}

Rational intervals for the sphere-map coefficients bound $s$; exact dyadic
Gram and residual calculations bound $\gamma,r$.  Numerical eigenvectors
only propose $Q$.  At $a=v=1$, the cases $R=1,2,3$, $k=1,2,3,4$, $m=1,4$,
and the additional cases $R=4,5$, $k=1,3$, $m=1$ give 28 complete matrices:
16 certified two-dimensional kernels, six invertible matrices and six
unresolved kernel counts.  A six-level degree-three window contains two
exact zeros and four nonzero levels, rather than six protected zero modes.

Let $d>0$ be the distance of all remaining spectral intervals from zero.
The staggered particle-hole map and its product with inversion give a
symplectic zero frame $W$ whose site blocks satisfy
$W_x^\dagger W_x=\rho_x I_2$, $\sum_x\rho_x=1$.
Filling every negative mode and allowing both zero-mode occupations gives
a four-dimensional free ground space, with projector $P$ and particle
numbers $V-1,V,V,V+1$.  The filled-sea Wick calculation yields
\begin{equation}
 P\mathcal WP=C+U\mathcal I
       \bigl(n_1n_2-\tfrac12(n_1+n_2)\bigr),\qquad
 \mathcal I=\sum_x\rho_x^2\geq V^{-1}.
 \label{eq:cdc-contact-compression}
\end{equation}
For $w=UV/4<d/4$, the complementary Feshbach inverse bounds each of the
four low-energy corrections by $\varepsilon=w^2/(d-2w)$ and the associated
projector angle by $t=w/(d-2w)$.  Indeed, a low eigenvalue is within $w$
of the free ground energy, so the complementary block is at least $d-2w$;
its Schur correction has norm at most $w^2/(d-2w)$.  Thus, if
$U\mathcal I_{\rm low}/2-2\varepsilon>0$ and $3t<1/2$, the exact ground
is a Kramers pair at particle number $V$.  The unique neighboring charged
states have equal gap
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_c\in[U\mathcal I_{\rm low}/2-2\varepsilon,
                  U/2+2\varepsilon].
 \label{eq:cdc-charged-gap}
\end{equation}
Their addition/removal residue matrices have rank two and operator error
at most $3t$ from $WW^\dagger/2$.  The full Fock complement enters these
bounds; its matrix need not be materialized.  Applying
$U\in\{2^{-16},2^{-24},2^{-40}\}$ gives 84 cases, of which 34 satisfy
these sufficient inequalities.  Failure of an inequality
is an inconclusive certificate, not a negative physical mass.

\begin{proposition}[All conserved onsite-even currents]
\label{prop:cdc-local-currents}
For \eqref{eq:cdc-source} with the specified nonzero textures, every
conserved sum of even Hermitian onsite operators, modulo constants, lies
in $\mathfrak{su}(2)_\eta$, generated by
\begin{equation}
 \eta_z=(\mathcal N-V)/2,\qquad
 \eta_+=\sum_x D_xc_{x\uparrow}^\dagger c_{x\downarrow}^\dagger,
 \qquad \eta_-=\eta_+^\dagger,\quad D_x=(-1)^{\sum_jx_j},
 \label{eq:cdc-eta}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal N$ is total particle number.  Its number-preserving
subalgebra is $\mathfrak u(1)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The full even Hermitian onsite algebra has basis $I,n-1$, three
$c^\dagger\sigma_jc$, two pair quadratures, and $(-1)^n$.  On each edge,
the complete sixteen-state commutator equation has rank eight in sixteen
coefficients.  Its kernel forces both quartic coefficients to zero,
equal number coefficients, opposite pair coefficients, and spin transport
$A_{x+e_j}=\sigma_jA_x\sigma_j$.  Different edge commutators have distinct
odd--odd tensor supports and cannot cancel.  Pauli conjugations commute,
so transport back to the origin reduces the mass condition to
$[b\cdot\sigma,u(x)\cdot\sigma]=0$.  The transported directions at $e_1,e_3$
are nonparallel, forcing $b=0$.  The surviving operators are exactly
\eqref{eq:cdc-eta}.  Their commutators follow from the full anticommutation
relations.  Traceless onsite mass and centered contact commute with them.
\end{proof}

On the isolated interacting quartet, $\eta_\pm$ annihilate both ground
vectors $G_a$: there are no same-energy states at $V\pm2$.  If $C_-$ is
the normalized state at $V-1$, absence of a same-energy state at $V-3$
gives $\eta_-C_-=0$ and $\|\eta_+C_-\|^2=-2\langle\eta_z\rangle=1$.
Choosing $C_+=\eta_+C_-$ therefore constructs an exact current and
Hamiltonian intertwiner: two eta singlets and one eta doublet.  The full
field operators obey the same symmetry.  Define
$(A_+(x))_{sa}=\langle C_+|c_{xs}^\dagger|G_a\rangle$ and
$(A_-(x))_{sa}=\langle C_-|c_{xs}|G_a\rangle$.  Their explicit identity is
\begin{equation}
 A_+(x)=D_x i\sigma_2 A_-(x).
 \label{eq:cdc-pole-intertwiner}
\end{equation}
Indeed $[\eta_-,c_{x\uparrow}^\dagger]=D_xc_{x\downarrow}$ and
$[\eta_-,c_{x\downarrow}^\dagger]=-D_xc_{x\uparrow}$, while $\eta_-G_a=0$.

This exact eta action differs from a local spin assignment.  For the
radial witness $R=a=v=1$, $m=64$, $U=1/65536$, let $P_{\rm low}$ project
onto the entire interacting quartet and $S_j=c_0^\dagger\sigma_jc_0$.
The source antiunitary $\mathcal R D(i\sigma_2)K$ reverses $S_j$ and
preserves each unique charged state.  Number conservation then gives
$P_{\rm low}S_jC_\pm=0$.  Since $S_j^2=1-(n_0-1)^2$, the zero-frame
center weight $\alpha\leq2904/2905$ and projector bound
$t=27/16252874$ imply
\begin{equation}
 \|(1-P_{\rm low})S_jC_\pm\|^2
 \geq\frac{1-(2904/2905)^2}{2}-\frac{27}{16252874}
 >\frac1{4096}.
 \label{eq:cdc-spin-leakage}
\end{equation}
Here the weight bound follows from the origin coupling
$V_0^\dagger V_0=3I_2/2$ and complementary one-body norm at most $66$;
the charged reference has center parity $(1+\alpha^2)/2$ and its actual
effect differs by at most $t$.

No nonzero homomorphism embeds $\mathfrak{su}(3)$ into this local
$\mathfrak{su}(2)_\eta$: simplicity would require an injection.
Identifying a nontrivial weak algebra with eta also leaves no nonconstant
commuting local hypercharge generator.  This excludes a faithful Standard
Model current action within the classified domain.  It excludes neither
nonlocal emergent currents nor broken currents coupled to dynamical links.
Eta is not identified with electroweak, spatial or Lorentz spin.
Finite excitation gaps supply no moving-defect rest masses, texture selection,
repair protection or interacting continuum limit.  Exact matrix and rational
certificates are supplied in \path{model/certified_winding_core/} and
\path{model/radial_current_map/}, with independent complete-Fock controls.
