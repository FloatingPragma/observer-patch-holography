\paragraph{Controlled spin exchange and a growing-contact free boundary.}
In the exact onsite spin frame, the centered finite-graph source is
\[
 K=T+UD-\frac U2N,\qquad
 T=\sum_{xy,s}t_{xy}(c_{xs}^{\dagger}c_{ys}+c_{ys}^{\dagger}c_{xs}),
 \qquad t_{xy}=\pm\frac c2.
\]
For $V$ sites and edge set $E$, put $\tau=c|E|\geq\|T\|$.
In the half-filled sector, let $P$ project onto one particle at every
site, $Q=I-P$, and $H=K+UV/2=T+UD$. Then $PTP=0$, $QUDQ\geq UQ$,
and $(UD)TP=UTP$. The last identity retains the actual intermediate
doublon--hole configurations. Exact CAR multiplication gives
\[
 H_{\rm spin}=-\frac1U PT^2P
  =\sum_{xy}\frac{4t_{xy}^2}{U}
           \left(S_x\mathbin{\cdot}S_y-\frac14\right).
\]
The $S_x$ are hidden onsite spins; this is not an assignment of physical
Lorentz spin. Hopping signs cancel at this order, not necessarily at
higher orders.

For $U>\tau$, exactly $2^V$ eigenvalues of $H$ are nonpositive and
all the others are at least $U-\tau$. With $B=QTP$ and
$A=QHQ\geq U-\tau$, the Feshbach operator at $z\leq0$ is
$F(z)=-B^{\dagger}(A-z)^{-1}B$. Its low roots lie in
$[-\tau^2/(U-\tau),0]$, and the resolvent identity yields
\[
 \|F(z)-H_{\rm spin}\|\leq\frac{\tau^3}{(U-\tau)^2}.
\]
The ordered eigenvalues of $F(z)$ are nonincreasing in $z$, so their
crossings with $z$ pair every low source eigenvalue with its ordered
spin eigenvalue within this error, without requiring gaps between
spin levels.

A stronger condition gives a dynamical statement on every input spin
state. For $r=\tau/U\leq1/4$, the low spectral subspace is the graph
of $X:P\to Q$, with
\[
 \|X\|\leq\frac r{\sqrt{1-2r}},\qquad
 W=(P+X)(I+X^{\dagger}X)^{-1/2},\qquad \|W-P\|\leq2r.
\]
The Sylvester integral for its $Q$ component bounds the angle by
$r/(1-r)$; the graph Riccati equation and $(UD)TP=UTP$ then give
\[
 \|W^{\dagger}HW-H_{\rm spin}\|\leq\frac{3\tau^3}{U^2}.
\]
Consequently, including all leakage into $Q$,
\[
 \big\|e^{-itH}P-Pe^{-itH_{\rm spin}}\big\|
 \leq\min\left(2,\frac{4\tau}{U}
                   +\frac{3|t|\tau^3}{U^2}\right).       \tag{SC1}
\]
On the full Fock carrier,
$K+UV/2=T+(U/2)\sum_x(n_x-1)^2$.
Every charged sector $N\ne V$ is therefore separated from the global
ground by at least $U/2-\tau>0$ when $U>2\tau$; every global ground
has $N=V$. No quantitative neutral gap is inferred.

At fixed finite graph, $U=c^2/J$, with $J>0$ fixed and $c\to\infty$,
makes (SC1) tend to zero and retains the interacting Heisenberg
exchange $J$. Adjacent bond terms do not commute. A dimer takes
$|\uparrow,\downarrow\rangle$ to a maximally entangled spin state at
$t=\pi/(2J)$. For $c=128$, $U=16384$, $J=1$, its full-source error
is less than $5/64$, preserving a Bell-state fidelity above $1/2$.
This is a controlled interacting finite-graph limit. For a fixed
physical cubic box with $L$ sites per side, however,
$|E|=3L^2(L-1)$ and $c=vL$ give $\tau=O(L^4)$.
The displayed global errors vanish for $U\asymp L^p$ with $p>6$,
where $J=O(L^{2-p})$ tends to zero. The fixed-$J$ scaling fails this
volume-dependent hypothesis. That failure is a limitation of the
bound, not a no-go theorem for another local expansion.

\paragraph{An unbounded coupling class with a free charged limit.}
The centered-ground composite $d=(2n_{\rm opposite}-1)c$ has the
source-derived occupation bound $\Delta\leq11(U/c)^2/8$.
For either positive-energy charge branch, uniformly over all unit
lattice smearings, the existing Gaussian estimate gives
\begin{align*}
 \|1_{[0,R]}(L_a)d(f)\Omega_a\|^2&\leq M_a(R),\\
 M_a(R)&\leq \min\left\{1,e^{R^2/(2c^2\delta^2)}
 \left[1536\delta^6+\frac{3U}{c\delta}
 +624\sqrt{\frac{11}{8}}\frac Uc e^{81/(2\delta^2)}\right]\right\}.
\end{align*}
For kinetic packets bounded by $P$, the two vacuum Duhamel errors
are at most
\[
 \frac U2\left[|t|\sqrt{M_a(R)}+
                       \frac{2+|t|P}{R}\right]\|f\|.
\]
Taking $\delta=9/\sqrt{\log c}$ and $R=\sqrt c$ proves
\[
 U=o\big((\log c)^{3/2}\big)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad U^2 M_a(\sqrt c)\to0,
 \qquad U/\sqrt c\to0.                                  \tag{SC2}
\]
The initial positive-band annihilator and negative-band creator
bounds vanish by the same split, determining the pure free
filled-sea initial state.

Unbounded $U$ does not satisfy the earlier uniform time-Lipschitz
hypothesis. Instead, take cluster states of the universal bounded
algebra of all-time, all-valley smeared fields. Its state space is
weak-star compact without assuming time continuity. Equal-time CAR

finite-propagation estimate is uniform in the onsite coupling, so
each difference from the free field graded-commutes with the initial
algebra outside a bounded region; free Weyl evolution itself enlarges

that outside algebra: compact support of a vector orthogonal to its
one-particle ranges would give an entire Fourier transform, whereas
continuation around a simple square-root branch point of the Weyl

 U_k\sqrt{M_k(R_k)}<\frac{56}{k},\qquad U_k/R_k=1/k.

Unit-time unit-packet vacuum errors are bounded by $\min(2,30/k)$.
