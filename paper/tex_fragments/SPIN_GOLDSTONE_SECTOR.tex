\paragraph{An ordered, gapless internal-spin sector.}
The fixed-spacing cubic limit with
$c=J|E|^4$, $U=J|E|^8$ has
$H_J=J\sum_{\langle xy\rangle}(\mathbf S_x\cdot\mathbf S_y-1/4)$,
where $\mathbf S$ is the internal spin-$1/2$ operator.
The finite-torus Duhamel bound of Dyson--Lieb--Simon
(\emph{J.\ Stat.\ Phys.}\ \textbf{18}, 335--383, 1978,
\href{https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01106729}{doi:10.1007/BF01106729}),
with its factor-two zero-temperature spectral limit, gives
$\chi(k)\le[4JE(k+Q)]^{-1}$. Combined with the exact symmetric-ground
first moment $2JeE(k)/9$, this gives the infrared bound used by
Kennedy--Lieb--Shastry
(\emph{J.\ Stat.\ Phys.}\ \textbf{53}, 1019--1030, 1988,
\href{https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01023854}{doi:10.1007/BF01023854}). The bound applies
with positive isotropic exchange and even periodic cubic boundaries.
An exact rational upper sum, including the singular origin cell, gives
\[
 I=\frac1{\pi^3}\int_{[0,\pi]^3}
 \left(\frac{\sum_i\cos x_i}{3}\right)_+
 \sqrt{\frac{3+\sum_i\cos x_i}{3-\sum_i\cos x_i}}\,d^3x
 <0.382612<\frac25.
\]
The energy sum rule and the N\'eel trial bound $e\ge3/4$ then give
staggered weight $b\ge e/9-\sqrt{e/18}\,I>1/1000$.
The finite-torus infrared sums converge by an explicit integrable
$1/|k-Q|$ bound, so the full finite-volume staggered averages have the
same positive lower limit. Biasing the invariant ground by $1+M$,
then projecting its at-most $3J/N$ energy excess into
$[0,J/\sqrt N]$, constructs an ordered ground.
Koma's maximal-magnetization and pure-ground theorems
(\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.09018v4}{arXiv:1712.09018v4},
Theorems~2.1--2.2) therefore apply with $m_s\ge m_0=1/1000$.

For a smooth, $L^2$-normalized transverse momentum packet supported in
$p/2\le |k|\le p\le1$, the susceptibility bound and Ward identity,
with the exact current $j_{xy}^a=J(\mathbf S_x\mathbin\times\mathbf S_y)^a$,
give a smooth energy-filtered vector $\Psi_p$ satisfying
\[
 \|\Psi_p\|^2\ge\frac{m_0^2p}{96},\qquad
 \operatorname{supp}\mu_{\Psi_p}
 \subset\left(\frac{m_0Jp}{16},\frac{64Jp}{m_0^2}\right),\qquad
 \frac{\langle\Psi_p,H\Psi_p\rangle}{\|\Psi_p\|^2}
 \le\frac{48Jp}{m_0^2}.
\]
The susceptibility and filtered thermodynamic-limit input is specified
in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.04970v2}{Koma's 2021 analysis},
Eqs.~(3.4), (4.1), (5.3)--(5.12).
The spectral support excludes the entire zero-energy subspace;
positive spectrum consequently approaches zero in the infinite-volume
field-selected ground. A pure ordered gapless ground also exists by
the cited theorem; purity of that particular field-selected state is
not inferred.
The complete-record source comparison transfers these selected initial
states and filtered correlations along the same growing-coupling
trajectory. No field is added to the source dynamics. This establishes
an accessible neutral internal-spin sector, without asserting that every
exact source-ground subsequence is ordered.
The result concerns a fixed-spacing interacting spin system; neither
a sharp particle pole, Lorentz dispersion, measured particle identity,
relativistic rest mass, nor Einstein dynamics follows.
