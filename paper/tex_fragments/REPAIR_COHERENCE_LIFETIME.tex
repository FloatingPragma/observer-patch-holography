\paragraph{Complete-record coherence lifetimes.}
The bare binary endpoint instrument admits a quantitative strengthening of
its finite fixed-algebra classification. For a connected graph with positive
normalized seam weights $q_e$ and fair endpoint coins, let
$D_x=1_S(x)-1_T(x)$ for two occupation configurations. The total absolute
coefficient of the matrix unit $|S\rangle\langle T|$, summed over every
classically retained record, is
\[
 r_C(S,T)=1-\frac12\sum_{ab}q_{ab}|D_a-D_b|.
\]
The E instrument refines these records and cannot increase this quantity.
Writing $A=\{D=1\}$ and $B=\{D=-1\}$ expresses the sum as the weights of
$\partial A$ and $\partial B$. If $\lambda$ is the least proper cut weight,
the off-diagonal entrywise norm contracts by $1-\lambda$ within a fixed
number sector and by $1-\lambda/2$ outside the full/empty cat block. Partial
injections preserve off-diagonality, so this estimate iterates with all
records retained. It also applies to classical history-dependent selectors
with a uniform conditional cut bound; coherent recovery is outside its domain.

For the actual thirty-seam graph, exact enumeration of all $4094$ proper
cuts and $531441$ ternary fields gives $\lambda=1/6$. Thus the factors are
$5/6$ and $11/12$. The first is attained by every one-particle off-diagonal
matrix unit under uniform C repair. A two-configuration superposition has
coherence at most $(5/6)^{26}<0.01$ after $26$ full repair ticks. These ticks
have no inferred conversion to physical time or particle mass.

Diagonal populations follow symmetric exclusion with edge rates $q_e/2$.
The weighted-graph spectral-gap theorem of Caputo, Liggett and Richthammer
(\emph{J.~Amer.~Math.~Soc.}~23 (2010), 831--851, Theorem~1.1 and
Eq.~(4.1)) is imported with its finite connected symmetric-rate premises
checked. The exact native adjacency polynomial is
$(x-5)(x+1)^5(x^2-5)^3$, giving
$g=(5-\sqrt5)/60>11/240$ in each nontrivial number sector. With
$d=\binom{12}{N}$,
\[
 \|\Phi_*^t(\rho)-I_N/d\|_1
 \leq\sqrt{d-1}(1-g)^t+(d-1)(5/6)^t.
\]
Here the stationary-state channel traces out the classical tape; the joint
source and tape need not mix. This supplies a faithful finite-sector reference and a convergence rate;
it does not prepare the centered Hubbard ground or select sector populations.

There is also a precise refinement boundary. On the periodic cubic graph
of spacing $a$, $V$ vertices and $3V$ edges, uniform C repair gives a
one-particle coherence factor $1-2/V$. A Poisson clock of rate $\nu$ and
fixed diffusion coefficient $D=\nu a^2/(6V)>0$ therefore gives
$\exp(-12Dt/a^2)$ for every off-diagonal matrix unit. For position pinching
$\Pi$, this gives the uniform trace-norm bound
$\|(\Phi_t\otimes\mathrm{id})(\rho-\Pi\rho)\|_1\leq2\exp(-12Dt/a^2)$,
including an untouched reference. It vanishes at each positive macroscopic time.
This bare, classically recorded diffusive family
does not retain reversible one-particle coherence; quantum memories, active
codes and separately derived Hamiltonian fields require their own analysis.
