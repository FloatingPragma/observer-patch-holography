\section{One local carrier for defect states, currents and mass coefficients}
\label{sec:defect-current-mass-bridge}

The radial defect operator admits a direct realization of the native matter
currents and a prescribed family mass operator on the same localized modes.
The construction is a choice of simulator carrier and coherent pairing
service. Its finite operator identity is exact; the choice is not claimed
to follow uniquely from overlap consistency.

Let $h$ be the radial operator on
$\ell^2(\Lambda)\otimes\mathbb C^2$, with its normalized kernel frame
$W:\mathbb C^2\to\ker h$. Use the strong texture domain, in which the
kernel has dimension two and the complementary spectrum has absolute
energy at least $d>0$. With
\[
 J=i\sigma_2,\qquad D_x=(-1)^{x_1+x_2+x_3},\qquad C=DJ,
\]
the source identities are
\[
 C^T=-C,\quad C^\dagger C=I,\quad hC=-C\overline h,
 \qquad hW=0,\quad W^\dagger W=I_2,\quad C\overline W=WJ.
\]
The last identity fixes the quaternion frame through evaluation at the
origin and its scalar normalization Gram matrix.

Choose the canonical anticommutation relation algebra on
$\ell^2(\Lambda)\otimes\mathbb C^2\otimes M_{15}\otimes\mathbb C^3$.
Here $M_{15}$ is the native matter representation, transported by its
actual source intertwiner; the three family copies are declared inputs.
For a symmetric all-left Higgs kernel $K\in M_{45}(\mathbb C)$, define
\[
 \Delta=C\otimes\overline K,\qquad
 B=\begin{pmatrix}h\otimes I&\Delta\\
                  \Delta^\dagger&-\overline h\otimes I\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad

\]
The antisymmetry of $\Delta$ makes it a valid local creation pairing.
Multiplication gives the exact isometric intertwiner
\begin{equation}\label{eq:defect-current-mass-intertwiner}
 BE=EB_0,\qquad
 B_0=\begin{pmatrix}0&J\otimes\overline K\\
                    (J\otimes\overline K)^\dagger&0\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Every singular value of $K$ therefore appears with both signs and twice
the multiplicity in the localized Nambu operator, including zero values.
Nambu coordinates duplicate annihilation and creation operators; they
introduce no physical mirror carrier.

For every actual anti-Hermitian native current $G$, the map $W\otimes I$
intertwines $I\otimes G$ with $I_2\otimes G$. In Nambu coordinates the
generator is $\operatorname{diag}(G,\overline G)$; Hermitian charges use

with matter under the uniform internal gauge action. A fixed broken vacuum does not
make every gauge generator commute with its mass operator. The resulting
map of canonical anticommutation relation algebras intertwines quadratic
Heisenberg evolution. On even states, tensoring with the normalized trace
on the orthogonal complement gives an exact state inclusion with
partial-trace recovery. This complement state is not a vacuum.

Use the positive family matrix $M(P)$ supplied by the memory construction,
whose eigenvalues are the retained charged-lepton coefficients

An explicit record service realizes the same Hamiltonian in a controlled

transition is independent of the spatial field. For the updated memory

\[
 H_r=H_0+\mathbf1_C(r)\,g L(P_j),
\]

mean $\overline H=H_0+L(gZ_C)$, precisely the Hamiltonian above.

\[
 \left\|\rho_N-e^{-iT\overline H}\rho_0e^{iT\overline H}\right\|_1

       +\left(\frac8{1-p}+4\right)\frac{M^2T^2}{N}.
\]

The full complement is retained. Indeed, with
$T=\operatorname{diag}(I,C^\dagger\otimes I)$,
\[

\]
