\section{Native populations and conditional thermal laws}
\label{sec:native-thermal-records}

The native population process, a reversible bath extension and a calibrated
free particle gas have separate dynamical laws. Their observation counts,
costs and temperature parameters are kept distinct. The finite constructions

\subsection{Stationary native populations and the thermal obstruction}

Consider the closed triangular cell with three loads $x_i\in\{0,\ldots,5\}$
and opposite face classes $k,-k\in\mathbb Z_6$. Its unchanged native
seam repair and register acceptance give $1296$ states and $7776$ original
seam/coin records, each attempted event having weight $1/6$. Rejected
twists retain their load repair, and no-operation records count as attempts.
The conserved total load is $M=\sum_i x_i$. The native cost
\[
 E(x,k)=5\sum_i x_i^2+2\bigl(C(k)+C(-k)\bigr),\qquad
 C=(0,5,8,10,8,5),
\]
does not increase on any positive transition of the row kernel $K$.

\begin{proposition}[Full-support equilibrium obstruction]
In every sector $1\leq M\leq14$, no full-support probability law is
stationary for $K$. In particular, $\exp(-E/T)/Z_M(T)$ is neither
stationary nor detailed-balanced for any finite $T>0$. The endpoint
sectors $M=0,15$ have identity dynamics on their six flux states.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The pointwise drift $d=KE-E$ is nonpositive. Each nontrivial sector has
an executed strict-descent edge, so $d(s)<0$ for some state $s$. A
stationary law would satisfy
$\sum_s p(s)d(s)=\langle pK,E\rangle-\langle p,E\rangle=0$,
contradicting full support. A strict-descent edge also has zero reverse
probability. The generic finite push/pull argument is kernel-checked in
\path{Lean/Dynamics/StationaryLyapunov.lean}; the source supplies its
exact drift premises.
\end{proof}

The positive-edge graph has $46$ closed classes: $36$ absorbing states
$(q,q,q,k)$ and ten three-state classes of balanced load permutations
with zero flux. Each three-state kernel is
$\frac12I_3+\frac16\mathbf1\mathbf1^T$. Thus all stationary measures form
the simplex of arbitrary mixtures of the $46$ class-uniform laws. There
are $66$ recurrent states and $1230$ transient states. Gibbs weights
restricted to this recurrent set are stationary at every positive
temperature, but their inter-class weights are an additional choice.
The classification and every original attempt record are reproduced by

\subsection{Exact absorption, lifetimes and boundary response}

$t(s)=\mathbb E_s\tau$, $g(s)=\mathbb E_s\tau^2$ and
$u_c(s)=\mathbb E_s[\tau\mathbf1_{X_\tau\in c}]$.

$t=g=u_c=0$. On transient states,
\[
 h_c=Kh_c,\qquad t=1+Kt,\qquad g=2t-1+Kg,\qquad u_c=h_c+Ku_c.

\subsection{A declared reversible bath and its cooling records}

A thermal extension can be constructed explicitly on the same states.
For $x\ne y$ set $s_{xy}=\max(K_{xy},K_{yx})$ and
$\Lambda=2\max_x\sum_{y\ne x}s_{xy}=12$.
The symmetric support adds $3924$ reverse edges to $4104$ native directed
nonidentity edges. It has $26$ connected components: the fourteen
nontrivial load sectors and twelve endpoint singletons. This reverse
support is necessary for full-support detailed balance; its rates are
declared choices.

For $0<q\leq1$ and $\beta=-\log(q)/2$, propose $y$ with probability
$s_{xy}/\Lambda$ and accept with probability
$\min\{1,q^{(E_y-E_x)/2}\}$. Keep rejected proposals and idle records.
The $17352$ complete records per attempt give
\[
 \frac{P_{xy}}{P_{yx}}=e^{-\beta(E_y-E_x)}.
\]
Hence each component has its Gibbs equilibrium, while component weights
are conserved and free. Exact rational checks use
$q=1,3/4,1/2,1/4$. The construction is a bath extension; the obstruction
for unchanged native repair is unaffected.

For $p'=pP$, let $Q=\sum_{xy}p_xP_{xy}(E_y-E_x)$ be heat into the
system and let $S$ use natural logarithms. Detailed balance gives
\[
 S(p')-S(p)-\beta Q
 =\sum_{xy}p_xP_{xy}\log\frac{p_xP_{xy}}{p'_yP_{yx}}\geq0.
\]
The right side is relative entropy between normalized forward and
reverse joint laws. Accepted records reverse their endpoints; rejected
proposal labels and idle marks are retained. The identity therefore
holds for the complete marked law as well.

attempts per setting, retaining full state laws and finite-time departures

\[
 \Pi_{g,n}=\prod_{\ell\in L_g\setminus\{n\}}
