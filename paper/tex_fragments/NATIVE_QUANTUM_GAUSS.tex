\subsection{A finite local Gauss law for the native currents}
\label{sec:native-quantum-gauss}

The native fifteen-state matter representation decomposes into invariant
blocks $V_r$ of dimensions $6,3,3,2,1$. A declared finite link design
preserves those blocks. For an oriented edge $x\to y$, set
\[
 \mathcal L_{xy}=\mathbb C|0\rangle\oplus
       \bigoplus_r(V_{r,x}^*\otimes V_{r,y}),
 \qquad U_{ab}=|\bar a,b\rangle\langle0|,
\]
where $U_{ab}=0$ when $a,b$ belong to different blocks. The link dimension
is $1+36+9+9+4+1=60$. For any native anti-Hermitian generator $T$, its
endpoint generators are $0\oplus\bigoplus_r(-T_r^T\otimes I)$ and
$0\oplus\bigoplus_r(I\otimes T_r)$. Adding the local fermion charge
$d\Gamma_x(T)$ defines $G_x(T)$ on the complete Fock and link space.

For real $J$, the same finite Hamiltonian transports matter and compensating
link charge:
\[
 H_{xy}=J\sum_{a,b}c_{xa}^\dagger U_{ab}c_{yb}
         +\mathrm{h.c.}+\epsilon(I-|0\rangle\langle0|),
 \qquad [G_v(T),H_{xy}]=0,\quad\epsilon\geq0.
\]
Indeed, the two link commutators are
$[L_x(T),U_{ab}]=-\sum_iT_{ai}U_{ib}$ and
$[L_y(T),U_{ab}]=\sum_jT_{jb}U_{aj}$. They cancel the creation and
annihilation commutators of $d\Gamma(T)$ after relabeling the summed
indices. The link-energy projector commutes with both generators.
This proves the identity for all occupation sectors and finite sums of
edges. It also proves preservation of the Gauss kernel by evolution and
by complete instruments whose Kraus operators commute with the generators.

For an explicit nontrivial sector, supply a static dual reference at $y$.
Within one block of dimension $d$, the vectors
\[
 A=\sum_b|y,b\rangle|\bar b\rangle|0\rangle,
 \qquad B=\sum_{a,b}|x,a\rangle|\bar b\rangle|\bar a,b\rangle
\]
are Gauss singlets, with norms $\sqrt d$ and $d$.
The Hamiltonian sends $A$ to $JB$ and $B$ to $dJA+\epsilon B$.
Its orthonormal two-state restriction is
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&J\sqrt d\\J\sqrt d&\epsilon\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
All twelve native generators are checked on every block. Exact Cayley
steps and complete endpoint readouts retain all eighty histories obtained
from a five-outcome initial block measurement and four binary observations.
The change of this same Hamiltonian's energy under each observation is
included as detector work; the detector reservoir is a supplied service.

reference, coherent hopping and readout are chosen inputs, with no asserted
compilation into bare C/E repair. The localized compact band has a separate
