\paragraph{The centered bounded-contact limit is a free field theory.}
The preceding centered construction admits a stronger conclusion.
For $c(a)=v/a$, fixed $v>0$ and $0\le U(a)\le U_{\max}<\infty$, every
retained continuum net of the canonical charged fields is the free
eight-valley Weyl vacuum net. This determines every order of the field hierarchy. Its particles and holes have
$E(p)=v|p|$ and zero rest masses. These modes are not identified with
measured species; the bounded-contact branch does not supply the
required interacting theory or its massive sectors.

The proof begins with the exact canonical composite
$d=(2n_{\rm opposite}-1)c$ and the centered occupation bound
$\Delta\le11u^2/8$, where $u=U/c$. At microscopic time $s/c$, write
$F_c(s;f)=\omega(\{\tau_{s/c}(d(f)),d(f)^*\})$, and let $F_0$ denote
the free filled-sea response at normalized hopping speed one.
A degree-$m$ CAR polynomial has centered onsite interaction frequencies
in $[-m/2,m/2]$. Bernstein's bound and Duhamel give the degree-three
dynamical error. For the state error, the cubic/cubic anticommutator
has nine single contractions leaving degree-four words; the two
cubic/linear terms leave degree-two words. Their local Wick errors
are at most $8\sqrt\Delta$ and $2\sqrt\Delta$. Absolute row and
column sums of the free propagator are at most $e^{3|s|}$, and the
contracted factor retains the Schur kernel joining the two smearing
indices. Consequently, uniformly over all lattice smearings,
\[
 |F_c(s;f)-F_0(s;f)|\le
 \bigl(3u|s|+312\sqrt\Delta\,e^{9|s|}\bigr)\|f\|^2.
\]
No divergent $\|f\|_1^2$ estimate is used.

The free onsite spin covariance is $I_2/2$, so normal ordering gives
$d=2\mathopen{:}n_{\rm opposite}c\mathclose{:}$ with no linear
component. It creates three positive-energy excitations, whose total
energy is the sum of three values
$e(k)=(\sum_j\sin^2k_j)^{1/2}$. The spin and antisymmetrization bounds
give coefficient sixteen per charge branch. Two independent internal
momenta must have $e(k)\le\epsilon$; each such set has normalized
volume at most $\epsilon^3$. Thus the complete signed free measure obeys
\[
 \mu_{0,f}([-\epsilon,\epsilon])\le32\epsilon^6\|f\|^2.
\]
All eight nodes and both positive physical excitation branches are
retained. Negative response frequency labels removal, not a negative
ground excitation energy.

Gaussian smoothing bounds the actual centered composite weight
in any fixed physical energy window:
\[
 \mu_{c,f}([-R,R])\le \eta_c(R)^2\|f\|^2,
\]
\[
 \eta_c(R)^2\le\min\!\left\{1,
 e^{R^2/(2c^2\epsilon^2)}
 \left(1536\epsilon^6+\frac{3u}{\epsilon}
       +624\sqrt\Delta\,e^{81/(2\epsilon^2)}\right)\right\}.
\]
First send $a\to0$ at fixed $\epsilon$, then $\epsilon\to0$.
This gives $\eta_c(R)\to0$ uniformly in unit smearings. The composite
retains its equal-time CAR norm while its ground spectral weight
escapes every finite energy interval.

The next steps use both charge branches in the positive ground GNS
space. For the annihilation map $Af=c(f)\Omega$ and composite map
$Df=d(f)\Omega$, the source equation is
$LA+Ah=-(U/2)D$, with conjugate input space understood.
On $h\ge\delta>0$, integration against the two contraction semigroups

\[
 \|\mathbf1_{[0,R]}(L)A\|\le\frac{U_{\max}\eta_c(R)}{2\delta},
 \qquad
 \|\mathbf1_{(R,\infty)}(L)A\|

\]
The negative-band creator has the same bound. Taking the two limits
and using the CAR determines the entire initial-time state as the
pure free filled sea. On a general bounded kinetic window

\[
 \|[C_a(f,t)-C_a(e^{ith_a}f,0)]\Omega_a\|
 \le\frac{U_{\max}}2

The adjoint difference satisfies the same estimate. Passing to the
continuum therefore gives both vacuum identities for the free Weyl
generator $h_b=i v\sum_j(-1)^{b_j}\sigma_j\partial_j$.

locality supplies the missing implication. The free filled-sea vacuum
