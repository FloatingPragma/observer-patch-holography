\section{Massive operational clocks and resolved local reads}
\label{app:massive-operational-clocks}

The tetrahedral flight process of Appendix~\ref{app:m1-quantum-transport}
admits a massive extension in the same native time. The graph, coherent
onsite gates, mass parameters and readout operations are supplied. Ideal
onsite gates take zero duration in this grammar; a physical gate overhead
must be charged separately. These assumptions define a mathematical
construction, not a source-selected physical instrument. Throughout,
$a,c,m>0$ and $\hbar=1$.

\paragraph{Complete massive dynamics.}
With the coin $C$ and tetrahedral sign vectors $s$ defined above, put
$W_a(k)=\operatorname{diag}(e^{-ia k\cdot s})C$,
$\tau=\sqrt3a/c$, $\mathcal R=I_2\otimes C$ and
$\beta=\sigma_x\otimes I_4$. One parallel flight and onsite mixing give
\[
 U_m(k)=e^{-i\tau m\beta}\operatorname{diag}(W_a(k),W_a(k)^*).
\]
For $0<m\tau<\pi/2$, define
$\sin^2\epsilon=\sum_i\sin^2(ak_i)/3$ and
$\omega=\arccos(\cos(m\tau)\cos\epsilon)$, with
$0\le\epsilon\le\pi/2$. The eight eigenvalues are
$e^{\pm i\omega}$ and $e^{\pm i(\pi-\omega)}$, each twice.
All eight valleys are retained. Every smooth band has group speed at most
$(c/3)\cos(m\tau)$, a sharp supremum. At fixed spacing, the ballistic
distribution of any fixed normalized one-particle preparation has the
same speed bound; this long-time statement is distinct from refinement.

Set $v=c/3$, $B(k)=\operatorname{diag}(k\cdot s)$,
$X(k)=\sqrt3(B(k)+CB(k)C)/2$ and
$H_m(k)=m\beta+v\sigma_z\otimes X(k)$.
The Clifford identities give $H_m(k)^2=(m^2+v^2|k|^2)I_8$.
For $|k|\le K$, $t=n\tau$, $b=\sqrt3aK$ and $\mu=m\tau$,
\[
 \|U_m(k)^n-\mathcal R^n e^{-itH_m(k)}\|
 \le D_n:=\lfloor n/2\rfloor(b^2+4\mu b)
                  +(n\bmod2)(b+\mu b).
 \tag{MC1}
\]
Pairing consecutive ticks and bounding the mass/flight commutators proves
this full-channel estimate, including leakage and the odd-tick carrier.
The carrier at valley $\pi z/a$ is $(-1)^{\sum z_i}\mathcal R$.

\paragraph{Actual spatially supported operations.}
For $f\in H^2(\mathbb R^3;\mathbb C^8)$ let
$S_af(x)=a^{3/2}f(ax)$,
$M_2(f)=\||k|^2\widehat f\|_2$ and
$A_a(f)=(a/\pi)^2\sqrt{4\pi^2+\pi^4/45}\,M_2(f)$.
Poisson summation bounds the sampling aliases by $A_a(f)$; (MC1) then gives,
for $0<K<\pi/(2a)$,
\[
 \|U_m^{\pm n}S_af-\mathcal R^{\pm n}S_ae^{\mp itH_m}f\|
 \le Q_a(f):=2A_a(f)+D_n\|f\|_2+2M_2(f)/K^2.
 \tag{MC2}
\]
The full Dirac field has propagation speed $v$, including both energy
signs. Let compactly supported $f,g\in H^2$ have separation greater than $vt$,
and normalize their nonzero lattice samples to $f_a,g_a$.
For the CAR number effects $N_h=a(h)^*a(h)$, encoding by
$e^{i\theta N_{g_a}}$ changes the later $N_{f_a}$ probability by at most
\[
 \min\{1,\,2|\theta|Q_a(f)/\|S_af\|_2\}.
 \tag{MC3}
\]
This follows from the CAR commutator and Duhamel's formula, uniformly over
Fock states and spectator ancillas. Finite products obey the sum bound.
At fixed duration and envelopes, $K=a^{-1/4}$ in fixed units gives
$Q_a=O(\sqrt a)$. The specified operation class is essential: a single-site
particle produces a perfect record at speed $c$ in one flight.

\paragraph{A readable inertial clock.}
Take two coherently labelled masses $m_1<m_2$ and a fixed
$|u|<v$. For $\gamma=(1-|u|^2/v^2)^{-1/2}$, prepare Gaussian envelopes
of width $\sigma$ around $k_j=\gamma m_j u/v^2$, with a common unit spinor
$\xi$ satisfying $H_{m_j}(k_j)\xi=E_j\xi$, $E_j=\gamma m_j$, and
$\mathcal R\xi=\xi$. The normalized initial state is
$(\Psi_1,\Psi_2)/\sqrt2$, where
\[
 \Psi_j(x)=(2\pi\sigma^2)^{-3/4}
       e^{-|x|^2/(4\sigma^2)}e^{ik_j\cdot x}\xi.
\]
The actual walk starts from the normalized lattice samples of this state.
These are common reference velocities; finite-walk drift is included in
(MC1). Write $\Delta m=m_2-m_1$, $\Delta k=k_2-k_1$ and
$\Delta E=E_2-E_1$.
A Gaussian spatial acceptance of radius $R_d$, centred at $ut$, and the
mass-label effect $|+_\theta\rangle\langle+_\theta|$, with
$|+_\theta\rangle=(|1\rangle+e^{i\theta}|2\rangle)/\sqrt2$, give the
rigid-packet reference probability
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 p_\theta(t)=\tfrac12\{p_R+V_R\cos(\Delta m\,t/\gamma+\theta)\},
 \qquad p_R=(1+\sigma^2/R_d^2)^{-3/2},\\
 V_R=p_Re^{-s^2|\Delta k|^2/2},\qquad
 s^2=\frac{\sigma^2R_d^2}{\sigma^2+R_d^2}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{MC4}
\]
The beat follows from $\Delta E-u\cdot\Delta k=\Delta m/\gamma$,
not an assumed proper-time accrual rule. The continuum norm error through
time $T$ is bounded by
$e=\max_j\{\sqrt3v/(m_j\sigma)+\sqrt{15}Tv^2/(8m_j\sigma^2)\}$.
Choose $\max_j|k_j|<K<\pi/(2a)$ and require
$a|\Delta k|\le\pi$ and $b_\sigma\le1/2$, where
\[
 B(z)=\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb Z^3\setminus\{0\}}e^{-z|\ell|^2},
 \qquad b_\sigma=B(2\pi^2\sigma^2/a^2),\qquad
 b_s=B(\pi^2s^2/(2a^2)).
\]
Writing $A=\max_jA_a(\Psi_j)$, $D=\max_jD_n(a,K,m_j)$ and $h$ for
the common Fourier tail norm beyond $K$, the actual lattice probability
error at tick $n$ with $n\tau\le T$ is at most
\[
 4(e+D+4A+2h)+4(b_s+b_\sigma)
       +e^{-L_d^2/(2R_d^2)}+e_{\rm prep}.
 \tag{MC5}
\]
The detector is cut off at radius $L_d$. For a product $C^2$ preparation
cutoff taking values in $[0,1]$, equal to one on $[-P,P]^3$, and tapering to zero within
$[-P-\sigma,P+\sigma]^3$, with $P>a$, one may use
$e_{\rm prep}=2\sqrt{3\operatorname{erfc}((P-a)/(\sqrt2\sigma))}$.
The complete formulas and proof are in
\href{https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/r2056/docs/research/MASSIVE_OPERATIONAL_CLOCKS.md}{\emph{Massive flights, resolved reads and observable clocks}}.
With $c=3$, $(m_1,m_2)=(100,100.1)$, $u=(0.6,0,0)$,
$\sigma=10$, $R_d=100$, $L_d=600$, $P=80$ and
$a=10^{-9}$, outward interval arithmetic bounds the probability error below
$0.035153$ through a full moving cycle and the next tick. The reference
fringe exceeds $0.7457$ and the actual click-probability swing exceeds
$0.6753$ after all errors. This is a finite analytic existence witness;
separate coarse periodic waveguide runs test evolution and readouts.

The positive Floquet accounting observable $|i\log U_m|/\tau$ has an
empty Fock vacuum and bounded preparation cost under refinement for these
packets, including their high-band leakage. It is not the signed generator
or a derived physical energy. The construction supplies free inertial
clock comparisons within its declared operations; physical source admission,
mass selection, calibration, interacting or accelerated clocks, and
distributed record-collection latency remain separate.
