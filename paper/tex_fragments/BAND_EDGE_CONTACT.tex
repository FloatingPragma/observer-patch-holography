\paragraph{An interacting band-edge limit with explicit errors.}
The full eight-valley $K=0$ Weyl/contact family has a positive continuum
scattering result after a declared change of comparison. Set
$c_a=\sqrt3/a^2$, $B_a=6/a^2$ and $J_a=B_a-H_a$. This shifts the diverging
upper edge and reverses Hamiltonian time, so $S_J=S_H^*$.
It is not a physical vacuum or clock identification. Near each
$k_j=\pm\pi/2+ap_j$, the free edge deficit converges to $|p|^2$, with error
at most $a^2[\sum_jp_j^4+(\sum_jp_j^2)^2]/3$.

Write $C(k)=\sum_j\cos^2 k_j$ and $W(\eta)=\langle(C+\eta)^{-1}\rangle$
for the normalized full-torus average. Then $2/3\leq W_0=W(0)<5$ and
\[
 g_a(B_a+z)=\frac{a^2}{2}\left(1+\frac{a^2z}{6}\right)
 W\left(a^2z+\frac{a^4z^2}{12}\right).
\]
The substitution $q_j=\cos k_j$ retains all preimages and gives measure
$\pi^{-3}\prod_j(1-q_j^2)^{-1/2}d^3q$. On $|q|\leq1/2$, its Jacobian
minus one lies between zero and $|q|^2$. The spherical mean has expansion
$A(t)=\sum_n A_nt^n$ with $A_0=1$ and $0\leq A_n\leq1$.
Exact ball integration, a geometric-series principal-value bound and
the exterior bound $C\geq1/4$ give
\[
 \left|W(\eta)-W_0+\frac2\pi\sqrt\eta\right|\leq18\eta
 \quad(\eta\geq0),\qquad
 \left|W(-t+i0)-W_0+\frac{2i}\pi\sqrt t\right|\leq36t
 \quad(0\leq t\leq1/8).
\]
In particular, with the upper-half-plane square root,
\[
 \left|\frac{g_a(B_a+z)-a^2W_0/2}{a^3}
          +\frac{\sqrt z}{\pi}\right|\leq19a|z|,
 \qquad |a^2z|\leq1/8.
\]
The principal-value estimate is analytic; finite imaginary broadening
and numerical quadrature are not used as a proof.

Choose the repulsive coupling by the exact source-integral prescription
\[
 U_a^{-1}=\frac{a^2W_0}{2}-\frac{a^3\gamma}{\pi},\qquad
 \gamma>0,\quad a\gamma\leq1.
\]
Here $\gamma$ is an explicit model parameter, not a measured mass or a
derived constant of nature. For $0\leq s\leq S$, $a^2S\leq1/8$ and
$836aS\leq7\gamma$, the rank-one stationary formula gives
\[
 S_H(B_a-s)\longrightarrow\frac{\gamma+i\sqrt s}{\gamma-i\sqrt s},
 \qquad
 S_J(s)\longrightarrow\frac{\gamma-i\sqrt s}{\gamma+i\sqrt s},
 \qquad
 \sup_{[0,S]}|S-S_{\rm limit}|\leq\frac{1672aS}{7\gamma}.
\]
This controls all normalized compact-window packets in the declared
cyclic contact ac energy representation, in operator norm. The
orthogonal free channels remain present. A full momentum-space
comparison or many-body field limit is not inferred.

Strict monotonicity of $g_a(E)$ above the band gives exactly one pole
$E_a=B_a+\delta_a$. If $a\gamma\leq1/256$, the same estimate proves
\[
 \frac{\gamma^2}{4}\leq\delta_a\leq4\gamma^2,
 \qquad |\delta_a-\gamma^2|\leq\frac{5016}{7}a\gamma^3.
\]
Thus the reflected bound energy tends to $-\gamma^2$; the original
energy diverges as $6/a^2+\delta_a$. Neither is identified with a physical
rest mass. The proof, exact error schedules, independent full-torus
checks, and missing-countertuning and reversed-time controls are in
\path{model/band_edge_contact/README.md}. Lattice contact renormalization
and repulsively bound pairs are established mechanisms; compare
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04425}{Koerber--Berkowitz--Luu (2019)}
and \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0605196}{Winkler et al.\ (2006)}.
The new attachment is the quantitative full-valley calculation for this
source family.

\paragraph{The unchanged-zero obstruction remains.}
At fixed model energy zero with $c=v/a$, the distinct earlier theorem
gives $\int_{-M}^{M}|S-I|^2dE\leq1024M^2/(63c)$ for every real
finite $U(a)$ and $M\leq c/4$. Hence every fixed energy-normalized ac
packet becomes transparent, even though tuned shrinking resonances
retain operator-norm distance two. The band-edge result explicitly
uses a different scaling and diverging energy comparison. Neither
theorem supplies nonzero-total-momentum control, a stable many-body
vacuum, relativistic massive/massless sectors, or a physical detector map.
