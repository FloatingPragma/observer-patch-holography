\paragraph{A source lattice realization of the Weyl stress directions.}
The new attachment in \path{model/lattice_weyl_response/README.md}
specializes to the exact free source, $U=0$ and $c=v/a$, in its filled-band
ground state. It realizes the preceding nine Weyl shell perturbations by
compact lattice CAR operators and supplies a controlled joint lattice and
detector-radius limit. All eight valleys are retained; the selected shell
excites the $(1,1,1)$ source valley, while the seven other limiting sectors
remain in their vacuum. This is a matter-response input to the existing
G5 interface, not the complete geometric hypothesis.

In the selected source frame, use the coordinate $x^0=vt$ and set
\[
 h=\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\boldsymbol p,\qquad
 h_a=\sum_j\sigma_j\sin(ap_j)/a.
\]
Write $\Pi,\Pi_a$ for their negative-band projectors and $J_a$ for
normalized averages on centered cubes of side $a$.
The exact translation intertwiner gives
\[
 \|(h_aJ_a-J_ah)f\|
 \leq \frac{a^2}{6}\sum_j\|\partial_j^3f\|.
\]
For every $f\in H^3\cap L^1$ and $\varepsilon>0$, a spectral split at
$|p|=\varepsilon$ gives the gapless estimate
\[
 \|(\Pi_aJ_a-J_a\Pi)f\|
 \leq \frac{a^2}{\varepsilon}\||p|^3f\|
       +\varepsilon^{3/2}\|f\|_1 .
\]
On each high-energy off-diagonal band the Sylvester equation has
generators $A\geq0$, $B\geq\varepsilon$ and solution
$X=-\int_0^\infty e^{-tA}\Pi_a(h_aJ_a-J_ah)e^{-tB}\,dt$.
The low band is controlled by its Fourier volume. Thus no lattice gap or
band-limited sampling assumption is inserted.

The normalized shell of radius $L$ is weakly $H^4$: its first three
boundary derivatives vanish. Exact radial integration of its harmonic
degrees $0,1,2$ gives Sobolev majorants
$(K_1,K_2,K_3,K_4)=(5,36,352,5036)$. With $q=a/L$, $F=\Pi f$ and
$F_a=\Pi_aJ_af$, the choice $\varepsilon=a/L^2$ yields
\[
 \|F_a-J_aF\|\leq359q,\qquad
 \|p_{a,j}F_a-J_ap_jF\|\leq\frac{15389q}{3L},\qquad
 1-\|J_af\|^2\leq225q^2.
\]
These estimates also hold in every source spin frame. They control
derivative observables directly, rather than deducing them from
distributional convergence of field correlations.

Let $f_a=J_af/\|J_af\|$, with its source modulation restored, and put
\[
 B_{a,n}=c^*(f_{a,n})c(f_{a,n})
       -\omega_a(c^*(f_{a,n})c(f_{a,n}))I.
\]
On the common interior algebra the functionals
\[
 \rho_{a,\theta}(X)=\omega_a\bigl((I+\sum_n\theta_nB_{a,n})X\bigr)
\]
form a positive normal affine state family for $\|\theta\|_1<1$.
For $u=(5/4,3n/4)$ set
\[
 A_u=u^0I-\boldsymbol u\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma,\qquad
 D_{a,u}=u^0h_a-\boldsymbol u\cdot\boldsymbol p_a,\qquad
 Q_{a,u}(w)=\tfrac12\{w_aA_u,D_{a,u}\}.
\]
Compact $w$ makes this a finite-rank selfadjoint matrix and its
second quantization a bounded finite-support CAR detector. If its support
and nearest neighbours miss the shell, exact Wick contraction gives
\[
 S_{a,u,n}(w)
 =\left.\partial_{\theta_n}\rho_{a,\theta}
       (:d\Gamma(Q_{a,u}(w)):)\right|_0
 =-\frac{\langle F_a,Q_{a,u}(w)F_a\rangle}{\|J_af\|^2}.
\]
For $q\leq1/30$, $q\leq1/16$, the corresponding continuum response obeys
\[
 |S_{a,u,n}(w)-S_{u,n}(w)|
 \leq171560q\,\frac{\|w\|_\infty}{L}
       +80q\,\operatorname{Lip}(w).
\]

Take $w_R=(R^2-|x|^2)_+/(2R)$, $\rho=R/L$, and normalize $2\pi S_a$
by $(8\pi^2R^4/15)\gamma_L$, where
$\gamma_L=c_\gamma/(\pi^3L^4)$ and
$c_\gamma=4249388/150088295$. Entrywise, the resulting response matrix
satisfies
\[
 |M_{a,ij}-D_{ij}|\leq C_{\rm lat}\frac q{\rho^4}
                           +C_{\rm ball}\rho^2,\qquad
 D_{ij}=\bigl(5/4+(3/4)n_i\cdot n_j\bigr)^2,
\]
where $C_{\rm lat}=193298715130500/1062347$ and $C_{\rm ball}$ is the
previous exact small-ball bound. For
$0<\rho\leq2^{-28}$ and
$0<q\leq\min\{1/16,1/30,\rho^7/(1+C_{\rm lat})\}$,
the Neumann budget is strictly less than $1/4$ and
$\|M_a^{-1}\|_\infty\leq272/15$. This supplies one explicit common
refinement of all nine compact source perturbations and detectors.

These are lab-slice stress probes. Only the rest-vector continuum form
is identified here with the unboosted ball modular kernel; lattice
