\paragraph{Conserved periodic momentum and its locality boundary.}
On a declared periodic box, the same local Weyl/contact action has exact
graded translations $T_i=\Gamma(t_i)$: they permute creation operators with
the full occupation-permutation signs, satisfy $T_i^{L_i}=I$, commute with
one another and commute with $H$.  For
$\zeta=e^{2\pi i/L}$ and the integer representative
$b(j)\in(-L/2,L/2]$, put
\[
 \Pi_j=\frac1L\sum_{r=0}^{L-1}\zeta^{-jr}T^r,
 \qquad P=\frac{2\pi}{L}\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}b(j)\Pi_j.
\]
These complete orthogonal projectors give a Hermitian conserved momentum
$P$, with $e^{iP}=T$ and $\|P\|\leq\pi$.  The branch uses $+\pi$ at $-1$;
subtracting $2\pi\Pi_{L/2}$ gives the distinct $-\pi$ convention on even
rings.  Exact Clifford arithmetic checks all three translations on the
complete 54-mode $3\times3\times3$ torus.  On the existing four-site
periodic source, all 256 Fock states and four rank-64 projectors are retained.
This is global lattice conservation under a supplied periodic boundary,
not a new symmetry of the previous open box.

The logarithm is nonquadratic.  If $p$ is the same one-particle logarithm,
then on momentum occupations
\[
 Q=d\Gamma(p)=P+2\pi W,
 \qquad W=\frac{\sum_{\rm occupied}b(j)-b(\sum_{\rm occupied}j)}L\in\mathbb Z.
\]
On the four-site ring $W=-1,0,1$ have multiplicities $4,152,100$ and
$W^3-W=0$.  The opposite-spin pairs with labels $(1,1)$ and $(-1,-1)$
both have $P=\pi$, whereas $Q=\pi,-\pi$.  Their exact contact matrix
element is $U/4$, and
$\langle\mathrm{out}|[H,Q]|\mathrm{in}\rangle=\pi U/2$.
Thus the contact transfers additive momentum by $2\pi$ while preserving
the conserved crystal momentum; $[H,Q]=2\pi[H,W]$ retains the winding term.
The vacuum and one-particle restriction fix any proposed scalar-plus-quadratic
formula for $P$ to $Q$, while the filled ring has $P=0,Q=2\pi$.

There is an all-branch locality obstruction on an odd-prime ring.
Any number-preserving Hermitian logarithm of $T$ restricts to a logarithm
of $t$; its two-dimensional spin block at momentum $j$ has trace
$(2\pi/L)(2j+Ln_j)$ with arbitrary integers $n_j$.  Vanishing of a
nonzero-separation spin-trace kernel would make
$\sum_j(2j+Ln_j)z^j$ divisible by
$\Phi_L(z)=1+\cdots+z^{L-1}$.  Its coefficients would all be equal,
contradicting their distinct residues $2j\bmod L$.  A sum of arbitrary
many-body densities supported in sets of graph diameter at most $R$ has
zero one-particle matrix elements between sites farther than $R$.
Consequently no choice of these logarithm branches yields a uniformly
finite-range density.  For the principal branch, the stronger bound is
\[
 |p_{xy}|=\frac{\pi}{L\sin(\pi r/L)},\qquad
 \|P-A_R\|\geq\frac1{R+1},\quad L>2(R+1),
\]
where $r$ is the positive site separation up to $L/2$ and $A_R$ is any
range-$R$ density sum.  This is a full-Fock norm bound; the same numerical
lower bound is not asserted for arbitrary branches or restricted states.

An initially smooth sector nevertheless has a controlled local
approximation.  Let $\Omega$ retain $N\leq N_{\max}$ and
$|k_j|\leq\kappa$ for every occupied momentum, with $N_{\max}\kappa<\pi$.
For the nearest-neighbor quadratic $S_j=d\Gamma(\sin p_j)$,
\[
 \|(P_j-S_j)\Omega\|\leq\delta:=\frac{N_{\max}\kappa^3}{6},\qquad
 \|(P_j-S_j)e^{-itH}\Omega\|
 \leq\delta+(\pi+N_{\max})
       \min\{1,|t|U\lfloor N_{\max}/2\rfloor\}.
\]
The second bound uses free invariance of $\Omega$ and a Duhamel contact
leakage estimate; it is uniform in volume at fixed $N_{\max}$.
The four-site zero-momentum pair couples with matrix element $U/4$ to
$(1,-1)$ outside a cutoff below $\pi/2$, so interaction leakage cannot be
deleted.  The bounds do not establish a finite-density or continuum limit.

time and the Einstein-register construction open.  The exact receipt,
