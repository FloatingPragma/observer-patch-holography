\paragraph{An all-valley positive-energy continuum field net.}
There is now a spacing-limit construction beyond fixed-lattice response.
Take $c(a)=v/a$, fixed $v>0$, and $0\leq U(a)\leq U_{\max}<\infty$.
At each spacing retain the full original Weyl/contact dynamics and a
translation-invariant full ground.  On centered cells
$C_{a,n}=a(n+[-1/2,1/2)^3)$ define
\[
 (J_af)(n)=a^{-3/2}\int_{C_{a,n}}f(x)\,dx,
 \qquad P_a=J_a^*J_a.
\]
Cellwise Cauchy--Schwarz and coordinate averaging give
$\|J_a\|\leq1$ and
$\|P_af-f\|\leq a\sum_j\|\partial_jf\|$ for $f\in H^1$.
Thus $P_a\to I$ strongly.  For every $b\in\{0,1\}^3$ set
$M_bu(n)=(-1)^{b\cdot n}u(n)$ and
$C_{a,b}(f)=c(M_bJ_af)$, with the conventional antilinear annihilator.
All eight valleys evolve under the original full Hamiltonian.
Writing $D_{a,j}f=[f(\cdot+ae_j)-f(\cdot-ae_j)]/(2a)$ gives
\[
 h_aM_bJ_af=M_bJ_a\!\left[iv\sum_j(-1)^{b_j}\sigma_jD_{a,j}f\right],
 \qquad \|h_aM_bJ_af\|\leq v\sum_j\|\partial_jf\|.
\]
For $b\ne d$, shifting one lattice step in a coordinate where they
differ reverses the cross-valley pairing.  Subtracting the shifted
pairing proves
\[
 |\langle M_bJ_af,M_dJ_ag\rangle|
 \leq\frac a2\left(\|\partial_jf\|\|g\|
                         +\|f\|\|\partial_jg\|\right).
\]
The limiting equal-time fields therefore have the full continuum CAR
over $L^2(\mathbb R^3;\mathbb C^2)\otimes\mathbb C^8$.

The necessary time estimate holds in the full CAR operator norm.
For $B(u)=\sum_{x\sigma}\overline{u_{x\sigma}}
n_{x\bar\sigma}c_{x\sigma}$, the onsite anticommutator is positive and
obeys $\{B(u_x),B(u_x)^*\}\leq\|u_x\|^2I$.
Different-site odd anticommutators vanish, hence $\|B(u)\|\leq\|u\|$.
The exact contact commutator consequently gives
\[
 \|\tau_{a,t}(C_{a,b}(f))-\tau_{a,s}(C_{a,b}(f))\|
 \leq |t-s|\left(v\sum_j\|\partial_jf\|+U_{\max}\|f\|\right).
\]
This avoids an extensive interaction-norm estimate.

A diagonal subsequence of spacings makes every word in a countable
dense set of smooth smears, rational times and all eight valleys
converge simultaneously.  The displayed time and norm estimates extend
the limits to all $L^2$ smears and real times.  Polynomial positivity,
norm bounds and the CAR pass to the limit and construct bounded fields
in one GNS representation.  Common time shifts are strongly continuous.
Nearest \emph{even} lattice translations approximate every physical
translation while keeping all valley phases equal to one, so continuous
spatial translations also act on the limiting net.  For every multi-time
polynomial $X$, the correlations
$\omega_a(X_a^*\tau_{a,t}(X_a))$ have nonnegative energy support and
converge uniformly on compact time intervals.  Testing against smooth
negative-energy spectral functions and using dominated convergence
therefore proves a nonnegative generator on the entire limiting GNS
space, not only on linear-field vectors.

Graded locality also survives with an explicit bound.  Suppose $f$ is
supported in $[-r,r]^3$, $g$ outside $[-R,R]^3$, and put
$\delta=(R-r)/2-48vT>0$.  For
$0<a\leq\min\{1,(R-r)/8\}$ and $|t|\leq T$, the original full-CAR
bound, with lattice radii $\lceil r/a\rceil+1$ and
$\lfloor R/a\rfloor-1$, yields
\begin{align*}
 \|\{\tau_{a,t}(C_{a,b}(f)),C_{a,d}(g)^\#\}\|
 &\leq192vT(2r+5)^3(2R+1)^2\|f\|\|g\|
                          a^{-6}e^{-\delta/a}\\
 &\leq192\cdot7!vT(2r+5)^3(2R+1)^2\|f\|\|g\|
                          \frac{a}{\delta^7}.
\end{align*}
Here $\#$ allows an adjoint or no adjoint.
Inserting this norm estimate between arbitrary polynomial vectors makes
the limiting anticommutator the zero operator.  A finite partition of
unity extends the result to separated compact supports.  The continuum
net thus has exact graded locality at sup-norm distance greater than
$96v|t-s|$.  This is a conservative propagation cone, not a Lorentz metric.

There is a further uniform response bound on every physical momentum
window $|p|\leq P$ around any retained valley.  These windows reduce the
translation-invariant response and have kinetic norm at most $vP$.
The exact Schur formula gives
\[
 \|\mathcal G_a(z)^{-1}\|_{P}
 \leq |z|+vP+\frac{U_{\max}}2
                   +\frac{U_{\max}^2}{4\operatorname{Im}z},
 \qquad
 -\operatorname{Im}\mathcal G_a(z)
 \geq\frac{\operatorname{Im}z}{(|z|+vP+U_{\max})^2}I_P.
\]
These bounds are uniform in spacing away from real frequency.  The
cell-average map differs from the exact band isometry by at most
$a^2P^2/24$, so the two conventions have the same limiting correlations.
Weak convergence alone does not allow interchanging the inverse and limit.

\paragraph{A norm-controlled free limit and the remaining interacting step.}
A Duhamel comparison with the free lattice evolution uses only
$\|B(u)\|\leq\|u\|$ and gives an error at most $|U(a)t|\|u\|$.
The central-difference remainder then gives, for $f\in H^3$ and
$h_b=iv\sum_j(-1)^{b_j}\sigma_j\partial_j$,
\[
 \|\tau_{a,t}(C_{a,b}(f))-C_{a,b}(e^{it h_b}f)\|
 \leq|t|\left(U(a)\|f\|
             +\frac{va^2}{6}\sum_j\|\partial_j^3f\|\right).
\]
If $U(a)\to0$, the limiting dynamics is exactly the free eight-valley
Weyl dynamics.  Positive energy forces positive-band annihilators and
negative-band creators to annihilate the ground.  The one-particle
zero-energy kernel is zero in $L^2$; spectral-band approximation and the
CAR recurrence therefore uniquely determine the filled-sea quasifree
state and all its moments.  This is a controlled free continuum, not the
required interacting one.

For bounded positive limiting $U$, the construction still gives the full
positive-energy continuum field net, but it has not proved that a connected
interaction survives, selected a unique limiting state, or derived Lorentz
covariance.  Its propagation cone and positive energy supply no common
physical stress, geometric modular flow, renormalized area stationarity
or completed G1--G6 Einstein package.
