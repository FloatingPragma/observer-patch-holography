\section{The bound pairs of the OPH law}
\label{sec:bound-pairs}

The executable model of the theory runs the OPH law \cite{ophmodel}. Each constituent moves by reflected routing on a torus with three
spatial axes and a private cyclic register. Two constituents that share an address in
matched slots receive a contact coin and step together; one of them carries two rails
whose contact phases differ, and their relative phase is a clock hand. A reversible
capture vertex turns a head-on collision into such a pair plus a marker that leaves on its
own wire, and a newborn pair opens its hop by a reversible partial swap on the profile
$(1-\cos\pi u)/2$ over $128$ updates and then hops fully.

\begin{proposition}[Bound sector]
\label{prop:law-bound-sector}
The matched coincident states of the contact law form an invariant subspace of the update
and of its inverse, and so does their complement. A bound pair never separates under the
contact law, and two separated constituents never bind without the capture vertex.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The contact coin acts inside the matched coincident states and leaves the unmatched
coincident pairs unchanged; away from coincidence the product coin does not move either
constituent. Routing moves both constituents of a matched coincident state by the same
step into matched partner slots, so it maps the matched coincident states onto themselves
and their complement onto itself.
\end{proof}

A particle assertion about the model concerns the joint and conditional laws of the
records of its simulated observers, under its declared preparations and complete
detectors. A test compares what those observers read with what observers in our world
record; the state of the computation is a tool of the calculation and contains no
physical space. On these records two separately launched constituents formed a persistent
pair through the capture vertex, and none with the vertex disabled;
ordinary incoming wave trains sent $96.56$ per cent of the late crossing pairs the
intended way, and $0.53$ per cent of the pairs separated after the marker's second return;
the rail clock of a moving pair kept proper time within the registered band on five
journeys; and formed matter obeyed $\omega^2=m^2+c^2|k|^2$ on all fourteen held-out
directions at three chart periods, while the same probes with half the spatial weight of
the formed vector failed. The conserved invariant of a bound pair is its pairing, and no
record of a class, a charge or a spin is produced. The carrier, the preparations and the
detectors are supplied.
