\subsection{A quartic interaction on the complete compact band}
\label{sec:interacting-localized-response}

The spatial source is the cubic operator $h=T+Mn\cdot\sigma$,
$\|T\|<2$, $M\geq8$. Its direction is the normalization of
$(x,y,z^2-a^2)$ inside the cube of radius $a+1$ and a constant outside,
with zero amplitude at the two cores $(0,0,\pm a)$.
The four-band bound is $r=b^2/(M-4)$, where $b^2=7/4$ for $a=1$
and $b^2=3/2$ otherwise. The spectral and particle--hole identities
hold for this source by the compact-pair construction.
Let $W$ contain the four localized modes and set $A=W^\dagger hW$. The isometry $E=W\otimes I_{45}$ embeds 180 annihilators
into the complete finite field. The current and pairing intertwiners hold
for every complete memory record, with $A$ retained in the quadratic core.
Write $P=EE^\dagger$ and choose
\begin{equation}
 V=\lambda\bigl(N_W-90\bigr)^2,
 \qquad N_W=d\Gamma(P),\qquad \lambda\geq0.
 \label{eq:localized-quartic}
\end{equation}
This is an explicit controller design. The particle--hole identity
$C\overline W=WS$ ensures that the pairing has no localized/complementary
cross term. All terms are even, so
$H_r^{\rm full}=H_{r,W}\otimes I+I\otimes H_{r,\perp}$ on every record.
The interaction is parity even and commutes
with all twelve native internal currents.  In the localized occupation
basis it contains the quartic term
$2\lambda\sum_{i<j}n_i n_j$.  The pairing Hamiltonian does not conserve
$N_W$, and its commutator with $V$ need not vanish.  Every record preserves
the same localized algebra because $V$ acts entirely on that factor.

Hold the texture fixed throughout the memory averaging interval. The
complete record limit has a bound independent of exterior volume.
Writing the compact energies as $\{-e_2,-e_1,e_1,e_2\}$ with
$e_i\leq r$, the kinetic norm is $45(e_1+e_2)\leq90r$.
If $g$ is the source feedback gain, the pairing bound and
$0\leq N_W\leq180$ give
\[
 \|H_{r,W}\|\leq M_W=90r+90(1+g)+8100\lambda.
\]
The radial two-column zero-mode construction is a separate special case
with ninety annihilators, centre 45 and bound $45(1+g)+2025\lambda$.
The actual memory transition has the reset decomposition
$T=(1-p)R+pS$, and contracts zero-sum operator-valued memory blocks by
at most $p$.  At step size $t/n$, its reduced core state consequently
satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \|\rho_{W,n}(t)-e^{-it\bar H_W}\rho_W(0)e^{it\bar H_W}\|_1
 \leq\frac{4M_Wt}{n(1-p)}
 +\left(\frac8{1-p}+4\right)\frac{M_W^2t^2}{n}.
 \label{eq:localized-quartic-limit}
\end{equation}
The estimate includes arbitrary initial classical--quantum memory
correlations and a finite untouched reference.  The first term vanishes
for stationary product memory.  The mean Hamiltonian contains the same
memory-derived mass matrix and the interaction~\eqref{eq:localized-quartic}.
Uniformity concerns this fixed localized algebra under its source
isometries; it gives no uniform continuum estimate for all spatial
observables.

Equation~\eqref{eq:localized-quartic-limit} compares the unread core.
There is a separate estimate for the full native history register.
For a fixed initial memory label, purify the core/reference state.
Write $p_\omega$ for the field-independent history probability,
$\psi_\omega=U_\omega\psi$, and
$\bar\psi=e^{-it\bar H_W}\psi$.  If $\delta$ is the unread trace-norm
bound, then
$\sum_\omega p_\omega(1-|\langle\bar\psi|\psi_\omega\rangle|^2)
\leq\delta/2$.
The pure-state distance identity and Jensen's inequality bound the
full history/core trace distance from
$\sum_\omega p_\omega|\omega\rangle\langle\omega|
\otimes|\bar\psi\rangle\langle\bar\psi|$
by $\sqrt{2\delta}$.  Partial trace covers mixed inputs.  For initial
classical memory/core correlations, condition on the initial memory
label and retain that tag in the ideal history law; convexity preserves
the same bound.  The complete final record/core rate is therefore
$O(n^{-1/2})$.  Opposite qubit phase records saturate this square-root
relation.  No uniform estimate for rare conditioned histories or
intervening field measurements follows.

The interaction is defined through a spectral projector and need not be
strictly onsite. For the radial special case, the resolvent estimate at
texture coefficient 64 and $W_x^\dagger W_x=\rho_xI_2$ gives, for the projection $A_L$ onto
$|x|_\infty<L$,
\[
 \|(I-A_L)E\|^2\leq\epsilon_L
 =\frac{3}{2\cdot62^2}\,32^{-2(L-1)}.
\]
Thus $\|P-A_LPA_L\|\leq2\sqrt{\epsilon_L}$, and finite rank gives
$\|N_W-d\Gamma(A_LPA_L)\|\leq360\sqrt{\epsilon_L}$.
The radial interaction $\lambda(N_W-45)^2$ is exponentially localized about
the prescribed origin, without being a strictly onsite interaction.

On this radial subspace there is an exact obstruction for the onsite density alternative.
On the two-particle sector let
$D_g=\sum_xg_x\bigwedge^2 P_x$ and $E_2=\bigwedge^2 E$.  Direct
compression gives
\begin{equation}
 E_2^\dagger D_g(I-E_2E_2^\dagger)D_gE_2
 =\left[\sum_x g_x^2\rho_x^2-
              \left(\sum_xg_x\rho_x^2\right)^2\right]I.
 \label{eq:onsite-interaction-leakage}
\end{equation}
Writing $c=\sum_x\rho_x^2<1$, Cauchy--Schwarz bounds the bracket below
by $(1-c)\sum_xg_x^2\rho_x^2$.  Every nonzero member of this onsite
density class leaks from the source subspace on which it has support.
This statement does not exclude a dressed low-energy subspace or other
local interaction tensors.

\paragraph{Connected dynamics and spectral shifts.}
The compact mean pairing operator has 84 charged two-mode blocks and
twelve unpaired neutrino modes. Select the two spatial pairs of one
charged-lepton singular value $m$. Fix one occupation in each of the other
82 blocks and fill six neutral modes. Pair creation and annihilation kill
these spectators, whose number is 88. Subtracting their fixed energy gives
the exact reducing four-mode Hamiltonian
\[
 H_4=e_1(n_1-n_2)+e_2(n_3-n_4)
       +m(a_1^\dagger a_2^\dagger+a_2a_1+
       a_3^\dagger a_4^\dagger+a_4a_3)+\lambda(N_4-2)^2.
\]
This is an invariant sector of the mean Hamiltonian, not its asserted
vacuum or an invariant sector of every finite memory pulse.
Pair parities split its sixteen levels into the four even/even values
$0,4\lambda,2\lambda\pm2\sqrt{\lambda^2+m^2}$, four odd/odd values
$\pm e_1\pm e_2$, and eight mixed values $\lambda\pm e_i\pm m$.
Consequently
\[
 E_0=\min\{2\lambda-2\sqrt{\lambda^2+m^2},
            -e_1-e_2,\lambda-m-\max(e_1,e_2)\}.
\]

The full 180-mode ground also has an explicit variational bracket. List
the charged singular values, including colour multiplicities, as
$m_j$, $j=1,\ldots,21$. Each occurs in two pairing blocks at each spatial
energy $e_i$, with quadratic levels $\{\pm m_j,\pm e_i\}$.
The twelve neutral modes contribute $-3(e_1+e_2)$, giving
\[
 E_{\rm quad}=-2\sum_{j=1}^{21}
 [\max(m_j,e_1)+\max(m_j,e_2)]-3(e_1+e_2).
\]
Positivity of $V$ makes this a lower bound. A quadratic product ground has
mean occupation 90 and number variance one per even pairing block, zero

$E_{\rm quad}+\lambda b$. A completely blocked trial state has exact
occupation 90, zero interaction energy and kinetic energy $-45(e_1+e_2)$.
Thus, retaining every localized mode,
\[
 E_{\rm quad}\leq E_{\rm full}\leq
 \min\{E_{\rm quad}+\lambda b,-45(e_1+e_2)\}.
\]
The specified coefficient witness has $b=28$. These bounds constrain the
full vacuum energy without identifying its excitation spectrum.

The radial control sets $e_1=e_2=0$. Its ground energy is
$2\lambda-2\sqrt{\lambda^2+m^2}$, and its single-mode spectral poles and weights are
\[
 \omega_\pm=2\sqrt{\lambda^2+m^2}-\lambda\pm m,
 \qquad w_\pm=\frac{1\mp m/\sqrt{\lambda^2+m^2}}2.
\]
In this radial control the cross-pair density cumulant is
$-\lambda/(4\sqrt{\lambda^2+m^2})$, while its normal and anomalous
cross-pair two-point contractions vanish.  Hence positive $\lambda$
gives a non-Gaussian connected interaction.  These spectral shifts
preclude identifying the quadratic mass coefficients with the
interacting excitation energies without further calculation.

\paragraph{A common finite energy and modular response.}
For the same full localized Hamiltonian choose
$\rho_\beta=Z^{-1}e^{-\beta\bar H_W}$, $\beta>0$.
This state is faithful.  A global spectral reset with Kraus operators
$\sqrt{p_i}|i\rangle\langle j|$ prepares it and preserves total
probability.  That reset is a declared service, not an implementation
by the original site repair.  For $H(s)=\bar H_W+sX$, Duhamel
differentiation gives
\[
 \rho'_\beta(0)=-\beta\int_0^1
 \rho_\beta^u(X-\langle X\rangle)\rho_\beta^{1-u}\,du.
\]
The positive logarithmic-mean kernel yields
$F'=\langle X\rangle$,
$F''=-\beta C_{\rho_\beta}(X,X)\leq0$, and
$S'=\beta(E'-\langle X\rangle)$, where $C_\rho$ is the
Bogoliubov--Kubo--Mori covariance.  The noncommuting choice
$X=(N_4-2)^2$ is checked against an independent matrix-exponential
derivative.  These are finite identities for one nonquadratic
Hamiltonian.  They supply no geometric modular flow, local
relativistic stress, area normalization or geometric entropy
stationarity, and therefore imply no Einstein dynamics.
Exact characteristic polynomials, complete finite spectra, record controls and
the onsite leakage calculation accompany the proof.
