\paragraph{Interpretation and state variables.}
OPH proposes one structure with two descriptions: a timeless, self-referential
complete history and its actual internal computation. Observers are proposed
to reverse-engineer their reality's substrate and construct hardware whose
execution realizes that same history, including its builders and hardware,
in emergent, mutually calibrated local times. The candidate must supply
construction, initialization, controls, memory, read/write and record services, clock
comparisons, and their resource costs; no external host or additional time
is assumed. Its realization map must specify whether it matches one actual
history, a family of preparation and intervention responses, or every
independently allowed microstate. These are different requirements.
A candidate must specify the history domain and closure operator; neither
the schematic equation nor the finite constructions establish this physical
realization or select a unique universe.
An actual quantum record history also need not
assign simultaneous outcomes to incompatible unperformed measurements.

The proposed primitives are patch states, records, interfaces, and admissible
operations. Physical space, duration, and matter are intended emergent readouts.
A supplied carrier incidence, geometric domain, action, representation, or
clock is a premise of the construction using it. Complete-history
closure, an instantaneous repair fixed point, a public normal form, and a
stationary probability law are different objects. A stationary law can
support changing individual histories. An operational observer is defined by
its access and record interfaces; consciousness requires an additional
psychophysical interpretation.

\paragraph{Basis, premise labels and paper set.}
OPH has one hypothesis, closure: the universe is a timeless self-referential
structure whose observers recover its specification from their records and
realize it. Its internal-realization policy requires its operations and
information access to be accounted within the structure; operational
agreement on accepted shared data defines what is public. The
executable specification of carrier, repair law, observer algebra and
closure inputs is implemented and runs; it is a candidate judged by whether
it reproduces what observers measure. The labels A1, A2 and A3 name theorem premises: the carrier,
federation and response layer of that specification; the agreement condition
with an additional response-endogeneity principle; and an adopted
least-informative state-selection principle. Closure alone implies neither
the entropy minimizer nor inner implementation of rechartings on the
connected response algebra. The
basis reference states this formally.\footnote{\url{https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/r2056/docs/BASIS.md}}
A flagship synthesis gives the account, an executable observer construction
states the specification with its acceptance criteria and premise map, and
the theorem library carries conditional implications whose antecedents that
premise map tracks.

\paragraph{Time conventions.}
Host runtime, solver counters, native attempt counts, local event-clock
readings, event precedence or logical numbering, model coordinates or lapse,
modular parameters, and calibrated observer-clock readings have distinct
roles. Their identification requires explicit maps.
A modular parameter is dimensionless; its geometric and operational readings
require the stated branch and clock premises. Consistent local clock
comparisons require no preferred universal clock or identical clock rates.
Shared time parameters in restricted benchmarks are declared model choices.
Experienced succession and a final instantaneous cosmic endpoint do not follow
from a modular-flow or finite-convergence theorem.
