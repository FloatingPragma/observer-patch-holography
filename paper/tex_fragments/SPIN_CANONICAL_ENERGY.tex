\paragraph{Actual continuum field energies.}
For the same ordered cubic spin reference and the scaling
$X=px$, $T=Jpt$, the canonical fields
$Q_p(F)=p^2\sum_x(-1)^xF(px)S_x^3$ and
$P_p(G)=-p\sum_xG(px)S_x^2$ have actual Stone vectors in the
regular continuum representation. Their energy measures have no atom at
zero. The exact bond coefficient
\[
 \kappa=-\omega(S_0^1S_e^1+S_0^3S_e^3),
 \qquad m^2/2\leq\kappa\leq1/2,
\]
is strictly positive because the selected order obeys $m>1/1000$.
Exact microscopic twist energies converge to
\[
 D(F,G)=\frac{\kappa}{2}\int|\nabla G|^2+6\kappa\int F^2.
\]
This gives an upper bound on continuum twist energy; equality is not
inferred from convergence of bounded histories.

The inverse-energy bound and exact first moments transfer to the actual
field vectors by an enlarged Gram construction. Projection onto the
original reducing history space identifies those vectors through the
integrated Weyl derivative, without strong convergence of unbounded
fields. The resulting moments are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \int E\,d\nu_P&\leq\frac{\kappa}{2}\int |k|^2|\widehat G|^2,
 &\int E^{-1}\,d\nu_P&\leq\frac1{12}\int|\widehat G|^2,\\
 \int E\,d\nu_Q&\leq6\kappa\int|\widehat F|^2,
 &\int E^{-1}\,d\nu_Q&\leq\int |\widehat F|^2/|k|^2.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Fourier integrals use $d^3k/(2\pi)^3$.
For a unit-normalized test supported in $r/2\leq|k|\leq r$,
the canonical relation and static upper bounds imply, for $0<m_0\leq m$,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \nu_P\!\left([m_0^2r/8,\,24r/m_0^2]\right)&\geq m_0^2r/48,\\
 \nu_Q\!\left([m_0^2r/16,\,12r/m_0^2]\right)&\geq m_0^2/(2r).
 \end{aligned}
\]
These are positive weights of the original field vectors, without
conditioning on a shrinking energy filter. They prove nonstatic,
gapless dynamics in the actual continuum field representation. They
do not prove a sharp mass shell, Lorentz covariance, a surviving
connected interaction, or an identification with measured particles.
