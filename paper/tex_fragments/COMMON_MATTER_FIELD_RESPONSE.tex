\subsection{The common matter, field and response law}
\label{sec:common-matter-field-response}

The official executable model is the common matter, field and response
construction, designated CCF-LT-2 (CCF-2 in the source algebra).
\path{model/SPEC.md} specifies its complete coefficients, carriers,
preparations and instruments; \path{model/ccf_lt2/} implements them.
These are supplied construction inputs, including empirical calibration.

Each vertex of a finite oriented complex carries 96 native fermion modes,
with canonical anticommutation relations and ordering
$i(v,s,c,f)=96v+48s+3c+f$. Each edge carries integer flux
$E_e\in[-L,L]$, default $L=6$, and a raising shift $U_e$ that vanishes
at the upper wall. Lowering is $U_e^\dagger$, not a finite-wall inverse.
For vertex--edge incidence $B$ and face boundaries $C$, require
$BC^{\mathsf T}=0$. Native charge is $n=3(T_3+Y)$; a declared reference
charge $r$ gives Gauss operator $G=BE-n-r$.

The parent is the complete 114-factor, 22,273-monomial source. Its retained
binary64 coefficients are promoted to exact rationals, preserving calibrated
masses, mixing and every normal-order scalar. Replication places one local
block at each vertex and one residual bond on each edge, recovering the
parent exactly on one edge. The complete matter and field source is
\begin{align}
 H_S={}&H_{\mathrm{parent,spatial}}(U)+K_D(U)+V_{LL}+D_F+V_{QQ}
 \nonumber\\
 &+\frac{g_E}{2}\sum_eE_e^2
   +g_B\sum_f\left[I-\frac{W_f+W_f^\dagger}{2}\right],
 \qquad W_f=\prod_eU_e^{C_{fe}}.\label{eq:common-source-law}
\end{align}
The signed Dirac term retains every charged and neutral lepton spin,
family and interband term. Defaults are kinetic speed, Wilson coefficient
and field weights equal to one. The actual mass matrices anticommute
with its three kinetic matrices. Both weak additions are full left-current
squares with coupling $1/4096$; the parent semileptonic term occurs once.
Supplied quark-family shifts are $(0,2,8)$ and $(0,1,4)$, with down-family
rotation cosine $4/5$ and sine $3/5$. These choices are not physical mass
or mixing predictions. Dressing each charge-homogeneous word $O$ by

invariant. Adjoint pairing and actual hard-wall shifts preserve Hermiticity.

\subsection{Local energy and capacity}
\label{sec:common-total-energy}

Allocate $H_S=\sum_vh_v$ by sharing each word over its vertex and link
support, electric energy over edge endpoints, magnetic energy over face
vertices and scalars uniformly. Each shard is Hermitian and gauge invariant.
At each vertex, a three-state coordinate $\Phi_v\in\{-1,0,1\}$ has
lapse $N_v=I+\Phi_v/4$. Its allocated energy is
\[
 g_v=j\mathcal L_{3,v}+\frac\kappa2(\rho+b_v)\Phi_v^2
       +\frac\kappa4\sum_{u:uv}w_{uv}(\Phi_u-\Phi_v)^2,
\]
where $\mathcal L_3$ is the path Laplacian; defaults are
$j=\kappa=1/2$, $\rho=w_{uv}=1$, $b_v=0$.

Independently at every vertex, a 16-state register carries volume
$\nu\in\{1,3/2\}$, capacity $k\in\{1,2\}$ and two hardware memory bits.
Its positive lapse $M_v$ has values $3/4,5/4$. Its auxiliary energy $A_v$
has volume and capacity path-kinetic coefficients $1/2,1/8$ and diagonal
potential $\nu/(32k)+(\nu-1)^2/16+(k-1)^2/16$.
Active capacity exposes $2^k$ memory labels; the full four-state hardware
memory is retained. The law is
\begin{align}
 k_v&=g_v+N_v(h_v-e_vI)+e_vI,\nonumber\\
 H_{\mathrm{total}}&=\sum_vK_v,\qquad
 K_v=A_v+M_v(k_v-FI)+FI.\label{eq:common-total-law}
\end{align}
Local references $e_v$ and the single common outer reference $F$ default
to zero. Uniform shifts $h_v\mapsto h_v+cI$, $e_v\mapsto e_v+c$ and
$F\mapsto F+c$ add only $|V|cI$. Nonuniform shifts cannot generally be
absorbed by $F$; fixed references make source scalars affect the response.

Every factor preserves Gauss. Disconnected graph components have commuting
Hamiltonians and factorized unitaries. The full charge current weights each
shard's matter link-phase derivative by $M_vN_v$; second derivatives enter response
formulas. Energy currents $J_{vw}=i[K_v,K_w]$ obey
$\dot K_v+\sum_wJ_{vw}=0$, regional cancellation and composable coarse maps.
Free evolution is $e^{-i\tau H_{\mathrm{total}}}$ with no interleaved repair
or reset. Preparation retains its syndrome, cutoff and capacity failures;
meter work is accounted for separately. The intrinsic finite carrier has
dimension $2^{96|V|}(2L+1)^{|E|}48^{|V|}$; external apparatus adds its own.
