\paragraph{Recorded repair and a growing-volume interacting spin limit.}
\label{sec:protected-spin-limit}
For the centered two-flavour signed Hubbard source let
$H=T+UD-(U/2)N+U|V|/2$, $\tau=c|E|$, and $U\geq4\tau$.
Let $P$ include the one-fermion-per-site spin carrier. The complete
coloured field-repair instrument of
\path{model/defect_field_bridge/full_domain.py} obeys
$M_{b,r}P=\delta_{r0}P/\sqrt2$ for either repair law and coin $b$.
This uses the specified two-flavour field extension, rather than
identifying its carrier with the bare binary occupation law.
The canonical low-band estimate of
\path{model/strong_coupling_refinement} gives
\[
 H_{\rm spin}=\sum_{xy}\frac{c^2}{U}
       (\mathbf S_x\!\cdot\!\mathbf S_y-\tfrac14),\qquad
 \|W^*HW-H_{\rm spin}\|\leq3\tau^3/U^2.
\]
Telescoping full instruments, including an arbitrary untouched reference,
therefore bounds the trace norm distance of complete recorded histories
with $m$ free intervals and total absolute time $T$ by
\[
 \min\{2,8m\tau/U+6T\tau^3/U^2\}.
\]
All raw outcomes are retained. This estimate allows finite record-adaptive
repair choices; the infinite construction below uses a consistent local
clock family.

For $E=|E|\geq2$ take $c_E=JE^4$, $U_E=JE^8$, with fixed $J>0$.
Then the effective exchange is $J$, while the generator remainder is
$3J/E$. Independent rate-$\Gamma$ clocks on every edge give the averaged
full-history error
\[
 \min\{2,8(1+\Gamma ET)/E^3+6JT/E\}\longrightarrow0.
\]
The spin bond norm is $J$. On the cubic lattice the commutator-chain
estimate is bounded by
$\|A\|\|B\||X|2^{-d}(e^{48J|t|}-1)$.
The resulting boundary estimate constructs the strongly continuous
interacting quasi-local spin dynamics at fixed lattice spacing.
If $\nu_E$ is the canonical pullback of a full source ground state,
\[
 \nu_E(A^*[H_{\rm spin},A])\geq-6J\|A\|^2/E.
\]
Weak-* limits consequently provide spin ground states with positive-energy
GNS dynamics. The charged source gap instead diverges at least as
$JE^8/2-JE^5$.

An additional finite theorem uses the explicit periodic extension on
$L\times W\times W$ tori, $L$ even and $W\geq3$ odd. If the spin ground
is unique it is a singlet; the two opposite $2\pi/L$ twists are
orthogonal to it because the transverse slice contains an odd number
of spins. Their mean energy gives
$E_1-E_0\leq\pi^2JW^2/L$. A degenerate ground gives zero first spacing,
where eigenvalues count multiplicity. Canonical low-band stability adds
at most $6J/E$ to the source spacing. With $L=2W^4$ and $E=6W^6$,
\[
 (E_1-E_0)_{\rm source}\leq5J/W^2+J/W^6\longrightarrow0.
\]
This is not a bound on the gap above an entire degenerate ground space
and does not identify an infinite-volume massless particle. The complete
proof and exact/full-output checks are in
\path{model/protected_spin_limit/}. The result constructs a neutral,
interacting fixed-spacing limit; relativistic continuum scaling, physical
species, and Einstein's geometric premises remain separate obligations.
