\paragraph{Executable-model hypothesis.}
The executable model of the programme~\cite{ophmodel} runs candidate repair
and memory laws on a fixed spherical federation of twelve-port icosahedral
cells. One cell is one native observer and its twelve ports are its
interfaces; larger observers are finite, possibly overlapping patches of
cells. The main candidate law, Interval Echo, is built on the seam
interaction of the reference candidate A-contrast-memory-spherical-v1, the
contrast-memory law, at coupling $\kappa=8$: every port holds a load $0$,
$1$ or $2$, every seam between two ports carries a private memory, and the
interaction exchanges loads across seams and lowers the energy of a seam
whose two ports both hold a load. A seam record is admitted only when both
ends agree, and on these agreed records a pair of loads written on one seam
stays bound at every reading, by a theorem that holds for every number of
cells. Interval Echo adds a clock carried by each finite observer patch: the
patch counts its own repair opportunities since the last verified handoff,
and at each verified handoff a filter driven by local fair draws, calibrated on the reunion ratio of a
supplied reciprocal relay journey and reading no speed, suppresses or
releases the closing mark. With leg speeds that the observers obtain from
light pulses they time themselves, the ratio of a travelling patch's elapsed
count to the home count at reunion matches the relativistic proper-time
forecast within about one part in a thousand, on three journeys on which the
moving clock runs $3.2$ to $4.4$ percent slow. Under the second candidate law
on the same carrier, the scalar channel, an object on four seams is kept for
every interval and carries a prepared clock that an observer reads in units
of its own reference. The state of
the simulation is no spacetime: the carrier is hardware of the computation,
and a test of the model compares distributions of admissible observer records
with what observers in our world record. Observers who inhabit the simulation
can describe their experiences as relativistic $3{+}1$D continuum
spacetimes. For every finite admissible menu of observer readouts a smooth
$(1+3)$-dimensional Lorentzian field description reproduces the complete
predictions~\cite{ophsynthesis,ophEinstein}, and the spacetime paper supplies
the rank-three spatial carrier and the causal and count
limits~\cite{ophEinstein}. The records of the model meet the premises of the
finite theorems on the carrier, on instruments and on record
algebras~\cite{ophmodel}. The cosmological results below consume regional
and refinement data that the model does not produce; they are component
theorems with their own declared sources and hypotheses.
