\section{Native quantum dynamics with live noisy records}
\label{sec:noisy-native-source-refinement}

The massive process of Appendix~\ref{app:massive-operational-clocks} has
a realization in a declared coherent source, including its preparations,
fermionic operations, feedback and public reads. Its apparatus consists of
bounded patches with local processor state, ports, retained records and
repair operations. Quantum protection and a maintained classical archive
allow its complete named history to converge despite noisy local services.
This construction retains the specified source, coherent transport,
external schedule and clock parameters. It does not select them from
A1--A3.

\paragraph{The source supplies and the constructed gates.}
The primitive processor is $M_6$, with twelve central record ports and
the complete response algebra $\mathfrak u(3)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(3)$.
The existing port generators satisfy
$\sum_p D_p=\operatorname{diag}(iI_3,0)$.
The first two coordinates form a qubit code; their rotations are native
$\mathfrak u(3)$ controls. In the four-coordinate pair code, the uniform
port pulse restricts, up to a common phase, to
$\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,e^{i\theta})$.
At $\theta=\pi$ it supplies controlled-$Z$. Packing and separation use
fixed coordinate isometries, while every active basis change uses a source
pulse. The pair code is a composite experiment, not an extra primitive
fifteen-dimensional response.

Coherent code gluing requires agreement of complementary noncentral read
experiments on the transported code. Classical record agreement alone
allows complete phase loss and supplies no such channel. Pure blank
preparation, destructive readback, resets and finite private inventories
are declared interfaces; a pure blank is not inferred from an unconstrained
entropy minimum. From these interfaces the circuits construct the massive
walk on all occupation sectors, resolved CAR number instruments and a
quartic interaction. The fermionic encoding uses the credited
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0003137}{Bravyi--Kitaev edge construction};
it does not derive fermionic statistics from classical records. Complete
derivations are given in
\href{https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/r2056/docs/research/COHERENT_SOURCE_CLOCKS.md}{\emph{Coherent source codes, entropy selection and charged clocks}}
and
\href{https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/r2056/docs/research/FERMIONIC_SOURCE_CLOCKS.md}{\emph{Fermionic clocks and reads from the proper-code source}}.

\paragraph{Complete leakage reduction and causal instruments.}
For $d=2,4$, let $V_d:\mathbb C^d\to\mathbb C^6$ be the fixed code
embedding and $P_d=V_dV_d^*$. The admitted transfer distinguishes the code
from its complement. Continue its failure output by the admitted reset,
retaining the diagnostic flag. The complete Kraus family is
\[
 A_{\rm ok}=V_d^*,\qquad A_j=|0\rangle_d\langle j|_6\quad(d\le j<6),
 \qquad \sum_\alpha A_\alpha^*A_\alpha=I_6.
\]
All $A_j$ have one public failure label; $j$ is a private Kraus index.
Ignoring that flag only when defining the application marginal gives
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal R_d(\rho)=V_d^*\rho V_d+
       \operatorname{Tr}((I-P_d)\rho)|0\rangle\langle0|,
 \qquad \mathcal R_d(V_dxV_d^*)=x.
 \label{eq:native-complete-reduction}
\end{equation}
No experiment is conditioned on success. A noisy reduction, including a
wrong flag or failed reset, belongs to its complete native gate box.
Each input is reduced before a gate; the four-dimensional pair processor
is internal to a two-operand box. Helpers never supply an uncounted third
data operand. The noisy leakage-reduction theorem of
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0511065}{Aliferis and Terhal, Lemma 2},
then converts such boxes to ordinary local circuit faults with enlarged
finite library constants. Their mathematical dilation grants no physical
control of an environment or reversible access to all of $M_6$.

For a source instrument with public label $y$ and private branches
$K_{y\alpha}$, use the isometry
$\psi\mapsto\sum_{y,\alpha}K_{y\alpha}\psi\otimes|y\rangle
\otimes|y,\alpha\rangle$ and discard only the private factor.
Reversible Boolean decisions and controlled operations preserve the
resulting diagonal history blocks. Induction at successive instrument
cuts therefore gives the complete channel
\[
 \mathcal I(\rho)=\sum_h |h\rangle\langle h|\otimes\Phi_h(\rho),
\]
including rejected application outcomes and arbitrary input references.
Protection diagnostics are retained separately and never command a global
abort. Private protected records drive feedback; the public archive consists
of actual noisy central bits.

\paragraph{Noise hypotheses and the fixed-rate regime.}
Every gate, transfer, preparation, reset, flight and wait is a counted
service, including its outcome register. For fresh-environment Markov
noise with additional Hamiltonian $B_t$ and Lindblad operators $L_{\alpha t}$,
put $\Gamma(t)=2\|B_t\|+2\sum_\alpha\|L_{\alpha t}\|^2$.
Duhamel's formula and complete-channel contractivity give
$\delta\le\int\Gamma(t)\,dt$ in diamond norm, independently of the
ideal drive amplitude. Any intrinsic event error must also enter $\delta$.
The
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0605009}{Stinespring continuity theorem
of Kretschmann, Schlingemann and Werner}
gives a local dilation-fault amplitude $\eta\le C\sqrt\delta$.
This amplitude is not a failure probability; dissipative channels need not
contain a positive multiple of the ideal channel.

For fixed bounded $\Gamma$, fixed finite local classical fault rates,
$a=L/q$, complete-service exposure $O(a/c)$ and intrinsic event errors
bounded by $O(q^{-1})$, one has
$\delta=O(q^{-1})$ and $\eta=O(q^{-1/2})$.
The measurement-free computation-code construction of
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9906129}{Aharonov and Ben-Or,
Sections 4 and 7--8}, applies with the punctured RM$(5,11)$ CSS code
$[[2047,1,63]]$. Its $31$-error capacity exceeds the conservative
correction-and-gate spread $16$. Disjoint rectangles tolerate one bad
child, so a non-sparse six-level rectangle requires at least $64$ native
faults. The active volume is $O(q^5\log^{16}(2q))$; its bad amplitude
is consequently $O(q^{-27}\log^{16}(2q))$.

An all-to-all private-voter archive with $4r+1$ bits, $r=\lceil\log_2(2q)\rceil$,
keeps its live majority under $r$ old errors and $r$ fresh faulty locations.
Its refresh size is $O(\log^4 q)$, and its marked-fault tail beats every
inverse power of $q$ in this shrinking-service-error regime. Summed
rotation-synthesis error then gives
\[
 \epsilon_{\rm joint}=O(q^{-20}\log^{16}(2q)),\qquad
 \epsilon_{\rm accounting}=O(q^{-16}\log^{16}(2q)).
\]
Six is a fixed concatenation depth here because complete-service errors
shrink. A fixed nonzero error per service requires the separate construction
below. The full-interface derivation is in
\href{https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/r2056/docs/research/LEAKAGE_SOURCE_REFINEMENT.md}{\emph{Full-interface leakage, dissipation and source refinement}}.

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-strength computation and live public history]
\label{thm:fixed-strength-public-history}
For the declared source and prepared experiments above, assume
fresh-environment full-interface Markov noise and the specified local
classical faults, with a joint local fault expansion: for every set $S$
of scheduled locations, the sum
$F(S)$ of paths faulty on $S$, with other locations unrestricted, satisfies
$\|F(S)\|\le\eta^{|S|}$. Uniform proximity to the ideal complete
services supplies this condition through the dilation bound above.
There is a fixed
$\eta_*>0$ such that every fixed $0<\eta\le\eta_*$ admits a cofinal
apparatus family with $O(\log\log q)$ quantum protection levels and
$O(\log q)$ bits per public record. For a fixed library constant $\kappa$,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \epsilon_{\rm joint}&=O(q^{-20}\log^{16+2\kappa}(2q)),\\
 \epsilon_{\rm accounting}&=O(q^{-16}\log^{16+2\kappa}(2q)).
 \end{aligned}
\]
The first estimate compares decoded quantum outputs and the complete
majority-decoded named history, with every application abort retained.
The public majority remains correct throughout each successful refresh.
No elementary fault strength has to tend to zero in this regime.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use the Margulis--Gabber--Galil graph on $\mathbb Z_m^2$, with the eight
labelled neighbours
$(x\pm2y,y)$, $(x\pm(2y+1),y)$, $(x,y\pm2x)$ and
$(x,y\pm(2x+1))$, retaining loops and multiplicities. The
\href{https://isa-afp.org/browser_info/current/AFP/Expander_Graphs/Expander_Graphs_MGG.html}{formalized absolute spectral bound}
for its adjacency $A$ is $5\sqrt2/8$ after normalization.
For $N=m^2$, $D=8^{12}$ and $P=A^{12}/D$,
$\|P-J/N\|^2\le(25/32)^{12}<1/16$.
If an error set $E$ has $e\le N/8$ positions, voters with at least half
their inputs in $E$ satisfy
$|T|(3/8)^2\le\|P(1_E-e/N)\|_2^2\le e/16$;
thus $|T|\le4e/9$.

Copy the $D$ permutation matchings into private voter lanes, sort each
lane using bounded Boolean services, and copy its lower middle bit into
a separate output bank. Count every live-wire idle. A fault touches at
most one shared input and one private voter. With at most $N/16$ old
errors and $F\le N/64$ faulty services, the union of initially wrong
and ever-damaged shared positions has size $e\le5N/64$. New errors
are at most $(4/9)(N/16+F)+F\le29N/576<N/16$; the old bank keeps
its majority throughout. Integer floors strengthen these inequalities.
The complete cycle has at most $C_A N$ locations, with fixed finite $C_A$.

The sum of paths with at least $M$ faults has the exact marked-set
expansion
\[
 \sum_{|S|\ge M}(-1)^{|S|-M}\binom{|S|-1}{M-1}F(S).
\]
A path with $k\ge M$ faults has coefficient one by the binomial identity;
otherwise its coefficient is zero. Summing absolute coefficients bounds
the norm by
$\beta(V,M)\le e^{V\eta}(eV\eta/M)^M$.
Taking $M=\lfloor N/64\rfloor+1$ and
$\eta\le(1024eC_A)^{-1}$ gives $\beta_{\rm archive}\le e^{-N/32}$.

Export uses independently protected probes copied from a protected,
dephased logical record. Decode each probe one concatenation level at a
time, using lower-level protected operations. At each stage first correct
its $2047$ child blocks separately. The computation code's total-correction
property also covers arbitrary damaged child inputs. After these entry
corrections, at most $31$ child positions carry arbitrary logical damage.
The classical record decoder is exact on their entire support subspace:
descending-degree cube parities decode RM$(5,11)$, since each coefficient
has at least $64$ disjoint votes and at most $31$ are damaged. Try both
puncture fills and retain only re-encodings within punctured distance $31$;
distance $63$ makes the accepted word unique. An uncorrectable word returns
a fixed fallback with its failure diagnostic, not a postselected experiment.
The two correctable logical-label subspaces are orthogonal. Reversible
evaluation is $|\psi\rangle\mapsto|a\rangle W_a|\psi\rangle$ on each,
so discarding workspace preserves any conditional spectator state.
The logical record is dephased before export; this is not an unknown-qubit
coherent decoder.

For finite rectangle constants $A_0,S_D$, the sparse-path bound gives
$\eta_j\le A_0^{-1}(A_0\eta)^{2^j}$.
If $\theta=A_0\eta\le1/2$, all decoder stages and the terminal read have
summed bad strength
$t\le S_D\eta/(1-\theta)+\eta$.
Their union norm is at most $e^t-1\le C_E\eta$ for a fixed $C_E$ and
sufficiently small $\eta$. Distinct islands use disjoint native locations;
their marked bounds multiply without conditioning on successful protected
copying. Hence $\beta_{\rm export}\le e^{-N/8}$ when
$C_E\eta\le(256e)^{-1}$.

Enlarge $A_0$ to absorb the finite native constants and require
$A_0\ge\max(3S^2,768C_A,192C_E)$, where $S$ bounds a complete
correction/gate rectangle. The nonempty fixed interval
$0<\eta\le1/(4A_0)$ meets all the preceding conditions. Put
\[
 \ell=\lceil\log_2(2q)\rceil,\quad
 m=\lceil\sqrt{2048\ell}\rceil,\quad N=m^2,\quad
 k=\lceil\log_2(16\ell)\rceil.
\]
Then $\eta_k\le A_0^{-1}(2q)^{-32}$ and each archive/export contribution
is at most $(2q)^{-64}$. Partition native locations among protected
rectangles, export stages and refresh cycles, including every intervening
wait. For their bad majorants $\beta_i$, disjoint marked sets give the
joint bound $\|B_{\rm any}\|\le\prod_i(1+\beta_i)-1
\le\exp(\sum_i\beta_i)-1$.
This is a coherent norm estimate, not a probability union bound.
The charged volume below and rotation synthesis to $q^{-24}$ give the
stated joint error after complete decoding. Pulling back a positive logical
observable bounded by $E_{\max}=O(q^4)$ through that TP decoder multiplies
the error bound by at most $E_{\max}$ and proves the accounting estimate.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Causal schedule, cost and interpretation.}
If $s\ge2$ bounds one protection level's width and depth overhead, set
$\kappa=\lceil\log_2s\rceil$. A conservative lifetime ledger is
\[
 \begin{array}{ll}
 \text{active inventory}&O(q^4\log^{8+\kappa}(2q)),\\
 \text{scheduled depth}&O(q\log^{8+\kappa}(2q)),\\
 \text{active native volume}&O(q^5\log^{16+2\kappa}(2q)),\\
 \text{retained diagnostic bits}&O(q^5\log^{17+2\kappa}(2q)),\\
 \text{complete storage-time volume}&O(q^6\log^{25+3\kappa}(2q)).
 \end{array}
\]
Passive diagnostics are never reused by control; their independent local
Markov noise cannot change the active reduced state. This does not assert
decoupling from an arbitrary common bath. A longest local depth
$B_q=O(\log^{8+\kappa}(2q))$, service time $a/(1024cB_q)$ and local
cluster radius at most $a/(131072B_q)$ fit all compiled local work into
fixed padded source slots. Shared processors serialize; separate lanes
operate in parallel. Encoded constituents cross each original link
concurrently, with duration at least length$/c$ and correction at endpoints.
A common $q$-independent wall-time dilation preserves the native clock and
velocity comparison. Faster drives, parallel interfaces, resets and growing
inventory are paid resources, not fixed-device predictions.

The fixed noise interval has a concrete inhabitant: apply the independent
full-carrier channel
$\mathcal E_p(\rho)=(1-p)\rho+p\operatorname{Tr}(\rho)|5\rangle\langle5|$
and classical bit flips at every counted native component and storage
location. Its dilation distance from the ideal is
$\sqrt{2-2\sqrt{1-p}}\le\sqrt{2p}$. A sufficiently small fixed $p>0$
therefore meets the complete-box bound for every $q$.
No physical selection of this noise law is inferred. The expander degree
$8^{12}=68{,}719{,}476{,}736$ and the library constants are large; no
practical hardware threshold is numerically estimated.
