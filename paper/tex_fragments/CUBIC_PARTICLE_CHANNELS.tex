\paragraph{Actual spatial channels of the finite spectrum.}
\label{sec:cubic-particle-channels}
On the unchanged centered cube, lift each proper rotation about its center
by $D(q)=P_q\otimes U(q)$ in the original Weyl frame. The 48 lifts form
the binary octahedral group and the full CAR lift has central element
$(-1)^N$. In the hidden frame, $D(q)=S_q\otimes W_q$; the unique ground
is A1, while $\operatorname{Ad}W_q$ has character A2+E, rather than
the spatial-vector character T1. Exact character orthogonality, source
covariance and central projectors are verified in
\path{model/cubic_particle_channels/}.

The signed adjacency obeys $A^2=3I$ and its automorphism commutant is
$\operatorname{span}\{I,A\}$. Each of the 228 certified charged residues
is $w(I\pm A/\sqrt3)\otimes I_2$ and has visible representation
G1+G2+H, with complete-probe trace fractions $1/4,1/4,1/2$.
Each neutral channel has 57, 54 and 24 positive lines in Walsh degrees
one, two and three. Density transforms respectively as T1,T2,A2;
the three hidden-spin components as T1+2T2, 2T1+T2, A1+E.
In particular, the first full-Fock triplet at energy
$[0.01502125417612,0.01502125417614]$ is A1+E, not a spatial vector.
The first density line is T1 and every charged line has the same
G1+G2+H visible channels. These are finite spatial quantum numbers and
source-clock gaps. Cubic labels alone do not fix continuum spin, a
relativistic rest mass, or an observed species.
